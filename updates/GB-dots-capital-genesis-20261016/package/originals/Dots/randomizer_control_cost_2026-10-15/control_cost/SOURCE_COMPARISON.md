Checked 2026-10-15: He Wang, [Continuous-Process Randomized Compilation for Quantum Process Tensors, arXiv:2610.01521v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01521v2), version dated October 3, 2026; [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01521).

Sections V.3 and Appendix F.5 compare finite-width controls with ideal jumps using a uniform full joint Hamiltonian mismatch norm. Appendix J.7–J.8 charges expected Poisson pulse count, infeasible overlaps and boundary controls, and discusses a finite optimum of its sufficient envelope. The final inverse must be counted when the implementation needs it. This is a global operational comparison. It does not supply the spatial all-subset CP fault interface required here.

The present note therefore does not infer stochastic fault probabilities from that norm comparison. Its CP theorem adds an explicitly stronger branch premise; its coherent theorem instead uses local residual action, a mixed Fourier/Duhamel proof, and a separate character-support condition. The finite-width and rate/error tradeoff mechanisms are established prior art. No literature priority claim is made.
