# Exact stabilizer-randomization interface for completed local correction

Status: independently derived candidate lemma, with exact rational and floating-point finite-code checks. This is a bounded mathematical bridge, not a microscopic hardware robustness theorem or a novelty claim.

## 1. Completed proposal and exact two-frame identity

Let Pi_1,...,Pi_7 be orthogonal stabilizer-function projectors, Pi_0=I-sum_j Pi_j, Q_0=I, and Q_j Pauli corrections. Write K_j=Q_j Pi_j and C(X)=sum_j K_j X K_j^dagger.

For every stabilizer S, S K_j S=+/-K_j; hence Ad_S C Ad_S=C on ALL input operators, with arbitrary untouched reference. This uses commutation of S with each Pi_j and Pauli commutation with Q_j, not a restriction to the code space.

If U=exp(-i theta P) and SPS=-P, then SUS=U^dagger and

    (Ad_U C + Ad_S Ad_U C Ad_S)/2
      = cos(theta)^2 C + sin(theta)^2 Ad_P C.

The two actual framed implementations therefore produce an exact state-independent mixture with q=sin(theta)^2. Merely declaring a Pauli twirling approximation does not establish this identity for a physical device.

## 2. Arbitrary fixed local unitary: precise assumptions

Let A be the bounded error patch. Ignore overall Pauli phases when discussing Pauli subgroups. Choose a stabilizer subgroup G with the following detection-completeness property:

    P supported on A commutes with G iff P commutes with the full code stabilizer group S.

Assume no local logical operator on A:

    Pauli(A) intersect centralizer(S) = Pauli(A) intersect S, modulo phases.

Let M_1,...,M_m generate S_A, the stabilizers supported in A, and let R_r be their nonzero joint spectral projectors. Define D_M(X)=sum_r R_r X R_r and

    C_ref = C D_M,
    L_{j,r}=Q_j Pi_j R_r.

This is a genuine extra stabilizer measurement, retaining the original correction decision. The aggregate inactive Pi_0 MUST NOT be treated as measuring all syndrome bits.

Every L_{j,r} has its range in one definite M sector: Pauli Q_j merely flips specified M eigenvalues. Thus D_M C_ref=C_ref. Also C_ref has the same stabilizer covariance as C.

For a bounded-degree geometrically local code, G may be generated by all local checks intersecting A. Their union lies in a bounded neighborhood of A. At most 2|A| independent commutation functionals suffice. For codes with nonlocal checks, bounded A alone does NOT imply a bounded-support implementation of G. S_A itself consists of operators supported in A and has a commuting generating set of at most |A| independent elements. Measuring them still has physical circuitry/reset/latency costs.

## 3. Full-input CP-domination theorem and proof

Let U supported in A be unitary, epsilon=||U-I||<1, and choose q with epsilon^2<=q<1. Let

    T_U = average_{g in G} Ad_{g U g},
    T = average_g Ad_g Ad_U C_ref Ad_g = T_U C_ref.

Then

    T >=_CP (1-q) C_ref.

More strongly there is a CPTP map F_A acting only on A such that

    T=(1-q) C_ref + q F_A C_ref.

For q=0 the conclusion is T=C_ref and no residual map is needed. Global phase may be optimized in epsilon, since Ad_U is phase-invariant.

Proof: decompose U into conjugation characters,

    U_chi = |G|^-1 sum_g chi(g)^* g U g.

Character orthogonality gives T_U(X)=sum_chi U_chi X U_chi^dagger and sum_chi U_chi^dagger U_chi=I. The trivial block U_0 contains only zero-syndrome patch Paulis. The assumptions imply that U_0 is a polynomial in the M operators, hence scalar a_r on R_r.

Let D=U-I and E_G be conjugation averaging. Then

    sum_{chi != 0} U_chi^dagger U_chi
      = E_G(D^dagger D) - E_G(D)^dagger E_G(D)
      <= epsilon^2 I.

Therefore U_0^dagger U_0 >= (1-epsilon^2)I and |a_r|^2>=1-q.

The map T_U D_M-(1-q)D_M has explicit Kraus operators

    sqrt(|a_r|^2-1+q) R_r,
    U_chi R_r   (chi != 0).

Its adjoint maps I to qI; dividing by q defines CPTP F_A on A. Since D_M C_ref=C_ref, the claimed factorization follows. This support refinement was independently pointed out by the verifier.

Alternatively direct residual Kraus for T-(1-q)C_ref are sqrt(|a_{j,r}|^2-1+q)L_{j,r} and U_chi L_{j,r}. The argument proves a Choi/operator CP order on all inputs, not merely a bound on encoded states or average fidelity.

Every nonempty character block can also be written U_chi=P_chi f_chi(M) for a representative patch Pauli P_chi: two patch Paulis with the same character differ by a member of S_A. After measuring M, each block is a scalar times P_chi on each sector. Its probability may depend on the measured sector. The constant q minorant is what restores a state-independent Bernoulli representation.

## 4. Compatibility and limits of the memory interface

Every definite full-syndrome Pauli orbit EV is already an eigenspace of every added M. Thus C_ref and C agree on each orbit density E V rho V^dagger E^dagger. The classical correction transition rules are unchanged. The simultaneous-Pauli-history argument tolerates the extra stabilizer projectors: each individual history survives or is killed. This is NOT equality of C and C_ref on arbitrary syndrome coherences; their full generators differ.

For rate-kappa Poisson proposal times,

    kappa(T-Id)
      = kappa(1-q)(C_ref-Id) + kappa q(F_A C_ref-Id).

Independent thinning yields clean correction proposals at rate kappa(1-q) and fault proposals at rate kappa q. The clean rate reduction must be charged. The complete fault event F_A C_ref has the enlarged correction gadget support, even though its post-correction fault factor F_A is supported on A.

Fresh independently chosen frames and fixed frame-independent U suffice for composition of the averaged channels. This does not establish the same statement for gate-dependent noise, unmodeled classical feedback, or joint bath errors. Revealing/using the random frame record downstream can invalidate a claim based only on the marginalized averaged channel.

Charge the pulses, their union of supports, ancillas, resets, refinement measurements, timing, and errors during latency. Virtual frame bookkeeping does not automatically conjugate a physical error. An exact ideal-frame identity is not a proof that real noisy pulses satisfy it.

## 5. Failure without refinement, and failure with local logicals

Four-qubit seven-active-branch counterexample: take S generated by Z_1,...,Z_4. For computational basis indices j=0,...,6, choose Pi_j=|j><j| and Pauli Q_j taking |j> to |0000>. Let Pi_0 project onto the remaining nine basis states. (The active index naming may be shifted to 1,...,7.)

The vector psi=(|0111>+|1000>)/sqrt(2) lies in the inactive sector. Set U=exp(-i theta Z_1). All stabilizers commute with U, so every stabilizer twirl leaves it unchanged. C fixes |psi><psi|, whereas Ad_U C gives a different pure state for generic nonzero small theta. A rank-one output cannot dominate any positive multiple of the different rank-one input projector. Thus no coefficient p>0 in T>=_CP p C exists, despite absence of all local logicals. Measuring Z_1 repairs this example exactly.

The independent verifier supplied an even smaller example: S=<Z_1,Z_2> on three qubits (third qubit logical), C resets qubit 1 and leaves qubit 2 untouched, U=exp(-i theta Z_2). For the unaffected qubit-2 subchannel, a 2x2 Choi principal minor of Ad_U-lambda Id has determinant -4 lambda sin(theta)^2. This proves failure for every lambda>0 when sin(theta)!=0.

If A contains a logical Pauli L, its rotation survives all stabilizer measurements and twirls. A pure logical superposition gives the same obstruction. The no-local-logical condition is essential to this construction.

## 6. Exact algebraic certificate

Run: python check_exact.py. Only installed SymPy is used; no downloaded code.

Use the four-qubit seven-branch C above and refine by Z_1,Z_2. Put c=99/101, s=20/101 and

    U=(c I-i s X_1)(c I-i s X_1 Z_2).

Each factor is exactly unitary. Its distance squared to I is 2-2c=4/101, so the triangle inequality gives ||U-I||^2<=16/101. Set q=16/101.

The only nonzero twirl character blocks are

    U_0=c^2 I-s^2 Z_2,
    U_1=-i c s X_1(I+Z_2).

For output Z_2=-1: a=1, nontrivial probability 0, residual identity weight q=16/101.
For output Z_2=+1: a=9401/10201, nontrivial probability 15681600/104060401, residual identity weight 803216/104060401>0.

## 7. Bounded joint bath-instrument extension

This is DISTINCT from independent rarefault decomposition.

Let U_AB be a joint unitary with ||U_AB-I_A tensor V_B||<=delta for a bath unitary V_B. Assume the same local detection/no-logical/refinement conditions, and ideal frame randomization acting only on the memory. Expand U in patch Paulis with bath operator coefficients. Character blocks then satisfy

    U_chi L_y = (P_chi L_y) tensor A_{chi,y},

where y=(j,r), L_y=Q_j Pi_j R_r, P_0=I, and A depends only on the corresponding output M sector (notation permits repeated y). The equality follows by factoring each block's representative P_chi and evaluating its remaining stabilizer polynomial on that output sector. Thus the twirled refined channel has these joint Kraus operators.

For each feasible output sector, exact joint unitarity gives

    sum_chi A_{chi,y}^dagger A_{chi,y}=I_B.

Apply the same Fourier estimate to D=U-I tensor V_B. Its nontrivial character blocks equal those of U, so

    sum_{chi != 0} U_chi^dagger U_chi <= delta^2 I_AB,
    sum_{chi != 0} A_{chi,y}^dagger A_{chi,y} <= delta^2 I_B.

Hence, even for a bath entangled with the memory/reference, the conditional probability of chi != 0 after any feasible measured y is at most delta^2. The chi labels define a valid Kraus instrument/unravelling; they need not be observable hardware records.

At a fixed, exogenous ordered list of proposal slots, with fresh frame averaging at each slot and a uniform conditional version of these hypotheses, the tower property yields

    Pr(all slots in a specified set J have nontrivial chi) <= product_{i in J} delta_i^2.

Past bath states and past instrument outcomes may be arbitrarily correlated. This is a local-stochastic upper bound on labels. It is NOT an input-independent schedule law or independent coin decomposition. The probability law may depend on the initial joint state. No claim is made here about arbitrary continuous system-bath coupling or gate-dependent/noisy randomizers.

A corresponding memory theorem would require an explicit instrument/trace-decreasing good-branch argument, not direct substitution into the existing theorem's fixed-schedule CPTP conditioning step. On good histories the same-decoder Kraus factorization may remain usable, but the probabilistic and channel-completion steps must be stated afresh.

Counterexample to common-bath-channel CP domination: let the measured local stabilizer be Z_A and U_AB=exp(-i theta Z_A tensor Z_B). It is uniformly close to I and has zero nontrivial character weight. The two measured sectors instead induce bath unitaries V_+=exp(-i theta Z_B) and V_-=exp(+i theta Z_B). If T >= p(D_Z tensor Lambda_B) for some p>0 and CPTP Lambda_B, restriction to either memory sector forces J(Lambda_B) to lie in the rank-one support of J(Ad_{V_+}) and of J(Ad_{V_-}). For generic nonzero small theta these supports differ, so their intersection is zero. Such Lambda_B cannot exist. The bath operation can depend on the measured syndrome even when there are no bad labels at all.

## 9. Further candidate: non-refined encoded-orbit instrument transfer

This section assesses the root's proposed extension. It is distinct from the full-input CP minorant of Sections 2-4. The classical monotonicity/certificate premise below must be checked separately; it is not established by the unitary algebra.

### 9.1 Refinement is unnecessary for the encoded-orbit argument

Use the original C, without D_M, followed by the exact JOINT memory-bath stabilizer twirl of U_AB. By detection completeness and absence of local logicals,

    U_chi=P_chi f_chi(S_A; bath),

where f is a stabilizer polynomial with ordered bath-operator coefficients. For any full-syndrome Pauli orbit EV,

    U_chi (EV tensor I_B) = P_chi EV tensor A_{chi,s(E)}.

This remains true for arbitrary logical-bath entangled input states. The trivial character has P_0=I and preserves that orbit; it need not act as memory identity on a general superposition of syndrome sectors. In particular it can enact a syndrome-dependent bath filter. This nuance is essential.

A fixed record of correction Kraus indices and twirl characters therefore follows one Pauli orbit, rather than a coherent sum of several possible Pauli support histories. The original aggregate inactive projector merely preserves or kills that orbit; it need not resolve all full syndromes. Every original correction proposal still occurs first, at its original rate kappa, and a nontrivial character adds a bounded patch Pauli afterwards at the same time. Character zero leaves the error configuration unchanged.

The global estimate

    sum_{chi!=0} U_chi^dagger U_chi <= delta^2 I_AB

does not require refinement. It bounds the conditional probability of a nontrivial character for every current joint state and every causal history satisfying the uniform norm assumption. The characters are Kraus instrument labels, not necessarily physically read measurement results.

### 9.2 Conditional domination and exact order

Condition on a full exogenous clock realization h, independent of the initial input/bath. At the i-th slot, provided its conditional joint randomized channel obeys the hypotheses for every past record, the bad indicator B_i satisfies

    Pr(B_i=1 | h, complete previous instrument record)<=p_i,

with p_i=delta_i^2 (or a uniform upper bound). It follows also after conditioning only on previous bad indicators. Standard sequential uniform-variable coupling gives an input-dependent coupling B_i<=Z_i with independent Bernoulli(p_i) Z_i conditional on h. Thus every increasing event in the bad indicators is upper-bounded by its reference independent-mark probability.

This is NOT a state-independent physical mixture. Different initial states may induce different B laws and couplings, but the numerical bound is uniform. Marginalizing h produces independent Bernoulli-marked reference Poisson clocks. Births and corrections share the same parent clocks; they are not independent streams in this route. The exact same-time order is correction then birth. A classical certificate must respect that order or explicitly overbound it.

The clock flags (including any fast/slow-window flags) depend only on h. For each fixed h the chosen failure envelope must be increasing in added bad marks. Do not assume monotonicity of the actual correction evolution itself: extra errors may trigger a correction differently. A universal support-history infection envelope can be monotone even when the physical dynamics is not. The required monotonicity belongs to that auxiliary envelope/certificate and must be verified there.

Exogenous clocks and nonanticipating operations are sufficient for conditioning on the complete clock realization if the same norm bound holds on every conditional causal history. An averaged calibration bound is insufficient. Unbounded continuous interactions between clock times are not automatically covered.

### 9.3 Trace-nonincreasing good-map completion

Suppose a location/mark certificate certifies every recorded Pauli history selected as good, and the common decoder maps every such history to scalar logical identity. After averaging over h and summing good Kraus records, the decoded good map has form

    G=Id_L tensor Lambda_g,

with Lambda_g CP and trace-nonincreasing, not generally TP. Let the complete decoded channel be Gamma, and the bad CP map B=Gamma-G. Trace preservation implies

    B^*(I)=I_L tensor D,
    D=I_B-Lambda_g^*(I),  0<=D<=I.

If the uniform classical/instrument bound gives bad probability at most epsilon for every encoded joint input, then D<=epsilon I. Fix any bath output state sigma and define

    Lambda_b(X)=Tr(DX) sigma.

Lambda_g+Lambda_b is CPTP. Both B and Id_L tensor Lambda_b have diamond norm ||D||<=epsilon (the diamond norm of a CP map equals its maximum trace effect). Therefore

    ||Gamma-Id_L tensor (Lambda_g+Lambda_b)||_diamond <= 2 epsilon.

After tracing the output bath this gives

    ||Tr_B' Gamma-Tr_B||_diamond <= 2 epsilon.

The domain includes the joint encoded logical-bath input, so initial logical-bath/reference correlations are allowed. This argument repairs the gap that would arise from treating a fixed good Kraus-record subset as a CPTP conditioned circuit.

### 9.4 Mandatory joint-frame premise

The random frame must realize the actual joint average on memory and persistent bath. It must be fresh and erased/discarded, without an accessible retained frame label that changes future channels. Gate/frame-dependent U does not obey the displayed conjugation average merely because the frame is random. A memory-only averaged channel can hide surviving bath correlations. Syndrome records and character labels used for mathematical unravelling are distinct from a leaked physical frame record. These are physical assumptions, not consequences of the norm estimate.
