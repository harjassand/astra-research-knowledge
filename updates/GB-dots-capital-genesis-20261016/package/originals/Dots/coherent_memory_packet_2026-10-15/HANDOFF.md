## Capability

Under the explicit ideal completed-proposal, joint-twirl and uniform joint-norm hypotheses, coherent memory–bath post-errors at every correction attempt admit a bounded-mark support comparison. The composed bound is min(2, A L⁴(1+κT) exp(−cL)) for the decoded joint-input logical error, for sufficiently weak stipulated noise and sufficiently large compatible L=bM. The result accommodates an arbitrary finite persistent bath and initial logical–bath–reference entanglement.

## Missing physical operation

Build a repeatable completed syndrome-conditioned correction module together with its randomization, ancilla/controller reset, connectivity and finite duration, and establish the required bounded-patch joint error model. A memory-only twirled marginal or small average infidelity does not establish the theorem's uniform joint-bath premise. The clocks must remain exogenous; accessible frame records and uncontrolled interactions between proposals are excluded.

## Consequence

The construction closes a specified mathematical post-proposal coherent-error interface, including bath-filter cases excluded by a common independent rare-fault mixture. It leaves hardware feasibility, arbitrary noisy gates, continuous system–bath coupling, noisy readout and useful numerical operating thresholds unresolved. Fixed-support 4D geometry is an architectural assumption, not a demonstrated 3D implementation.

## Decisive test

Specify one complete correction/randomization module and two overlapping primal/dual modules. Test active and inactive branches on coherent inputs, reset and frame leakage, correction-before-error ordering, finite duration and spatial error spread. Establish a state-uniform joint norm/instrument bound, fixed bounded patches and exogenous clocks, or identify the failed premise. Then evaluate increasing code sizes under that verified model. An unsuccessful module or a pessimistic sufficient constant does not prove physical impossibility.
