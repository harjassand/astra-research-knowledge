# Eight-site Schlögl: bounded research result

## Capability and evidence
A portable exact rational certificate rules out a useful initial bound from every positive static product envelope with the actual target prescribed exactly one and a scalar exponential time prefactor, for the specified eight-site deadline-one first-hit problem. Its 325 positive weights balance all 326 orbit coordinates; the independent checker additionally expands four graph symmetries and checks all 1,304 site-specific coordinates. The exact killing charge is about 2.666777495494622e-22, below 3e-22. Thus every such envelope starts above 1−3e-22. This is an envelope obstruction, NOT a probability lower bound. Adding the previously established overflow allowance gives a trivial clipped upper bound of one.

## Arbitrary common scalar: a distinct, weaker floor
The same exact occupation certificate also excludes useful rare-event bounds from g(t,n)=a(t) product_i h_i(n_i), with arbitrary static positive site factors and any positive differentiable common scalar with a finite nonnegative terminal limit. The initial envelope is strictly greater than 1/4. The exact checker verifies target flux H=1, occupation mass W<8/11 and the rational exponential bounds used by the analytic proof. This is a separate weaker floor; the near-one result above applies to a single exponential prefactor only. Neither floor bounds the physical hitting probability.

## Exact scope
The model has an open 2×4 grid, initial counts all 50, target all counts at most 15, deadline 1 and killing on first count 163. The leading certificate uses actual fixed target payoff one in its two target-entering rows. Its log-coordinate balance is zero in every coordinate, with no low-profile restrictions. Negative scalar prefactors reduce to zero without changing the initial value, as proved in OVERWRITTEN_PRODUCT_OBSTRUCTION.md. A separate 324-state native-boundary certificate is retained as an independently checked intermediate; its 15 target-entry rows cannot simply be reused for overwrite. Genuinely time-varying profiles, pair factors, sums of products and tensor approximations remain outside these obstructions.

## Closest prior methods
The proof uses ordinary linear-programming duality/occupation balances and the tangent bound for exponential Lyapunov barriers. The related construction interfaces use standard Feynman–Kac reasoning, max-sum variable elimination, tensor-network evolution and Markov-semigroup residual estimates. No foundational method novelty is claimed. Nicholson–Gingrich's 2023 PRX absorbing-target tensor evolution is the strongest acquired comparator; its published experiment and timings are not a matched certified solve of this instance.

## Missing operation
No full-cap, full-horizon tensor first-hit integration with a validated complete time-integrated residual has been performed. The adapted generator has an exact MPO bond bound at most 16; that is an OPERATOR bound, not a bound on state or residual rank. Acquired source defects are conditional static findings; author code was not executed. The weighted-residual interface is mathematically checked but has not certified an actual eight-site trajectory.

## Consequence
This packet closes a broad static-product route and specifies a stronger, matched next comparison. It does not solve the rare-event probability, establish a speedup, or exclude tensor methods. The reported splitting mean about 3.8e-8 and overflow allowance about 7.57e-10 are inherited source results, not rerun or independently re-proved by this packet.

## Decisive next test
Use the actual eight-site graph and all 163 local states. First verify an exact sparse-support/TT operator and weighted-residual contraction on a short polynomial segment, including success/failure sinks and all temporal cross terms. Then measure one complete conditional reaction/diffusion splitting sweep against two half-sweeps, reporting cut ranks and tails. A probability certificate still requires the full continuous reconstruction, validated time integral, initialization/jump/compression errors and independent overflow allowance. Isolated gates or sampled residuals do not meet that stopping condition.
