## 1. Result and scope

Exact trusted stabilizer measurements at both endpoints allow the independently uniform initial stabilizer frame and the tracked final inverse to be introduced purely mathematically. They need not be physically implemented. The Fourier character of the mathematical frame is exactly the actual observed syndrome mismatch, including the deterministic syndrome shift of the correction that precedes the weak interval.

For a fixed exogenous finite schedule, clean fully completed pulses, independent rate-gamma_ia stabilizer-jump streams, and a common frame-independent dilation satisfying the locality/toggling conditions below, the actual measured joint-bad effect obeys

    E_actual[ Delta_i != 0 for every i in S ]
      <= product_(i in S) q_i I,

    q_i = min{1, sum_(a=1)^m_i B(J_i,gamma_ia,tau_i)}.

In particular, if all generator rates in pulse i equal gamma_i,

    q_i = min{1, m_i B(J_i,gamma_i,tau_i)}.

Here E_actual denotes a positive effect, summed over all genuine quantum/classical measurement and dilation outcomes and averaged over the selected jump streams. This is an operator inequality on the full input memory and shared bath, uniform under an untouched reference. It is not a bound conditioned and normalized on an intermediate measurement history.

The function is the exact one from the direct theorem:

    B(J,gamma,tau) = ( exp(-gamma tau)
       [cosh(d tau)+(gamma/d)sinh(d tau)] - 1 )/2,
    d = sqrt(gamma^2+4J^2).

The continuous gamma=0 value is sinh^2(J tau).

The effect assertion needs neither detection completeness nor absence of a local logical. Those additional assumptions enter only when a zero mismatch is interpreted as adding no memory support in a common-decoder history.

## 2. Exact single-pulse identity

Let G be generated by commuting Hermitian Pauli stabilizers S_a, without -I. Generator dependencies are harmless: discard inconsistent syndrome labels and, if desired, use an independent generating set. Using redundant bits merely weakens the final union bound.

For a consistent syndrome s, write P_s for its joint spectral projector and

    S_a P_s = (-1)^s_a P_s.

Let Q_j(s) be the actual prescribed Pauli correction, completed before the weak interval. It need not reset the measured checks to their +1 sectors. Define

    r = s xor syn_G(Q_j(s)),
    chi_u(g) = the eigenvalue of g on P_u.

Then, as a full operator identity,

    g Q_j(s) P_s = chi_r(g) Q_j(s) P_s.

Let U_eta be the actual weak-interval propagator including its ideal instantaneous stabilizer jumps. Let eta_T be their cumulative product. It belongs to G even though no controller reads or tracks it. For the actual postmeasurement outcome t, the branch operator is

    A_(s,t,eta) = P_t U_eta Q_j(s) P_s.

Introduce a mathematical uniform g in G immediately after Q_j(s), and a mathematical inverse (g eta_T)^dagger immediately before the postmeasurement. Its branch is

    Atilde_(g,s,t,eta)
      = P_t (g eta_T)^dagger U_eta g Q_j(s) P_s
      = chi_(r xor t)(g) chi_t(eta_T) A_(s,t,eta).

All characters are real signs in this commuting stabilizer setting. Thus the factor has unit modulus. The actual and mathematical circuits have identical CP maps for every genuine endpoint-record branch and every fixed jump history, after physical records are treated as classical outcomes or their inaccessible measurement environments are traced.

The initial g is an analytical variable, not a claimed physical randomness resource. The inverse is also analytical. The identity follows from both endpoint projectors and correction-first ordering; it does not follow merely from stabilizer membership of the jumps.

The mismatch is exactly

    Delta = r xor t = s xor syn_G(Q_j(s)) xor t.

Replacing it by t, or by s xor t, is generally wrong. Those simplifications require, respectively, r=0 or a correction preserving every measured check.

## 3. Global branch identity, including crossing intervals and reuse

Fix a complete genuine physical record alpha, including every relevant pre/post syndrome and correction branch, but do not normalize on that record. Insert the mathematical initial and final wrappers at each pulse's own endpoint.

An initial g_i is immediately adjacent to the corrected sector map Q_i(s_i)P_(s_i), and its inverse is immediately adjacent to P_(t_i). Therefore the single-pulse scalar identities strip the wrappers directly inside the full chronological record map. This operation does not move any bath interaction or serialize overlapping intervals. It yields

    Atilde_(g,alpha,eta)
       = [product_i chi_(Delta_i(alpha))(g_i)
                    chi_(t_i(alpha))(eta_i,T)] A_(alpha,eta).

This phase identity itself does not need disjoint pulse supports. Disjoint full reservations are needed later to derive the additive toggling interface with the stipulated strengths.

Fourier transforming all selected mathematical initial frames gives

    K_(chi,alpha,eta)
      = E_g [conjugate(chi(g)) Atilde_(g,alpha,eta)]
      = 1_{chi=Delta(alpha)} c_(alpha,eta) A_(alpha,eta),

where |c_(alpha,eta)|=1. With only a subset transformed, the equality is restricted to those coordinates.

Consequently, for every jump history and every syndrome-character predicate F,

    sum_(alpha: F(Delta(alpha))) A_(alpha,eta)^dagger A_(alpha,eta)
      = sum_(chi: F(chi)) sum_alpha
                   K_(chi,alpha,eta)^dagger K_(chi,alpha,eta).

This is exact equality of positive effects. It justifies transferring a Fourier-effect estimate to the ACTUAL measured event. It is stronger than merely constructing an unrelated complete Kraus family.

The record assumption matters. A reversible coherent syndrome computation whose outcomes are later erased or recombined is not automatically an actual measurement instrument with these separately retained branches. In that model the branch-dependent phases can change later interference. A standard exact instrument, with ordinary feedback through a genuine classical record and its common dilation, is sufficient.

## 4. Why the direct finite-rate Fourier theorem applies

For pulse i let eta_i(t) be the cumulative product of its physical stabilizer jumps since the weak interval began. The mathematical circuit is represented in the toggling frame by

    H_(g,eta)(t) = H_base(t)
          + sum_i Ad(g_i eta_i(t))[V_i(t)].

Conjugation/inverse conventions agree because the commuting stabilizer products are Hermitian involutions; an equivalent convention gives the same signs.

This representation requires:

1. The starts, endpoints, reserved patches, and clean/pending selection are fixed by exogenous geometry, independently of the selected jump streams and genuine quantum measurement outcomes, or have an explicitly verified common-dilation generalization with fixed allowed windows. One may not establish it by conditioning on an endogenous branch and changing the model there.
2. A pulse's entire physical stabilizer, endpoint-measurement, correction, ancilla, and error support is included in its reservation. Throughout its weak interval, any concurrent operation outside the pulse is memory-disjoint from the FULL reservation, or is independently proved to commute with every relevant frame generator. Sharing the bath is allowed.
3. All ideal correction operations are completed before the weak interval, and the endpoint projector really measures the same G. No uncharged overlapping operation contributes to the syndrome change during the interval.
4. Every memory-coupled weak term assigned to pulse i is in V_i, supported on its stipulated error patch A_i and the shared bath, with a uniform operator norm at most J_i. Bath-only dynamics can remain in H_base. Noise outside these stipulated pulses is charged separately rather than silently ignored.
5. Selected Poisson streams are mutually independent, exogenous, and not used by another physical controller or coefficient. A fixed common measurement/control dilation is used under every seed value and every selected telegraph trajectory. Ordinary outcome-mediated control is allowed only when this structural interface remains true in the common dilation.
6. The ideal jumps are instantaneous and stay within the unchanged reservation. Finite-duration or faulty jump hardware, correlated streams, changed endpoints, and enlarged resource conflicts require a separate model qualification.

Under these conditions, all active mathematical frames conjugate only their own weak terms. Their removal at completion leaves no selected path/seed dependence in future operations, so later reuse of the same support is allowed. Crossing intervals on disjoint reserved memory supports are also allowed, despite noncommuting shared-bath interactions.

For each marked pulse choose one generator witness a_i for a nonzero syndrome coordinate, and condition only the OTHER initial frame coordinates and update trajectories. Let f_i be the remaining mathematical initial bit and xi_i(t)=(-1)^N_ia_i(t) its selected physical telegraph sign. Decompose the conditioned pulse interaction into its even and odd parts under S_ia_i:

    Ad(g_i eta_i(t))[V_i(t)]
       = V_i^+(t) + (-1)^f_i xi_i(t) V_i^-(t),
    ||V_i^-(t)|| <= J_i.

The even term is independent of both f_i and the full selected xi_i trajectory. It joins H_base. The exact direct theorem now bounds the effect of odd selected Fourier bit on every marked pulse by the product of their B values. Summing witness choices gives product_i sum_a B_i,a. Dropping constraints for factors above one gives the independent caps in q_i.

The selected jump average must remain INSIDE the quantum effect estimate. Conditioning on a no-jump path generally destroys the finite-rate improvement. One may fix such a path for the exact phase/support identities and average it afterward for the moment bound.

## 5. What this does and does not prove for support locality

The existing memory-only Dyson regrouping argument transfers because the mathematical toggled circuit has exactly the pulse-local conjugation form, and its record Kraus operators differ from the actual ones only by scalar phases. At a clean pulse completion, its net Pauli E_i is supported on A_i and has

    syn_(G_i)(E_i) = Delta_i.

No bath coefficients are reordered. Each fixed actual measurement record can still be a coherent sum of many chronological Pauli histories; a positive classical Pauli mixture is not asserted.

To replace Delta_i=0 by no additional local support, require the precise detection property

    for every Pauli E supported in A_i,
    syn_(G_i)(E)=0 implies E belongs to the global code stabilizer group
    up to phase.

A standard sufficient pair is: G_i detects the full global stabilizer syndrome of every A_i-supported Pauli, and A_i supports no nontrivial logical Pauli. Mere absence of a local logical does not make an incomplete measurement group sufficient. Conversely, complete local syndrome detection alone does not eliminate a local logical.

For nonzero reachable Delta, choose one A_i-supported representative. Paulis with the same measured character differ by an A_i-supported code stabilizer, which acts by a scalar on a Pauli-error orbit of the full code space. The same fixed-record support/common-decoder argument follows, with actual Delta labels in place of inaccessible global Fourier labels. Pending pulses without completed trusted endpoints remain charged under the original pending/full-reservation treatment.

The postmeasurements are additional exact stabilizer instruments if they were not already present in the source model. They preserve the Pauli-orbit support argument but change general syndrome coherences. This is a theorem for the measured model; it is not equality with an unmeasured original channel.

## 6. Sharp failure tests

- Missing initialization sector: without an actual premeasurement or another proved sector preparation, g need not act by a scalar. For example, G=<Z>, initial |+>, V=0, no correction, and an arbitrarily declared r=0 gives postmeasurement t=1 with probability 1/2 even though B(0,gamma,tau)=0.
- Wrong correction bookkeeping: with G=<Z>, initial s=0, correction X, and V=0, the correct mismatch is Delta=(s xor 1) xor t=0. Calling t alone a bad event makes it certain while B=0.
- Uncharged overlapping memory action: with G=<Z>, V=0 but an additional X gate between the endpoints, the actual mismatch can be certain. Toggling conjugates that X gate, so it cannot be left in a selected-seed-independent baseline. Full reservation/commutation is substantive.
- Incomplete measured group despite no local logical: take code stabilizers X1X2 and Z1Z2, with a separate logical qubit 3, error patch A={1}, and measured G=<Z1Z2>. There is no nontrivial logical on A, yet Z1 is invisible to G and is not a code stabilizer. It creates an unmeasured syndrome, so Delta=0 cannot mean no support.
- Local logical despite full measured syndrome: for the two-qubit repetition code with stabilizer Z1Z2 and A={1}, the logical Z1 is undetected. A weak Z1 Hamiltonian gives Delta=0 while changing the logical state. The measured-effect theorem remains true; the no-support interpretation fails.
- No causal conditional claim: even genuine endpoint records do not turn the global product-moment theorem into a small normalized conditional fault probability after arbitrary overlapping partial histories.
