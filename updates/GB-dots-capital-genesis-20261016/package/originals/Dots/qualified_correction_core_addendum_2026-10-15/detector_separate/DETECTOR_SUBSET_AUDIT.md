# Independent proof and audit: deletion-null actual-detector subsets

Date: 2026-10-15. Separate bounded construction.

## Verdict

PASS for the abstract lemma and for the ideal pre/post stabilizer-detector application under the qualifications below. Private frames are unnecessary. Actual protected detector records replace them, and a pointwise whole-dilation deletion-null identity replaces Fourier cancellation. This does not establish noisy detectors or a small chronological conditional probability.

The intended comparator is essential: it must use the ideal correction determined by the trusted prelabel, not a potentially wrong internal gadget decision. An exact counterexample and an exact unframed crossing-detector example were constructed and run.

## 1. Precise abstract assumptions

Let U_z be one finite-dimensional unitary dilation generated by

H_z(t)=H_0(t)+sum_i z_i V_i(t),

where the terms are integrable and self-adjoint and z_i is real in [0,1]. Fixed intervening unitary/dilated-record operations are allowed. Initialization is one fixed isometry W, independent of z, from the entire allowed input space into the dilation. The input can include arbitrary memory-bath correlations; an untouched reference can be adjoined.

Let Q_i be fixed commuting orthogonal output projectors. They represent actual retained bad-record predicates, possibly comparing several record fields. They must have the same meaning in every interpolated experiment. The protected records are retained without corruption until the final predicate is evaluated. Define a_i=integral ||V_i(t)|| dt.

The key hypothesis is

Q_i U_(z_i=0) W=0

for every i and every choice of the other parameters. This is an operator annihilation identity, not a statement about one initial state or an averaged failure rate. If it holds only on a restricted allowed input subspace, incorporate that restriction in W; the conclusion then has exactly that restricted domain.

## 2. Proof

For S of size m, define Q_S=product_(i in S) Q_i. Since the projectors commute, Q_S is an orthogonal projector and can be written Q_(S without i) Q_i for each selected i.

Hold unselected parameters at their actual values. Multivariate fundamental calculus gives

Delta_S U=integral_[0,1]^S partial_S U_z dz,

where Delta_S is the alternating sum over the selected 0/1 boundary vertices. Every boundary vertex other than the all-one vertex has some z_i=0. Its contribution is annihilated by Q_S after W, by deletion-nullness. Therefore

Q_S U_1 W=Q_S integral partial_S U_z W dz.

Repeated Duhamel differentiation inserts each selected V_i once, with a sum over all m! chronological orders. Unitary propagators occur between insertions. After taking norms, the ordered time simplices partition the full Cartesian product of the labeled insertion times. Thus

||partial_S U_z|| <= product_(i in S) integral ||V_i(t)|| dt = product_(i in S) a_i.

There is no factorial loss. Projection contraction, ||W||=1, and the triangle inequality now give

||Q_S U_1 W|| <= product_i a_i.

Squaring yields

W† U_1† Q_S U_1 W <= product_i a_i² I.

This is the desired joint actual-flag effect inequality. No Fourier transform, L2 averaging, independent classical marks, or normalization of a selected outcome appears. The stronger stated null property for every other real interpolation is sufficient; for the boundary cancellation alone, the relevant vertices would suffice. All real interpolation propagators are nevertheless needed for the derivative estimate.

## 3. Isometries, discarded ancillas, and fixed record predicates

A family of unrelated isometries or normalized conditional maps is not enough: the family must arise from the stipulated common unitary dilation with the same W and record operations. Fixed initialization/final isometries or contractions can be included by compression; discarding an output ancilla means summing its Kraus effects. Apply the inequality before that trace, with Q_i tensored by identity on the discarded subsystem.

A comparison such as 'postlabel differs from f(prelabel)' is a valid fixed projector: it is the sum of the orthogonal classical record basis projectors satisfying that fixed truth table. It may depend on recorded values without depending on the unknown input state. Overlapping predicates on shared classical fields still commute when diagonal in the same protected record basis.

By contrast, choosing a different predicate after examining the unknown state, redefining the record meaning under deletion, overwriting the evidence without retaining an equivalent final flag, or normalizing a selected record branch breaks the stated argument. For arbitrary noncommuting Q_i there is no asserted joint classical event and the proof does not apply.

## 4. Ideal stabilizer-detector application: deletion-nullness

Let A contain the whole noisy gadget data footprint and every intended correction Q_j. Choose a bounded stabilizer-check group G that both:

- resolves every original correction decision Pi_j; and
- detects the full stabilizer syndrome of every Pauli supported in A.

Premeasure the SAME required group G, retaining trusted label s. Its decision checks determine the intended j(s). Compute and protect the expected postlabel s'=s xor syndrome_G(Q_(j(s))). Run the noisy correction gadget, then ideally measure G again and retain t. The actual flag is t != s'.

The noisy gadget may have its own internal labels or imperfect selection, but these must not replace or corrupt the trusted expected record. Pre/post check extraction, faithful recording, expected-label computation, and comparison remain ideal resources in this construction.

Deleting V_i must remove all perturbations charged to this gadget and leave an exact bath-independent implementation of its intended correction. The initial ancilla preparation and every remaining ideal component are fixed in the common dilation. On a premeasurement sector R_s, that exact correction maps to R_s', irrespective of the initial bath state or memory-bath entanglement.

During the entire reserved detector/gadget interval, other memory operations are disjoint from the FULL reserved data/check/ancilla support. They may share the bath, but the noise-deleted ideal gadget is bath-independent. Thus arbitrary other interpolated perturbations cannot produce this gadget's mismatch. Its final protected bad-record projector annihilates the deleted experiment for every other real interpolation: the required deletion-null identity follows.

A reduced isolated-gadget calibration or average detector error is not a substitute for this argument. Any uncharged shared-bath interaction with the gadget data, or any corruption of its protected records, can destroy nullness.

## 5. Support correctness of a matched record

The abstract probability lemma alone does not establish that a match is harmless. For that, assume no logical Pauli is supported on A. Expand the entire gadget's memory operators in patch Paulis, with chronological bath/private-ancilla coefficients. Under the same full-reservation cleanliness, move only these memory factors to completion; retain all auxiliary factors in physical chronological order.

For a net memory Pauli P, postselection by the actual projector R_t after prelabel s imposes character_G(P)=t xor s. If t=s', this equals character_G(Q_(j(s))). Therefore Q_(j(s))† P is supported in A and centralizes the full stabilizer group. By the no-local-logical assumption it is a code stabilizer up to phase.

On any encoded Pauli orbit E V_code, that stabilizer is scalar independent of the logical vector. Every surviving matched-record term therefore has exactly the intended memory transition Q_(j(s)) E V_code, with its scalar absorbed into the chronological auxiliary coefficient. The genuine internal gadget decision may be wrong, but any matched net action is still equivalent to the intended target on that orbit.

The entire matched gadget can consequently be represented by the ideal refined correction in each support history. Its memory factor may be relocated to the prescribed start under the same disjointness/gatewise-independence restrictions; no joint bath channel is moved. A mismatched gadget is charged over its WHOLE bounded reservation, including skipped/misdirected correction and internal operations. Pending and externally dirty gadgets remain charged independently of detector outcomes.

A universal common-decoder certificate covers all coherent terms admitted by these records. The good CP subchannel is trace-nonincreasing and bath-factorized after decoding; the previous completion argument applies once the separate classical subset-contour theorem bounds the total bad effect. No positive Pauli mixture or independent detector failures are asserted.

## 6. Wrong-internal-branch counterexample

The exact checker ../checks/detector/check_detector_internal_branch.py uses a stabilized data qubit and a syndrome-extraction ancilla. Ideal extraction copies the data Z syndrome into the ancilla. A weak ancilla rotation with cos(theta)=99/101 and sin(theta)=20/101 occurs before its otherwise ideal measurement; the actual measured internal value selects exact Pauli feedforward.

The intended reset should always leave data syndrome zero. A wrong internal result instead leaves the wrong syndrome with probability 400/10201. If the detector predicts the postlabel using that same actual internal result, it reports no mismatch on every branch: it merely confirms that the chosen, possibly wrong Pauli was physically applied. Comparing against the intended correction derived from the independent trusted prelabel detects the error with probability 400/10201. Deleting the ancilla perturbation makes this correct detector exactly null.

This is why trusted expected-record separation is a substantive hypothesis, not cosmetic notation.

## 7. Exact unframed crossing illustration

../checks/detector/check_crossing_detectors.py uses two disjoint memory qubits sharing one bath, with crossing echo interactions H=X_A X_B and K=X_C Z_B. It performs actual pre/post Z measurements around each interval and uses no frames. The script verifies complete instrument normalization, both all-input deletion-null identities, marginal and joint effect budgets, and the large chronological conditional probability.

For equal angles with cos(theta)=99/101 and sin(theta)=20/101, the actual conditional remains

Pr(C_bad | A_bad)=9801/10201,

while the joint bad effect is 4 cos^4(theta) sin^4(theta) I. The integrated action of each two-part pulse is 2theta. Analytically, the joint probability is at most 4 theta^4, hence at most the product (2theta)^4 of squared action budgets. The script also verifies conservative rational upper budgets using 2theta<2/5.

Thus genuine records can satisfy the subset theorem and simultaneously fail any proposed small completion-order conditional bound. There is no contradiction and no need to manufacture private frames.
