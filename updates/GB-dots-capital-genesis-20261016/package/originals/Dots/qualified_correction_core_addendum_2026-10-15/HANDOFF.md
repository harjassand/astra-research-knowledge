# Scientific handoff

## Capability

The restricted scheduled memory theorem now accepts fixed noisy Clifford correction cores obeying actual full-support Pauli conjugation covariance and deletion-stable common-dilation assumptions. It charges any nonzero gate character to the entire gadget interval, with p_g=Σ_e a_e², and transfers the refined syndrome instrument through the same universal orbit/decoder argument.

## Evidence

The gate-interface and scheduled support/probability audits pass under the explicit premises. Exact symbolic examples check positive fixed-core compilation, the compiler-following calibration obstruction, mutable-record failure, syndrome refinement and two separately scoped actual-detector constructions. The proof retains global subset effects, local-stochastic contour bounds and a trace-nonincreasing good-map completion. These are mathematical reconstructions and finite certificates, not external validation or a complete formal proof.

## Closest prior work

Pauli randomization, randomized compiling and stabilizer syndrome projection are established. The core report cites Wallman–Emerson, Winick et al. and Beale–Wallman, and distinguishes their results from this particular common-dilation/whole-gadget interface. The argument's contour and decoder imports are also named. No priority claim is made.

## Missing operation

A physical implementation must justify the fixed seed-independent noisy core and actual covariance while preserving exact or separately qualified wrappers, fresh resets, measurement, protected records, classical lookup, private frames and padded timing. A generic noisy controller or detector does not satisfy those premises merely because its calibration error is small. Useful thresholds, a practical embedding and complete resource/noise budgets remain absent.

## Plausible consequence

This removes one exact-quantum-core assumption from the earlier restricted model and clarifies what a matched implementation would have to demonstrate. It supports a more realistic research interface toward robust memory, but does not yet establish an achievable device, autonomous controller or practical storage advantage.
