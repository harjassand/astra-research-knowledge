# Exact restart state

## Retain

The proof candidate is analytic anchoring of invariant holomorphic expectations, with an explicit paired-quartic tube/accretivity certificate. The key identity is proved in product form Z(zeta) E O = integral O exp(-S), before any division by Z. The observable O=W gives Z'=Z E W and then nonvanishing. Parameter analyticity is proved using finite implicit holomorphic resolvents, contraction, moments, and a uniform strong-order-one limit, NOT analyticity in stochastic time.

The finite construction charges coefficients, certificate checks, implicit roots, Gaussian generation, bias, endpoint statistics, quadrature, precision, output, and memory. It has no inverse-average-phase factor. It is a classical algorithm on explicit input models, not a laboratory proposal or a learned-model guarantee.

## Do not overstate

Complex Langevin, strips, implicit numerical integration, Gaussian concentration, zero-free interpolation, and thermodynamic integration are old. Historical novelty of the integrated result is unknown. The paired quartics are an actual target restriction, not a free regularizer. Full-rank C is allowed, but fixed positive accretivity and tube margins are essential.

The tests are not a practical certified algorithm. All 841 exact finite algebra checks passed. Two-variable and connected ten-variable stochastic tests ran without Newton failures, but their output errors include unremoved step bias. Empirical batch errors are not confidence certificates. Chain transfer quadrature was much faster. The conservative n=2, epsilon=.01 recipe prescribes about 3.31e23 ideal implicit steps and was NOT run.

## Highest-value mathematical attack

Acquire a holomorphic noise kernel K=B B^T or an equivalent same-cycle geometry for standard onsite-quartic fields with imaginary neighbour couplings, without paired-quartic assumptions. Prove all of:
1. K and its derivatives are computable from explicit coefficients at charged finite cost, with no inaccessible roots/poles.
2. A real parameter interval gives real positive-definite K and the correct real Gibbs anchor.
3. Complex-parameter stochastic evolution is nonexplosive, the diffusion vectors satisfy boundary tangency for its invariant domain, and quantitative moment/mixing bounds hold.
4. Parameter-holomorphic expectations can be established uniformly, not guessed from finite diagnostics.
5. Finite-resource constants support useful accuracy on nonfactorising instances and survive a matched comparison with implicit CL, contour and tensor/transfer methods.

Known failed operations: constant imaginary boxes for unshielded off-diagonal imaginary forcing; quadratic whitening which moves the phase into a problematic quartic; curved boxes with unchanged horizontal Brownian noise; arbitrary contractive kernels on a wrong cycle.

Independent review must check holomorphy/expectation exchange, the variational-inequality resolvent proof, the strong-error recursion, local moment constants, and finite-bit reduction. To avoid circularity in moment estimates: first use global Ito moments for a provisional convergence result, then pass discrete Gaussian concentration to the continuous process and sharpen constants.

A successful restricted certificate is not permission to stop at another slightly enlarged field class. The decisive missing capability remains a same-answer acquired geometry with useful end-to-end guarantees and a consequential demonstrated regime.
