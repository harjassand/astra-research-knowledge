Consider an infinite, translation-invariant spin chain with
\[ H=-\frac{t}{2}\sum_i(X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1})+H_{\mathrm{diag}}, \]
where \(H_{\mathrm{diag}}\) contains arbitrary bounded, finite-range interactions diagonal in the occupation basis. These interactions need not be weak. They generally do not commute with the hopping part.
The physical dynamics is
\[ \mathcal L O = i[H,O]+\gamma\sum_i(Z_iOZ_i-O), \qquad d=4\gamma>0. \]
Here \(d\) is the decay rate of a coherence differing on two sites. Dephasing is part of the target system, not a mathematical regularizer that can later be removed without cost.
The response is defined in the infinite-temperature product state at mean occupation \(1/2\). With \(n_i=(I-Z_i)/2\) and susceptibility \(\chi=1/4\), define
\[ D= \lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{1}{2T\chi} \sum_x x^2 \left\langle e^{T\mathcal L}(n_x-\tfrac12),\,n_0-\tfrac12 \right\rangle . \]
This is the long-time coefficient of the second moment of the density response. It is not, by definition alone, a Gaussian hydrodynamic limit.
Now define a local quantity:
\[ \Xi = \mathbb E_{\mathrm{uniform}} \left[ \big( H_{\mathrm{diag}}(\eta^{i,i+1}) -H_{\mathrm{diag}}(\eta) \big)^2 \;\middle|\; \eta_i\ne\eta_{i+1} \right]. \]
The configuration \(\eta^{i,i+1}\) is obtained by exchanging two neighbouring occupations. Thus \(\Xi\) is the mean squared energy mismatch encountered by one allowed hop.
Only spins within the interaction range enter this quantity.
