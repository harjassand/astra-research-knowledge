# Complete quantum information orders

This package keeps the two supplied channel-order manuscripts together because they address complementary parts of the same question: which complete divergence/metric orders coincide, and when that order admits a finite Hermiticity-preserving observable lift. The original PDFs are retained byte-for-byte. Their page-separated extracted text files are convenience views only; use the PDF whenever equation layout or symbol extraction is ambiguous.

## Source-reported results and exact routes

| Source | Main claimed result | Useful route |
|---|---|---|
| [`finite_order_kernel_one.pdf`](finite_order_kernel_one.pdf) | For finite-dimensional channels with common input, after compressing to fixed output supports, if the source adjoint has complex kernel dimension at most one, complete SLD order is equivalent to the finite Gram SDP and to a common unital Hermiticity-preserving observable lift. The PDF claims that reference dimension `b²` suffices when the target matrix algebra has dimension `b`. It also gives a rank-one imaginary-gradient obstruction in the general-kernel separation argument. | [page 1](EXTRACTED_finite_order_kernel_one.txt#L6) and [page 2](EXTRACTED_finite_order_kernel_one.txt#L59) for definitions and theorem; [pages 3–4](EXTRACTED_finite_order_kernel_one.txt#L115) for the null-vector score and one-dimensional-kernel proof; [page 5](EXTRACTED_finite_order_kernel_one.txt#L244) for the rank-one extension, entropy-order consequence, and precision caveats. |
| [`complete_information_order.pdf`](complete_information_order.pdf) | The PDF claims equivalence of unrestricted complete Umegaki relative-entropy order, complete SLD order, and complete modular Petz order; it further claims that one fixed operator-convex generator with finite left endpoint and nonzero interior measure suffices. It treats interior Petz Rényi orders and sandwiched Rényi order two, gives endpoint counterexamples, explicit finite witness bounds, and a finite complex-linear lift for complete Petz order two. | [page 2](EXTRACTED_complete_information_order.txt#L45) and [page 3](EXTRACTED_complete_information_order.txt#L101) for definitions and the main equivalence; pages 4–8 beginning at [page 4](EXTRACTED_complete_information_order.txt#L162) for Hessian/filter arguments; pages 10–13 beginning at [page 10](EXTRACTED_complete_information_order.txt#L561) for finite witness bounds, endpoint limits, and operational consequences; [pages 14–16](EXTRACTED_complete_information_order.txt#L782) for the distinct Petz-order-two complex-linear lift. |

These summaries report the manuscripts' claims; they are not independent proof reconstructions. In particular, complete means all finite untouched references and unrestricted input-state pairs, without requiring equal reference marginals. The witness-reference bound in the broad order-equivalence paper depends on the violation gap and spectral conditioning; it is not a dimension-only bound. Petz orders zero and two are explicitly distinguished from the interior equivalence class. The order-two lift is complex-linear and need not preserve Hermiticity, so it must not be substituted for the HP lift in the SLD results.

## Relation to existing Astra records

- Read [N661](../../../../frontier/dossiers/N661.txt) and its cited source in `updates/CX` first. N661 already records complete SLD/modular-Petz order equivalence and a finite sufficient HP-lift certificate, while leaving necessity of a common lift open in general. This intake reports a narrower source-kernel-dimension-`≤1` necessity claim and broader order/witness details. It is a source-reported scoped refinement, not independent validation or a status promotion of N661.
- [N659](../../../../frontier/dossiers/N659.txt) concerns ordinary measured-chi-square order and task-specific decoding/recovery transfers. It is nearby context, not an interchangeable interface for the unrestricted all-pairs complete order in these PDFs.
- The first PDF's kernel dimension is measured after output-support compression. Do not extrapolate its finite criterion to arbitrary kernel dimension. Its rank-one dual-gradient statement does not prove every separator has rank one.
- The second PDF's endpoint counterexamples distinguish Petz orders zero and two; its finite complex lift for order two has no HP constraint. Do not infer a general finite reference bound, a physical simulator, a contraction-coefficient identity, or an unconditional polynomial-time exact SDP decision procedure.
