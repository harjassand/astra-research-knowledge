This intake preserves three source documents in two separate research families. The two quantum-information-order manuscripts are closely related to the existing N661 record; the local-to-global compatibility manuscript is a separate finite-dimensional marginal-gluing line. Their topics are not treated as a single theorem or as a transfer between families.

Start with the [quantum information orders guide](package/quantum_information_orders/README.md) or the separate [compatibility-gluing guide](package/global_compatibility_gluing/README.md). The order papers report (i) a complete-order equivalence for finite-dimensional channels and endpoint distinctions, and (ii) a finite necessary-and-sufficient Hermiticity-preserving lift criterion when the compressed source adjoint has complex kernel dimension at most one. These are source-reported manuscript claims and have not been independently reconstructed here. The second paper extends the scope of the existing N661 discussion only in a restricted kernel regime; it does not settle general lift necessity.

The gluing note reports matching-polytope and stable-set sufficient extension criteria, sharp universal radii on complete graphs and star marginals, and a state-centered channel self-compatibility formula. It explicitly leaves general marginal compatibility, prior-art priority, and independent correctness unresolved. Its reference to EPR cards N190–N193, N635, and N637 is contextual only; it closes none of their gates.
