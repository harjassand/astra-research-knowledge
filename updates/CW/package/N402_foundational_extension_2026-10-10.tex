\textbf{Outcome.} The strongest developed result is a conditional universality theorem: N402's single Choi SDP also computes the exact complete contraction coefficients of a regular class of modular Petz divergences, every fixed Petz R\'enyi order $0<\alpha<1$, and quantum Chernoff divergence. A matching converse is constructed, rather than only an upper bound. This substantially extends the proposed capability beyond KL/CMI. The extension is internally derived and adversarially checked; its all-channel validity depends on N402/N403, which remain externally unverified. No historical-priority certification or solution of a major open problem is claimed.

\textbf{What N402 would add.} The complete coefficient $\eta_D^c$ is the supremum of $D((\id_R\otimes\N)\rho\|(\id_R\otimes\N)\omega)/D(\rho\|\omega)$ over arbitrary finite $R$, $\rho_R=\omega_R$, and $0<D(\rho\|\omega)<\infty$. The conditional coefficient $\eta_D^\Delta$ optimizes
$[D(\N_R\rho\|\N_R\omega)-D(\rho_R\|\omega_R)]/[D(\rho\|\omega)-D(\rho_R\|\omega_R)]$ over arbitrary pairs with finite terms and positive denominator, where $\N_R=\id_R\otimes\N$. The CMI coefficient optimizes $I(B:U|R)/I(A:U|R)$. Write $\eta$ for the common value N402 claims for these and conditional KL contraction. It proposes
\[
 \eta(\N)=1-M(\N)^{-1},\qquad
 M(\N)=\min_{\sigma\in\D(B)}
 \left\|\Tr_B[(I\otimes\sigma)J^+(I\otimes\sigma)]\right\|_\infty,
 \quad \ran(I\otimes\sigma)\subseteq\ran J.
\]
Here $J$ is the unnormalized Choi matrix; infeasibility means $M=\infty$.
Equivalently, minimize $t$ over densities $\sigma$ and $Z$ subject to
$\begin{psmallmatrix}J&I\otimes\sigma\\I\otimes\sigma&Z\end{psmallmatrix}\succeq0$ and $\Tr_B Z\preceq tI_A$.
The closest prior paper [1] already proves conditional KL $=$ CMI, uses binary auxiliaries, and develops networks and memory applications. It explicitly leaves equality with the equal-reference complete coefficient open, including for depolarizing noise. Thus the prospective new power is the exact finite Choi optimization and its matching converse, not generic information propagation. Signed lifts and quadratic-to-entropy comparison are prior machinery [2,3].

\section*{1. Strongest result: exact Petz and Chernoff universality}

\textbf{Conditional extension theorem.} Assume the N402 signed-lift certificate and N403 rectangular sharpness identity are valid. Let $f$ be nonaffine and operator convex on $(0,\infty)$, normalized by $f(1)=f'(1)=0$, with $f(0+)<\infty$ and $\ell_\infty=\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)/x<\infty$. For the \emph{modular Petz} divergence
\[
 D_f(\rho\|\sigma)=\langle\sigma^{1/2},f(L_\rho R_\sigma^{-1})\sigma^{1/2}\rangle_{\rm HS},
 \qquad L_\rho X=\rho X,\quad R_\sigma X=X\sigma,
\]
define complete and conditional coefficients as above with $D$ replaced by $D_f$. Define generalized conditional information explicitly by
\[
 I_f(U:A|R)=D_f(\rho_{URA}\|\rho_U\otimes\rho_{RA})
             -D_f(\rho_{UR}\|\rho_U\otimes\rho_R).
\]
Then, for every finite channel,
\[
 \boxed{\quad \eta_f^c(\N)=\eta_f^\Delta(\N)=\eta_{I_f}(\N)=1-M(\N)^{-1}.\quad} \tag{P1}
\]
The same statement holds for every fixed \emph{Petz} R\'enyi order $0<\alpha<1$, with $D_\alpha=(\alpha-1)^{-1}\log\Tr\rho^\alpha\sigma^{1-\alpha}$, and for the quantum Chernoff divergence
$C_Q(\rho,\sigma)=-\log\inf_{0<\alpha<1}\Tr\rho^\alpha\sigma^{1-\alpha}$.
For these two divergences, generalized conditional information means the displayed \emph{difference definition}, not an optimized or sandwiched R\'enyi CMI. Every coefficient optimizes positive finite denominators. The reference bound $\dim R\le(\dim B)^2+1$ and binary classical $U$ suffice for sharpness.

\textbf{Exact capability.} A single Choi SDP now evaluates a continuum of reference-assisted distinguishability-contraction constants and a sharp iid binary-testing exponent contraction constant. No state search, order-by-order optimization, or growing number of copies is needed to evaluate the coefficient from exact Choi data. This is not a formula for arbitrary pairs of channels, adaptive channel discrimination, or a fixed-equilibrium entropy-decay rate.

\textit{Upper proof.} Put $\eta=1-1/M$ and $\mathcal E=\mathcal E_\eta$, the erasure channel retaining $A$ with probability $\eta$. N402's \emph{state-independent} signed observable lift $\mathcal J$ satisfies $\mathcal J^*\mathcal E=\N$ and, separately for two faithful states,
\[
 \mathcal J^*L_{\mathcal E(\rho)}\mathcal J\preceq L_{\N(\rho)},\qquad
 \mathcal J^*R_{\mathcal E(\sigma)}\mathcal J\preceq R_{\N(\sigma)}.
\]
Add the second inequality with weight $s>0$ and use the inverse quadratic variational identity at $z=\mathcal E(\rho-\sigma)$. This proves
\[
 Q_s(\N\rho,\N\sigma)\le Q_s(\mathcal E\rho,\mathcal E\sigma),\qquad
 Q_s(\rho,\sigma)=\langle\rho-\sigma,(L_\rho+sR_\sigma)^{-1}(\rho-\sigma)\rangle.
\]
Direct modular functional calculus identifies $Q_s=D_{f_s}$ for $f_s(x)=(x-1)^2/(x+s)$. The positive operator-convex representation [10] and the endpoint assumptions give
\[
 f(x)=\int_{(0,\infty)}f_s(x)\,d\nu(s),\qquad
 \int(1+s^{-1})\,d\nu(s)=f(0+)+\ell_\infty=:k_f\in(0,\infty).
\]
Thus integration and erasure's orthogonal branches yield, at every reference amplification,
\[
 D_f(\N_R\rho\|\N_R\sigma)\le
 \eta D_f(\rho\|\sigma)+(1-\eta)D_f(\rho_R\|\sigma_R). \tag{P2}
\]
The trivial $\eta=0,1$ cases are respectively replacer invariance and ordinary data processing.

\textit{Matching lower proof.} Import N403's algebraic identity
$\sup_{C,B}q_{C'}(B')/q_C(B)=\eta$, where $q_C(B)=\Tr B^*C^{-1}B$, $C\succ0$ is a state on $R_0A$, each block of $B:A\to R_0A$ has trace zero, and primes mean blockwise application of $\N$, with inverses restricted to its fixed output support. Use
\[
 \omega_p=\operatorname{diag}(qC,p\tau),\qquad
 H_p=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt{pq}B\\\sqrt{pq}B^*&0\end{pmatrix},\quad q=1-p,
\]
with faithful $\tau$. Schur positivity permits $\omega_p\pm hH_p$ on an $h$ interval independent of $p$, and both local marginals remain fixed. If $c_i,v_j$ are eigenvalues of $C,\tau$, the new resolvent calculation is
\[
 \left.\frac{d^2}{dh^2}D_{f_s}(\omega_p+hH_p\|\omega_p)\right|_{h=0}
 =2pq\sum_{ij}|B_{ij}|^2\left(\frac1{qc_i+spv_j}+\frac1{pv_j+sqc_i}\right).
\]
After division by $2p$, the summand is bounded by $(1+s^{-1})|B_{ij}|^2/c_i$ and converges to that value. Dominated convergence therefore gives
\[
 \lim_{p\downarrow0}\frac{D_f''(\omega_p;H_p)}{2p}=k_fq_C(B). \tag{P3}
\]
The output has the identical factor $k_f$. First take $h\to0$ at each fixed $p$, then $p\downarrow0$, then optimize $C,B$. The resulting ratio is $\eta$. A uniform bit selecting $\omega_p\pm hH_p$ gives the same generalized-information ratio, because its reference marginal is independent of the bit. This proves (P1), including sharpness; no limits are interchanged.

\textit{R\'enyi and Chernoff steps.} The Hellinger generator $(1-x^\alpha)/(1-\alpha)$ is in the regular class after removing its linear term. Write $Q_\alpha=\Tr\rho^\alpha\sigma^{1-\alpha}$. Equation (P2) gives $Q_{\alpha,\rm out}\ge\eta Q_{\alpha,\rm in}+(1-\eta)Q_{\alpha,R}$, and log concavity with $\alpha-1<0$ yields
\[
 D_{\alpha,\rm out}-D_{\alpha,R}
 \le\frac{\log[1-\eta+\eta e^{(\alpha-1)(D_{\alpha,\rm in}-D_{\alpha,R})}]}{\alpha-1}
 \le\eta(D_{\alpha,\rm in}-D_{\alpha,R}). \tag{P4}
\]
Local R\'enyi and Hellinger ratios agree at $Q_\alpha=1$, proving sharpness. For the binary witness the block $Q_\alpha$ is the branch average, so its logarithm has the same second-order ratio. Taking the supremum of $(1-\alpha)D_\alpha$ gives
$C_{Q,\rm out}\le\eta C_{Q,\rm in}+(1-\eta)C_{Q,R}$; subtraction gives the conditional bound, and application to the product comparison defining $I_f$ gives its generalized-information version. For its lower bound, the exact cross-sector kernel is
\[
 K_\alpha(a,b)=\frac{(a^\alpha-b^\alpha)(a^{1-\alpha}-b^{1-\alpha})}{(a-b)^2},\qquad
 K_{1/2}(a,b)=\frac1{(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)^2}.
\]
It is maximized at $\alpha=1/2$ by AM--GM. At each faithful $\omega_p$, Taylor expansion is uniform over $\alpha\in[0,1]$, so the local Chernoff metric is exactly $K_{1/2}$. Since $aK_{1/2}(a,b)\to1$ as $b/a\to0$, the same rare-flag limit proves the matching lower.

\textit{Boundary and scope checks.} The kernel perspective $(a-b)^2/(a+sb)$ extends continuously to PSD boundaries and is bounded by $a+b/s$; the integrability above passes this to $D_f$. Powers are continuous for fixed $0<\alpha<1$. For Chernoff, common faithful mixtures satisfy $Q_\alpha(\rho_\epsilon,\sigma_\epsilon)\ge(1-\epsilon)Q_\alpha(\rho,\sigma)+\epsilon$ by joint concavity; fixed interior-$\alpha$ convergence supplies the reverse limit for $\inf_\alpha Q_\alpha$. Hence all upper bounds extend to the finite-divergence domains. The limits are not uniform in $\alpha$. The $\alpha=1$ Umegaki case is supplied by N402; the zero-order endpoint is excluded for a decisive reason below.

\textbf{Prior art and obstruction.} HGNW [1] already gives $f$-specific conditional/generalized-CMI identities, and explicitly excludes R\'enyi divergences from that structural proof. Hiai--Ruskai [11] studies ordinary coefficients without references. The coefficient collapse in Hirche--Tomamichel [12] concerns its hockey-stick integral divergence; the older maximal-divergence collapse is also a different interface. No matching complete Petz/Choi formula was located, but this is not a priority certification. The Chernoff operational interpretation itself is established [13,14]: it concerns iid discrimination of two states after a channel. At order zero, $D_0(\rho\|\sigma)=-\log\Tr(P_\rho\sigma)$. For qubit depolarizing shrinkage $1/2$,
$\N_R\rho\succeq\rho_R\otimes I_2/4$ and
$\supp(\N_R\rho)=\supp(\rho_R)\otimes\mathbb C^2$.
Consequently $D_{0,\rm out}=D_{0,R}$ for arbitrary pairs: all three zero-order coefficients vanish. Positive input denominators exist, e.g. $|0\rangle\langle0|$ versus $I_2/2$, and a bit encoded as $|0\rangle,|1\rangle$. Thus the coefficient jumps from zero at $\alpha=0$ to $3/8$ for every fixed interior order, conditional on (P1). This directly exposes the nonuniform rare-flag limit.

The range $1<\alpha\le2$ remains unresolved: the logarithm step no longer gives the required linear bound, and finite tests do not settle it. Exact same-coefficient transfer to \emph{every} R\'enyi order is false: qubit depolarizing shrinkage $1/2$ has N402 value $3/8$, but diagonal $P=(1,0)$, $Q=(.9,.1)$ give a Petz order-10 ratio $0.3874364\ldots>3/8$ (exact rational-power check in the evidence; order ten lies outside the usual Petz data-processing range).


\section*{2. Consequence: exact sparse noise geometry}

By Section~1, every formula for $\eta$ below simultaneously applies to the complete, conditional-difference and generalized-information coefficients for regular Petz, fixed interior-order Petz R\'enyi, and Chernoff divergences, conditional on the same source dependencies.

\textbf{Exact error-basis lemma.} Let $\{U_g\}_{g=1}^{d^2}$ be any orthogonal unitary basis, with $\Tr(U_g^*U_h)=d\delta_{gh}$, and let $\N_p(X)=\sum_g p_gU_gXU_g^*$. If every $p_g>0$, then the Choi optimization is solved exactly by
\[
 \boxed{\ M(\N_p)=\frac{1}{d^4}\sum_g\frac1{p_g},\qquad
 \beta(\N_p):=M^{-1}=\frac{d^4}{\sum_g p_g^{-1}}.\ }
 \tag{1}
\]
If one probability is zero, $M=\infty$ and $\beta=0$. These are algebraic identities for the Choi objective; $\eta=1-\beta$ uses N402.

\textit{Proof.} Normalized Choi eigenvectors are $|u_g\rangle=|U_g\rangle\!\rangle/\sqrt d$, so $J=d\sum_gp_g|u_g\rangle\langle u_g|$ and
$\Tr_AJ^{-1}=\Tr_BJ^{-1}=cI$, where $c=d^{-2}\sum_gp_g^{-1}$.
For any density $\sigma$ and $T_\sigma=\Tr_B[(I\otimes\sigma)J^{-1}(I\otimes\sigma)]$,
\[
 \|T_\sigma\|_\infty\ge\frac{\Tr T_\sigma}{d}
 =\frac c d\Tr\sigma^2\ge\frac c{d^2}.
\]
The choice $\sigma=I/d$ attains equality. The block SDP has the exact primal witness $Z=J^{-1}/d^2$, $t=M$. If $p_g=0$, the range constraint forces $(I\otimes\sigma)|u_g\rangle=0$. Full Schmidt rank of $|u_g\rangle$ forces $\sigma=0$, a contradiction. No covariance argument or numerical optimizer is needed.

\textbf{Shortest-noise-word theorem.} Now suppose the basis is a projective Weyl representation of a finite label group $G$, $|G|=d^2$. For positive rates $\gamma_s$, let
\[
 L=\sum_{s\in S}\gamma_s(\Ad U_s-\id),\qquad T_t=e^{tL}.
\]
If $S$ generates $G$, let $\ell(g)$ be the shortest directed word length reaching $g$, $r=\max_g\ell(g)$, and
\[
 c_g=\frac{1}{\ell(g)!}
 \sum_{s_1\cdots s_{\ell(g)}=g}\prod_j\gamma_{s_j},
 \qquad c_e=1.
\]
Then, at fixed group and fixed rates,
\[
 \boxed{\ 1-\eta(T_t)=
 \frac{d^4}{\sum_{\ell(g)=r}c_g^{-1}}t^r+O(t^{r+1}).\ }
 \tag{2}
\]
If the jumps generate only a proper subgroup, $\eta(T_t)=1$ at every finite time.
Indeed, the compound-Poisson expansion gives
$p_t(g)=e^{-\Gamma t}\sum_{k\ge0}(t^k/k!)\sum_{s_1\cdots s_k=g}\prod_j\gamma_{s_j}
=c_gt^{\ell(g)}+O(t^{\ell(g)+1})$.
Substitute in (1): the reciprocal sum is dominated by all farthest labels. Weighted shortest-path data cost $O(|G||S|)$ arithmetic operations by BFS and dynamic programming. This is not a polynomial-in-qubit-count claim when $|G|=4^n$.

\textbf{Exact example and capability.} For one qubit with $X,Z$ jump rates $a,b>0$,
\[
 1-\eta(T_t)=(1-e^{-4at})(1-e^{-4bt})=16ab\,t^2+O(t^3).
 \tag{3}
\]
Its generator has spectral gap $2\min(a,b)>0$, yet
$\lim_{t\downarrow0}-\log\eta(T_t)/t=0$.
Thus no positive $\kappa$ can make $\eta(T_t)\le e^{-\kappa t}$ hold uniformly from time zero. This concerns arbitrary compared states with quantum references. It is compatible with positive fixed-equilibrium complete log-Sobolev constants [5].

For $n$ independent qubits with equal $X,Z$ rates $\gamma$, the exact formula, not a nonuniform small-time expansion, gives
\[
 \eta_n(t)=1-(1-e^{-4\gamma t})^{2n},\qquad
 T_n(\epsilon)=-\frac1{4\gamma}\log\!\left[1-(1-\epsilon)^{1/(2n)}\right].
 \tag{4}
\]
At $t=(\log(2n)+c)/(4\gamma)$, $\eta_n(t)\to1-e^{-e^{-c}}$.
This exactly determines the transition for this coefficient using a scalar formula instead of a growing Choi SDP. The standard CP-replacer Doeblin coefficient is
$\alpha_n(t)=(1-e^{-2\gamma t})^{2n}$; its bound $\eta_n\le1-\alpha_n$ reaches a fixed threshold around $\log(2n)/(2\gamma)$, twice the exact center. General tensor-product quantum mixing cutoffs are established prior art [6]; the scoped contribution is this exact coefficient and comparison. No improvement over every possible fixed-target or mixing method is claimed.

\section*{3. Secondary connection and decisive failed transfer}

For a classical channel with $k$ input laws $W_x$, release $n$ iid observations of one fixed input. Put $O_n=\sum_{y^n}\min_x W_x^{\otimes n}(y^n)$. N405's harmonic formula yields
\[
 O_n\le1-\eta(W^{[n]})\le kO_n,
 \qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}-\tfrac1n\log(1-\eta(W^{[n]}))
 =-\log\min_{\lambda\in\Delta_k}\sum_y\prod_x W_x(y)^{\lambda_x}.
\]
Indeed, a weighted harmonic mean is at least the minimum, and uniform weights bound it by $k$ times the minimum. The resulting exponent is the established multivariate Chernoff divergence for classical antidistinguishability [7]. The same exponent follows from the Doeblin baseline, so this removes no new exponent-computation barrier. A proposed ternary separation from ordinary KL contraction was already present, with the same example and numerical values, in [8, Sec.~9.4]; ordinary KL also admits the known binary-input reduction [9]. Full calculations and exact finite checks are retained in the evidence.

The bridge fails decisively for noncommuting outputs: the CQ channel preparing $|0\rangle$ or $|+\rangle$ has no common output support and hence N402 deficit zero, while its binary state-elimination error is $(1-1/\sqrt2)/2>0$. This does not resolve quantum antidistinguishability.

\section*{4. Important obstruction: the coefficient is not robust at pure reset}

Let $\mathcal A_\gamma$ be qubit amplitude damping, $0<\gamma<1$, with Kraus operators
$K_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt{1-\gamma})$ and $K_1=\sqrt\gamma|0\rangle\langle1|$.
Then
\[
 \eta_D^c(\mathcal A_\gamma)=\eta_{\rm CMI}(\mathcal A_\gamma)=1,
 \qquad \eta_D^c(\mathcal A_1)=\eta_{\rm CMI}(\mathcal A_1)=0. \tag{7}
\]
The lower bound one can be proved directly, without N402's universal upper argument. Take $C=\operatorname{diag}(1-r,r)$, $B=|1\rangle\langle0|$, $\tau=I/2$, $0<r<1$, $q=1-p$, and
\[
 \omega_p=\begin{pmatrix}qC&0\\0&p\tau\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 H_p=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt{pq}B\\\sqrt{pq}B^*&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both partial traces of $H_p$ vanish, and $\omega_p\pm hH_p\succeq0$ whenever $h^2\le r/2$, independently of $p$. Put $s=1-\gamma>0$. The input relative-entropy Hessian is
$2pq\log(2qr/p)/(qr-p/2)$; the output Hessian is
$2pqs\log[2qsr/(p(1+\gamma))]/[qsr-p(1+\gamma)/2]$.
Both are asymptotic to $2p\log(1/p)/r$. Taking the perturbation $h\to0$ and then $p\downarrow0$ gives KL ratios tending to one. A uniform classical bit selecting $\omega_p\pm hH_p$ has $I(A:U|R)=\frac12\sum_\pm D(\omega_p\pm hH_p\|\omega_p)$, because its reference marginal is independent of the bit. The same holds at the output, proving the CMI claim too. Data processing supplies both upper bounds. The same direct construction proves coefficient one for every divergence in Section~1: $q_C(B)=1/r$ and $q_{\mathcal A_\gamma(C)}(\mathcal A_\gamma(B))=1/r$, so its new Hessian limits give the matching ratio without importing N402's universal theorem. The conditional-difference and generalized-information variants follow from the same fixed-reference witnesses and data processing. At $\gamma=1$ the channel is a replacer, so all these coefficients vanish.

Common Stinespring isometries yield
\[
 \|\mathcal A_\gamma-\mathcal A_1\|_\diamond
 \le2\sqrt{2(1-\sqrt\gamma)}\longrightarrow0. \tag{8}
\]
Consequently no algorithm with a uniformly bounded number of black-box channel uses can estimate $\eta_D^c$ to any additive error $\varepsilon<1/2$ with success probability at least $2/3$ for every channel. The two required estimate intervals are disjoint; an adaptive $N$-use distinguishing strategy has output trace distance at most $N\|\mathcal A_\gamma-\mathcal A_1\|_\diamond/2$. Choose $\gamma$ sufficiently close to one to contradict the required constant distinguishing advantage. This is an information-access obstruction, not an impossibility for exact supplied Choi matrices or promised spectral margins.

\section*{5. Verification, unresolved obstacles, and next step}

\textbf{Verification status.} Sixteen Luna workers at maximum reasoning investigated distinct mechanisms and prior art. Two focused extension reviews found no fatal flaw. The three supplied exact N402 finite certificates were replayed successfully; exact qubit/qutrit Choi, symbolic noise, GAD and repeated-release checks also passed. The new Petz witness check verifies the scalar Hessian identities symbolically, certifies 16 finite $\alpha=1/2$ ratios by exact arithmetic, and gives the ordered limit $3/8$ for depolarizing shrinkage $1/2$. A seeded search of 3,000 noncommuting faithful state pairs on four channels found no violation for $\alpha=.2,.5,.8$ or resolvents $s=.1,1,10$; equal-reference Chernoff checks used a 401-point grid. These are scoped finite checks, not universal proof certificates. The analytical extension remains conditional on the source lift and rectangular optimum; no formal kernel, external specialist validation, or exhaustive priority audit was obtained.

\textbf{Why the ambitious routes did not close.} Capacity and recovery inequalities mostly instantiate established information-disturbance, squashed-entanglement, or less-noisy machinery. The scalar self-contraction coefficient does not compare a receiver with its complement. Arbitrary pairs of channels still need a state-dependent quotient/comparison argument; an injective denominator channel can have an infinite relative coefficient. Circuit, network, memory and classical HMM forgetting transfers are already covered in prior frameworks or require additional acquisition and uniform-risk assumptions. Near-extremal N403 witnesses can carry vanishing absolute information; imposing a positive total-information floor alone also fails to prevent tensor-power coefficients tending to one, by ordinary repetition coding.

\textbf{Highest-value next step.} Obtain an external line-by-line audit of the N402/N403 dependency and this extension together. The decisive points are the centered minimax/polar reduction supplying a state-independent lift, its use with two different states, the rectangular optimum, and the ordered coherent-flag limits. This is now a precise theorem with a matching witness, a computational capability and falsifiable examples, rather than a proposal. A priority check should target common complete \emph{modular Petz} coefficients specifically; maximal and hockey-stick divergences do not settle it. Robust sample-based acquisition additionally needs an explicit Choi support/spectral-margin promise. No major open problem is marked solved.
