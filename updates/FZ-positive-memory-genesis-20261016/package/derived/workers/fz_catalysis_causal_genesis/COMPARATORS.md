# Comparators and novelty collision

## Known operations constrain the claim

Xu and coauthors have already reported the dynamic Pd/AC selectivity operation. The final-paper abstract attributes it to time-dependent polarization and adsorption energetics. The current artifact does not invent that response and has not independently replicated it. The supplied package's exact numbers come from its transcribed 2025 preprint, with an explicitly separate final-SI 10-second/1-second illustration. [Xu et al., final-paper author repository](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/78g8x2hb), [final DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-026-02107-8).

Ardagh and coauthors' 2020 primary theory already describes dynamic control of competing reaction pathways and selectivity beyond static conditions; it is theory/simulation, not an acquired response in this electrode. Canavan and coauthors' 2025 work explicitly examines interpretation of programmable-catalyst kinetic measurements. A pulse-associated advantage over a convex static mixture therefore does not establish a new elementary physical operation. [Ardagh et al.](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2020/sc/c9sc06140a), [Canavan et al.](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.5c03878).

Carbon-source attribution has direct same-reaction prior art. Al-Ammar and Webb used carbon tracers to separate ethylene/acetylene contributions to ethane in 1979. Gulyaeva and coauthors used 13C2-acetylene SSITKA to determine whether gas-phase ethylene participates on two Pd/fiberglass catalysts in 2015. Those are different substrates/regimes and do not supply this electrode's response, but they decisively preclude inventing isotope branch attribution anew. [1979 tracer study](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/1979/f1/f19797501900), [2015 kinetic/isotope study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apcata.2015.09.016).

The method comparator receives exactly the same acquired isotope, flow and electrical data. Conventional transient analysis and an appropriate joint reactor/surface transfer-function model can estimate those cohort fates too. Complete collection's cancellation of conservative output delay is a mass-balance fact. Shannon and Goodwin already developed convolution/transfer-function treatment of nonideal SSITKA reactors. No computational or information advantage over that incumbent is established here. [1997 primary modelling study](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0926-860X(96)00259-1).

Electrochemical promotion also has state/hysteresis prior art in other chemistry. Pt/YSZ ethylene oxidation studies connect promoted rates with surface carbon/oxygen state and show that catalytic response and measured bulk/state signals need not map simply onto one another. They do not identify this Pd mechanism. [2010 study](https://doi.org/10.1002/cphc.200900936), [2021 operando study](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2021/cp/d1cp02757c).

**Novelty status:** generic dynamic selectivity, catalyst memory, source labelling, pump/read transients and transport correction are established. Novelty of this particular post-write fresh-feed causal protocol is UNKNOWN; no historical-first claim is supported. Its value is an exact falsification target for the actual unanswered question.

## Same useful product, current strong processes

This is a targeted primary-literature screen, not a proof of a globally best catalyst or complete industrial ranking.

The correction's text could not be fetched; its precise changes are unverified. Use the retrieved current page as source-reported context only, and reconcile the correction/SI before quantitative promotion.

The Cu process offers an instructive alternative to trying to force Pd to distinguish similar adsorbates: selective acetylene absorption upstream of a common Cu electrode changes which reactant reaches the active interface. That operation already exists in the cited paper. It raises the burden for a pulsed-Pd invention: demonstrate a throughput/work/lifetime advantage under the same outlet requirement, or a distinct consequential capability that selective transport and static catalysis cannot obtain economically.

## Complete comparison boundary

Compare at the same A inlet composition and outlet assay/detection limit, B cofeed preservation, H2 availability, pressure, humidity/impurities and useful flow. Report net B added, total B recovered and residual A separately. Match catalyst/electrode area and material consumption, lifetime and regeneration, counterreaction products, full terminal and wall-plug work, hydrogen/water/solvent inputs, heat and compression, conditioning and reset, and product/solvent separation.

The correct unit of merit is useful product at the required purity per full resource and time boundary. A lower working-electrode current, a better percentage labelled selectivity, or a large gain per total Pd mass cannot establish this comparison. Programmable-catalyst energy flow accounting has explicit prior art. [Abdelrahman and Dauenhauer](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.3c00522).

**Current decision:** retain the causal assay as a laboratory acquisition lead; stop claims of a discovered fundamentally new operation or present process superiority. Essential data and an actual experimental interface are absent in the current access boundary.
