This addendum is part of the mathematical statement in `REPORT.md` and takes
precedence wherever the literal display omits its denominator condition.

Equations (14)-(16), the conjugate `T_psi`, and every formula involving `1/Q`
are defined and quantified only for compatible periodic psi satisfying

\[
\langle\psi_1\rangle_x=0,\qquad Q>0.
\]

The complete equivalent criterion for quasiconvexity of the unchanged J_1 at
I is: inequality (16) holds for **every such psi with Q>0**, together with the
following separate degenerate case. If Q=0, the compatibility argument in
Section 3 forces Dpsi=0, and the original energy is `<h^4>/8>=0` for every
mean-zero shear h and every scalar background s. Define its optimized defect
as Delta(psi)=0. Do not evaluate `C^2/(4Q)`, `C M/Q`, or `T_psi` by a formal
zero-over-zero substitution.

The opposite determinant-sign version is understood with the corresponding
condition Q_D>0; Q_D=0 is handled separately by the same compatibility
argument with the two coordinate/component roles exchanged. These
qualifiers were present in the preceding existence proof but omitted from
the literal display (16). No all-gradient source inequality is claimed to
have been proved.

The formulas for scalar-Hessian or other fixtures that contain `1/Q` likewise
apply only to their nonzero-gradient case. For example, the nonseparable
three-wave fixture has Q>0 unless all its amplitudes vanish; the zero map
has defect zero.
