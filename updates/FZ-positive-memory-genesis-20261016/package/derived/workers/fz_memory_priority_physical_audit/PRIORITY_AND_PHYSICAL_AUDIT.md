## Decision

No inspected primary theorem directly supplies the full original conjunction for the **specified** trailing-vertex asynchronous sweep. The potentially additional result is a classical stochastic/topological estimate for that fixed process, followed by a standard stabilizer quantum lift. The existing synchronous sweep threshold, classical eroder stability, and quantum/classical equivalence do not alone prove that estimate. This is a coverage finding, not proof of priority or correctness.

For the modified generator, positive-Choi regularization, Poisson proposal channels, and correction of a linear span are established tools. The potentially additional conclusion is their application to a universal support-controlled schedule with a uniform local-generator error ball. It depends on the producer's universal good-schedule and accumulated-homology premises. It does not establish coherent robustness for the original active-jump generator.

The geometric obstruction in the producer report is valid after stating the placement and density assumptions. It proves a growing maximum edge range, not that every interaction is long or that all 3D autonomous quantum memories are impossible. A stronger mediator-space bound is available under a separately stated graph-minor routing assumption. Neither result turns the abstract 4D model into a device; details are in `COMPARATOR_AND_RESOURCE_LEDGER.md`.

## What is already classical, and what still needs a theorem

For syndrome-conditioned Pauli jumps acting on code input, the quantum trajectory can be represented by a logical-input-independent Pauli error process. If the corrected logical channel is a Pauli mixture with identity weight p_I, its unhalved diamond distance to identity is exactly 2(1-p_I). A maximally entangled input proves tightness. Protecting all six logical qubits instead of one is operationally useful, but this conversion and reference handling are established QEC algebra.

For C1, Kraus expansion into local Paulis is also established linear-span correction. Choi interior makes D+E/nu CPTP for ||E||_diamond<=nu/d^2; it is elementary regularization, not a new physical protection principle. Earlier tools deserve explicit credit: [Knill-Laflamme quant-ph/9604034v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9604034v1) for scalar recovered Kraus and linear closure; [Horodecki-Ekert quant-ph/0111064v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0111064v1) and [Augusiak et al. 1008.5056v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1008.5056v3) for depolarizing Choi regularization; and [Wolf et al. 0711.3172v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.3172v2) for isotropic noise at generator level. Those tools do not create a good schedule.

The actual potentially new conjunction is: (i) an asynchronous support envelope that tolerates continual births and all local Pauli choices; (ii) uniform forcing from finite fresh primitives with correct dependence/quantifier order; (iii) transfer of an infinite-volume classical cluster tail to stopped periodic histories without changing the law; (iv) accumulated homology at every physical time; and (v) one recovery working for the entire error span on each good schedule. If any of these fails, collecting predecessor theorems does not repair the claim. If they hold, the memory mechanism is a classical stochastic estimate plus a quantum code transfer, not a new law of physics.

No theorem inspected here directly makes all those bridges redundant. That conclusion is narrower than originality: searches are finite, historical priority remains UNKNOWN, and no external proof validation is claimed. A rigorous external review should focus on those bridges and the exact static-versus-scheduled distinction.

## Meaningful conditional consequences and next discriminator

`CONDITIONAL_CONSEQUENCES.md` derives an additional time-dependent perturbation corollary and endpoint-error budget. Both are conditional on the universal good-schedule theorem. They are not evidence that an implementation meets the assumptions.

A useful next mathematical discriminator is an explicit lower bound on the allowed rate/tolerance for a small specified patch catalog, or removal of the floor while retaining local-generator robustness. A useful physical discriminator is a hardware-specific channel realization with measured error, routing latency, entropy removal and noisy recovery, compared with the same-access qLDPC or 3D incumbent. Until those interfaces are acquired, no practical superiority or historic breakthrough is established.
