ERRATA AND EVIDENCE PRECEDENCE — 2026-10-10

All investigator reports are preliminary research records. A FAIL or
UNRESOLVED verdict is not an endorsement of every intermediate calculation.
The following errors were found; this is not a claim that no others remain.

Precedence: the five retained scoped derivations and this errata index,
then the explicit dated errata and focused audits, then the preliminary
reports. Later errata take precedence over earlier conflicting arithmetic.
Only claims explicitly discussed in a focused audit were audited. Do not
treat a filename containing 'freeze' or a report's self-description as a
cryptographic commitment; version-complete immutable snapshots were not made.

round1/01.txt: A scan-based simulation does not itself prove the invoked
O(t log t) universal-simulation overhead. That overhead must be imported from
an appropriate efficient universal-simulation theorem. No new hierarchy
theorem or self-contained proof of that simulation result was established.

round1/03.txt: For u=a cos(N x_1)(e_2+e_3) on a periodic domain containing
whole periods, ||u||_2^2=a^2 Vol and ||curl u||_2^2=a^2 N^2 Vol. The report's
values were twice these. Energy control is supercritical for the regularity
problem; the terminology 'subcritical' in that discussion must be corrected.

round1/12.txt: A width-w bucket has at most 2*3^w clauses containing either
polarity of the eliminated variable, not 3^w. The larger displayed headline
9^(w+1) bound still accommodates this count. See round1/12_challenge.txt.
The method is standard Davis–Putnam elimination with a supplied width bound.

round1/23_challenge.txt: For pure amplitudes (1,1,i)/sqrt(3), the matrix entry
rho_12 is -i/3, not +i/3 (zero-based labels). Reverse the corresponding block
phase. The phase-alignment principle and adjacent-coherence formula survive.

round1/24.txt: If two rounds each measure points with error at most rho,
true old positions have minimum separation Delta, and displacement is at
most eta, the sufficient nearest-neighbour margin is
2 eta + 4 rho < Delta. The weaker reported 2 eta + 2 rho margin is inadequate
under that measurement model.

round2/05.txt: The flat-profile overlap 1-n/(2L) is valid only for n<=L;
the general expression is E[(1-W/L)_+] for W~Bin(n,1/2). The uniform sine
upper bound, mixed-state lower bound, energy cost, and exact output/reference
contract are in COORDINATOR_ENERGY_DERIVATION.txt. A fixed input's JOINT error
does not lower-bound the reduced-system diamond norm. Shared and separate
references do not have equal numbers of resource preparations. See also
round2/05_prior_art.txt and round2/coordinator_energy_audit.txt.

round3/07.txt: See round3/07_erratum.txt. The Vandermonde Frobenius bound is
r*max(1,B)^(r-1), not sqrt(r)*max(1,B)^(r-1). The consequent lower bound must
use r^(2(r-1)) in its denominator. Finite-step Cauchy bounds need a smaller
interior radius. Certified root isolation and nonzero denominator bounds
are indispensable to the claimed interval procedure; no full implementation
was supplied. Unknown dimension cannot be certified by excluding arbitrarily
weak unseen modes from finite-noise data.

round3/10.txt: See round3/10_erratum.txt. Covariance drift is bounded by A_s/s,
not A_s/s^2 for general s. The proposed weighted closure is false. Even under
that hypothetical closure, the displayed constant-4 argument was invalid;
a safe conditional constant is 8. The first erratum also contained an
incorrect substitution, corrected there on the coordinator's second check.
The source N75 full technical proof was unavailable; the reconstruction is
source-dependent and cannot be relabelled a certified source theorem.

round3/13.txt: See round3/13_erratum.txt. The rank-two example has nonzero
off-diagonal blocks. Its correct q_2 value is 13/4, not 5/4. It disproves a
naive rank-preservation induction, not the all-copy positivity target.

round3/15_gauge.txt: The then-unresolved full complex invertible local-gauge
case is superseded by COORDINATOR_GL_OBSTRUCTION.txt and
round3/15_GL_audit.txt. Their obstruction also covers identical serial powers.
It does not cover arbitrary multiwire or heterogeneous grouping and implies
no general counting lower bound or impossibility of an FPRAS.

Other source-based assertions retain their original conditional or

CONTINUATION THROUGH ROUND FIVE

round5/01_independent.txt and initial final report: Calling the energy-derived
bound 'subcritical' was misleading PDE terminology. The energy class is
supercritical under Navier–Stokes scaling; the intended statement is that the
estimate is insufficient for critical-norm control. The final report is
corrected; the preliminary freeze is preserved as written.

round4/13.txt: Exact sampling of rational probabilities with a bounded number
of fair random bits is false (probability 1/3 is a counterexample). The
coordinator's exact conic compression supersedes the Monte Carlo output
argument. A stronger exterior IMAGE-space test uses D-dimensional vectors,
not a span of D-by-D matrices, and gives the fixed-instrument D-step cutoff.
See COORDINATOR_CHANNEL_COMPRESSION.txt, round4/01_exterior_audit.txt and
round4/13_caratheodory.txt. Correct raw product bit growth is O(t d^2 B+t log d)
for separately denominated dense rational inputs, not O(tB). This preserves
only the forgotten-outcome terminal channel, not original monitored records.

round4/21_matching_audit.txt: The initial hypergraph extension objection was
too broad. Swapping whole connected two-color components preserves coverage
even for hypergraph matchings. Branching defeats the source's path/cycle
surgery argument, not the legality of those whole-component swaps. Corrected
in place; no hypergraph FPRAS or impossibility conclusion follows.

round4/24_epr_extension.txt: Local untwisting does not remove global odd-loop
parity constraints on nonbipartite schedules. A common bipartite unitary gives
six-vertex weights (a+d,a+c,c+d,0), which satisfy ordinary triangles but need
not satisfy square triangles. The witness (5,8,5,0) fails 64<=25+25. Thus the
cited SQ-SUM algorithms do not cover it, and this is not an escape from the
local GL obstruction. The reciprocal two-gate block is a tensor feedback
construction, not an acyclic physical subcircuit. Its existence is no general
reduction. The legal connected variance witness is proved separately in
COORDINATOR_LOOP_VARIANCE.txt. Neither variance statement is counting hardness.

round5/17_independent.txt: For a deterministic output Q=f(H), the proposed
conditional mutual information I(Q;Y|H) is zero. It cannot detect anticipatory
capability. The corrected report uses context-conditioned match rate and ATP
flux; no mass-action implementation or occurrence in nature was established.

round5/22_independent.txt: Its diagonal-loop polynomial assertion is false.
In Wick's formula for a product of distinct Gaussian coordinates, each
coordinate occurs only once, so covariance diagonal entries are never paired.
For a PSD shift Sigma=A+lambda I, the moment directly equals haf(A), with no
coefficient extraction. The corrected report states the classical identity
and its iid estimator's exponential variance on disjoint edges; neither is
a new scalable counting capability.

round5/24.txt: A physical interaction tree is not necessarily an acyclic
factor graph on individual periodic-wire variables. Zero variance of full
tree-factor integration requires the latter property after justified
preprocessing. The one-use physical-tree exact formula instead follows from
the separate operator leaf-partial-trace identity. This distinction has
been corrected in the final report.

Other round-four and round-five independent freezes are preliminary derivations,
not validated claims. Source comparisons establish some direct antecedents,
not exhaustive novelty clearance. Only the explicitly retained coordinator
proofs received the scoped checks recorded in VERIFICATION.txt.

round6/15.txt: Corrected coefficient notation. The positive weight is the
square of a coefficient of P, not a coefficient of P squared. The canonical

round6/24.txt: Every merge OR signed split connects all four ports in the
underlying unsigned constraint graph; its component count is fixed by the
input. The signed connectivity bound can therefore be trivial on a connected
input. The iid estimator's explicit exponential variance on two qubits is
the decisive computational diagnosis, not a new efficient cluster algorithm.

round7/15.txt: The inverse-filter parent counterexample concerns that specified
