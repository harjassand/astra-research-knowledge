MATHEMATICS RETAINED, WITH ITS LIMITS

1. Joint return of a quantum energy reference.

For preparation of n zero-state qubits into |+>^n by a total-energy-conserving
unitary, require the entire output to be close to |+>^n tensor the SAME initial
reference, uncorrelated with it. If the reference has mean ladder energy mu
and D is the joint trace distance, the bounded Laplace witness proves

  D >= exp(-s mu) [1 - ((1+exp(-s))/2)^n]  for every s>0,
  D >= n/[8e(mu+n)].

An explicit supplied sine reference gives an upper bound of order n/L for
length L, establishing optimal mean-energy order Theta(n/epsilon) for this
particular small-error task. The proof also handles mixed references and
unbounded support in its lower bound. It does not acquire free coherence,
solve fault tolerance, or establish a reduced-system channel optimum.

The sine profile, conserving lift, and shared-battery reuse have established
antecedents, particularly Chiribella, Yang and Renner (2021). The exact
same-reference formulation's novelty was not established. Gate: NOT PASSED.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.021014

2. Exact Gaussian norm evaluation hides a counting computation.

There is an explicit polynomial-time Turing reduction from the permanent of
a 0/1 matrix to exact E[p(G)^2] evaluation for integer arithmetic circuits
and independent standard real Gaussians G. It uses a 0/1 Gram construction,
an explicitly proved Gaussian pairing identity, polynomial interpolation,
and an exact integer square root. Variable count, circuit size, query count,
and bit lengths are all polynomial. With Valiant's permanent theorem, this
establishes #P-hardness of the specified EXACT norm-evaluation problem.

It gives no hardness conclusion for approximate norms, norm-zero testing,
randomized identity testing, or deterministic identity testing. The argument
rejects an uncharged exact-norm primitive; its novelty is unverified and it
does not solve the intended complexity problem. Gate: NOT PASSED.
Imported theorem: https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(79)90044-6

3. An obstruction to a positive local matching representation.

For the explicit four-port tensor
  F = [[4,0,0,1],[0,9,4,0],[0,4,9,0],[1,0,0,4]],
no four independent invertible complex 2-by-2 port transformations turn F
into a nonnegative weighted matching-gadget signature. The same holds for
every identical serial power F^k, k>=1. The proof combines a directly proved
local-group invariant with Cai–Liu's necessary product inequalities.

The invariant root multiset {13,5,5,3} fails every required pair partition:
65>17 or 39>25. These inequalities extend to every positive integer power.
This allows the full complex local invertible group, not only orthogonal
changes of basis. It leaves heterogeneous/multiwire gadgets, nonlocal or
approximate representations, and the general counting problem unresolved.
It is no complexity separation. Novelty is unverified. Gate: NOT PASSED.
Imported condition: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10493, Theorem 4.1.

PRECISE UNSOLVED GATES

The latest search has no selected foundational direction. Its most concrete
attempt was deterministic acquisition for real min-plus computation. A valid
conditional greedy construction hits large sets defined by known pivots, but
the full algorithm needs small rank-prefix sets and their unknown pivots.
At that scale the available construction bound exceeds cubic time; witness
isolation is also unresolved. This rejects the proposed route, not every
deterministic algorithm. The source already proves a Las Vegas real-input
result, so extending bounded integer weights to real weights is not ours:
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06783v1 , Theorem 35.

The older equations below are retained for provenance. In particular the
first, NPT track, is shelved under the user's current steering.

There is no single selected foundational candidate. The most concrete
outstanding equations from the final pass are:

* NPT entanglement: prove or refute, for EVERY n>=1 and EVERY complex
  3^n-by-3^n matrix C of rank at most two,

    sum over S subset {1,...,n} of (-1/2)^|S| ||Tr_S C||_F^2 >= 0.

  This is the all-copy rank-two positivity gate for the boundary qutrit
  Werner state (2I_9-F_swap)/15. Partial trace can increase the matrix rank;
  the proposed induction therefore fails. The corrected two-copy example
  has value 13/4, not a negative value. Neither an all-copy proof nor a
  counterexample was obtained. A proposed coupled block inequality is
  algebraically equivalent to the target, so it supplies no independent
  premise that can honestly be called progress through this obstruction.

* Approximate counting of the specified physical tensor networks: a
  polynomial-cost global normalization/variance mechanism is missing.
  Positive individual determinants do not control total relative variance;
  width-dependent dynamic programming does not solve arbitrary width.
  The local basis-change approach above is now excluded for the test gate.

* General arithmetic-circuit identity testing: no efficiently acquired
  deterministic hitting direction or representation was obtained. Exact

* KLS via noisy observations: the required weighted-tail control of
  E[Var(f(X)|Y) ||Cov(X|Y)||op] remains missing. An exponential-prior tail
  disproves the proposed simple closure. Average covariance evolution does

* Finite-noise nonlinear identification: a uniform, efficiently acquired
