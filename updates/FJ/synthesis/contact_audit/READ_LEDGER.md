## Primary research and exact read depth

1. T. J. Meijer et al., A General Non-Strict Finsler's Lemma, arXiv:2403.10306v1. Primary arXiv HTML/PDF. Read the abstract and introductory caveat on non-strict Finsler, Lemma 2 statement (HTML lines 76-90 / PDF page 1), and its proof discussion (HTML lines 251-277). This supports only the indefinite non-strict quadratic decomposition. The incoming note also gives an elementary ratio proof; the result is not applied to semidefinite determinant forms.

   https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10306v1

2. S. Gutiérrez, A Necessary Condition for the Quasiconvexity of Polynomials of Degree Four, Journal of Convex Analysis 13 (2006), 51-60. Primary publisher PDF. Read title/abstract (PDF lines 0-15), the introduction's scope (lines 78-101), and Proposition 3.3 plus proof (lines 363-427, PDF pages 4-5). This is a prior cubic-contact closure antecedent; it does not state the present quantitative Type-II estimate.

   https://www.heldermann-verlag.de/jca/jca13/jca0514_b.pdf

3. D. D. Haroske and H.-J. Schmeisser, Gagliardo-Nirenberg Inequalities for Spaces with Dominating Mixed Derivatives, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 206 (2017), 117-135. Primary publisher record only. Read title/authors/publication data and abstract (lines 11-39). Full chapter text was not available through that record. Its general mixed-smoothness theory is contextual; Lemma 3.1 is proved directly above and is not attributed as a verbatim theorem.

   https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-10-6119-6_5

4. G. Bruno and F. Pasqualotto, Quasiconvexity for the Dacorogna-Marcellini Energy, arXiv:2608.06367v1. Primary arXiv HTML. Read title/abstract (lines 27-34), Theorem 2 and invariant class (lines 97-109), and heat-flow/exact-form proof strategy (lines 112-125; also lines 132-145). It treats quartics invariant under both left and right rotations, a narrower class than the full left-only cone here; its heat-flow proof is not imported.

   https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1
