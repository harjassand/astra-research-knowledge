## Primary literature inspected

1. **Sergio Gutiérrez (2006), “A Necessary Condition for the Quasiconvexity of Polynomials of Degree Four,” Journal of Convex Analysis 13, 51–60.** Official publisher PDF: <https://journalofconvexanalysis.com/articles/jca13004/jca13004.pdf?v=2> (DOI <https://doi.org/10.68381/jca13004>). Read the PDF text through page 7, including the abstract/introduction (PDF lines 0–101), the compensated-compactness cubic contact condition (Proposition 3.1, lines 194–254), its planar-gradient discussion (Propositions 3.2–3.3, lines 272–427), and the quartic three-wave moment condition and gradient corollary (Proposition 4.1 and Corollary 4.2, lines 428–678).

   **Comparison.** This is close prior art for the resonance/cubic-null part. Proposition 3.1 gives a necessary cubic-derivative vanishing condition for three characteristic pairs with frequency directions of rank below three; for gradients those pairs lie on the rank-one wave cone. Propositions 4.1–4.2 use a three-wave cosine sequence and quartic Taylor expansion to obtain a finite-dimensional inequality involving the Hessian and fourth derivative of a quartic. Gutiérrez explicitly observes that the earlier cubic condition does not separate rank-one convexity from quasiconvexity in the planar (2\times2) case. The present result does not claim invention of cubic resonance tests, compensated compactness, or three-wave conditions. It gives a different quantitative statement: an (L^2\)-energy by (L^4\)-total and (L^4\)-transverse estimate for the average cubic over every compatible (W^{1,4}) gradient, including arbitrary resonances and concentration, under a local Hodge-symbol-ideal criterion. The (b^2c) sequence shows why the wave cone and cubic restriction alone do not imply this stronger estimate.

2. **Jean Van Schaftingen (2013), “Limiting Sobolev inequalities for vector fields and canceling linear differential operators,” Journal of the European Mathematical Society 15, 877–921.** Official EMS PDF: <https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/31890?nt=1> (DOI <https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/380>). Read the theorem statement and abstract, PDF lines 0–30. It characterizes the (L^1\)-endpoint estimate \(\|D^{k-1}u\|_{L^{n/(n-1)}}\lesssim\|A(D)u\|_{L^1}\) by ellipticity and cancellation. **Comparison.** This is primary operator-theoretic context, but it is not the present trilinear estimate: our Hodge correction uses (L^p)-bounded order-zero multipliers at (p=2,4), and the cubic bound additionally needs a contact polynomial identity. No conclusion that cancellation alone suffices is made.
