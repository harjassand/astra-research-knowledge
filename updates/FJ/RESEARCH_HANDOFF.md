global tube test fails but positive-vector certificate succeeds. State which
normalizers, global inequalities and endpoint laws are acquired, and their
cost. Supply an independent end-to-end commonly rounded Gaussian target with
certified primitive/acceptance arithmetic, abort mass, noise covariance and
full nested runtime accounting. An ideal-real node cap is not sufficient.

the required mean field is not supplied, both methods receive identical model
and certificates, and the composed sampler improves complete cost against
FORS supplied the same oracle, established branching/supersolution solvers,
multilevel Picard and deterministic defect correction. Charge certificate,
predictor and covariance construction. The coupled 8D benchmark's deterministic
interval answer already meets 1% and must remain a control, not be discarded.
For multiple draws or rare conditioning, set joint-law error accordingly.

Do not transfer deterministic-query bump lower bounds to randomized output
sampling: the Gaussian-mixture counter invalidates that transfer. N687's

## Theoretical Pro: moving-direction compensated Schur residual

Operation: control the all-background quartic Jensen gap near a rank-one
contact without assuming a fixed soft Hessian direction or bounded optimizer.
For the decomposition in the global-contact report, the missing statement is

    |r_A-k^T D^-1 r_W|^2 <= C delta^2 (E s^4) (h-k^T D^-1 k).

Inputs: an actual mean-zero periodic/zero-boundary compatible gradient
`X=sR+Y`, small **relative** L2 and L4 transverse norms, the exact full-cone
contact structure, and positive complement D. Specify fixed-integrand versus
uniform-over-integrands/contact quantifiers. Fixed-direction cubic compensation
and range closure are available; they do not provide the displayed reduced
denominator. The rank-two covariance example with H>0 and h*/h→0 rejects a
uniform h*>=theta h shortcut. The concentration family rejects uniformly
bounded optimizing backgrounds. Both have positive optimized gaps and are
mandatory controls. L4 weakening and wave-cone-only cubic cancellation also
have compatible counterexamples.

Acceptance test: a complete differential-compatible proof stable under moving
contacts and concentration, or an exact **compatible** negative optimized gap
with all source assumptions certified. Abstract determinant-balanced matrix
laws, positive H and additional finite Fourier agreement do not pass this gate.
The operator-criterion generalization may guide representation search, but its
Hodge/symbol hypotheses must be proved for each new constraint.

## Natural Sciences Pro: assembled-response shunt holdout

Operation: identify the intervention-visible effective path without reconstructing
all internal components. Under fixed reciprocal wet matrices and an isolated
known passive shunt `t uu^T`, acquire baseline and two positive-load hydraulic
response matrices. If the fingerprint is nonzero, they identify `(q,F)` in
`Delta H(t)=t F/(1+tq)`. Predict a third independent load before measuring it.
Field reversal predicts transposition under unchanged wet conditions.

First gate: obtain same-device, zero-longitudinal-bias, steady K,G and reciprocal
neutral-liquid/electronic cross-coefficients C in both directions. Existing
droplet signals and theoretical high coupling do not supply that gate. Measure
noise, leakage, ionic/electrochemical/displacement effects, temperature and
gate power; controls must support invariant wet law under the shunt.

Acceptance test: uncertainty intervals retain nonzero fingerprint, rank-one
load dependence and the third-load prediction, plus reciprocal/open-path and
field-reversal controls. A direct hydraulic response change can counterfeit
the load curve; no-direct-effect isolation is essential. Include the cold
noise audit's full frequency model for real finite-time measurements. At
common temperature, loss/noise must obey complete FDT. Report gross donor
work, receiver work, internal shunt heat and independent auxiliary inputs
without double counting. Weak couplings can make sample cost scale as the
inverse fourth power of their amplitude in the stated Gaussian experiment.

Only after this test should a device comparison be proposed. Compare directly
with conventional isolated pump/valve/sensor control at useful throughput,
bandwidth, lifetime and complete installed power. The model's topology-specific
efficiency ceiling and unmeasured material constants give no energy-generation
or civilisation-scale performance claim.
