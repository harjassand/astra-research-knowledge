1. What this establishes

The conditional-equilibrium transport certificate in the submitted kinetic
packet has an exact Poisson/resolvent interpretation. The least possible action
is the weighted H^-1 norm of the conditional score. A changing gate population
necessarily pays its overlap with slow modes; a slow mode orthogonal to that
score need not impose a cost. This is established transport/potential theory
reconstructed here, not a new Wasserstein principle.

The new explicit cross-capital falsifier gives a finite reversible family whose
entire static density, strict affine rank decoder and exact static sampler are
cheap and identical, yet useful lifetime certification requires Omega(2^n)
local conductance queries. It refutes the named transfer from acquired ordinal
or static representations to acquired kinetic gate information. It does not
refute the supplied unit-mobility potential-oracle model or prove a general
computational lower bound for molecular kinetics.

2. Continuum action theorem, with exact domain assumptions

Let p=p_s>0 be a normalized differentiable family of conditional densities on a
compact connected smooth domain with reflecting or periodic boundaries. Let
D=D_s(y) be a supplied symmetric uniformly positive definite transverse
mobility. Assume sufficient smoothness for integration by parts; all statements
also have their usual weak-energy formulation. Put

    h=partial_s log p,       E_p h=0,
    L f = p^-1 div(p D grad f).

At this fixed s define

    K_min = inf_v E_p[v^T D^-1 v]
      subject to div(pv)=-partial_s p and zero reflecting normal flux.

Let psi be the mean-zero solution of

    -L psi=h, equivalently -div(p D grad psi)=partial_s p.       (A1)

Then

    v_*=D grad psi,
    K_min=E_p[grad psi^T D grad psi]
         =<h,(-L)^-1 h>_p.                                   (A2)

Proof. Equation(A1) makes v_* feasible. If v=v_*+w is another feasible
velocity, div(pw)=0. Integration by parts yields

    E_p[w.grad psi]=0.

Expanding the quadratic energy gives

    E_p[v^T D^-1v] = K_min + E_p[w^T D^-1w].                  (A3)

This proves minimality, its exact excess energy and(A2). Constants are removed
in the inverse; on this compact connected uniformly elliptic domain the
mean-zero inverse is well-defined. Unbounded domains need the additional
energy/domain assumptions stated below, not an automatic inverse.

For a stationary reversible process Y_t with generator L,

    K_min=integral_0^infinity <h,exp(t L)h>_p dt
         =integral_0^infinity Cov_p[h(Y_0),h(Y_t)]dt.          (A4)

Proof. The spectral theorem gives exp(tL) on the nonnegative spectrum of -L.
Integrate exp(-lambda t) over t and interchange with the nonnegative spectral
measure of h. The result is integral lambda^-1 dmu_h(lambda), possibly infinite.
For compact uniformly elliptic domains this equals(A2). On unbounded domains,
this finite spectral integral is the appropriate H^-1 condition; an L^2 Poisson
solution need not exist. A4 asserts no central limit theorem and requires no
unproved interchange of thermodynamic and long-time limits.

If -L phi_k=lambda_k phi_k with orthonormal mean-zero eigenfunctions, then

    K_min=sum_k |<h,phi_k>_p|^2/lambda_k.                     (A5)

In particular K_min>=|<h,phi>_p|^2/lambda for every normalized eigenmode.
A global gap lower bound is sufficient for K_min<=Var_p(h)/gap, but is not
necessary. Small gap with zero score overlap is harmless for this action.

For a Gibbs conditional p_s proportional to exp(-beta U_s),

    h=-beta[partial_s U_s-E_p partial_s U_s],
    K_min=beta^2 integral_0^infinity
           Cov_p[partial_s U_s(Y_0),partial_s U_s(Y_t)]dt.    (A6)

Thus estimating instantaneous fluctuations alone does not acquire the action.
The integrated dynamical response or an equivalent source-conservative field
must be acquired and certified.

3. Consequence for the submitted lifetime construction

The packet has m(s,y)=w(s)p_s(y), M(s)=integral_0^s w(t)dt and Z=M(1).
Its feasible current is J_s=M p and J_y=M p v. With unit longitudinal
mobility and the transverse D above, its added dual energy is exactly

    (1/Z) integral_0^1 [M(s)^2/w(s)] K(s)ds.

For this specific conditional-equilibrium current ansatz, the smallest possible
added energy is obtained by K_min in(A2). If a slow conditional gate mode has
score overlap at least a(s) and eigenvalue at most lambda(s), every current in
this ansatz pays at least

    (1/Z) integral_0^1 [M^2/w] a(s)^2/lambda(s)ds.           (A7)

This lower bound concerns the ansatz's added certificate energy. It is NOT a
lower bound on the true lifetime, its primal-dual gap, or computational work.
The more general cumulative-mass Piola compiler need not impose J_s=M p; a
multichart or different-fiber construction is not ruled out by(A7).

The translated-bath sector supplies an explicit cancellation. On the line or
R^d with D=I, p_s(y)=p_0(y-b(s)) admits v=b'(s), so K_min<=|b'|^2 regardless
of its global spectral gap. If linear psi=b'.y is in the energy domain and the
integration-by-parts assumptions hold, it solves(A1) and equality follows.
On a periodic domain the constant current remains feasible and gives the bound;
claiming that it is always the gradient minimizer would be incorrect.

This is the exact structural opening: acquire a bounded-energy divergence
representation of the score, or acquire a certificate that its low-frequency
spectral weight is small. Naming a representation or fitting density does
neither. A genuinely reweighting slow gate with O(1) score overlap cannot be
made cheap within the conditional-equilibrium ansatz merely by changing its
transport velocity.

4. Finite reversible version and a two-state gate

Let B be incidence on a connected finite graph, C=diag(c_e)>0 its physical
conductances, A=B C B^T, and p a positive normalized probability vector.
The reversible generator is Q=-diag(p)^-1 A. A tangent dot p has zero sum.
Then the exact minimum flow energy is

    min_(Bj=dot p) j^T C^-1 j
      = dot p^T A^+ dot p
      = <h,(-Q)^+h>_p,       h_i=dot p_i/p_i.              (A8)

A^+ denotes an inverse modulo constants; a grounded solve gives the same
quadratic form. The proof is the finite integration-by-parts identity(A3).
The sign convention for the physical continuity current can be reversed
without changing energy. This is standard Thomson theory for a signed source.

Take p_s(1)=exp(s)/(1+exp(s)), p_s(0)=1-p_s(1), and, for r>0,

    k_01(s)=2r p_s(1),       k_10(s)=2r p_s(0).

All equilibrium laws and their scores are independent of r. The nonzero
eigenvalue of -Q is 2r and c=2r p(1-p), so

    K_min=(p')^2/[2r p(1-p)].

At s=0, p=1/2, p'=1/4 and

    K_r=1/(8r),       K_(2r)=1/(16r).                      (A9)

Full exact static samples, exact potential values and exact potential ranks
cannot distinguish these two kinetics. Their mobility is different. If the
calibrated generator is supplied, computing(A9) is immediate; no computational
hardness follows from its large value.

With only passive jump trajectories at s=0, the total exit rate is r in either
state. Couple the r and2r processes using the same rate-r jump clock, adding
an independent rate-r clock in the faster process. Until that extra clock rings,
transcripts and all adaptive decisions agree. For any stopping protocol with
total observed trajectory time T, the first mismatch probability is at most
r E_r T. Thus

    TV(output_r,output_(2r)) <= r E_r T.                    (A10)

Fresh static samples/restarts add no distinction. The result also applies to
parallel replicas with T their summed exposure. Any decision distinguishing
the two rates with wrong probability<=alpha and UNKNOWN probability<=delta
in both cases needs

    E_r T >= max(0,1-delta-2alpha)/r.                       (A11)

A multiplicative action estimate with relative error<1/3 supplies such a
decision because the two permitted estimate intervals in(A9) are disjoint.
This is a finite CTMC observation-time result. Ideal continuously observed
diffusion paths can expose mobility through quadratic variation; A11 must not
be transferred to that different interface.

5. Exponential local-query obstruction despite complete static decoding

The following theorem removes even equilibrium-data acquisition as a possible
explanation for a kinetic advantage. Its access model explicitly withholds
physical conductances.

For an integer n>=1 let N=2^n. The N transient states i=1,...,N form a known
path to absorbing state0. Raw state x in F_2^n encodes i=1+integer(x). Give
each transient state the known rational mass

    m_i=(N+i)/N in(1,2],       Z=sum_i m_i=(3N+1)/2.

These masses, their normalizer, their whole probability law and their strict
order are known. Their energies U_i=-log(m_i)/beta have the strict rank
Q(x)=bitwise_complement(x), an invertible affine triangular decoder with no
nonlinear terms. Chosen-pair comparisons and all decoder operations are
therefore identical and trivial across the family.

An exact equilibrium-conditioned sample is also cheap: draw x uniformly, draw
an (n+1)-bit uniform integer z in{0,...,2N-1}, accept if z<N+i, and repeat.
The acceptance probability m_i/2 is at least1/2, so expected proposals<=2.
All randomization and integer work have O(n) bit cost per proposal.

The sole kinetic oracle accepts an edge index i and returns its calibrated
conductance c_i for edge(i-1,i). The possibilities are:

    base: c_i=1 for every i;
    gate(j): c_j=eta=1/(100N^3), c_i=1 for i!=j,
             with one unknown j in{1,...,N}.

The rates are k_i,i-1=c_i/m_i and their reversible counterparts; grounding
state0 gives the absorbing model. Every instance has finite hitting means.
Masses and conductances require O(n) bits. The known path is an implicit O(n)
adjacency computation, not a supplied exponential table.

Let H_i=sum_(k=i)^N m_k. Conservation on a path forces current H_i along
edge i, so the exact equilibrium-conditioned mean is

    tau=(1/Z) sum_(i=1)^N H_i^2/c_i.                        (A12)

Equivalently, this is m^T A^-1 m/Z for the grounded physical Laplacian A.
The base mean is at most4N^2: H_i<=2N and Z>=N. For every gate j,

    tau_gate(j)>=H_j^2/(eta Z)>=1/(eta 2N)=50N^2.           (A13)

Thus any factor-two estimate distinguishes base from every gate: all allowed
base estimates are<=8N^2 and all allowed gate estimates are>=25N^2.

Theorem. Suppose an arbitrary randomized adaptive algorithm, using the static
inputs above and local conductance queries, outputs base/gate/UNKNOWN. On each
instance let wrong probability be<=alpha and UNKNOWN probability<=delta.
If Q is its total number of conductance queries on base, then

    E_base Q >= max(0,1-delta-2alpha) N.                     (A14)

Proof. Choose j uniformly and couple base and gate(j) using the same internal
randomness and same free static sample stream. Along the base run let S be the
set of distinct queried edges. The two transcripts remain identical unless
j is in S. Hence

    TV(output_base, mixture_j output_gate(j))
      <= E_base |S|/N <= E_base Q/N.

The event output=base has probability>=1-delta-alpha on base and<=alpha
under the uniform gate mixture. Its separation is at least1-delta-2alpha,
proving(A14). Infinite expected cost is harmless. The same lower bound applies
to factor-two lifetime estimation, after the deterministic threshold test
between8N^2 and25N^2. QED.

This is an exact countermodel to statics/ordinal acquisition implying cheap
kinetic acquisition. It is elementary unstructured search in the named oracle,
not a new complexity principle. It gives Omega(2^n) queries despite a perfect
affine decoder, cheap exact conditional sampling and O(n)-bit numeric inputs.
It does NOT apply when the hidden conductance is supplied, a nonlocal aggregate
conductance oracle is legal, or the calibrated energy/geometry/mobility model
allows its cheap derivation. It does NOT prove this hardness for molecular
potentials with fixed unit mobility and supplied smoothness bounds.

6. The decisive missing mathematics

For the actual energy/force-oracle molecular objective, prove a construction
and validation theorem that acquires a full-source-covering map or branching
current with a useful residence-weighted gap at cost below matched specialized
methods. State the allowed potential/mobility/geometry class and all oracle,
precision, quadrature and tail costs. A useful sufficient opening would acquire
either(a) an explicitly source-conservative low-energy representation of the
conditional score, or(b) its low-frequency spectral-weight certificate, without
equivalent global gate/committor computation. Neither is proved here.

If the score really overlaps a depleted gate mode, A7 says that changing only
the transport velocity cannot close the certificate. A different Piola or
multichart representation may reconstruct that resistance cheaply from known
geometry, but its acquisition and integration are the remaining theorem.
N486/N490's verified continuation/second-moment certificates face the same
equivalent-problem acquisition risk. Their guarantees do not supply the
Poisson field, mobility or global source coverage needed here.
