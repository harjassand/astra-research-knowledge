MATCHING VALIDATION AND ROBUST PARTIAL QUADRATIC FLAG ACQUISITION
2026-10-13. Internal proof. Novelty/external review UNKNOWN. Mission NOT_ACHIEVED.

Contract. Q:F_2^n -> F_2^n is a bijection with quadratic Boolean coordinates,
ordered most significant first. There exists a length h=n-r partial translation
flag: independent w_1,...,w_h, where Delta_wj q_i=0 for i<j and Delta_wj q_j=1.
Directions, coefficients, ranks, inverse and conditional samplers are NOT
supplied. Chosen-pair oracle C is fixed; a fixed unknown B of density at most
epsilon satisfies C(x,y)=1[Q(x)<lex Q(y)] whenever x,y are outside B. All
incident-B answers may be arbitrary, persistent, nontransitive and inconsistent
under reversal. Assume epsilon<1/16. Source/class/noise acquisition is separate.

1. Matching cut validator (proved).
Suppose acquired q_1,...,q_k and their own-toggles v_1,...,v_k are TRUE. Their
exact common translation stabilizer S_k and exact uniform fiber sampler are
computed from acquired quadratic coefficients. Fix any candidate f of degree
at most two and v!=0 in S_k with Delta_v f=1 identically. This algebraic gate
is B_f(v,.)=0 and f(v)+f(0)=1; it makes f balanced on every true prefix fiber.
The true next bit q=q_(k+1) is also balanced there, by bijectivity.

One batch chooses a uniformly random prefix a, then draws m independent
uniform positions from each F_a intersect {f=0} and F_a intersect {f=1}.
Obtain each half-sample by projecting a uniform fiber sample along v; every
half-point has exactly two preimages. The two lists, including any repeated
raw items, are the graph vertices. Add edge (i,j) iff observed C(y0_i,y1_j)=0.
The halves are disjoint so no equal-input comparison is needed. Canonical
raw orientation and complemented reverse answers may be used, but are not
needed: every clean forward answer already has the required ideal value.
Score Z=matching_size/m in [0,1]. A maximum OR ANY MAXIMAL matching works.
Greedy left-by-left scanning unmatched right vertices produces a maximal
matching with at most m^2 comparisons and O(m^2) elementary graph operations.

If f=q, every edge touches a sampled bad position. Thus any matching is
bounded by the total number of sampled bad positions. If b_a is bad density
inside F_a, E[Z|a]<=2b_a and E Z<=2epsilon. This does not assume iid item
corruption: only the fresh sample positions are iid conditional on a.

If f!=q, let e_a be conditional disagreement and let b0_a,b1_a be bad
densities in the two f-halves. Both balanced functions have the same mismatch
fraction e_a in each half. The clean wrong fractions p0,p1 satisfy
  p0>=e_a-b0_a, p1>=e_a-b1_a,
  min(p0,p1)>=max(0,e_a-2b_a), b0_a+b1_a=2b_a.
The counts X,Y of sampled clean wrong positions are independent binomials
Bin(m,p0),Bin(m,p1). Every pair of these positions is an inversion already
at true bit q, hence they induce K_(X,Y). Any maximal matching has size at
least min(X,Y): otherwise both sides have unmatched wrong positions whose
connecting edge would extend it. Arbitrary extra edges cannot spoil this.

Put c_m=binom(2m,m)/(2*4^m). Then
  E min(X,Y)/m >= min(p0,p1)-c_m,
  E Z >= E_a e_a-2epsilon-c_m >= 1/4-2epsilon-c_m.
The last inequality uses the degree-two minimum distance 1/4. The binomial
bound is proved by coupling the larger success probability down to the
smaller p. For equal p, loss is E|X-Y|/(2m). X-Y is an m-step symmetric lazy
walk with activation probability 2p(1-p); E absolute displacement increases
with the activation probability (the absolute value is convex and the
ordinary symmetric-walk expected absolute displacement is nondecreasing
in its step count). Hence the largest loss is at p=1/2. There X-Y is half a
2m-step symmetric walk, whose expected absolute displacement is
2m*binom(2m,m)/4^m. Thus the stated c_m is exact as a uniform binomial loss.
An elementary alternative is c_m<=1/sqrt(8m), by variance/Cauchy-Schwarz.

Choose m with Delta=1/4-4epsilon-c_m>0. Threshold tau=1/8-c_m/2 separates
the means. Average T fresh independent batches, accept iff mean<=tau. For
both a true candidate's false rejection and a fixed wrong candidate's false
acceptance, Hoeffding gives exp(-T Delta^2/2). Set
  T=ceil(2 ln(1/delta)/Delta^2).
Fresh prefixes are needed across batches. Sampling many pairs within ONE
prefix does not give the global Hoeffding statement. Candidate, B and C are
fixed before these new random seeds; conditioning on a correct adaptive
history preserves the proof. No claimed finite-m optimality.

2. Local RM correction used below (established component, explicit version).
A raw label oracle differs from a true degree-two polynomial on at most rho
of the global cube, where gamma=1/8-rho>0. It can also be randomized with
independent internal randomness per evaluation and global expected error
at most rho. To correct at x, draw l independent uniform raw n-bit columns A,
K=2^l with l>=3 and K>=4/gamma^2. Query at x+A t for all t in F_2^l.
Nonzero t images are pairwise independent uniform points, even when A is
noninjective. Error fraction Z has E Z<=rho+1/K and Var Z<=1/(4K), so
Pr[Z>=1/8]<=1/(K gamma^2)<=1/4 (1/K<=gamma/2). Classical checksum-majority
decoding of degree two on this K-point cube recovers the value at t=0 when
Z<1/8. Odd R>=8 ln(1/eta) independent cubes and majority of corrected
origin values give failure<=eta. Comparisons per corrected raw label are KR
times the cost of one raw-label evaluation. Cube construction O(n l K),
checksum decoder O(K*(1+l+binom(l,2))) bit work per repetition.

Primary source checked live: Gopalan, Klivans, Zuckerman, author-hosted
List-Decoding Reed-Muller Codes over Small Fields, PDF page 2, lines 110-115:
https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~diz/Sub%20Websites/Research/List_decoding_reed_muller_codes_over_small_fields.pdf
It identifies Reed majority-logic unique decoding and its local adaptation.
Our proof does not import list decoding, optimality, or an unexamined stronger
algorithm. K is material and may be large near the corruption threshold.

3. Capped flagged-prefix acquisition.
At correct stage k<h, a uniform v in S_k is a genuine next own-toggle with
probability at least p=2^(-r-1). Proof: dim S_k<=n-k because its free translation
orbits lie inside a true fiber of size 2^(n-k). The remaining original flag
span has dimension h-k and is contained in S_k intersect rad B_q. The linear
toggle response is nonzero there, so its one-coset occupies at least
2^(h-k-1) of S_k, a fraction at least p. Sampling includes zero, which is skipped.

For nonzero v, raw g_v(x)=1+C(x,x+v) agrees with q whenever both endpoints are
clean IF v is good; its error density is then at most 2epsilon. Thus use the
RM corrector with rho=2epsilon, gamma=1/8-2epsilon. At the M=1+n+binom(n,2)
unisolvent anchors, correct each raw label with eta=1/(4M), interpolate the
unique quadratic f, and perform the exact own-toggle gate. On good v the
entire body is true with probability at least 3/4. On other v its body is an
arbitrary quadratic; no correction promise is made. Reject failed gates.
Run the matching validator with fresh seeds on every gate-passing body.

For h>0, 0<beta<=1/2, choose
  Rcap=ceil((2/p) ln(4h/beta)),
  delta=beta/(4h Rcap^2),
  T=ceil(2 ln(1/delta)/Delta^2).
On any correct-entry trial, true acceptance probability is at least
p*(3/4)*(1-delta)>=p/2. Thus Rcap exhaustion contributes at most beta/4
over h stages. Before the first wrong acceptance, each fixed false body
passes with conditional probability at most delta, so its total probability
is at most h Rcap delta=beta/(4Rcap). Candidate-body correction failures
need NOT have a global semantic union budget: their effect on availability
is in the 3/4 factor, and every false body's soundness is handled by fresh
matching validation. This saves confidence overhead in rejected candidates.
The prefix and its uniform sampler are true until a first wrong acceptance.
After it, keep all caps and make no semantic assumptions.

For every accepted f, compute S_(k+1) exactly by adjoining f's polar radical
and zero translation-response equations. The earlier accepted toggles are
independent. Complete them to a raw basis with complement W. Sequential
projection by adding v_i when q_i disagrees with a requested prefix gives
exactly one point per toggle-span coset, so uniform W seeds are exact uniform
fiber samples. These algebraic facts hold for every gate-passing acquired
model, including bounded bad histories; truth is needed only to interpret it.

4. Robust residual completion: TWO-bad-item rank lemma (proved).
Let F be any true prefix fiber of even size L=2t. Enumerate it and label x by
  z(x)=1[sum_(y in F,y!=x) C(y,x)>=t].
Let b0,b1 be numbers of bad items in the true next-bit halves; b=b0+b1. For
clean x let j be its predecessor rank among clean items. Observed count is
j+s_x with 0<=s_x<=b, without any transitivity requirement. Clean zeros have
j=0,...,t-b0-1; a wrong zero requires j>=t-b and there are at most b1 such
j. Clean ones have j=t-b0,...,L-b-1; a wrong one requires j<t and there are
at most b0 such j. Therefore clean mislabels<=b, bad mislabels<=b, total<=2b.
This improves the looser 3b bound and is tight even for a total-order oracle
(move b true-zero bad items above the cut; b clean ones move below it).
Summing over all equal-size true prefix fibers yields global error<=2epsilon.

After acquiring h true flagged bits, enumerate each h-prefix fiber via its
2^r acquired complement seeds. At suffix step j=1,...,r, filter it using the
already acquired j-1 suffix bits; on a true history this is exactly the current
fiber, size L_j=2^(r-j+1). The rank-threshold raw label therefore has global
error<=2epsilon and can be uniquely RM corrected at all M anchors. Interpolate
q_(h+j). Give all rM suffix corrected labels a combined failure budget beta/2.
No new own-toggle is needed. Check filtered fiber cardinality and return
UNKNOWN on inconsistency; all enumeration and correction calls remain capped
even after a first error. The overall wrong-or-UNKNOWN probability is at most
beta/4+beta/(4Rcap)+beta/2<=beta. When h=0 use only this residual routine,
which costs exponential 2^n as explicitly charged; fixed r is the polynomial
regime. When r=0 no residual calls are made.

5. Acquisition, execution and controls.
Let K be the local RM cube size, Rbody the least odd integer >=8 ln(4M), and
Rsuffix the least odd integer >=8 ln(2rM/beta) (zero suffix charge if r=0).
One direction costs at most M K Rbody + T m^2 comparisons. Hence
  Qmax <= h Rcap (M K Rbody+T m^2)
          + M K Rsuffix (2^(r+1)-r-2).
The tail sum is sum_j (L_j-1); it is not an uncharged noiseless rank lookup.
On correct-entry histories expected attempts<=2/p. Bad-entry probability
<=beta/(4Rcap), each has <=Rcap attempts, so unconditionally
  E Q <= h(2/p+beta/4)(M K Rbody+T m^2)
         + M K Rsuffix (2^(r+1)-r-2).
For fixed r, beta and positive gap from epsilon=1/16, this is polynomial in n;
these are comparison bounds, not all-resource advantage or optimality claims.

Each comparison transmits 2n input bits and returns one bit. Uncached source
rank work is at most 2Q true evaluations plus all actual oracle setup/work;
memoization charges unique endpoints and retained source storage separately.
Each matching batch uses k+2m(n-k) random bits, 2m fiber projections, m or
fewer retained matching edges, and <=m^2 comparisons. Directions draw dim S_k
bits. Every RM cube draws l*n bits, constructs all raw points, and decodes.
Residual raw labels enumerate 2^r acquired points, filter by acquired suffix
bits, and make exactly L_j-1 comparisons on correct histories. Bad histories
have a hard 2^r-1 per raw-label cap and return UNKNOWN on wrong cardinality.
Thus Qmax as stated uses expected-cardinality checks before querying; if a
wrong cardinality is seen, stop immediately, preserving the stated L_j cap.

Store all raw quadratic bodies while learning. After validation, change basis
to V=(v_1,...,v_h,W), with residual coordinates z in F_2^r. For i<=h,
q_i(Vt)=t_i+H_i(t_1,...,t_(i-1),z), deg H_i<=2: later accepted toggles preserve
q_i and its own toggle has constant derivative one. This compresses the
flagged coefficient payload to
  Dprefix=h+binom(n,2)-binom(r,2)+binom(n,3)-binom(r,3),
plus rM suffix coefficients and n^2 basis bits. This is a source-free storage
optimization; it does not erase the M corrected candidate labels already
acquired per attempted direction. Basis conversion, stabilizer linear algebra,
output and sampler execution are charged Boolean work.

Source-free inverse: project/enumerate the requested h-prefix fiber's 2^r
points and select the unique point whose learned full rank matches. Prefix
sampling through h uses exact projection; longer prefixes filter that fiber
and uniformly choose from its exactly power-of-two subset. Each operation's
2^r arithmetic/enumeration cost remains charged.

Strong same-input controls: classical RM decoding on complete raw cubes;
the noiseless partial-flag algorithm with repeated persistent pair voting
(cannot repair corruption); the complete triangular robust square-tournament
compiler ONLY when its stronger full-triangular promise holds; this identical
matching/decoder reduction implemented by another informed algorithm; and
the direct robust median control being checked in MEDIAN_CONTROL.txt. No
claim of superiority over every method, optimal query counts, novelty,
recognized class membership, physical application, or mission achievement.

6. Sharp noise radius, scope and falsifiers.
The family includes complete degree-two triangular maps whose last-bit
monomial flip changes N/8 disjoint adjacent ideal comparisons. An observed
total order swaps half of these pairs. One ideal is explained by marking one
endpoint of the swapped pairs; the other by one endpoint of the unswapped
pairs. Each budget is N/16 and the ideal full decoders differ. Thus no uniform
full decoder learner can pass epsilon=1/16 in this class. Our result attains
every fixed strict subthreshold gap with polynomial n cost at fixed r.
For requested prefixes with special structure, no separate sharpness claim.
The scientific transfer remains unacquired: a real chosen-pair source with
certified quadratic bijection, partial flag and item-corruption promises.
