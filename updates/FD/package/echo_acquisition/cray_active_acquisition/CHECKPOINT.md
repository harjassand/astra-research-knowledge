## What not to claim

No physical thermodynamic decision, no experimentally demonstrated control-error
allowance, no historical novelty, no generic compact successful map, no quantum
advantage over strong Hamiltonian learning. The positive extension is deliberately
constructed; all 40 generic modification controls failed the chosen local search.
The favorable null count record is 100 million synthetic conditional-probability
trials and requires gamma about 1e-8 for its exact presented sign certificate.
A separate, stricter simulation propagating an actual unreset memory state gives
7 failures and an inconclusive interval even with ideal apparatus at 100 million
trials. Do not present the favorable record as a robust runtime demonstration. Do not quietly drop this cost.

## Useful research directions, not completed obligations

An important next test is a genuinely independently specified device with a
physically meaningful near-intertwiner and realistic uniform control calibration.
A second is an acquisition bound exploiting verified residual structure or a
noise model without importing an equilibrium oracle. Neither was established here.

Do not simply enlarge the collar: general local matrix/circuit costs are
exponential and success remains unpromised. Do not report a few observed matching
transitions as evidence of full spectral matching; the singular-map example
explicitly defeats that inference.
