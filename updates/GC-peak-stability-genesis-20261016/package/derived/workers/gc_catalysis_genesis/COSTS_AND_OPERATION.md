# Obtainable operation, decisive falsifiers, and cost contract

Status: proposed operation only. No reactor was operated and no causal-response dataset was acquired. Physical control: NOT_ACQUIRED.

## Minimum operation for the limited claim

Use the existing mixed-feed productive-reactor configuration. Randomize a scheduled write versus a duration-matched sham after the same recorded preparation policy. Admit enriched read carbon only after the write, at the same fixed guard-delay/admission clock in both arms. Acquire the common-read exposure criterion through calibration; retain failures and penalize them conservatively rather than excluding them after treatment. The first packet should tag both acetylene and ethylene with the same fresh-carbon tag to distinguish newly processed carbon from old release; source-specific attribution requires a later reciprocal-label experiment.

Collect absolute species amounts through a common read/chase, or supply a conservative bound on all unrecovered fresh carbon. Record fresh acetylene removal and net fresh ethylene gain as well as ethane. Calibration must include isotope backgrounds, admission, all relevant species, species-specific retention, side products, C-C scrambling if present, detector gains and line reactions. An inert tracer alone does not bound reactive product retention.

The history-dependent sequential-randomization formulation in PROOFS section 5 identifies a finite excursion effect without claiming a globally restored state. This relaxes a known-state requirement only by changing the estimand. A claim specifically about a restored static equilibrium, an unmodified periodic equilibrium, or complete memory lifetime still needs the corresponding acquired history/state/horizon assumptions.

The minimum chemical detector need not resolve a 0.1 s pulse. Integrated fresh-cohort recovery can carry the causal information. The electrical and admission system must still resolve and bound the actual experimental inputs. The common-read criterion must concern the reacting specimen sufficiently to bound the null, not merely a terminal display.

## Decisive falsifiers

1. If calibrated equal-duty data retain decreasing b with period, reject N887's shared fixed-rate scalar family and every positive parallel mixture of that response form. Do not refit different rates for each waveform and call it the same mechanism.
2. If actual short-pulse exposures are appreciably different, repeat after acquired matching or calculate a conservative exposure-null envelope. Disappearance of the trend then supports an exposure explanation, with scope limited to the matched regime.
3. If full fresh-cohort recovery eliminates the apparent deficit, reject subsequent-fresh-turnover control at the tested delay/horizon. A missing-carbon allowance large enough to absorb the deficit leaves the result inconclusive.
4. If a direct cohort effect appears only in a later delay window, reject an immediately written single-exponential inhibitor when admission/transport response is bounded. If it is immediately present and decays monotonically, the specific precursor-to-inhibitor example is disfavored, although other sequential mechanisms can survive.
5. If a full measured static response lower bound over the actual exposure domain exceeds the periodic mean, reject a memoryless static-law-plus-waveform-filter explanation. Nine isolated static points do not supply that lower bound.
6. If a positive fresh ethane deficit occurs without positive useful ethylene benefit and required acetylene removal, the useful purification-control claim fails even though subsequent chemistry changed.
7. If the measured full preparation/read/reset work and output specification lose to a matched continuous baseline, stop the process-advantage claim. A causal chemical effect can remain scientifically informative.

## Closest acquired predecessor and novelty stop

Lim, Hülsey and Yan (JACS Au 2021, DOI 10.1021/jacsau.1c00044) already used optimized potential pulses, offered a surface-sweeping interpretation, correlated promotion with double-layer capacitance, and executed dynamic-to-static/OCP withdrawal with repeated recovery of the static conversion. Their reported response matched the apparatus residence time. Their liquid-electrolyte ethylene-only system is distinct from the mixed-feed PEM apparatus here. The generic preparation, sweeping and withdrawal ideas are therefore prior art; their numerical response times do not transfer to this reactor.

Randomized fresh-cohort recovery, exposure penalties, and the history-dependent estimand are an analytical acquisition design here, not a historically cleared invention. No breakthrough or acquired physical service is claimed.
