# Prior entropy comparisons for the four selected findings

## External entropy precedents and the exact data boundary

Inferring positive hidden entropy despite time-symmetric observations is established prior work. Skinner and Dunkel's optimization uses rates of successive observed transitions I->J->K and permits hidden realizations; their switching example has completely time-symmetric observed paths and a positive dissipation estimate. Those transition-count constraints are different from exact two-point kernels at fixed lags tau and 2tau. This comparison does not identify their estimator with H1/H2 or establish novelty for the latter. [Skinner and Dunkel, PNAS 118 (2021), e2024300118, especially pp. 2–4](https://math.mit.edu/~dunkel/Papers/2021SkDu_PNAS.pdf).

Bauer, Seifert and van der Meer consider same-state generator reconstruction and thermodynamic inference from stroboscopic data. Their finite enumeration in Section 3.3 explicitly assumes nondegenerate sampled eigenvalues, and their comparison sets the estimator to zero in the excluded degenerate cases. The Pro example instead has a repeated positive eigenvalue and optimizes a continuous family of logarithms. This is a specific difference of coverage, not a priority certificate for the exact Pro optimization. [Stroboscopic measurements in Markov networks, arXiv:2412.15642v2, Sections 3.3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.15642v2).

The entropy-to-skew inequality is also prior machinery. Kolchinsky, Ohga and Ito relate entropy production to the antisymmetric part of a stationary similarity transform, including the elementary quadratic bound used in the source notes. The reviewed observation-only branch floor and trace/current-cycle conversion must be assessed as a particular combination and refinement, rather than a discovery of entropy/skew control. [A thermodynamic bound on spectral perturbations, arXiv:2304.01714v4, Appendix C.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.01714v4).

Internally, N512/LA235 already contains the exact path-affinity linear transfer reused by H2. The scoped source comparison and payload hashes are in [the hidden-state review](../hidden_proof/Report.md) and its exact existing-repository proof-range references. That reuse is stronger and more direct than a broad thematic literature resemblance. The current review checks the reused lemma but leaves unrelated N512 claims and their historical authority unchanged.

These are bounded primary-source comparisons for the selected entropy findings. They do not exhaust inverse entropy-production, operator-compression, or quantum-information literature. No authors were contacted, and private mathematics was not published.
