FROZEN FIRST DERIVATION — H04 COUNTING
Date: 2026-10-10 (Australia/Brisbane)
Status: internal derivation, before primary-source comparison.

Target idea. For the hard-core model on a supplied bipartite graph G=(L,R,E), replace configuration-wise local updates by the positive marginal obtained after analytically summing all occupations on R. This is an exact representation that may expose phase mass without enumerating all independent sets.

For S subset L, let N(S) subset R be its neighborhood. Given I intersect L=S, the allowed right-side occupied vertices are precisely subsets T subset R\\N(S), independently. At activity lambda>=0,

  Z_G(lambda) = sum_{S subset L} lambda^{|S|}(1+lambda)^{|R\\N(S)|}.                 (1)

The joint law can be sampled by first drawing S with mass proportional to the summand in (1), then including each vertex of R\\N(S) independently with probability lambda/(1+lambda). This representation has nonnegative weights and avoids summing over right-side configurations explicitly. Its direct evaluation cost is O(2^{|L|} (|V|+|E|)); graph acquisition costs O(|V|+|E|), and the representation itself is not a polynomial-time algorithm unless the sum compresses.

Discriminating hard family: G=K_{n,n}, lambda=1. The only possible right choices are all subsets of R when S=empty, and T=empty whenever S is nonempty. Therefore (1) gives

  Z_{K_{n,n}}(1) = 2^n + sum_{nonempty S subset L} 1 = 2^{n+1}-1.

The original single-vertex heat-bath chain has a phase bottleneck: the left-only phase and right-only phase each have mass about 1/2, while every path between them must pass through the empty independent set, whose stationary mass is 1/(2^{n+1}-1). Thus its conductance is exponentially small (up to a polynomial n factor), so local mixing is exponentially slow.

The positive marginal (1) does NOT remove the bottleneck by itself. Under its marginal on S, the empty subset has weight 2^n and all nonempty subsets collectively have weight 2^n-1. For a single-site heat-bath chain on S, conditional on all other left bits being empty, adding a specified left vertex has probability 1/(2^n+1). Hence the cut separating S=empty from S!=empty still has exponentially small flow. The representation makes the two phases visible algebraically, but an algorithm must additionally discover/use a phase label or a compressed neighborhood-union structure. On K_{n,n} a hand-supplied phase label solves this instance, but that label is not a general construction.

Initial falsifier / limitation. The exact positive decomposition is exponential in |L| and its most obvious induced chain inherits the metastability. It does not establish any improvement beyond correlation decay or any scalable phase decomposition. The central open subproblem is to construct, from G alone and at polynomial cost, a positive phase-resolved representation with controllable overlap and efficient conditional samplers on a class that includes nontrivial hard instances.
