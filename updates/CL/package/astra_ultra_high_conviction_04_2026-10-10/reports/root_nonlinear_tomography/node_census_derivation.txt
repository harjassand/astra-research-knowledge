GENERAL CONSEQUENCE
Consider xdot=-Kx-alpha*x^3+e1*u, y=x1, x(0)=0. Here alpha is an unknown
common positive scalar; K is symmetric positive definite with nonpositive
offdiagonal entries and connected offdiagonal graph. Physical N is unknown.

Let W be the smallest K-invariant, ternary-product-closed subspace containing
e1. The canonical realization proof in s43_proof/canonical_reduction.txt
recovers its minimal reduced model exactly from a finite adaptive IO jet,
even without a supplied upper bound on N.

Since K is an irreducible M-matrix, K^-1 e1 is strictly positive. One proof:
choose s>lambda_max(K). A=sI-K is nonnegative and irreducible, rho(A)<s,
and K^-1=(1/s)sum_(j>=0)(A/s)^j has a positive first column. K-invariance
of finite-dimensional W, and invertibility of K, imply K^-1 e1 belongs to W.

The weighted ternary-subspace classification therefore has no inactive
coordinates and no mixed-sign block. With homogeneous alpha its canonical
orthonormal block vector is 1_B/sqrt(|B|). The reduced cubic coefficient is
alpha_bar_B=alpha/|B|. The port is a singleton, so alpha is itself observable:
alpha=-G^3 Fh(0)/6. Hence
  |B|=alpha/alpha_bar_B,
  N=sum_B alpha/alpha_bar_B.
The ratios are integers under the exact model assumptions. This identifies
physical size even when the within-block linear interactions are invisible.
It does not identify those interactions, individual members, or their graph.
Heterogeneous unknown alpha permits harmonic-weight refinements and destroys
this census. Signed couplings can allow inactive coordinates even in a
connected graph, so ordinary connected SPD is not a sufficient replacement.

EIGHTH-ORDER EXPLICIT PROTOCOL FOR A SYNCHRONIZED FAMILY
Let there be m hidden nodes. Choose a,d,c,alpha>0 with ad>c^2 and a hidden
weighted Laplacian L. Define
  K=[[a, -(c/sqrt(m))*1^T],[-(c/sqrt(m))*1,dI+L]].
This is SPD and an irreducible M-matrix. Starting at zero, every hidden
coordinate equals z/sqrt(m) and the exact observed model is
  ydot=-a*y+c*z-alpha*y^3+u,
  zdot=c*y-d*z-(alpha/m)*z^3.
All hidden Laplacians yield the same IO. For every m the linear IO model
has dimension two and the same transfer when a,d,c are held fixed.

For a constant input u=U, write y(t,U)=sum y_(j,p)t^j U^p. Then
  a=-2 y_(2,1),
  c^2=6 y_(3,1)-a^2,
  d=(-24 y_(4,1)-a^3-2ac^2)/c^2,
  alpha=-4 y_(4,3).
The hidden sign is a gauge, and c>0 fixes it. Compute, from these recovered
parameters, the reference cubic coefficient yref_(8,3) for the same reduced
ODE with its hidden cubic coefficient set to zero. This is a calculation,
not an extra supplied physical measurement.

Put beta=alpha/m. Since z_lin=c U t^2/2+O(t^3), the hidden cubic changes
zdot first by -beta*c^3*U^3*t^6/8, z by -beta*c^3*U^3*t^7/56, and y by
-beta*c^4*U^3*t^8/448. Consequently
  D=y_(8,3)-yref_(8,3)=-beta*c^4/448,
  m=-alpha*c^4/(448D), N=m+1.
The formal bivariate Taylor calculation is implemented using exact rational
arithmetic in node_census.py. Cases m=1,2,3,7,64,1000 pass. It checks this
family and extraction formula, not the entire canonical realization proof.
