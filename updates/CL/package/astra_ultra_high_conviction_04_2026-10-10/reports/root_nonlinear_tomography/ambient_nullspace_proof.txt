ROOT: EXACT AMBIENT KERNEL AND THE SURVIVING TARGET FUNCTIONAL
2026-10-10. Algebraic completion of the earlier finite-field rank test.
Internal proof, not independently externally validated or priority-cleared.

Fix distinct real lambda_1,...,lambda_n and nonzero modal overlaps a_i.
For a symmetric order-four tensor T define the first-order response map
  R_T(s1,s2,s3)=T(v(s1+s2+s3),v(s1),v(s2),v(s3)),
where v_i(z)=a_i/(z+lambda_i). Real or complex nonpole frequencies suffice;
equality on any open set means equality of rational functions.

Put D(z)=product_i(z+lambda_i) and b_i(z)=a_i D(z)/(z+lambda_i).
The b_i form a basis of polynomials of degree at most n-1: evaluation at
z=-lambda_j gives a nonzero diagonal basis matrix. Multiplying the four
denominators identifies symmetric T bijectively with the space of symmetric
four-variable polynomials
  P_T(z0,z1,z2,z3)=T(b(z0),b(z1),b(z2),b(z3))
having degree at most n-1 in EACH variable. This space has dimension
binom(n+3,4); symmetrized basis monomials indexed by multisets give this count.

R_T=0 iff P_T vanishes on z0=z1+z2+z3. Polynomial division makes
L0=z0-z1-z2-z3 a factor. Symmetry makes each
  Lj=2zj-(z0+z1+z2+z3), j=0,1,2,3,
a factor. These four linear polynomials are pairwise nonassociate prime
elements of the polynomial ring; therefore their product Delta divides P_T.
Delta is symmetric and has degree exactly four in each variable. The quotient
Q=P_T/Delta is symmetric and has degree at most n-5 in each variable, since
degrees in one variable add over the integral-domain coefficient ring in the
other three variables. Conversely every such Delta*Q vanishes on the required
hyperplane and has the required separate degree bounds.

Thus for n<=4 the ambient map is injective. For n>=5 its kernel is precisely
  {P_T=Delta*Q : Q symmetric, degree_zj Q<=n-5 for every j},
and its dimension is binom(n-1,4). Hence the exact characteristic-zero rank is
  binom(n+3,4)-binom(n-1,4),
with the second term zero for n<=4. The previously executed ranks
5,15,35,69,121 for n=2,...,6 agree with this proof. Those finite-field tests
alone did not establish the formula; divisibility is the full argument.

WHY THE PHYSICAL CONTRACTION SURVIVES
The physically admissible weighted quartic has form
  T=alpha_1 a^(tensor4)+S,
where S annihilates a in every slot (hidden physical rows are orthogonal to
the measured row a). Let w=Lambda*a. With c=(s-t)/2,
  v(c+R)=a/R-(Lambda+cI)a/R^2+O(R^-3),
  v(c-R)=-a/R-(Lambda+cI)a/R^2+O(R^-3).
Every term containing a is killed by S. Therefore
  lim_(R->infinity) R^4 S(v(s),v(c+R),v(c-R),v(t))
      =S(v(s),w,w,v(t)).
If such a hidden tensor S is in the response-map kernel, this bilinear form
vanishes for all s,t. The vectors v(s) span R^n by the same Cauchy-basis
argument, so S(.,w,w,.) is the zero matrix. Thus this contraction is an
observable linear functional despite nonidentifiability of the ambient T.

For the physical weighted orthogonal decomposition it is exactly
  U^T diag(0,alpha_2 K_21^2,...,alpha_n K_n1^2) U.
This is the matrix recovered by s41. The proof explains mathematically why
the failed full-tensor representation does not refute coordinate recovery.
It does not supply a stable physical measurement of the surviving functional;
that distinct gate is treated in the s41 and s45 reports.
