FIRST DERIVATION FROZEN BEFORE LITERATURE SEARCH — 2026-10-10

The all-order construction in NETWORK_GATE.txt is algebraically correct on
its stated generic first-column locus. It yields structural identifiability
and does not yet yield a finite-noise end-to-end acquisition capability.

EXPLICIT NONTRIVIAL EXACT LINEAR AMBIGUITY AND UNIFORM SMALL NONLINEAR SIGNAL.
Take n=3, a=4, b=(1,2)^T, B=diag(2,3),
 K_e=[[4,e,2e],[e,2,0],[2e,0,3]], 0<e<=1/sqrt(5).
Let R=[[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]], Q=diag(1,R), K'_e=Q^T K_e Q:
 K'_e=[[4,11e/5,2e/5],[11e/5,66/25,12/25],
       [2e/5,12/25,59/25]].
Both are SPD with smallest eigenvalue at least 1. They have identical linear
single-port transfer functions, simple eigenvalues, and nonzero port overlap
with every eigenvector. Their first-column entries are nonzero with distinct
squares when e is small. They are not related by a signed permutation fixing
the port: even their hidden diagonal multisets differ, by an amount independent
of e. Thus this pair lies in the exact generic-reconstruction locus.

With local cubic alpha>0 and arbitrary measurable input |u|<=M from zero,
their nonlinear outputs satisfy, uniformly over all time,
 sup_t |y_e(t)-y'_e(t)| <= (25/16)*alpha*e^4*M^3.

Proof: Transform the primed solution by Q, making both linear drifts K_e.
The two hidden cubic maps are C(v)=v^{componentwise 3} and
R C(R^T v). Both are monotone, and their norm is <=||v||^3. With lambda_min
K>=mu, c=lambda_min B, both full solution norms are <=M/mu and hidden norms
are <=e||b||M/(c mu). Difference dynamics has monotone common drift and a
hidden forcing defect of norm <=2alpha*(e||b||M/(c mu))^3, so full difference
norm is <=2alpha*e^3||b||^3*M^3/(c^3*mu^4). The port's cubic remains the
same and monotone; its forcing is e b^T times the hidden difference. Therefore
the port difference is bounded by 2alpha*e^4||b||^4*M^3/(a c^3 mu^4).
Substitute a=4,c=2,mu=1,||b||^2=5 to obtain 25/16.

Consequences: arbitrary bounded deterministic measurement error >=half this
bound admits indistinguishable observations for the two fixed separated
matrices, for every bounded-amplitude experiment and duration. With independent
Gaussian output noise variance sigma^2, N measurements have KL at most
N*(25alpha e^4 M^3/16)^2/(2sigma^2), so constant-success discrimination needs
N=Omega(sigma^2*e^-8*M^-6/alpha^2). This is a genuine resource obstruction,
not an exact nonlinear indistinguishability counterexample. If e is allowed
exponentially small in n, constant-noise sample demand is exponential. Sensor
precision bits alone scale logarithmically in 1/e; do not mislabel this as an
unconditional exponential bit-complexity lower bound.

EXACT NONLINEAR COLLISION OUTSIDE LINEAR CYCLICITY (SCOPE LIMITATION).
For n=3 let K_d=[[a,b,b],[b,c+d,-d],[b,-d,c+d]] with a>0,c>0,
ac>2b^2 and d>0. The invariant subspace x2=x3 is preserved by the local cubic
and by input at node1. Zero initial state thus gives identical IO for every d,
although changing d changes the antisymmetric eigenvalue c+2d. All networks
are connected when b!=0 and d!=0, SPD, and generically non-gauge-equivalent.
This collision has an exactly unobservable antisymmetric linear eigenmode,
so it does not refute a theorem restricted to nonzero port overlaps.

FORMAL JET CHECK.
[g,f]=-Ke1-3alpha*h^2*g, so k=-[g,f]-3alpha*h^2*g is constant.
For z=c dot x, L_k^2 L_f z=-6alpha sum_i c_i*k_i^2*x_i, so the stated
z_m=sum_i k_i^(2m+1)*x_i and L_k z_m=sum_i k_i^(2m+2) are correct.
Recovering n distinct nonzero k_i^2 from power sums and inverting a Vandermonde
is mathematically valid with exact data. It requires high-order IO derivatives,
including quantities whose very first cubic signal can scale e^4 as above.
No first-principles argument found here forces the Lie-bracket derivation to
use physically negative drift time: one-sided multitime IO jets can encode
the words. The acquisition/conditioning gap is real; a claim of impossibility
from unavailable reverse drift would be unjustified without more work.

Current provisional verdict: generic structural identifiability survives.
Broad reliable large-network recovery without quantitative observability and
noise assumptions fails. An 8+/10 new capability is not established by these
formulas alone. Need historical comparison and a matched finite-resource test.
