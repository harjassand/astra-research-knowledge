FIRST DERIVATION — frozen before source comparison, 2026-10-10

Independent audit target: does the oscillator third-order kernel route reduce
hidden-state reconstruction to finite, physically accessible one-port input /
output experiments, or does it still require an unobservable state quantity?

Model assumed for this derivation:
  qddot + gamma qdot + K q + diag(alpha) q^{odot 3} = e1 u,
  y=e1^T q,
where K is real symmetric positive definite, gamma>0 is known/calibrated,
alpha_i>0 are known or unknown constants, and only u(t), y(t) are controlled/
recorded. Its linear transfer vector is
  q(s)=(s^2+gamma*s+K)^(-1)e1.
The cubic Volterra coefficient (up to the declared kernel convention) is
  H3(s1,s2,s3)=-sum_r alpha_r q_r(s1+s2+s3)q_r(s1)q_r(s2)q_r(s3).

For chosen low complex frequencies s,t let c=(s-t)/2 and use input frequencies
R+c, -R+c, t; the output frequency is s. Put F_R=-H3. For an oscillator,
q_1(z)=z^-2+O(z^-3), while for r>1, q_r(z)=-K_{r1}z^-4+O(z^-5).
The product in opposite high-frequency pairs is even in R. Thus, writing
p(R)=q_1(R+c)q_1(-R+c)=R^-4+p6 R^-6+p8 R^-8+..., one gets
  F_R = C(s,t) R^-4 + C(s,t)p6 R^-6
        + [C(s,t)p8+B(s,t)]R^-8 + O(R^-10),
  C(s,t)=alpha_1 q_1(s)q_1(t),
  B(s,t)=sum_{r>1} alpha_r K_{r1}^2 q_r(s)q_r(t).
Therefore c4=lim R^4 F_R=C, c8=lim R^8(F_R-c4 R^-4-c6 R^-6), and
  B(s,t)=c8-p8*c4.
No hidden coordinate is read out: c4,c8 are functionals of the measured
one-port H3 and p8 is computed from H1. With K=U Lambda U^T and
a=U^T e1 (sign fixed by a_i>0), this yields B(s,t)=v(s)^T A v(t),
v_i(s)=a_i/(s^2+gamma*s+lambda_i),
A=U^T diag(0,alpha_2 K_21^2,...,alpha_n K_n1^2) U.
If H1 reveals all distinct modes and nonzero a_i, and the hidden beta_i are
positive and pairwise distinct, n-by-n bilinear samples of B recover A and its
eigenvectors recover U up to signed row permutations. This is an exact generic
identifiability mechanism, contingent on exact H3 acquisition and inverse
conditioning.

Finite physical acquisition: use three real tones at positive frequencies
Omega+omega_c, Omega-omega_c, and omega_t, with independently controlled
phases. The target output frequency is omega_s=2*omega_c+omega_t and its
target cubic monomial uses the + first tone, the negative-frequency component
of the second, and the + third tone. For generic non-colliding frequencies,
lock-in at omega_s and the phase signature phi_1-phi_2+phi_3 isolates that
third-order coefficient. A 7-phase grid per tone is a simple finite cycle
that separates all cubic phase exponents in [-3,3]; small-amplitude scaling or
amplitude extrapolation is required if higher odd orders are present. The
complex frequencies in the formula are realized by s=i*omega_s, t=i*omega_t,
c=i*omega_c, R=i*Omega. Every observation is therefore a one-port forced
response; no state access is required.

The target H3 is not a free oracle: its coefficient is order rho^3 in drive
amplitude and the hidden part is smaller than the port term by order Omega^-4.
Finite R recovery must use the rational structure from H1. For each fixed
(s,t), F_R has common denominator D(R+c)D(-R+c), where
D(z)=det(z^2+gamma*z+K) is known from H1 and has degree 2n. The product is an
even degree-4n polynomial in R, equivalently degree 2n in R^2. Its numerator
has degree at most 4n-4 (port response begins at R^-4). Thus 4n-3 generic
non-pole complex samples suffice for exact coefficient interpolation, or
roughly 2n in the even variable if parity is used. Repeating for O(n^2) pairs
(s_j,t_k) yields O(n^3) raw cubic frequency conditions and no infinite-frequency
probe. This count assumes a known model order, exact modal poles from H1, and
calibrated gamma; it excludes phase-cycle runs and repeats needed for noise.

The remaining physical gate is quantitative stability: the modal inverse at
s_j=i*sqrt(lambda_j) can be bounded by explicit port-overlap and weak-damping
conditions, but the map from finite noisy H3 samples to c8 and then B may be
ill-conditioned. Because the desired hidden coefficient is R^-8 while the
port contribution is R^-4, raw relative precision must improve as roughly
Omega^-4 in an asymptotic implementation. Exact identifiability alone is not
a resource-bounded practical reconstruction claim.
