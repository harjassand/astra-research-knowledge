RESULT
The all-dimension dense-port argument is mathematically sound after its
information interface is made explicit. A binary, ordered IO word jet of
depth 10n-6 suffices in the homogeneous known-alpha model, under nonzero
and distinct squared entries of Ke1. Ordinary derivatives from one fixed
input are not that information interface.

There is a stronger internally derived classification that handles sparse
and linearly noncyclic networks: full recovery is possible exactly when the
smallest K-invariant, alpha-weighted ternary-closed subspace containing e1
is all of R^n. The proof supplies a finite IO-jet algorithm. Its conservative
general jet bound is prohibitively large, and no uniform noisy recovery is
possible over the unrestricted parameter class even for n=2.

Root-suggested consequences also close internally: the IO map has a
canonical minimal cubic realization, discoverable without a supplied
physical dimension; all equivalent physical networks admit an exact
weighted signed-block parameterization. Under the additional homogeneous
cubic and irreducible SPD M-matrix promises, the canonical cubic
coefficients determine every block size and therefore the PHYSICAL NODE
COUNT. This census remains arbitrarily ill-conditioned under weak coupling.

1. EXACT MODEL, QUANTIFIERS, AND GAUGE

Fix a known integer n>=1. The physical system starts at x=0 and obeys
  xdot=-Kx-alpha*(componentwise x^3)+e1 u, y=x1,
where K is a real symmetric positive-definite n-by-n matrix. Initially
consider alpha_i=alpha>0 supplied exactly. A verified extension below
allows arbitrary unknown alpha_i>0 while retaining the exact cubic model.
The input amplitude and output scale/sign are known; no hidden state is
prepared, actuated, or measured.

A hidden signed permutation S with Se1=e1 sends K to SKS^T and permutes
alpha, while preserving every zero-initial IO response. Recovery is modulo
precisely this unavoidable gauge. It does not identify named hidden nodes.
Known n is material: an arbitrary unobserved disconnected block can always
be added if larger competing state dimensions are permitted.
The later canonical-reduction/census addenda distinguish recovery of
minimal dimension from physical node count, and give the extra promises
under which physical node count becomes identifiable.

The statements compare the complete zero-initial IO germs, or the stated
finite word jets, among ALL models with this known n and exact structural
form. They are global uniqueness statements within this model class,
not just full-rank Jacobian/local-parameter statements.

2. WHAT THE IO JET ACTUALLY CONTAINS

Use [u,v]=Dv*u-Du*v, so [L_u,L_v]=L_[u,v]. Put F=L_f and G=L_e1.
For a word w in the binary alphabet F,G define c_w=(L_w h)(0).
The finite ordered IO jet J_D consists of c_w for |w|<=D.

These are genuinely determined by one-port forward experiments in the
ideal derivative interface. For a=(a1,...,ar) with ai in {0,1}, define
the endpoint map for consecutive constant input pulses
  Y_a(t)=h(phi_(f+a_r g)^(t_r) o ... o phi_(f+a1 g)^(t_1)(0)).
Differentiating once in each separate pulse duration at t=0 gives
  partial_(t1)...partial_(tr)Y_a(0)
       =[(F+a1 G)...(F+ar G)h](0).
For target G positions S and F elsewhere, Boolean finite differences
over ai in {0,1} at i in S, with ai=0 elsewhere, extract its individual
ordered word coefficient. All pulse durations may approach zero from
above. Inverse drift, negative-time experiments, and physical bracket
synthesis are unnecessary.

In contrast, y^(r)(0) under a single constant input a gives only
[(F+aG)^r h](0), a sum of ordered coefficients. Substituting those
symmetrized numbers for the word jet is an acquisition gap.

The observational algebra gives an explicit finite-order justification.
Let A_d be the polynomial algebra generated by the functions L_w h(x)
with |w|<=d. F and G map A_d into A_(d+1); multiplication uses the
maximum depth of its factors. Products, sums, commutators and Leibniz
expansions consequently evaluate at zero from finite J_D. Coefficients
obtained from earlier exact IO numbers are treated as fixed constants.
The proof never substitutes a field's value at zero for its entire germ.

3. HOMOGENEOUS DENSE-PORT THEOREM AND PROOF

Let k=Ke1. Suppose all k_i!=0 and all k_i^2 are distinct. This condition
is open and dense in the unrestricted SPD space, but excludes most
sparse port columns and some exactly unidentifiable connected networks.

Because [g,f]=-k-3alpha h^2 g, the exact constant-field operator is
  A=L_k=-[G,F]-3alpha h^2 G.
The state-dependent multiplication term is essential. Symmetry gives
  z0=-Fh-alpha h^3=k dot x.
Define
  z_(m+1)=-A^2 F z_m/(6alpha).
Two differentiations of the cubic along constant k prove inductively
  z_m=sum_i k_i^(2m+1)x_i,
  p_(m+1)=A z_m(0)=sum_i (k_i^2)^(m+1).

Newton's identities recover the elementary symmetric functions e_l of
t_i=k_i^2 from p1,...,pn:
  e0=1; l e_l=sum_(j=1)^l (-1)^(j-1)e_(l-j)p_j.
The roots of t^n-e1 t^(n-1)+...+(-1)^n e_n are the distinct positive
t_i. K11=-GFh(0)>0 singles out t_1; choose k1=K11 and arbitrary hidden
signs for the other square roots.

To recover the ENTIRE matrix rather than only Kk, construct constant
fields
  v0=k; v_(m+1)=-[k,[k,[v_m,f]]]/(6alpha).
They satisfy v_m,i=k_i^(2m+1). Then at zero
  B_mr=-L_(v_r) F z_m(0)=v_m^T K v_r.
With Q_mi=k_i^(2m+1), 0<=m<n, B=QKQ^T and
  det Q=(product_i k_i) product_(i<j)(k_j^2-k_i^2)!=0.
Thus K=Q^(-1)B Q^(-T). This step closes the prepared-state gap: it
uses scalar IO operators at zero, with no spanning set of hidden states.
Only measuring -L_k Fz_m would recover Kk, not all of K.

For derivative accounting, A shifts observational depth by at most 2;
z_m belongs to A_(5m+1); L_(v_m) shifts it by at most 5m+2. Hence
  p_(m+1) belongs to A_(5m+3),
  B_mr belongs to A_(5m+5r+4).
All needed quantities lie in J_(10n-6). This is a sufficient upper
bound, not an optimality claim. It also holds for n=1, where order 2
already identifies K11.

4. UNKNOWN HETEROGENEOUS CUBIC COEFFICIENTS

The assumption of supplied identical cubic coefficients can be relaxed.
Let all alpha_i>0 be unknown and define
  alpha1=-G^3 Fh(0)/6, k=-[g,f]-3alpha1 h^2g,
  z0=-Fh-alpha1 h^3=k dot x.
For beta_i=alpha_i k_i^2, set
  z_(m+1)=-L_k^2 Fz_m/6,
  v_(m+1)=-[k,[k,[v_m,f]]]/6, v0=k.
Then z_m=sum_i k_i beta_i^m x_i, v_m,i=k_i beta_i^m and
  p_m=L_k z_m(0)=sum_i w_i beta_i^m, w_i=k_i^2.

If all k_i!=0 and beta_i are distinct, p0,...,p_(2n-1) identify the
positive n-atomic measure. H=(p_(i+j))_(i,j<n) is positive definite.
Solve H c=-(p_n,...,p_(2n-1))^T; the roots of
t^n+sum_(j<n)c_j t^j are beta_i. Vandermonde inversion gives w_i>0.
Now k_i=+/-sqrt(w_i), alpha_i=beta_i/w_i. The port is the unique atom
beta1=alpha1 K11^2. Q_mi=k_i beta_i^m and the same B formula recover K.
The moment jet order is at most 10n-2 and dominates the matrix order
10n-6. Thus J_(10n-2) suffices for this extension.

This removes numerical calibration of the coefficients from the supplied
interface. It retains the exact diagonal cubic constitutive law, positivity,
known n, known input/output scales, and model symmetry. Unknown quintic
or cross-node nonlinear terms invalidate the displayed recursions.

5. SPARSE/NONCYCLIC RECOVERY: AN EXACT IFF BOUNDARY

Full proof and detailed bounds are in s43_proof/sparse_closure_derivation.txt.
Let T(u,v,w)=alpha*u*v*w, with componentwise products. Define W as
the smallest linear subspace containing e1 with KW subset W and
T(W,W,W) subset W. Then:

  W=R^n implies finite-jet recovery of K and all alpha_i, modulo gauge.
  W proper implies exact nongauge ambiguity even from complete IO.

The nontrivial constructive ingredient is that, given any paired constant
field v and scalar observable z_v=v dot x, Kv can be constructed without
already knowing the physical coordinates in support(v).

Set beta_i=alpha_i v_i^2 and recursively form paired q_m,z_m as in the
heterogeneous moment argument. Moments p_m=sum_i v_i^2 beta_i^m recover
the polynomial P of DISTINCT POSITIVE beta values. Repeated beta values
aggregate positive weights; coordinates with v_i=0 do not contribute.
Its degree r<=n and P(0)!=0. The linear fields
  D_j=-[v,[q_(j-1),f]]/6=diag(beta_i^j)x, 1<=j<=r,
combined with the polynomial 1-P(t)/P(0), yield exactly
  E=P_support(v) x.
This interpolation uses rational moment operations; no roots are needed.

Let R=-[v,f]=Kv+3alpha*x^2*v. Directly,
  [E,R]=3alpha*x^2*v-P_support(v)Kv,
  C=R-[E,R]=Kv+P_support(v)Kv,
  Kv=C-[C,E]/2.
The scalar partner is
  z_(Kv)=-(I+L_E/6-L_E^2/6) Fz_v.
Here L_E has eigenvalues 0/1 on the linear terms and 3 on the supported
cubic. The polynomial equals 1 at 0,1 and 0 at 3, proving the formula.
Weighted ternary products are also paired:
  T(u,v,w)=-[u,[v,[w,f]]]/6,
  z_T=-L_u L_v Fz_w/6.

Starting from e1,h, form K and ternary candidates and add independent
directions, detected by observable Gram entries L_v z_w(0)=v dot w.
At most n-1 additions occur. If no new direction occurs, the current
span is exactly W. Once it has dimension n, a combination
q(t)=sum_(r=0)^(n-1)t^r v_r has nonzero entries and distinct alpha_i q_i^2
for some t among 0,...,n^2(n-1). There are at most n^2(n-1) bad roots
across the n coordinate polynomials and the two signed equality
polynomials for each pair. Full moment Hankel rank tests select q.
Prony plus Q^(-1)B Q^(-T) then recover the model as above. The port
is anchored by q1=Gz_q(0) and beta1=alpha1 q1^2.

For necessity, if W is proper, every zero-initial trajectory stays in W.
For any epsilon>0, K_epsilon=K+epsilon P_(W perpendicular) is SPD and
agrees with K on W. It has identical IO for EVERY input but different
trace, hence is not signed-permutation equivalent. This is an actual
impossibility witness within the model class.

If e1 is cyclic for K, the K operation alone spans the whole state space,
so sparse cyclic networks are covered. Cyclicity is not necessary:
K=4I+u u^T, u=(1,2,3,4), alpha_i=1, has linear cyclic dimension 2,
but k=(5,2,3,4) has distinct nonzero squares and cubic closure spans 4.

A conservative finite bound is d0=1,
  d_(r+1)=(4n+1)d_r+2n+1,
  J_n=4n d_(n-1)+2n-2,
where d_r=((6n+1)(4n+1)^r-(2n+1))/(4n). This gives J1=4,
J2=114,J3=3208. It proves finite determination and does not claim
that these derivative orders can be acquired usefully.

6. EXACT DEGENERACY AND FATAL UNIFORM-ROBUSTNESS COUNTEREXAMPLES

Connectedness does not ensure identifiability. The SPD matrices
  K=[[4,1,1],[1,3,1],[1,1,3]],
  K'=[[4,1,1],[1,3.5,0.5],[1,0.5,3.5]], alpha_i=1,
both preserve x2=x3 from zero. On that subspace their dynamics are
  ydot=-4y-2z-y^3+u, zdot=-y-4z-z^3.
Every IO response is identical, while their antisymmetric eigenvalues
are respectively 2 and 3. W has dimension 2. All port-column entries
are nonzero, but the two hidden squared entries coincide.

Even the dense generic theorem has no uniform finite-noise guarantee.
For n=2 take
  K_c(epsilon)=[[2,epsilon],[epsilon,c]], c=3 or 4,
  alpha1=alpha2=1, 0<epsilon<=1/2.
Both matrices satisfy the dense theorem's hypotheses. With |u|<=U,
lambda_min(K)>=1 gives ||x(t)||<=U. The hidden state satisfies
|x2(t)|<=epsilon U/3. Monotonicity of y^3 and contraction rate 2 in
the first equation imply, for the same bounded input and every time,
  |y_3(t)-y_4(t)|<=U epsilon^2/3.
The hidden diagonal parameters differ by 1. No fixed observation
tolerance can uniformly distinguish these models as epsilon tends to
zero, even with arbitrary adaptive bounded-input protocols. For N
independent Gaussian readouts of variance sigma^2, the KL bound is
O(N U^2 epsilon^4/sigma^2), requiring
  N=Omega(sigma^2/(U^2 epsilon^4))
for constant-error discrimination. Increasing input scale, averaging,
or duration is an acquisition cost, not a free consequence of uniqueness.

7. RESOURCE INTERFACE AND REMAINING GAP

The required exact word jet is an idealized information resource. A
complete J_D has at most 2^(D+1)-1 binary coefficients. Obtaining it by
all pulse-amplitude patterns and all mixed time derivatives is an
exponential upper-bound procedure. This is not a proved exponential
lower bound: a compressed acquisition scheme might avoid most words.
No such efficient, robust scheme has been supplied by this proof.

For independent raw noise, a naive mixed derivative of r separate pulse
durations, approximated on a step delta cube, has noise variance
sigma^2 2^r delta^(-2r) before averaging. Taylor remainder control also
needs parameter/analyticity bounds. Arbitrarily accurate derivative
values cannot be assigned unit measurement cost.

Newton/Prony and Vandermonde inversion additionally need precision set
by port weights, beta gaps and determinant size. These have no positive
uniform bounds here. Exact Hankel-rank decisions used in sparse closure
are unstable near degeneracy. Formal operator circuits may share work,
but physical queries to their values are not supplied derived oracles.

Repeated trials require resets to the same zero state. Passive relaxation
is asymptotic, and its cost is unbounded without a lower bound on the
stability rate. The forward-pulse lemma avoids drift reversal but does
not make resets, timing, input calibration, read noise, energy, model
error, or arithmetic precision free.

The result supplies structural recovery and an exact impossibility
boundary. A landmark single-port physical tomography capability remains
OPEN until an efficient acquisition protocol is proved and tested in a
specified parameter/noise regime against an equally informed baseline.

8. STRONGEST PRIMARY PREDECESSORS CHECKED AFTER FIRST FREEZE

Albertini and Sontag, For Neural Networks, Function Determines Form,
Neural Networks 6 (1993), 975-990:
  https://sontaglab.org/FTPDIR/net-ident.pdf
This paper already establishes generic IO determination of recurrent-net
weights up to signed permutations, and also considers unknown activations.
Its Sec5.1 uses positive pulse durations to obtain ordered Lie derivatives.
Its conditions distinguish nonzero input-vector entries by absolute value.
Its broad technical model is Dx+sigma(Ax+Bu)+Gu; the general theorem uses
saturating derivative behavior, while its B=0 special result fixes D=aI.
Those hypotheses do not directly cover our arbitrary -K and port e1 cubic
self-damping. The broad mechanism and gauge reduction are nevertheless
historical; only a precise model-specific distinction could support novelty.

Vanelli and Hendrickx, Local Identifiability of Networks with Nonlinear
Node Dynamics, current checked version v4 (31 August 2026):
  https://arxiv.org/html/2412.08472v4
This stronger version covers recurrent digraphs and heterogeneous known
analytic node activations. For almost all analytic nonlinearities through
the origin, it proves generic local weight identifiability with one
excitation per source component and one measurement per sink component
of the graph condensation. A strongly connected graph therefore admits
one excitation and one measurement. It assumes known topology and known
node functions, in a discrete-time activation model. Recurrence,
heterogeneity, and single-port nonlinear identifiability must not be
presented as new by themselves. Its generic local statement does not
directly establish the global exact cubic-model classification,
unknown-dimension canonical reconstruction, or homogeneous M-matrix
census proved here. Those are precise distinctions, not a novelty claim.

Vizuete and Hendrickx, Nonlinear Network Identifiability with Full
Excitations (2024):
  https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07636v1
It assumes all nodes excited, known topology, and ideal IO functions for
nonlinear edge dynamics. Its hypotheses differ materially from a single
physical actuator/sensor here. It explicitly distinguishes ideal
identifiability from potentially impractical information acquisition.

No priority or novelty conclusion is inferred from this limited search.

10. CANONICAL REDUCTION, COMPLETE EQUIVALENCE CLASS, AND NODE CENSUS

Detailed proofs are in s43_proof/canonical_reduction.txt and
s43_proof/physical_equivalence_and_census.txt. These are additions to the
known-n full-recovery statements, with distinct conclusions.

For unknown finite physical N, diagonal scaling by sqrt(alpha_i) turns
the closure W into a real subspace closed under componentwise triple
products. Such a subspace is exactly the span of disjoint signed block
indicators, with possibly inactive coordinates. A generic vector's first
dim(W) odd powers and Vandermonde inversion prove this classification.
Normalizing back gives Euclidean orthonormal vectors
  v_B,i=s_i/(sqrt(alpha_i)*sqrt(Z_B)), Z_B=sum_(i in B)1/alpha_i.
On W the dynamics is exactly a smaller cubic model with
  Kbar=V^T K V, alphabar_B=1/Z_B.
The measured-node block is singleton. The reduced model has full closure.

Positive Hankel flatness supplies adaptive support sizes without N: the
first H_s>0,H_(s+1) singular, using p0,...,p_(2s), certifies exactly s
positive atoms because integral P^2 dmu=0 for every compatible positive
measure. Gram closure then detects r=dim W. The final reconstruction
identifies the canonical minimal r-node cubic model, globally across
different physical dimensions. All equalities require exact real IO
numbers and the exact model promise, not noisy rank tests. A conservative
complete adaptive certificate has binary word depth <=2d_r-1, with
d0=1,d_(j+1)=(4r+1)d_j+2r+1; r=1,2,3 give 15,261,6955.

All equivalent physical models are obtained by choosing active weighted
signed blocks with harmonic sums fixed by alphabar, arbitrary inactive
positive cubic coordinates, and
  K=V Kbar V^T+H_on_(W perpendicular),
where the latter is any SPD self-adjoint operator on W perpendicular,
extended by zero on W. This parameterization is both necessary and
sufficient; hidden residual directions may couple across blocks and
to inactive coordinates. Physical N is generally unidentifiable.

There is a concrete census exception. Suppose alpha_i=alpha>0 for all
physical nodes, K_ij<=0 for i!=j, and the off-diagonal graph is connected.
The Neumann series shows K^-1e1 is strictly positive. It belongs to W,
so every coordinate is active and every signed block is unsigned.
Consequently alphabar_B=alpha/|B|, with alpha inferred at the port, and
  physical N=sum_B alpha/alphabar_B.
Every ratio is a positive integer under the promised class. This counts
physical nodes even when hidden antisymmetric dynamics prevent recovery
of K. Arbitrary signed SPD connected models or heterogeneous coefficients
do not have this census guarantee; explicit collisions are in the addendum.

The census is still a structural theorem. A matched pair with physical
counts 2 and 3 can have identical linear response and, for bounded input
|u|<=U, uniformly separated nonlinear outputs by at most
  U^3 epsilon^4/162,
while both are homogeneous irreducible SPD M-matrices. Independent Gaussian
readout discrimination then needs Omega(sigma^2/(U^6 epsilon^8)) samples
in this weak-coupling family. Thus no uniform finite-noise census capability
follows from exact identifiability. Novelty and practical significance are
still OPEN.
