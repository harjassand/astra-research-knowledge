FROZEN INDEPENDENT FIRST DERIVATION — 2026-10-10, before prior-art search

Question. Can any universal physical law upper-bound how much a trajectory lets an observer infer by the system's thermodynamic entropy production alone?

Candidate answer: no. For a broad, exact family of equilibrium dynamics, entropy production vanishes identically while the path carries positive and arbitrarily large information about its kinetic timescale. The natural quantity controlling local inference is dynamical activity (expected jump count), not dissipation.

Construction. Let X_t be a finite-state continuous-time Markov chain with base rates q_ij for i != j and escape rate lambda_i=sum_{j!=i}q_ij. Scale every rate by e^theta, q_ij(theta)=e^theta q_ij, with initial law mu independent of theta. Observe the full continuous-time path on [0,T], including jump times. Let N_T be its jump count and A_T=integral_0^T lambda_{X_t} dt, the base integrated activity along that path. The path-density ratio is

  log (dP_theta/dP_0)(X_[0,T]) = theta N_T - (e^theta-1) A_T.

The score is U_theta=N_T-e^theta A_T, a counting-process martingale. Its quadratic variation is e^theta A_T, hence the path Fisher information for theta is

  I_path(theta)=E_theta[U_theta^2]=E_theta[N_T].                 (1)

For any statistic Y of the trajectory, its score is E_theta[U_theta | Y], so data processing gives I_Y(theta) <= E_theta[N_T]. An unbiased estimator from any such observation therefore has variance at least 1/E_theta[N_T]. This gives an exact operational inference budget in expected resolved transitions.

Exact counterexample to an entropy-production-only inference law. Choose a finite irreducible detailed-balanced Q with stationary pi and initialize at pi. Multiplying every rate by e^theta leaves pi and detailed balance unchanged, so stationary entropy production is zero for every theta. Nevertheless (1) is positive whenever expected activity is positive. For the two-state symmetric chain with base rate k in both directions, the path is a Poisson jump process of rate k e^theta, so

  I_path(theta)=k T e^theta,
  D(P_theta || P_0)=k T [e^theta(theta-1)+1] > 0 for theta != 0.

The second expression follows by taking expectation of the log path-density ratio at theta. Thus positive KL distinguishability, and by increasing kT arbitrarily large distinguishability, coexist with zero thermodynamic entropy production. The source of the evidence is the timing of reversible transitions, not a thermodynamic arrow of time. Any inference bound based only on entropy production is false without extra restrictions on the observable, detector bandwidth, rate changes, or cost of preparing and reading the clock.

Physical interface and limitation. The observable is the resolved transition count/timing. The result assumes complete continuous-time observation; finite-bandwidth sampling can alias fast rates and erase the information. It charges neither detector cost nor system preparation, so the theorem is only about information in a supplied path, not net information obtainable by an embodied observer. For sampled/coarse observations the data-processing inequality retains the upper bound but the equality need not hold.

Assessment at freeze. The exact calculation is a useful discriminating lemma/counterexample to a broad inference-versus-dissipation principle. It is not yet a high-significance new physical law: the mechanism is standard likelihood calculus for jump processes, and the activity/Fisher connection may be prior art. The foundational target remains OPEN pending a primary-source audit and a stronger consequence beyond this restricted negative result.
