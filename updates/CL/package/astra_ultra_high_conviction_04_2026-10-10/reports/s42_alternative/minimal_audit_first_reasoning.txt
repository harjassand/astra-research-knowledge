FROZEN INDEPENDENT AUDIT FRAMEWORK — before reading the requested S43 files
2026-10-10. This is a targeted final check, not a new research campaign.

1. Candidate canonical state space should be the smallest real linear subspace
   S containing e_1 and invariant under K and the weighted coordinatewise triple
   product B(u,v,w)=diag(alpha_i)*(u*v*w). Mere linear Krylov minimality is too
   restrictive: the frozen fifth-order dark-mode example is linearly noncyclic
   but nonlinearly distinguishable. Conversely any proper K/B-invariant S makes
   dynamics outside S invisible from initial zero.

2. To call S a MINIMAL realization rather than merely a smallest invariant
   linear reduction, one should establish both (i) accessibility on S and
   (ii) observability on S, or give an explicit IO-determined reconstruction of
   a spanning family of linear coordinate functions and their Euclidean Gram
   matrix. General nonlinear realization uniqueness is only up to analytic
   conjugacy, not automatically orthogonal signed permutation.

3. Cross-dimensional uniqueness is plausible if exact IO data determine all
   Gram matrices of the constant directions generated from e_1 by K and B.
   Zero Gram residuals then determine every linear dependency identically in
   both candidate dimensions, and IO data determine the induced K and B on S.
   An isometry of these metric triple algebras would classify the canonical
   reduced systems. Any gap in constructing/measuring those Gram values is
   therefore central, not a harmless implementation omission.

4. A finite positive moment flatness certificate can work WITHOUT a supplied n:
   for p_j=sum_i w_i*beta_i^j with positive w_i, an annihilator q with
   sum_i w_i*q(beta_i)^2=0 vanishes at every support point. Hankel rank-flatness
   plus exact positivity can certify all support, not just observed moments.
   But weights w_i=0 hide physical coordinates, and one moment family says
   nothing about K-closure. Exact Gram-zero closure checks are additionally
   required. Approximate rank/flatness does not certify a physical dimension.

5. Classification of weighted-cubic invariant subspaces: on each active block
   j an orthonormal basis vector should have disjoint support and coordinates
   v_{ji}=sign_i*sqrt(beta_j/alpha_i), with
   beta_j=(sum_{i in block j}1/alpha_i)^(-1).
   Coordinates outside all blocks are zero on reachable S. For homogeneous
   known alpha, beta_j=alpha/|block j|; in that narrower setting the effective
   cubics reveal active-block multiplicities and hence the size of active
   physical support. It would be wrong to say physical n is unidentifiable for
   exactly the same reason in homogeneous-known and heterogeneous-unknown
   classes. Additional inactive coordinates can still be appended unless the
   class rules them out.

6. If all equivalent models are to be called hidden refinements, the definition
   must include arbitrary inactive coordinates and arbitrary SPD linear action
   on S-perp, with K leaving S invariant. A block splitting by itself need not
   exhaust them. For unrestricted signed K, even a topologically connected
   network can have inactive zero coordinates via balanced cancellations.
   Irreducible M-matrices exclude that zero-support phenomenon under positive
   forcing, so known homogeneous alpha may then reveal physical n.

7. The requested theorem must distinguish formal exact IO coefficient access
   from finite-noise acquisition. A mathematically finite adaptive stopping
   certificate in unbounded exact derivatives is not a finite-resource
   physical identification protocol. This audit will assess exact uniqueness
   separately from the campaign's still-unmet practical capability.
