TARGET
Reconstruct symmetric positive definite K, including unknown topology, using only
u at node 1, y=x_1, initial state 0, known n and known alpha>0, in
 xdot=-Kx-alpha x^[3]+e_1 u.

EXACT GENERAL-CLASS COUNTEREXAMPLE
For n=3 consider
 K_0=[[4,-1,-1],[-1,4,0],[-1,0,4]],
 K_1=[[4,-1,-1],[-1,5,-1],[-1,-1,5]].
Both are SPD symmetric connected M-matrices. In basis e_1,
w=(0,1,1)/sqrt(2), v=(0,-1,1)/sqrt(2), their bright blocks are
[[4,-sqrt(2)],[-sqrt(2),4]]; their dark eigenvalues are 4 and 6.
For every admissible input u and initial state 0, x_2=x_3=s is invariant and
 ydot=-4y+2s-alpha y^3+u,
 sdot=y-4s-alpha s^3.
Consequently their complete nonlinear input/output maps are identical, although
K_0 has a missing hidden-hidden edge and K_1 has that edge. These are not related
by a hidden signed permutation. Neither unlimited amplitude, duration, precision,
nor arbitrary adaptive one-node control can recover the difference. Thus the
unrestricted reconstruction target is false even for a connected passive network.

DIFFERENT POSITIVE MECHANISM: FIFTH-ORDER DARK-MODE SPECTROSCOPY
Linear noncyclicity need not imply nonlinear invisibility. Let
w=(0,cos(theta),sin(theta)), v=(0,-sin(theta),cos(theta)), and let
K in basis (e_1,w,v) equal [[a,beta,0],[beta,c,0],[0,0,mu]],
where a,c,mu>0 and ac>beta^2. For beta<0 and mu>=c this is a connected
M-matrix for theta in (0,pi/2). Every mu gives exactly the same linear boundary
transfer (s+c)/((s+a)(s+c)-beta^2).
Write x=y e_1+q w+r v. Let
A=cos(theta)^4+sin(theta)^4,
B=sum_i v_i w_i^3=-sin(theta)cos(theta)cos(2theta),
C=2 sin(theta)^2 cos(theta)^2.
Then
 ydot=-ay-beta q-alpha y^3+u,
 qdot=-beta y-cq-alpha[A q^3+3B q^2 r+3C q r^2-B r^3],
 rdot=-mu r-alpha[B q^3+3C q^2 r-3B q r^2+A r^3].

For u=epsilon u_0, expand odd analytic coefficients:
y=epsilon y_1+epsilon^3 y_3+epsilon^5 y_5+...,
q=epsilon q_1+epsilon^3 q_3+epsilon^5 q_5+...,
r=epsilon^3 r_3+epsilon^5 r_5+....
The first and third boundary coefficients are independent of mu. But
 r_3dot=-mu r_3-alpha B q_1^3,
 q_5dot=-beta y_5-cq_5-3alpha A q_1^2 q_3-3alpha B q_1^2 r_3.
Hence the fifth boundary response includes the dark convolution
 3alpha^2 B^2 q_1(t)^2 integral_0^t exp(-mu(t-s))q_1(s)^3 ds.
This is a go-return nonlinear channel: cubic mixing excites the linearly dark
mode at order 3, and a second cubic interaction reads it at order 5.

EXACT RECONSTRUCTION FORMULA FOR THIS NONCYCLIC 3-NODE FAMILY
Linear transfer determines a,c,beta^2; choose beta<0 as the hidden sign gauge.
q_j can be obtained from the measured y_j and applied u_j using the first equation.
Third order determines A whenever q_1 is nonzero. The angle is determined modulo
the hidden signed-permutation equivalence, and
 B^2=(1-A)(2A-1)/2.
Define
 F_5=q_5dot+beta y_5+cq_5+3alpha A q_1^2q_3,
 Z=-F_5/(3alpha q_1^2)=B r_3.
On an interval where q_1 is nonzero and integral_s^t Z !=0,
 mu= -[Z(t)-Z(s)+alpha B^2 integral_s^t q_1^3]/integral_s^t Z.
Thus complete order-5 output coefficients reconstruct this class whenever
1/2<A<1. At A=1/2 the two hidden nodes synchronize and mu is exactly invisible.
At A=1 a node disconnects and is invisible. The exceptional cases are physical
invariant subspaces, not a failure of the formula alone.

RESOURCE AND SIGNIFICANCE LIMITS
Volterra/Taylor derivatives are not free measurements. With amplitude epsilon,
the distinguishing raw output first occurs at epsilon^5 B^2. Extrapolating
order-5 coefficients from finite amplitudes amplifies measurement noise by
epsilon^-5 and requires an amplitude-bias/error tradeoff. To resolve a fixed mu
difference near synchrony theta=pi/4-delta, B^2=Theta(delta^2), so a noise floor
must resolve O(epsilon^5 delta^2) times the dynamical conditioning. Smoothing or
harmonic fitting can replace numerical time differentiation but cannot remove
the signal-size loss. Acquisition time also depends on 1/lambda_min(K) and dark
pole separation. No uniform finite-resource guarantee exists on the unrestricted
class, because the exact synchronized counterexample lies inside it.

The proposed positive mechanism is a concrete enlargement beyond linear
controllability, not yet a broad efficient network reconstruction theorem.
High-order dark-mode recovery might extend via the smallest K- and coordinatewise
cubic-invariant subspace containing e_1. That extension and a stable large-n
acquisition algorithm remain OPEN. This first derivation does not claim a
landmark or novelty before literature comparison.
