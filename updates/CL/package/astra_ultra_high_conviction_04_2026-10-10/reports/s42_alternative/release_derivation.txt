INDEPENDENT FINITE-WAVEFORM ALTERNATIVE, DEVELOPED AFTER FIRST FREEZE/GATE READ
2026-10-10. Candidate internal derivation; no external proof review.

MAIN REPRESENTATION
Write x=(y,z), K=[[a,b^T],[b,A]], m=n-1. Suppose each b_i is nonzero and
the magnitudes |b_i| are distinct. Hidden sign gauges permit b_i<0. Define
c_i=(-b_i/alpha)^(1/3)>0, with distinct c_i. The broad sparse case is NOT covered.

Clamp the measured port at y=r^3 until the hidden state equilibrates. Set
epsilon=r^-2 and z=r w. Its equilibrium equation becomes
 alpha w^[3]+b+epsilon A w=0,
so w(0)=c-epsilon*(Ac)/(3alpha c^[2])+O(epsilon^2).
Then clamp y=0 and observe the port reaction q=b^Tz. On the fast time
tau=r^2 t, hidden evolution is
 w'=-alpha w^[3]-epsilon A w.
Let d_i=2alpha c_i^2 and h_i(tau)=(1+d_i tau)^(-3/2). Expansion gives
 w_i^0(tau)=c_i/(1+d_i tau)^(1/2),
 w_i^1(tau)=-h_i(tau)*[(Ac)_i/(3alpha c_i^2)
                 +sum_j A_ij c_j I_ij(tau)],
 I_ij(tau)=integral_0^tau (1+d_i s)^(3/2)/(1+d_j s)^(1/2) ds.
Therefore
 q/r=Q_0(tau)+epsilon Q_1(tau)+O(epsilon^2),
 Q_0=-alpha sum_i c_i^4/(1+d_i tau)^(1/2),
 Q_1=sum_ij A_ij F_ij(tau),
 F_ij=[c_i*c_j/3+alpha*c_i^3*c_j*I_ij(tau)] h_i(tau).
For symmetric A use F_ii for diagonal parameters and F_ij+F_ji for i<j.

HOW THE DIRECT COUPLINGS ARE LABELLED
Q_0 has a square-root branch point at -1/d_i for every i, with a nonzero
coefficient. Thus its exact positive-time analytic function uniquely determines
the unordered d_i and hence c_i and b_i (up to the permitted signs/permutation).
This uses a single positive-time scalar transient in principle. Analytical
continuation is a uniqueness argument, not a physically supplied operation.
Numerical recovery from finitely many noisy positive-time samples is a separate
ill-conditioned mixture-fitting problem, not solved by this observation.

ALL-m INJECTIVITY OF THE SUBLEADING COUPLING MAP: MONODROMY ARGUMENT
An explicit antiderivative of I_ij, with a=d_i,b=d_j, is
 [sqrt(1+b*t)*sqrt(1+a*t)*(2(a/b)(1+b*t)+5(1-a/b))]/(4b)
 +[3(1-a/b)^2/(4b*sqrt(a/b))]
     log(sqrt(a/b)*sqrt(1+b*t)+sqrt(1+a*t)).
Subtract its value at 0. For i!=j the log coefficient is nonzero because a!=b.
Up to constants, this logarithm is
 L_ij(t)=log(sqrt(t+1/d_i)+sqrt(t+1/d_j)).
All remaining terms lie in the field obtained by adjoining the individual
square roots sqrt(t+1/d_i). Its branch monodromies commute.

Let M_i be analytic continuation around only the branch point -1/d_i.
For the logarithm L_ij, continuation around i sends L_ij to C_i-L_ij;
around j it sends it to C_j-L_ij, where C_i-C_j is an odd multiple of pi*i.
Each h_i changes sign under M_i and is fixed by the other M_j. Consequently
 (M_i M_j-M_j M_i)(F_ij+F_ji)
is a nonzero constant times p_i h_i+p_j h_j with p_i,p_j nonzero. It annihilates
the algebraic terms and every F_kl+F_lk whose unordered pair {k,l}!={i,j}.
If a constant linear combination of all response basis functions vanishes on
a positive real interval, analytic continuation and this commutator force
every offdiagonal coefficient to vanish. The remaining diagonal functions
have distinct individual branch points and are nonzero, forcing their
coefficients to vanish too.

For completeness the diagonal function simplifies to
 F_ii=(c_i^2/3+alpha*c_i^4*(tau+d_i*tau^2/2))*h_i.
Its numerator at tau=-1/d_i is c_i^2*(1/3-1/4)=c_i^2/12, hence its branch
singularity does not cancel. This proves the distinct-branch-point diagonal
step. Offdiagonal coefficients p_i,p_j have positive real factors in the
original gauge, so their combination is not identically zero.

This is an all-m candidate proof of injectivity of A -> Q_1 for distinct
nonzero direct-port magnitudes. It requires independent review of the analytic
continuation bookkeeping. It does not establish numerical stability.
Exact finite-field time-series ranks support the lemma for m=1,...,12 with
dimensions 1,3,6,10,15,21,28,36,45,55,66,78. The time derivative at tau=0 of Q_1
vanishes (equilibrium preparation), and the first N+1 rows have rank N in all
these cases, N=m(m+1)/2. These are finite checks, not the proof of all m.

PHYSICALLY FINITE CLAMP IMPLEMENTATION
The exact instantaneous clamp is not free. A realizable approximation uses
measured-port feedback and a smooth target v(t):
 u=vdot+a*v+alpha*v^3-L*(y-v), with L=ell*r^2.
The measured deviation drives a stable scalar error equation. During the
release phase v=0, after scaled transient settling, y is O(r^-1), and
 q=-ydot-(a+L)y-alpha*y^3 is obtained from the measured y and commanded u.
No hidden-coordinate measurement is used. A fixed smooth ramp of duration
Delta in fast time can replace the instantaneous step. Its leading hidden
state is computable from independent scalar cubic equations; its first
correction is linear in A. In the ensuing free release, these initial-correction
terms are algebraic in tau and do not alter the pair-log commutator argument,
provided the new leading hidden amplitudes remain nonzero and distinct.

With L=ell*r^2 the release output naturally filters Q on the fast time; no
high-order time derivatives are required. One can fit the known filter to
the response basis. Imperfect tracking during the ramp should be recorded
and included as the actual scalar input. Quantitative preparation/tracking
error bounds remain to be supplied for a complete numerical guarantee.

RESOURCE ACCOUNT
1. Strong preparation requires y=r^3 and hence u=alpha*r^9+O(r^3), with large
   state range. Holding duration and actual energy are charged, not omitted.
2. The transient has physical bandwidth O(r^2). A smooth target ramp on this
   time scale needs vdot=O(r^5), below the r^9 holding force asymptotically.
3. Q_1 is only an epsilon=r^-2 correction. In direct reaction force it is of
   size O(r^-1), while the leading reaction is O(r). With force inferred from
   y under bandwidth-L feedback, its raw y signal is O(r^-3).
4. Thus larger r suppresses model truncation but demands finer measurement;
   amplitudes r and sqrt(2)*r can extrapolate the epsilon correction, with
   remainder still amplified by the response-basis inverse condition number.
5. At least N=m(m+1)/2 independent scalar data values are needed to reconstruct
   dense A, even when b and leading response are supplied. Dense output itself
   costs Theta(n^2) storage. Naive basis fitting costs polynomial work but may
   require precision or samples much larger than polynomial n.
6. Near-coincident |b_i| make both leading mixture recovery and edge-basis
   fitting ill-conditioned. The synchronized exact counterexample is a real
   limiting case, not an artifact of extrapolation.
7. Sparse networks with absent direct-port edges have c_i=0; this representation
   does not presently reconstruct layers beyond the direct neighbors.

STATUS
The representation may be a meaningful constructive alternative to acquiring
very high-order noncommutative IO jets. It does not yet deliver a broad,
well-conditioned large-network capability. Positive-time conditioning must
be evaluated before any significance claim. Novelty is UNVERIFIED; the current
primary literature already establishes much of the nonlinear-identifiability
motivation. Generic identifiability alone is not the campaign objective.
