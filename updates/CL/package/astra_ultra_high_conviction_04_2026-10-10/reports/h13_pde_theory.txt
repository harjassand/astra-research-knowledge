Working target: 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes enstrophy growth.

PRE-LITERATURE DERIVATION (frozen before source comparison)

Candidate mechanism. For a smooth divergence-free solution on the periodic 3-torus,
separate the exact cubic vortex-stretching production from viscous enstrophy
loss, and monitor only the positive surplus. This is intended to exploit signed
cancellation that norm-only criteria discard.

Let omega=curl u, S=(grad u+grad u^T)/2,
Y(t)=integral |omega|^2 dx, Z(t)=integral |grad omega|^2 dx, and
P(t)=integral omega dot S omega dx. Multiplying the vorticity equation by
omega and integrating gives

  (1/2)Y'(t) = P(t) - nu Z(t).

For Y>0 set B(t)=[P(t)-nu Z(t)]_+/Y(t). It follows exactly that

  d(log Y)/dt <= 2 B(t),
  Y(t) <= Y(0) exp(2 integral_0^t B(s) ds).

Thus finite integral of B up to T bounds H^1 (since mean velocity is
controlled separately by conserved/decaying L^2 energy), and the standard
strong-solution continuation argument extends the solution past T. This is
scale invariant under u_lambda(x,t)=lambda u(lambda x,lambda^2 t) on R^3 (or
across the corresponding rescaled tori): Y scales as lambda, P and nu Z as
lambda^3, B as lambda^2, and dt as lambda^-2.

Exact discriminant. The observable is computed from one state (u,omega) and its
first two spatial derivatives; it distinguishes positive net vortex stretching
from stretching already canceled by viscosity. For a plane shear
u=(A exp(-nu t) sin y,0,0), one has P=0 and B=0. A zero-mean Beltrami field
also has P=0. These are exact examples where a magnitude-only strain bound can
be large relative to the actual net enstrophy growth.

Initial falsification. The candidate is an exact reformulation of the upward
variation of log enstrophy: the identity implies
B=(1/2)(d(log Y)/dt)_+. Therefore the theorem says that finite upward variation
of the very norm needed for continuation prevents that norm from diverging.
It does not yet bound B from initial data, lower regularity norms, or a finite
spectral observation. Its state acquisition already requires the full field
and second spatial derivatives. This circularity is the central obstacle; no
major regularity advance is claimed. I will test whether dyadic localization
avoids spatial/frequency cancellation without worsening the criterion, then
compare primary-source results.

POST-FREEZE PRIOR-ART AND ADVERSARIAL AUDIT (2026-10-10)

Exact foundational target. The ambitious target selected was global smooth
existence for every smooth finite-energy datum for unforced 3D incompressible
Navier-Stokes, or a singularity counterexample. This report obtains neither.
The [Clay announcement](https://www.claymath.org/news/navier-stokes-announcement/)
of 2026-09-11 says the Navier-Stokes problem has "apparently been settled" and
that evaluation is deliberately unhurried. I did not audit the announced work;
this local candidate is not a verification of it.

Strongest primary-source baseline. The closest and substantially stronger
prior result is Evan Miller, *A regularity criterion for the Navier-Stokes
equation involving only the middle eigenvalue of the strain tensor*,
arXiv:1710.05569v4 (2019; published in Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 235 (2020),
99–139; [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1710.05569),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-019-01419-z)). With viscosity normalized to one, it derives the strain-only
identity

  d/dt ||S||_2^2 = -2 ||S||_{Hdot1}^2 - 4 integral det(S),

and proves, for 2/p+3/q=2 and 3/2<q<=infinity, an estimate

  ||u(T)||_{Hdot1}^2 <= ||u(0)||_{Hdot1}^2
      exp(C_q integral_0^T ||lambda_2^+||_q^p dt),

where lambda_2 is the middle eigenvalue of the trace-free strain. Finite
integral prevents blow-up; finite-time blow-up forces it to diverge. This is a
scale-critical, geometrically resolved control of enstrophy growth. It is the
closest and more informative baseline: B is only a scalar restatement of the
global enstrophy derivative and does not identify a new coercive quantity. The classical endpoint benchmark is Escauriaza–Seregin–Šverák,
L_{3,infinity}-solutions of the Navier-Stokes equations and backward
uniqueness (2003), which proves smoothness for the scale-critical
L_infinity_t L^3_x class ([journal record](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/rm609)).
A proposed frequency-localized detour is also already occupied in a different
form: Kim–Kwak–Yoo prove scale-critical regularity criteria from high spectral
components ([arXiv:1405.6838](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.6838), published 2015).
I did not develop a blockwise positive-budget theorem after finding this prior
work; clipping shellwise signs would in any event strengthen the scalar upper
bound and would need its own useful tail estimate.

Actual mathematics obtained. For a classical periodic solution, the scalar
criterion is a correct one-way continuation test: if integral_0^T B(t)dt is
finite, then H^1 stays bounded and the strong solution continues. If a finite
maximal time occurs, the integral diverges. It is not an equivalence as a scalar calculus statement: a bounded positive
function can have infinite upward variation; I have not constructed such an
actual Navier–Stokes trajectory. By the exact balance, B(t) is precisely one
half of the positive derivative of log Y.
This makes the test a reformulation of the continuation norm's observed
growth rather than an estimate of that growth from initial data.

Exact adversarial fields. The finite Fourier script
`h13_pde_theory/triad_witness.py` checks the following divergence-free
2D-plus-one-component periodic field, independent of z:

  psi=cos(2x+y), W=cos(x)+cos(x+y),
  u=(partial_y psi,-partial_x psi,W).

Exact trigonometric integration with normalized torus measure gives
Y=14, Z=65, and

  P = mean(psi_xy (W_y^2-W_x^2) - (psi_yy-psi_xx) W_x W_y) = -1/4.
 Reversing u changes P to +1/4 while leaving Y and Z
fixed; multiplying u by a positive amplitude a makes these quantities
14a^2, 65a^2, and +/-a^3/4. Thus cubic stretching has no sign-definite
coercivity, and B can be arbitrarily large at initial time as amplitude grows.
The script reproduces P=-0.25, Y=14, and Z=65 to numerical precision; this is
a finite Fourier check of the exact formula, not validation of a PDE claim.

The exact shear u=(A exp(-nu N^2 t) sin(N y),0,0) is a global solution with
P=0 and B=0. For every finite Fourier cutoff K, choosing N>K makes its observed
low modes identically zero while Y(0)=A^2 N^2/2 and Z(0)=A^2 N^4/2 grow
unboundedly. More decisively, let v be the reversed triad above, with
(P,Y,Z)=(1/4,14,65), and define v_m(x)=c m v(mx) for integer m. Its Fourier
support lies entirely above m, while the exact scaling gives
B(v_m)=m^2(c/4-65 nu)/14 whenever c>260 nu. Thus, for any fixed low-mode
cutoff, the observed coefficients can all be zero while the true B is positive
and arbitrarily large; the zero field has the same low-mode observation but
B=0. This falsifies finite-low-mode evaluation of B without a certified tail
bound. It does not falsify the analytic continuation lemma.

Resource interface. The continuum criterion requires the full solution state
u(t), spatial first derivatives for P, second derivatives for Z, and a
continuous-time integral to the candidate endpoint. On an M-point 3D Fourier
grid, direct spectral evaluation costs O(M log M) arithmetic and O(M) memory
per snapshot, plus temporal quadrature. To certify the continuum value one
must also bound unresolved modes and quadrature/rounding errors. Neither an
exact solution trajectory nor such tail bounds are supplied by finite initial
data or by this campaign. No external data, oracle, training, or physical
measurement was used.

Why it misses the decisive obstacle. The lemma is mathematically sound and
scale critical, but its integrand is the positive growth rate of the very
H^1/enstrophy quantity whose blow-up is in question. It provides no estimate
that makes the integral finite for arbitrary smooth data, no new cancellation
inequality, and no finite-observation closure. Miller's middle-eigenvalue
criterion provides a more structured and scale-critical observable; the
proposed scalar budget adds no foundational capability on top of it.

Strongest falsifier: the candidate's claimed leverage collapses under the
identity B=(1/2)(d log Y/dt)_+. This is not a counterexample to its conditional
lemma, but it is decisive against treating the lemma as a new regularity
mechanism. High-frequency shears independently destroy a finite-mode
certification claim unless a tail estimate is provided.

OPEN. Unforced 3D global regularity remains OPEN in this work. No proof,
blow-up construction, novelty claim, or independent validation is offered.
