FIRST DERIVATION FREEZE — s43_proof — 2026-10-10
Before literature comparison; independent reconstruction from CHARTER/NETWORK_GATE.

MODEL AND SCOPE
Known n>=1 and alpha>0; f(x)=-Kx-alpha x^(componentwise 3),
g=e1, h=x1, K real symmetric positive definite, initial state zero.
Assume every k_i=(Ke1)_i is nonzero and all k_i^2 are distinct.
This is a generic dense-port condition, not an arbitrary-network condition.

BRACKETS AND OBSERVABLE OPERATORS
[u,v]=Dv*u-Du*v; consequently [L_u,L_v]=L_[u,v].
Let F=L_f, G=L_g. Since [g,f]=-k-3alpha h^2 g,
A:=L_k=-[G,F]-3alpha h^2 G exactly as a derivation on smooth functions.
It is essential that h^2 multiplies G; treating it as a constant is wrong.

z0=-Fh-alpha h^3=k dot x by symmetry of K.
z_(m+1)=-(6alpha)^(-1) A^2 F z_m.
If z_m=sum_i k_i^(2m+1)x_i, differentiating the cubic twice along
the constant field k proves z_(m+1)=sum_i k_i^(2m+3)x_i.
Hence p_(m+1)=A z_m(0)=sum_i (k_i^2)^(m+1).
p1,...,pn determine all k_i^2 by Newton identities. K11=-GFh(0)>0
anchors the measured coordinate among the distinct roots. Hidden signs
and permutations remain gauges.

CONSTANT DIRECTIONS AND K
Let v0=k and v_(m+1)=-(6alpha)^(-1)[k,[k,[v_m,f]]].
Inductively v_m is the constant vector k^(componentwise (2m+1)).
Thus B_mr=-L_(v_r) F z_m(0)=v_m^T K v_r.
Let Q_mi=k_i^(2m+1), 0<=m<n. Then B=Q K Q^T and
det Q=(product_i k_i)(product_(i<j)(k_j^2-k_i^2)) is nonzero.
Consequently K=Q^(-1)B Q^(-T), modulo a signed permutation fixing e1.
No prepared hidden states or directly measured hidden coordinates occur.

FINITE INPUT-OUTPUT JET BOUND
Let J_w(x)=L_w h(x), w a word in F,G; A_d is the polynomial algebra
generated by J_w for |w|<=d. F,G send A_d to A_(d+1).
A sends A_d to A_(d+2), because h^2 belongs to A0.
z_m belongs to A_(5m+1). As an operator L_(v_m) shifts depth by
at most 5m+2. Hence p_(m+1) belongs to A_(5m+3), while B_mr
belongs to A_(5m+5r+4). All needed numbers are polynomial expressions
in the binary IO word coefficients J_w(0) of length <=10n-6.
For n=1 this bound is 4, although K11 alone needs only order 2.

WHY THESE ARE IO DATA
For a1,...,ar in {0,1}, define the forward-pulse endpoint map
Y_a(t1,...,tr)=h(phi_(f+a_r g)^(tr) o ... o phi_(f+a1 g)^(t1)(0)).
Its mixed derivative at t=0 is
[(F+a1 G)...(F+ar G)h](0). Boolean finite differences in the a_i
recover each ordered binary word coefficient. Positive pulse durations
suffice to define the derivatives as limits, so inverse drift is not
required. This establishes exact finite-jet identifiability, not a
finite-precision or finite-sample recovery guarantee.

DEGENERACY ALREADY FOUND
Connected 3-node matrices [[a,b,b],[b,c,d],[b,d,c]] leave x2=x3 invariant
from zero under every input. IO depends on c+d and not c-d. For example
(a,b,c,d)=(4,1,3,1) and (4,1,3.5,0.5) are both SPD, have all entries
of the port column nonzero, and have identical IO for all inputs, but
different spectra (the antisymmetric eigenvalues are 2 and 3).
They are not signed-permutation equivalent. Thus simple port squares
cannot simply be dropped, and connectedness does not imply recovery.

RESOURCE STATUS
The proof uses exact high-order, ordered IO derivatives. An uncompressed
complete binary jet has exponentially many coefficients in n. The proof
does not establish an exponential lower bound, nor an efficient compressed
acquisition scheme. There is no uniform conditioning over the stated class:
port couplings can approach zero and squared-root gaps can approach zero.
Exact generic structural recovery is established by this reconstruction;
practical single-port acquisition and landmark significance remain OPEN.
