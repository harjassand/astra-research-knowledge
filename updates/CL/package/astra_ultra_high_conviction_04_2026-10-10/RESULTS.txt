1. STRONGEST EXACT RESULT

The model is
  xdot=-Kx-diag(alpha_i)x^(componentwise 3)+e1*u,
  y=x1, x(0)=0,
with unknown finite physical dimension N, real symmetric positive-definite K,
and unknown positive alpha_i. Input/output scales, the driven/measured coordinate,
and this exact constitutive model are part of the information contract.
Data are the exact endpoint germs of ordered positive-duration pulses with
amplitudes 0 and 1. They determine ordered Lie-derivative coefficients. This
is an ideal exact-data theorem; derivatives and equality tests are not supplied
at unit physical cost in any practical-performance claim.

Define W as the smallest linear subspace containing e1, invariant under K,
and closed under T(u,v,w)=alpha*(componentwise u*v*w). Then:

A. For known N, K and alpha are globally identifiable in this model class,
   up to hidden signed permutations fixing e1, if and only if W=R^N.
   A finite ordered response jet suffices. If W is proper, K+epsilon P_Wperp
   has exactly the same full IO map for every input and is not gauge-equivalent.

B. Without knowing N, the IO map determines a unique minimum-dimensional
   realization WITHIN the positive heterogeneous cubic class, up to that gauge.
   Its dimension is r=dim W. Exact positive-Hankel flatness and Gram closure
   supply an adaptive finite certificate without a supplied dimension bound.

C. Every physical realization of the same IO map is explicitly classified.
   Its active coordinates split into disjoint weighted signed blocks B_b,
   with B_1={1}; other coordinates may be inactive. Write
     Z_b=sum_(i in B_b)1/alpha_i,
     V_ib=s_i/[sqrt(alpha_i)*sqrt(Z_b)] on B_b,
     alpha_bar_b=1/Z_b.
   The columns of V are orthonormal. All and only equivalent physical models
   have these harmonic coefficient sums and
     K=V K_bar V^T+R,
   where R is an arbitrary positive-definite self-adjoint operator on
   range(V)^perp, extended by zero. Residual directions may mix across blocks
   and with inactive coordinates. Physical N is generally not identifiable.

D. There is a concrete physical-node census exception. If every physical node
   has the same unknown positive alpha, K has nonpositive offdiagonal entries,
   and its offdiagonal graph is connected, then
     N=sum_(b=1)^r alpha/alpha_bar_b.
   Every ratio is an integer block size. The common alpha is measured at the
   port. Hidden topology can remain unidentifiable while physical N is known.
   This conclusion is not valid for arbitrary signed couplings or unknown
   heterogeneous node coefficients.

2. INDISPENSABLE CONSTRUCTION AND PROOF IDEAS

The key internal invention is a way to apply the unknown K to constructible
directions using only scalar response identities. A paired direction consists
of a constant field v and its observable z_v=v dot x. Let beta_i=alpha_i v_i^2.
Repeated cubic differentiation produces q_m,i=v_i beta_i^m and the moments
  p_m=sum_i v_i^2 beta_i^m.
These form a positive atomic measure. Its support polynomial P produces the
support Euler field E=P_support(v)x by interpolation; no hidden coordinate is
measured or actuated. With bracket [u,v]=Dv*u-Du*v and R=-[v,f],
  C=R-[E,R], Kv=C-[C,E]/2.
The paired scalar is
  z_Kv=-(I+L_E/6-L_E^2/6)L_f z_v.
Triple brackets construct T. Observable Gram entries detect exact closure.
Once the closure is full, a generic direction's positive moments separate the
coordinates, and a measured matrix congruence recovers the entire K.

For the canonical quotient, rescale W by diag(sqrt(alpha_i)). A real linear
subspace closed under coordinatewise triple products is exactly a span of
disjoint signed block indicators. A generic vector separates the coordinate
evaluation functionals up to sign; its odd powers and a Vandermonde matrix
isolate every block. This proves the quotient and the exhaustive equivalences.
For the census, K^-1 e1 is strictly positive in an irreducible SPD M-matrix.
It lies in W, ruling out inactive coordinates and mixed-sign blocks. Normalized
blocks have cubic coefficient alpha/|B|, yielding physical multiplicities.

A short explicit census construction strengthens the general existence proof.
One port with m synchronized hidden nodes has reduced equations
  ydot=-a*y+c*z-alpha*y^3+u,
  zdot=c*y-d*z-(alpha/m)*z^3.
Its linear transfer has order two for every m. After recovering a,d,c^2,alpha
from low response coefficients, the hidden cubic contributes

to the t^8 U^3 coefficient of a constant-input response, relative to a
computable reference. It therefore determines m. Exact Fraction checks cover
m=1,2,3,7,64,1000. Adjacent sizes have coefficient gap
alpha*c^4/[448m(m+1)], so the calculation exposes its own precision cost.
See reports/root_nonlinear_tomography/node_census_derivation.txt and script.
