\textbf{Outcome.} A concrete result outside entropy-contraction theory emerged: measured-$\chi^2$ channel dominance transfers \emph{physical message decoding and quantum recovery}, with dimension-dependent fidelity bounds and a sharp classical error factor of two. It yields a stronger finite-code reliability--secrecy inequality for more than two messages. The derivation is independent of N402's unverified universal formula. The recovery map and near-optimal-decoding machinery have established antecedents; the scoped contribution is their cross-channel order consequence and the many-message refinement. No major open problem, universal simulator, new capacity mechanism, or computational hardness barrier is marked solved.

\section*{1. Strongest developed result: decoding and recovery transfer}

For $\theta\succ0$ on its support define
\[
 \Sld_\theta(X)=\tfrac12(\theta X+X\theta),\qquad
 q_\theta(X)=\langle X,\Sld_\theta^{-1}X\rangle_{\rm HS}.
\]
Measured Pearson divergence satisfies
$\chi^2_{\rm m}(\rho\|\sigma)=q_\sigma(\rho-\sigma)$, with the usual extended support convention.
For channels $\M,\N$ with common input, write $\M\succeq_{\rm SLD}\N$ when
\[
 \chi^2_{\rm m}(\M\rho\|\M\sigma)\ge
 \chi^2_{\rm m}(\N\rho\|\N\sigma)\quad\hbox{for every state pair }\rho,\sigma. \tag{1}
\]
The superscript $c$ requires this after every finite untouched quantum reference.
Set, for $k\ge2$,
\[
 h_k(p)=\frac1k+\frac{(p-1/k)^2}{1-1/k}
       =p^2+\frac{(1-p)^2}{k-1}. \tag{2}
\]

\textbf{Theorem 1 (operational transfer).} Suppose $\M\succeq_{\rm SLD}\N$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every $k$-letter input ensemble, with arbitrary priors, its optimal guessing probabilities obey
\[
 \boxed{\ p_{\rm guess}(\M)\ge h_k(p_{\rm guess}(\N)).\ } \tag{3}
\]
A single explicit physical POVM on the $\M$ output achieves the bound.
\item For every fixed encoding $\mathcal E$ of a $d$-dimensional logical system, $d\ge2$, let $F^*(\Phi\mathcal E)=\max_{\mathcal D}\Fe(\mathcal D\Phi\mathcal E)$, where $\Fe$ is overlap with the normalized maximally entangled logical state. Then
\[
 \boxed{\ F^*(\M\mathcal E)\ge h_{d^2}(F^*(\N\mathcal E)).\ } \tag{4}
\]
An explicit CPTP decoder on the $\M$ output achieves the right side.
\end{enumerate}
Thus optimal average message error and logical entanglement infidelity transfer with
$\varepsilon_\M\le2\varepsilon_\N-k\varepsilon_\N^2/(k-1)$, using $k$ letters in (3) and $k=d^2$ in (4). These are averaged criteria, not worst-case input or diamond-norm guarantees. For $k=1$ or $d=1$ the task is trivial.

\textit{Proof of (3).} Write $\theta_x=\M(\rho_x)$, $\theta=\sum_xp_x\theta_x$, and use $\beta_x,\beta$ for $\N$. Compress to $P=\supp\theta$; every $\theta_x$ with nonzero prior lies in $P$. The inverse is completely positive there because
\[
 \Sld_\theta^{-1}(C)=2\int_0^\infty e^{-t\theta}C e^{-t\theta}\,dt. \tag{5}
\]
Consequently $G_x=\Sld_\theta^{-1}(p_x\theta_x)$ is a POVM on $P$, since $\sum_xG_x=\Sld_\theta^{-1}(\theta)=I_P$. Complete it arbitrarily on $P^\perp$. Its success is
\[
 Q_\M=\sum_xq_\theta(p_x\theta_x)
 =\sum_xp_x^2[1+\chi^2_{\rm m}(\theta_x\|\theta)]. \tag{6}
\]
Zero-prior terms are omitted in the right side of (6). Apply (1) to each $(\rho_x,\bar\rho)$ to obtain $Q_\M\ge Q_\N$.
For a target POVM $\{F_x\}$ with success $p$, set $A_x=p_x\beta_x-\beta/k$ and $B_x=F_x-I/k$. Then
\[
 \sum_xq_\beta(A_x)=Q_\N-1/k,\quad
 \sum_x\Tr A_xB_x=p-1/k,\quad
 \sum_x\langle B_x,\Sld_\beta B_x\rangle
 =\sum_x\Tr\beta F_x^2-1/k\le1-1/k.
\]
Metric Cauchy--Schwarz gives $(p-1/k)^2\le(Q_\N-1/k)(1-1/k)$. Optimize $p$ and use the source POVM. No common simulation map or positivity of a signed lift is used.

\textit{Proof of (4).} For $L$ with $d$-dimensional logical input put $\theta=L(I/d)$ and, on its effective output support, define
\[
 \mathcal R_L=\frac1dL^*\circ\Sld_\theta^{-1},\qquad
 F_{\rm SLD}(L)=\Fe(\mathcal R_LL)
 =\frac1{d^3}\sum_{ij}q_\theta(L(E_{ij})). \tag{7}
\]
Equation (5) proves complete positivity. Moreover $\mathcal R_L^*(I)=\Sld_\theta^{-1}(\theta)=I$, so it is trace preserving. Extend to unused output levels by a CPTP replacer, discarding cross blocks. The ambient Moore--Penrose superoperator alone need not be CP.

For any decoder $\mathcal D$ set $U_{ij}=L(E_{ij})-\delta_{ij}\theta$ and $V_{ij}=\mathcal D^*(E_{ij})-\delta_{ij}I/d$. Direct expansion gives
\[
 \sum_{ij}q_\theta(U_{ij})=d^3F_{\rm SLD}(L)-d,\quad
 \sum_{ij}\langle U_{ij},V_{ij}\rangle=d^2[\Fe(\mathcal DL)-d^{-2}].
\]
Since $\mathcal D^*$ is unital CP, Schwarz and $\sum_{ij}E_{ij}^*E_{ij}=dI$ yield
\[
 \sum_{ij}\langle V_{ij},\Sld_\theta V_{ij}\rangle\le d-d^{-1}.
\]
Thus
$[\Fe(\mathcal DL)-d^{-2}]^2\le[F_{\rm SLD}(L)-d^{-2}](1-d^{-2})$.
The optimal fidelity is at least $d^{-2}$, by a replacer decoder, so $F_{\rm SLD}(L)\ge h_{d^2}(F^*(L))$.

Finally, (1) is equivalent to the local inequalities $q_{\M\sigma}(\M H)\ge q_{\N\sigma}(\N H)$ for traceless Hermitian $H$ at faithful $\sigma$: apply (1) to $\sigma+tH,\sigma$, and cancel $t^2$; the reverse uses $H=\rho-\sigma$. Sum at $\sigma=I/d$ over a Hermitian orthonormal basis. The identity direction $I/\sqrt d$ contributes exactly $d$ on each side, hence $F_{\rm SLD}(\M)\ge F_{\rm SLD}(\N)$. Input preprocessing preserves (1), proving (4) for every encoder. Common faithful regularization covers boundary input pairs.

\textbf{Exact sharpness and a failed stronger claim.} Let $0<a<1$. On a classical binary input, let $\M$ reveal the input with probability $a^2$ and otherwise erase it; let $\N$ be the BSC of bias $a$. The signed observable lift
\[
 T=\begin{pmatrix}(1+1/a)/2&(1-1/a)/2\\
 (1-1/a)/2&(1+1/a)/2\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}
\]
has row sums one, $\M T=\N$, and $D_{n_x}-T^{\mathsf T}D_{m_x}T=0$ for each input letter. On off-diagonal target matrix units the remaining outer square defect has nonnegative entries $n_x(0),n_x(1)$; the input first dephases, so that defect is CP. This certifies dominance even for every quantum reference. Uniform-prior success is
\[
 p_\M=(1+a^2)/2=h_2((1+a)/2)=h_2(p_\N),\qquad
 \frac{\varepsilon_\M}{\varepsilon_\N}=1+a\longrightarrow2. \tag{8}
\]
Thus (3) is exact at $k=2$ and no universal linear error factor below two is possible. Yet $p_\M<p_\N$: a universal physical simulator and exact guessing-probability dominance are false. The quadratic bridge provides a weaker, quantitatively controlled operational order.

\section*{2. Finite private codes: a concrete improvement}

Suppose the environment $E$ dominates receiver $B$ in measured $\chi^2$ for the block channel under consideration. A uniform $k$-message code with receiver average error $\varepsilon$ and secrecy
$\delta=\tfrac12\|\rho_{XE}-I_X/k\otimes\rho_E\|_1$ obeys
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \delta\ge\frac{[(1-\varepsilon-1/k)_+]^2}{1-1/k}.
 \quad} \tag{9}
\]
Indeed (3) gives an actual environment decoder with success at least $h_k(1-\varepsilon)$ when $1-\varepsilon\ge1/k$, and secrecy bounds every decoder's success by $1/k+\delta$. If the given error is larger, the displayed positive-part bound is trivial.

Hirche [1, Theorem 4.1] proves $\delta\ge(1-2\varepsilon)^2/2$ for $\varepsilon\le1/2$ from binary midpoint-MMSE dominance at each blocklength. That hypothesis already supplies the local SLD inequalities: encode $\sigma\pm tH$ at faithful $\sigma$ and cancel the exact $t^2$ midpoint form. The local-to-global argument above therefore makes (9) applicable under the same blockwise hypothesis. At $k=2$ it reproduces the prior bound. For $k>2$ it is strictly stronger throughout $\varepsilon\le1/2$; the difference is
\[
 (k-2)\left[\frac1{2k}-\frac{\varepsilon^2}{k-1}\right]>0.
\]
At zero decoding error it forces $\delta\ge1-1/k$, the largest possible cq secrecy defect. For example $k=1024$, $\varepsilon=.01$ gives $\delta\ge.9791$, whereas the stated pairwise bound gives $.4802$.

Complete SLD order tensorizes: replace one channel at a time, treating the other output as the untouched reference. Hence (3)--(4) hold for every parallel block code with the same encoder. They transfer vanishing classical and quantum decoding errors, and do not decrease achievable reliability exponents. Capacity monotonicity and zero-capacity consequences are not claimed as new. The existing general exponential private strong converse [5] uses a different ideal-key criterion; (9) is a finite-code bound on separate decoding and trace-distance secrecy errors.

\textbf{Closest antecedents and significance.} B\'eny [3, Eqs.~(5)--(6)] already defines the Bures metric adjoint; at maximally mixed logical input it is exactly (7). Barnum--Knill [4] already establishes squared near-optimal recovery and pretty-good discrimination. The transpose/Petz decoder has at least as much maximally mixed entanglement fidelity as this SLD decoder: its eigenbasis kernel $1/\sqrt{\lambda_i\lambda_j}$ dominates $2/(\lambda_i+\lambda_j)$. The SLD score is useful here because it respects the assumed channel order. We claim neither a better recovery map nor a new near-optimal-decoding paradigm. No explicit primary statement of the cross-channel bounds (3)--(4) or the $k$-dependent refinement (9) was located in the focused review. These are scoped theorem candidates with direct proofs, not a certified historical first or a foundational breakthrough.

\section*{3. A general channel-order equivalence and its finite certificate}

\textbf{Theorem 2 (complete Petz order).} For arbitrary finite channels, the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item complete measured-$\chi^2$/SLD dominance;
\item complete dominance for the \emph{modular Petz} divergence with $f_1(x)=(x-1)^2/(x+1)$;
\item simultaneous complete dominance for every operator-convex modular Petz divergence $D_f$.
\end{enumerate}
Affine tangents at one are immaterial. The all-state statement uses standard extended Petz support values, allowing infinity. This result needs no N402 premise.

\textit{Derivation.} For faithful $\rho,\sigma$, $X=\rho-\sigma$, and $s>0$, introduce an untouched qubit and
\[
 \Omega_s=\frac{\operatorname{diag}(\rho,s\sigma)}{1+s},\qquad
 H=\begin{pmatrix}0&X\\X&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Solving its off-diagonal SLD equation gives the exact identity
\[
 q_{\Omega_s}(H)=4(1+s)\langle X,(L_\rho+sR_\sigma)^{-1}X\rangle
              =4(1+s)D_{f_s}(\rho\|\sigma),\quad
 f_s(x)=\frac{(x-1)^2}{x+s}. \tag{10}
\]
Apply the complete local SLD order to this block test. Every normalized operator-convex function has the positive representation
\[
 f(x)=b(x-1)^2+\int_{[0,\infty)}f_s(x)\,d\nu(s),\quad
 b\ge0,\quad\nu\ge0,\quad\int\frac{d\nu(s)}{1+s}<\infty. \tag{11}
\]
Take $s\downarrow0$ and $sD_{f_s}\to D_{(x-1)^2}$ as $s\to\infty$, then integrate the inequalities. Conversely
$D_{f_1}(\sigma+tH\|\sigma)=t^2q_\sigma(H)/2+O(t^3)$, so triangular dominance implies the local SLD order, which is exactly global measured-$\chi^2$ order. On PSD boundaries use the spectral perspective
$\sum_{ij}(\lambda_i-\mu_j)^2(\lambda_i+s\mu_j)^{-1}|\langle e_i,v_j\rangle|^2$, omit $0/0$, and apply positive integration/Tonelli. This proves the extended statement without assuming invertible ambient outputs.

This connects complete binary estimation with all modular Petz orders, including pointwise Petz R\'enyi comparison in its data-processing range. It does \emph{not} imply that their linear contraction coefficients all coincide. The coherent-qubit and integral machinery is closely related to Hirche [1]; the all-$f$ equivalence is assessed as a short corollary-level extension.

\textbf{Exact finite sufficient certificate.} A state-independent HP unital lift $J:\mathcal L(B)\to\mathcal L(C)$ with $\M^*J=\N^*$ and complete square domination certifies $\M\succeq^c_{\rm SLD}\N$. Let $C_\Phi$ denote the unnormalized Choi matrix and, in row-major Hilbert--Schmidt coordinates, set
\[
 D_\N=C_\N\otimes I_B,\quad D_\M=C_\M\otimes I_C,\quad T=I_A\otimes[J].
\]
Zhu--Wang's CP outer-defect criterion [2] is $D_\N-T^*D_\M T\succeq0$. Together with the linear HP, unitality and reconstruction constraints, this is precisely the affine SDP
\[
 \boxed{\begin{pmatrix}D_\N&T^*D_\M\\D_\M T&D_\M\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.} \tag{12}
\]
The equivalence follows by congruence to
$\operatorname{diag}(D_\N-T^*D_\M T,D_\M)$ and is valid for singular $D_\M$.
Its main block has order $d_A(d_B^2+d_C^2)$. A second exact formulation uses a contraction between minimal Stinespring spaces intertwining every lifted observable. These are finite formulations of an established criterion, not new characterizations of general entropy dominance. A generic CVXPY builder is included, but was not solved because that solver stack is absent; the explicit qutrit witness was reconstructed numerically. Exact or interval certificates and conditioning promises remain necessary for rigorous numerical decisions.

\textbf{Remaining ordering gap.} Necessity of a common signed lift for unrestricted complete measured-$\chi^2$ order is still open in general; a full-output-span sufficient structural case is known [1]. Theorem 2 does not close it, and the finite reference bound for the signed-lift criterion cannot simply be transferred to the weaker unsmoothed order. No polynomial algorithm for arbitrary complete entropy-order membership has been established.

\section*{4. What N402 adds, and why more ambitious routes fail}

N402 proposes that complete equal-reference KL, conditional-difference KL and CMI contraction all equal
\[
 \eta(\N)=1-M(\N)^{-1},\quad
 M(\N)=\min_{\sigma\in\D(B)}
 \left\|\Tr_B[(I\otimes\sigma)C_\N^+(I\otimes\sigma)]\right\|_\infty,
 \quad\ran(I\otimes\sigma)\subseteq\ran C_\N. \tag{13}
\]
Infeasibility means $M=\infty$. Its proposed new capability is the exact one-channel Choi optimization with matching sharpness, beyond the conditional identities and networks already in [6]. Its lift compares $\N$ with the erasure channel revealing its input with probability $\eta$. If valid, this is a computable entry point into Theorems 1--2.

\textbf{Retained conditional extension.} The preceding investigation derived that the same SDP also computes the exact complete, conditional-difference, and explicitly difference-defined generalized-information coefficients for every nonaffine regular modular Petz generator with finite $f(0+)$ and finite recession slope, every fixed Petz R\'enyi order $0<\alpha<1$, and quantum Chernoff divergence. Upper bounds follow by applying the two square orientations to different states, adding $L_\rho+sR_\sigma$, and integrating (11); erasure gives the affine reference term. N403's rectangular sharpness identity supplies the matching lower through rare coherent flags. The ordered limits are perturbation first, rare-flag weight second, rectangular optimization last. Full derivation and both focused reviews are preserved in the archive. This remains conditional on N402/N403, unlike Theorems 1--2.

\textbf{Decisive obstructions.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Universal simulation and common controls.} The exact classical pair in (8) already separates divergence order from physical postprocessing. Map an erasure flag to a fair bit using a common output readout: the comparator becomes a BSC of bias $a^2$, whereas the target remains bias $a$. Its SLD order reverses at the uniform input. Hence the order cannot be wired through arbitrary common adaptive controls. Retained memory is valid as an untouched reference in a one-use inequality; it need not suffer multiplicative decay.
\item \emph{New incapacity or activation mechanism.} Zhu--Wang v3 [2] already proves observable-shadow incapacity beyond PPT and antidegradability, and a fixed-channel activation theorem for every positive-private-capacity partner. The scalar self-coefficient does not compare a receiver with its own complement. Conditional on the Choi formula, both the qutrit identity and their zero-capacity channel have $\eta=1$; $\eta$ alone is not a positive-capacity test.
\item \emph{All-order or robust estimation claims.} At Petz order zero, depolarizing shrinkage $1/2$ has output support $\supp\rho_R\otimes\mathbb C^2$, so all three difference/equal-reference/information coefficients are zero, versus the conditional interior-order value $3/8$. Above one the common linear coefficient is unresolved; an exact order-ten counterexample excludes an all-order claim. Amplitude damping has complete KL coefficient one up to pure reset, where it becomes zero despite diamond convergence. This directly rules out a uniformly bounded-query estimator to additive error below $1/2$ without spectral/support promises.
\item \emph{Computational barriers.} A coherent joint output--environment scalarizing filter has candidate success $1/M$, but requires an unusually strong physical interface and gives no standard algorithmic speedup. Cross-corpus Gaussian, memory, broadcasting and sparse-noise links did not remove their acquisition or uniform-risk barriers. Supplied Choi matrices can be exponential in circuit size; neither (12) nor (13) is a polynomial-in-qubit-count algorithm.
\end{itemize}

\section*{5. Verification and highest-value next step}

\textbf{Verification.} The existing 16-worker Luna-max swarm was reused for distinct ordering, capacity, decision, adaptive, complexity, corpus and proof tasks. Only the concrete decoder-transfer candidate was escalated to two Sol-max workers. One Sol audit independently reconstructed and passed both centered transfer proofs, support extension, tensor step and the precise comparison to [1]; the other audited prior art and assessed the result as a useful quantitative corollary rather than a major advance. Separate Luna reconstructions support both transfer proofs and Theorem 2. Exact arithmetic checks verify the noncommuting block identity at six instances, the complete classical sharpness family for 99 rational parameters, and recovery Choi positivity/trace preservation and fidelity formulas for depolarizing, nonunital amplitude-damping and erasure channels. All six new replay scripts passed, in addition to the preceding eight. The NumPy qutrit Stinespring reconstruction is diagnostic, not an exact certificate. No general SDP solve, formal proof kernel, independent external specialist review or exhaustive historical-priority certification was obtained. Compilation checks document syntax only. Sharpness of the cross-channel bounds for general $k>2$ or quantum recovery remains unresolved.

\textbf{Next step.} Submit the short centered SLD-decoding proof and finite-$k$ secrecy refinement for specialist review against [1,3,4]; this is the strongest complete argument outside contraction found here. Separately, an external audit of N402/N403 remains the gate for its conditional coefficient claims. The highest-value foundational open question is whether complete unsmoothed SLD/Petz dominance admits a common signed lift without the known span assumption. Neither worker agreement nor a numerical SDP instance settles that question. The original ambition of a major foundational or computational breakthrough remains unmet.
