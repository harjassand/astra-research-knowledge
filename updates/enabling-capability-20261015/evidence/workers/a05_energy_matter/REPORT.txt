A05 ENERGY/MATTER — PULSED ELECTROCHEMISTRY AUDIT
2026-10-15

Outcome: HOLD for a new CO2-to-CO energy/control capability. STOP promoting
mean-current, mean-voltage or single-electrode-power gains as net efficiency.
No new physical operation or comparative advantage has been demonstrated.

This lane began with a consequential target: use nonlinear forcing to lower
the complete cost of producing CO at a matched useful product rate/purity.
It acquired open experimental data before theory. It did not revisit thermal,
snapshot, or attached-loop devices. The substantive output is an exact
conditional static-occupation comparator, a data-grounded reference-invariance
failure, and a replay of the strongest incumbent's actual full-cell waveforms.

Exact reference-invariance failure from source numbers

Treating the V2 power column as the reported electrode-power proxy, normalize
by CO-equivalent current. For the two 0.3 V RMS protocols:

    0.1 Hz: P/q_CO = 2.5441625 V, net I/q_CO = 2.5287376
    100 Hz: P/q_CO = 2.4715763 V, net I/q_CO = 3.0205888.

Adding a reference offset c to cathodic voltage magnitude changes the proxy
to P/q_CO+c*(net I/q_CO). The order reverses at c=0.1475775 V. At c=0.6562 V,
the paper's Ag/AgCl-to-RHE conversion magnitude for pH 7.8, the normalized
values are 4.2035201 V and 4.4536867 V. A real total-cell energy ranking cannot
depend on an arbitrary electrode-reference zero. This is a decisive failure
of the proposed promotion to full energy, not a claim that CO formation or
selectivity gains are fictitious. It is also not a full comparison of these
two unequal-throughput protocols; it diagnoses the metric itself.

The mathematical condition is exact: a single-electrode energy-per-CO
contrast is reference-invariant precisely when both protocols have equal
net-charge/CO ratios. Matching all faradaic product currents would fix that
ratio, but would still leave counter-electrode, separator, converter and
auxiliary costs unmeasured. CONSTRUCTION.txt gives the proof and a passive
unobserved-series-loss completion of the measured cathode trajectories.

Actual full-cell incumbent replay

The power-aware 2025 dataset records simultaneous current density and total
cell voltage for 25%-labeled modulation near 1 A/cm2. Time-weighted integration
over the source-cut intervals yields:

    mean current A/cm2       0.99976018         0.99969610
    mean power W/cm2         1.70726178         1.70698251
    product of means         1.69526557         1.69534146
    covariance W/cm2         0.01199621         0.01164105
    power missed by
      product of means       0.70763%           0.68665%

The standalone source steady-power label is 1.6947 W/cm2 with source standard
deviation 0.0025 W/cm2. The source's 25%-amplitude mean power summaries exceed
that label by 0.398–0.895% over all 22 supplied frequencies. At 50% they exceed
it by 1.131–2.072% over all 22 frequencies. At 10% the differences run from
-0.135% to +0.169%, with 11 mean values on each side; do not claim a resolved
increase throughout this small-amplitude group. Each source summary uses
three measurements. These are source-mean comparisons, not newly constructed
independent significance tests.

This dataset measures electrical input, not an independently acquired hydrogen
gas rate. Equal hydrogen production from equal mean current uses the source's
100% faradaic-efficiency assumption. It also does not measure full plant wall
energy. The 50 cm2 cell is held at 80 C, with 100 mL/min water flow and
200 mL/min hydrogen purge; heating, pumping, purge, converter and gas-handling
costs are outside the tabulated terminal power. Those missing costs cannot be
treated as zero.

Nontrivial construction and its limit

For a periodic one-state electrode model

    i=f(x)+C(x)*x',  u=x+R*i,

with product rates r(x), the exact arbitrary-waveform identity is

    <u*i> = <x*f(x)+R*f(x)^2> + R*<C(x)^2*x'^2>.

The first term is the cost of a static ensemble with exactly the same
distribution of electrode potentials, hence the same complete product-rate
vector. The remaining term is nonnegative. This excludes an energy benefit
over that static-occupation comparator if double-layer charge is the only
memory. It allows nonlinear kinetics and voltage-dependent capacitance, and
does not assume a convex single-product cost curve. The saved proof specifies
periodicity, integrability, admissible controls and finite-switch overhead.
The independent algebraic replay has residual -2.0e-16 for a nontrivial
nonlinear waveform; that is a mathematics check, not an experiment or proof
that the source electrode obeys the model.

This construction is related to established circuit/Jensen analysis. Its
selective-product comparator formulation has not undergone exhaustive prior-
art review, and no originality claim is made. Concentration, adsorption,
morphology, degradation and temperature memory can violate the model. A real
advantage would have to measure and exploit such a mechanism, then survive
an equally informed product-matched static/control comparator.

The public flow-cell static points provide a concrete finite comparator
calculation: pair enumeration constructs minimum-current mixtures at the
four pulse CO rates. Across two older static batches, six of eight constructed
mixtures use less net current than the corresponding pulse, and two use
slightly more. This cannot adjudicate pulse performance: dates, preparation,
gas flow, peak voltage and H2 output are unmatched. It does establish that
comparison only with a static point at the same nominal mean potential
leaves an obvious stronger comparator unresolved. The actual mixtures and
source coordinates are retained in RESULTS.json instead of being promoted
to physical superiority.

Full accounting and next decision

Acquisition: 11,059,712 bytes of publisher-verified experimental archives and
README files, plus 3,671,814 bytes of source PDFs. API metadata and extracted
copies add storage, documented by the saved files. Data are publicly acquired,
not generated or supplied by an imagined instrument. replay_audit.py ran in
4.514 s in the recorded run; this is local script wall time only. Inference
tokens, monetary cost and energy are UNKNOWN; no telemetry was provided.

Physical acquisition/preparation: source apparatus includes silver electrodes,
diamond polishing, sonication, electrolyte, pH and gas control, thermostat,
potentiostat, GC and either high-flow liquid pumping or a 2500 rpm rotor.
These operations were performed by the source researchers, not this worker.
Their capital, material lifetime, calibration, waste, reset, converter energy,
labor and total elapsed experimentation are not fully costed in the release.
The described flow trial holds last 40 min; RDE trials last 25 min. Those are
source protocol durations, not our elapsed work or independent replication.

Precision and errors: reference conversion uses bulk pH; source flow arithmetic
has a stated ideal-gas approximation. Raw GC calibration traces, complete
dynamic coproduct measurements, full-cell terminal work and a same-electrode
steady curve spanning the entire dynamic range are not jointly available for
the CO2 record. The 100 Hz export also leaves bandwidth/reduction unresolved.
Unresolved reproduction differences are preserved, not corrected by fitting.

Matched incumbent: a calibrated static/control policy that achieves the same
CO rate, coproduct/purity vector, feed, temperature, area, material state,
peak-control limits and lifetime, with measured terminal/wall power and all
transitions charged. Static occupancy provides a mathematical lower boundary;
finite switching is an implementation cost, not an assumed free operation.
Existing NFR and power-aware circuit analysis already supply serious theory.

Decision: HOLD this route as a new energy/matter capability. The missing
interface is a jointly calibrated product-and-total-power experiment with
same-electrode static controls. No further theory on the available single-
electrode proxy closes that interface. The existing null-model mechanism
cannot create an advantage over the static-occupation baseline, and existing
full-cell data show measurable pulse power penalties at the larger amplitudes.
This does not rule out selective CO2 chemistry using additional material or
concentration states. It rules out claiming that the present data demonstrate
a new net-energy enabling operation.
