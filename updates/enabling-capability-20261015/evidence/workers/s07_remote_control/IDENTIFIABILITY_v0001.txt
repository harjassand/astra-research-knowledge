Remote receiver control does not by itself identify ionospheric multipath
2026-10-15. Internally derived elementary structural countermodel, not a
novelty claim or a statement about the actual KFS receiver's impulse response.

Fix one stationary epoch. Let s be the emitted waveform, h the propagation
impulse response, g the unknown receiver impulse response, and q_u a known
downstream LTI digital filter/gain chosen by action u. The noiseless recording
is y_u = q_u * g * h * s, where * is convolution. Assume the admissible receiver
class contains both g and g*h.

World A has receiver g and multipath propagation h. World B has receiver
g' = g*h and single-path propagation h' = delta. Associativity gives exactly
y_u(A) = y_u(B) for every u and every source waveform s. Equal admissible noise
laws preserve equality in distribution. For an adaptive policy, the histories
are equal at the first observation and inductively yield the same next action
and next observation. Therefore no number of these downstream actions can
distinguish the two worlds. A concrete causal example is g=delta and
h=delta + (1/2)delta_D for D>0: World B puts the same echo in the instrument.

This is conditional on the receiver class admitting that echo. It does not
prove that the real KFS front end can create an arbitrary millisecond echo.
Independent engineering or calibration bounds that exclude such responses
break the countermodel. The public sample does not itself supply those bounds.
Nor does the countermodel exclude differential changes measured across epochs
when an independently justified stable receiver and bounded clock error are
assumed. Such a narrower experiment still needs current source identification,
waveform/version control, and receiver/time stability checks.

Absolute delay has the related ambiguity between instrument latency and
propagation latency. GNSS timestamps reduce clock uncertainty only to the
extent their mapping to the actual RF samples has been calibrated. The 61.5 ns
maximum residual in ACTUAL_IQ_AUDIT_v0001.json is consistency of transmitted
metadata with an affine sample clock; it is not 61.5 ns independently measured
absolute timing accuracy. AGC was on in the live smoke acquisition, so its raw
I/Q amplitude is not a calibrated incident-field amplitude either.

A legitimate candidate certificate first obtains an independently supported
receiver family Gcal and noise bound epsilon, and then excludes every model
q_u*g*(a delta_tau)*s with g in Gcal. If any such model fits within epsilon,
return UNKNOWN. Generalized likelihood/profile-likelihood and robust system
identification are strong incumbents for this task. This worker establishes
neither novelty nor an advantage of a set-membership formulation over them.
