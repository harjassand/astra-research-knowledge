LiF strongest-null audit: early model/interface checkpoint, 2026-10-15

Scientific target: decide whether the acquired XUV/NIR data require a discrete
dark excitonic coupling, rather than merely require a particular source fit.

Actual evidence: one same-scan raw polarization tensor [100 delays, 2 relative
polarizations, 10 camera repeats, 1340 pixels], on/off and separate background;
independent-day static LiF/membrane spectrum; separate stabilized-day helium
timing scan; four precomputed model output arrays. The exact source forward
TDSE/fitting solver is absent from the deposit. Existing output arrays cannot
be treated as refitted nulls. Nine polarization scans are public in metadata,
but only scan000 was acquired by the initial worker at this checkpoint.

Strongest physical alternative to audit: XUV-created bright coherences evolve
under diagonal, state-specific, tensor AC Stark phases and nonnegative
field-induced decoherence/ionization rates, with the independently calibrated
phonon response, convolution over unknown pulse structure and delay jitter,
and nuisance detector/background/reference transfer. Relative polarization
dependence is allowed by the excited alignment and the rank-four optical
response even in a polycrystalline sample: A+B*cos(theta)^2 does not itself
certify a resonant dark state. Pure scalar ponderomotive phase is a narrower
null. Broad causal continuum self-energy is a still stronger incumbent but
cannot be distinguished from an eliminated dark-state representation by the
bright absorption channel alone without independent restrictions.

Proposed executed diagnostic: refit a modest positive-spectrum bright-peak
deformation model (shift, broadening, oscillator-strength loss) to training
camera repeats, compare held-out raw-repeat spectral errors to refitted
source Stark and full-output templates under identical nuisance treatment.
This is an honest restricted comparator, not a solver replay or proof that it
spans the strongest physical null. Use 35--55 eV reference band; source script
first1025-pixel band reaches 59.53 eV and includes the 59.2 eV target, whereas
paper prose says35--55 eV. Fit background, edge mapping, baseline, and model
coefficients using training only. Global source-selected energies/time zero
and same-scan repeat dependence remain unavoidable leakage/selection limits.

Exact inference gate: a target certificate requires disjoint observable sets
after profiling ALL admissible null parameters and calibration uncertainty.
For finite data, reject only a stated constrained null by calibrated
held-out likelihood or robust confidence-set exclusion. Residual against one
ablation is not a model-independent dark-state certificate. New independent
quadrature plus NIR-frequency/phase control and matched pulse calibration can
shrink the sets; access to run those controls is not acquired.

Provisional status: GO for restricted model-falsification computation; HOLD
for target identification and any new enabling scientific capability.
