The first completed diagnostic run's random arm had a trajectory-order bug:
np.setdiff1d sorted its sampled continuation indices. Consequently its prefix
budgets were not uniform sequential random samples. All initial output files
remain preserved in this directory; the random-arm claims from those files are
invalid. GP/EI arms and prediction diagnostics are unaffected by this bug.

The corrected script draws the continuation in random order from the unobserved
pool. Corrected complete results are written into results_v2. No old results are
overwritten. The earlier parent checkpoint reported only GP prediction metrics,
which are unaffected. Code execution wall times are run-specific.

Also a one-off exploratory summary command failed on a spacing typo ('or81');
it produced no results and is not evidence. It is superseded by the saved audited
summary script. No data or labels were changed in either correction.
