2026-10-15 materials-output route checkpoint

Scientific target: identify a material with high magnetic coercivity at a specified
temperature, field geometry, sweep rate, and preparation history, with Kerr response
as a distinct optical functional output. Search performance on source Coer labels
is only a finite within-library result. Permanent magnet utility would additionally
need calibrated magnetization, hysteresis energy product, stability and bulk/process
validation, none of which are supplied here.

Acquired evidence: NIST REMI public MATLAB data, 289480 bytes, 921 unique
composition rows, 921 x 89 diffraction intensities over 42.6--47 deg (2 theta),
921 coercivity values [mT], 921 Kerr values [mrad]. Pinned REMI source commit
596b04435fb577175264a70c4434f5539b809510. See ACQUISITION_RECEIPT.json.
The dataset is actual measured, expert-cleaned source data, not independent truth.
No raw hysteresis curves, uncertainty fields, specimen repeats, phase labels,
instrument standard, exposure time, stage time or multiple synthesis batches exist
in the acquired MATLAB file. Composition column element order is not declared in
its public description; calculations retain C1,C2,C3 pending independent mapping.

Closest primary incumbents:
Wang et al. (2022), doi:10.1093/oxfmat/itac006, GP Expected Improvement wins
overall. CAMEO already phase maps then segments optimization; authors specifically
recommend replacing its UCB stage with EI on the rough coercivity target.
Iwasaki et al. (2020), doi:10.1080/14686996.2019.1707111, already combines
experimental XRD, NMF phase rates, KKR-CPA and weighted magnetic moments to map
Kerr response on this same Fe-Co-Ni source library. Thus diffraction-based phase
transport, phase-aware EI, and NMF property mixture are known operations.

Potential operation: establish whether an independently calibrated structural state
can transport useful target information across preparations and material systems.
First falsification: does this real diffraction channel improve target prediction
or observed-label search once given to an equally informed GP/EI comparator?
Second falsification: do the source data identify a preparation/intervention effect
or target calibration? The acquired single prepared library cannot do so by itself.

Decision now: HOLD as a new physical enabling operation. Continue the bounded
actual-data test to quantify whether phase/structure is useful here and to specify
the missing validation interface precisely. Do not interpret a finite benchmark
gain as novelty, new material discovery, transfer or physical control.
