Mechanism: write F(s)=product_i(1-p_i s). The full click-pattern law is determined
by F at finitely many subset-loss values s_j. For m distinct nonzero values,
F_epsilon(s)=F_0(s)+(-1)^(m+1)epsilon*s*product_j(s-s_j) agrees at every value.
Choose F_0 with m distinct roots >1. For small positive epsilon all old roots
remain >1 and a new positive root tends to infinity. Thus F_epsilon is again
the generating polynomial of strictly positive independent emitters, now m+1.

already known (Fiurasek, arXiv:2503.17480v2). No novelty claim.
