Four exact, internally proved obstructions/identities are saved with full
proofs in EXACT_RESULTS.txt:

1. The inverse-multiplicity KL projection and its actual entropy gain
   tensorize, but the variance/log^2|F| abundance correction vanishes on
   Cartesian powers. Strict gain is not a uniform constant improvement.

2. For a Boolean d-cube followed by alpha*2^d upper-chain members, with
   fixed alpha>0, the centered log-multiplicity variance is Theta(d^2),
   while every available entropy gain is O(d). This refutes a universal
   gain>=kappa*variance repair even with max frequency at most 1/2+epsilon
   for any prescribed epsilon>0. These frequencies are above one half;
   the construction is not a Frankl counterexample.

3. With an upper chain of length about 2^d*d^(3/4), the variance diverges
   but ALL uniform-input-marginal union couplings have gain at most
   log(2)+o(1). Thus no diverging lower bound on gain from variance alone
   can work. This family has maximum frequency tending to one.

4. The inverse-multiplicity matrix is indefinite already for a Boolean
   cube plus one extra top. A 2x2 minor has the sign of
   2^(d+1)+1-3^d<0 for d>=3, while max frequency tends to 1/2.
