INDEPENDENT SANITY CHECK OF a09'S FOUR-TRANSMISSION THEOREM

Reviewed workers/a09_photon_invention/MEASUREMENT_OPERATION_AND_PROOFS.txt
lines63-151. Verdict: the local identifiability proof is sound under its
stated ideal model. This is an analytic check, not a physical validation,
finite-sample estimator, novelty clearance or proof-assistant verification.

For a in{0,r,s,1},0<r<s<1, independence gives
  S_a(t)=exp[-D(t)-aB(t)] product_i(1-a p_i H(t)).
With the same D,B,H across transmissions, the observable
  J_a=log S_a-a log S_1-(1-a)log S_0
cancels both Poisson backgrounds exactly. Write
  A_a(x)=sum_{k>=2}c_k(a)m_k x^k,
  c_k(a)=(a-a^k)/k, m_k=sum_i p_i^k.
Positive finite emitter brightness gives c2(r)m2>0. The positive local
analytic square root of A_r has nonzero derivative, so two models with the
same paired curves are related by an analytic K=phi(H),phi'(0)=c>0.
The interval in the range of H is needed to turn observations into an
analytic identity; a finite set of time bins cannot replace this premise.

If d_n x^n,n>=2,is phi's first nonlinear term, only the quadratic term
generates its first correction at order n+1. Equality at r,s requires
  c_(n+1)(a)[m_(n+1)(p)-c^(n+1)m_(n+1)(q)]
       =2c2(a)m2(q)c d_n.
But c_k(a)/c2(a)=(2/k)sum_{j=0}^{k-2}a^j is strictly increasing for k>=3.
The two equations force d_n=0, a contradiction. Hence phi is linear and
all moments of order>=2 agree after scaling.

The rational functions sum_i p_i^2/(1-p_i x) and
sum_j(cq_j)^2/(1-cq_j x) are identical. Their positive poles and nonzero
residues recover each brightness and its multiplicity, including repeated
brightness. Thus the number of positive emitters agrees. Zero brightness
is invisible. The statement that the multiset is identified up to scale
is sufficient for count and does not overstate the conclusion.

For a polished statement, make p_i in(0,1] and locally finite B,D / positive
survival on the interval used explicit. These are ordinary meanings of the
stated probability/Poisson model, not a newly discovered mechanism failure.
Common H, independent emitters, linear calibrated physical attenuation,
isolated trials and reset, and genuinely acquired observations at all four
settings remain substantive premises. An arbitrary antibunched contaminant
does not belong to the Poisson nuisance class and can change biological
identity without being removed by the contrast.

There is no uniform finite-sample count guarantee without a positive lower
brightness/activity condition: an extra emitter of sufficiently small
detection probability changes any fixed-budget law by arbitrarily little.
The all-orders exact proof supplies no stable finite estimator or finite
cost advantage. a09's known-H first-arrival shot lower bound is explicitly
compared with an ideal coincidence policy on the same two hypotheses;

claimed. Known detector-decoy actions and full-data likelihood remain the
