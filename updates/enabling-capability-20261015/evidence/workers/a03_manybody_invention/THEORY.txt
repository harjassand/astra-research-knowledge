Connected local obstruction to certifying a global ground energy from source moments
2026-10-15. Internal mathematical result; no novelty or physical speedup claimed.

Purpose and exact information restriction

The proposed shortcut retains signed source moments P H^k P, source residuals,
or a finite Krylov transcript, and tries to infer a certified global ground
energy without acquiring a complementary-space lower bound or ground-overlap
guarantee. The following obstruction holds even for a connected, interacting,
particle-conserving nearest-neighbor fermion Hamiltonian with a unique ground
state and strictly positive source overlap. No absolute-value relaxation or
supplied exact spectral gap appears in the proof.

This is an information-interface theorem. It does NOT make two fully supplied
Hamiltonians indistinguishable: their remote onsite coefficients differ openly.
An algorithm allowed to inspect and reason about those coefficients can tell
them apart. The theorem identifies what a claimed compressed certificate must
add beyond the source transcript. It does not prove a general computational
lower bound for electronic structure, tensor networks, or local Hamiltonians.

Proposition 1: an explicit interacting family

Let N>=2, d>=1 and L>=2N+d-1. Work in the fixed-N sector of L spinless fermion
sites. Let n_j=c_j^*c_j, t>0, U>=0, epsilon>0, V>2U+4t+epsilon, and M>V.
Set t_N=epsilon and t_j=t at every other bond. Define

 H0 = -sum_{j=1}^{L-1} t_j(c_j^*c_{j+1}+c_{j+1}^*c_j)
      + U sum_{j=1}^{L-1} n_j n_{j+1} - V sum_{j=1}^N n_j,
 H1 = H0 - M sum_{j=N+d}^L n_j.

The source is the determinant psi with sites 1,...,N occupied. Its energy in
both models is a=-VN+U(N-1), and ||(Hb-a)psi||=epsilon for b=0,1. Put

 g=V-2U-4t-epsilon>0,
 delta=(sqrt(g^2+4epsilon^2)-g)/2
      =2epsilon^2/(sqrt(g^2+4epsilon^2)+g).

Then:

 (i) H0^k psi = H1^k psi for every integer 0<=k<=d.
 (ii) <psi,H0^k psi> = <psi,H1^k psi> for every 0<=k<=2d.
 (iii) a-delta <= E0(H0) <= a, while E0(H1)<=a-N(M-V).
 Consequently E0(H0)-E0(H1)>=N(M-V)-delta.
 (iv) Both determinant graphs are connected and both ground eigenvectors can
 be chosen strictly positive in the ordered occupation basis. In particular
 the source-ground overlap is nonzero in both models. No invariant dark block
 is being appended.

Proof of (i) and (ii)

The interaction and onsite terms are diagonal. A hopping application moves one
particle by one neighboring site. Starting from psi, at least d hopping
applications are needed before a particle can occupy site N+d or beyond.
Consequently the perturbation W=H1-H0 annihilates every H0^r psi for r<d.
Induction in H1^(r+1)psi=H1 H0^r psi then gives (i), including r+1=d.
For k<=2d write k=i+j with i,j<=d. Hermiticity gives

 <psi,Hb^k psi>=<Hb^i psi,Hb^j psi>.

Both vectors agree between the models, proving (ii). The argument also works
with centered powers Hb-aI. It is purely algebraic and allows exact replay.

Proof of (iii), including the independently obtained complement bound

Let R=sum_{j>N} n_j count particles outside the first N sites, and P=|psi><psi|.
In the fixed-N sector, R=0 occurs only on psi; thus R>=1 on Q=I-P.

The bulk hopping, with the weak N,N+1 bond deleted, preserves R. In the sector
R=r, the left N-site segment contains r holes. Its hopping energy is bounded
below by -2tr: the one-body hopping spectrum lies in [-2t,2t], its trace is
zero, and filling N-r orbitals equals the negative energy of r holes. The
right segment contains r particles and also has hopping energy >=-2tr.

Removing r particles from the completely occupied left segment breaks at
most 2r occupied nearest-neighbor pairs. All other density interactions are
nonnegative because U>=0. Hence the interaction energy is at least
U(N-1)-2Ur. The left well contributes -VN+Vr. Therefore, after deleting the
weak bond, H0-a >= (V-2U-4t)R as an operator form inequality.

The omitted weak-bond hopping operator has norm epsilon. Thus

 Q(H0-a)Q >= (V-2U-4t-epsilon)Q = g Q.

This is a coefficient-derived form bound, NOT a supplied exact spectral gap.
Only the hop of the particle at site N to site N+1 acts nontrivially on psi,
so ||QH0psi||=epsilon and PHP=aP. For x=alpha psi+q, q perpendicular to psi,

 <x,(H0-a)x> >= g||q||^2 - 2epsilon |alpha| ||q||
                >= -delta (|alpha|^2+||q||^2).

The last inequality is the explicit lowest eigenvalue of the 2x2 real matrix
[[0,-epsilon],[-epsilon,g]]. This proves the H0 lower bound. The source gives
its upper bound. In H1 use the trial determinant occupying N consecutive
remote sites N+d,...,2N+d-1. Its expectation is -MN+U(N-1)=a-N(M-V), proving
the H1 upper bound and energy separation.

Proof of (iv)

In the ordered occupation basis, nearest-neighbor hopping has matrix element
-t_j: there are no occupied sites strictly between the two hopping sites.
The N-particle exclusion configuration graph of a path is connected whenever
all t_j>0. Shift -Hb by a sufficiently large scalar identity to obtain an
irreducible nonnegative matrix. Perron-Frobenius gives a unique positive
ground eigenvector of Hb, with a positive coefficient on every determinant.

Scaling and physical content

Take L=3N, d=N+1, t=U=1, V=10, M=11, epsilon=1/16. The Hilbert dimension is
binomial(3N,N), all term norms remain bounded independently of N, and the
interaction U n_j n_{j+1} is nonzero. The first 2N+2 scalar source moments
agree while ground energies differ by at least N-delta, where delta<0.001.
The first N+1 full Krylov vectors also agree. This is an interacting local
model, not a free one-particle Hamiltonian dressed with disconnected states.
It is deliberately a theoretical countermodel, not a substitute molecular
benchmark. The conclusion already holds without a fermionic sign problem.

Exact finite replay

For N=4,L=12,d=5, the fixed-number dimension is 495 and a=-37. The script
local_moment_counterexample.py uses integer arithmetic on 16*(Hb-aI).
Every scalar moment through degree 10 agrees exactly. Krylov vectors through
degree 5 agree exactly. At degree 11 the scalar difference is -11/256; this
is the shortest path to the first remote site, one onsite perturbation, and
the return path. H0 is in [a-delta,a] with delta approximately 0.000991814;
H1 has trial energy at most -41. The approximate eigenvalue/overlap values
in LOCAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE.json are diagnostics only and are not used in proof.
In particular, a tiny floating-point overlap is not a certified overlap value.

Corollary: bounded source information cannot certify this family uniformly

An algorithm receiving only the source transcript in (i) or (ii), together
with the shared source energy and residual, receives identical inputs for
both models. A valid common energy interval must have width at least
N(M-V)-delta. A lower endpoint valid for H1 cannot give an accurate two-sided
energy interval for H0. Knowing connectivity, a fixed particle number,
nonzero interaction, and a nonzero source-ground overlap does not repair this.
One must acquire more information: sufficient depth, a quantitative overlap
bound, an independently valid low-energy complement bound, or another global
coverage mechanism. The theorem does not say these are impossible to acquire.

Proposition 2: weak coupling also creates a finite-time precision barrier

Suppose H_b=[[A,B],[B^*,C_b]] relative to P and Q, with the same A and B in
both models and ||B||<=epsilon. Let K_b(t)=P exp(-it H_b)P. For real t,

 ||K_b(t)-exp(-it A)|| <= epsilon^2 t^2/2,
 ||K_0(t)-K_1(t)|| <= epsilon^2 t^2.

To prove it, solve the Q component by variation of constants and substitute
it into the P equation. The resulting double time integral has two B factors,
unitary propagators for A and C_b, and P exp(-iuH_b)P of norm at most one.
The integration triangle has area t^2/2. The second inequality follows by the
triangle inequality. Our family has rank-one P and ||B||=epsilon exactly.

Thus at a fixed observation horizon T a source survival-amplitude interface
with uniform error larger than epsilon^2 T^2 cannot separate all such tails.
T>=sqrt(eta)/epsilon is a necessary condition for a signal difference of size
eta, not a sufficient identification guarantee. This statement concerns
unitary source data; it does not silently assume free imaginary-time filtering,
postselection, or state preparation.

The randomized escape, and why this is not a universal no-go

Let C be any fixed real symmetric matrix and G contain r independent
Rademacher columns in its full D-dimensional space. For a fixed unit ground
eigenvector u, Z=u^T g has E Z^2=1 and E Z^4<=3. Paley-Zygmund yields

 Pr(|Z|>=1/4) >= (1-1/16)^2/3 = 75/256.

Therefore, except with probability at most (181/256)^r, ||u^T G||_2>=1/4.
On this single event, for every degree m and every computed upper norm bound
Y_m>=||T_m(C)G||_F, a ground eigenvalue lambda<-1 obeys

 T_m(|lambda|) <= 4Y_m.

Since T_m is increasing on [1,infinity), this bounds lambda from below.
If lambda>=-1 the same lower bound is automatic. No spectral gap is needed.
The event holds simultaneously for all degrees, so adaptive lower endpoints
do not require a union bound. Using the full-vector random interface removes
the near-dark source problem probabilistically; its acquisition and memory
cost scale with the actual determinant dimension.

Arithmetic in randomized_signed_certificate.py

The script sets c=(b+u)/2 and s=(b-u)/2 from a Gershgorin upper b and checked
variational upper u, constructs the stored floating matrix C=fl(H/s-c/s I),
and uses ||H-sC-cI||<=gamma_4(||H||_infinity+|c|) plus underflow allowance.
All claimed intervals are for the exact real matrix of saved H entries.
The FCIDUMP-to-H bridge and physical model error are separate obligations.

For the computed Chebyshev recurrence, local dot/subtraction residual norms
are bounded by standard gamma_n estimates. If alpha bounds ||C||, the global
recurrence error at step m is at most

 e_1 U_{m-1}(alpha) + (m-1) delta_max U_{m-2}(alpha).

This follows by solving the forced Chebyshev recurrence; max_{|x|<=alpha}
|U_j(x)|<=U_j(alpha) for alpha>=1. Positive-coefficient expansions and exact
Fraction arithmetic bound U_j and invert a 12-term positive lower polynomial
for T_m(1+x). No unverified transcendental root solver is used. Each improved
lower bound refines alpha for the next check on the same random event.

For r=48 the failure bound is (181/256)^48, about 5.9e-8. The implementation
records local OS random bits; independence and unbiasedness remain explicit
probabilistic assumptions, and a hash does not establish them. Floating-point
guarantees also retain the documented arithmetic assumptions. This is a
conditional probabilistic certificate, not a deterministic one or a proof
of physical molecular accuracy.

Prior-art boundary

The source-overlap obstruction and randomized polynomial escape are old in
general numerical linear algebra. The primary SIAM 2000 paper by van
Dorsselaer, Hochstenbach and van der Vorst explicitly develops probabilistic
Lanczos, Ritz and Chebyshev bounds without supplied spectral separation and
notes that small Ritz residuals do not establish extremality. Its analysis
does not include rounding error. Dixon (1983) is earlier probabilistic norm
and eigenvalue-estimation prior art. Our explicit connected interacting
specialization is an internally proved failure witness; independent novelty
has not been established. No new many-body operation is claimed.
