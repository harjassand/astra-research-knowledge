Measurement/inverse-problem initial investigation — 2026-10-15
Status: HOLD for a qualifying new enabling capability. GO for reproducible data access and targeted model falsification. No novelty or comparative advantage established.

Selected lead: controlled dark-state spectroscopy in LiF.
Desired operation: determine which otherwise dark electronic couplings are required by observations, with a falsifiable uncertainty set, rather than merely selecting a visually plausible few-level fit. Such an operation could support discovery across solid-state excitons, molecular photochemistry, and correlated electronic systems. The currently available operation of polarization-dependent dark-state detection is already published; it is not our invention.

Actual access acquired: Zenodo 14783448. Twelve source files totaling 16,048,312 bytes, each matching the repository MD5, include one 10,790,240-byte polarization scan, a 3,490,984-byte helium calibration scan, static sample/membrane data, processing scripts, and four model-ablation arrays. Receipt: DATA_ACQUISITION_RECEIPT.json. Acquisition wall time measured by our script: 66.823877584 s; this includes serial network requests and file writes, not model inference time. Model tokens, inference time, hardware energy, instrument costs, and new experimental access are unavailable.

The acquired polarization tensor is raw uint16 camera values with shape [100 delay positions, 2 waveplate positions, 10 repeats, 1340 pixels] separately for dressing-on and dressing-off; backgrounds have [10,1340]. Its delay and wavelength calibration are hardcoded in the processing script, rather than embedded as full axis metadata. Waveplate 0 and 45 degrees give parallel and perpendicular fields. The helium scan is from a different, temporally stabilized experiment; it is not a simultaneous pulse-field measurement for the polarization scan. One-scan inspection with direct SVD edge referencing (not a bitwise source replay) finds the 62.5 eV, near-zero-delay feature at 53.05 versus 4.44 mOD after background/reference subtraction. This is a successful access check of an already reported feature, not independent discovery, full replication, or a mechanism certificate.

Strongest equally informed incumbents: source seven-level TDSE/few-level fit with phenomenological AC Stark and phonon phases, bright-state calibration from linear absorption, and DFT/BSE orbital calculations; direct temporal dipole reconstruction (PRL 121,173005, 2018); complex ATAS/CATS quadrature measurement (Optica 10,142, 2023); polarization-selective cross-peak TA (PNAS 119, 2022); and two-NIR-pulse XUV FWM separating full-cycle resonant response from half-cycle Stark response (Photonics Research, DOI 10.1364/PRJ.588447, 2026). Reproducing any of these operations is not a new capability.

Decisive gates: the archive separates stable subcycle timing from polarization control. The source explicitly does not infer whether the subcycle oscillations disappear with crossed polarization. Its pure-Stark model array is an ablation of the fitted full model, not an independently optimized composite null. A fair new test must refit admissible no-resonant-transfer alternatives with the same calibration, noise, pulse-shape, and spectral-response information. Finite-band absorption does not automatically provide the complex response needed to distinguish phase modulation from amplitude transfer. The source itself treats the 2.4 fs fitted coherence lifetime as an approximate upper bound because pulse structure limits it.

Narrow invention opening: a finite-band, nuisance-calibrated certificate that excludes all physically specified instantaneous-phase alternatives, plus an experiment-selection rule that identifies the missing independent quadrature/frequency control. This opening is not novelty-cleared, and could reduce to existing causal response reconstruction, model discrimination, or optimal experimental design. Do not promote it without a decisive matched benchmark. A new experimental validation would need simultaneous stabilized polarization and phase-sensitive response, intensity/frequency sweeps, independent pulse calibration, and a held-out material or control setting. Access to run that experiment is not established.

Other lead 1: single-molecule FRET kinetics.
Actual access: Zenodo 5701310 metadata inspected and exp_dataset1_Fig5.zip acquired (385,895 bytes, matching repository MD5). It contains experimental time/donor/acceptor traces already background corrected and truncated before bleaching. Strongest comparator must include detector-aware Poisson/Gaussian or integrative-detector Bayesian nonparametric kinetics, not just a historical Gaussian HMM. The 2022 multi-lab benchmark has known synthetic truth but experimental kinetic ground truth is not supplied; the 2024 critique and reply establish regime-dependent detector assumptions. Broad low-photon kinetic inference is already active prior art. HOLD absent a new controlled intervention or independently validated kinetic target; processed camera intensities are not photon arrival data.

Other lead 2: ptychographic X-ray tomography.
Explicit downloadable raw archive verified: Zenodo 3702583 files.zip, 16,127,523,646 bytes. Its small README was acquired (2,790 bytes, matching MD5). It describes 174 projections, each with 13,689 diffraction frames of 128x128 pixels, positions, masks, geometry, and example reconstructions. Strongest comparator includes least-squares maximum likelihood, multimode/multislice, scan-position correction, and Bayesian parameter tuning. The existing BO literature warns that reconstruction precision/FRC need not mean structural accuracy. No independent structural ground truth or new beamtime/control access was acquired. HOLD for a new capability; archive reconstruction alone would be another algorithm benchmark.

Recommendation to principal: prioritize LiF only if a small formal countermodel exercise and a finite-band calibration test uncover a mechanism certificate beyond the incumbents. Otherwise STOP this candidate operation and preserve these acquired datasets as concrete interface evidence. Do not increase the worker wave for generic reconstruction improvements.
