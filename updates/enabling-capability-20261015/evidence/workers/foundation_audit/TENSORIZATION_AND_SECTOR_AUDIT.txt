INDEPENDENT AUDIT: GROUPED REFERENCE, TENSORIZATION, AND SECTOR SCOPE
2026-10-15; internal research result, no formal verification or full endpoint claim.

1. Exact definitions and conventions

For each local dimension d, Λ_d(X)=Tr(X)I-X/2, with ordinary unnormalized
partial trace. For a two-site block Φ=Λ_p⊗Λ_q and any nonzero reference u,
A=Φ(uu*), L_a=Φ(|a><u|), M_u(E_ab)=L_a A^{-1} L_b*,
C=Σ_ab E_ba⊗Φ(E_ab), S=Σ_ab E_ba⊗M_u(E_ab).
Indices are lexicographic input index followed by lexicographic output index.
The map M_u is completely positive. The co-Choi matrix S need not be PSD.
The co-Choi matrix C is positive definite: after a factor permutation it is
(I-F_p/2)⊗(I-F_q/2), with minimum eigenvalue 1/4.
A is positive definite because A=(<u|⊗I) C (|u>⊗I) >= ||u||² I/4.
Scaling u by any nonzero complex scalar leaves M_u and S unchanged.

A useful exact identity, requiring only the displayed definitions, is
(C-S)(u⊗I)=0. Indeed, its block b equals
Σ_a u_a Φ(E_ab) - (Σ_a u_a L_a) A^{-1}L_b*
=L_b* - A A^{-1}L_b*=0.
It forces generalized eigenvalue 1; it does not prove C-S>=0 on the
orthogonal complement. Normalized numerical maxima near 1 are therefore
not evidence of a strict spectral gap.

Local unitary covariance covers all complex references. If V=V_A⊗V_B,
M_{Vu}(X)=V M_u(V*XV)V*, and
S(Vu)=(V⊗V)S(u)(V*⊗V*).
The same covariance fixes C. Thus every complex u is equivalent to a real
nonnegative Schmidt vector Σ_i s_i|ii>, including rectangular spaces and
zero Schmidt coefficients. This is a reduction, not a universal bound.
For ||u||=1, r_i=s_i², A on |ij>, i!=j, is the scalar
1-(r_i+r_j)/2. On the span of |ii>, A=diag(1-r_i)+ss*/4.
For nonproduct u, all 1-r_i>0 and this inverse is exactly
 diag(1/(1-r_i)) - tt*/(4+Σ_i r_i/(1-r_i)), t_i=s_i/(1-r_i).
The product limit is handled separately; this formula must not divide by
zero at r_i=1.

2. Bell obstruction, verified over exact rationals

Take p=q=2 and u=(1,0,0,1), whose normalization does not affect S.
The normalized reference would instead give A=(1/2)I+(1/4)|Bell><Bell|.
For w=(3,0,0,1,0,0,2,0,0,2,0,0,1,0,0,3), independent exact code gives
 ||w||²=28, w*Cw=9, w*Sw=-11, w*(C+S)w=-2.
Hence the proposed lower domination S>=-C is false. The complete spectrum
of C^{-1/2}SC^{-1/2} is
 -11/9 with multiplicity 1;
 -1/9 with multiplicity 3;
 1/3 with multiplicity 5;
 1 with multiplicity 7.
For this one reference, C-S>=0 and S+(11/9)C>=0 are exact PSD facts.
This is neither a proof for arbitrary Schmidt coefficients nor a
counterexample to S>=-3C. It is not a Werner-distillation counterexample.
The rational witness is a vector on the full co-Choi space, and need not
be a product test vector of the positive-map inequality.

3. Single-site spectrum reconstructed directly

Set u=|0>, P=|0><0|, Q=I-P, A0=I-P/2, and
ell_a=δ_a0 I-|a><0|/2. Let C0=I-F/2 and
S0=Σ_ab E_ba⊗ell_a A0^{-1}ell_b*.
Both matrices reduce the following spaces:
 - span{|00>}: C0=S0=1/2;
 - span{|0a>,|a0>}, a>0:
   C0=[[1,-1/2],[-1/2,1]], S0=[[1,-1/2],[-1/2,0]];
 - Q⊗Q: C0=I-F_Q/2, S0=F_Q/2.
In each two-dimensional block,
 det(S0-λC0)=-(1-λ)(3λ+1)/4.
On the Q⊗Q swap eigenspaces, the generalized eigenvalues are 1 and -1/3.
Consequently R0=C0^{-1/2}S0C0^{-1/2} has exactly
 1, multiplicity d(d+1)/2;
 -1/3, multiplicity d(d-1)/2.
For d=1 only eigenvalue 1 occurs. The proof handles arbitrary complex
single-site references by unitary covariance.

4. Exact conditional tensorization proposition

Assume, for a chosen two-site reference u12, the FULL Loewner bounds
 -3C12 <= S12 <= C12.                 [UNPROVED IN GENERAL]
Take any number m>=1 of additional single-site product reference vectors,
and put u=u12⊗u3⊗...⊗u_{m+2}. Let Φtot=⊗_{i=1}^{m+2}Λ_{d_i}.
Then A_tot and M_tot factor across this block partition. After a factor
permutation the normalized co-Choi matrix of M_tot is
 R12⊗R3⊗...⊗R_{m+2}.
Each Rj for j>=3 has eigenvalues 1 or -1/3. Thus the remaining product
has eigenvalues (-1/3)^r, 0<=r<=m; its largest eigenvalue is 1 and its most
negative eigenvalue is -1/3 if any additional dimension is at least 2.
For λ in [-3,1], λ(-1/3)^r<=1 for every r. Therefore
 coChoi(Φtot-M_tot)>=0,
so Φtot-M_tot is completely copositive and in particular positive.
Applying it to |x><x| gives the switched Schur inequality
 Φtot(|x><x|)-Φtot(|x><u|) A_tot^{-1} Φtot(|u><x|)>=0
for every x.

The lower constant 3 is exactly the threshold for THIS co-Choi tensor
argument when an extra site has dimension at least 2: an eigenvalue
λ<-3 would multiply the local eigenvalue -1/3 to exceed 1. Likewise
λ>1 fails against the product eigenvalue 1. No lower restriction is
needed for m=0. With two entangled two-site blocks, bounds [-3,1] do not
suffice: two negative eigenvalues may have product exceeding 1. This
argument proves no general multiblock or arbitrary-reference closure.

For any k, define q_k(K)=Σ_{J⊆[k]}(-1/2)^{|J|}||Tr_J K||²_HS.
If rank K<=2 and range K contains u, write
 K=|u><v|+|x><y|.
Put A=Φtot(uu*), D=Φtot(xx*), L=Φtot(xu*). The swap contraction gives
 q_k(K)=a+d+2 Re g,
 a=<v,Av>, d=<y,Dy>, g=<y,Lv>.
Cauchy-Schwarz and the switched Schur inequality give
 |g|² <= a <y,LA^{-1}L*y> <= ad,
so q_k(K)>=(sqrt(a)-sqrt(d))²>=0.
The statement also holds if range K* contains the reference, because
q_k(K*)=q_k(K). At k=3 this is the proposed biseparable-support-ray
sector. It remains conditional on the grouped bound for the chosen ray.
For a fully product ray the single-site argument already proves this
for every k, without the grouped conjecture. Extending the incumbent's
three-site product proof to arbitrary k is immediate from its tensor
mechanism and should not be advertised as a new difficult theorem.

5. Exact separation from the cited individual three-site sectors

In (C^3)^⊗3 use the following unnormalized mutually orthogonal vectors:
 u=|000>+|011>+|022>, x=|101>+|212>,
 v=|100>+|111>+|122>, y=|002>+|210>,
and K=|u><v|+|x><y|.
Both support planes contain a biseparable ray (u or v). Each plane has
NO fully product ray: projection of αu+βx onto first-site |0> gives
α(|00>+|11>+|22>), forcing α=0 for a fully product vector; x itself has
Schmidt rank 2 across first site versus the last two. For the input plane,
project onto first-site |1> and apply the same argument to v,y.
All six minimal one-site support projections are I_3, so their pairwise
output-input overlaps equal 3 and the <=2 criterion fails at all sites.
rank K=2, K²=0, K is nonnormal, and the combined support has dimension 4.
All three one-site partial traces are nonzero. Neither support can even
be CONTAINED in a larger locally orthogonal tripartite product code:
in such a code the first-site Schmidt rank of a superposition equals
its number of nonzero codeword coefficients. A first-site product ray
would therefore be one fully product codeword, whereas u and v have
first-site Schmidt rank one and are entangled on the last two sites.
Exact evaluation gives q_3(K)=21/2>=0.
Thus the proposed general biseparable-ray hypothesis would strictly
extend the product-ray hypothesis and cover operators outside the
incumbent's separately stated normal/common-plane/local-overlap/
local-traceless/product-code criteria. The one explicit positive example
does NOT prove the whole sector, and absence from those stated criteria
does NOT establish global literature novelty.
