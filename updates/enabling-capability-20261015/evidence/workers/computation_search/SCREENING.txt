Physically grounded scientific computation: initial screening, 2026-10-15

Mission scope: a mathematical/computational operation whose output is a scientific quantity on a real system. Computational model truth is allowed but must not be called experimental truth. No thermal, snapshot, or manufacturing construction is reused.

Lead A -- molecular electronic energy certification (selected for access test).
Target: a two-sided interval for the ground electronic energy of an actual molecule, in a stated finite orbital basis and electronic sector. Candidate operation: bound the omitted determinant block by a verified positive comparison matrix, then use a Schur-complement lower bound alongside the variational upper bound. A local test must report whether determinant-space acquisition and certification erase any solve-time saving.
Benchmark access: downloadable FCIDUMP integrals from a primary quantum-chemistry software test/benchmark repository, with the exact geometry, basis, core and spin conventions retrieved. Primary official PySCF examples also permit regenerating and checking integrals. No synthetic Hamiltonian may be substituted silently.
Incumbents: PySCF direct FCI/Davidson on the same integrals; for selected spaces, CIPSI/SHCI plus PT2/extrapolation and standard residual/Temple-type bounds. The incumbent receives the same selected determinants, integrals, prior calculations, and preprocessing.
Current access status: an Apple Silicon PySCF 2.14.0 wheel exists on PyPI; local installation and actual integrals are not yet acquired. No cost or advantage is claimed.
Prior art: selected CI is established, as are Schur/Feshbach elimination, variational upper bounds, Temple/Weinstein bounds, and eigenvector continuation. Novelty UNKNOWN. The experiment is a search for a useful composition or a decisive failure.
Error ledger: finite basis/model error; molecular integral precision/truncation; determinant-space and sector correctness; selected-space error; numerical solver error; certificate rounding; omitted physical corrections. A certificate for the printed FCIDUMP does not certify the true molecule.

Lead B -- molecular force field and rare-event propagation (HOLD).
Target: forces, then a scientifically meaningful trajectory/path observable on real molecular geometries. The sGDML site provides MD17 and CCSD(T) force datasets and an executable incumbent. However held-out force MAE is not itself a rare-event rate or stable trajectory certificate. Download/training/validation and missing tail exposure would have to be charged before claiming scientific capability. Do not quietly substitute force MAE for kinetics.

Lead C -- experimental X-ray inverse problem (HOLD).
Target: a reconstruction or target functional with a checked dual/residual interval using real raw tomography. TomoBank is a primary repository of experimental and simulated data with scripts and a TomoPy incumbent. Experimental data access is via Globus; actual size, downloadability, calibration and independent target truth need verification. Do not substitute a simulated phantom without explicitly reopening scope.

Initial choice: A is the strongest immediately testable route because the physical model is explicit and a matched FCI answer is locally obtainable at modest basis size. This is not evidence that A has the largest scientific upside or a novel algorithm.
