Assay-access result, 2026-10-15

Decision

GO for a public, experimentally measured, reduced test of standards-free
regioisomer RATIO estimation. The Kozlowski proof-of-concept data and a separate
NMR mixture benchmark have actually been acquired. HOLD for an absolute,
structure-specific yield operation on Cernak's CF3-containing nanomole wells.
No public substitute dataset establishes that transfer. No new scientific
operation, novelty, or advantage is claimed.

The target is Y_j = n_j / n_limiting_initial for a specified product structure j.
It is not spectral reconstruction error and is not the fraction of detector
signal attributed to component j. A relative composition n_j/sum_i n_i also
does not by itself establish absolute yield.

A. Same-mixture isotope ratio check (Kozlowski SI S35, Fig S6 and Table S1).

   Measured mean loop vector M+1,...,M+5:
   [9.18, 59.18, 100.00, 46.41, 6.32], base-peak normalized.
   Triplicate measured vectors are retained in the output JSON.
   The NMR-derived fingerprint matrix has rank 3.

   Replayed source OLS with an intercept gives fractions
   [20.0449, 33.7395, 46.2156]%, agreeing with the printed
   [20.1, 33.7, 46.2]%. The independently calibrated UV reference is
   [20.8, 42.7, 36.5]%. Maximum absolute fraction error is 9.7 percentage points.

   The source's MS response factors for the three deutero regioisomers are
   [1.16 +/- .02, 1.00, 1.54 +/- .07]. Those were measured using purified
   targets whose relative concentrations were determined by qNMR. An
   inverse-response correction illustration applied to the printed OLS
   fractions gives [21.3848, 41.5908, 37.0244]%, max error 1.1092 points.
   This is one posthoc numerical illustration, not a newly validated assay.
   The source says that including correction factors did not improve agreement
   globally. No general improvement claim overrides that statement.

   Identifiability check: known isotope fingerprints P and observations
   y = P diag(rho) n identify response-weighted amounts rho_j n_j when P has
   full column rank. For any positive q, choosing rho'_j = rho_j n_j/q_j gives
   the identical observations with composition q. The script exhibits two
   different compositions and positive responses with observation difference
   below 1e-14. These are mathematical countermodels, not new measurements.
   A known total concentration does not eliminate all relative-response
   ambiguity. Extra time points or wavelengths only help when they constrain
   these responses or product identities independently.

C. Published calibration-free HPLC endpoint audit.

   Recomputing the 60 archived real-reaction yield pairs gives MAE 7.77498 pp,
   RMSE 12.39126 pp, and maximum error 44.50937 pp. Two manual reference yields
   exceed 100%; they were retained without clipping. The paper quotes
   8.3%/12.8%; that mismatch with the archived table is exposed, not rounded
   away. This is an audit of source predictions, not a replay of the ML model.

   The reported main model was trained on proprietary Reaxys data (38k
   measurements); the public archive supplies the Deep4Chem model. Therefore
   the supplied public model must not be called the exact published incumbent.
   The paper explicitly states that the method cannot differentiate isomers or
   isobars, and used a reference elution profile for two amination isomers in
   Reaction 2. Severe coelution and similar UV spectra defeat its MCR assignment.

Reference integrity limits found by inspecting raw NMR metadata

SI S106/S108/S110 report independent-internal-standard yields for protio
validation mixtures: A1 [14,16,15]%, B2 [31,20,17]%, C3 [16,19,37]%. Initial
reaction scales are 250umol, 1mmol, and 1mmol. These are source-reported yields,
not freshly reproduced truth. The archived crude FIDs are 400.172MHz while
figure captions say 600MHz. Their metadata gives D1=1s for A1 and 20s for B2/C3,
8 scans, zg30. The crude folders contain processing metadata but no processed
1r arrays. No T1 measurements or reconciliation of the frequency discrepancy
was found. Therefore raw-to-published-reference mapping and quantitative
relaxation sufficiency remain HOLD. No isolated-yield number is substituted for
the qNMR reaction yield: purification recoveries differ substantially.

The frequency discrepancy could be a caption error or a different deposited
acquisition; present evidence does not select between them. It is not repaired
by asserting that both are NMR. D1=1s alone also does not prove invalid qNMR:
the archived acquisition time is approximately 3.998s and the pulse is 30deg.
For a steady-state single-pulse experiment, recovery relative to full relaxation
is (1-exp(-TR/T1))/(1-cos(30deg)*exp(-TR/T1)). At minimum TR approximately 4.998s,
this factor is 0.9991 for hypothetical T1=1s but 0.8287 for T1=10s. Unequal target
and internal-standard T1 values could therefore bias ratios. These examples are
model sensitivity calculations, not measured relaxation times. B2/C3's longer
delay reduces that concern but does not supply a measured T1 bound.

Strongest incumbents and complete-cost boundary

For the actual Cernak target, authentic-product NMR assignment plus calibrated
multiwavelength LC-UV unmixing is already published. A fair comparison must
include that method, and direct 19F qNMR if target/side product signals can be
assigned, resolved and measured with adequate sensitivity at the actual well
scale. The present work did not obtain those samples or raw Cernak spectra.

Partial-isotope coding is published prior art. Its 0.3min column-bypass run is
not its entire cost: substrate deuteration, site-specific NMR characterization,
reaction reruns, KIE/label-scrambling checks, dilution, detector optimization,
and response/total-scale calibration must be charged. It is not a postprocessing
operation that can be applied to already unlabelled Cernak observations.

Magnetstein and normal qNMR are published incumbents. Source Exp10 acquisition
uses 256 transients, each with 3.28s acquisition and 16.72s delay: nominally
5120s per spectrum before preparation or instrument overhead. Reference
library spectra and complementary high-field truth acquisition add cost.
These costs cannot be hidden behind the speed of an NNLS solve. The code's
recorded NNLS wall seconds are only local numerical inference time.

McDonald's method uses an 8min LC run, sample dilution/filtration, PDA and MS,
known expected product structure, an extinction model/training set, and an
internal standard to reduce handling/injection error. Its public reduced
benchmark does not resolve the coeluting-isomer identity requirement.

Inference tokens, monetary inference cost, and inference energy are UNKNOWN:
no actual telemetry is available to this worker. Download elapsed times where
recorded are acquisition timings, not inference timings or physical assay cost.

Can a no-purified-target-standards operation be decisively tested?

Yes, at restricted scope: withhold the target-library spectra, reference
calibrations, reference yields and truth labels from a candidate, then compare
its structure-specific amounts against an independently obtained validator.
The validator may use purified targets; that does not give the candidate those
standards. Public data here allow a retrospective ratio test and a separate
HPLC yield test. They are useful falsification interfaces, not a blinded new
validation or proof of transfer. The source isotope method already fails exact
mole-ratio recovery on its own measured example despite identifiable patterns.

For a decisive Cernak result, acquire actual raw multichannel observations for
those target/side-product mixtures; independent structure assignments; an
independent absolute amount scale and uncertainty; and a dilution/matrix-
matched set with independently varied target, regioisomer and starting material.
Hold target spectra/calibration out of the candidate but supply them to the
strong incumbent. Report component-specific yield error and decision errors,
including near-zero target / large side-product cases, rather than spectral MSE.
Neither this worker nor another public mixture archive supplies that missing
experiment. No hardware access or physical validation is implied.

Minimum repair for each interface's OWN target

Kozlowski proof-of-concept ratios: a bounded retrospective test is already
possible using the printed triplicate channel observations and independently
calibrated UV ratios. A full raw replay additionally needs conversion of the
acquired Waters files to a readable exchange format, the exact peak-combination
mapping, and the raw qNMR/calibration runs supporting reference ratios and their
uncertainty. The large-scale protio crude yield endpoint needs sample-ID
reconciliation of 400/600MHz, validated relaxation/baseline/integration settings,
and exact input/internal-standard amounts. Protio and deutero mixtures must not
be equated because of KIE. A corrected caption alone would not establish qNMR
quantitative validity.

Magnetstein Exp10 relative composition: four actual low-field observation arrays
and separate high-field reference fractions are acquired. For a stronger raw
validation, obtain the corresponding low-field and high-field FIDs, reference
integration windows and reference uncertainty. Withhold measured pure-target
library spectra from a standards-free candidate. Supply that library to the
incumbent. Absolute target yield would need an independently known total amount
or an unrelated quantitative internal standard and initial limiting input; no
renormalization of composition fractions supplies it.

McDonald HPLC yield: the acquired plate-1 raw PDA/MS files can test prediction
against source-calibrated yields; get the exact public model outputs or a
reproducible model pin, the manual calibration data/uncertainty, and an explicit
well-to-reference mapping for a full replay. Do not silently replace the paper's
38k model with the supplied Deep4Chem model. Its own isomer-assignment limitation
requires independent structural information or resolved peaks before claiming
structure-specific yield. Downloading more ordinary-reaction files does not fix
that identifiability limit.

Cernak-scale target: the minimum new physical evidence is a matrix/dilution-
matched independently quantified mixture series of the actual desired product,
regioisomer and starting material, including target-absent and side-product-rich
cases at the actual well scale. It needs structural assignments, raw detector
channels, initial limiting input/aliquot recovery, and independently measured
target-specific mole amounts with uncertainty. qNMR must establish resolution,
T1 control and sensitivity there. The operator can remain free of purified
target standards while the blinded validator and incumbent use them. No
publicly acquired dataset in this worker closes this exact transfer gate.
