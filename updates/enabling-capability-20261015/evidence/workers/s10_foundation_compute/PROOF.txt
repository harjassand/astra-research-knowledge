Statement
---------
Let H=C^p tensor C^q, p,q finite positive integers, and
Lambda_d(X)=Tr(X) I_d-X/2, Phi=Lambda_p tensor Lambda_q.
For a nonzero u in H of Schmidt rank at most two define
 A=Phi(uu*), L_a=Phi(|a><u|),
 C=sum_ab |b><a| tensor Phi(|a><b|),
 S=sum_ab |b><a| tensor L_a A^{-1} L_b*.
Here a,b enumerate a product orthonormal basis of H. Then

                    -(11/9) C <= S <= C.                 (1)

All spaces/vectors may be complex. A is positive definite. The 11/9
constant is sharp, attained by the embedded flat rank-two reference.
The proof below is a finite exact symbolic certificate, not a finite
sampling claim. Its machine-checkable part uses rational polynomial
arithmetic and matrices of order at most eight.

Reduction to one real parameter and positivity of the denominator
----------------------------------------------------------------
Scaling u by any nonzero complex scalar leaves S unchanged. For a local
unitary U=U_A tensor U_B, covariance of Phi gives
 A(Uu)=U A(u) U*,
 S(Uu)=(U tensor U) S(u) (U* tensor U*).
C commutes with U tensor U since, after reordering factors,
C=(I-F_A/2) tensor (I-F_B/2). Thus local Schmidt decomposition and scaling
reduce every rank-two u to u=|00>+t|11>, with real t>0. Rank-one references
are t=0. If p=1 then Lambda_1 is multiplication by 1/2, so C and S are
both 1/2 times their single-site counterparts; q=1 is the same argument.
The single-site generalized spectrum is contained in {-1/3,1}, directly
verified by the 1-by-1/2-by-2 decomposition stated below, and gives (1).

Write N=1+t^2 and Delta=t^4+4t^2+1. In the product basis A is diagonal
except for its 00,11 block
 [[t^2+1/4,t/4],[t/4,1+t^2/4]],
whose determinant is Delta/4. The 01 and 10 diagonal entries are N/2.
An active index 0 with an inactive index has entry (1+2t^2)/2; an active
index 1 with an inactive index has entry (2+t^2)/2. Entries with both
indices inactive are N. Therefore A>0 for all t>=0 and

          h(t)=4 N Delta (t^2+2)(2t^2+1)>0                 (2)

clears all nonconstant denominators of S. Rational scalar denominators
are harmless. No numerical inversion or eigenvalue tolerance is used.

Ambient-dimension reduction
---------------------------
The stabilizer of u contains independent diagonal phases theta_0,theta_1
on the active A indices and their negatives on the active B indices.
Each inactive A label and each inactive B label has its own independent
phase. On a co-Choi basis vector |ab> tensor |cd>, the phase weight is

  e_A(a)+e_A(c)+e_B(b)+e_B(d),

where e_B(0)=-e_A(0), e_B(1)=-e_A(1), and all inactive e_A/e_B labels are
independent. Covariance implies C and S preserve each weight space.

A basis vector has only two A positions, a,c, and two B positions, b,d.
Consequently each weight uses at most two distinct inactive A labels and
two distinct inactive B labels. Once those labels are fixed by the
weight, every basis vector in that weight space uses exactly those
inactive labels with exactly those multiplicities. Relabeling them as
2,3 embeds the entire weight space into the p=q=4 problem. The entry
formulas below depend only on equality of labels and whether they are
0,1, or inactive. They do not depend on the number of unused ambient
labels. More explicitly, a row r=(e,f) of L_i, i=(a,b), is supported only
on internal columns
 r, (b,f) when b is active, (e,a) when a is active, and (0,0),(1,1).
Thus no new inactive label is introduced into the internal summation
L_j A^{-1} L_i*. A^{-1} is diagonal except on the active 00,11 pair,
and therefore also introduces no inactive label. Unused ambient labels
make exactly zero contribution to every block entry; there is no hidden
dimension factor from an internal trace or inverse.
Therefore every arbitrary-p,q weight block is identical, up to a basis
permutation, to a block of the 4-by-4 local problem. When an ambient
dimension is 2 or 3, only some of the same blocks occur.

There are 84 phase blocks at p=q=4, with dimension at most eight. Exact
entry equality reduces these to 23 distinct ordered matrices, allowing
different orderings/signatures of equivalent blocks to remain separate.
All 84 are checked. Inactive labels appearing at both sites remain
independent; there is no identification between an inactive A phase
and an inactive B phase.

Exact entry formulas and polynomial certificate
----------------------------------------------
For i=(a,b), j=(c,d), r=(e,f), s=(g,h), the verifier defines

 Phi(E_ij)_{rs}
 = [a=c][b=d][e=g][f=h]
   -[b=d][e=a][g=c][f=h]/2
   -[a=c][f=b][h=d][e=g]/2
   +[e=a][f=b][g=c][h=d]/4.

Brackets are exact 0/1 indicators. It constructs A from uu*, then
 L_i(r,s)=Phi(E_{i,00})_{rs}+t Phi(E_{i,11})_{rs},
 C_{(i,r),(j,s)}=Phi(E_{j,i})_{rs},
 S_{(i,r),(j,s)}=(L_j A^{-1} L_i*)_{rs}.
The reversed j,i order is explicit. All entries are rational functions
over Q(t); the reference has real entries but this suffices by covariance.

For each of the 23 exact phase matrices, the verifier forms
 H_upper=h(t)(C-S),
 H_lower=9 h(t)(S+11C/9).
It also checks h(t)(S+3C), although this follows from (1).
Every H is real symmetric, with polynomial entries in Q[t]. It computes

               det(zI+H)=z^m+c_1(t)z^{m-1}+...+c_m(t).

Each c_j is proved nonnegative on t>=0 in one of two exact ways:
(a) all expanded polynomial coefficients are nonnegative; or
(b) its rational scalar factor is nonnegative and each irreducible
    factor either has even multiplicity or has nonnegative polynomial
    coefficients. Zero coefficients are explicitly accepted.
The final active-block lower determinant has (t-1)^2(t+1)^2, which is why
merely checking expanded coefficient signs would be insufficient there.

Why coefficient positivity proves PSD: H is Hermitian. If an eigenvalue
lambda<0 existed, z=-lambda>0 would be a root of det(zI+H). But its leading
coefficient is one and all other coefficients are nonnegative, so the
polynomial is strictly positive at every z>0. Contradiction. This holds
also at t=0 and t=1 and does not require positive definiteness of H.
The representation covers every finite t. The apparent t=infinity
endpoint is another product reference and is already included by
permuting active labels and using t=0.

The complete factorizations are in rank2_p4_q4.json. Recompute them with
 python3 rank2_blocks.py --p 4 --q 4
The script is standalone except for SymPy and standard Python libraries.
No full 256-by-256 co-Choi or switched matrix is constructed. Only the

Sharpness
---------
For p=q=2 and u=|00>+|11>, in input-major/output-minor ordering, let
 w=(3,0,0,1,0,0,2,0,0,2,0,0,1,0,0,3).
Direct rational arithmetic yields w*C*w=9 and w*S*w=-11. Thus the lower
constant in (1) cannot be decreased below 11/9. This is the exact witness
already independently audited by worker foundation_audit.

Consequence for a restricted all-copy sector
--------------------------------------------
On a single product reference site, C_0^{-1/2}S_0 C_0^{-1/2} has spectrum
contained in {-1/3,1}; this follows by direct 1-by-1/2-by-2 blocks and is
also recorded exactly by foundation_audit. If the reference factors as
one two-site Schmidt-rank-at-most-two vector and product vectors on all
other sites, the normalized switched co-Choi is the tensor product of
these operators. Its eigenvalues are products of a number in [-11/9,1]
and numbers in {-1/3,1}. Every such product is at most one: a negative
group eigenvalue requires an odd number of negative single-site factors
to become positive, reducing its absolute value to at most 11/27.
Therefore C_total-S_total>=0 for every number of extra product sites.

The co-Choi matrix of Phi_total-M_u is C_total-S_total, so this difference
map is completely copositive and hence positive. For every x,
 D=Phi_total(xx*) and L=Phi_total(xu*) consequently satisfy
                   D-L A^{-1} L* >=0.
For K=|u><v|+|x><y|, let a=v*Av, d=y*Dy, g=y*Lv. The partial-trace form
q_k obeys q_k(K)=a+d+2 Re(g), and the switched bound gives |g|^2<=ad.
Hence q_k(K)>=0. Any rank-at-most-two operator whose range or adjoint
range contains such a reference admits the required representation.

In particular, the three-copy conclusion covers a biseparable ray whose
entangled pair has Schmidt rank at most two. It does not cover arbitrary
biseparable rays with higher pair Schmidt rank. It does not establish
the unrestricted three-copy problem, all-copy Werner undistillability,
or NPT bound entanglement. Two entangled groups cannot be inferred from
this certificate: two Bell-group extremal eigenvalues multiply to
121/81>1, defeating this switched co-Choi domination method.
