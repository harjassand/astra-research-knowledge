2. Contraction span completeness
Write a co-Choi coordinate as (a,b,c,e), with A indices a,c and B indices
b,e. Define four embeddings from d-by-d matrices X:
 T1(X)_(a,b,c,e)=delta_ab X_ce,
 T2(X)_(a,b,c,e)=X_ab delta_ce,
 T3(X)_(a,b,c,e)=delta_ae X_cb,
 T4(X)_(a,b,c,e)=X_ae delta_cb.
Let V be the sum of their images. These are exactly the adjoints of the
four possible contractions of an A index with a B index. Therefore
K=V-perp is the simultaneous kernel of all four input contractions.

For X=I, T1=T2 and T3=T4. The two remaining vectors have Gram
G0=[[d^2,d],[d,d^2]], positive definite for d>1.
For traceless X,Y, direct Kronecker contraction gives
 <Ti(X),Tj(Y)>=G1_ij Tr(X*Y),
 G1=[[d,0,1,1],[0,d,1,1],[1,1,d,0],[1,1,0,d]].
Its eigenvalues are d+2,d-2,d,d. Thus d>=3 gives four independent copies
of the traceless matrix space. They are orthogonal to the two scalar
vectors because every scalar-traceless inner product is a multiple of
Tr(X). This proves
 dim(V)=2+4(d^2-1)=4d^2-2,
 H=V_scalar direct-sum V_traceless direct-sum K.
There are no unaccounted contraction directions or required irreducibility
assumptions. Operator action on all traceless X follows from the displayed
tensor formulas, without selecting a special matrix X.

3. The complement and why it has only scalar flip sectors
On K every source diagram with an input A-B cap is zero. A through pair
partition with the correct A/B orientations is one of I,F_A,F_B,F_AF_B.
The source's remaining S expression is
 P=(F_A+F_B)/(4(d-1))
    +(d^2-9d+4) F_AF_B/(4(d-1)(5d-4)).
C=(I-F_A/2)(I-F_B/2). Both local flips preserve V and K. Their commuting
eigenvalues are independently +/-1, so each realized part of K is a
scalar sector. Checking all four sign patterns is sufficient even when
some patterns have zero dimension for small d.

The source diagrams explicitly preserve V. C and S are Hermitian, so K
is also invariant. There is no cross term between V and K. The producer's
symbolic trivial/adjoint action matrices therefore cover all of V, and
its four flip scalar expressions cover all of K.

certificate. Leading coefficient1 plus Hermiticity rules out a negative
