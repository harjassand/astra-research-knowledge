INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE SCHMIDT-RANK-TWO GROUPED CO-CHOI BOUND
Worker a08_grouped_werner, 2026-10-15. This is an internal mathematical audit,
not independent external review. Producer: s10_foundation_compute.

Statement being audited
----------------------
Let p,q>=2, H=C^p tensor C^q, Lambda_d(X)=Tr(X)I-X/2 and
Phi=Lambda_p tensor Lambda_q. For any nonzero u in H of Schmidt rank at most
two, set A=Phi(uu*), L_a=Phi(|a><u|),
C=sum_ab |b><a| tensor Phi(|a><b|),
S=sum_ab |b><a| tensor L_a A^{-1}L_b*.
The proposed exact bound is -(11/9)C <= S <= C. No claim here is made for
Schmidt rank greater than two. This is a statement about a reference vector;
it is not a bound on the rank of an arbitrary matrix substituted for u.

1. Local unitary and scale reduction
-----------------------------------
Phi(WXW*)=W Phi(X)W* for W=U tensor V. For any such W,
A(Wu)=WA(u)W*, and
L_a(Wu)=W[sum_c conjugate(W_ac)L_c(u)]W*.
Consequently S(Wu)=(W tensor W)S(u)(W* tensor W*).
The co-Choi matrix C commutes with W tensor W: after grouping corresponding
local factors it is (I-F_A/2) tensor (I-F_B/2). Thus the Loewner bounds are
preserved by Schmidt reduction. Scaling u by any nonzero complex scalar
leaves S unchanged. A nonzero rank-two vector therefore reduces to
u=|00>+t|11>, t>=0; the rank-one case is t=0. If the first Schmidt coefficient
vanishes, exchange the active basis labels. No limit at t=infinity is needed.

2. Analytic inverse and all denominator signs
--------------------------------------------
Write N=1+t^2 and D=t^4+4t^2+1. On span{|00>,|11>},
A=[[t^2+1/4,t/4],[t/4,1+t^2/4]], det(A)=D/4, and
A^{-1}=D^{-1}[[t^2+4,-t],[-t,4t^2+1]].
Every other basis vector |ab> is an eigenvector of A with eigenvalue
N-(s_a^2+s_b^2)/2, where s_0=1,s_1=t,s_j=0 for j>=2.
These eigenvalues belong to
{N/2, (2t^2+1)/2, (t^2+2)/2, N}.
Every one is strictly positive for all real t. In particular t=0 is safe;
there is no inverse of a zero Schmidt coefficient.
Every entry of C and S is cleared by the strictly positive polynomial
DEN=4(1+t^2)(t^4+4t^2+1)(t^2+2)(2t^2+1).
The lower-bound certificate uses DEN*(9S+11C), so it adds only a positive
factor 9. This checks the denominator condition globally, not at sample t.

3. Why 4 by 4 covers all ambient dimensions
-----------------------------------------
Use the torus with phases theta_0,theta_1 on the first two A basis labels,
-theta_0,-theta_1 on the first two B labels, and independent phases on every
inactive A and every inactive B label. It fixes u for every t, including t=0.
The preceding covariance makes C and S block diagonal in its characters.
On |ab> tensor |cd>, the character is
theta_a+theta_c+eta_b+eta_d,
where eta_0=-theta_0, eta_1=-theta_1 and all inactive phases are independent.

An inactive A label occurs with nonnegative multiplicity (0,1,or2), and so
does every inactive B label. A fixed character therefore fixes exactly the
inactive A multiset and the inactive B multiset. It can contain at most two
distinct inactive A labels and at most two distinct inactive B labels.
No further inactive label can occur in a basis state in that same block:
there are no negative occurrences of inactive characters to cancel it.
Relabel these fixed inactive sets into {2,3} separately on A and B. This
injects the entire block into the p=q=4 model and gives exactly the same
character block there (or a principal submatrix when a side has fewer than
two active labels, which can instead be handled by embedding).

Matrix entries also survive this relabeling. The independent formula is
<cd|L_ab = delta_ab*s_a<cd|
           -(s_b/2)delta_ca<bd|
           -(s_a/2)delta_db<ca|
           +(delta_ca delta_db/4)(<00|+t<11|).
It depends only on the active labels, the named inactive labels, and
equality of labels within each local space. The analytic inverse above has
the same property. It has no off-diagonal couplings outside {|00>,|11>}, so
summing intermediate indices cannot introduce a dependence on p or q.
Thus each ambient block is permutation-congruent to a 4x4 template.
The 4x4 partition has 84 character blocks, totaling dimension 256, with
maximum dimension 8 and 23 distinct ordered matrix pairs (C,S).
This is an exact template exhaustion, not a numerical dimension extrapolation.

4. Why the polynomial certificates prove PSD
-------------------------------------------
For each template form H=DEN(C-S) and H=DEN(9S+11C). These are real symmetric
polynomial matrices. If every coefficient of det(zI+H) is nonnegative for
t>=0, this determinant is strictly positive at every z>0: its leading
coefficient is one. A negative eigenvalue of H would yield a positive zero
z=-lambda, a contradiction. Hermiticity is essential in this implication.
Each scalar coefficient is certified either by nonnegative monomial
coefficients in t, or by a product of positive constants, even powers of
real polynomials, and polynomials with nonnegative monomial coefficients.
This includes a (t-1)^2 factor in the sharp lower-bound determinant and
permits zero eigenvalues at t=1. It also permits the exact zero coefficients
caused by the upper-bound kernel; strict positivity of H is not claimed.

6. Scope and unresolved obligation
----------------------------------
The sharper lower constant 11/9 is necessary: the exact Bell-rank-two
example in foundation_audit/exact_audit.json gives w*Cw=9, w*Sw=-11.
Together with the single-site generalized interval [-1/3,1], this interval
is sufficient for the source-derived one-entangled-pair tensorization
interface, subject to separately checking the switched Schur reduction.
This audit does not establish arbitrary-Schmidt-rank grouped bounds,
unrestricted three-copy Werner nondistillability, or all-copy NPT bound
entanglement. Two entangled Bell groups invalidate the naive repeated
grouped-subtraction argument: the independently reported tensor witness is
9^2-(-11)^2=-40. Physical capability and comparative practical advantage are
not consequences of this certificate.
