THEOREM
Let d>=3 be any integer, H=C^d tensor C^d, and let u=sum_i|ii> in H.
Define Lambda, Phi, A, L_a, C and S exactly as in AUDIT.txt. Then

  -[(5d+1)/(9(d-1))] C <= S <= C.                         (U1)

Both endpoints are attained. In particular -C<=S<=C, and the lower
endpoint is at least -8/9 for every d>=3. Local-unitary covariance extends
this to every full-Schmidt-rank maximally entangled reference on C^d x C^d.
The pair dimensions must equal the Schmidt rank in this proof. Unequal
ambient dimensions or extra inactive levels are not covered by (U1).
For d=2 the sharp endpoint is -11/9, covered by the separately audited
rank-two theorem; hence the uniform formula agrees at that boundary.

1. EXACT ROW FORMULA AND INVERSE
Put a=d-1, b=5d-4, and Omega=sum_i|ii>. Directly from Phi,

 A=a I+Omega Omega*/4,
 A^{-1}=I/a - Omega Omega*/(a b),
 L_ij=delta_ij I-(E_ij tensor I+I tensor E_ji)/2
                         + |ij><Omega|/4.               (U2)

All denominators are strictly positive for d>=3. Vectors are ordered as
|ab> tensor |cd>, and the S block with input row (a,b), column (e,f) is
L_ef A^{-1} L_ab*. Thus the reversed block order is explicit.

2. FOUR CONTRACTION MAPS AND AN EXPLICIT OPERATOR IDENTITY
For any d-by-d complex matrix X define maps T_i: M_d -> H tensor H by

 (T_1 X)_{abcd}=delta_ab X_cd,
 (T_2 X)_{abcd}=X_ab delta_cd,
 (T_3 X)_{abcd}=delta_ad X_cb,
 (T_4 X)_{abcd}=X_ad delta_cb.

Their adjoints are the corresponding index contractions with respect to
the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product. Put f=T_1(I)=T_2(I), so
f_{abcd}=delta_ab delta_cd. Let F_A exchange a,c and F_B exchange b,d.
Expansion of the four terms in each L in (U2), followed by contraction
of A^{-1}, gives the following Hermitian identity:

 S= T_1 T_1*/a
   -[(T_3+T_4)T_1*+T_1(T_3*+T_4*)]/(2a)
   +d(T_2 T_1*+T_1 T_2*)/(a b) - |f><f|/(a b)
   +(T_3 T_4*+T_4 T_3*)/(4a)
   +(F_A+F_B)/(4a)
   +(d^2-9d+4) F_A F_B/(4a b).                          (U3)

This can be checked index by index using only Kronecker deltas. The
standalone script uniform_schmidt.py performs precisely those contractions:
each internal closed index loop contributes the symbol d, with no fixed
dimension sampling. The expansion contains 13 delta diagrams. C is

 C=I-(F_A+F_B)/2+F_A F_B/4.                              (U4)

A shorter way to verify the expansion by hand is to put k=(d-4)/4 and
write S=U_0/a-U_Omega/(ab). Since L_ij Omega=delta_ij Omega+k|ij>,

 U_Omega=|f><f|+k(T_2 T_1*+T_1 T_2*)+k^2 F_A F_B.

The sixteen products in U_0=sum_ij |j><i| tensor L_i L_j* collect as

 U_0=T_1 T_1*
    -[(T_3+T_4)T_1*+T_1(T_3*+T_4*)]/2
    +(T_2 T_1*+T_1 T_2*+T_3 T_4*+T_4 T_3*+F_A+F_B)/4
    +(d-8)F_A F_B/16.

Substitution and collection give (U3) immediately.

3. COMPLETE INVARIANT-SPACE DECOMPOSITION
Let V=sum_i ran(T_i). Every X uniquely splits into a scalar multiple of I
and a traceless matrix. Therefore V is the orthogonal sum of

 V_0=span{f,g},     g=T_3(I)=T_4(I), g_abcd=delta_ad delta_cb,
 V_1={sum_i T_i(X_i): Tr(X_i)=0}.

For any traceless X,Y,
 <T_i X,T_j Y>=(G_1)_ij Tr(X*Y), where

 G_1=[[d,0,1,1],[0,d,1,1],[1,1,d,0],[1,1,0,d]].

Its eigenvalues are d+2,d-2,d,d, all positive for d>=3. Thus there are
exactly four independent copies of the traceless-matrix space. On V_0,
the Gram matrix in basis (f,g) is
G_0=[[d^2,d],[d,d^2]], also positive definite for d>=3.
The maps F_A and F_B permute the T_i, so (U3)-(U4) preserve V_0 and V_1.
Since C,S are Hermitian, they preserve K=V^perp as well. On K every T_i*
vanishes. Consequently (U3) reduces on K to the last two permutation
terms. The commuting self-adjoint involutions F_A,F_B split K into
sectors indexed by (epsilon,eta) in {+1,-1}^2, some possibly zero-sized.
This proves completeness without assuming an unlisted representation.

4. THE TWO SMALL MATRICES
The coordinate matrices on V_0 are

 C_0=[[5/4,-1],[-1,5/4]],
 S_0=[[5/4,-(10d^2-25d+12)/(2ab)],
      [-1,(11d^2-37d+20)/(4ab)]].

The coordinate matrices on each traceless-matrix copy are

 C_1=[[1,1/4,-1/2,-1/2],
      [1/4,1,-1/2,-1/2],
      [-1/2,-1/2,1,1/4],
      [-1/2,-1/2,1/4,1]],

 S_1=[[1,(d-5)/(4a),-(10d^2-37d+20)/(4ab),-(10d^2-37d+20)/(4ab)],
      [1/4,0,(9d-4)/(4ab),(9d-4)/(4ab)],

These coordinate matrices need not be symmetric in ordinary Euclidean
coordinates. The relevant Hermitian matrices are G_j C_j and G_j S_j.
Their generalized eigenvalues are the roots of det(S_j-lambda C_j);
the positive Gram matrix cancels from that determinant. This distinction
is necessary: declaring the displayed S_j symmetric would be wrong.

5. EXACT GENERALIZED SPECTRUM AND ITS BOUNDS
Write ell=-(5d+1)/(9a), which satisfies -1<ell<0 for d>=3.
On V_0 the generalized eigenvalues are exactly 1 and ell.

On V_1 they are
  1,
  v=-(6d^2-13d+4)/(3ab),
  the two roots of
  Q(lambda)=27ab lambda^2+(15d^2-90d+24)lambda
                                    -(6d^2+19d+4).      (U5)

The leading coefficient of Q is positive and its constant term negative,
so it has one negative and one positive real root. Direct evaluation gives

 Q(1)=16(d-2)(9d-4)>0,
 Q(ell)=2d^2(41d+28)/(3a)>0.

As ell<0, these identities place both roots strictly between ell and 1.
Moreover
 v-ell=(d+4)(7d-4)/(9ab)>0,
and v<0 for d>=3. Thus every V_1 generalized eigenvalue lies in [ell,1].

On K, C and S have scalar values
 C_epsilon,eta=(1-epsilon/2)(1-eta/2),
 S_epsilon,eta=(epsilon+eta)/(4a)
                    +(d^2-9d+4)epsilon eta/(4ab).
Their ratios are
 w_--=(d^2-19d+12)/(9ab),
 w_-+=w_+-=-(d^2-9d+4)/(3ab),
 w_++=(d^2+d-4)/(ab).

Their differences from ell are respectively
 2(13d-4)/(9b),
 2(11d^2+6d-8)/(9ab),
 2(d+1)(17d-20)/(9ab),
all strictly positive for d>=3. The differences from the upper endpoint
1 are respectively
 (44d^2-62d+24)/(9ab),
 (16d^2-36d+16)/(3ab),
 (4d^2-10d+8)/(ab),
also strictly positive for d>=3. Hence ell<=w<=1 on every K sector.

Combining the complete orthogonal sectors proves (U1).

6. SHARPNESS AND A SANITY CHECK
The vector f lies in the generalized eigenvalue-one space. An eigenvector
at the lower endpoint is

 w_d=(4d-5)f+(5d-4)g.

Its C quadratic form is
 <w_d,C w_d>=9d(d-1)(d+1)(5d-4)/4>0,
and <w_d,S w_d>=ell<w_d,C w_d>. At d=2, w_d is three times
the exact Bell witness in foundation_audit/exact_audit.json. This yields
ell=-11/9 at d=2 and explains why the bound -C cannot include that case.
