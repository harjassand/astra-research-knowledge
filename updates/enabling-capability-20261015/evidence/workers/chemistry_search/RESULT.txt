CHEMISTRY/MATERIALS SEARCH: NO DISTINCT ENABLING OPERATION VALIDATED
Recorded 2026-10-15. Investigator: chemistry_search.

Three routes were probed. The strongest accessible route was controlled C–N
reaction data with separately labeled desired and regioisomer products. Genuine
measured outcomes were acquired, decoded, and screened; no synthetic proxy was
used. Broad reliable metal-free synthetic access did not emerge.

1. Complementary-condition synthesis panels
Primary prior art: Sivilotti, Friday and Jackson, Active learning high coverage
sets of complementary reaction conditions, Digital Discovery (2025),
https://doi.org/10.1039/D4DD00365A.
It already combines complementary conditions to broaden reactant coverage and
benchmarks active learning. A portfolio optimizer would be an implementation of
the incumbent, not a distinct enabling operation. Do not concentrate here.

2. Experimental inorganic functional-property atlas
Primary data infrastructure: https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.53 and
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2021.100373;
API examples: https://github.com/NatLabRockies/htem-api-examples.
The published interface includes measured conductivity, optics, and diffraction.
The historical API hostname failed DNS resolution in this session; no functional
sample was acquired from it. This is a session-specific access failure, not proof
that HTEM data no longer exist. No prospective fabrication access was established.

3. Controlled target-product access (strongest acquired route)
Primary article: https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c05959
Public text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13339639/
Source code: https://github.com/cernak-lab/cernakHTE
Dataset: ORD ord_dataset-805ad863feef48579d95d86a728035f4.
The article already develops matched metal-free treatment effects and authentic-
standard spectral deconvolution. It is the strongest equally informed incumbent,
not a baseline that can fairly be omitted. The code separately evaluates desired
DP and side-product SP effects. Correcting a total-yield score to target DP does
not produce a new operation.

Acquisition and actual calculations
The complete public parquet is 5,088,296 bytes. Its SHA256 matches the LFS object
at pinned ORD commit 83f971f586f6ad18f358ae4ae99d045e94ed2066; see the receipt.
It contains 50,688 unique reactions/samples: 6,336 no-metal wells and 44,352 metal
treatments. All treatments have an exactly matching chemistry control in the same
named experiment, after deleting only catalyst identity/amount and retaining all
other inputs, solvent volumes, setup, workup, temperature and time. Controls may
be reused across multiple treatments; this is not 44,352 independent control
experiments. Source sample names contain mixed well naming and eight anomalies;
140 parsed analytical prefixes are not a certified plate count. There are 33
experiment+temperature blocks.

All rows supply source-reported calibrated target and side-product labels and
SMILES. Authentic/internal-standard flags are true. These flags do not reacquire
raw calibration curves, chromatograms, uncertainty, or purity. Coded-recipe
identifiers can disagree with structured reagent fields; matching uses structured
reagent identity and amount instead. Source analysis detail says caffeine 0.0067 M,
whereas the workup says 0.0667 M. This tenfold metadata ambiguity remains unresolved.

The retrospective diagnostic asks for >=50% desired-regioisomer assay yield and
desired/(desired+reported regioisomer)>=0.95. No no-metal well meets that screen.
Only one no-metal well has DP>=50%:DP 52.474346%, SP 45.76049%, desired fraction 0.53417.
Metal-treated wells have 85 source-label hits;20 report DP>100%. Preserving those
anomalies and excluding impossible reported totals leaves 65 metal candidate
wells versus 0 no-metal. These are finite descriptive counts, not prospective
predictions, recovered products, total purity, or independent experimental
validation. At a 20% target threshold, four no-metal wells have SP=0; three share
one experiment. Their DP values are 20.717–26.98%, not a broad rescue capability.

The SI assigns nonobserved products 0% (p33). Thus SP=0 is censored detection, not
certified absence. Its p40 low-signal warning applies specifically to deconvolving
products3/13 below 20% combined assay yield; the four >=20% DP hits are above that
threshold, so that warning alone does not refute them. Their uncertainty, detection
limit, batch replication, isolation and purity remain unacquired. The two target
structures cannot establish broad chemical-scope generalization.

What a genuinely different operation would need
The possible enabling problem is obtaining structure-specific, uncertainty-
bounded product quantities when chromatography/mass overlap and standards are
scarce. Merely fitting the supplied labels cannot accomplish it. Authentic-
standard UV deconvolution is already the source incumbent. A distinct operation
must produce additional independently checked identifiability or target recovery
under equal standards, raw signals, calibration, chemical interventions and full
cost. No such difference survived this probe. Raw spectral mixtures and blind
reference quantities were not acquired; the source retrieval notebook requires
Globus credentials. The separate chemistry evidence audit preserves that gate.

Recommendation
Retain the real dataset, parser, descriptive falsification and incumbents. Do not
promote catalyst deletion, treatment effects, target-aware scoring, or spectral
least squares as new capability. Further concentration should be contingent on
acquiring a genuinely different physical measurement/intervention with blind
mixture validation or independently recovered target product. The present lane
is NOT_ACHIEVED and provides no broad advantage claim.
