1. Preferred consequential target
For every finite d>=3 and every complex operator X on (C^d)^{tensor 3} of rank at most two, prove or disprove
q3(X)=||X||F^2 - (1/2)sum_i||Tr_i X||F^2 + (1/4)sum_{i<j}||Tr_ij X||F^2 - |Tr X|^2/8 >=0.
This is the three-copy endpoint Werner-distillability question. A negative rational/algebraic rank-two witness is a closed disproof; a dimension-free valid inequality is a proof. Finite numerical searches are neither.

Exact residual in Wu/Zou:
For two orthonormal pairs (u1,u2),(v1,v2), A_r=|u_r><v_r|, the Gram determinant condition is
|b3(A1,A2)|^2 <= q3(A1)q3(A2).
Writing R=product_i(I-F_i/2), b3(A1,A2)=<u1 tensor v2,R(u2 tensor v1)> has crossed input pairing. Ordinary Cauchy-Schwarz has the wrong two diagonal terms. Their claimed sector theorems would force a negative witness to be nonnormal with distinct product-ray-free support planes, all local input/output support overlaps >2, and no vanishing one-site partial trace. Three-copy success would still not establish all-copy NPT bound entanglement.

2. Concrete new structural interface under investigation
Let Lambda_d(X)=Tr(X)I_d-X/2 and Phi=Lambda_p tensor Lambda_q. For a normalized reference u in C^p tensor C^q, define
A=Phi(|u><u|), L_a=Phi(|a><u|),
C=sum_ab E_ba tensor Phi(E_ab),
S=sum_ab E_ba tensor L_a A^{-1} L_b^*.
All basis labels a,b enumerate the two-site space lexicographically.
Conjectured auxiliary bound: -3 C <= S <= C, for all p,q,u.
Reason this is useful: a single product reference site has generalized spectrum {-1/3,1}; after tensoring one two-site group with any number of product reference sites, this weaker bound would imply the switched Schur complement. Hence q_k(X)>=0 whenever either range contains a ray with entanglement confined to one pair of sites. At k=3 this includes a biseparable ray, a larger class than a fully product ray. It is only a restricted sector, not the unrestricted target.

Naive stronger auxiliary bound -C<=S<=C is false. For p=q=2, u=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2), vector
w=(3,0,0,1,0,0,2,0,0,2,0,0,1,0,0,3)
gives w*(C+S)w=-2 in a numerical calculation with rational-looking entries. An independent exact check is pending. The generalized minimum is numerically -11/9, maximum 1. This failure blocks a contractivity shortcut but does not block the weaker -3 bound. All numerical scope remains finite.

Local-unitary covariance reduces u to Schmidt form sum_i s_i|ii>, s_i>=0, sum s_i^2=1. With rho1,rho2 its marginals,
A=I-(rho1 tensor I+I tensor rho2)/2+|u><u|/4,
L_ab=delta_ab s_a I-(s_b E_ab tensor I+s_a I tensor E_ba)/2+|ab><u|/4.
These explicit blocks are the next analytic entry point; no positivity proof has yet been supplied.
