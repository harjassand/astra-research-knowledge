EXACT CONDITIONAL INTERFACE FOR A BISEPARABLE SUPPORT RAY
2026-10-15. Internally derived; independent team audit requested. Novelty UNKNOWN.
The grouped two-site inequality below is an OPEN OBLIGATION, not a premise silently established by numerics.

Definitions
Let Lambda_d(X)=Tr(X)I_d-X/2. For a tensor product of such maps Phi and a nonzero reference u, put
 A_u=Phi(|u><u|), L_a=Phi(|a><u|),
 M_u(E_ab)=L_a A_u^{-1}L_b^*,
 C=coChoi(Phi)=sum_ab E_ba tensor Phi(E_ab),
 S_u=coChoi(M_u)=sum_ab E_ba tensor L_a A_u^{-1}L_b^*.
The co-Choi convention is fixed here. Its positivity means complete copositivity: a map E with coChoi(E)>=0 is E=Psi o transpose for a completely positive Psi, and in particular E maps PSD operators to PSD operators.
For k sites, C is unitarily regrouped from product_i(I-F_i/2), so C>=2^{-k}I. The compression identity A_u=(<u| tensor I)C(|u> tensor I) implies A_u>=2^{-k}||u||^2 I and legitimates the inverse. Rescaling u does not change M_u or S_u.

Lemma 1: sharp single-site interval
For one site and any normalized u, -C/3<=S_u<=C. When d>=2 both spectral endpoints are attained.
Proof. Covariance reduces u to e0. Write P=|0><0|, Q=I-P, A0=I-P/2 and L_a=delta_a0 I-E_a0/2. Direct multiplication using A0^{-1}=I+P gives the following common reducing subspaces.
(i) On P tensor P, C=S=1/2.
(ii) On span{|0a>,|a0>}, a!=0, the matrices are
 C=[[1,-1/2],[-1/2,1]], S=[[1,-1/2],[-1/2,0]].
 det(S-zC)=(3z+1)(z-1)/4, so the generalized eigenvalues are -1/3 and 1.
(iii) On Q tensor Q, C=I-F_Q/2 and S=F_Q/2. Symmetric and antisymmetric eigenvectors have respective generalized ratios 1 and -1/3 whenever their sectors are present.
Thus C^{-1/2}SC^{-1/2} has spectrum in {-1/3,1}. This proves both bounds and sharpness for d>=2. For d=1 only eigenvalue 1 remains. End proof.

Lemma 2: product-reference factorization
For Phi=Phi_G tensor Phi_P and u=u_G tensor u_P, A_u=A_G tensor A_P, the L matrices factor, and M_u=M_G tensor M_P. Hence C and S factor under the same canonical regrouping. For any number m>=1 of single product sites, normalized S_P has spectrum contained in [-1/3,1] because it is a product of eigenvalues each belonging to {-1/3,1}.

Conditional theorem 3: one entangled pair plus product sites
Assume, for Phi_G=Lambda_p tensor Lambda_q and a particular nonzero bipartite reference u_G, that
               -3 C_G <= S_G <= C_G.                 [OPEN GROUPED OBLIGATION]
Then for every number m>=0 of further sites, every reference
 u=u_G tensor u_1 tensor ... tensor u_m,
and every x, the switched Schur complement is PSD:
 Phi(|x><x|) - Phi(|x><u|) A_u^{-1} Phi(|u><x|) >=0.
Proof. If m=0 only S_G<=C_G is needed. If m>=1, let R_G=C_G^{-1/2}S_G C_G^{-1/2} and similarly R_P. Their eigenvalues lie in [-3,1] and [-1/3,1]. Every eigenvalue of R_G tensor R_P is at most 1: same-positive products are <=1, opposite-sign products are <=0, and same-negative products are <=3(1/3)=1. Therefore
 C_G tensor C_P - S_G tensor S_P >=0.
This is the co-Choi matrix of Phi-M_u. Consequently Phi-M_u is completely copositive, hence positive. Applying it to |x><x| gives the displayed inequality. End proof.

Conditional corollary 4: rank-two endpoint sector
Define q_k(X)=sum_{J subset [k]}(-1/2)^{|J|}||Tr_J X||F^2, with Tr_empty X=X and Tr_all X=Tr X. Under the grouped obligation in theorem 3, q_k(X)>=0 whenever rank X<=2 and either ran X or ran X^* contains a nonzero ray u whose entanglement is confined to the grouped pair.
Proof. Suppose the range contains u, normalized. Choose x so the range is contained in span{u,x}, and write X=|u><v|+|x><y| for arbitrary v,y. No mutual orthogonality of v,y is required. A direct coefficient contraction of partial traces gives
 q_k(X)=a+d+2 Re g,
 a=<v,A_u v>, d=<y,Phi(|x><x|)y>, g=<y,Phi(|x><u|)v>.
By weighted Cauchy-Schwarz,
 |g|^2 <= a <y, Phi(|x><u|) A_u^{-1} Phi(|u><x|)y> <= ad.
Thus q_k(X)>=(sqrt(a)-sqrt(d))^2>=0. The input-range version follows from q_k(X^*)=q_k(X). Rank one is included by setting the second covector to zero. End proof.

Unconditional corollary 5: fully product support ray
If u is fully product across all k sites, lemma 1 and tensor factorization already give S_u<=C without any grouped obligation. The argument in corollary 4 then proves q_k(X)>=0 for all k and rank-at-most-two X with a fully product ray in either support plane. This is an arbitrary-copy extension of the fully product-reference calculation in Wu/Zou arXiv:2608.02647v1 Lemma 7.4. The extension is immediate from that source proof; novelty has not been established and it is not represented as a breakthrough.

Failure shield
The stronger grouped interval [-1,1] is false. foundation_audit/exact_audit.json verifies Bell2 u=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2), a rational vector w with w*C*w=9 and w*S*w=-11. The weaker interval [-3,1] remains unproved here. It must not be replaced by the finite numerical observation [-11/9,1]. Multiple arbitrary entangled pairs are not covered by the sufficient interval argument: two negative grouped eigenvalues smaller than -1 can multiply to a number greater than 1. No unrestricted three-copy or all-copy Werner-undistillability theorem follows from these conditional sector statements.

Exact four-site limit of this proof mechanism
For two Bell references grouped as (12)|(34), factorization gives C4=C tensor C and S4=S tensor S. The Bell vector w above therefore satisfies
 (w tensor w)^*(C4-S4)(w tensor w)=9^2-(-11)^2=-40.
Thus the co-Choi subtraction Phi4-M_u is not completely copositive for this reference. This is a proof-mechanism failure, not an example with q4<0 and not a disproof of positivity of Phi4-M_u.
