Exact mathematical result
Let Lambda_d(Z)=Tr(Z)I_d-Z/2 and Phi=Lambda_p tensor Lambda_q. For any nonzero u in C^p tensor C^q of Schmidt rank at most two, define
 A=Phi(|u><u|), L_a=Phi(|a><u|),
 C=sum_ab |b><a| tensor Phi(|a><b|),
 S=sum_ab |b><a| tensor L_a A^{-1} L_b^*.
Then A is positive definite and
                         -(11/9)C <= S <= C.
The lower constant 11/9 is sharp. All vector/operator spaces may be complex; the reference is reduced by local unitaries and scaling, not restricted to real physical inputs.

For k>=2, let q_k(X)=sum_{J subset [k]}(-1/2)^{|J|}||Tr_J X||F^2. Then q_k(X)>=0 for every rank-at-most-two X whose range or adjoint range contains a nonzero vector of the form
         u_pair tensor z_3 tensor ... tensor z_k,
where u_pair has bipartite Schmidt rank at most two. The two entangled sites may be any pair. At k=3, this covers a biseparable ray with pair Schmidt rank at most two. It does not cover an arbitrary biseparable ray with higher pair Schmidt rank or an arbitrary two-dimensional support plane.

Proof mechanism and verification
Schmidt reduction sets u=|00>+t|11>, t>=0. The inverse A^{-1} is rational in t with a denominator that is explicitly positive on the entire interval. Stabilizer phases divide the co-Choi matrices into blocks; each block uses at most two inactive labels per side, so the p=q=4 case contains every possible block in arbitrary ambient dimensions. This is a proved template reduction, not extrapolation from small dimensions.

s10_foundation_compute/PROOF.txt and rank2_blocks.py reconstruct 84 phase blocks, 23 distinct ordered templates of maximum order eight. Characteristic-polynomial coefficients of positive-scaled C-S and 9S+11C are certified nonnegative for every t>=0. A Hermitian matrix with a negative eigenvalue would make det(zI+H) vanish at some z>0, impossible with nonnegative coefficients and leading coefficient one.

a08_grouped_werner/audit_rank2.py independently constructs the map formulas and analytic inverse without importing the producer code. Its rank2_independent_audit.json records all 226 coefficients reconstructed/certified and all symbolic off-block entries zero. The arbitrary-dimension proof was separately checked in a08_grouped_werner/AUDIT.txt. This is same-team internal review, not authenticated external expert review.

The exact one-site generalized interval is [-1/3,1]. Tensoring one grouped pair in [-11/9,1] with product reference sites therefore keeps every generalized eigenvalue <=1. The resulting co-Choi domination makes the switched Schur difference completely copositive and hence positive. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz then gives q_k(X)>=(sqrt(a)-sqrt(d))^2>=0. foundation_audit independently checked the full complex contraction, transpose conventions, and both range/corange cases. The derivation is written in TENSOR_INTERFACE_PROOF.txt and foundation_audit/TENSORIZATION_AND_SECTOR_AUDIT.txt.

Strict source-scope separation
On (C^4)^{tensor3}, let
 u=000+011, x=101+212+323+330,
 v=100+111, y=002+210+021+332+303,
 X=|u><v|+|x><y|.
The four computational supports are disjoint. X has rank two, X^2=0, and all six local input/output supports have rank four. Neither global support plane contains a fully product vector. Its combined input/output support dimension is four, and every one-site partial trace is nonzero. The biseparable non-product u cannot belong to any locally orthogonal product-code subspace. Hence this example escapes the located normal, common-plane, product-ray, local-overlap, local-traceless and product-code source sectors. Full local rank four also excludes the October candidate's qutrit tensor-support scope. check_ququart_sector.py and an independent foundation_audit checker give q3(X)=65/4 exactly. The positive example establishes strictness of hypotheses, not the universal theorem or novelty.

Decisive failed shortcuts
1. The naive grouped contraction -C<=S<=C is false. For Bell2 reference, an exact rational vector w gives w*C*w=9 and w*S*w=-11. The generalized minimum is exactly -11/9.
2. Iterating the same co-Choi subtraction over two entangled Bell pairs fails: w tensor w gives 9^2-(-11)^2=-40 for C tensor C-S tensor S. This is a proof-mechanism failure, not a negative q4 witness. No repeated-pair or unrestricted all-copy result follows.
3. Source two-copy results do not induct: partial traces need not preserve rank two, and two-positivity has no generic tensor closure. Source publication/DOI/replay is not a substitute for a proof.
