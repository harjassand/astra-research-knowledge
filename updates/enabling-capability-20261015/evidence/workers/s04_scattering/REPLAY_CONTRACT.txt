Frozen scattering replay contract, 2026-10-15

Decision: GO for admitting this as a reference-linked structural benchmark once
the software/input gates below are met. HOLD the atomic-hand replay today. STOP
the claim that chirality repair or selecting a few reflections is a new enabling
operation. No new scientific capability or comparative advantage was established.

Scientific endpoint and exact ambiguity
--------------------------------------
The target is the atomic handedness of the STW_HPM-1 zeolite pore framework:
P 65 2 2 (No. 179) versus its inversion-related P 61 2 2 (No. 178). These are
different chiral atomic structures, rather than image-quality alternatives.
For real kinematical form factors, inversion conjugates F(hkl), so every
intensity is identical. More kinematical data alone cannot resolve this class.
Multiple-scattering interference and resonant scattering are established ways
to break this ambiguity. They are the required incumbent methods here.

Reference provenance and its scope
---------------------------------
Klar et al. report measuring one approximately 50 micrometre HPM-1 crystal by
Cu Kalpha single-crystal XRD, then dismounting it by dissolving its paraffin wax
in acetone, crushing/grinding it between glass slides, and measuring a fragment
by continuous-rotation ED at room temperature. Thus the XRD label and ED raw
data are linked to the same parent crystal, rather than unrelated samples from
a racemic batch. The source reports each parent crystal is a single enantiomorph.

The acquired XRD CIF reports P 65 2 2, CuKalpha 1.54178 A, T=298(2)K and
Flack x=0.06(2), determined from 828 Parsons quotients. This is an independent
measurement modality, not an independent laboratory replication. It establishes
the dominant parent hand under the XRD model. It does not independently certify
the hand of every possible fracture domain. Parent-to-fragment homogeneity and
orientation conventions remain explicit premises.

Source ED dynamical refinement reports wR_all=0.234 for P 65 2 2 and 0.306
for P 61 2 2. Those values were located in the source article, not rerun here.

Bounded raw-endpoint audit completed
-----------------------------------
Run: python3 audit_hpm_endpoint.py
Result: HPM_endpoint_audit.json; per-file detail sources/HPM_frame_audit.json.

All 22 TIFFs whose names end in i are pixelwise EXACT ceil((previous TIFF +
following TIFF)/2), at all 266,256 pixels in each file. They have ImageJ
metadata; other supplied TIFFs have instamatic metadata. Thus 451 supplied
TIFFs include 429 non-i detector-file records and 22 deterministic fills.
This is not a claim that the original structural result is wrong. It means
the filled files cannot count as independent physical observations.

The supplied PETS2 imagelist includes every fill with use=1. It uses 15-frame
virtual frames stepped by 5 source frames, so virtual frames also overlap.
Any confidence calculation must propagate reuse and fill dependence, or use
measurement blocks that do not share underlying detector observations.

The 429 non-i files are not certified untouched detector bytes. The relationship
between a physical Timepix detector, its gap handling and the supplied 516x516
files still needs upstream documentation. No electron-count truth is inferred
from unsigned 16-bit TIFF values.

Software and precise replay gates
---------------------------------
The original pipeline uses PETS2, Superflip, Jana2006 and Dyngo Bloch-wave
dynamical diffraction. PETS2 exports integrated intensities for dynamical
refinement without its kinematical frame-scale/Lorentz corrections. Refinement
must fit both hands with identical parameter budgets, one thickness per data
set, virtual-frame scales and the same orientation/shape treatments.

Current PETS2 site: https://pets.fzu.cz/ . Academic use is free; download points
to https://pets-login.fzu.cz/login and explicitly requires registration.
No new account may be created under WAVE_3, and none was created. The Jana
download site timed out; its login site returned 403. No pets/pets2/jana/
jana2006/jana2020/dyngo executable was found on the current shell PATH. Existing
licensed installations elsewhere have not been ruled out. Solver binaries
were not acquired or run. The paper says Dyngo source is available on request;
no external contact was made.

The raw RT archive includes reduction inputs, but the Zenodo file inventory
has only HPM-1_100K_JANA.zip, not a public RT HPM-1 JANA refinement archive.
The published detailed HPM-1 CIF also concerns 100K. The exact RT starting
model, refinement constraints, output reflections, solver version and stopping
settings therefore remain missing for an exact reproduction of 0.234/0.306.
An independently specified fair reanalysis is possible after solver access,
but must be labeled reanalysis rather than exact source replay.

To materialize reviewable PETS2 branches when solver access exists, run
python3 prepare_hpm_replay.py /absolute/staging/directory . It verifies the
raw archive pin, extracts the source files and writes two configurations:
source_with_fills.pts2 (unchanged inclusion) and detector_files_only.pts2
(the 22 deterministic fill use flags are set to 0). Both retain the same
source-supplied geometry. They do not independently calibrate the instrument.

Fair comparison and admissible result
------------------------------------
- Fix detector viewing convention, goniometer rotation sense and orientation
  calibration before revealing the XRD reference hand. A mirrored detector
  convention can swap the inferred hand even in a dynamical calculation.
  Using the parent XRD label to choose this convention is calibration; the
  same label cannot then be counted as an independent validation case.
- Build both enantiomorphs consistently: transform all coordinates, symmetry
  operators, displacement tensors and disorder/occupancy relations. Negating
  atoms while silently retaining the old enantiomorphic space group is invalid.
- Run the strongest applicable full dynamical incumbent on the identical raw
  files, atom types, calibration, reference model and computational budget.
  A kinematical method is the exact nonidentifying control, not the comparator
  for any advantage claim. Preserve fit failures and return UNKNOWN when the
  hands cannot be distinguished after nuisance uncertainty.
- Run both source-compatible and fill-excluded branches, with paired nuisance
  fits and identical observation masks. Report both; do not select whichever
  gives the larger hand gap. Treat interpolated and overlapping frames as
  correlated. Use held-out angular blocks with overlap guards if reporting
  out-of-sample scoring, and disclose coordinates/orientation learned globally.
- Sweep thickness, tilt/excitation error, shape/thickness distribution, frame
  scales, background, inelastic/absorption treatments, detector masks, dose
  drift and guest disorder. A ten-point fixed-thickness grid is not a bound
  over continuous nuisance parameters.
- Output the hand assignment with its defined model/nuisance scope, or UNKNOWN.
  Report source agreement separately from independent truth, novelty and cost.
  One source-linked parent/fragment pair is an admission benchmark, not general
  error-rate validation. A future validation needs additional independent
  known-hand crystals with frozen calibration and a held-out hand key.

independent exposures would count filled frames. Neither number is full beam-on

Computational costs must include raw integration and optical/orientation
calibration, both-hand dynamical simulation, all nuisance fitting, held-out
checks and measurement selection. Retrospectively selecting two reflections
after reading a complete indexed structure/data set has not reduced the
acquisition cost already paid. No such dose/time saving is claimed here.

Actual local measured execution times are recorded in the audit JSON files.
They are file/provenance and source-prediction audits, not complete solver time.
Inference tokens, cost and energy are UNKNOWN; no telemetry estimate is made.
