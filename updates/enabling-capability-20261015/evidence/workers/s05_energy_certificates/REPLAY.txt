are implied. Installation/download costs are in inputs/ACQUISITION.json.

paths are fallback for the original workspace. The binary64 matrix snapshot

adds the one declared Pauli split. The initial full-input bridge timing is kept
in EXACT_BRIDGE.json. A replay overwrites these current-run result JSON files
with fresh execution timing and updates the receipt when compose_receipt runs.
Preserve a copy before a replay if the original execution timing is needed.

The full matrix factor and one-shot selected certificates are computation_search
work. Frozen source and result snapshots are under inputs; their saved vector
and runnable upstream environment remain in that worker directory. Their
rounding model is explicitly recorded. This receipt transfers their checked
matrix intervals by the exact spectral-norm bridge; it does not replace a
factor replay with a numerical eigensolver estimate.

The exact input-to-matrix bridge consumes the full determinant matrix and is
not itself an omitted-space-free operation. The compact pair lower method does
avoid that matrix, but fails the scientific interval target. Its upper bound
comes from the separately paid incumbent and is included for a matched output.
