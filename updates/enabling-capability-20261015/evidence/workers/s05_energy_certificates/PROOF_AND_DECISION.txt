1. Exact decimal-to-matrix bridge
--------------------------------
Every one- and two-electron input field is an exact integer divided by 10^16.
Expand the real chemists' integrals by their eightfold permutation symmetry,
checking duplicate consistency. Order spin orbitals alpha first, beta second.
For i<j and k<l, define the antisymmetrized pair coefficient

  V_ij,kl = (i k | j l) delta_spin(i,k) delta_spin(j,l)
            -(i l | j k) delta_spin(i,l) delta_spin(j,k).

The operator is

  H = Ecore + sum_pq h_pq a_p^* a_q
            + sum_{i<j,k<l} V_ij,kl a_i^* a_j^* a_l a_k.

Build the declared Ag determinant list independently using XOR characters of
occupied orbitals. For each monomial, remove/create particles in its action
order and multiply by (-1) to the number of occupied lower-index orbitals.
Accumulate all terms as unbounded Python integers; verify the final int64
storage range explicitly. This produces an exactly symmetric integer matrix
A_num with 167424 nonzero entries and denominator S=10^16. No floating-point
arithmetic occurs in the Hamiltonian coefficient sum or Slater signs.

Let A_f be the exact real matrix defined by computation_search's saved binary64
entries. Convert each binary64 entry to its exact integer ratio. A common
denominator S*2^K then permits exact integer row sums of

3. Compact orbital-pair certificate: proof and real-data failure
--------------------------------------------------------------
Let m be the number of spin orbitals and N the particle number. Define the
positive pair lift

  L2(K) = sum_{i<j,k<l} K_ij,kl a_i^* a_j^* a_l a_k.

If K>=0, factor K=R^*R. For each factor row, set
B_alpha=sum_{k<l} R_alpha,kl a_l a_k. Then
L2(K)=sum_alpha B_alpha^* B_alpha >=0 on Fock space.

Let D2(h) be the action of a one-particle matrix h on the antisymmetric
two-particle space (h acting on each of the two particles). On the N-particle
space, each particle appears in N-1 pairs, so

  L2(D2(h)) = (N-1) dGamma(h),
  L2(I_pair) = N(N-1)/2 I.

Consequently, if

  K = V + D2(h)/(N-1) >= ell I_pair,

then the exact N-particle Hamiltonian obeys

  H >= Ecore + N(N-1)/2 ell.

This does not acquire the N-particle determinant matrix. Its coefficient
matrix has dimension C(m,2), here 190, and it is a sum-of-squares certificate
with quadratic pair-annihilation operators. It is already the pair-positive
D fragment of the v2RDM dual, rather than a new representation.

Apply the exact particle-hole transform a_p -> b_p^*. For real integrals, the
two-hole coefficient matrix is V^T=V, the full-occupancy shift is

  E_full = Ecore + Tr(h_spin) + sum_{p<q} V_pq,pq,

and the spatial hole one-body matrix is

  h_hole[p,q] = -h[p,q] - sum_r (2(p q|r r)-(p r|r q)).

There are 6 holes. The same pair-lift certificate applied to these holes is the
Q fragment of the fermionic SOS cone. The transform and all coefficient sums
in compact_pair_certificate.py are exact integer calculations. Conversion of
each 190-dimensional matrix to binary64 is bridged by the same exact rational
row-norm calculation. A PSD factor and a residual bound certify its ell. The
explicit numerical arithmetic assumptions are the standard IEEE roundoff
model, BLAS dot-product gamma_n bounds, absence of under/overflow, and correctly
rounded positive summation. These assumptions are distinct from a formal
machine-checked floating-point proof.

Actual results, without an oracle ground vector in the lower-bound method:

  electron D-pair lower:  -318.5566434072258 Eh
  hole Q-pair lower:      -110.33189667663824 Eh
  best Ag interval width:    2.6777742295587217 Eh

The lower bound applies to the entire N-electron sector and hence its Ag
compression; the upper bound is the separately paid incumbent Ag upper.
Thus the cheap lower representation fails the same 1 kcal/mol target by more
than three orders of magnitude. The computational target was not silently
changed to an estimated energy or a residual score.

One additional Pauli-aware split was fixed without tuning. For any one-body F,

  H = E_full + dGamma(F)
      + L2(V + D2(h_hole-F)/(Nhole-1)).

The minimum of dGamma(F) in the Nhole-particle sector is the sum of its Nhole
lowest one-particle eigenvalues. Here choose F exactly equal to the supplied
orbital diagonal of h_hole, so this sum is obtained by sorting exact integers.
The certified split floor is -111.97307580878275 Eh, weaker than the unsplit
hole-pair floor. No longer tuning campaign is justified. This split also lies
inside the existing SOS/v2RDM cone, because its free-fermion positivity and pair
positivity are certificates in that algebra plus the particle-number ideal.
