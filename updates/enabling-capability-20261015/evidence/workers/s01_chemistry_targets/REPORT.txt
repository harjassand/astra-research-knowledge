The useful mathematical operation is a conditional decision certificate. If every target/side observation has absolute error<=epsilon, then a matched utility gap g=dT-lambda*dS lies in [g-2epsilon(1+lambda),g+2epsilon(1+lambda)], including arbitrary dependence and reused controls. Strict preference is certified only when this interval excludes zero. The bound is sharp without further joint constraints. Common positive affine calibration of T and S preserves Pareto order and merely reparameterizes lambda; unknown cross-talk, per-well gains or censored zeros do not.
