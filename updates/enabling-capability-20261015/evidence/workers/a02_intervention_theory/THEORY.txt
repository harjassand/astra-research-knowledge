NONLINEAR INTERVENTION TARGETS WITH FINITE CALIBRATION
2026-10-15 | a02_intervention_theory | source_derived_unreviewed

Decision: STOP a generic enabling-operation claim from this interface. Retain
the exact countermodels and proofs as an intervention/calibration audit.
No novel physical capability, physical experiment, or novelty-cleared theorem
is claimed. All positive repairs below have strong established incumbents.

Scientific target
-----------------
Determine the physical delivered dose theta needed to attain a specified
response q, without learning an entire unknown nonlinear response curve or
actuator. This is a control-relevant physical quantity, unlike prediction
error in instrument coordinates. Dose is normalized to a calibrated [0,1]
range; it is not an arbitrary latent variable once physical units are fixed.

Information interface
---------------------
A command c generates an unobserved delivered dose X with law K_c. Conditional
on X=x, an assay output has law R_x. Let r(x)=E[Y|X=x] be strictly increasing,
and theta=r^{-1}(q). The experimenter may choose arbitrary commands, including
adaptively from previous outputs. Dose is reset independently between trials
conditional on command. The entire observable kernel Q_c is available in the
infinite-data idealization; the obstruction is not a small-data problem.

Calibration exposes exact endpoint anchors and the first m population moments
of one reference delivered-dose law. These are the only dose-calibration
observables. In particular, this is NOT an interface exposing arbitrarily
many raw calibrated physical-dose samples, a known additive-noise actuator
family, a known delivered-dose CDF, or a known conservation/additivity law.
Any of those additional structures can invalidate this countermodel and must
be independently acquired or justified rather than silently supplied.

1. Exact observational equivalence, including adaptive interventions
------------------------------------------------------------------
For an increasing bijection psi of [0,1] fixing its endpoints, define

  K'_c = psi pushforward of K_c,
  R'_z = R_{psi^{-1}(z)}.

Then integral R'_z K'_c(dz) = integral R_x K_c(dx) for every command c.
Thus all command/output laws agree. Inductively, an adaptive policy sees
the same distribution of histories, makes identically distributed commands,
and receives identically distributed outputs at the next step. Randomized
policies are covered by coupling their internal randomness. This holds for
every horizon and stopping rule for which the transcript is defined.

The transformed physical threshold is theta'=psi(theta). Assay noise can be
arbitrary inside R_x; it need not be removed or estimated for the equivalence.
The two worlds are distinct physical actuator/response models in the same
fixed dose units, not a declaration that physical units may be renamed.

2. Explicit analytic mean/variance-preserving counterexample
----------------------------------------------------------
Let reference X be Uniform[0,1], response r_A(x)=x, and target q=1/4. Add any
common independent zero-mean assay noise if desired. Define

 psi(x) = x(38808 x^4 - 97020 x^3 + 83314 x^2 - 27951 x + 4888)/2039.

Exact rational identities are

  psi(0)=0, psi(1)=1,
  integral_0^1 psi(x) dx = 1/2,
  integral_0^1 psi(x)^2 dx = 1/3.

For u=(2x-1)^2,

  psi'(x) = (24255/4078)(u-89/210)^2 + 34469/81560 > 0.

So psi is an analytic increasing diffeomorphism. Model B delivers psi(X) and
has response r_B=psi^{-1}. Both models have exactly the same full output law,
endpoint anchors, reference mean 1/2 and variance 1/12. Nevertheless,

  theta_A = 1/4,
  theta_B = 55777/260992 = 0.21371153138793525...

The dose differs by about 14.5154% of theta_A. No estimator on this interface
can have uniformly smaller absolute error than half this separation. For
any confidence set with coverage at least 1-alpha in each world, the chance
that it contains both thresholds is at least 1-2alpha, under the common data
law. On that event its diameter is at least the separation. These are ordinary
two-indistinguishable-world statistical consequences, not new optimality
claims.

3. Any finite number of reference moments and anchors leaves local ambiguity
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theorem. Let f be a reference density positive almost everywhere on (0,1),
m>=1 a finite integer, A a finite set of calibrated interior anchors, and
theta in (0,1) outside A. For all sufficiently small real t there is an
increasing polynomial diffeomorphism psi_t fixing 0,1 and A, preserving
integral psi_t(x)^k f(x)dx = integral x^k f(x)dx for k=1,...,m, and satisfying
psi_t(theta)=theta+t.

Proof. Put w(x)=x(1-x) product_{a in A}(x-a)^2. Define v0=w/w(theta) and
vj=w(x)(x-theta)^2 x^{j-1}, j=1,...,m. Set

  psi_{t,b}(x)=x+t v0(x)+sum_j b_j vj(x),
  Phi_k(t,b)=integral [psi_{t,b}(x)^k-x^k] f(x)dx.

Phi is a polynomial map in the finite parameters (t,b). At (0,0), its
b-Jacobian has entries

  J_kj=k integral x^{k+j-2} w(x)(x-theta)^2 f(x)dx.

J=D H where D=diag(1,...,m) and H is the Gram matrix of the monomials
1,x,...,x^{m-1} in the positive weight w(x)(x-theta)^2 f(x)dx. A nonzero
polynomial is nonzero off a finite set, so this Gram matrix is strictly
positive definite. J is invertible. The analytic implicit-function theorem
gives b(t), with b(0)=0 and Phi(t,b(t))=0. For small t, the derivative of
psi_{t,b(t)} is uniformly close to 1, hence positive. All perturbations
vanish at the endpoints and anchors. Each vj(theta)=0 and v0(theta)=1,
so psi_t(theta)=theta+t exactly. QED.

Analyticity of the unknown response is therefore not a repair: start from
an analytic response and compose with the analytic inverse of psi_t.
The theorem is for finitely many moments of ONE reference ensemble. Do not
misstate it as a theorem about arbitrary collections of calibrated ensembles:
special linked ensembles can enforce rigidity, as the next section shows.

4. A useful limitation of the no-go: coupled calibration can kill the gauge
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
If X has reference law K0 and mu=E[X]>0, consider the physically size-biased
law K1(dx)=x K0(dx)/mu. Suppose psi preserves E_K0[X], E_K0[X^2], and
E_K1[X]. Then E_K0[X psi(X)] = E_K0[X^2]. Hence

 E_K0[(psi(X)-X)^2]
 = E_K0[psi(X)^2] + E_K0[X^2] - 2 E_K0[X psi(X)] = 0.

Thus psi(X)=X almost surely; with full support and continuity, psi is the
identity. Approximate versions bound the displayed squared discrepancy by
the sum of the corresponding moment errors.

This is an elementary coupling identity, NOT a new control operation. The
load-bearing input is an independently verified physical size-bias coupling.
Preparing or recognizing it requires information proportional to the actual
dose, not merely to the command. Supplying K1 by definition would introduce
the very physical measurement operation the task is supposed to acquire.
This also explains why a blanket claim that every finite calibration system
must fail would be false.

5. Sharp global ambiguity for first two moments
-----------------------------------------------
In the reference experiment suppose the full noiseless response is uniform,
Y~Uniform[0,1], r is an unknown smooth increasing diffeomorphism of [0,1],
and the actual delivered dose has a smooth strictly positive density with

  E[X]=1/2, E[X^2]=1/3.

Then the exact identified set for theta=r^{-1}(1/4) is (0,2/3), with closure
[0,2/3]. This is a statement about this specified interface, not all control
systems.

Necessity. F_X(theta)=1/4. Strict positivity gives theta>0. Also
E[X] > theta P(X>theta) = 3 theta/4, so theta<2/3.

Sufficiency. Fix any theta in (0,2/3). Let p=1/4. Choose a>0 sufficiently
small that a<theta and b-s>theta, b+s<1, where

  b=2/3-a/3,  s^2=4a(1-a)/9.

The three atoms a,b-s,b+s with weights 1/4,3/8,3/8 have the required first
two moments and put exactly 1/4 of their mass below theta. To obtain a smooth
strictly positive density rather than atoms, mix in a sufficiently small
delta>0 fraction of Uniform[0,1]. Change the low-cluster weight to

  w=(p-delta theta)/(1-delta).

Replace the three atoms by symmetric smooth compactly supported bumps with
common small variance nu and sufficiently narrow supports. Set

  b=(1/2-w a)/(1-w),
  s^2=[1/3-w a^2-(1-w)b^2-nu]/(1-w).

At delta=nu=0 this reduces to the original strictly separated construction;
continuity makes the choices valid for small positive delta and nu. Mean
and second moment are exactly the required values. All the low bump lies
below theta and both high bumps lie above theta, so the final CDF satisfies
F_X(theta)=delta theta+(1-delta)w=p. The density is smooth and at least delta
on [0,1]. Define r=F_X. Then r is a smooth increasing diffeomorphism and
r(X) is Uniform[0,1]. The target is exactly theta. QED.

An output-noise channel applied after r(X) preserves indistinguishability.
Other command laws can be transformed as in section 1, preserving their
entire observable laws too. The numerical script checks explicit positive-
density, piecewise-smooth versions at eight different target doses; the
smooth-bump proof above is the regularity argument, not that finite replay.

6. Attainable repair and strongest incumbent
--------------------------------------------
If a reference physical-dose CDF F is independently calibrated, and the
response is monotone without unmodeled trial-to-trial biological response
variability, one does not need the full response curve. For threshold q,
the ideal binary assay B0=1{r(X)<=q} has P(B0=1)=F(theta). If its calibrated
false-positive and false-negative probabilities are alpha,beta with
lambda=1-alpha-beta>0, observed success frequency p satisfies

  theta=F^{-1}((p-alpha)/lambda).

This is standard transformation/quantile matching, not a novelty candidate.
For known F, alpha,beta and density f>=fmin>0 near the target, Hoeffding gives
the dose error bound sqrt(log(2/delta)/(2n))/(lambda fmin). A uniform CDF
calibration error epsilon_F adds epsilon_F/fmin. If alpha,beta each have
certified error at most epsilon_L, a valid additional term is
epsilon_L/(lambda_hat fmin) when the plug-in lambda_hat is positive.
The probability budget for the separate calibration events must also be
included. Sample dependence, variable error rates, or stochastic latent
response invalidate this simple formula and require their own analysis.

Acquiring F to uniform accuracy epsilon_F from physically measured doses
itself needs an appropriate reference sensor and, for the elementary DKW
bound, at least log(2/delta_F)/(2 epsilon_F^2) independent dose samples.
Acquiring assay-error rates requires actual known-positive/known-negative
standards. None of these samples, standards, or devices were obtained by
this worker. They cannot be credited as free inputs or replaced by a command
distribution. An equally informed incumbent can use the same quantile method.

7. Prior-art boundary
---------------------
Santos (2011), Instrumental variable methods for recovering continuous
linear functionals, Journal of Econometrics 161(2), 129-146,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2010.11.014
already estimates task functionals when the whole response and nuisance
models are not identified. Therefore 'identify a target without identifying
the full model' is not by itself a new mathematical operation. Our threshold
and actuator-gauge construction is nonlinear, but no exhaustive novelty
claim for this specific construction has been established.

Hothorn, Moest and Buehlmann (2018), Most Likely Transformations,
https://stat.ethz.ch/Manuscripts/buhlmann/mlt-SJS2018.pdf
develop conditional transformation-model estimation. This is close prior
art for the calibrated-CDF/quantile repair. A known reference distribution
and model restrictions supply the scale; they do not arise from moment
calibration alone.

Zhang and Hyvarinen (UAI 2009; arXiv posting 2012), On the Identifiability
of the Post-Nonlinear Causal Model,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.2599
show that restrictive noise/structural assumptions can identify nonlinear
causal direction in broad cases. Their result is not a contradiction to an
unknown-actuator/unknown-response gauge, and their identifying assumptions
must not be silently imported as acquired physical controls.

Zhu, Gultchin, Gretton, Kusner and Silva (UAI 2022), Causal inference with
treatment measurement error: a nonparametric instrumental variable approach,
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v180/zhu22a.html
is direct prior art for causal treatment-error recovery with additional
instrument/proxy structure. It prevents presenting generic nonlinear
measurement-error correction as new.

Villaverde, Tsiantis and Banga (2019), Full observability and estimation of
unknown inputs, states and parameters of nonlinear biological models,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6685009/
analyze joint unknown-input/state/parameter observability and separate
structural identifiability from finite-data estimation. Their supplied ODE
structure differs from this nonparametric interface; both emphasize that
unknown inputs and unknown plant behavior cannot be audited independently.

Duleba and Sasiadek (2001; earlier IFAC paper 1998), Calibration of controls
in steering nonholonomic systems,
https://doi.org/10.1016/S0967-0661(00)00096-4
already use Lie-bracket direction generation and control calibration to
reduce remainder effects. A bare bracket-probing alternative was rejected
as an enabling novelty candidate before deeper derivation.

8. Evidence and decision
------------------------
The exact polynomial identities, derivative positivity and target movement
are checked using standard-library rational arithmetic. The adaptive noisy
trace and eight global-range constructions are explicitly synthetic.
No real physical threshold benchmark satisfying this interface was acquired.
The work therefore provides a falsification benchmark for inference claims,
not an achieved scientific control operation. A physical benchmark must
retain delivered-dose calibration data, target-threshold standards, noise
conditions, and an equally informed quantile/measurement-error incumbent.

Next useful decision: require the candidate operation to name which acquired
interface breaks this dose gauge. If only commands, outcomes, endpoint
anchors and finitely many reference moments are available, STOP physical-
threshold point-identification claims. If a verified CDF or paired physical
readout is available, test task-specific estimation against the established
incumbent and charge that acquisition. No generic full-model bypass advantage
has survived the present audit.
