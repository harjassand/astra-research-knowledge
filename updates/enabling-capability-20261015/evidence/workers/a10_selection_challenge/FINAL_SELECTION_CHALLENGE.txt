FINAL INDEPENDENT SELECTION CHALLENGE — a10
2026-10-15. Scope pinned to WAVE_3_PIN.json and acquired sources/results below.

Decision
Do not replace the chemistry allocation with this gravitational-wave route. Do not increase chemistry invention effort on the strength of the ORD row count alone. The alternative was tested on actual open observations and yields a useful conditional certificate and decisive countermodels, but it fails the new-capability and equally informed incumbent gates. Mission status: NOT_ACHIEVED. Generic mixed-polarization norm recovery: STOP under the acquired four-channel interface. Restricted scalar-only reanalysis: technically obtainable, scientifically conditional, already covered by established inference machinery; HOLD as a new enabling operation.

The constructive alternative tested
Use an independently localized astrophysical transient and a geographically distributed physical detector network to test whether its strain lies in the tensor response subspace, without supplying a detailed waveform. GW170817 is consequential because extra polarization would constrain gravitational physics. Its optical counterpart fixes the sky independently of the strain fit. The acquisition interface is real and open: H1, L1, V1 and the often overlooked G1/GEO600 strain streams; instrument quality flags; detector geometry; source-calibration uncertainty summaries; and published incumbent methods. The unexpected fourth stream suggested a concrete hypothesis: a weak detector with a distinct orientation might recover an otherwise almost invisible scalar direction. This was measured, then challenged against the matched incumbent and physical limits.

Actual evidence acquired
The four 32-second, 4096-Hz HDF5 clips contain 131,072 calibrated strain samples each and total 4,111,304 bytes. ACQUISITION_RECEIPT_v2.json records final URLs, HTTP status, hashes, byte counts, and per-request elapsed times. The GWTC-1 event-specific calibration archive is 6,038 bytes and contains H,L,V tables. The independently observed optical position is RA 197.450374 degrees, Dec -23.381495 degrees from the Dark Energy Survey primary source. Detector responses and arrival-time shifts were computed with LALSuite 7.26.16 (lal module 7.7.1), with the official response source saved. The data represent source-calibrated strain, not independently acquired raw photodetector truth or new experimental control.

Calibrated-frequency and data-quality limits
The official GW170817 release says GEO600 was not in science mode during the event because of a technical noise issue below 1 kHz. Its event-specific calibration uncertainty is stated as within 15% amplitude and 15 degrees phase in 1–4 kHz. The HDF5 G1 DQmask nevertheless reads 127 throughout: blindly treating every set bit as unrestricted scientific validity would be wrong. The general GWOSC usage guidance limits 4096-Hz GEO products near 1620 Hz; the O2 documentation warns that near/above 1700 Hz one must use 16384-Hz data. Accordingly the corrected comparison uses only 1000–1500 Hz. Exploratory v1 values at 1750 and 1900 Hz are not admissible scientific results. HLV calibration tables give medians and marginal one-sigma uncertainties, not deterministic joint bounds. The raw L1 clip contains the documented glitch at GPS 1187008881.389, 1.041 s before merger; its Burst CAT2 flags are cleared over GPS 1187008880–1187008884. The 32-second clips also omit much of the BNS inspiral. Public access to bytes is not acquisition of every inference premise.

Concrete mathematical mechanism
MATHEMATICAL_MECHANISM.txt gives the complete derivation. For whitened observations y=C(Ah+Bv)+n, eliminate the arbitrary two-component tensor waveform with the null projector of A. With three independent long-wavelength extra modes, HLV leaves an extra-mode nullspace of dimension two; adding G1 reduces it only to dimension one. Explicit unit extra-mode vectors with compensating tensor modes leave residual norms 1.24e-16 (HLV) and 2.61e-16 (HLVG) in floating-point replay. The proof is dimensional: rank(P_A B)<=number_of_detectors-2. Arbitrarily small nonzero mixed alternatives are exactly indistinguishable in the observation model. Arbitrarily large norm is unbounded within that linear model, not a claim that every large-amplitude alternative extends to a full physical gravity solution. More frequency samples do not fix the rank when waveforms are arbitrary. Finite-arm corrections do not allow four channels to identify six unrestricted modes.

For exactly three channels, a second mechanism gives a closed-form robust compatibility certificate. If |C_i-1|<=epsilon_i<1, inverse gain g_i lies in the disk centered at c_i=1/(1-epsilon_i^2) with radius r_i=epsilon_i/(1-epsilon_i^2). Let w* A=0 and ||w||=1. The exact minimum of |w* C^(-1)y| is
    m(y) = max(|sum conjugate(w_i)c_i y_i| - sum |w_i y_i|r_i, 0).
This follows because the sum of independent complex disks is a disk. The code constructs minimizing gains explicitly. Dividing m(y)^2 by sum |w_i|^2/(1-epsilon_i)^2 yields a conservative conditional noise-normalized statistic. Under exactly known independent complex Gaussian noise it has an exponential upper-tail bound; no such physical p-value is claimed for the small data clip. Ten thousand finite numerical checks found no false rejection of the constructed tensor-plus-permitted-gain cases; the maximum formula error was 2.71e-15. These checks support the implementation, while the algebra supplies the conditional proof. Novelty is unresolved, and no novelty claim is made.

Measured restricted-mode benefit and equal-information comparator
If vector modes are excluded and calibration/response are supplied, the scalar Fisher information is b*W P_(WA) Wb. A weak fourth detector helps when the first three detectors nearly confuse scalar response with tensor response. With the real 12-second post-event PSD estimate, adding G1 gives a median ideal information ratio 15.5267 across 1000–1500 Hz, with 5th/95th percentiles 2.4406/64.7916; the median amplitude-precision ratio is 3.9404. These are conditional per-frequency geometry/PSD diagnostics, not a measured upper limit on astrophysical scalar power.
An independent ordinary weighted-least-squares Schur-complement implementation obtains the same information. The maximum relative difference is 5.23e-12. The baseline sees exactly the same detector streams, sky, PSD, response, and excluded-vector assumption. Thus the information gain comes from sensor inclusion, not a new computational operation. A calibration-profile likelihood with the same uncertainty set can also be at least as sharp as the conservative disk lower bound. Full cWB/BayesWave pipelines were not rerun; their more extensive glitch/background handling was not credited to our small replay.

Actual model-misspecification counterexample and preserved correction
The first diagnostic incorrectly smoothed the PSD over 25 Hz, erasing narrow instrumental lines. It produced nominal HLV null energies around 135–165 even in ordinary windows. This was a preprocessing failure, not a signal. NETWORK_AUDIT_v1.json, NETWORK_RUN_v1.txt and analyze_network_v1.py preserve it; the unsuffixed NETWORK_AUDIT.json is the same superseded v1 result. Corrected v2 retains resolved spectral lines and restricts the frequency band. Ordinary 50–500-Hz windows then have mean null statistics about 0.75–1.60. The documented L1 glitch has nominal statistic 28.5391, and remains 16.1489 under the expanded three-sigma calibration-envelope sensitivity scenario. Those envelopes are not hard bounds; the comparison illustrates the failure even after substantial gain robustness. An anomalous null residual beyond calibration tolerance can still be instrumental noise. Calibration robustness does not acquire a glitch-free physical cause. REAL_DISK_DIAGNOSTIC.json preserves all windows and assumptions.

Novelty and strongest incumbent
Pang et al., arXiv:2003.07375 (2020), already applied waveform-independent null-stream polarization tests to GW170817 with an electromagnetic counterpart. Wong et al., arXiv:2105.09485 (2021), developed a generic polarization basis framework. Cheung et al., arXiv:2507.02335 (2025), used a Gaussian-process extra-polarization model on 42 catalog events; its structured stochastic assumptions must not be confused with uniform recovery of arbitrary coherent mixtures. Wong et al., arXiv:2510.06327 (2025), explicitly studied waveform-independent null-stream calibration for closed-geometry future networks. cWB/X-Pipeline also have long-established coherent null-energy machinery. These are prior-art constraints on this selection, not claims that our calculation refutes their scoped results. The exact disk expression is a small analytic certificate; it has not been established as novel or practically superior.

Challenge to the overall allocation
The pinned wave brief describes multiple routes with a common risk: spending deep invention effort after the desired scientific target is blocked by an unacquired interface or an equal-information incumbent. For chemistry, desired/regioisomer labels and matching controls do not replace raw spectral/calibration access or independent mixture truth; authentic-standard UV least squares is already the incumbent. For the small N2 computation, the brief reports a very fast Davidson incumbent and a weak certificate. For LiF and microscopy, physical target identifiability and known methods remain gates. These are observations about the pinned brief, not independent audits of other workers' later results. Foundational mathematics should retain its separate exact-statement/proof gate and should not be penalized for lacking a laboratory interface. None of this supports selecting a familiar measurable proxy as the new scientific output.

Constructive allocation rule supported by this experiment
Require a candidate to show (1) the actual measurement/control bytes or executable physical-truth interface, (2) the target's observational-equivalence class, and (3) the strongest equally informed decision comparator before increasing its allocation. A new sensor, calibration experiment, perturbation, or representation may change item 2; a faster certificate may change cost. More rows, more residual energy, or more investigators by themselves change neither. For the tested gravity route, all available data remain worth preserving as a small reproducible falsification benchmark. No wholesale reallocation is justified. Additional work on scalar bounds would be ordinary scientifically conditional reanalysis and would need longer independent noise data, validated calibration correspondence and uncertainty coverage, finite-arm response, and a source-specific scientific question. None is asserted acquired merely from this replay.
