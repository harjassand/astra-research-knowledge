CALIBRATION-ROBUST NULL CERTIFICATE AND ITS LIMITS
2026-10-15; a10 independent selection challenge. Source-derived internal development. No novelty claim.

Scientific target and observed interface
A network of calibrated strain observations y at a known optical sky location contains tensor response A h, possible extra-polarization response B v, detector calibration gains C, and additive noise n:
    y = C (A h + B v) + n.
Whitening can be absorbed in A,B,y,n because diagonal whitening commutes with diagonal gains. Every frequency may have arbitrary complex h,v. No phase/waveform family is supplied. Observed public strain is already an instrument-calibrated product, not unprocessed photodiode truth.

1. Rank obstruction, including a fourth weak detector
With known gains, noise, and sky, eliminate the arbitrary tensor waveform by P_A = I - A A^+. The only observable extra content is P_A B v. If A has rank 2 and B has q=3 independent long-wavelength extra modes (breathing, vector x, vector y), rank(P_A B) <= m-2 for m detectors. HLV with m=3 leaves at least two invisible extra directions; HLVG with m=4 leaves at least one. If v0 is in ker(P_A B), put h0=-A^+Bv0. Then A h0+Bv0=0. For any waveform g(f), adding (h0 g,v0 g) changes no observation, while the extra-mode norm can be arbitrarily large. This remains true if calibration uncertainty is admitted, because known nominal calibration is a member of that model. More frequency bins do not remove the obstruction when the mode waveforms are arbitrary; a frequency-dependent null vector can be used. Model-specific regularity/coupling laws may change the question.
The arbitrary large amplitude statement is a linear observation-model identifiability statement. It does not assert that all amplitudes extend to a globally physically consistent strong-field gravity solution. Arbitrarily small nonzero alternatives already establish local nonidentifiability.

The saved GW170817 long-wavelength response produced rank 3 for HLV and rank 4 for HLVG. Unit extra vectors with tensor compensators give residual norms 1.24e-16 and 2.61e-16 in floating-point replay. The dimensional argument is the proof; these decimals are a source-specific witness. Longitudinal and breathing are degenerate in the long-wavelength trace-free detector model. Finite-arm response changes that exact degeneracy, but never rescues full six-mode identifiability from only four output channels without further assumptions.

2. Restricted scalar measurement and weak-sensor mechanism
If vector modes are EXCLUDED, write B=b. With known Gaussian noise covariance S, the profile Fisher information for scalar amplitude is
    I_b = b* S^(-1/2) P_(S^(-1/2) A) S^(-1/2) b.
Adding a sensor cannot reduce I_b. It can help strongly when the existing b is nearly in the tensor column space, even if that sensor is individually noisy. This is standard generalized least squares and sensor geometry, not new physics or a new algorithm. A detector with poor sensitivity outside its characterized frequency band is not thereby a valid new measurement channel. Actual GW170817 PSD estimates do show an idealized high-frequency information gain from G1, but equally informed GLS gets this identical gain. Excluding vector modes is a material scientific assumption.

3. Exact three-detector gain-disk compatibility formula
Assume m=3, rank(A)=2, and let w be a unit vector with w* A=0. Suppose each complex calibration gain satisfies |C_i-1| <= epsilon_i < 1. Inversion maps this disk EXACTLY to
    g_i = C_i^(-1) in disk(c_i,r_i),
    c_i = 1/(1-epsilon_i^2), r_i = epsilon_i/(1-epsilon_i^2).
Proof: |1/g-1|<=epsilon is equivalent to |1-g|^2<=epsilon^2 |g|^2; complete the square.
For a noiseless tensor-only observation, compatibility is equivalent to
    sum_i conjugate(w_i) g_i y_i = 0.
Because the g_i vary independently in complex disks, the left side fills exactly a disk of center
    z0 = sum_i conjugate(w_i) c_i y_i
and radius
    R = sum_i |w_i y_i| r_i.
The Minkowski sum of disks is a disk. Therefore the exact minimum absolute transformed residual is
    m(y) = max(|z0|-R,0).
The tensor-only noiseless model is gain-compatible iff m(y)=0. A constructive minimizing set of inverse gains is saved in calibration_disk.py. This is a direct disk-geometry calculation; no claim that the general idea is absent from robust inference prior art.

4. Conditional noisy certificate
For additive complex noise n with covariance I, at any fixed permissible C the scalar w* C^(-1)n has variance V(C)=sum |w_i/C_i|^2. This variance is at most
    Vmax = sum_i |w_i|^2/(1-epsilon_i)^2.
Consequently
    Tlower = m(y)^2 / Vmax
is no larger than |w* C^(-1)y|^2/V(C) for the true permissible C. Under tensor-only signal and independent circular complex Gaussian noise with exactly known whitening, the latter is Exp(mean=1). Thus P(Tlower>=t)<=exp(-t). Independence across Fourier bins would allow a Gamma bound for sums. Neither exactly known whitening nor independent bins nor hard calibration disks has been acquired from the small downloaded clip, so no physical p-value is asserted here.
For deterministic ||n||<=eta and arbitrary extra modes,
    ||v|| >= max(m(y)-eta sqrt(Vmax),0) / ||w*B||.
This is only a lower bound on visible extra content and does not overcome section 1's upper-bound impossibility. Instrumental noise outside the asserted n-set also triggers the certificate. Calibration robustness is not glitch robustness.

5. Exact local calibration countermodel
Let a tensor signal be s=A h with s_i nonzero. If an extra response e=Bv obeys |e_i|<=epsilon_i |s_i|, the mixed observation s+e with nominal calibration equals the pure-tensor observation C's where C'_i=1+e_i/s_i remains in the gain disk. Therefore small extra content can be exactly indistinguishable from allowed calibration, even before noise. A prior over C may choose one explanation, but cannot supply a uniform discrimination guarantee across both states.

6. Equal-information incumbent and scope
Known cWB/X-Pipeline/Pang/Wong methods already project arbitrary waveform responses; Cheung's GP method gives a structured stochastic extra-mode model. Our disk expression is a cheap explicit conservative alternative to calibration optimization/marginalization; exact nuisance-profile likelihood with the same gain set can only be equally sharp or sharper. At epsilon=0 the disk statistic reduces exactly to ordinary normalized null energy. There is no information gain, no attained scientific discovery, no proven novelty, and no demonstrated practical superiority over the strongest equally informed pipeline.
A useful scope-preserving artifact is an explicit conditional certificate plus exact countermodel, which makes the missing calibrated response and noise interfaces reviewable. It is not a reason to move the whole campaign into gravity.
