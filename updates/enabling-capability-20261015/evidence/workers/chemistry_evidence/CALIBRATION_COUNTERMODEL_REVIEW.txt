Disposition: algebra valid under the stated observation/model contract.
The exact Fraction-based two-channel fixture was independently read and run.
It gives identical observations (1,5/4) for concentrations(1/4,3/4), gains(1,1),
and concentrations(7/20,13/20), gains(1,25/27). These are rational model inputs,
not acquired spectra, measured drift, practical error magnitudes or chemical data.

For positive s,t and a,b, a small epsilon perturbation
(a',b')=(a+epsilon*t0,b-epsilon*s0) keeps both components positive and preserves
s0*a+t0*b. The prescribed positive componentwise gain ratio therefore gives
exactly the same vector and has g0=1. Continuity places the gains arbitrarily
close to1 when epsilon tends to0. Repeating the same mean observations with the
same additive-noise law does not identify this unresolved gain. No algebra flaw
was found. If physical total-yield bounds are required, choose concentrations in
their interior and epsilon small enough also to preserve those bounds.

Scope qualifications:
1. The observation contract uses the product wavelength vector and a scalar
   caffeine270nm response. Raw PDA contains a full caffeine wavelength spectrum.
   Independently stable, clean, contemporaneous caffeine spectral response can
   constrain relative gains, provided gain transfer from caffeine retention time
   to product retention time is bounded. The source pipeline uses only270nm,
   but the unacquired raw data may contain a discriminator it does not use.
2. A single unknown gain for a sample is not an arbitrary gain that automatically
   survives an entire independently calibrated pure-standard/known-mixture
   panel. If gains are physically common across runs/samples, those shared
   restrictions must be imposed in the countermodel. The lemma does not defeat
   such extra information without checking it.
3. A full identical-noise observation law is a legitimate countermodel under
   the stated additive-noise family. It does not assert that actual detector
   shot noise, gain/noise coupling, or thermal drift obey that family.
4. The physically allowed gain shape, magnitude, time dependence and smoothness
   must come from actual calibration. The construction alone gives no incidence
   or practically significant error bound for the source apparatus.
5. After relative gain is calibrated, an unknown coeluting absorber with spectral
   contribution in span(s,t) remains a separate composition ambiguity. Full
   caffeine response alone does not remove that ambiguity. Independent known
   mixtures, matrix controls, or same-sample orthogonal structural quantitation
   are still needed for the claimed matrix.

Scientific consequence: this is a useful explicit missing-calibration witness,
scoped inverse-problem reasoning. It is not a new chemistry operation, a claim
of novelty, an allegation that the source labels are wrong, or an experimental
demonstration that any such gain occurred.
