Strongest alternative physical model
--------------------------------------
Let x be the wavelength vector after peak fitting and caffeine normalization.
The published model is x=A*c+e, with fixed pure-standard basis A=[a3,a13].
A physically broader model is x=(A+DeltaA)*c+B*z+b+e, where DeltaA is wavelength-
dependent instrument/matrix/speciation drift, B*z is coeluting reagent/byproduct
absorbance, and b is residual baseline/peak-shape error. The caffeine scalar can
remove common injection scaling but cannot remove all these wavelength-dependent
terms. ALS and separate Gaussian fits need not preserve a crude mixture's pure-
standard spectral ratio. Different matrix backgrounds may correlate with recipe
or injection order, producing a plausible condition-dependent yield ranking.

This defeats a residual-only certificate exactly: if B*z+b=A*delta, then the
observed vector is identical to a sample with concentrations c+delta and zero
background. Arbitrarily small residuals do not bound delta. Calibration error
DeltaA*c in the same span has the same effect. Off-span residual tests also fail
to detect an in-span contaminant. This is an identifiability countermodel, not
evidence that a particular unknown contaminant actually occurred in these data.

The source code's covariance uses residual variance and inverse(J^T J) treating
the fitted pure spectra as fixed and wavelength observations as independent.
It does not propagate standard-purity/dilution uncertainty, calibration slope
uncertainty, shared caffeine noise, wavelength covariance or nuisance-model
selection. Its unconstrained least-squares fit can return negative concentrations.
Its displayed fit uncertainty alone therefore cannot establish calibrated
coverage, especially below the source's20% combined-yield cutoff. Better fit
or recovery of source labels can still be produced by this alternative model.

Minimal discriminator and prospective benchmark
------------------------------------------------
The smallest decisive check of one disputed real-sample result is an orthogonal
quantitative structural assay on the SAME actual sample, paired with authentic
3/13 co-spikes and an independently quantified reference. Use validated19F qNMR
if material and sensitivity permit, or an independently demonstrated resolving
LC method (authentic co-injections establish baseline resolution before calling
its integrals truth). Measure reference uncertainty and recovery; do not infer
truth from the published spectral basis. At HTE volumes, sufficient material for
qNMR is not assumed. Pooling or scale-up changes the scope and must be recorded.

A small blinded pure3, pure13,1:1 mixture and quenched-matrix blank test can
falsify a severe matrix/false-positive error quickly. Passing it establishes
only that pilot's scope. Reliable minor-isomer yield and accuracy across recipes
require the fuller contract in DECISIVE_ASSAY_CONTRACT.json and planned design
generator prospective_discriminator.py. The design is an unperformed experiment;
all acquired measurements, reference concentrations, costs and timings remain
null until measured. The incumbent and candidate must receive the same raw files
and calibration budget and be scored against independent truth after freezing.
