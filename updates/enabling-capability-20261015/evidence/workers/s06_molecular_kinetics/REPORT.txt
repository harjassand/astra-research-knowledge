Rate replay

Using all complete alternating-arrival intervals after the first actual basin
transition in each trajectory:
  A-to-B unfolding: 26 intervals / 91.5310 us = 0.2840567676524894 per us.
  B-to-A folding: 29 intervals / 11.4854 us = 2.5249447124175037 per us.
These reproduce the newer source reference to its reported precision. The
supplied older source's additional within-trajectory pairing omits one complete
1.1735 us unfolding interval, giving 0.2766787483053426 per us. Both older stored
rate arrays are reproduced exactly to numerical precision. All conventions and
empirical bootstrap intervals are retained in KINETIC_REPLAY.json; the bootstrap
does not prove independence or adequate sampling of rare tails.

The direct label scan finds 59 switches (30 unfolding,29 folding). The older
source pairing retains 29 in each direction, i.e.58; the paper also reports58.
These event counts are not the26/29 complete MFPT interval counts after removing
initial intervals. Keeping the counting convention explicit avoids a false
claim of extra independent kinetic information.

The 2,552-byte event skeleton reproduces the finite-record rate samples exactly.
This is ordinary sufficient-statistic compression after expensive data exists.
The published rate arrays themselves are already only 144 bytes each, so this
does not establish new output access, information gain or trajectory savings.

Matched representation challenge

On the identical acquired committor/state/time arrays, a reversible one-dimensional
MSM with 10/30/100 committor bins overestimates the two direct rates by about
15-18 times at 0.1 ns lag. At 10 ns lag the ratios are about 1.34-1.42; at 100 ns
they fall to 0.54-0.68. These are explicit conditional model results, not a claim
that all MSMs fail. The test does not isolate projection memory from learned
committor error, aggregation, or initial-distribution differences.

Retaining the static ensemble exactly but randomly permuting its time order gives
412.25 and 2448.04 per us. This is an intentionally nonphysical negative control.
Uniform thinning from 100 ps to 100 ns loses observed switches (59 to 37) and
changes the two rates to 0.2134 and 1.3333 per us. A learned configuration or
unclocked path distribution therefore cannot be scored as a molecular kinetics
operation merely because it matches the equilibrium structure distribution.

Strongest incumbents and full-cost comparison

Published RFPS-AIMMD is the direct matched-physics comparator, with AIMMD/TPS and
brute-force controls. At a source-reported 4 us combined production budget, the
three RFPS unfolding/reference ratios are 1.85,1.03,1.34, versus 4.17,3.73,2.53
for TPS-AIMMD. These six results are extracted from the acquired author notebook;
their enhanced-sampling runs were not independently replayed here.
https://zenodo.org/records/14922168

WE, exact milestoning, TPS with basin flux, and history-augmented MSMs are relevant
incumbents. No numerical ranking against them is justified without their own
matched runs. They must receive identical force field, integrator, source basin
ensembles, seed path, calibration information and production budget. Charge seed
generation, the 1 ns initial equilibration, additional free-state preparation,
shooting, training, full-system solvent evolution, and reweighting separately.
The source's 4 us number is simulated production exposure, not this worker's
wall time or a complete experimental acquisition cost. Current source reports
about 20 s training versus about half an hour per shooting simulation on its
hardware; original seed-path and complete hardware/energy costs are unknown.

A July 2026 submitted-manuscript implementation, slicedcommittor, already separates
static-density fitting from diffusion-clock acquisition. Its mobility/metric and
diffusion reduction assumptions must be checked; it is prior art, not a verified
new theorem or a performed comparator in this worker.

Next validation interface

An actual new kinetics operation must acquire correctly distributed short paths,
their local holding times and basin normalization with less total preparation
and production than RFPS/WE/milestoning. A prospective comparison needs fixed
rate/pathway targets, identical initial information, independent replicas and
an uncertainty criterion selected before seeing the reference. In experimental
use, measured physical clock/kinetics at the target conditions and force-field
discrepancy controls are additionally required. Neither interface was supplied
by this worker. Mathematical limits are in MATHEMATICAL_GATE.txt.

All executed timings, payload bounds, unresolved costs and replay instructions
are in COST_LEDGER.json and REPLAY_CONTRACT.json. Inference tokens, cost and energy
are UNKNOWN. A saved benchmark and successful replay establish their stated
finite computational scope only.
