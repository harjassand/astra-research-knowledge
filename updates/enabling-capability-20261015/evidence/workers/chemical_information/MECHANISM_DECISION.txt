ASTRA chemical information mechanism investigation, 2026-10-15

DECISION
STOP the proposed isotope connectivity encoding as a new-operation campaign for
the supplied two-substrate Cernak benchmark. HOLD any broader standard-free,
structure-resolved discovery claim pending a physically acquired assay and
independent product identities. This is not a conclusion that chemical discovery
is unimportant or exhausted. It is a conclusion about this proposed mechanism,
this target, and the evidence available in this run.

The target matters: correctly selecting a desired regioisomer can change which
reaction is pursued. But the existing source already measures these two pairs
with authenticated standards. Discovering an already catalogued ratio, fitting
an improved regressor, or reproducing source labels does not acquire new
structure-resolved product discovery. A novel physical operation would need to
beat the actual standard-calibrated workflow plus established orthogonal NMR,
not a deliberately impoverished mass-only baseline.

DECISION CONSEQUENCE FROM THE ACTUAL RECORDS
This descriptive audit chose yield cutoffs 10, 50, 80 percent and desired-isomer
fraction 0.95 before calculating counts. It did not fit or test a predictive model.
149 records have a negative component and 21 have summed yield over 100 percent;
these source values are retained and flagged, not clipped. All finite pairs
with nonnegative components and total at most 100 comprise 50,518 records.

At combined yield >=50 percent, 276 records are selected. In the producer labels,
199 have desired yield <50 percent. 211 fail the joint requirement desired yield
>=50 percent and desired fraction >=0.95; 65 pass it. For the CF3/morpholine pair
alone there are 25 selected, 12 below the desired-yield threshold, and 22 failing
the joint requirement. These are disagreements between two decision rules on
source-provided molar assay labels. They are not independently established assay
errors and not reconstructed raw-MS classifications. There is no uncertainty
estimate or orthogonal validation for these thresholds in this audit.

Therefore the chemical distinction is decision-relevant, but these counts do
not demonstrate new discovery, a new sensor, or an advantage over the incumbent.

COMPETING PHYSICAL IDEA AND ITS LOAD-BEARING REQUIREMENTS
Proposed mechanism considered: prepare an aryl electrophile enriched in 13C at
the carbon originally bonded to the leaving group, use a 15N-labelled amine,
and read the coupling between those labels. An ipso C-N product has direct
13C-15N connectivity; a cine regioisomer places the nucleophile one carbon away.
This replaces reliance on pure-product UV fingerprints with a bond observable.
It is physically meaningful as an isotope-labelled NMR experiment, but actual
couplings, detectability, quantitative responses, label preparations, and
matrix compatibility have not been measured for these exact compounds here.

This would require all of the following together, not as separately assumed
ideal controls: correctly placed and quantified labels; preserved label position;
known direct- and longer-range NMR responses; instrument capability and pulse
calibration; adequate signal at the actual sample amount; quantitative relaxation
control in Pd/Ni/Cu/ligand/base mixtures; retained product identity under workup;
and a bound on how isotope substitution changes the reaction distribution.
An unknown one-bond versus two-bond response is not automatically a binary mask.
One-bond and longer-range couplings are not guaranteed to occupy disjoint
numerical ranges. A null signal may mean insufficient sensitivity, relaxation,
or the pulse transfer function, rather than absence of the desired bond.

For a fraction known independently to contain exactly two specified products,
let q_D,q_S be amounts, let y_T=q_D+q_S be a calibrated product-only total, and
let y_B=a*q_D+b*q_S be a calibrated bond-sensitive readout. If a != b,
q_D=(y_B-b*y_T)/(a-b). If |noise_B|<=epsilon_B, |noise_T|<=epsilon_T,
and response errors obey |delta_a|<=eta_a, |delta_b|<=eta_b, then the error in
that reconstruction is at most
(epsilon_B+|b|*epsilon_T+eta_a*q_D+eta_b*q_S)/|a-b|.
The denominator is an experimental conditioning requirement. Neither y_T nor
the coefficients a,b have been supplied by the available ORD records.

The isotope readout observes a connectivity functional, not the entire molecular
structure. Any different compound carrying the same labelled C-N bond can
contaminate the signal. Extending the claim to unknown product discovery needs
additional structural observations. Those are precisely the burden one hoped
to remove; they cannot be discarded by defining a smaller output afterwards.

CLOSEST PRIMARY ART AND DISPOSITION
1. Roberts, Simmons, Carlsmith, Vaughan, JACS 1953, 75, 3290-3291,
   https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01109a523
   The classic isotope-labelled aryl-halide amination experiment already used
   carbon origin to discriminate rearranged substitution. This rules out a claim
   that isotopic origin tracking is a newly invented physical principle.
2. Kupce and Wrackmeyer, J. Magn. Reson. 2017, 279, 68-73,
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmr.2017.04.015
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28475948/
   The (H)CNMQC experiment measures one-bond and longer-range C-N couplings in
   small organic molecules, including natural-abundance measurement. It directly
   addresses a nonprotonated carbon, relevant to the aryl attachment site.
3. Singh and Frydman, J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 2024, 15, 5659-5664,
   https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.4c01190
   https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/0bb99647-8ec1-4816-be75-138f2f68a5a5
   A 1D C-N multiple-quantum filter, 15N labels and dissolution dynamic nuclear
   polarization already recover C-N coupling information. Cryogenic polarization,
   melting and transfer must be charged if used; hyperpolarized intensities cannot
   silently be assumed equal quantitative response across unknown products.
4. Heinrich, Kondratiuk, Goossen, Wiesenfeldt, Nature Protocols 2024, 19, 1529-1556,
   https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-023-00951-3
   Established quantitative benchtop 19F NMR is a serious comparator for the
   CF3-containing pair. The source reports less than one minute per sample with
   a relaxation agent under its conditions. That speed is NOT validated for
   these exact nanomole crude mixtures. The correction on B(OH)3-dependent T1
   is https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-024-00982-4 and reinforces matrix calibration.

There may be a useful application or method-development project here. This
bounded search does not establish absolute priority. It does establish that
the proposed essential observables and isotope strategy have close incumbents.
The known UV workflow is already deployed on these exact products; no acquired
evidence shows that the candidate wins cost, speed, sample consumption, accuracy,
or the ability to identify an otherwise inaccessible product. STOP this mechanism
as the present new-operation candidate. Do not attach the word original to it.

WHY UNCALIBRATED INTERVENTION CODING DOES NOT RESCUE THE CLAIM
For a known linear response y=Aq and target l^T q, global exact identification
over nonnegative q requires and suffices that l belongs to row(A). Sufficiency:
if A^T w=l, use w^T y. Necessity: otherwise there is v in ker(A) with l^T v!=0;
an interior nonnegative q plus sufficiently small positive and negative v gives
identical observations but distinct targets. The same-mass channel [1,1] thus
does not identify [1,0]. This is standard linear algebra, not a novel theorem.

Many reaction interventions with unknown optical fingerprints give Y=S*C.
They need not acquire S: invertible mixing transforms S'=S*T, C'=T^-1*C can
preserve positivity and even each sample's total amount. An exact rational
counterexample, with two distinct positive spectra and two varied reactions,
is saved in INFORMATION_BOUNDARY_CHECK.json. Therefore more uncalibrated
conditions, an NMF fit, or a known total do not by themselves label the factors.

Blocking an ortho position, deuterating it, adding an aryne trap, or varying
metal/base may create informative experiments but also changes pathway rates
and final distributions. A successful intervention needs a measured response
map or a valid chemical exclusion argument. Merely naming the interventions
is not an acquired code matrix. Aryne traps can compete for reactive species;
inhibition cannot be assumed specific to one productive pathway. Products may
arise by multiple pathways, so isotope connectivity does not separately assign
the metal-catalysed fraction of an ipso product.

VALIDATION INTERFACE THAT WOULD CHANGE THE DECISION
The decisive next evidence would be exact-sample paired measurements, not a
synthetic spectrum benchmark: raw multiwavelength traces, both authentic-product
calibrations on the instruments used, and independent product identities/amounts
from matched aliquots of the same crude reactions. At least the two pure
endpoints, deliberately prepared mixture ratios, crude matrices spanning metals
and bases, dilution/noise conditions, and interference controls are needed.
The published calibration files and relevant raw mzML subset are not present in
the acquired worker directory. The repository notebook points to Globus and
uses an OAuth/application-client transfer workflow. No authentication or external
messages were attempted in this worker. No exact isotope/NMR experiment was run.

A concrete held-out assay trial, if resources become available:
- Freeze the two product identities and a 50 percent desired-yield / 95 percent
  desired-fraction decision before observing candidate results. Include positive,
  negative, and close-to-boundary mixtures, as well as actual crude reactions.
- Split each sample between the published multiwavelength method, improved
  chromatography where available, ordinary quantitative NMR with structural
  assignment, and the candidate. Preserve common sample amounts and histories.
- Determine identity independently through appropriate 2D NMR/isolation and
  quantify with validated qNMR plus recovery/mass balance, including the side
  product. Do not validate a candidate solely against the UV labels it seeks
  to replace. Blind assignments and report abstentions and missing data.
- Reject if isotope-labelled reactions change the original product distribution
  beyond the decision tolerance, if either product has an undetectable/ambiguous
  bond response, if matrices invalidate response bounds, or if additional
  same-bond products generate an accepted desired-product call.
- Compare error/abstention rates at the frozen decisions and full elapsed cost:
  label synthesis and purification, standard acquisition, method development,
  calibration, workup/recovery, sample consumption, instrument time, automation,
  cryogenics if any, energy, and analysis. Every unmeasured quantity is UNKNOWN.

This is a reviewable experiment specification, not evidence it has succeeded.
For the current already-calibrated pair, the existing approach is the default.
Broader usefulness would have to be demonstrated on consequential products whose
identity the incumbent genuinely cannot supply within the same total budget.
Do not replace that target with convenient simulated spectra or arbitrary
cross-validation gains on these supplied labels.
