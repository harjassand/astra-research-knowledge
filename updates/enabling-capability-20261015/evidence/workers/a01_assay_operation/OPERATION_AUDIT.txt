STANDARD-FREE STRUCTURE-RESOLVED ASSAY: TRANSFER-CALIBRATION AUDIT
Worker a01_assay_operation. 2026-10-15.

DECISION

STOP the claim that common chemical reporters, coupled universal detectors,
or closed-pool response-factor regression are a new enabling operation.
Strong primary prior art implements each underlying mechanism. HOLD any
Cernak-specific quantitative capability claim: the raw calibration/spectra,
independent mixture truth, and a validated new physical intervention have not
been acquired here. No physical experiment was performed.

The useful output is an exact failure test: perfect, full-rank mass-balance
fitting can overstate a target concentration by 25% at every positive time,
even with a known pure starting material and a separately measured conserved
total. A missing parallel product can be absorbed into the response factor.
The constructive conditional calibration theorem is also proved below so
that the failure is not mistaken for a claim that calibration is impossible.

SCIENTIFIC TARGET AND PROPOSED OPERATION

Target: absolute amount of each identified regioisomer in a crude reaction,
without synthesizing and purifying each product for instrumental calibration.
The target is molecule amount, not a UV coefficient, relative ion count,
two-product-only purity, or an existing source-calibrated assay label.

Candidate operation: subject aliquots to known moiety-conserving transfers
among assigned species. Observe separate, linear but uncalibrated channels
for each species before/after the transfers. Different changes identify
relative detector responses; one known initial amount or independent common
elemental reporter anchors the absolute scale. In an ideal closed network,
transfer extents and equilibrium constants need not be known.

This would avoid isolated target standards if its physical assumptions were
independently established. It does not establish the structure of an unknown
species, create selective channels for coeluting isomers, prove that no hidden
product exists, or validate transfer stoichiometry by fitting its own output.

EXACT CONDITIONAL CALIBRATION RESULT

Let n assigned species have amounts q_i(j)>=0 in aliquot/condition j.
Let u_i(j)=r_i q_i(j), with unknown r_i>0 constant over conditions. This is a
species-specific diagonal observation model; it is a substantive acquired
interface, not a consequence of having a multichannel spectrum.
Let w_i>0 be known moiety counts. An independently known amount is
  m_j = sum_i w_i q_i(j).
Set theta_i=w_i/r_i and U_{ji}=u_i(j). Then exactly
  U theta = m.

Proposition. Under this model, theta is globally identified among positive
response factors iff U has full column rank n.
Proof. Full rank gives at most one solution. If rank(U)<n, choose nonzero
h in ker U. Since theta is strictly positive, theta+epsilon h stays positive
for sufficiently small nonzero epsilon, gives the same m, and changes at least
one response factor. This is a local positive ambiguity, not a numerical
failure of a solver. Known zeros or fixed response factors can change the
dimension and must be stated separately.

For a common closed amount m_j=M>0, let D consist of all observation changes
u(j)-u(0). Then D theta=0. If rank(D)=n-1, ker(D)=span(theta). The absolute
row u(0).theta=M determines the scale uniquely, so rank(U)=n. Conversely,
rank(U)=n and common total imply rank(D)=n-1: rank(U)<=rank(D)+1 and
D has the nonzero null vector theta. At least n-1 independent changes plus
one absolute scale constraint are needed in this scalar-total design.

Physical realization by transfers. A pure transfer on edge i->k of a known
species graph has
  Delta q = xi*(e_k/w_k - e_i/w_i).
Here xi is transferred moiety amount. If nonzero transfers on a connected
spanning tree can be independently realized, their n-1 changes span the
weighted conservation hyperplane. Multiplication by diag(r) preserves rank,
so the preceding proposition applies. Connectivity alone is insufficient:
the independently realized changes, not a drawn reaction graph, must span.

For two species the inverse response ratio is obtained from one nonzero
transfer:
  (r_k/w_k)/(r_i/w_i) = -Delta u_k/Delta u_i.
That equation is valid only when these are the only two moiety-changing
species in the intervention and detector response is stable.

Noise and cost do not disappear. If measured positive difference magnitudes
a,b have absolute difference errors bounded by da,db and a>da, b>=db, the
ratio lies within [(b-db)/(a+da), (b+db)/(a-da)] before allowing any chemical
stoichiometry error. If each before/after signal has error bound sigma, a
valid difference bound is 2*sigma. A weak transfer can be arbitrarily poorly
conditioned. An informative graph must therefore also have adequate transfer
amplitudes at the actual concentration and nuisance matrix.

EXACT FAILURE 1: A CONNECTED REACTION GRAPH CAN HAVE INSUFFICIENT EXCITATION

Suppose A branches completely into B and C at a fixed 4:1 ratio:
  q_A=100*(1-t), q_B=80*t, q_C=20*t.
Take responses r_A=1,r_B=2,r_C=3. The observation matrix has rank 2, not 3,
for any number of time samples: the B and C columns are proportional.
All three species are visible and the total is closed. More time points of
the same branching ratio cannot calibrate the two product responses.
An independently varied branching ratio can restore rank; the script gives
an exact rank-3 example. Producing that variation without introducing
unmeasured products or changing response factors is the experimental task.

EXACT FAILURE 2: ZERO RESIDUAL AND FULL RANK DO NOT CERTIFY CLOSURE

For every real t in [0,1], consider true chemistry
  q_A=100*(1-t), q_B=80*t, q_H=20*t,
where H is an unmonitored product carrying the same conserved moiety.
The A/B detector responses are both one; observations are
  u_A=100*(1-t), u_B=80*t.
A separately measured total over A,B,H is exactly 100 at every time.
The initial material is pure A, in known amount 100.

A fitted model that assumes only A and B chooses responses
  r_A'=1, r_B'=4/5,
and infers q_A'=100*(1-t), q_B'=100*t. This produces exactly the same
A/B signals and total at all times. Its observation matrix has full rank 2
and its fitted mass-balance residual is zero. Yet q_B' overstates the true
target by 25% whenever t>0. At t=1 it falsely reports 100% target instead
of 80%. The universal total is correct in both models; it cannot reveal
the structural allocation of the missing 20%.

This is a model counterexample, not an accusation that a reported experiment
contains such a product. It specifically refutes the universal inference
that a constant fitted mass balance establishes complete species accounting.
Residuals can falsify a model when nonzero; zero residual does not verify it.

Information lower bound. At t=1 the two indistinguishable worlds have target
amounts 80 and 100. Every estimator from these observations has worst-case
absolute error at least 10 (ten yield percentage points for input 100).
The bound follows from |z-80|+|z-100|>=20 and is attained at z=90.
For relative error, the optimal equal-error choice is z=800/9 and its
worst-case error is 1/9. Thus even noiseless, unlimited time-course and total
measurements of this form cannot guarantee relative error below 11.11%.
Randomization cannot improve the expected absolute-error minimax bound:
take expectations in the same triangle inequality. More computation cannot
repair the missing information. Additional measurements must distinguish
these worlds, rather than repeat an invariant channel.

More generally, a hidden branch fraction eta makes a closed-transfer fit
underestimate the target response by factor 1-eta and overestimate its amount
by 1/(1-eta). The relative amount error is eta/(1-eta). To guarantee error
<=epsilon by this route alone requires independently bounding
eta<=epsilon/(1+epsilon). Conservation of atoms across the entire sample does
not give that bound because hidden molecules retain those atoms.

All-element extension. H may have the same elemental formula as B. Then
every elemental total and total molecular mass agrees across the two worlds,
not merely one scalar moiety balance. Nonselective dilution and mixing of
aliquots preserve the ambiguity. This is an observational model construction;
it does not assert an actual H in this dataset. A selective B-versus-H
intervention or independently quantitative structure-specific channel can
break it, but its physical specificity and recovery must be acquired.

EXACT FAILURE 3: COMMON REPORTERS DO NOT SPLIT UNASSIGNED COELUTERS

For spectra Y=S X with unknown dense S, a measured total does not supply the
diagonal-channel assumption. The script constructs positive matrices
  S=[[2,1],[1,2]], T=[[4/5,1/5],[1/5,4/5]],
  S'=S*T^-1=[[7/3,2/3],[2/3,7/3]], X'=T*X.
Then S'X'=SX and 1^T X'=1^T X. A sample with target fraction 9/10 changes
to 37/50, with every spectrum and total unchanged. Both representations are
strictly positive. More unknown-spectrum mixtures do not by themselves
remove this ambiguity. This boundary overlaps chemical_information and
capital N820; it is not offered as new mathematics.

MATCHED COST CONDITION

Let K be the number of distinct products needing purified standards and N
the number of samples. Let C_std be the full total cost avoided by omitting
those standards, including synthesis/isolation/purity assignment/calibration.
Let C_interface include extra hardware setup, species assignment, closure
validation, transfer chemistry, common calibration, and transfer conditioning.
Let delta_c be the additional per-sample cost relative to the strongest
matched assay, including material, preparation, acquisition, precision,
analysis, reset, and waste. A necessary break-even inequality is
  C_interface + N*delta_c < C_std.
This does not prove a benefit: no term is measured here.

For the present N=50,688 and K=4, if standards hypothetically have equal cost
c_std, the extra-per-sample allowance before interface cost is only
  delta_c < c_std/12,672.
Thus high reaction-row count does not itself make purified standards the
dominant bottleneck. The operation would have to be tested on many distinct
product identities, or demonstrate a sufficiently cheap change to the actual
screen. Existing qNMR, CLND+UV, isotope encoding with measured response
corrections, and complete-mass-balance calibration get the same information
and full-cost accounting in the comparison. A renamed solver on the same
physical observations has no acquired-information advantage over an equally
informed incumbent.

DECISIVE ACTUAL TEST ROUTE, NOT EXECUTED

Gate 0, access: obtain raw chromatograms/spectra and calibration files, or a
new accessible dataset of physical unpurified mixtures with independent
composition. The ORD labels do not satisfy this gate for replacing the assay.

Gate 1, physical operation: identify a genuinely obtained selective channel
and realizable transfer for each edge needed for rank. Check before/after
structures and all observed products with orthogonal separation/structure
measurements. A common-element balance by itself cannot establish closure.
For the Cernak pairs this gate currently fails: no such transfer is measured.

Gate 2, blinded quantities: an independent preparer makes known mixtures of
the identified target, competing isomer, and plausible same-moiety nuisance
products across concentration, ratio, and crude-matrix variation. Standards
may be used by the independent evaluator while remaining hidden from the
candidate method; otherwise no standard-free claim can be falsified fairly.
Use the same material budget and target outputs for standard UV unmixing,
an appropriate quantitative NMR method, and any physically compatible
universal detector. Include a nuisance branch whose amount varies with the
apparent target so the exact counterexample is represented experimentally.

Gate 3, performance: predeclare absolute target-amount errors and the decision
thresholds relevant to selecting chemistry; record uncertainty coverage and
false target calls, not spectral residual alone. Report failure/UNKNOWN when
the chemical closure, assignment, response stability, or rank conditions
fail. Quantify extra acquisition, conditioning, sample, and maintenance cost.

Gate 4, expansion: after passing on held-out mixtures, test held-out product
identities and crude reaction conditions. Performance on one pair is not a
general structure-resolved assay. Only then assess savings against the best
incumbent that uses the same available auxiliary measurements.
