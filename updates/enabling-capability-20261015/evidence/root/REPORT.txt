Enabling scientific capability research decision
15 October 2026

Decision: HOLD the selected chemical quantitation direction. No new physical or computational capability with demonstrated advantage was established. Stop the defeated standard-free calibration and molecular-certificate advantage claims. Preserve the internally proved restricted mathematics for independent review; neither exceptional significance nor priority has been established.

The selected scientific output is the absolute amount of an identified regioisomer in a crude reaction, with enough uncertainty control to choose a synthesis recipe. A method that reliably obtained this output without preparing a pure standard for every new product could compound across reaction discovery, catalysis, materials and manufacturing. The acquired evidence does not yet support such a method. The immediate research need is an independent measurement interface on the same actual samples, not another construction that presumes an unknown calibrated channel.

A consequential target and an actual failure test

The Cernak experimental archive supplies 50,688 C–N reaction records with separate desired-product and regioisomer assay labels. All 44,352 metal treatments have otherwise matched no-metal controls within their experiment. Source code already uses authentic-standard multiwavelength spectral deconvolution and target-specific treatment effects. These are incumbent capabilities, not Astra inventions. Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13339639/ ; code revision 449db5a012aca0cb24181ddcbb51d3fad6c2f98e.

Two independently written parsers and a root arithmetic replay exposed a real objective mismatch. Among 276 nominally consistent records with combined reported yield at least 50%, 199 have desired yield below 50%. Only 65 records satisfy both desired yield at least 50% and desired/(desired + reported side product) at least 95%; none are no-metal wells. These exploratory thresholds are not preregistered validation, and two-product fraction is not total purity. We retained 149 negative-component records and 21 totals above 100%, excluding them only from the explicitly labeled consistency view.

A threshold-free audit confirms the issue. For the bromopyridine feed, maximizing the combined label selects (desired, side)=(52.630211,47.211540), while (99.158844,0) dominates it for the intended output. An equally informed incumbent simply selects using the existing target labels. No new information operation is needed to fix that objective. The top two desired labels differ by only 0.003112793 percentage points; a unique ordering under a symmetric absolute-error model needs per-action error below 0.001556396 points, which has not been measured. Assigned zeros are censored observations, not certified absence.

The proposed transfer-calibration operation had a precise appeal. With unknown fixed response factors r_i, observed species channels u_i(j)=r_i q_i(j), known moiety counts w_i and known total m_j, solve U theta=m with theta_i=w_i/r_i. Independent moiety-conserving transfers can identify theta when U has full column rank. But the core operation already exists in reaction-progress mass-balance calibration; a connected reaction graph does not guarantee independent transfers. Primary prior art: Deem and Hein, DOI 10.1021/acs.joc.2c02737, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9872085/ .

More decisively, perfect mass-balance fitting does not establish complete species accounting. The exact two-world construction is:
  true amounts A=100(1-t), B=80t, unseen same-formula H=20t;
  observed channels u_A=100(1-t), u_B=80t; independently measured total=100;
  fitted closed model A'=100(1-t), B'=100t, response r_B'=4/5.
The worlds have identical observed channels, every elemental total and known pure starting material for every t. The fitted matrix has full rank and zero residual. At t=1 the target is either 80 or 100. Every estimator therefore has worst-case absolute error at least ten yield percentage points; optimal worst-case relative error is 1/9. This is an exact information obstruction within the stated measurement model, not evidence that a particular published sample contains H. Unlimited repeated time points do not distinguish the worlds. A selective physical intervention or independently quantitative structure-specific channel must do so.

Common carbon/nitrogen reporters, isotope fingerprints, linear unmixing and standard additions have substantial prior art. Their usefulness depends on actual selectivity, response, conversion and recovery at the sample concentration. They do not automatically identify unknown coeluters. The current source amortizes four product standards across 50,688 reactions, so an alleged calibration saving must also confront that strong amortized incumbent.

The validation interface that would change the decision

The exact source SI and all ORD rows were acquired. The current Globus raw-spectra endpoint requires authentication; no credentials were used. Pure-standard preparation and calibration plots are available, but numerical wavelength calibrations, mixture spectra, calibration covariance and independently quantified blind mixtures were not acquired for these Cernak wells. High pure-calibration R-squared values and reaction-fit residuals cannot supply the missing independent quantities.

The minimum discriminator is an orthogonal structural and quantitative measurement on the same disputed actual crude sample, with authentic co-spikes, recovery control and reference uncertainty. For the fluorinated pair, quantitative 19F NMR is a candidate only after amount, spectral separation and relaxation requirements are established; it cannot be assumed feasible on an individual nanomole well. Repeating the synthesis at a scale that permits a reference measurement requires an explicit transfer check back to the screening scale.

The saved prospective contract specifies separate calibration and validation stocks, raw time-by-wavelength data including the full caffeine peak, instrument/run metadata, matrix blanks, mixed standards and independently prepared batches. Its larger design contains 60 held-out mixtures, 20 calibration preparations and four blanks. These are planned counts, not acquired data or a power calculation. Passing the mixtures would not adjudicate every unknown real-sample interferent.

Both candidate and incumbent must receive the same allowed measurements, uncertainty specification and computational resources. The comparator includes calibrated GLS/NNLS, full temporal spectral methods where applicable, standard additions, and independently resolved LC or quantitative NMR. Calibration, synthesis, purification, acquisition, conversion, rejected samples, references and rework must be charged. A two-minute injection assumption alone would imply 1,689.6 instrument-hours and 81.1008 L mobile phase for 50,688 rows; actual method mix and complete costs remain unknown. No new chemical measurements were performed.

The expanded search improved access without closing this target. Public isotope-assay archives now provide actual LC-MS and NMR files, but archived crude FIDs are at 400.172 MHz where SI captions say 600 MHz; one sample has a one-second relaxation delay. Their connection to quantitative reference values remains unresolved. Public electron-diffraction data offer a particularly useful different validation interface: a source measured the absolute hand of one crystal by X-ray diffraction before crushing it and measuring a fragment by electron diffraction. The original CIF and raw ED files were acquired. Twenty-two supplied ED images are deterministic interpolations, so they cannot count as independent detector observations. The source dynamical-refinement workflow remains the strong incumbent; acquisition of its software/input interface is still required before claiming a new chirality operation. Neither alternative is silently substituted for the chemical target.

Strongest mathematical result

Let Lambda_d(Z)=Tr(Z)I-Z/2, Phi=Lambda_p tensor Lambda_q, and for a nonzero reference u define
  A=Phi(|u><u|), L_a=Phi(|a><u|),
  C=sum_ab |b><a| tensor Phi(|a><b|),
  S_u=sum_ab |b><a| tensor L_a A^{-1} L_b*.
For every complex reference of Schmidt rank at most two, in arbitrary finite p,q>=2,
                   -(11/9)C <= S_u <= C.
The constant 11/9 is sharp. Schmidt reduction, an analytic inverse and a phase-block argument reduce every ambient dimension to 23 exact templates represented among 84 blocks at p=q=4. Positive polynomial coefficient certificates establish the inequalities for every Schmidt parameter, not just sampled vectors. A separate implementation reconstructs all 226 certificate coefficients and verifies zero off-block terms. Root replayed the full symbolic certificate.

The consequence is
  q_k(X)=sum_{J subset [k]}(-1/2)^|J| ||Tr_J X||_HS^2 >=0
for rank-at-most-two X whose range or adjoint range contains a nonzero reference with entanglement of Schmidt rank at most two confined to one pair of sites and product factors elsewhere. A separately checked tensor and switched-Schur argument proves arbitrary k within this restriction. An exact rank-two, square-zero example on three ququarts has all local supports of dimension four and q_3=65/4, escaping the located earlier individual sector hypotheses.

The expanded team also obtained an internally proved analytic uniform-reference extension. For p=q=d>=3 and u=sum_i |ii>, the sharp interval is
            -[(5d+1)/(9(d-1))]C <= S_u <= C.
An explicit contraction-space decomposition reduces it to a two-dimensional block, four copies of the traceless matrix space, and scalar flip sectors. The decomposition, direct d=3 action, symbolic sharp endpoints and extremizer have separate internal checks; root also replayed the sharp-endpoint audit. There is no claim for general nonuniform higher Schmidt rank or unused ambient levels. Any finite collection of disjoint full uniform pairs with d>=3, together with product reference sites, has normalized spectrum inside [-1,1] and gives the corresponding restricted rank-two q_k consequence.

Two failures matter: the stronger bound -C<=S is false for a Bell pair, with exact witness values 9 and -11; using the same construction for two Bell groups gives 81-121=-40. That is failure of a proof mechanism, not a negative q_4 witness. The unrestricted three-copy problem, all-copy NPT bound entanglement and novelty remain unresolved. Located 2026 source claims include AI-origin manuscripts and a Codex-origin candidate certificate; neither a DOI nor source agreement constitutes independent validation. This result is self-contained internal mathematics, not a formal proof or a new physical operation. See workers/foundation/REPORT.txt, workers/a08_grouped_werner/UNIFORM_SCHMIDT_PROOF.txt and their independent audits.

A physically grounded computational comparison that did not survive

The molecular lane acquired an actual N2/STO-3G Hamiltonian at 1.1 angstrom and tested ground-energy interval certification in its declared Ag block. The code's declared tolerance is 1.59360143764 mEh (one kcal/mol within this computational model), with a nominal 1.5 mEh construction margin. Actual outward-rounded widths are about 1.5000000084 mEh for native selection and 1.5000000066 mEh for retaining. These pass the declared tolerance; they are not strict width-at-most-1.5 certificates. The same certificate is available to an equally informed incumbent that retains a solution vector it paid to compute.

An independent auditor caught a timing provenance error: the initial comparison charged a fast solve while using a vector saved by a different solve. That claim was withdrawn and preserved in CORRECTION_004. A fresh solve saved the exact vector used in a new replay. Required post-matrix stages were 0.046969 s for retaining 256 determinants versus 0.055887 s for native selection of 512. This is one local observation, not a statistically established speed ordering. Shared Hamiltonian assembly, approximately 0.9514 s exact-decimal bridging, software/import costs and all audit attempts are separately recorded. Both meet the same computational guarantee; no new advantage or molecular physical accuracy follows from the small-basis benchmark.

A connected interacting-chain counterexample rules out a tempting escape. Two 12-site, four-particle nearest-neighbor Hamiltonians with every hopping nonzero share signed source moments through degree ten and Krylov vectors through degree five. Their seed residuals agree, yet a structural bound gives ground-energy ambiguity exceeding 3.999 energy units. Thus the missing information need not sit in a disconnected, exactly dark block. Random full-vector probes escape some information restrictions but meet existing probabilistic Lanczos methods and substantial probe costs. STOP the proposed capability claim.

What the other attack angles changed

The decision ledger preserves all remaining directions. Refitting a restricted bright-only LiF model gave lower held-out residual than the source output templates; an unrefitted source ablation is therefore not a strong identification test. NaF control data lack the jointly sampled polarization/delay and complex readout needed for the proposed discriminator. Photon timestamps support existing correlation/localization analyses, but brightness dominance does not establish emitter count; a conditional four-transmission first-arrival identifiability argument still lacks stability and matched physical controls. Magnetic-material feature gains disappear as free benefits once all supplied diffraction acquisition is charged. Pulsed-electrolysis power requires terminal voltage and jointly measured products. Mechanical latch reset loses to a simple reverse-drive incumbent unless a physical one-sided constraint is actually acquired. Gravitational-wave extra-mode inference has exact detector-null ambiguities; the restricted gain from a fourth detector is also obtained by ordinary GLS. Union-closed-set counterexamples defeat the attempted variance and positive-kernel routes without improving the abundance bound.

An eight-second GPS-tagged IQ recording was actually acquired from a public receiver. This establishes lawful receive-only observation access, not environmental actuation or a new scientific operation. For the proposed multipath target, y=q*g*h*s permits unknown receiver response g to absorb propagation h for every downstream digital control q. An independently bounded receiver response or reference channel is required. Thus this campaign did perform a live measurement, although it performed no new chemical or device intervention experiment.

The premise and the stopping rule

The campaign supports the narrower admission rule: close the loop on the desired output using jointly acquired inputs, and give the incumbent the same information. It does not establish a causal theory of invention. Exact proofs can be known or insufficiently consequential; real measurements can be non-identifying; a verified computation can lose to an incumbent. Worker count is not scientific evidence.

The most useful next expenditure for the selected direction is access to raw calibration/spectra plus independent structural quantitation of the same disputed samples. Further deep invention that assumes those quantities should remain paused. Reopen only when a measurement distinguishes the preserved countermodels, or when a mathematical construction genuinely removes a missing premise without importing an equally hard oracle. The restricted foundational theorems merit independent checking and priority assessment; exceptional foundational success remains NOT_ACHIEVED.
