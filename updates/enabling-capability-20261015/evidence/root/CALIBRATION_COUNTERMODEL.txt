Root mathematical contribution: target-specific calibration ambiguity
Status: exact model lemma, not a novel theorem, experimental observation, or allegation about the source instrument.

Context. Cernak's acquired code uses authentic desired/side-product spectra s,t across wavelengths and normalizes the product peak by a caffeine scalar measured at270nm. For perfectly calibrated response, ordinary multivariate least squares already estimates concentrations. N820 in current Astra Capital (updates/FO/synthesis/causal_operation/REPORT.md, lines29–100) supplies the standard row-space target criterion and bounded-calibration feasible intervals. Repeating that result would not be new capital.

A specific surviving calibration obligation. Let s,t have strictly positive wavelength entries and let lambda0 denote the internal-standard wavelength. Fix interior concentrations a,b>0. Define v=(t_0,-s_0). For nonzero sufficiently small epsilon, set (a',b')=(a,b)+epsilon*v so both concentrations remain positive. Then s_0 a'+t_0 b'=s_0 a+t_0 b. Define
    g_j=(s_j a+t_j b)/(s_j a'+t_j b').
Each g_j is positive and g_0=1. Therefore the two models
    model A: concentrations(a,b), spectral gain identically1;
    model B: concentrations(a',b'), relative wavelength gains g_j
produce exactly identical product vectors, and the same scalar standard response atlambda0. Desired yield changes by epsilon*t_0. A detector model with identical additive noise on these means has identical observation laws too. This remains true with arbitrarily many repeat observations at that sample: random noise reduction does not estimate the uncalibrated gains.

If the admissible gain set contains a neighborhood of1, a nonzero ambiguity always exists. The ambiguity size depends on actual spectra and calibrated gain bounds; neither has been acquired from the raw source files here, so no numerical magnitude or practical failure for Cernak's apparatus is asserted. The family is a countermodel within the stated gain-uncertainty class. Real drift need not have this shape, and independent multiwavelength calibration/known mixture data can exclude it. Positivity, a perfect fit, authentic historical spectra, a scalar internal standard, and many wavelengths alone do not exclude it.

Two-channel rational fixture (algebra check only). s=(1,2), t=(1,1), lambda0=first channel; (a,b)=(1/4,3/4); epsilon=1/10 gives(a',b')=(7/20,13/20). Observed y=(1,5/4) is identical with gains(1,25/27), while desired fraction changes from1/4 to7/20. These numbers are not chemical spectra or lab measurements.

Decision. Stop treating a smaller fit residual or improved prediction against the producer's derived labels as independent evidence of better chemical quantitation. Acquire randomized known-mixture truth with calibration scans repeated across the sample runs. A wavelength-dependent reference (or independently bounded gain transfer) addresses this family. Unknown coeluting species remain a separate composition/model gate. A suitable benchmark must include such interferents or bound their contribution and compare robust standard-informed regression and qNMR under equal inputs/costs.

Novelty: standard inverse-problem/calibration reasoning, scoped application only. Scientific consequence: states one exact failure mode and the missing observation, but establishes neither incidence nor importance for the actual assay. It does not disprove source chemistry or quantify performance loss.

Independent audit correction/addition. Chemistry_evidence verified the algebra and identified an existing raw-data repair: the PDA records a full caffeine spectrum, whereas the pipeline uses only270nm for normalization. A concurrently stable clean wavelength-resolved caffeine signal could bound/cancel common spectral gains, provided gain transfer from its retention time to the product peak is validated. This information may already be in the unavailable raw files. Thus this countermodel concerns the currently processed vector+single scalar contract, not the entire raw PDA record. No claim that a global fixed gain can evade every known-mixture panel is made. Concentration perturbations must remain interior to any physical amount/yield constraints. This repair belongs in the strongest equally informed incumbent and has no claimed novelty.
