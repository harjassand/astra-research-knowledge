Exact zero quantum and private capacity
of rational channels
11 October 2026
Research proof draft; internally checked, external review pending
Abstract
A computable complementary-channel completion is given for the uniformly gapped
inverse-Jordan kernels produced by the supplied companion channel-order reduction.
The completion is a genuine rational Stinespring construction. Its soundness holds for
coherent inputs, arbitrary Hermitian tangents, and every finite quantum reference, with
no state-conditioning or reference-size bound. An exact purification duality controls all
leaf scores, and sector-copy padding absorbs the remaining error. A zero-rate coding
argument compiles every finite-dimensional half-erasure helper into finitely many uses of
the fixed qubit half-erasure channel. Together with the companion source theorem, the
unrestricted complete information-order equivalence, and established capacity bridges,
the assembled proof gives the classification: exact zero quantum capacity and exact zero
private capacity are co-recursively enumerable complete for finite rational channels, while
their positive-capacity complements are recursively enumerable complete. The proof relies
on the supplied companion research theorem and has not undergone external review. It is
an exact zero-versus-positive result; no capacity approximation, fixed-gap promise, efficient
reduction, or computable positive-capacity witness bound is asserted.

12 Scope and verification status
The completed reduction is an exact qualitative test. It does not imply noncomputability of
arbitrary-precision numerical capacity approximation or of a fixed-gap promise problem. The
positive-capacity witness can occur at an unknown, arbitrarily large blocklength; no computable
witness bound, practical code construction, or polynomial-time reduction is claimed. The
zero-rate helper code transports a fixed finite logical system with vanishing error while using
increasingly many helper qubits. It does not transmit quantum information at positive rate
through the half-erasure channel alone.
The new completion applies to the supplieduniformly gappedsource family. It is not a
compiler for every arbitrary ungapped POVM pair. All actual reference quantifiers in its
soundness proof are unrestricted and finite; no equal-reference-marginal restriction, scalar-
denominator replacement, realification, or state condition-number assumption is used.
This is a research proof draft. Its components and assembled reduction have received
internal analytic checks; external review remains pending. The classification relies on the
explicitly supplied companion source theorem and complete-order equivalence, together with
the complementary completion and helper proofs given here. No author attribution, peer-review
claim, or historical originality claim is made.
