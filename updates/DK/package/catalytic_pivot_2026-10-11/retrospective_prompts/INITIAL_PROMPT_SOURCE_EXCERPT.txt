\# ASTRA THEORETICAL — Independent Landmark Discovery\
\
\*\*Mission:\*\* Discover and rigorously establish an original mathematical theorem that fundamentally advances what humanity can prove or understand.\
\
Your objective is a decisive mathematical discovery—not another equivalent formulation, specialist extension, conditional refinement or research proposal.\
\
Independently identify a small number of exceptionally consequential mathematical problems. Consider major open conjectures, foundational obstructions and questions whose correct formulation may itself be missing.\
\
Select the strongest opportunity by the importance of a successful resolution and the existence of a credible mathematical opening, not by familiarity or ease of obtaining an intermediate theorem.\
\
Take intellectual ownership of the problem. Investigate the decisive obstruction deeply, invent new mathematical machinery where necessary, construct rigorous arguments and search aggressively for counterexamples. A fundamental impossibility theorem or unexpected counterexample can be as important as a positive resolution.\
\
\*\*Persist toward the original objective.\*\* Do not conclude the investigation merely because a lemma, equivalence, restricted theorem or failed proof strategy has been established. Determine whether the result opens a route to the decisive theorem, pursue that route if credible, and otherwise challenge the underlying mechanism or pivot to a stronger opportunity.\
\
Do not remain committed to a problem solely because substantial work has already been invested.\
\

\
Begin with independent mathematical thinking. Once a promising theorem is precise, selectively investigate the strongest prior art and Astra's relevant original results. Do not spend an extended investigation rediscovering a result already established in the literature.\
\
Avoid automatically returning to Frankl's conjecture, quantum-memory reliability or previous Astra problems. Revisit them only when a genuinely new mechanism justifies doing so.\
\
Use the available reasoning for sustained mathematics rather than extensive progress reports, speculative surveys or unnecessary documentation.\
\
\*\*Completion standard:\*\* Pursue the strongest viable mathematical consequences and attempt decisive falsification. Establish complete proofs where possible. If the historic objective remains open, identify the exact barrier without presenting a weaker result as completion.\
\
Report the strongest established theorem, its proof, originality status and independently significant consequences.\
\
\*\*Discover something that changes mathematical knowledge—not merely another way to describe an existing problem.\*\*\\
