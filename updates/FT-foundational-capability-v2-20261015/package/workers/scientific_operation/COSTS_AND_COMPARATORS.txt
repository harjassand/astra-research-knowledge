Scalar control-map acquisition: costs, prior art, and claim boundary
2026-10-15

Result: an explicit generic exact acquisition schedule, plus decisive failures
of universal low-order qualification and of uniform robustness. No laboratory
demonstration, novelty finding, total-cost win, or mission achievement.

1. Strong primary comparators, read in this investigation

A. Sol, Prod'homme, Le Magoarou and del Hougne, Nature Communications 15, 2841
(2024), "Experimentally realized physical-model-based frugal wave control in
metasurface-programmable complex media."
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46916-2

This is the strongest directly relevant physical baseline: acquiring a compact
reusable wave-response model from externally measured random configurations.
Their study includes prediction of one complex transmission coefficient and
larger accessible scattering matrices, then experimental wave control. The
reported 68-control cavity is reciprocal; it is not evidence for this new
nonreciprocal closed-form schedule. Their SISO calibration and task-focused
ambiguity tolerance already preempt a broad claim that scalar external data
can newly enable reusable control prediction. An equally informed comparator
must fit the same nonreciprocal rational model family to the same scalar data,
with multiple starts, noise treatment, calibration and independent joint
configurations. This comparator was not numerically executed here.

B. Del Hougne, "Virtual VNA 3.0: Unambiguous Scattering Matrix Estimation for
Non-Reciprocal Systems by Leveraging Tunable and Coupled Loads" (2025).
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07239v1

This experimentally characterizes nonreciprocal networks using multiple
accessible VNA ports, three known individual terminations at each hidden port,
and known coupled loads. Its objective is an unambiguous full scattering
matrix. The quoted closed-form configuration count is 1+3m+m(m-1)/2 for more
than two accessible ports, with an extra setting in the two-port case.
Each setting returns a matrix, not one scalar. The present construction
predicts only a fixed scalar channel under future independent loads and
therefore permits diagonal-gauge and weighted-transpose ambiguity. It supplies
less information and uses a different physical interface. It has not beaten
Virtual VNA experimentally. The paper also explicitly distinguishes useful
channel prediction from unnecessary removal of internal ambiguity.

C. Aravind, Chatterjee, Ghosh, Gurjar, Raj and Saha, "Learning Read-Once
Determinants and the Principal Minor Assignment Problem" (2026; STOC 2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04255v1
https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3798129.3800875

This provides randomized polynomial-time exact algebraic reconstruction of
read-once determinants and black-box principal-minor assignment. It is a
stronger general algebraic comparator than an exponential table of control
settings. Its field-operation model is not a noisy physical-measurement model.
A scalar transfer-response oracle is not automatically a determinant oracle;
converting between them can introduce pole-cancellation and precision gates.
The current quadratic schedule is derived directly from scalar responses and
does not implement or claim to improve their general learner.

The primary-literature search did not establish priority for this particular
generic anchored-triple construction. The underlying Woodbury response model,
load-modulation acquisition, and task-preserving ambiguity are established
ideas. No "first" or new physical-capability claim is justified.

2. Matched information and exact configuration counts

One acquisition configuration means: set the actual operator through calibrated
actuators, allow settling, apply the fixed ordinary source, and read the fixed
detector. Source profile can remain unchanged while the operator changes.
Each configuration still incurs operating-point acquisition and settling.

At m=32, the present exact schedule uses:

    baseline                         1
    singleton configurations        64
    pair configurations             992
    anchor triples                  30
    other anchored triples          435
    total                           1522

Each read is one signed real scalar for a real steady circuit. A complex
coherent wave coefficient contains two real quadratures and needs a phase
reference. Intensity-only measurements do not supply the signed/complex values
used in this proof. Bit precision, repetitions, integration time and ADC noise
must be charged independently of the configuration count.

The hardware needs three calibrated values per actuator: reference, t1, t2.
The triple value t3 may equal one of the nonzero values, although the script
uses a third nonzero value for convenience. The proof's exhibited algebraic
separation witness uses t3=1; a three-state hardware schedule can instead choose
t1=1,t2=2,t3=1, and the same generic-separation argument applies. The script's
initial tested values are reference 0, t1=.5,t2=1.5,t3=1 (four physical levels).
The separate check_ternary_acquisition.py subsequently executed t1=1,t2=2,t3=1
on eight m=32 seeds per noise level. Its 1522 scalar configurations use exactly
three physical levels {0,1,2}. Both result sets are preserved. These are scalar
configuration counts, not binary-tuner counts. A binary-only apparatus cannot
execute the present singleton identification. No binary-tuner query bound was
proved.

If every query is reset independently to reference, the upper count of scalar
actuator writes (set and reset both counted) is

    4m + 8 C(m,2) + 6[(m-2)+C(m-2,2)]
      = 7m^2-9m+6,

or 6886 writes at m=32. Better ordering can reduce writes. Reset hysteresis,
thermal memory and actuator latency were not measured. Neither the physical
source energy nor controller/ADC energy was measured.

3. Full cost ledger and reuse limits

Acquisition hardware: one stable source and one fixed detector (possibly at
the same port); m independently switchable calibrated rank-one controls;
known reference impedances or conductance increments. Fabrication and access
to m controllable terminations are supplied inputs, not gained by algebra.

Calibration: actual actuator values and independence, source gain/phase and
loading, detector gain/phase, stationary reference planes, linearity and
repeatability. Unknown actuator cross-coupling can invalidate A+PDP^T. Multiple
calibration settings can test the premise but finite tests do not certify an
arbitrary unknown nonlinear actuator law.

Time: at least Q distinct settled operating points for the exact schedule,
multiplied by repetitions and integration required by precision. A nominal
single measurement is not a fixed number of seconds. Calibration lifetime
must exceed the complete acquisition and reuse block.

Precision: the singleton inverse uses differences of scalar responses; pair
reconstruction divides by another response difference; quadratic roots and
triangle selection require further separation. An exact nonzero margin may
be far below available noise. For independent Gaussian measurement noise of
standard deviation sigma0, distinguishing a known candidate separation gamma
by repeated averaging ordinarily requires at least order sigma0^2/gamma^2
samples. This is not a sufficient end-to-end bound because estimated pair
candidates also carry uncertainty. Weak-anchor separations approach zero,
so no uniform finite precision follows from genericity.

Control/reset: every experiment changes real controls; objective disturbance,
switching wear, settling and reset are costs. The high-order counterexample
needs collective amplitudes O(m) and total conductance O(m^2) for its constant
future response gap; those are declared resources, not a free amplification.

Computation/storage: O(m^2) scalar coefficients and reconstruction work in the
ideal field model; ordinarily O(m^3) arithmetic for each dense future response.
Many binary configurations still create a combinatorial optimization problem.
No polynomial global optimizer was supplied.

Reuse: all predictions concern the same A,b,c and the same additive calibrated
control family. Drift, altered source illumination, a new objective detector,
frequency change, nonlinear operating-point change or beyond-diagonal controls
can require new calibration. A response-model ambiguity that is harmless for
the original task need not be harmless for the changed task.

4. What would count as an advantage

For q requested future scalar predictions, a direct measurement baseline uses
q actual configurations and naturally includes the same actuator/readout
noise. An acquired model pays Q repetitions plus calibration, then digital
prediction costs. Its nominal amortization only begins when q is sufficiently
larger than Q and the plant stays fixed. This is the same reuse benefit already
provided by measured-model methods; it is not newly established here.

The relevant other baseline is physics-compliant rational model fitting using
the same observable scalar, same known actuator values, same allowed joint
configurations and same acquisition budget. It can fit all data jointly and
regularize small interactions, avoiding brittle hard branch choices. Its
optimization may be nonconvex; a favorable initialization is a possible use
of the present exact construction. No matched comparison has shown that this
improvement dominates total experimental cost.

For one or a few immediate control decisions, direct finite interventions or
stochastic optimization can be cheaper than acquiring an entire reusable map.
Coordinate finite differences with two measurements per parameter are not the
only legitimate baseline. The candidate has no demonstrated advantage over
well-tuned stochastic control on a matched physical task.

5. Stop decision

The broad scientific operation already has strong physical prior art. The
explicit generic exact schedule may be useful mathematical capital, but its
fixed-anchor robustness is decisively limited and its additional measurement
levels are real hardware requirements. The proper-subset counterexample
shows that weak assumptions about collective controls cannot be repaired by
more singleton/pair validation. This route should be preserved as secondary
exact acquisition and negative robustness work, without further capability
promotion until a measured, matched apparatus test changes the evidence.
