# Admission experiment for event-conditioned isotope selection

This is a proposed laboratory assay, not an experiment performed by this investigation. No acquisition budget, laboratory access or apparatus performance is claimed. Stop after each failed admission gate; do not proceed directly to a device demonstration.

## Contract

Input: a structurally qualified, electron-transparent graphene region; independently assayed starting isotope fraction; a carbon reservoir whose actual incorporated isotope fraction is known; a calibrated 95 kV beam and event detector; a qualified independent isotope readout; finite limits on dose, elapsed time, heating, defects and specimen consumption.

Output: an occupied target position with measured heavy-isotope probability at least a preregistered threshold, retained through the remaining protocol, and a complete record of all rejected attempts, repairs, side reactions, missed/ambiguous events and final damage. Selecting only visually successful specimens is invalid.

The model target is nominal .999 to allow a conservative actual-purity target of .99. This is a planning choice, not an achieved threshold. The parent renewal formula supplies the attempt dose cap after same-specimen cross-section calibration. Do not use the pure-sheet literature table as a certified rate for a chemically changed target.

## Stage 0: qualify the event record and reset

1. Use separately isotope-labelled positive-control specimens and natural-composition specimens. Measure local ejection rates, vacancy persistence, chemical etching, correlated neighbour loss and the complete beam profile at the same voltage, geometry, temperature and actual source conditions intended for screening. Keep the calibration and withheld specimens separate.
2. Explicitly compare source on, source off, and source enabled only after a rejected attempt. Source-off must include an assay of residual adatoms and same-atom return; nominal gas-valve state alone does not certify absence of refill.
3. Create known vacancies and determine how often the proposed detector misses an ejection, merges repeated events, detects a false ejection or sees a vacancy only after neighbouring damage. An independent audit must define the true-event label. If no physically valid independent label is available, the no-ejection record is unacquired and this stage stops.
4. Determine the isotope fraction of incorporated replacement atoms, rather than the isotope fraction in the feed bottle. Use labelled feed controls to distinguish return of a displaced atom from incorporation of a fresh atom. Freeze an upper bound on same-atom return and reset dependence.
5. Measure actual blanking/switching latency, event contrast, beam tails and drift. Include imaging and independent isotope-readout dose in the ledger. A vacancy that refills faster than the detector must either be included in a calibrated hidden-event likelihood or eliminate the exact-event renewal claim.

For the conservative mixture model, nominal acceptance a=.00715550 and nominal purity .999 admit at most epsilon=6.5050e-5 additional worst-case light false acceptances per attempt for an actual .99 target. With zero independently identified failures, a simple one-sided 95% binomial upper confidence limit reaches this scale after 46,052 audited attempts. This is deliberately a transparent conservative planning calculation. A justified likelihood for imperfect observation may change the required sample size; fitting it without independent true-event information does not.

**Kill:** unknown true-event audit; inconsistent local rate distributions; no independent replacement law; unbounded false-accept process; persistent chemical/structural alteration. Do not treat the table's approximately 27-fold central isotope ratio as a substitute for these measurements.

## Stage 1: one-site renewal and protection

On withheld specimens, execute the frozen protocol: initial independent fill; irradiate until an observed ejection or cap; reject/refill after ejection; accept only after the complete no-event interval; protect the accepted site. Record fluence at the target and at every already accepted neighbour, beam-state transitions, isotope/readout uncertainty, all failures, and reset duration.

Independently identify the accepted atom's isotope using qualified vibrational spectroscopy or another scope-matched isotope readout. The readout itself must not overwrite the atom or merely identify a mixed group of neighbours. Retain ambiguous cases as ambiguous; do not impute the model posterior as an experimental isotope label.

Repeat protection checks after later screening operations. A tiny isolated target with no subsequent neighbours processed can test selection first. A dense pattern is a separate transfer because beam tails accumulate over many failed trials. Counterbalance scan order and include a sham-protected control.

**Kill:** no enrichment beyond the independently calibrated starting fraction; enrichment disappearing under independent isotope readout; excessive unqualified lattice damage; accepted atoms later overwritten; required record quality or dose incompatible with the fixed budget. A failure at the budget cap is an inconclusive or failed operation, not a negative general theorem about isotope manipulation.

## Stage 2: matched useful endpoint

Only after Stage 1 succeeds, choose a qualified scientific endpoint such as a retained mass pattern used to distinguish isotope and structural-defect effects. Compare complete acquisition and execution with optimized isotope-controlled CVD, growth into a crack, and direct vibrational-isotope identification plus conventional atomic manipulation. Include enriched-precursor procurement in the comparator and include all ejection/refill failures in the proposed route.

Match final isotope distribution, pattern geometry, lattice defects, chemical composition, retention, substrate and measurement uncertainty. Charge membrane manufacture/transfer, source preparation, heating, vacuum, beam generation and dump/recovery, detector power, image storage/analysis, controller latency, drift alignment, all feed and reset, unsuccessful specimens, verification and cleanup. Report incident beam kinetic energy, absorbed specimen heat and wall-plug energy separately.

**Success definition:** a reproducible complete operation satisfying the final contract at a demonstrated useful cost/performance advantage. A fitted exponential tail, a theoretical .999 posterior, a source-reported fast fill or a beautiful final image alone does not satisfy this definition.

No Stage 0, 1 or 2 evidence was acquired in this campaign. The physical construction stops here.
