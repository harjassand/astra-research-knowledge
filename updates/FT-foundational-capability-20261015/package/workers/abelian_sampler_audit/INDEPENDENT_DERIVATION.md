Let G be a supplied, explicitly represented finite abelian group. Replace each scalar coefficient by a supplied endomorphism A:G→G. At one original layer, a pending site-time z carries a homomorphism T_z:G→H, H=G^a, where a is the number of requested raw G-valued coordinates. Aggregate coincident dependencies by addition of homomorphisms; process them in descending time. On a gate, the parent contribution is T_z∘A, not a division or a preimage. Retain T_z(F_z(lower)) as an unevaluated forcing term.

Maintain V≤H generated by the images of the retained Haar homomorphisms. A pending dependency can be discarded precisely when im(T_z)⊆V. For a Haar event, add im(T_z) to V. No Haar values are exposed before all dependencies and lower forcing prerequisites are selected.

If U is independent and uniform on V and Z takes values in V, then U+Z is uniform on V, including jointly with any other variables independent of U. This statement fails when U and Z are correlated. Here descending-time substitution ensures every discarded same-layer ancestor precedes the revealed Haar events; conditional on the shared category field and lower-layer histories, those Haar coordinates are independent of the older same-layer process. Strict triangularity makes the entire lower-layer process independent of upper-layer Haar values conditional on the category field. These two facts supply the required independence. Category-only adaptive selection does not bias the unexposed Haar coordinates.

Each exposed Haar event has im(T_z) not contained in V and therefore strictly enlarges V. Every strict subgroup inclusion increases order by an integer factor at least two. The maximum chain length in G^a is a Ω(|G|), where Ω counts prime factors with multiplicity. Thus fresh categories at this layer are dominated by NB(a Ω(|G|),s). The algorithm needs no factorization to run: a log2|G| is a valid looser bound.

For cyclic G=Z/MZ, T_z is represented by a coefficient vector v_z∈(Z/MZ)^a. The image is the cyclic subgroup generated by v_z, so testing v_z∈V suffices. Propagation is v_z A mod M, including nonunits. Uniform sampling from V may be implemented by independent uniform G inputs through every retained Haar homomorphism and summing; no inversion or canonical basis is needed for that step. Membership needs an exact modular subgroup algorithm. Explicit subgroup enumeration costs exponential in a log M and cannot establish polynomial bit complexity.

With L=Ω(|G|), h=kc², and original batch size m, the original recursive accounting gives

    E Q ≤ (Lm/s) Σ_{j=0}^{c-1}(1+Lh/s)^j.

At fixed original depth c this is polynomial in L,m,k,1/s. It is an expected query bound and an almost-sure termination statement. Geometric waiting can be arbitrarily long; no finite worst-case query bound follows. Polynomial expected bit complexity additionally requires supplied finite group/endomorphism representations, polynomial subgroup membership, charged gate descriptions/evaluation, exact rational category sampling, and charged site/time addresses. It does not follow from a finite group black box alone.

Comparator warning from the parent: for Z/p^aZ, base-p digits turn multiplication/addition into affine same-digit F_p updates with carry terms depending only on lower digits. Order levels lexicographically by original layer then digit. This realizes the same operation inside N610 at depth ac. A direct subgroup resolver may improve the dependence on a; it does not by itself establish a new operational model class. CRT/general composite and arbitrary forcing need separate care, since arbitrary forcing can couple prime components.
