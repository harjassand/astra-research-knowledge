the accepting vibrations differently. Simultaneously phase-modulate the IR
pump by the accumulated target Stark shift. In the tracked-pump frame the
desired vibration stays resonant while accepting channels acquire relative
phase. The proposed application was selective chemical deconstruction before
intramolecular vibrational redistribution (IVR).

MATHEMATICAL INTERFACE

One accepting channel has unperturbed detuning Delta, coupling J and memory
kernel exp[-kappa s], kappa>0. The target-tracked differential phase is
u(t)=exp[-i theta(t)], theta'(t)=q(t). Let C(s) denote the stationary or
long-time-averaged autocorrelation of u. Assume C is positive definite,
C(0)=1, u has a mean-square derivative, and

  mean |u'(t)|^2 = mean q(t)^2 <= A^2.                    (1)

These hypotheses hold for ordinary smooth periodic/quasiperiodic controls
and stationary finite-variance level shifts with a randomized global phase.
They deliberately exclude an ideal white frequency noise with infinite
instantaneous variance. The analysis is the controlled weak-transfer limit
J -> 0, observed at times O(J^-2); its rates are not asserted to be exact
at arbitrary strong J or for a fully nonlinear reactive potential.

Bochner/Parseval supplies a nonnegative normalized spectral measure m such
that

  C(s)=integral exp[-i omega s] m(d omega),
  integral m=1, integral omega^2 m(d omega)<=A^2.          (2)

At second order in J, the long-time leakage rate is

  Gamma=2 J^2 Re integral_0^infty exp[(-kappa+i Delta)s] C(s) ds
       =2 J^2 integral kappa/[kappa^2+(Delta-omega)^2] m(d omega). (3)

The interchange is justified by |C(s)|<=1 and kappa>0. This formula is a
redistribution of the original acceptor spectrum by a normalized control
filter, not destruction of its spectral weight.

THEOREM: STATIC DETUNING IS OPTIMAL FOR A RESONANT CHANNEL AT FIXED RMS

For Delta=0 and every control obeying (1),

  Gamma >= 2 J^2 kappa/[kappa^2+A^2].                    (4)

Proof: f(x)=kappa/(kappa^2+x) is decreasing and strictly convex on x>=0.
Apply Jensen to x=omega^2 in (3), then use (2):

  integral f(omega^2)dm >= f(integral omega^2 dm) >= f(A^2).

The bound is attained by the constant detuning q(t)=A, whose measure is a
single spectral line. Hence unlimited modulation bandwidth, random noise,
phase synchronization and elaborate waveforms cannot improve upon simple
static Stark detuning at the same RMS differential shift in this model.
Phase synchronization preserves pump resonance but adds no new leakage
suppression beyond the known static-detuning control operation.

For a channel initially detuned by Delta,

  Gamma >=2 J^2 kappa/[kappa^2+(|Delta|+A)^2],             (5)

using integral (Delta-omega)^2 dm <= (|Delta|+A)^2.
Unlike (4), (5) need not be sharp simultaneously across channels.

COROLLARY: MANY RESONANT CHANNELS AND ONE GLOBAL FIELD

Let channel j have differential Stark slope d_j, so q_j(t)=d_j E(t),
and suppose mean E(t)^2 <= E_rms^2. Independent weak accepting channels give

  Gamma_total >= sum_j 2 J_j^2 kappa_j/
                     [kappa_j^2+d_j^2 E_rms^2].           (6)

A static field E=+/-E_rms attains every term simultaneously if each initial
Delta_j=0. Thus even when every molecule sees the same waveform and the pump
is tracked perfectly, the entire protection scheme is dominated by this
static-field baseline for resonant Lorentzian channels. Correlations among
accepting channels and strong/nonlinear dynamics are outside this corollary.

If some d_j=0, that channel is exactly invariant under the waveform, before
any weak-coupling approximation. For a Fermi-resonant interaction

  V=J(b c^dagger d^dagger+b^dagger c d),
  H_control/hbar=E(t)(a_b n_b+a_c n_c+a_d n_d),

  [H_control,V]=0 whenever a_b=a_c+a_d.                  (7)

Such a physically permissible channel cannot be isolated by this global
diagonal control at any waveform strength or bandwidth. A universal claim
therefore needs acquired nonzero *transition* slope differences for every
significant multiphonon leakage channel, not merely different single-mode
frequencies or slopes.

DENSE NEAR-IMMUNE CHANNELS: AN EXACT INTEGRAL EXAMPLE

For common kappa and a uniform coupling-weight distribution of differential
slopes d in [-d_max,d_max], all initially resonant, (6) and its saturating
static field give

  Gamma_total/Gamma_total(0) = atan(x)/x,
  x=d_max E_rms/kappa.                                  (8)

At large field this is pi/(2x), not 1/x^2. The channels with differential
slope near zero dominate. Tenfold suppression requires x=15.04...; hundredfold
requires x=156.44.... Field-energy density grows as x^2. Large external
bandwidth cannot remove this near-immune-slope cost.

PHYSICAL RESOURCE STATEMENTS

For a single resonant linear-Stark channel, reducing leakage by factor S
requires

  |d| E_rms >= kappa sqrt(S-1).                          (9)

The vacuum electric-field energy density alone is >=epsilon_0 E_rms^2/2.
This is stored energy, not a claim about dissipated work or required wall
power: static electrodes can recycle field energy. In a specified propagating
wave architecture the corresponding irradiance is epsilon_0 c E_rms^2,
with RMS-field convention. An AC-Stark implementation proportional to E^2
must use its actual differential polarizability and includes a fourth field
moment in (1); substituting a linear coefficient would be incorrect.

Perfect pump tracking supplies the full target frequency/shift calibration
along the anharmonic excitation ladder, waveform synchronization, sample
orientation consistency and sufficient pump bandwidth. These are assumed
in (4), not obtained by it. A molecular cleavage yield, reaction-coordinate
barrier crossing, off-target photochemistry, ionization, controller losses,
field damage limits and continuous material throughput were not proved.

STOP DECISION

The attractive extra operation (noise-protected resonance tracking) loses to
the known static-detuning baseline in an explicit matched model. Channels
in the control commutant remain completely unprotected. No experimentally
credible nonlinear regime beating these controls was identified. This route
is retained as a precise negative and is not a proposed new technology.
