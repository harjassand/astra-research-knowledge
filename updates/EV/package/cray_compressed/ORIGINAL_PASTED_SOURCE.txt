Consider a strip of \(m\) sites containing \(K\ge1\) vacancies. Choose one of its vacant sites uniformly and cyclically rotate the strip so that the chosen vacancy reaches site zero.
An adjacent empty strip supplies a hard-KA implementation: every adjacent transposition inside the active strip has the required extra vacant neighbour at both endpoints. A rotation needs at most \(m(m-1)\) elementary instructions.
The important fact is not the forward route length. It is the exact output density.
Starting with Bernoulli equilibrium restricted to nonfull strips, the output density relative to the same equilibrium measure is
\[ \boxed{ \rho_m(\eta)=\frac{m}{K(\eta)}\mathbf1_{\{\eta_0=0\}}. } \tag{1} \]
Proof. For any output whose first site is vacant, each of the \(m\) rotations has one inverse input. Rotation preserves equilibrium weight and vacancy count. Each inverse contribution has weight \(1/K\). Their sum is \(m/K\).
Periodic configurations do not invalidate the identity: group elements are counted with their multiplicity.
Consequently, the exact squared-density moment is
\[ V_m(p) = m\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac1k {m\choose k}q^kp^{m-k}, \qquad q=1-p. \]
It has a scalar recurrence:
\[ \boxed{ V_1=q,\qquad V_m=\frac{mp}{m-1}V_{m-1}+1-p^m. } \tag{2} \]
For example, writing \(F_m=V_m/m\),
\[ F_m = \int_0^1\frac{(p+qt)^m-p^m}{t}\,dt = \sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{p^{m-k}(1-p^k)}k, \]
which immediately gives (2).
Thus the contributions of exponentially many strip configurations can be summed through one scalar recurrence.
Equation (1) is also optimal for endpoint concentration under these requirements.
Within a sector containing \(K=k\) vacancies, the target event \(\eta_0=0\) occupies a fraction \(k/m\) of the sector. Any particle-number-preserving kernel that sends the full sector measure into that event must have average output density \(m/k\) there.
Cauchy–Schwarz minimises the second moment when that density is constant. The balanced rotation attains equality in every sector.
This is optimality of the endpoint density—not of the microscopic current energy or the complete cooperative module. It nevertheless separates avoidable history-counting losses from the concentration imposed by the chosen target.
