Stability of approximate quantum broadcasting
Results and limits of a finite research campaign
ASTRA ULTRA campaign∗
9 October 2026
Abstract
The campaign did not establish a historic-scale breakthrough. Its strongest internally
checked results concern approximate quantum broadcasting. First, a deduction from Kitaev’s
approximate factorization theorem gives a dimension-independent, linear bound on the distance
of a self-compatible, nearly idempotent channel to an entanglement-breaking channel. An ex-
plicit antisymmetric example proves that a weaker, unentangled-input hypothesis cannot replace
thecompletenorm. Second, reconstructionofanarchivedpure-stateargument, apairwisecoher-
ence estimate, and a second-order Gram-matrix measurement construction improve its reported
fourth-root modulus to a linear modulus. An elementary lower example shows that the linear
order cannot be improved uniformly. Complete deductions, assumptions, resource boundaries,
and unresolved novelty questions are recorded below. Neither result establishes a new physical
phenomenon or a dimension-independent computational capability.

2 Nearly idempotent broadcastable channels
A channel Φ :T (H)→T (H) is self-compatible if one channelC :T (H)→T (H⊗H) has both
marginals Φ. This requirement concerns the entire channel, including reference systems. It is
substantially stronger than broadcasting a specified state family.

2.4 Consequences and resource limits
For any reference system, replacing one use ofΦ by Λ changes the joint state in trace norm by at
mostβ. Replacingt uses costs at mosttβ by telescoping. AlsoΛΦt−1 is EB and withinβ of Φt for
every t≥ 1. These are mathematical channel comparisons, not claims about a measured physical
apparatus.
When A = Ck, the classical transition DE retains each label with probability at least 1−
γ/2. The conclusion does not identify a physical preferred basis, prove independent fragments
in the actual broadcaster, or solve tomography. The factorization is existential here: obtaining
Φ, certifying a diamond defect, constructingD,E , implementing measurements, and establishing
numerical source constants are distinct acquisition and computation tasks. Their costs are not
dimension independent.

4 What the campaign established and did not establish
Thetworesultshavedifferentpremises. Theorem2usesacompletenear-idempotenceconditionand
gives a complete channel approximation, through a substantial imported theorem. Theorem 4 uses
only a promised pure-state family and gives a uniform statewise reconstruction with an explicit
small-error modulus. Proposition 3 proves that a tempting weakening of the first premise fails.
None of these statements settles general mixed-family approximate broadcasting, the best constant
in the pure-family modulus, or a major open conjecture.
