# Focused mathematical and prior-art check

Date: 2026-10-09. Scope: one bounded AI-assisted mathematical/code check of the marked-pool candidate and terminal-product extension. This is an internal source-AI check, not external expert review, formal proof verification, a validated numerical certificate, or a comprehensive priority search.

## Bottom line

The exact marked-pool construction is mathematically sound under the stated model. Its useful contribution, if not already published, is a specialized positive state-space reduction and implementation, not a new Poisson/uniformization or likelihood-interval principle. Fox, Neuert & Munsky (2016) already derive monotone single-cell data-likelihood bounds from FSP truncation. The one-enzyme product benchmark is a narrow capability demonstration. Novelty, superiority to the best existing output reduction, and transformative practical significance remain unestablished.

Two substantive multi-catalyst code defects and a vacuous regression were found before repair. The C=1 results do not exercise either defective branch. Repair and independent labelled-molecule checks are recorded separately below when complete; none of the original C=2 evidence should be cited as verification.

## Mathematical check

1. **Deferred revelation survives rejected attempts.** Give each label independent initial state and first-order transition randomness. Reveal a label only when a free catalyst samples it; every such label is permanently marked, including a non-A label whose binding fails. The adaptive selection rule depends only on previously revealed labels and catalyst histories, never on the unrevealed paths. Conditional on this revelation filtration through time t and its unrevealed label set, those labels therefore remain independent with common law p(t)=mu0 exp(Qt). Mixing over revealed histories with the same (b,n) preserves the same product law. This statement is not conditioning on future observations, or on the entire physical population history. Marking only successful binders would fail.
2. **Exchangeability closure.** A marked free molecule's future depends only on its current q-state, and each bound substrate has the same memoryless koff/kcat release law. No binding identity or age is needed. Free counts n and bound count b are sufficient; total permanent tags are m=|n|+b. Candidate untouched weight is precisely (C-b)/C times (N-m)/N times p_i(t). Adding bound-catalyst, bound-slot, and marked-free-slot alternatives gives total weight one.
3. **Finite dimensions.** For N≥K, D=sum_b binom(K-b+q+1,q+1), with b=0,...,min(C,K). This equals the README's double sum and is O(K^(q+1)) at fixed q,C. It is a containing state space, not a claim that every listed state is reachable. With an absorbing, initially absent product capped at r, dimension is sum_b sum_s=0^min(r,K-b) binom(K-b-s+q,q), hence O(K^q) at fixed r. q=4,C=1,K=12,r=1 gives 5,551.
4. **Terminal observations.** Conditional untouched counts are multinomial; for a partial observation, merge all unobserved categories into a single residual category. Product-only evaluation reduces to n_P=r when p_P(t)=0. If P is absorbing and cannot bind, paths with product count above r cannot contribute and can be killed exactly. Extra product killing means total retained mass is generally LESS than the Poisson CDF, even though the target likelihood error remains bounded by the same candidate tail. The code's observed_bound argument denotes a JOINT endpoint event, not the normalized conditional probability given B(T)=b.
5. **Truncation.** With candidate count J~Poisson(C lambda T), exact nonnegative event layers give 0≤L-L_K≤Pr(J>K). Delta/L_K bounds relative error conservatively. This does not include floating-point ODE, matrix-exponential, quadrature, multinomial, or tail-evaluation error. Discarding negative numerical components does not itself preserve a certified lower bound. A full certified implementation needs a separate numerical enclosure. The one-candidate product-path integral is a correct mathematical positive lower bound, but its floating-point quadrature is not a validated lower endpoint.
6. **Cost qualifications.** Fixed-K dimension is independent of N; fixed-relative-error cost need not be when likelihoods become small. At fixed concentration, lambda can remain fixed as N and volume grow. At fixed volume, physical lambda generally grows with N, so the favorable bound does not persist. Large C lambda T, q, or first-order stiffness can dominate cost. For the particular P=1 experiment the positive one-candidate path supplies an N-independent likelihood lower bound, so fixed-accuracy N-independent state size is justified within that parameter/model family.

### An avoidable event coordinate

The candidate event coordinate j is not required for a valid reduced FSP. Aggregate over j, retain (b,n), and kill only when the number of distinct permanent tags would exceed K. Since m is nondecreasing and m≤J, lost probability is at most Pr(J>K). This admits additional histories with many repeated/no-op candidates, so its likelihood lower bound is at least as large as the event-truncated one, with the same conservative tail. Dimension becomes sum_b binom(K-b+q,q)=O(K^q), and O(K^(q-1)) under a fixed product cap. Retained mass is not generally the Poisson CDF. This is a mathematical simplification identified by this check, not a benchmarked implementation or a new priority claim.

## Code findings before repair

- Base solver's Q transitions for b>0 used destination b=1, incorrectly changing bound occupancy when b≥2 and a free tag switches.
- Both base and partial solver used U=N-|n| in candidate untouched weights, omitting bound tags. For 0<b<C, outgoing candidate weights exceed one by ((C-b)/C)b/N. Correct U is N-|n|-b.
- Existing two-catalyst test used N=16, free observation (9,5,0), and observed_bound=0. The observation contains only 14 molecules, so both probabilities are zero and the test passes vacuously.
- Exact product-pruning guards initially accepted near-zero Q entries by tolerance. A nonzero outgoing P rate permits return from an over-target state; it must not be silently treated as exact structural zero. Main benchmarks use literal zeros and are unaffected.

## Closest primary prior and what it does / does not establish

- **Mjolsness, 2013, TOPE.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.5231), DOI 10.1088/1478-3975/10/3/035009. Splits a stochastic generator into an analytically propagated part and event insertions, yielding time-ordered series and algorithms. Here the split L=L0+Lambda(P-I), or W0=L0-Lambda I and W1=Lambda P, gives the candidate-count expansion directly. The possible contribution is efficient positive compression of its terms via permanent marks and untouched multinomials, not the expansion itself.
- **Sunkara, 2019, reaction-count CME.** [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7515091/), DOI 10.3390/e21060607. Counts reaction firings and exploits forward-stepping histories/path chains. Counting auxiliary attempts while analytically eliminating all bulk monomolecular firings is a specialized difference; event counting and lower approximations alone are established.
- **Ohkubo, 2017, dual transition-probability computation.** [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.07193), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.95.023304. Extended Doi–Peliti dual evolution computes transition probabilities and supports reuse across rate constants. Its coefficients need not form a probability distribution, unlike this positive tagged construction. No matching finite tagged-count closure was identified in the inspected derivation.
- **Sturm & Swart, pathwise duals.** [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.06284), DOI 10.1007/s10959-016-0721-5. Poisson graphical constructions and pathwise duality for monotone/additive finite-state processes establish a broad surrounding tradition. The present arbitrary Q model is not automatically within their monotone/additive hypotheses; a graphical interpretation is not itself evidence of a new duality theorem.
- **Aranyi & Toth, 1977.** [Original article abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/613716/). Exact stochastic Michaelis–Menten time courses with one enzyme, extendable to few enzymes, predate this candidate. **Grima & Leier, 2017**, [primary article record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27959536/), DOI 10.1021/acs.jpcb.6b08891, gives exact product-formation rate results without substrate abundance assumptions; this is not the same as the present full endpoint distribution, but rules out broad claims that finite-pool low-copy enzymology is newly tractable.
- **Jahnke & Huisinga, 2007**, DOI 10.1007/s00285-006-0034-x; and **Vastola, 2021**, [primary article record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34635944/), DOI 10.1007/s00285-021-01670-7: monomolecular multinomial solutions and broader first-order Doi–Peliti algorithms are existing analytical machinery.
- **Output reduction is a serious untested competitor.** [Lopez-Caamal & Marquez-Lago, 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0103521) explicitly approximates product-count-range probabilities in enzyme kinetics, including an example reducing 5,151 states to 16, with setup cost and output error analysis. This directly qualifies broad superiority claims; its error norm is not automatically a pointwise likelihood-relative certificate. [Kazeev et al., 2014](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003359) and [Einkemmer, Mangott & Prugger, 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11792) establish tensor/QTT and hierarchical dynamical low-rank competitors. None was benchmarked here.

The search inspected biochemical likelihood, low-copy enzyme, reaction-count, operator expansion, graphical/particle-duality, and current tensor-reduction lines. It did not establish a direct anticipation of this precise count closure, and did not establish its absence. An independent expert priority assessment and stronger matched computational comparisons would be needed before calling the candidate a new or transformative algorithm.

## Independent verification status

The repaired solvers passed all 15 comparisons in `check_labeled_reference.py`, which constructs the physical labelled-molecule CTMC without reusing either count-FSP or marked transition generators. Coverage includes iid mixed initial states, b=0/1/2 endpoints, a general partial-state marginal, C>N and N<K boundaries, and C=2 irreversible-product pruning. All measured likelihood differences were nonnegative and below their respective Poisson tails; the test permits a 3e-11 numerical slack, but none of these likelihood comparisons needed it. Uncapped retained mass matched the Poisson CDF to 2.3e-16. Product-capped retained masses were correctly smaller. These are numerical checks, not rigorous solver enclosures.
