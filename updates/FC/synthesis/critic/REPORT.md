## Decision

The principal restricted collision, quantum reweighting and Te kinematic
arguments survive this review. Their scopes are materially different. None
establishes a civilization-enabling operation, an acquired real material, or
superiority over the strongest matched method. The physics branch is a
retained-event hypothesis, not a demonstrated physical mechanism.

The most consequential surviving quantum advance changes the input operation:
actual coupled equilibrium measurements replace an uncoupled-bath information
hierarchy for a restricted diagonal finite-temperature design family. This
does not contradict N785/N786. It introduces equilibrium and control obligations
which are absent from passive spectroscopy and remain unclosed.

No decisive mathematical counterexample to the stated source theorems was
found. Explicit counterexamples do refute three tempting transfers: histogram
alone to quantum reweighting, a stationary-looking metastable histogram to
global Gibbs equilibrium, and ideal continuous-law sampling to a literal
floating-point TV guarantee.

## Collision sampling: exact surviving scope

The homogeneous isotropic-cutoff three-dimensional Maxwell equation closes on
centered Gaussian scalar scale mixtures. Uniform angular measure induces a
uniform averaging fraction; scalar variances remain in the supplied [a,b].
The triangular moment equation and its finite-mark process are consistent.
Effective separation at block size q has rate (q-1)/(q+1), and each successful
split conserves the total mark count. The k-1 split bound follows exactly.
Shared ancestry is essential: independent initial draws per mark give the
wrong transient law, already at time zero.

The Laguerre/Cauchy relative-density envelope is valid for the stated
parameters. The signed value is converted to a legal p-coin before applying
Huber's credited linear Bernoulli factory. I checked the primary theorem and
its Section 2.2 pseudocode; the multiplier-two margin has the needed form.
The source prototype preserves initialization before epsilon clipping and
the repeat-until structure. The actual mathematical acceptance probability is
f_t/(C G_g). Integrating the reciprocal lower envelope is finite precisely
under g<2a, yielding the stated sufficient b<3a/2 parameter window.

Query cost can correlate with query outcome without invalidating the expected
sum: request indicators are measurable before each fresh query. The same
renewal reasoning handles cost/acceptance correlation between proposal cycles.
This proves expected ideal work, not bounded runtime or finite-bit work.
Reading/representing t, increasingly large factory integers, exact comparisons,
random primitive implementation and output encoding remain separately charged.

For the broader approximate sampler, conditional first-order Taylor terms
cancel because the marked empirical mean is conditionally unbiased. The exact
mean-square identity and Gaussian second-derivative L1 bound have the advertised
constants. The TV theorem concerns the ideal continuous output. A literal
floating-point output is supported on a countable set, which has continuous
target probability zero, so their literal TV distance is one. Quantized output
or an observation model is a necessary changed contract.

My independent program constructs exact rational finite marked-partition
generators for n=1,...,8 and checks 32 propagated moment/variance cases against
an independently written moment ODE and the analytic second-moment identity.
It also evaluates 3,321 Laguerre envelope points and a reciprocal-envelope
Gaussian integral. These finite floating diagnostics do not certify the
universal envelope or exact random-bit implementation. The recomputed
example bound is 292.39841191645786 signed calls per output.

The primary literature already includes recursive/time-relaxed Maxwell methods,
tractable deterministic moment hierarchies, and perfect-simulation work for
the one-dimensional Kac model. In particular,
[Corcoran, Jennings and VaughanMiller](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.01551)
give a finite-N transient Poisson algorithm and a limiting epsilon-perfect
sampler. Their exact finite-N and stationary tasks differ from the new
restricted nonlinear transient target; this is an additional comparator,
not proof that the source is anticipated. No exhaustive priority conclusion
follows. The source's very inexpensive degree-96 deterministic reference is a
serious matched-output baseline, and the source establishes no advantage over
it for approximate moment/density queries.

## Quantum reweighting: exact surviving scope

All traces must remain in one fixed finite conserved sector. The reference
histogram is measured in the actual coupled Gibbs state. D is the pinched
Hamiltonian; it is not the result of measuring or pinching the Gibbs state.
Only diagonal interventions V=sum v_i P_i are covered, unless a residual
operator norm is separately acquired and added to the error interval.

The blockwise scalar Jensen trace bound z_i<=c_i is sound; it does not require
the false operator-monotonicity claim for the exponential. The block norm graph
and Golden--Thompson give the stated C_norm. The kinetic expectation tau gives
log(Z_H/Z_D)<=-beta*tau by the Gibbs variational principle, with tau<=0 from
concavity. A lower confidence bound on tau is the correct conservative input.
Any adaptive kinetic stopping needs its own time-uniform guarantee.

The latent weights w_i=z_i/Z_H satisfy 0<=w_i<=p_i and sum w_i>=1/C. Minimizing
the reweighted mass supplies the opposite side of the quantum interval.
Trimming to equality is allowed because all exponential weights are positive.
The uniform strong-intervention claim really requires every relevant
p_i>eta=1-1/C. More label shots do not remove physical coherence error or an
initially tiny configuration weight. The design-specific interval remains
valid when the uniform corollary fails.

The sharp block-partition compression also survives scrutiny. Duhamel closed
words carry residence fractions totaling one. Generalized Schatten Holder
bounds use the block partition traces to those fractions, and off-diagonal
operator norms for transition factors. Embedding rectangular transitions in
the direct sum introduces no dimension multiplier. Scalar one-dimensional
blocks with real negative couplings attain the upper matrix exponential.
This sharpness has the block-information contract, not the histogram contract;
it does not establish minimax optimality of the final decision interval.

My independently coded tests include 96 complex-Hermitian block models with

## Explicit preparation counterexample

This is a review qualification of a substitution already disclaimed by the
source, not an additional claim of scientific novelty. Use beta=1 and four
classical states in order (label0,b0),(label1,b0),(label0,b1),(label1,b1), with

    H=diag(0,0,-10,10),   V=diag(1/5,-4/5,1/5,-4/5).

The label-changing T is exactly zero, so a correct equilibrium histogram would
give exact reweighting. A trapped preparation uniformly occupying the first
two states instead reports p=(1/2,1/2), remains stationary under H, and gives

    Delta_wrong=1/5-log[(1+e)/2]<0.

The genuine global Gibbs probability of label1 is

    p1=(1+exp(-10))/(2+exp(10)+exp(-10))<1/512.

Consequently its true design change is

    Delta_true=1/5-log[1+p1(e-1)]>1/5-1/256>0.

The strict bounds use e>2, e<3, exp(1/5)<5/4, and log(1+x)<x. Numerically the
false estimate is -0.4201145 and the true change is +0.1999220. A local label
histogram can therefore look stationary and have no label coherence while
missing the free-energy-dominating branch. A calibrated equilibrium discrepancy
or preparation/mixing guarantee remains substantive acquired information.
Small beta*kappa alone does not provide it.

## Coherent phonon addressing: qualified hypothesis

The recoil identity, three radial basis projections (v,-v/2,-v/2), small-signal
contrast, timing convolution and independent-hazard selectivity arithmetic
are internally consistent. I independently recomputed the numerical constants:
300 keV gives stationary maximum recoil 6.67346 eV, 0.0855284 eV per measured
picometre at 3.24 THz, basis splitting 0.128293 eV/pm, and equivalent beam
shift 3.13372 keV/pm. These sensitivities do not give a retained-event yield.

The primary Te report is a bulk polycrystalline reflectivity experiment.
Its largest displacement is optically inferred, with carrier diffusion and
phonon chirp; it is not a directly calibrated velocity for a single-domain
electron-transparent processing specimen. Under the explicitly literal
equal-amplitude interpretation, the coherent energy is 0.157845 eV/atom versus
estimated absorbed energy 0.0747619 eV/atom, ratio 2.1113. The source correctly
rejects importing this as a qualified operating point. The estimate does not
refute the primary experiment: coordinate normalization, depth/volume,
inference, damping and available energy reservoirs need reconciliation.

The source's matched opposite-velocity comparison additionally requires matched
positions/electronic states and a recoil-dominated retained kernel. Pump delay
alone does not establish those premises. Neighbour momentum, angular response,
photoinduced barrier changes and recombination can all alter retention. No
elemental-Te retained-event kernel or displacement threshold was acquired.
The CdTe threshold cannot qualify elemental Te.

FWHM timing factors reproduce 0.91081, 0.68819 and 0.22431 for 50,100,200 fs.
The independent irreversible-event 99% target/1% unwanted requirement is
458.2106 in integrated hazard ratio. Positive mixtures of exposures cannot
exceed their maximum ratio; feedback/healing changes the model and must be
analysed anew. This is a fidelity requirement, not evidence that the proposed
material meets it. Throughput, wall-energy per retained event and fatigue remain
unknown. A retained sublattice-resolved response under calibrated trajectories,
matched dose, phase and electronic-state controls is the next decisive evidence.
