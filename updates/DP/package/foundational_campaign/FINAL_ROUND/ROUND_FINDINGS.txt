1. STRONGEST END-TO-END SAMPLER PROOF CANDIDATE

File: r16_integrable_mean_mechanism.txt, frozen version 1.

For every FIXED a>0, globally L-Lipschitz conservative g=grad F, F C^{1,1},
and fixed sigma/L>0, the internally reconstructed construction targets
N(Eg(G),sigma^2 I_d) to TV epsilon with

  C_a d^a poly_a(1/epsilon)

whole-vector g-value queries on EVERY execution, and polynomial total real
arithmetic/storage. The written proof states sigma>=L; the fixed-ratio
extension uses a constant number of independent larger-noise calls and their
average, as in R20 section4. Convexity is unnecessary. No derivative, VJP,
potential-value, conditional-mean or hidden-basis oracle is supplied.

Mechanism: a symmetric Gaussian Stein resolvent gives an exactly invariant
skew flow; dimension-free one-slot derivative bounds control its finite
mean-value circuit; uncached Gaussian center-noise absorption recursively
eliminates every temporary exact mean; root replication restores the desired
noise. Root reconstructed the load-bearing proof. This is an internal proof
candidate extending/specializing the pinned OpenAI construction, not an
externally checked theorem or a new general subpolynomial sampling claim.

It does NOT imply polylogarithmic queries. Its constants, interpolation orders
and expression-tree height depend on fixed a. Substituting a=1/log d is
unjustified. R21's quantitative analysis of the present fixed-depth retuning
leaves an exp(c sqrt(log(d/epsilon))) type cost on that route, rather than the
requested polylog bound. That is a route limitation, not a problem lower bound.

The exact-real model includes Gaussian seeds. Finite-bit output must compare
to a commonly rounded target: a discrete output has TV one from an unrounded
continuous Gaussian. Unknown bit lengths of arbitrary oracle reals are not
claimed to be bounded.

2. EXACT TWO-QUERY BOUNDED STEIN ACTION

File: r22_polylog_mean_factory.txt, sections9–12.

Let H_s(c)=E Dg(c+sZ). Given v!=0, put u=v/||v||, draw independent
Z_perp~N(0,I-uu^*) and R=s chi_3, and query the two endpoints to return

 J_s(c;v)=||v|| [g(c+sZ_perp+Ru)-g(c+sZ_perp-Ru)]/(2R).

Then, exactly,

 E[J_s(c;v)|c,v]=H_s(c)v,
 ||J_s(c;v)||<=L||v|| on EVERY execution.

Proof: if U is independent uniform[-1,1], UR~N(0,s^2). The secant equals
the uniform integral of the weak directional derivative along its chord;
c+sZ_perp+URu is a full Gaussian. Lipschitzness gives the deterministic norm
bound. No limit of infinitesimal differences or Hessian continuity is needed.

For V uniform[0,b], b J_s(Vc;v) with s=sqrt(1-V^2) acquires
K_b(c)v in expectation with bound bL||v||. This includes b=1 almost surely,
and still costs TWO original g queries. Affine g gives an exact action in
every realization. Fresh actions compose unbiased mean matrix words.

The Gaussian source point can be retained explicitly. With fresh standard
Gaussian v, the action is a Rao–Blackwellization of Dg(X)v with X independent
Gaussian, hence is convex-order dominated by N(0,L^2 I). Fixed-center fresh
words inherit the corresponding Gaussian domination. Adaptive dependence
of a later center on the current word does not automatically inherit it.

This removes a derivative-action oracle assumption. It does NOT acquire a
single realized symmetric matrix, its square root, or the required Gaussian
law. The exact latent completion using Dg(X) is still unacquired.

Prior-art boundary: the uniform times chi_3 normal representation is classical
(Zhang–Brown–George–Zhao, arXiv:1511.06190, section3, tracing to Bryson–Johnson).
Gaussian Stein Hessian estimators also predate this campaign (for example
Balasubramanian–Ghadimi, arXiv:1809.06474). Priority of this particular bounded
whole-vector secant interface remains UNKNOWN.

R28 also constructed a two-query radius-conditioned orthogonal sphere
rotation. Its transformed source and output have an EXACT independent
Gaussian law. With a size-biased radial quantile, the ORIGINAL source and
new output have the desired heat-action cross moment in one supplied input
direction. That original pair is not proved jointly Gaussian; averaging
directions dilutes the cross block by 1/d. The radial quantile has no completed
bit-complexity certificate. This is a positive source-retaining channel lead,
not a full Gaussian completion. See r28_multiscale_gaussian_mean.txt section1.

3. EXACT SOURCE-RETAINING GAUSSIAN OUTPUT CHANNEL

Files: root_autonomous_ou_innovation.txt; R21 sections10,13.

Let delta_gamma K=h-mu, E_gamma h=mu, ||K||<=ell. Choose
a>ell^2/(2S^2), C(y)=[4S^2 I-(2/a)KK^*]^(1/2), and

 dY=-aYdt+sqrt(2a)dB,
 dQ=2(h(Y)-Q)dt-sqrt(2/a)K(Y)dB+C(Y)dW.

For stationary Gaussian Y and Q0=0, its innovation satisfies JOINTLY

 (Y_T,Q_T)~gamma_d tensor
 N((1-e^(-2T))mu,S^2(1-e^(-4T))I).

This is full Gaussianity and terminal-source independence, proved both by
joint characteristic-function deconvolution and a reverse-time martingale
with deterministic bracket. Successive innovation windows are independent.
Its continuous output stream is Brownian with constant mean drift in its
OWN output filtration, not in the larger filtration containing Y.

Writing K=Dk cancels the shared-B stochastic integral, giving an ordinary
time-integral observable plus a conditional Gaussian completion; the exact
formula and constant cancellation are in the root file. The shared-noise
fields commute because K is a row-Jacobian. This analytic fact holds even
for general vector g; reciprocity is required computationally to acquire
adjoint actions. None of h,K,C or their continuum integrals is a free oracle.

Additional proved regularity: full K_1 is C^1 even for raw C^{1,1} g, with
sum_j(partial_j K_1)^2<=L^2I and ||DK_1[v]||F<=L||v||. It does not have
the uniform higher derivatives needed to assert analytic time quadrature.

4. NEWTON STEIN NORMAL FORM — SERIOUS OPEN SAMPLER CANDIDATE

File: r21_polylog_mean_compiler.txt, final normal-form sections.

R21 found an exact algebraic right inverse of the baseline linearized
Gaussian Stein operator by allowing a divergence-free skew gauge. For a
symmetric matrix R_m in homogeneous Gaussian chaos m, use row divergence
delta and D_ij w=partial_j w_i, and define

 s=delta delta R_m, phi=-s/(m+2)^2, u=grad phi,
 w=delta R_m+grad delta u,
 B=(Dw-Dw^*)/(m+2), J_u=-R_m-Du^*+B.

Then delta w=0, delta B=w, delta J_u=u, and POINTWISE

 sym(J_u+Du^*)=-R_m.

Root independently reconstructed these identities. For m=0 they reduce to
u=-R_m Z/2, the first covariance correction. Unlike that correction alone,
the gauge supplies a right inverse for every symmetric chaos residual.

The baseline inverse also satisfies genuine dimension- and degree-independent
Gaussian L2 bounds (matrix norm is Frobenius):

 ||Du||_2<=||R||_2,  ||J_u||_2<=(2+sqrt(2))||R||_2.

These follow from Gaussian creation/annihilation norms and the orthogonal
divergence-free projection of delta R. The full perturbed operator includes
K D_Gu, which is unbounded on ordinary Gaussian L2. Thus a small operator
norm for K does not justify a Neumann series in that norm.

The landmark opportunity is a positive Newton construction with genuinely
quadratic FULL-LAW residual contraction and constant recursive branching.
Depth O(log log(d/epsilon)) could then produce polylogarithmically many
queries. A dimension-free bounded inverse for the FULL linearization,
acquisition of its actions, summable positive implementation, and every-run
query cap remain UNPROVED. Formal cancellation is not that theorem.

5. QUANTUM SELECTED-RANGE TRUNCATION WITHOUT ANGLE LOSSES

Files: r24_quantum_entangling_encoder.txt sections5–10;
root_tag_component_truncation.txt; root_kernel_occupation.txt.

Target: arbitrary rational k-local projectors of sufficiently small relative
rank p(k,Delta), bounded overlap Delta, prepare a trace-epsilon approximation
to some exact common-kernel state in time polynomial in n, explicit local
matrix sizes/bits, log(1/epsilon), with no spectral gap or angle promise.

Owned tags and a triangular graph map M yield a selected exact-kernel range
on a logical consistency space D. Logical QLLL dimension bounds control the
RANK of each connected tag component independently of graph amplitudes.

The central comparison lemma is elementary but decisive: if M,N are
injective on the SAME r-dimensional domain, then

 D_tr(unif RanM,unif RanN)<=rank(M-N)/r.

Proof: their images share dimension at least r-rank(M-N); remove this common
intersection and bound the remaining projector trace norms. There is no
inverse Gram or singular-angle factor.

R24 applies this to connected-polymer truncation and nested logical kernels:
uniform Ran(M_L|D_L) lies within delta_tag(L)+delta_cons(L) of an exact
selected common-kernel uniform state. Both tails decay geometrically at
sufficiently small fixed p,tag fraction. L=O(log(n/epsilon)) suffices.

This is an approximation theorem about uniform RANGES, not the state produced
by applying M_L to uniform logical input. Preparing that range and resolving
the logical constraints remain open. Dense maps on L sites cost d^O(L), which
is quasipolynomial in variable local dimension and violates the full target.
Five exact rational diagnostics check the stated finite triangular examples;
they are not evidence for a general preparation theorem.

6. QUANTUM LOCAL ACQUISITION AND GUARDED HISTORY LIFT

File: r23_quantum_guard_acquisition.txt.

An internally developed local bit-complexity argument combines canonical
shrunk-subspace cuts, operator scaling, and exact rational anchor/filter
selection. It gives proper-support anchors and actual normalized marginal
guards for explicit local rowspaces, with polynomial local matrix cost for
fixed arity. It uses FSG arXiv:2207.08311 and AGL arXiv:1804.01076; those
theorems do not themselves give a general quantum encoder.

For an old bad projector C and a fresh site B, splitting a new guarded row P
by its overlap with the old good space yields a guarded high channel and
an angle-independent low-source rank charge

 rank(P_lo)<=alpha d_B rankC/(1-eta).

The low residual has a normalized creation map whose INPUT effect retains
the fresh guard. Its output range need not. Coherent parent-channel mixing
prevents automatic conversion of this rank budget into local descendants.

Appending a fresh Bell pair converts an actual marginal guard into an
operator contraction. A bounded-branch rooted history lift can therefore
have norm less than one. Geometric reweighting also permits constant-cost
coherent history erasure when a common acquired decoder and an orthogonal
logical chart block are supplied. The exact K-valued source recurrence,
low-polar implementation and disconnected-component composition are still
missing. This conditional lift is not the full encoder.

Concretely, if branch count<=b, each lifted creation norm<=sqrt(alpha),
alpha<=1/(16b^2), and t=1/(2sqrt(b)), the weighted history resolvent has
norm<=2 and the compensating eraser norm<=2/sqrt(3). An acquired orthogonal
empty chart block then certifies success probability>=3/16. Controlled
edge/padding circuits give constant-gap polar normalization. These circuit
premises, the logical root preparation and the K-valued recurrence are
essential; exponentially many history labels alone supply none of them.

7. DECISIVE FAILURES AND SCOPE

* Matching covariance does not establish a Gaussian law. Random covariance
  completion leaves fourth and higher cumulants. A stochastic unbiased
  action is not a random symmetric matrix available inside a square root.

* R28 gives an explicit conservative one-dimensional stress test of a frozen
  central-secant Hamiltonian rotation: g_k(x)=sin(kx)/k has vanishing mean
  Hessian, but its secant at radius1/k is cos(kX)sin(v). The resulting flow
  has variance defect (t^2/2)E cos^2(kX)[1-exp(-2)-2exp(-1)]+O(t^4),
  uniformly nonzero at small fixed t as k grows. Root checked the coefficient.
  Antithetic time reversal preserves it. This refutes that particular frozen
  source channel, not the exact chi_3 factory or the sampling objective.

* The generic expected-matrix-action interface is too weak: A=Bernoulli(p)I,
  ||v||=sqrt(d), requires Omega(d) observations to resolve p by c/sqrt(d),
  while the corresponding Gaussian output means have constant TV separation.
  This is not a lower bound for active deterministic g-value queries; their
  source/endpoints and gradient geometry must be retained.

* An exact fixed-covariance Gaussian family obtained by a fixed finite linear
  Cameron–Martin translation of one Gaussian seed must have affine mean.
  Hence nonlinear exact channels cannot also have that overly strong cache
  interface. This does not exclude approximate polylog samplers; TV does not
  control exponential tails without an additional receipt.

* Bell-projector chains have exponentially small Hilbert–Schmidt word weight
  but normalized source/range rank fraction 1/d^2 at EVERY length. Replacing
  amplitudes by normalized ranges invalidates a proposed rank-decay proof.

* Keeping the unchanged quantum degree-zero row map and claiming a bounded
  contracting homotopy would imply a spectral gap for the original sum of
  projectors. Higher source/syzygy labels alone cannot remove that assumption.

* Global product-chart existence was already established in R01. Rediscovery
  of its Borel/QLLL proof was withdrawn as new progress. A chart does not
  acquire a normalized encoder. The special zero-free syzygy example does
  not refute a generic-tag theorem; a rational tag rotation removes it.

* The internally reconstructed nonconservative hidden-basis mean lower bound
  is Omega(sqrt(d/log d)); it is not a conservative-gradient lower bound.
  Root's strong-monotonicity/firm-nonexpansiveness reduction preserves this
  separation without claiming an inverse oracle or a smoothed hard family.
