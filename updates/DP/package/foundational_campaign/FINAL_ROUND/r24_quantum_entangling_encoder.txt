1. ANCHOR-FIXED DECODING: FILTERS AS AN INPUT PRECONDITIONER ONLY

This exact reduction removes physical forward-filter normalization from
a guarded-encoder route. It is not itself that encoder.

Let V_i be kept local spaces. More generally than r01 section40, for each
clause a let F_a be any product of invertible positive contractions, each
factor supported on some proper local block S within X_a. For each factor
G_{a,S}, introduce the orthoprojector A_{a,S} onto Ran(I-G_{a,S}), lifted
to X_a. Set R'_a=F_a Pi_(V_Xa)R_a, taking the ACTUAL range projector of
this image in the full kept tensor space. Retain the full-V projectors;
do not first compress them into the anchor-good space.

If a global vector phi is killed by every A_{a,S}, each G_{a,S} fixes phi.
The same is true of G_{a,S}^*, and therefore F_a^* fixes phi even when
the individual proper-block factors fail to commute. For every r in R_a,

  <F_a Pi_V r, phi> = <Pi_V r, F_a^* phi> = <r, E phi>.

Here E is the natural product embedding into the original spaces, and
outside cylinder vectors are arbitrary. Thus, UNDER all the anchors,
the filtered and original clause equations are exactly equivalent.
Every state in the augmented filtered common kernel is already an
original common-kernel state after the isometric embedding E. There is
no product-filter execution, decoder success probability, or global
normalization cost. A circuit within trace distance epsilon of the exact
augmented common kernel stays within epsilon of the original joint
kernel by trace-norm contractivity under E.

For the shared unary construction of r01 section40, take one A_i onto
Ran(I-H_i) at each site. Its relative rank is at most

  beta_i/dim V_i <= nu sqrt(p)/(1-nu sqrt(p))

in equal-d arity2 with incidence at most nu. Every unary A_i satisfies
the needed nonempty normalized partial-trace guard automatically. Each
two-site clause gains at most two anchor neighbors; each unary anchor
meets at most nu original clauses. The filtered clauses retain the
full-V marginal caps proved in40.4. One must not replace their actual
projectors by normalized compressed ranges in ker A:39.6 and36 show
that operation can erase the guards. The augmented input is a guarded
mixed-arity instance, rather than a smaller locally renormalized input.

The reduction also survives rational approximation of a local filter:
if an acquired rational F is EXACTLY identity on the rational anchor-good
subspace, exact kernel equivalence is preserved independently of its
approximation quality. Only the numerical cap certification needs a
constant slack margin. Acquiring such anchors/filters with polynomial
bit cost remains R23's separate task; no irrational-minimizer oracle is
silently supplied here.

2. CONDITIONAL RANK LOSS GIVES THE CORRECT TRUNCATION METRIC

An exact dimension lemma can turn discarded long-channel ranks into
trace distance to an exact nested kernel, without an energy/gap estimate.
It requires a PREPARED uniform base kernel and does not acquire one.

Let A be base constraints with projectors P_a and choose numbers x_a<1
satisfying the quantum-LLL conditional-dimension bounds: for every
S subset A and a not in S,

  dim K_(S union {a}) >= (1-x_a) dim K_S.

Write K0=K_A. For an extra local projector E_U of relative rank rho_U,
let N(U)={a in A:X_a intersects U}. Then

  [dim K0-dim(K0 intersect ker E_U)]/dim K0
       <= rho_U product_(a in N(U))(1-x_a)^(-1).           (2.1)

Proof. Constraints outside N(U) avoid U, so their kernel is
L tensor H_U. The rank of E_U on it is rho_U dim(L tensor H_U).
Restriction to K0 cannot increase this rank. Applying the conditional
dimension bound as the N(U) clauses are inserted gives
dim K0 >= dim(L tensor H_U) product_(a in N(U))(1-x_a).
Divide. This proves(2.1), with no subspace-angle bound.

For finitely many extra E_j supported on U_j, put
K1=K0 intersect intersection_j ker E_j. Subadditivity of codimension
of simultaneous linear equations on K0 gives

  delta:=[dim K0-dim K1]/dim K0
     <= sum_j rho_j product_(a in N(U_j))(1-x_a)^(-1).     (2.2)

If the right side is below1, K1 is nonzero. For nested nonzero spaces,
the trace distance (one half of trace norm) between their uniform states
is EXACTLY delta:

  D(ProjK0/dimK0, ProjK1/dimK1)=delta.                    (2.3)

Indeed the difference has eigenvalue1/dimK0 on K0 minus K1, and
1/dimK0-1/dimK1 on K1; summing absolute values proves the formula.
This supplies a principled possible truncation certificate for a positive
cluster compiler: construct a uniform tractable K0 and prove an exact
original-satisfying subcode K1 is obtained by adding the omitted long
channels. Exponential rank decay beats the boundary conditioning factor
in(2.2), which grows only exponentially in |U_j| at bounded incidence.

No conclusion for an arbitrary chosen pure seed follows: it may lie
entirely in K0 minus K1. No conclusion from small Hamiltonian energy
follows either. The exact positive nested-subcode relation, the uniform
K0 preparation, and acquired omitted-channel rank decomposition are
essential. Current source-space word estimates do not establish these.

3. COST AND COMPOSITION GATES THAT CANNOT BE SKIPPED

Even a decay rho_U<=theta^|U| and a log-size connected-cluster cutoff do
not by themselves yield a uniform polynomial algorithm in explicit local
dimensions. Dense algebra on L sites of local dimension d costs d^O(L).
With L=O(log(n/epsilon)) and d an input parameter, this is generally
quasipolynomial in n,d, rather than polynomial in n and the supplied
local matrix dimension d^k. A full result needs fixed d in the contract,
a stronger d-dependent cutoff, or a succinct quantum/classical block
compiler. The stricter uniform explicit-dimension cost is retained here.

Likewise a norm-one exact internal cluster unitary is useful only when
its circuit is acquired. Root's fresh-site proof permits it as an already
implemented contraction on visited sites; that does not implement an
arbitrary direct rotation from reflection oracles. Near-singular angle
data can make a black-box polar/reflection implementation costly even
though its final rotation angle and operator norm are bounded. Explicit
finite local dense algebra can bypass inverse-angle gate time only at
its fully charged explicit block dimension.

4. GUARDED FRESH INSERTION NEED NOT EXTEND EVERY OLD LOGICAL DIRECTION

This exact example tests a possible positive Petz/dilation construction:
try to append a fresh maximally mixed/purified site while preserving every
old code direction and then locally normalize with a Gram bounded below
by1-O(alpha). That uniform extension premise is false even when EVERY
original nonempty normalized marginal guard is arbitrarily small.
This is not a hard state-choice instance.

Let d be a perfect square, A have dimension d^2, and B have dimension d.
In A choose orthonormal c_0,...,c_(d-1),psi, and put
C=span{c_l}. Impose the old unary bad projector P_C, of relative rank1/d.
For j in Z/dZ define rational orthonormal Bell rows

  u_j=(1/sqrt(d)) sum_(l=0)^(d-1) c_l tensor |l+j>,
  h_j=psi tensor |j>,
  v_j=u_j+delta h_j,

where0<delta<=1/sqrt(d) is rational. The v_j are mutually orthogonal,
each with squared norm1+delta^2. Thus P onto their span is an explicit
rational rank-d orthoprojector of relative rank1/d^2.

Its B marginal is exactly Tr_A P=I_B. Its normalized A marginal equals

  (1/(1+delta^2)) [ I_C/d + delta^2|psi><psi|
       +(delta/sqrt(d))(|c_0><psi|+|psi><c_0|) ].          (4.1)

The nontrivial c_0,psi block has eigenvalues0 and
(1/d+delta^2)/(1+delta^2); the other C eigenvalues are
1/[d(1+delta^2)]. Therefore

  ||Tr_B P/d|| <=2/d,
  ||Tr_A P/d^2|| =1/d^2.                                 (4.2)

Its relative rank and every nonempty normalized marginal guard tend to
zero. If equal site dimensions are required, append to B a d-dimensional
ancilla and lift P by its identity. Both sites then have dimension d^2,
all displayed guards and relative ranks are preserved, and there are
only two clauses with overlap degree1.

Nevertheless, under the old constraint, all u_j overlaps vanish and
the new v_j equations are exactly the h_j equations, independent of
the nonzero magnitude of delta. Hence the exact joint kernel is

  K_new=(C directsum span{psi})^perp tensor B,              (4.3)

with the ancilla included if used. The old logical direction psi has
NO satisfying fresh-site extension, pure or mixed. For the canonical
Bell-append map J0:x->x tensor |Omega_BB'>, the final-kernel extension
Gram J0^*(ProjK_new tensor I_B')J0 annihilates psi. It cannot obey a
uniform lower bound1-O(alpha) on the old code even though alpha->0.

This does not conflict with root's uniform-kernel occupation/reset bound:
on the fresh maximally mixed site the one-step bad probability for psi
is only delta^2/(1+delta^2), while the normalized exact residual row
forbids its entire fresh fiber. Nor is it a selected-state hardness
argument: choose any basis vector of A outside C plus psi to solve it
immediately. A full causal encoder may discard/reencode logical
directions, but must acquire that normalized deletion; preserving every
old logical direction cannot be its invariant.

This also applies to the adaptive input gauge interface. A cap threshold
alpha0>2/d sees P as already guarded and need not add an anchor at all.
Choosing a fresh-site representative with the same guarded rows does
not make the old-code extension Gram positive. The needed new selector
is span{psi}, obtained from the normalized JOIN with P_C, rather than
from the original P marginal alone.

Python Fraction arithmetic verified P=P^*=P^2, both displayed marginal
identities, and residual rows delta psi tensor |j>. The largest marginal

check of the stated example, not evidence for a general preparation claim.

5. MONOTONE TAG EQUATIONS AND A CONNECTED SELECTED-GRAPH RECURRENCE

Status: exact finite-dimensional construction under an acquired owned-
port injection premise. It supplies a genuine RANK carrier absent from
physical projector words. Global positive preparation is not acquired.

Work inside the full selector-good factor spaces V_i. Suppose every
local R_a has an acquired rational basis B_a and an owned product port
projector Q_a such that Q_a|R_a is injective. Put

 G_a=F_a^*=Q_a B_a(B_a^* Q_a B_a)^(-1)B_a^*.

Then G_a^2=G_a, kerG_a=R_a^perp, and
RanG_a=A_a=Q_a R_a subset RanQ_a. In particular G_a is the identity
on its image. Its norm can be arbitrarily large; no norm estimate is
used below. This convention is the ADJOINT of section24's row-source
map, not the physical projector P_a and not F_a itself.

At site i let C_i be the orthogonal sum of all incident owned ports,
and T_i=C_i^perp inside V_i. Write c_i=dimC_i,t_i=dimT_i. For a site
set S let H[S]=C_S tensor T_(outside S). These mutually orthogonal
strata decompose the full tensor space. The exact block rule is

       G_a[U,S]=0 unless U=S union X_a.                 (5.1)

Indeed G_a outputs an all-a-tag vector on X_a, and is the identity on
its exterior. Thus G_a NEVER returns an already tagged site to T_i.
Products, sums, and rationally existing inverses within this triangular
algebra retain tag-inclusion triangularity. This is not true of physical
P_a words: Bell-projector transmission can detag an old site and retain
constant normalized rank along an arbitrarily long fresh path.

Here is a precise selected-code recursion. Its source is T_all. Put
M_empty=I. For each nonempty U, use all clauses with X_a subset U and
form the STACKED diagonal map

 D_U:C_U -> Y_U,
 Y_U=directsum_(a:X_a subset U) A_a tensor C_(U minus X_a),
 D_U=(G_a[U,U])_a.

For each such a define the incoming block

 (b_U)_a=sum_(S proper subset U) G_a[U,S]
                         (M_S tensor I_(T_(U minus S))).

Every summand acts only on U, by(5.1). Define using the exact Moore-
Penrose inverse (with D_Up=0 when D_U=0)

 M_U=-D_Up b_U:T_U -> C_U,
 E_U=(I-D_U D_Up)b_U:T_U -> Y_U.                         (5.2)

All these maps are tensored with the logical identity on exterior sites.
For rational matrices the pseudoinverse is rational and acquired by
ordinary exact rank-factorization algebra at the FULL charged matrix
dimension on U. This gives no succinct large-U algorithm.

For any logical x killed by every lifted E_U, let

       Mx=sum_(U subset sites) (M_U tensor I_out)x.       (5.3)

The summands occupy distinct tag strata. Its tag-free block is x, so M
is injective on that logical consistency code. At output U, every clause
equation is exactly D_U M_U x+b_U x=E_U x=0. By kerG_a=R_a^perp,
Mx belongs to the ORIGINAL exact common kernel. The stacked equations
are essential: replacing them by sum_a G_a permits cancellations.

The exact rank bounds are

 rankM_U <=c_U=product_(i in U)c_i,
 rankE_U <=R_U:=sum_(a:X_a subset U) r_a c_(U minus X_a). (5.4)

No amplitude, inverse-angle, or number of repeated words appears. In
relative logical dimension the consistency row bound reads

 rankE_U/t_U <=sum_(a:X_a subset U)
             [r_a/t_(X_a)] product_(i in U minus X_a)(c_i/t_i).
                                                               (5.5)
For c_i/t_i<=beta and arity<=k this is at most
sum_(a subset U)(r_a/t_(X_a)) beta^(|U|-|X_a|).
This rank carrier explicitly keeps every visited site tagged; it must
not be inferred from the small HS norm of an ordinary projector word.

DISCONNECTED REDUNDANCY (modulo proper consistency equations).
Let U have components U_1,...,U_q in the hypergraph consisting of all
clause supports fully contained in U, with q>1. Isolated sites count as
components. Restrict logical input to the kernel of every E_V with
V proper subset U. Induction gives that the lower-stratum coefficients
factor across these components. The tensor product of their selected
graph solutions therefore satisfies every equation on U. Its U-tag

Moreover kerD_U=tensor_j kerD_(U_j), since D_U stacks local equations
on disjoint factors. Each M_(U_j) has range in RanD_(U_j)^*, which is

norm solution, equal to M_U on the restricted domain, and E_U vanishes
there. If a component has no contained clause, its M is zero and the
same conclusion holds. Thus ONLY CONNECTED U need contribute new
consistency equations; this is a quotient-by-prior-equations statement,
not an unconditional polynomial identity between all matrix entries.

6. A GLOBAL PRODUCT CHART CAN REQUIRE UNTAGGED FREE TRANSPORT

This exact two-clause example separates the EXISTENCE of the global
transverse product chart (already proved in r01 sections2A--2C) from
the local zero-free-coefficient recurrence of section5. It is an easy
state-choice instance, not a complexity obstruction.

Let A,B,C each have dimension d>=3. At A choose orthonormal owned tags
q1,q2 and a logical a; at B choose tag qB and logical b1,b2; at C choose
tag qC and logical c. Their remaining basis vectors are logical. Put
u=q1+q2, v=q1-q2, and use rank-one local rows

 r1=u_A tensor(qC+c)_C,
 r2=u_A tensor qB + v_A tensor b1 + a_A tensor b2.          (6.1)

Clause1 owns q1 at A and qC at C; clause2 owns q2 at A and qB at B.
Each own-tag compression is nonzero and hence injective. Their overlap
degree is1. Their squared norms are4 and5. P1 has normalized singleton
marginal norms1/d. P2 has normalized singleton marginal norms2/(5d):
Tr_B P2=(u u^*+v v^*+a a^*)/5 and
Tr_A P2=(2qB qB^*+2b1 b1^*+b2 b2^*)/5.
Relative ranks are1/d^2. All input guards can therefore be arbitrarily
small, without unary constraints or unacquired old selectors.

Let T_A=span(q1,q2)^perp, T_B=qB^perp,T_C=qC^perp. The EXACT local
graph on T_all

 N=I-(1/2)(|v><a|_A tensor |b1><b2|_B tensor I_C)          (6.2)

satisfies both original rows: r1 kills T_A and v, while r2's logical
source a tensor b2 is canceled by v tensor b1 of squared norm2.
The T_all block of N is I, so K->T_all is surjective. Indeed
N^*N=I+(1/2)|a b2><a b2| on A,B, an explicitly acquired constant-
condition local polar encoder for this example.

Nevertheless the recurrence of section5 gives M_A=M_B=M_C=M_AC=0 and

 M_AB=-(1/2)|u qB><a b2|.

At U=ABC, the nonzero row coordinates of the stacked D_U are

       D_U = [1 1; 1 1],
       b_U = [-1;0] <a b2 c|.

Thus E_ABC=[-1/2;1/2]<a b2 c| is nonzero even though the full chart
exists. The missing freedom is the earlier kerD_A coefficient in(6.2).
It occupies physical tag stratum {A} but acts NONTRIVIALLY on logical B.
An invariant charging every site of PHYSICAL SUPPORT to a tagged site
cannot hold for these free choices. Enlarging the support to AB pays a
new logical site without increasing the forced-tag carrier rank.

7. A GUARDED LOCAL PORT INJECTION LEMMA (BIPARTITE ONLY)

This is a local acquisition aid, not a global normalization theorem.
Let R subset H_A tensor H_B have dimension r and actual projector P.
Suppose both normalized marginal operator norms are at most alpha:
Tr_B(P)/d_B<=alpha I_A and Tr_A(P)/d_A<=alpha I_B.
If an integer u satisfies

  u>=2 sqrt(r),  u>2 alpha(d_A+d_B),  u<=min(d_A,d_B),

then a generic product compression to u-dimensional ports at A and B
is injective on R. In equal dimension the second inequality is u>4alpha d.

Proof uses exactly the maximal-rank generic-compression incidence lemma
of r01 section22. Were its generic kernel nonzero with maximal tensor
matrix rank m, that lemma gives m<2r/u<=u/2 and

  dim(R intersect W_X)>=u m/2,
  W_X=Col(X) tensor H_B + H_A tensor Row(X).

On the other hand P_WX is bounded by the sum of the two slab projectors,
so actual marginal caps give

 dim(R intersect W_X)<=Tr(P P_WX)<=alpha m(d_A+d_B),

a contradiction. The incidence premise is a local geometric theorem;
this argument must not replace it by a rank-only genericity assertion.
Rational random frames and a local rank certificate acquire the ports
with polynomial LOCAL matrix work and sampling bit length. No lower
bound on the nonzero port singular values is implied. The two caps must
hold for the SAME actual row projector; different adaptive one-site
representatives do not automatically supply this hypothesis.

8. ANGLE-FREE SELECTED-RANGE CLUSTER TRUNCATION

This section supplies a finite-cluster approximation INTERFACE. It does
not acquire the truncated uniform range state, and is not a completion
of the target. In particular no graph amplitude is treated as a physical
probability and no inverse Gram cost is omitted.

Use the fixed owned-port convention of section5. Let the primal site
graph join every two sites in any source support. Let its maximum degree
be d0, let h bound the number of clauses incident to a site, and let A>=2
be a fixed exponential bound on the number of connected u-site sets
containing a given site (for example A=max(2,d0)^2). Take

 c_i/d_i<=tau<1,  c_i/t_i<=beta<1,
 p'=p/(1-tau)^k.

For each primal-connected nonempty U retain the consistency E_U from
(5.2), and write q_U=rank(E_U)/t_U. Equation(5.5) yields

 q_U<=h |U| p' beta^max(0,|U|-k).                       (8.1)

Let z>0 be fixed with A beta exp(z)<1, and define

 C=h sum_(u>=1) u A^(u-1) exp(z u) beta^max(0,u-k).

This constant is finite. Require s:=C p'<=min(1/2,z/2), and put
x_U=q_U exp(z |U|). For each site i,

 sum_(U contains i)x_U<=s.

Since x_U<=1/2, the product over every overlapping constraint V obeys
product_V(1-x_V)>=exp(-2s |U|). Thus
x_U product_(V overlaps U)(1-x_V)>=q_U, so the asymmetric QLLL applies
to the LOGICAL consistency projectors. This uses their actual ranks;
the coefficient matrices may have arbitrarily large entries.

Let D be the logical kernel of all connected E_U, and let D_out(U) be
the logical kernel of only those E_V whose supports avoid U, as a space
on the exterior tensor factors. The conditional dimension lemma gives

 dimD >= t_U dimD_out(U) product_(V touches U)(1-x_V)
      >= t_U dimD_out(U) exp(-2s |U|).                   (8.2)

Disconnected redundancy in section5 shows D is also killed by every
disconnected E_U, so S=Ran(M|D) is an EXACT original-kernel subspace.
M is injective because its all-logical block is the identity.

CONNECTED COMPONENT FACTORIZATION. On D, the coefficient of any tagged
site pattern S factors over its connected components in the PRIMAL
graph. Indeed primal separation implies that every contained clause is
confined to a single component, and the disconnected-redundancy proof
applies inductively. Consequently M|D equals the hard-core polymer sum

 sum_(separated connected U_1,...,U_j)
       (tensor_l M_(U_l)) tensor I_(logical exterior),   (8.3)

where the empty family contributes I and separated means disjoint and
with no primal graph edge between distinct polymers. This is an exact
operator identity ON D; unconditional raw coefficient factorization is
not asserted.

For a connected U define the physical projector

 F_U=C_U tensor T_(primal boundary U) tensor I_rest.

It selects tagged patterns in which U is an ENTIRE connected component.
Equation(8.3) gives

 F_U M|D=(M_U tensor N_out(U))|D,

where N_out(U) is the exterior polymer map with the boundary forced
logical. Because D subset T_U tensor D_out(U), this implies the RANK
bound

 rank(F_U M|D)<=c_U dimD_out(U).

Divide by dimD and use(8.2):

 rank(F_U M|D)/dimD <= beta^|U| exp(2s |U|).             (8.4)

The same bound holds for Tr(F_U rho_S), where rho_S=P_S/dimS: the
compression P_S F_U P_S is a positive contraction of rank at most
rank(F_U M|D). This is an occupation bound for the uniform SELECTED
subspace, independent of all graph coefficient norms and angles.

Let F_>L be the coordinate projector onto tag patterns containing a
connected component of size>L. The F_U commute, and
ker(F_>L M|D)=intersection_(|U|>L)ker(F_U M|D). Thus

 rank(F_>L M|D)/dimD
 <= sum_(connected U,|U|>L) beta^|U| exp(2s |U|)
 <= (n/A) sum_(u>L)(A beta exp(2s))^u =:delta_tag(L).    (8.5)

No union event is assigned multiplicative normalized range rank. The
rank sum follows from the common kernel of commuting coordinate tests.

UNIFORM SUBSPACE COMPARISON LEMMA. Let M,N be injective maps on the same
d-dimensional input D. If rank((M-N)|D)<=r, then

  (1/2)||P_RanM/d-P_RanN/d||_1 <= r/d.                  (8.6)

Indeed ker(M-N)|D has dimension at least d-r and its common image lies
in RanM intersect RanN. Remove this common orthogonal intersection;
the remaining two orthoprojectors have ranks at most r, and their
trace-norm difference is at most 2r. This LINEAR bound needs neither
singular-value lower bounds nor a comparison of M^*M and N^*N.

Define the SHORT polymer map M_L by the same sum(8.3), but only allowing
connected polymers of size<=L. It is defined on the whole logical
product space and remains injective because its all-logical block is I.
On D,

   M_L|D=(I-F_>L)M|D.

Therefore uniform Ran(M_L|D) is within delta_tag(L) of the exact-kernel
uniform state rho_S by(8.5)--(8.6).

Now let D_L be the logical kernel of only E_U with connected |U|<=L.
Then D subset D_L, and the conditional QLLL gives

 1-dimD/dimD_L <= sum_(connected U,|U|>L)x_U
 <= n h p' sum_(u>L)u A^(u-1)exp(z u)beta^max(0,u-k)
 =:delta_cons(L).                                      (8.7)

Since M_L is injective, Ran(M_L|D) subset Ran(M_L|D_L), with exactly the
same dimension ratio. Uniform states on these two nested spaces have
trace distance EXACTLY 1-dimD/dimD_L, regardless M_L's conditioning.
The mathematical approximation INTERFACE is therefore

 rho_L=P_Ran(M_L|D_L)/dimD_L,
 dist(rho_L, exact original kernel)
      <=delta_tag(L)+delta_cons(L).                     (8.8)

Both tails decay geometrically for fixed sufficiently small p,beta.
L=O(log(n/epsilon)) makes(8.8)<=epsilon. This strengthens a hypothetical
amplitude/Gram comparison by replacing it with actual image-rank and
conditional logical-dimension comparisons.

WHAT IS STILL MISSING. A polynomial quantum circuit preparing rho_L.
There are polynomially many connected polymers of logarithmic size

9. POSITIVE NORMALIZATION MECHANISM TEST: COMMON SOURCE LABELS

This exact model is a DIAGNOSTIC for the full encoder, not a substitute
objective or a claim that arbitrary local projectors reduce to it.
It pinpoints an interface that would simultaneously remove global Gram
normalization and the variable-d dense-cluster cost.

Let G be any bounded-degree graph on sites. Suppose the polymer graph
contains only explicitly supplied rational singleton maps L_i:T_i->C_i,
and compatible families are
independent sets of G. Its injective encoder is

 M=sum_(S independent in G) (tensor_(i in S)L_i) tensor I_(outside S).

Assume rankL_i/t_i<=beta and beta is below the elementary site-percolation
threshold used below. Acquire a local singular-value decomposition

 L_i u_(i,j)=sigma_(i,j) v_(i,j),  1<=j<=rankL_i,

with sigma>0; the remaining u vectors span kerL_i. For a product logical
label j=(j_i), call i active iff j_i<=rankL_i. The vector M u_j is a sum
over independent subsets of its ACTIVE vertices. An active site has only
the two orthogonal choices u_(i,j_i) in T_i and v_(i,j_i) in C_i, with
relative amplitude sigma_(i,j_i). Inactive sites stay u_(i,j_i).

Different product labels j give ORTHOGONAL graph columns: any site with
different labels has orthogonal logical outputs, orthogonal tag outputs,
and logical outputs orthogonal to tag outputs. Therefore uniform RanM is
exactly the uniform mixture over j of the NORMALIZED vectors M u_j.
This is stronger than merely saying the graph's tag strata are orthogonal.

Sample each j_i uniformly from {1,...,t_i}. Active-site flags are now
independent Bernoulli variables of rates rankL_i/t_i<=beta. With A the
connected-set counting constant for G,

 Pr(an active component has size>L)
 <=(n/A) sum_(u>L)(A beta)^u.                            (9.1)

If A beta<1, choose L=O(log(n/epsilon)). Resample the logical labels only
when this event occurs. Conditioning a uniform mixture of ORTHOGONAL
columns loses trace distance exactly the discarded label fraction, so
(9.1) bounds the final error. This is a known sampled event with directly
computable connected components, not a quantum postselection oracle.

On a retained label, M u_j factors over the active graph's components.
Each component of s<=L active sites has at most 2^s BINARY configurations,
not d^s configurations. Enumerate its independent sets, form their scalar
product amplitudes, normalize classically, and synthesize the resulting
s-qubit state. Local controlled basis changes embed its two choices into
u_(i,j_i),v_(i,j_i). Thus the total work is polynomial in n,1/epsilon,
the explicit LOCAL matrix dimensions, and their bit length. Arbitrarily
large or tiny local singular values cause only charged logarithmic
precision: local rational minor bounds and stable log-weight arithmetic
avoid an inverse-singular-value gate count.

WHY THIS DOES NOT CLOSE THE FULL GOAL. For general short polymers M_U,
the overlapping effects M_U^*M_U need not commute and do not admit the
local common source labels used above. A sequence of measurements of
their range projectors does not create independent source flags: later
overlapping measurements can disturb earlier zero-source conditions.
Nor does rank dilution by itself bound the dimension of their joint
noncommutative source algebra. The needed generalization is an ACQUIRED
positive source-label resolution whose active components are rare under
the uniform selected logical measure and whose conditional state carries
only a polynomially describable transfer space. Such a resolution would
be a meaningful target for local rank-QR or auxiliary-syzygy encoders;
none is established here.
