1. EXACT PROJECTOR DECOMPOSITION

Let P_a be arbitrary projectors on supports X_a of size at most k, with
relative ranks at most p and clause overlap degree at most Delta. Let
the site interaction graph connect any two sites appearing in a clause.
Its maximum degree is at most D0=(k-1)(Delta+1). Put h=Delta+1.
Choose ANY orthogonal site tag projectors C_i of relative ranks tau_i
at most tau, and T_i=I-C_i. Zero tags are permitted. No oblique norm,
gap, or special tag selector is needed for this decomposition.

For a fixed clause a, force all sites of X_a to be tagged. For every
possible connected tagged component U containing X_a, let boundary U
be the neighbors of U outside U, and define on sites outside X_a

 D_(a,U) = tensor_(i in U\X_a) C_i tensor_(j in boundary U) T_j,

with identity elsewhere. Distinct D_(a,U) are orthogonal and their sum
is I: every computational tag pattern outside X_a has a unique connected
component after X_a is forced tagged. Supports X_a are cliques, so the
forced component is connected, including unary clauses.

The controlled projectors

                     P_(a,U)=P_a tensor D_(a,U)

are actual orthogonal projectors, not normalized joins. Their sum for
fixed a equals P_a lifted to the full space, and their exact joint kernel
over all (a,U) equals the original common kernel K. Their relative ranks
are exactly

 q_(a,U)=p_a product_(i in U\X_a) tau_i
                    product_(j in boundary U)(1-tau_j)
          <=p tau^(|U|-|X_a|).                         (1)

Their support S_(a,U)=U union boundary U has size at most b|U|,
b=D0+1. The decomposition also applies to all-tag oblique equations,
because the controls are outside X_a; it does not rely on oblique angles.

2. A UNIFORM ASYMMETRIC QLLL CERTIFICATE

All constants below depend only on fixed k,Delta. Set
 B=max(2,D0), A=B^2, b=D0+1, eta=1/(4b),
and take tau<=1/(4A). A canonical depth-first traversal bounds the number
of connected site sets U of size u containing any specified site by
A^(u-1): the traversal has length 2(u-1) and each step has at most B
choices. At most h u clauses have X_a contained in U. If a site v is in
S_(a,U), then U meets the closed neighborhood of v, of size at most b.
Consequently the number of such pairs (a,U) of size u is at most

                         b h u A^(u-1).                (2)

Define x_(a,U)=q_(a,U) exp(eta |S_(a,U)|). From (1),(2), for every v,

 sum_(a,U:v in S_(a,U)) x_(a,U) <= C p,                 (3)

where a finite explicit constant is

 C=b h [sum_(u=1)^k u A^(u-1)e^(u/4)
        +A^(k-1)e^(k/4) sum_(r>=1)(k+r)(A tau e^(1/4))^r].

The final geometric first-moment sum converges since A tau e^(1/4)<1/2.
If p<=eta/(2C), every x is at most1/2 and
sum_(neighbors of (a,U)) x <= eta |S_(a,U)|/2. Thus

 product_(neighbors)(1-x) >= exp[-2 sum_neighbors x]
                          >= exp[-eta |S_(a,U)|],

so q_(a,U)<=x_(a,U) product_neighbors(1-x). The elementary conditional
dimension induction for QLLL applies to this FINITE projector family.
Its size may be exponential; only the mathematical certificate is used.

3. ANGLE-FREE UNIFORM-KERNEL TAIL

Let K_ell be the joint kernel retaining only component blocks |U|<=ell.
Then K subset K_ell. The conditional dimension version of the same
QLLL certificate, adding all omitted projectors in any order, gives

 dim K / dim K_ell >= product_(|U|>ell)(1-x_(a,U))
                   >=1-sum_(|U|>ell)x_(a,U).            (4)

For nested subspaces, their uniform density matrices satisfy EXACTLY

 TV_quantum(rho_K,rho_Kell)=1-dimK/dimKell.             (5)

Here TV_quantum is half the trace norm. This follows by decomposing
K_ell=K directsum(K_ell intersection K^perp) and summing eigenvalues.
Equations (2),(3), summed over all n sites, bound the omitted activity,
for ell>=k, by

 n b h p A^(k-1)e^(k/4)
      sum_(r>ell-k)(k+r)(A tau e^(1/4))^r.             (6)

This is O_(k,Delta)(n p (ell+1) theta^(ell-k)),
theta=A tau e^(1/4)<1/2. Thus ell=O(log(n/epsilon)) suffices for
trace-distance epsilon between the UNIFORM exact kernel states. No
nonzero eigenvalue lower bound, approximate-energy-to-kernel conversion,
or normalized small-word rank assertion occurs in this proof.

4. PREPARATION OBLIGATION

The theorem does not prepare rho_Kell. Its controlled projectors remain
noncommuting; a product state in K_ell need not be close to K. Closeness
in (4),(5) is specific to the uniform kernel states. Even all-untagged
input patterns can have all original clauses active. A dense computation
on ell sites costs d^O(ell), quasipolynomial in variable explicit d, and
is not a polynomial physical encoder. The live target is an acquired
positive factorization of this controlled kernel or the equivalent
triangular source system, with polynomial cost in n and input local
matrix sizes. The rank tail supplies a comparison interface, not that
factorization. It is preserved for the global construction workers.
