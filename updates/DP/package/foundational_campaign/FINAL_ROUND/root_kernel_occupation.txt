LOCAL DOMINATION OF THE UNIFORM QUANTUM-LLL KERNEL
Root, 2026-10-11. Exact corollary of the standard conditional-dimension
QLLL lemma, not a historic discovery or a kernel-preparation algorithm.

Primary premise: Ambainis--Kempe--Sattath, A Quantum Lovasz Local Lemma,
arXiv:0911.1696v1, Theorem14 / Lemma15. It gives, under asymmetric LLL
parameters x_a<1,

 dim K_(T union {a}) >= (1-x_a) dim K_T              (1)

for every constraint subset T not containing a. Here K_T is the exact
common good kernel for T. Local disjoint supports provide the required
mutual relative-dimension independence. No spectral gap is used.

Let K=K_all, rho_K=P_K/dimK, and S be a site subset. Write
N(S)={a:X_a intersects S}, D_S=dim H_S, and K_out for the common kernel
of all constraints avoiding S, on the exterior Hilbert space.
Then K is contained in H_S tensor K_out. Iterating(1) gives

 dimK >= D_S dimK_out product_(a in N(S))(1-x_a).    (2)

Projector inclusion gives P_K<=I_S tensor P_Kout. Partial trace and (2)
therefore yield the OPERATOR inequality

 rho_(K,S) <= [product_(a in N(S))(1-x_a)^(-1)] I_S/D_S. (3)

This is stronger than only an expectation bound for a chosen projector,
but gives no lower spectral bound. If incidence per site<=nu and x_a<=x,
the bracket is <=(1-x)^(-nu |S|).

For pairwise disjoint site blocks S_j and positive effects 0<=E_j<=I,
put beta_j=Tr E_j/dimH_(S_j). Their joint commuting measurement on rho_K
satisfies

 Pr[all E_j outcomes] <= product_j beta_j *
        product_(a touches union_j S_j)(1-x_a)^(-1).    (4)

Thus unary filter-failure anchors of rank fraction<=beta have rare joint
outcomes bounded by [beta(1-x)^(-nu)]^|S| for a set S of sites.

LOCAL RESET CONSEQUENCE (exact, but not a full repair process).
Let a cluster C be reset to the UNIFORM exact kernel of its INTERNAL
constraints, independently of an arbitrary exterior state. Suppose a
boundary clause has support X, Y=X intersect C nonempty. If its actual
input effect has normalized partial-trace cap alpha over Y, then its
bad-outcome probability in the reset product state is at most

 alpha * product_(internal a touches Y)(1-x_a)^(-1).

Indeed apply(3) to the reset state's Y marginal, tensor the arbitrary
exterior X minus Y marginal, and use the normalized partial-trace cap.
This requires an acquired uniform cluster-kernel reset. It does not say
that projective checks of different boundary clauses preserve each other.
The noncommuting check/repair problem and input-variable local-dimension
cost are still missing. Repeated successful local tests do not certify
that the final state lies in every earlier tested kernel.

RELATED EXACT RANK LOSS (independently derived by R24).
For an extra local projector E_S with rank fraction beta, its restriction
to H_S tensor K_out has rank beta D_S dimK_out. Restriction to K cannot
increase rank. Hence

 [dimK-dim(K intersect ker E_S)]/dimK
   <=beta product_(a in N(S))(1-x_a)^(-1).             (5)

For many extra equations their codimensions add as an upper bound. If
K1 subset K is the exact nested common kernel after all of them, then
the trace distance of the UNIFORM kernel states is exactly

 D(P_K/dimK,P_K1/dimK1)=(dimK-dimK1)/dimK.

This rank metric avoids an energy-to-kernel gap assumption. It does not
follow from small Hilbert-Schmidt amplitude of an unnormalized word:
an arbitrarily tiny nonzero operator may still have large exact rank.
No arbitrary pure seed enjoys this uniform-state conclusion.
