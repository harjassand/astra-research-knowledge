EXACT FAILURE OF THE NATURAL CUBIC-DECOUPLING GENERAL-r STRESS

This is failure of a pointwise stress calibration ansatz. It is not a
compatible-gradient Jensen counterexample and not a counterexample to
quasiconvexity.

Write alpha=3r/[8(r+2)], lambda=4alpha=3r/[2(r+2)], and
mu=r-4alpha=r(2r+1)/[2(r+2)]. The direct continuation of the r=1
cubic-decoupling stress is

 S1_nat=-(r+2)s^2*b+lambda*b^3,
 S2_nat=(r+2)s^3+(3r+4)s^2*a+(3r+2)s*a^2+mu*a^3.

The cubic coefficients lambda and mu cancel b^3c and a^3d, respectively,
in the quartic s=0 residual. The lower coefficients cancel the raw
quadratic cross terms and the a^2d part of the cubic coefficient. In fact,
for G_nat=J_r(sI+A)-(r+2)(r+3)s^4/6
 -(r+2)(2r+3)s^3*a/3-S1_nat*c-S2_nat*d,

 [s^2]G_nat=(r+1)^2*a^2+r(r+2)c^2/3+d^2,

 [s]G_nat=(r(2r+1)/3)*a(a^2+c^2)
          -2(r+1)abc+2ad^2-2bcd+r*c^2*d.

At the fixed rational tuple (a,b,c,d,s)=(1,3,1,1,1), exact substitution
gives

 G_nat=(10r^3-2r^2+115r-24)/[3(r+2)].

Thus at the strictly positive parameter r=1/10,

 G_nat=-139/70<0.

At r=0 this same tuple gives -4. The parameter polynomial shows why a
continuation that simply preserves the r=1 decoupling choices cannot
work for every r>0. The final all-r stress changes all four coefficients
and is anchored instead at the exact r=0 determinant-square residual.

The witness is a single point in matrix-variable space. It has not been
promoted to a periodic compatible field. The only conclusion is exact
failure of this declared pointwise ansatz.
