DERIVATION OF THE SINGLE PARAMETRIC GRAM ANSATZ

The proof does not use separate certificates at sampled r values. It uses
one polynomial identity after multiplying the rational integrand by r+2.

1. The r=0 determinant anchor

The direct natural continuation is not the correct anchor: it leaves a
negative residual at r=0 and at small strictly positive r. At r=0 the
integrand is det(A)^2. The determinant-square support gives instead

 S1_0=-2s^2b, S2_0=2s^3+2s^2a,
 G_0=[ad-bc+s(a+d)]^2.

Thus the lower stress coefficients must be allowed to retain an ad
quadratic cross term and an a^2d cubic term in the residual. Removing both
terms, as the r=1 cubic-decoupling continuation does, destroys this anchor.

2. New Taylor coefficients

The all-r stress selected in the final certificate has

 [s^2]G=(r+1)^2a^2+(r/2+2)ad+r(r+2)c^2/3+d^2.

The determinant of its (a,d) matrix is exactly

 (r+1)^2-(r/4+1)^2=3r(5r+8)/16>0.

At r=0 this quadratic reduces to (a+d)^2, as required by the determinant
anchor. Its cubic coefficient is

 [s]G=r(2r+1)a(a^2+c^2)/3+(5r/4+2)a^2d
      -2(r+1)abc+2ad^2-2bcd+r*c^2*d.

These are coefficients of the pointwise residual, before integration.
They are not asserted to be individually nonnegative.

3. A polynomial in r with exact endpoint support

Put P(r)=(r+2)G. It is a cubic polynomial in r, with quartic homogeneous
coefficients in a,b,c,d,s. Its constant coefficient is 2h^2, where
h=ad-bc+s(a+d). Its leading coefficient is

 [r^3]P=(a^2+c^2+2as)^2/6 + s^2(a^2+c^2)/3.

The leading term has precisely the three-square support used in z1:
(a^2+c^2+2as), as, cs. The constant term has the single support h used
in z0. The other quadratic monomials provide cross terms at r and r^2.

This motivates the all-positive-axis SOS ansatz

 P(r)=z0^T Q0 z0+r*z1^T Q1 z1,

with the ordered z0,z1 specified in the proof and exact certificate.
The stress parameters enter the coefficient constraints linearly after
clearing r+2. A numerical feasibility calculation first allowed the four
stress coefficients to vary. It then fixed them to the simple rational
functions displayed in the theorem and solved the resulting affine Gram
system. That fixed system has rank 110 in 191 scalar Gram entries.

4. Rational replacement and exact proof

The construction found an interior numerical Gram with target floor .02.
Rounding free Gram entries to denominator 64 and solving the affine pivot
equations exactly produced the saved constant rational Gram matrices.
All four block LDL decompositions are strictly positive in rational
arithmetic; the identity is independently reconstructed from J_r.
Q1-I/512 is also strictly positive definite exactly.

The numerical feasibility floor is not an assumption or a verification
result. It helped select rational coefficients. The theorem follows from
the saved rational identity and rational positivity checks, and therefore
holds for the entire continuum r>0 without sampling, continuity arguments,
or transfer from the single r=1 parameter.

5. Reproduction

full_parametric_gram.py simple .02 generates optional numerical scratch.
rationalize_all_r.py reconstructs a rational candidate and checks its exact
Gram positivity. verify_all_r.py verifies the published fixed certificate
without reading any numerical scratch. The final certificate's identity is
also checked by a separate standard-library Fraction receiving verifier.
