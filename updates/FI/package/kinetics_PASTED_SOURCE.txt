The strongest acquisition result is a distinction that energy-based discovery misses:

1. A sharper information obstruction: even perfect response measurements can miss the slow component
Before choosing the mechanism, I tested whether additional global response measurements could resolve hidden kinetics without reconstructing a microscopic law.
Consider a reversible killed system with source weights \(p_j\) and decay rates \(\lambda_j>0\). Its regularized response is
\[
F(s)=\sum_j\frac{p_j}{\lambda_j+s},
\qquad s>0,
\]
while its mean lifetime is
\[
\tau=F(0)=\sum_j\frac{p_j}{\lambda_j}.
\]
Start with rates \(1,2,3,4\), each carrying mass \(1/4\). The mean is \(25/48\), and the responses at three positive damping parameters are
\[
F(1)=\frac{77}{240},\qquad
F(2)=\frac{19}{80},\qquad
F(4)=\frac{533}{3360}.
\]
I added mass \(10^{-3}\) at a hidden rate \(\varepsilon\), then adjusted the original four weights through an exact \(4\times4\) linear system. All weights remain positive in the executed examples, and all three measured responses remain exactly identical.
Hidden rate \(\varepsilon\)	Mean lifetime
No added component	\(0.5208333\)
\(10^{-4}\)	\(10.5170007\)
\(10^{-8}\)	\(100000.517\)
\(10^{-12}\)	\(1000000000.517\)


This is not a noise problem. The observations agree exactly.
The general construction works for any fixed finite collection of distinct positive damping parameters: the finite interpolation corrections remain bounded as \(\varepsilon\to0\), whereas the hidden contribution grows as \(10^{-3}/\varepsilon\).
Consequently, finitely many regularized responses do not, by themselves, bound the source-weighted contribution of arbitrarily slow modes. Additional information must exclude those completions. This does not rule out response-based discovery within a justified finite-dimensional physical class.
I also investigated conditional pinning as a route to a complement bound. Its unresolved costs were obtaining a useful pinning set and validating the necessary conditional estimates globally. No successful end-to-end experiment is attributed to that route.
The implemented alternative was to acquire enough of the microscopic kinetic law to construct a global comparison, without first determining its important eigenfunctions.
2. The acquisition operation: observe fast reverse relaxation under the original potential
Equilibrium energies determine rate ratios under detailed balance, but leave a kinetic prefactor undetermined. The new acquisition step addresses that prefactor directly.
Local reversible law
For an elementary binary coordinate in a fixed context \(y\), write
\[
r(y)=\frac{W(1,y)}{W(0,y)}.
\]
The specified microscopic law is
\[
q_{0\to1}(y)=a(y)\frac{r(y)}{1+r(y)},
\qquad
q_{1\to0}(y)=a(y)\frac{1}{1+r(y)}.
\]
Here \(a(y)\) is the unknown context-dependent attempt rate. It can encode kinetic interactions that do not appear in the equilibrium energy.
Prepare the locally less probable state, then observe its downhill relaxation under the original potential. Its rate is
\[
d(y)=a(y)\frac{\max\{1,r(y)\}}{1+r(y)}.
\]
Therefore,
\[
\boxed{
a(y)=d(y)\frac{1+r(y)}{\max\{1,r(y)\}},
\qquad
\frac{a(y)}2\le d(y)\le a(y).
}
\tag{1}
\]
The interrogation never requires waiting for a thermodynamically suppressed uphill transition.
This replaced an earlier balancing-bias proposal. Measuring under the original potential avoids assuming that an energy-equalizing bias preserves the kinetic prefactor.
It does not eliminate intrinsically slow mobility, context preparation, resetting, or clamping costs. Physical use requires the prepared system’s conditional clock to remain the intended clock. No laboratory implementation was performed.
Discover the kinetic support rather than supplying it
The tested class assumes that each prefactor is a positive Boolean polynomial of degree at most two:
\[
a_i(y)=c_{\varnothing}
+\sum_j c_jy_j
+\sum_{j<k}c_{jk}y_jy_k.
\]
The coordinates on which it depends are unknown and need not be energy neighbors.
Interrogate contexts with zero, one or two other coordinates excited. The coefficients follow from
\[
c_{\varnothing}=a_i(\varnothing),
\]
\[
c_j=a_i(\{j\})-a_i(\varnothing),
\]
\[
c_{jk}
=a_i(\{j,k\})-a_i(\{j\})-a_i(\{k\})+a_i(\varnothing).
\]
Under the declared degree bound, this determines the prefactor on every unmeasured context.
For \(n=18\), the complete scan uses
\[
18\left[1+17+\binom{17}{2}\right]
=\boxed{2772}
\]
local reverse-rate interrogations. The pairwise energy graph and coefficients require another
\[
1+18+\binom{18}{2}
=\boxed{172}
\]
energy queries.
One separate test recovered a kinetic dependency absent from the energy graph. Thus the acquisition does not merely attach clocks to an assumed thermodynamic representation.
The degree and pairwise assumptions remain premises. A cubic kinetic perturbation can evade every degree-two probe. The scan establishes the whole law within its class; it does not establish the class from finite observations.
The executed queries are exact evaluations of an authored microscopic generator—not estimates of expensive metastable-to-metastable rates.
3. Replace an invariant slow space with an acquired reference that retains all local modes
The previous successful certificate separated an exact homogeneous magnetization mode from its complement. Its report explicitly restricted that argument to the symmetric cycle and identified a general complement construction as missing.    RESEARCH_NOTE
The replacement does not select a presumed global slow eigenspace.
Partition the acquired interaction graph into local blocks. The prototype searches bond-strength thresholds, rejects blocks above six coordinates, and scores the remaining partitions by local calculation size and cross-coupling cost. This is a heuristic search, not an optimal partition theorem.
Let \(\rho_b\) be the computed local equilibrium law in block \(b\), and define
\[
\rho_0(x)=\prod_b\rho_b(x_b).
\]
The actual equilibrium law need not factorize. Write its unnormalized mass as
\[
m(x)=\rho_0(x)w(x),
\qquad
1\le w(x)\le R_m,
\qquad
Z=\langle w,1\rangle_{\rho_0}.
\]
The bound \(R_m\) is calculated from the recovered cross-block interactions.
For each local reference clock, minimize the recovered attempt law over external contexts while retaining its internal dependence. This gives
\[
1\le \frac{a_i(x)}{a_i^0(x_{\mathrm{block}})}\le R_k.
\]
The actual dynamics retains the complete context dependence; the minimum is used only in the reference.
Let
\[
C=\sum_b L_b^0+k
\]
be the reference killed generator. Every local mode is retained. The actual weighted operator is
\[
A=w(-Q+k),
\]
self-adjoint in \(L^2(\rho_0)\).
Coupled comparison theorem
For the stated microscopic law,
\[
\boxed{
C\preceq A\preceq RC,
\qquad R=R_mR_k.
}
\tag{2}
\]
This bounds the whole configuration space.
To see why, the actual-to-reference conductance ratio across an elementary edge is
\[
\frac{a}{a^0}
\frac{(m_x^0+m_y^0)w_xw_y}
{m_x^0w_x+m_y^0w_y}.
\]
The second factor is a weighted harmonic mean of \(w_x,w_y\), so it lies between \(1\) and \(R_m\). Reaction conductances satisfy the same mass comparison. Summing the edge and reaction quadratic forms proves (2).
The source and conductance bounds are not independently supplied worst cases. They come from one acquired physical law.
What this says about hidden slow response
The generalized spectrum satisfies
\[
\operatorname{spec}\!\left(C^{-1/2}AC^{-1/2}\right)\subseteq[1,R].
\]
Consequently, exact preconditioned Richardson iteration contracts by
\[
\frac{R-1}{R+1}
\]
in the reference energy norm. That factor does not contain the smallest local kinetic eigenvalue.
A local trap can therefore become much slower without necessarily making the relative reference problem worse.
This is an exact-iteration result, not a guarantee of cheap tensor inversion or bounded tensor rank. The actual implementation uses conventional truncated preconditioned linear algebra and must certify the response afterward. Local splitting and tensor absorption-time solvers are established prior art, particularly Masetti and Robol’s 2019 work. arXiv
4. The certificate includes every omitted source contribution
The mean-time equation is
\[
Au=w,
\]
not \(Au=1\), because the actual mass differs from the reference mass.
For any computed response \(v\), define
\[
r=w-Av,
\qquad
L=2\langle w,v\rangle-\langle v,Av\rangle.
\]
The exact residual identity gives
\[
\langle w,A^{-1}w\rangle
=
L+\langle r,A^{-1}r\rangle.
\]
If an acquired bound establishes \(C\succeq\alpha I>0\), then
\[
\boxed{
\frac{L}{Z}
\le\tau_{\mathrm{eq}}
\le
\frac{L+\|r\|_{\rho_0}^{2}/\alpha}{Z}.
}
\tag{3}
\]
There is no boundary-only approximation and no discarded source outside selected configurations.
The residual principle is not new; functional error-majorant methods are relevant prior art. The question addressed here is how to obtain a useful reference and its bound without the previous exact slow-space formula. arXiv
The lower operator bound is acquired locally
The reference target block contains the reaction. Solve its 64-state equation
\[
C_tu=1
\]
numerically, round the result to dyadic rationals, and verify
\[
u>0,\qquad
\|C_tu-1\|_\infty\le\delta<1.
\]
Inverse positivity gives a valid bound
\[
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{1-\delta}{\max u}.}
\]
The other reference blocks contribute nonnegative operators, so the same \(\alpha\) bounds the global reference.
No approximate global eigenvalue is trusted.
The global response is stored as a tensor train—a product of small arrays. Its coefficients are converted into an exact rational witness, and the full source, operator action, normalization and residual norm are contracted exactly. Numerical fitting, inverse approximation and rounding errors therefore appear in the certificate.

9. The precise failure beyond modular structure
I tested the reference construction’s admissibility on a separate dense heterogeneous interaction law.
With 18 coordinates, all 153 pair interactions present, and block size restricted to six, every partition leaves at least
\[
\binom{18}{2}-3\binom62=108
\]
cross-block edges.
When each interaction factor is at least two, the implemented product comparison constant is at least
\[
\boxed{R_m\ge2^{108}.}
\]
The explicit cross-term representation is also prohibitive. The compiler stops instead of expanding an exponential expression.
This is not a hardness theorem for all methods, nor proof that every possible physical inequality must be that loose. It is a concrete failure of the present operation on collective structure that cannot be placed inside small, weakly recoupled regions.
That failure identifies the next mathematical target more sharply than “learn better slow modes”:
Construct a source-aware comparison for strongly recoupled, heterogeneous regions without requiring either a small low-contrast cut or an analytically closed global slow space.

The reference method controls unknown slow response only when the acquired local law supports a useful decomposition. It does not discover a universally useful decomposition.

The substantive change from the previous checkpoint is:

What remains unresolved is consequential rather than cosmetic. The physical model class must be justified; precise context-conditioned clock acquisition can be expensive; and strongly nonmodular gates defeat the current reference construction.
