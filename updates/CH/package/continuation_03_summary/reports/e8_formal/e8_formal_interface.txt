Creation-port metric lemma proved
-------------------------------
The candidate defines F_up(v) = F_down(-v) and assigns the creation input
the +h_B/2 shift in the four-weight theorem (amplifier_proof_candidate.tex:
286-307). With p,q the two spectral probabilities and
v = (log p - log q)/h, swapping p and q gives -v. Applying the passive
tilted-logMean identity after that swap and then logMean symmetry yields

  logMean(p,q) * F_up(v) = logMean((r+1)*p,r*q).

This is the required reversed thermal metric on the creation port. The
generic Lean theorem isolates and proves precisely this transport from the
passive identity. Its hypotheses are explicit fields; it does not postulate
the creation conclusion. To connect it to the pinned source, instantiate
X=R, combine=(mul), logMean=DiagonalForms.System.logMean,
frequency=ThermalMetric.frequency r, downWeight=ThermalMetric.weight r,
upWeight(v)=downWeight(-v), lowerThermal(p)=r*p, and
upperThermal(p)=(r+1)*p. The passive_metric field is exactly
ThermalMetric.tilted_logMean; logMean_symm is the pinned source lemma.

   [d_j,d_k^*]=delta_jk, and cross commutators vanish for a specified
