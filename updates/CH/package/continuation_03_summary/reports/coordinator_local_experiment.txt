Coordinator derivation: a local classical/classical-quantum experiment
2026-10-10. Internal mathematical derivation; not a capacity theorem.

This note separates an exactly defined statistical experiment from its
conjectural transfer to a many-photon thermal attenuator. The physical local
limit requires convergence of states and entropies, not just moments.

1. Exact experiment

Fix kappa=nu(nu+1)>0 and r>0. A real parameter U produces at Bob
Y=r U+N_B, N_B~N(0,1). Eve receives a classical observation
Z=U+N_E, N_E~N(0,1), together with a pure coherent state
|sqrt(kappa) U>. The noises are independent for this definition. Thus the
squared overlap of Eve's pure states for parameters u,v is
exp[-kappa(u-v)^2]. A controlled displacement depending on z would not
change Eve's Holevo information. The relevant difference is
F(P)=I(U;Y)-I(U;Z)-E_Z S(rho_{quantum|Z}).
For a discrete prior, the last entropy is the entropy of the finite Gram
matrix sqrt(p(u|z)p(v|z))*exp[-kappa(u-v)^2/2]. This is an exact finite
matrix formula. Numerical integration in the accompanying script is only
a floating-point diagnostic, with no certified integration error.

2. Gaussian modulation is exactly solvable

For U~N(0,V), conditional variance Var(U|Z)=V/(1+V). Mixing real coherent
states changes only their q quadrature. In vacuum-variance-1/2 convention,
the conditional covariance is diag(1/2+2*kappa*V/(1+V),1/2). Hence

 n(V)=(sqrt(1+4*kappa*V/(1+V))-1)/2,
 F(V)=0.5 log[(1+r^2 V)/(1+V)]-g(n(V)),
 g(n)=(n+1)log(n+1)-n log n.

The limiting value is log(r)-g(nu). Gaussian modulation therefore recovers
the usual thermal entropy threshold if the proposed physical limit holds.
In fact no finite V improves its positivity threshold, by the next lemma.

Lemma. Put n(t)=(sqrt(1+4*kappa*t)-1)/2. The function
phi(t)=exp[2g(n(t))] is concave for t>=0.

Proof. For t>0, n'=kappa/(2n+1), h=g'(n)=log(1+1/n). The sign of phi''
equals that of 2h^2-2h/(2n+1)-1/[n(n+1)]. Set x=1/(2n+1) in (0,1),
so h=2 atanh x. The coefficient of x^(2k) in that expression is
8 S_k/k -4/(2k-1)-4, where S_k=sum_{j=0}^{k-1}1/(2j+1).
It is zero for k=1,2 and strictly negative for k>=3. Indeed
S_k<=k^2/(2k-1): equality holds for k=1,2, and the increment of the
right-hand side from k to k+1 exceeds 1/(2k+1) by
2k(k-1)/(4k^2-1). The power series converges absolutely for 0<x<1.
Thus phi''<0 for t>0; continuity at zero extends concavity to [0,infty).

Taking t=V/(1+V) in [0,1), concavity gives
exp[2g(n(t))]>=1+(exp[2g(nu)]-1)*t.
If r<=exp[g(nu)], then 1+(r^2-1)*t is no larger than this right side,
so F(V)<=0 for every V. Conversely if r>exp[g(nu)], its positive limit
implies F(V)>0 for some finite V. This is a theorem of this experiment,
not a universal theorem about arbitrary thermal-channel inputs.

3. Prefix randomization is a different quantity

Let U=W+X with independent Gaussian X of variance V and W of variance
epsilon. The private-information difference for the message W equals
F(V+epsilon)-F(V). This may be positive while F(V) itself is negative.
It is not the coherent information of a pure-codeword ensemble unless
the missing inner entropy and purification/shield costs are included.
As V tends to infinity,

 F(V)=log(r)-g(nu)+C/V+O(V^-2),
 C=-1/2+1/(2r^2)+kappa*log(1+1/nu)/(2nu+1).

A positive asymptotic derivative requires C<0, equivalently
r>[1-2*kappa*log(1+1/nu)/(2nu+1)]^(-1/2).
At nu=1 this r is 3.6320734973751048, giving eta=r/(1+r)
=0.7841139609363542. This is an asymptotic sufficient private-information
criterion in this experiment, not a global optimum or capacity threshold.
In particular it does not reach eta_AD=0.75.

4. What the physical transfer would require

For adjacent large Fock inputs, with N the carrier photon number, the
proposed local scaling has squared parameter increment
(1-eta)/[eta*(2nu+1)*N] and r=eta/(1-eta). The independently derived
binary Chernoff exponents are consistent with this experiment. They do
not by themselves prove a uniform local limit for growing alphabets,
Gaussian priors, superpositions, or entropies. No such transfer is claimed
here. Eve homodyne plus Z simulates sqrt(1+4*kappa)*U+N, recovering the
exact antidegradable r=2nu+1 comparison within this experiment.

Consequential remaining step: construct genuinely collective non-Gaussian
pure codewords which remove Eve's coherent distinguishing information
without paying a larger inner coherent-information penalty. Neither
classical Gaussian modulation nor its uncharged prefixing supplies this.
