E4 ADVERSARIAL REPORT: THERMAL ATTENUATOR POSITIVITY
Date: 2026-10-10
Scope: unassisted, memoryless, energy-unconstrained quantum capacity of
Phi_{eta,nu}, especially nu=1 and eta=3/4+delta. Initial analysis independent
of other continuation candidates. The frozen amplifier candidate was not read.

STATUS
The exact positivity threshold is NOT SOLVED. No stronger primary-source
certified thermal-attenuator lower threshold than eta=0.7841 was found in the
targeted search. This is a search result, not a claim of exhaustive coverage.
The report supplies four exact route obstructions/converses, an exact finite-
energy test of non-antidegradability, and operational counterexamples.
Analytical arguments below are independently derived/internal reconstructions;
neither novelty nor independent external validation is claimed. Floating-point
diagnostics are separated from the proofs.

MAIN FINDINGS
1. Any code whose receiver first applies quantum-limited amplification of gain
   1/eta is defeated exactly throughout eta<=0.8 when nu=1: the resulting
   additive-noise channel is antidegradable. This holds for arbitrary subsequent
   quantum codes, blocklength, and energy. More generally, at eta=.75+delta,
   receiver gain >=1/(1-4delta) already crosses the antidegradable boundary.
2. At any fixed finite blocklength and for any fixed finite-energy nonvacuum
   pure direction, an arbitrarily rare mixture about the vacuum has NEGATIVE
   coherent information. An exact non-asymptotic Holevo bound proves this.
3. Encoding a qubit in vacuum versus a state with minimum total photon number
   n has decoded entanglement fidelity at most (1+t^{n/2})/2, where
   t=eta/[1+(1-eta)nu]<1. Merely increasing the Fock spacing cannot approach
   noiseless transmission; the logical coherence disappears exponentially.
4. For arbitrary deterministic m-use encoders/decoders, total mean input
   photons E, nu=1, and theta=acos(sqrt eta),

     F_e <= 3/4 + |theta-pi/6| sqrt(3E+m).                       (A)

   Consequently, at fixed mean photons per use N, achieving F_e>=1-epsilon
   with epsilon<1/4 requires blocklength Omega(delta^{-2}) as delta->0+.
   This is an energy/blocklength converse, not a zero-capacity interval.

1. INTERFACE AND CURRENT PRIMARY FRONTIER

Use vacuum covariance I/2, [q,p]=i, and bits for all entropies:

  V -> eta V + (1-eta)(nu+1/2) I.

The complement contains the environmental output AND a purifier of its thermal
input. The weak complement obtained by retaining only the beam-splitter bath
output is insufficient for coherent information.

The capacity is the supremum over finite energy budgets of regularized coherent
information. A positive coherent-information witness at one FINITE blocklength
and finite energy proves Q>0. To prove eta_c(1)=3/4, one needs such witnesses
for every eta=3/4+delta, delta>0, or a valid uniform coding argument. Proving
non-antidegradability, NPT, two-way distillability, or success on a postselected
branch does not meet that requirement.

2. EXACT RECEIVER-AMPLIFICATION OBSTRUCTION

Let A_G be a quantum-limited phase-insensitive amplifier:

  V -> G V + (G-1)I/2,  G>=1.

Restoring attenuation at the receiver produces

  A_{1/eta} o Phi_{eta,nu}: V -> V+v_post I,
  v_post=(1-eta)(nu+1)/eta.                                  (B)

At v=1/2 the additive-noise channel has a symmetric broadcast dilation:
amplify with gain 2, then split equally with vacuum. Each output has unit
amplitude gain and quadrature noise 1/2. Tracing either receiver gives the
same channel. Adding independent displacement noise preserves this property.
Thus v>=1/2 is antidegradable. At nu=1, (B) is antidegradable whenever
eta<=4/5. No subsequent deterministic code can restore positive Q.

This is stronger than saying that a particular lattice rate is nonpositive.
It excludes ALL codes after this fixed receiver preprocessing, even ideal
infinite-energy codes interpreted through any valid finite-energy limit.
It does not exclude direct GKP recovery on the thermal channel itself.

There is a sharper gain budget. Write doubled noise

  y=(1-eta)(2nu+1),  Delta=eta-y=2(nu+1)eta-(2nu+1).

For the relevant x=G eta>=1/2, receiver amplification gives

  y_after-x_after = G(y-eta)+(G-1)=G(1-Delta)-1.

The channel is therefore antidegradable when

  G>=1/(1-Delta).                                           (C)

At nu=1, eta=3/4+delta, Delta=4delta. For eta=.751, gain merely
1.004016... is enough to make the processed channel antidegradable. An
additional receiver pure-loss transmission s similarly kills Q when
s<=1/(1+4delta), i.e. s<=.996015... at eta=.751.

Sender amplification differs. It gives

  Phi_{eta,nu} o A_{1/eta}: V -> V+v_pre I,
  v_pre=(1-eta)(nu+1).                                     (D)

Positive Q of (D) would imply positive Q of the original attenuator. At nu=1,
eta just above .75, its variance is just below .5. The standard displacement
lower bound becomes positive only for v<1/e, so this route replaces the gap
by the separately unresolved additive-noise positivity interval (1/e,.5).
It does not solve that interval.

If sender gain G1>=1 and receiver gain G2>=1 are chosen to make overall unit
gain eta G1 G2=1, the final displacement variance is G2(nu+1)(1-eta).
The best of these conversions puts all amplification at the sender. Adding
sender gain does not cancel the receiver antidegradability condition (C).

3. EXACT RARE-EXCITATION NO-GO, INCLUDING ENTANGLED BLOCK DIRECTIONS

This argument avoids a potentially nonuniform entropy derivative or a
truncation assumption. Let m be finite; nu>0; eta<1; and write the exact
loss-then-amplifier decomposition

  Phi_{eta,nu}=A_g o L_t,
  g=1+(1-eta)nu,  t=eta/g,  s=g-1>0.                       (E)

Take the pure vacuum state |v>=|0>^{otimes m} and any normalized pure
|phi> with finite total mean photons N>0. Consider

  rho_epsilon=(1-epsilon)|v><v|+epsilon|phi><phi|.

The two vectors need not be orthogonal. Let B0, B1 and E0, E1 be their
receiver and full complementary outputs. Since each branch is a pure input,

  I_c(rho_epsilon,Phi^{otimes m}) = chi_B-chi_E.             (F)

The vacuum receiver output is the faithful product thermal state
B0=tau_s^{otimes m}. Donald's relative-entropy identity gives

  chi_B = epsilon D(B1||B0)-D(B_epsilon||B0)
        <=epsilon D(B1||B0).                              (G)

All quantities are finite. In fact, with L=log2[(s+1)/s],

  D(B1||B0)=m g_entropy(s)+eta N L-S(B1)
           <=m g_entropy(s)+eta N L =: D_max.             (H)

Here g_entropy(s)=(s+1)log2(s+1)-s log2 s; the symbol is distinct from
the amplifier gain g in (E).

Use the Stinespring dilation in (E), where the environment contains the loss
mode and amplifier-idler mode for each use. Measure the environment event K
that at least one loss mode contains a photon. Its probabilities are exactly

  Pr(K=1|vacuum)=0,
  Pr(K=1|phi)=kappa:=1-<phi|t^{N_tot}|phi> >0.              (I)

The subsequent amplifier never acts on the loss modes. Monotonicity of the
Holevo information under this environmental measurement yields

  chi_E>=I(X:K)=h2(epsilon kappa)-epsilon h2(kappa).

Combining with (G) proves the non-asymptotic inequality

  I_c <= epsilon D(B1||B0)-h2(epsilon kappa)
                            +epsilon h2(kappa).           (J)

Since h2(epsilon kappa)>=epsilon kappa log2(1/epsilon), (J) is strictly
negative whenever

  0<epsilon<2^{-[D_max+h2(kappa)]/kappa}.                  (K)

For |phi>=|n>, kappa=1-t^n. For a direction orthogonal to the m-mode vacuum,
kappa>=1-t, so the result is uniform on fixed m and a bounded total-energy
set. Displacement covariance extends the same conclusion about every
coherent-state base point after displacing the direction appropriately.

The exact obstruction is environment support leakage: the rare branch creates
an epsilon log(1/epsilon) environmental label, while the faithful thermal
receiver output permits only an O(epsilon) Holevo advantage.

Limits of the conclusion: m, N, and |phi> are fixed before epsilon->0.
Joint limits N(epsilon)->infinity or m(epsilon)->infinity can defeat the
uniform estimate. This does NOT rule out the rank-two residue-class states
in [P1], mixtures around noncoherent base states, or positive capacity near
eta=3/4. It blocks a proposed argument relying solely on making a finite-
energy excitation arbitrarily rare.

4. EXACT VACUUM-VERSUS-HIGH-PHOTON COHERENCE OBSTRUCTION

Suppose |phi_n> is orthogonal to vacuum and supported on total photon number
at least n, across any finite number m of modes. In the loss dilation, every
nonzero loss Kraus operator annihilates vacuum, so

  L_t^{otimes m}(|v><phi_n|)
       = |v><t^{N_tot/2} phi_n|.

Taking its trace norm and using contraction under the amplifier in (E),

  ||Phi^{otimes m}(|v><phi_n|)||_1 <= t^{n/2}.              (L)

CPTP contraction for general trace-class operators follows by duality from
operator-norm contraction of the unital completely positive adjoint; it is
not limited here to Hermitian operators.

Encode |0_L>->|v>, |1_L>->|phi_n>. For any deterministic decoder D to a
qubit, the maximally entangled input has decoded Bell overlap

  F_e=(a+b+2 Re z)/4,
  a,b<=1,
  |z|=|<0|D(Phi(|v><phi_n|))|1>|<=t^{n/2}.

Therefore

  F_e <= (1+t^{n/2})/2.                                  (M)

Equivalently, the output trace distance of logical |+> and |-> is at most
t^{n/2}, so their optimal discrimination succeeds with probability no more
than (1+t^{n/2})/2. This rules out reliable qubit transmission by merely
sending the minimum excitation spacing to infinity. It is valid even at
nu=0, although the rare-mixture lemma requires nu>0.

This excludes the specific vacuum/high-photon encoding. It does not exclude
all bosonic high-energy encodings, binomial/GKP codes, or an outer code that
uses a fixed inner code with a positive coherent-information rate.

5. ORIGINAL FINITE-RESOURCE CONVERSE NEAR ANTIDEGRADABILITY

Let m uses act on an arbitrary encoded qubit input, possibly entangled across
uses. Let E be its total mean input photons for a maximally mixed logical
qubit. The encoder and decoder are arbitrary deterministic CPTP maps and
are held fixed while comparing eta with eta0=3/4.

Use beam-splitter angle theta=acos(sqrt eta). The full dilation uses a fresh
thermal environment in every mode. On the input and bath modes define the
self-adjoint generator

  H=sum_j i(a_j^dagger e_j-a_j e_j^dagger).

The bath has <e_j>=<e_j^2>=0 and <e_j^dagger e_j>=nu; distinct bath modes
are independent. All cross terms between different j vanish, even for
entangled input states. Thus exactly

  <H>=0,
  <H^2>=(2nu+1)E+m nu.                                  (N)

Purify both encoded input and baths. For the corresponding dilation vectors,
the spectral inequality |exp(-ix)-1|<=|x| gives

  ||(U_theta-U_theta0)|Psi>||
           <=|theta-theta0| sqrt[(2nu+1)E+m nu].           (O)

Pure-state trace distance is at most this vector norm. Partial trace and the
decoder contract trace distance, so (O) also bounds the difference of decoded
logical Choi states. No dimension cutoff or upper photon-number constraint
was used; a finite mean photon number suffices.

At eta0=(nu+1/2)/(nu+1), antidegradability gives a symmetric extension of the
decoded Choi state to two receivers. For a d-dimensional reference, write
P1=|Phi_d><Phi_d|_{RB1} tensor I_{B2} and similarly P2. Their sum has norm
1+1/d. A symmetric extension has Tr(P1 omega)=Tr(P2 omega)=F_e, hence

  F_e(eta0)<= (d+1)/(2d).                                (P)

The property survives arbitrary deterministic encoders and decoders. For a
qubit, d=2, nu=1 and theta0=pi/6, (O)-(P) prove (A).

For target F_e>=1-epsilon, epsilon<1/4, necessary conditions are

  3E+m >= (1/4-epsilon)^2/|acos(sqrt eta)-pi/6|^2,

and, if E<=mN,

  m >= (1/4-epsilon)^2 /
       { |acos(sqrt eta)-pi/6|^2 (3N+1) }.                 (Q)

At eta=3/4+delta,
theta-pi/6=-(2/sqrt3)delta+O(delta^2), giving the explicit asymptotic

  m >= [3(1/4-epsilon)^2/(4(3N+1))] delta^{-2}(1+O(delta)).

For N=10 and target F_e=.99, floating-point evaluation of the exact formula
requires at least 1392 modes at eta=.751, 139337 at eta=.7501, and 13935299
at eta=.75001. These are necessary lower bounds, not sufficient designs.

This supplies a concrete adversarial test for alleged fixed-resource codes
that remain reliably transmitting arbitrarily close to the boundary. It does
not exclude positivity with growing resources. In particular, an arbitrarily
small positive coherent-information witness need not itself give an
F_e=.99 decoder at its original blocklength.

6. EXACT NON-ANTIDEGRADABILITY TEST AT ANY POSITIVE FINITE ENERGY

For this section use doubled covariance V_vac=I. Send one half of a two-mode
squeezed vacuum of mean photon number N>0 through the thermal channel. Its
reference/receiver covariance has blocks

  A=aI, B=(eta a+y)I, C=cZ,
  a=2N+1, y=(1-eta)(2nu+1), c^2=eta(a^2-1), Z=diag(1,-1).

By the n_B=1, k=2 Gaussian extension criterion [P2], the resulting state is
2-extendible exactly when V_RB >= i Omega_R direct-sum 0_B. Schur
complementation gives

  B-C^T(A-i Omega)^{-1}C = yI+eta i Omega,

whose minimum eigenvalue is y-eta. Thus at EVERY N>0 the output is
non-2-extendible exactly when

  eta>(nu+1/2)/(nu+1).

No infinite-energy Choi limit is needed to detect this property. This makes
the limitation of that property especially clear: at nu=1 and eta=.751,
finite-energy Gaussian Choi states already fail 2-extendibility, although
all one-mode Gaussian-input coherent information is nonpositive by [P1].
Nonextendibility is a faithful antidegradability test here, not a capacity
certificate.

7. OPERATIONAL COUNTEREXAMPLES AND CANDIDATE REVIEW TESTS

7a. Full complement versus weak complement.
For a thermal input of mean N, the receiver bath-only entropy difference is

  g_entropy(eta N+(1-eta)nu)
    -g_entropy((1-eta)N+eta nu).

As N->infinity it tends to log2[eta/(1-eta)], spuriously positive for every
eta>1/2. The full coherent information instead tends to
log2[eta/(1-eta)]-g_entropy(nu). At eta=.7, nu=1, N=100, our Gaussian
calculation gives weak difference +1.1822530961 and true coherent information
-0.7722159879. The channel is antidegradable. Missing the purifier therefore
produces a false positive inside an exactly zero-capacity region.

7b. Receiver flags are allowed; conditioning alone is not a rate.
For a receiver instrument with classical flag F,

  I_c(R>BF)=sum_f p_f I_c(R>B)_{sigma^f}.

For success S and erasure failure with pure receiver state |perp>,

  I_c = p_S I_c(success)-(1-p_S) S(R|failure).             (R)

When the code succeeds perfectly with input-independent probability p, and
otherwise erases a d-dimensional input, (R) equals (2p-1)log2 d for a
maximally mixed input. At p=.49 every accepted output is perfect but the
unassisted channel is antidegradable and has Q=0. Renormalizing a successful
projected density matrix and multiplying its apparent rate by p gives the
wrong answer. Bob reporting successes for recurrence/retry is backward
classical assistance. The cutoff-erasure construction in [P1] is valid
because it keeps the failure branch in a physical CPTP channel.

7c. Two-way versus unassisted.
At nu=1, eta=.6, [P6] implies Q_2>0 at every positive finite input-energy
budget, while antidegradability implies Q=0. A recurrence/distillation
protocol requiring Bob-to-Alice messages does not establish the requested Q.

7d. Finite block versus infinite energy and vanishing raw rate.
A finite-energy, finite-block positive I_c is enough. A family encoding one
qubit into m modes, with rate 1/m->0, may still prove Q>0 if some FINITE m
yields a decoded logical channel with positive I_c; concatenate that fixed
inner block with an outer quantum code. Rejecting it solely for 1/m->0 would
be wrong. Conversely an ideal, nonnormalizable GKP state is not a witness:
one needs a finite-energy approximation and an error bound that survives.
Resources may grow with delta, but must be finite for each fixed delta.

7e. Useful capacity upper bound and its boundary scaling.
In the non-entanglement-breaking region eta>nu/(nu+1), the exact amplifier-
then-loss decomposition has final pure-loss transmission

  t'=(nu+1)eta-nu.

Data processing and pure-loss capacity give

  Q(Phi)<= [log2(t'/(1-t'))]_+.

At nu=1, eta=.75+delta this is
log2[(1+4delta)/(1-4delta)]=8delta/ln2+O(delta^3).
A claimed near-boundary rate bounded away from zero contradicts this bound.
Primary decomposition: Rosati, Mari, Giovannetti, arXiv:1801.04731;
Nature Communications 9, 4339 (2018), Lemma 1 / Eqs.(36)-(37).
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-06848-0

7f. An exact sufficient converse target beyond antidegradability.
One possible zero-capacity certificate would be complete relative-entropy
domination by the complement: for every finite reference R and all valid
finite-energy pairs rho_RA,sigma_RA with compatible supports,

  D((id_R tensor Phi^c)(rho)||(id_R tensor Phi^c)(sigma))
   >= D((id_R tensor Phi)(rho)||(id_R tensor Phi)(sigma)).  (S)

In finite dimensions (S) tensorizes by successive channel replacement while
treating all other outputs as references. It implies domination of the
environment Holevo information for every pure ensemble at every blocklength.
By identity (F) all coherent information is then <=0. For bosonic channels
one must also justify passage from finite-energy/truncated references to
the full statement; finite cutoff SDP success alone is not (S).

The qutrit source [P7] reports such a mechanism. No signed lift or complete
relative-entropy domination for the non-antidegradable attenuator has been
constructed here. A single violated instance of (S) would kill that
particular sufficient converse; it would not automatically establish Q>0.
Gaussian test families or absence of violations in a finite scan cannot
prove (S) for arbitrary non-Gaussian, reference-correlated inputs.

8. ASSESSMENT AND CONTINUATION

"Non-antidegradable implies positive Q" for this exact attenuator family is
an OPEN proposition, not a known consequence of the channel classification.
The possibilities eta_c=3/4 and eta_c>3/4 both survive the present evidence.
The rate should vanish at the boundary, and explicit high-fidelity codes
require growing resources by (Q). The exact barriers (C), (J), and (M) make
several naive continuity, flash, and large-spacing proofs fail; they do not
constitute evidence sufficient to assert a strict zero-capacity gap.

Most discriminating next steps:
  - For any proposed near-boundary code, first identify its actual physical
    preprocessing and test (C); then compute the full flagged CPTP logical
    channel rather than a conditional success state.
  - Check any vacuum/rare-excitation limit against (J) before optimizing.
  - Check any vacuum/high-Fock asymptotic encoding against (M).
  - Record m,E and fidelity targets and compare with (Q).
  - A sender-amplified GKP proof needs a genuine new result about additive
    variance arbitrarily close to 1/2, or must avoid this reduction.
  - A zero-capacity proof above .75 needs a new all-blocklength mechanism,
    such as a valid infinite-dimensional version of (S), not merely vanishing
    one-use scans or nonpositive Gaussian rates.
