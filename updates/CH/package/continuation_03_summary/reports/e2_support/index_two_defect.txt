E2 EXPLICIT COLLECTIVE PURE-CODEWORD FAMILY: INDEX-TWO LATTICE DEFECT
Status: exact ideal-code identity and a rigorous sufficient finite-block gate.
No correlated seed attaining the near-antidegradability target is established.

1. ARITHMETIC REALIZABILITY.
Use [q_j,p_l]=i delta_jl and displacement commutator phase
exp(i lambda^T Omega mu). Let Lambda_0 be a full-rank symplectically selfdual
k-mode lattice: Lambda_0=Lambda_0^perp, vol(Lambda_0)=(2pi)^k.
Choose consistent Weyl phases on its commuting generators. Their common
ideal eigenspace is a one-dimensional pure qunaught state |Q>.

Let chi:Lambda_0 -> Z_2 be a nontrivial group homomorphism. Selfduality
provides a vector t, unique modulo Lambda_0, such that
exp(i t^T Omega lambda)=(-1)^chi(lambda). Then t is not in Lambda_0,
2t is in Lambda_0, and chi(2t)=0.

For a positive integer n define
Lambda_n={ (lambda_1,...,lambda_n) in Lambda_0^n:
                         sum_j chi(lambda_j)=0 mod 2 }.
This is an index-two sublattice of Lambda_0^n and a valid commuting lattice.
Its volume is 2(2pi)^(kn), so it encodes one qubit, and
Lambda_n^perp=Lambda_0^n union [(t,...,t)+Lambda_0^n].
The quotient Lambda_n^perp/Lambda_n has four logical Pauli cosets.
The pure computational basis is
|0_L>=|Q>^tensor n, |1_L>=[D(t)|Q>]^tensor n.
These are orthogonal ideal states because an odd-chi base stabilizer has
opposite eigenvalues on them. Thus the input contains no mixed thermal seed.

This construction uses an actual index-two defect. The tempting homothety
Lambda=s Lambda_0 with s=2^(1/(2kn)) generally destroys integral symplectic
pairings and cannot substitute for it.

2. EXACT INNER COHERENT INFORMATION.
Let f_v be the N(0,v I_(2k)) density and C_0 a fundamental cell of Lambda_0.
Define on that torus
g(s)=sum_{lambda in Lambda_0} f_v(s+lambda),
f_chi(s)=sum_{lambda in Lambda_0} (-1)^chi(lambda) f_v(s+lambda),
r(s)=f_chi(s)/g(s),   g_t(s)=g(s+t).
The function f_chi changes sign under odd base translations, but r(s)^2 is
well defined on the torus. The shifted density is an involution since
2t belongs to Lambda_0.

Let T be uniform binary and, conditional on T, let Y_1,...,Y_n be iid
samples with density g or g_t. Put
H_shift(n)=H(T|Y_1,...,Y_n),
H_parity(n)=E_{S_j iid g} h_2[(1+product_j r(S_j))/2].
The ideal analog stabilizer decoder for Lambda_n has exactly

I_c(n)=1-H_parity(n)-H_shift(n).                         (D1)

To see this, decompose the logical error into T, the coset of Lambda_0^n
inside Lambda_n^perp, and U, the lift parity in Lambda_0^n/Lambda_n.
The chain rule gives H(U,T|S)=H(T|S)+H(U|T,S). Unfolding the half-volume
syndrome cell to C_0^n identifies the first term with H_shift and the second
with H_parity. This does not assume that U and T are independent.
All analog syndromes remain at the receiver, with no message to the sender.

3. AN EXPLICIT SUFFICIENT FINITE-BLOCK GATE.
Let
rho(v)=integral_C0 f_chi(s)^2/g(s) ds,
beta(v)=integral_C0 sqrt[g(s) g_t(s)] ds.
Both lie in [0,1]. The binary entropy series gives

1-H_parity(n)=(1/ln2) sum_{j>=1}
   [E_g r(S)^(2j)]^n/[2j(2j-1)] >= rho(v)^n/(2ln2).

The pointwise inequality h_2(p)<=2sqrt[p(1-p)] gives
H_shift(n)<=beta(v)^n. Consequently

I_c(n)>=rho(v)^n/(2ln2)-beta(v)^n.                       (D2)

If rho(v)>beta(v), choose any integer
n>ln(2ln2)/ln[rho(v)/beta(v)]. Then (D2) is a strictly positive fixed-block
ideal margin, divided by kn physical additive-channel uses.
This gate involves computable lattice-Gaussian image sums, not an appeal to
the non-antidegradability of the original channel.

For fixed v>0 the base-torus densities are smooth, strictly positive, and
the likelihood ratio is bounded. When g differs from g_t, symmetry under
the halfshift makes the Chernoff minimizer exactly 1/2. Standard local-CLT
analysis of the tilted density sqrt(g g_t)/beta gives
H_shift(n) asymptotic to
 [sqrt(2pi)/(ln2 sigma)] beta^n/sqrt(n),
where sigma^2 is the variance of log[g/g_t] under that tilted density.
The leading term of 1-H_parity is rho^n/(2ln2). Thus rho<beta gives eventual
negative I_c; rho=beta generically gives eventual positive I_c through the
prefactor. Only the strict sufficient gate (D2) is needed for a certificate;
the equality statement is asymptotic and is not used as a finite witness.

4. EXPLICIT SEEDS TESTED: SPECIFIC FAILURES.
For the rectangular selfdual one-mode seed
Lambda_0=sqrt(2pi) diag(a,1/a) Z^2,
choose chi to be the parity of the q-coordinate lift. Then rho is a
one-dimensional q-period parity integral and beta is the one-dimensional
p-period halfshift Bhattacharyya integral. The exact commutation condition
holds for every a>0 because the q and p periods have product 2pi.

Floating positive-image quadrature diagnostics give:
a=1: rho=beta at v approximately 0.36098339505;
a=1.4: threshold approximately 0.36483579257;
a=1.68: threshold approximately 0.36549298622;
a=2: threshold approximately 0.36502363487.
A finite grid of aspects 1,...,2.5 in steps .01 has its largest threshold
at a=1.68. This is not a certified global optimization. It remains below
the Gaussian hashing threshold 1/e=0.36787944117.
At v=.49 and a=1, rho=.78037115932, beta=.95237256736: the pure repeated
qunaught family fails well before the target variance .5.

The broad valid CSS selfdual seed family is
Lambda_0=sqrt(2pi)(A Z^k)_q directsum
         sqrt(2pi)(A^(-T) Z^k)_p,
for any invertible real k-by-k matrix A. A parity c in Z_2^k on q lifts has
t_p=(sqrt(2pi)/2) A^(-T)c. This preserves all integral symplectic pairings.
Testing the area-one two-dimensional hexagonal A, with c=(1,0) or (1,1),
gives rho=beta at v approximately .36153710783. It also fails near .5.
The tested hexagonal lattices are k=2 seeds, so the repeated code uses 2n
physical modes for one logical qubit.

5. THE REMAINING CONSTRUCTIVE GATE.
At nu=1, sender preamplification gives v=2(1-eta). A proof that for every
v<.5 some finite selfdual base lattice Lambda_0 and nontrivial chi satisfy
rho(v)>beta(v), with controlled image sums and a physical GKP limit, would
prove the target through (D2). No such family is established here.
This reduces the proposed pure-codeword mechanism to a concrete conditional
criterion. It neither proves that correlated seeds can satisfy it nor
rules out positivity through other lattice codes if it fails.

The actual ideal codewords have infinite energy. Finite-energy envelopes,
Gram orthonormalization, a physical syndrome instrument, cutoff errors,
finite syndrome precision, and outer coding must be charged before calling
an ideal positive margin a physical thermal-channel witness. The additive
channel simulation itself is deterministic and costs one sender amplifier
and one thermal-channel use per physical mode.
