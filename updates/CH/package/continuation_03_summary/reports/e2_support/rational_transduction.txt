E2 DIRECT THERMAL RATIONAL TRANSDUCTION: MEASURED CANONICAL IMPLEMENTATION
Status: internally reconstructed restricted inner-coherent-information result.
This is NOT an n-use converse for the thermal channel or all rational lattices.

1. THE DUAL ACTION IS THE RELEVANT COMPATIBILITY TEST.
Let a=nu+1/2, eta=p/q in lowest terms, l=q-p>0, and h=sqrt(2pi).
For physical displacement operator D(z),
Phi^dagger(D(z))=exp[-a(1-eta)|z|^2/2] D(sqrt(eta) z).

The fact that sqrt(eta) times an input lattice has integral symplectic
pairings does not show that measuring its stabilizers preserves logical
coherences. To make an output stabilizer pull back to an input stabilizer,
one instead needs sqrt(eta) Lambda_out subset Lambda_in.

Consider the valid square lattices
Lambda_in=h sqrt(k p) Z^2, Lambda_out=h sqrt(k q) Z^2,
of logical dimensions kp and kq respectively. Then
sqrt(eta) Lambda_out=Lambda_in, and the law of the joint output stabilizer
syndrome is independent of every input logical state. Input independence
alone does not imply that dephasing the output syndrome preserves the input
state: coherence between different syndrome sectors can still contain it.

2. EXACT SELECTION OF SURVIVING LOGICAL OPERATORS.
An output normalizer label c in Z_(kq)^2 corresponds to
z_c=h c/sqrt(kq). Its pullback belongs to the input normalizer precisely when
p c/q is an integer vector. Since gcd(p,q)=1, this means q divides c.
Otherwise its compression to the input code is zero.

Assume gcd(k,pq)=1. Chinese-remainder decomposition factors the input code
into k and p dimensional systems and the output code into k and q systems.
Encode the k system with a fixed pure p-dimensional gauge. After measuring
the full output stabilizer syndrome, all output Weyl coefficients vanish
except those with labels q b, b in Z_k^2. Therefore the q-dimensional output
gauge is maximally mixed and uncorrelated with the surviving logical k system.

Let U_b and V_b denote the surviving input and output k-system Weyls. Their
projective symplectic multipliers are p/k and q/k respectively. Conditional
on syndrome s, the channel multiplier lambda_b(s) is a theta series. Up to
consistent unit phases fixed by the Weyl generators, its numerator is

sum_(m in Z^2) exp[-pi a l |b+k m|^2/k] exp[i phase_(s,b,m)],

and its denominator is the b=0 series. In particular, the denominator is
bounded below by 1-[theta(a l k)^2-1], with
theta(t)=sum_(j in Z) exp(-pi t j^2).

3. CONDITIONAL CHOI ALGEBRA AND MAXIMALLY MIXED INNER INFORMATION.
The normalized conditional Choi state is a linear combination of
C_b=U_b^T tensor V_b^dagger. These combined Weyls have symplectic multiplier
(q-p)/k=l/k. Put g=gcd(k,l), d=k/g.
The finite Weyl algebra has g^2 central characters. Each sector has an
irreducible d-dimensional representation with multiplicity d. Consequently
the conditional Choi state has the block form

J_s=directsum_z p_z(s) tau_z(s) tensor I_d/d,

with g^2 possible z and density operators tau_z. The conditioned logical
channel is unital, so its output for a maximally mixed k-dimensional input
has entropy log2 k. Hence exactly

I_c(s)=log2 g-H(Z|s)-sum_z p_z(s) S(tau_z(s)).            (T1)

If gcd(k,l)=1, then g=1 and I_c(s)=-S(tau_s)<=0 for every s, independent
of the detailed Gaussian coefficients. For k=1 no logical qubit is encoded.

4. THE REMAINING CENTRAL BRANCH AT nu=1 ALSO HAS NONPOSITIVE INNER RATE.
For g>=2 the central labels are b=d c with c in Z_g^2. Their Gaussian
exponents are pi a l' d |c+g m|^2, where l'=l/g>=1. Their nontrivial Fourier
coefficients therefore have total absolute value bounded by

epsilon <= [theta(a l' d)^2-theta(a l k)^2] /
             [1-(theta(a l k)^2-1)].

At nu=1, a=3/2, and g>=2 implies a l k>=6. Thus
epsilon <= [theta(3/2)^2-1]/[2-theta(6)^2] < .037.
The last strict numerical bound follows, for example, from
theta(t)<=1+2 exp(-pi t)/[1-exp(-3pi t)].
Any phases in the central Fourier transform do not affect this absolute
bound. Every sector probability satisfies p_z(s)<1.037/g^2, so
H(Z|s)>=2log2 g-log2(1.037). Equation (T1) gives

I_c(s)<=-log2 g+log2(1.037)<0,  g>=2.                    (T2)

Thus this canonical one-mode, measured-syndrome, pure-gauge, maximally mixed
logical-input transducer does not supply a positive inner coherent-information
seed for any allowed k at nu=1. This includes both coprime and shared-factor
values of k and q-p. It does not prove zero capacity of the induced finite
channel for arbitrary outer inputs, nor a converse for Phi.

5. PRECISE LIMITS AND NEXT GATE.
The calculation covers square input/output lattices of dimensions kp and kq,
full output stabilizer dephasing, CRT k-system extraction, and a maximally
mixed k-system input. It does not cover coherent syndrome processing,
incommensurate lattice instruments, correlated multimode arithmetic lattices,
or a different encoded subspace inside the kp-dimensional input code.

The main lesson is not that rational transduction is impossible. It is that
output commutation and input-independent syndrome statistics do not establish
a quantum decoder. Any stronger construction must provide the full channel
on a reference, retain the relevant syndrome coherences, and calculate its
actual inner coherent information. Merely replacing the quantum attenuator
by a classical displacement of a contracted comb is invalid.

6. SCOPE OF LOCAL VERIFICATION.
check_rational_algebra.py constructs the finite combined Weyl matrices for
(p,q,k)=(3,4,5),(7,9,2),(7,9,4),(11,13,6),(5,9,7). Random positive normalized
elements of the Choi algebra have the predicted spectral multiplicity d;
maximum observed spread was 1.64e-16 and the output/reference marginals were
maximally mixed to floating error. This checks the representation calculation.
It does not validate a physical Gaussian theta series, a decoder limit,
all possible channel inputs, or an all-blocklength capacity converse.
