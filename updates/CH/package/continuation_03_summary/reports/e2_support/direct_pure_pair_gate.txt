E2 DIRECT THERMAL PURE-PAIR GATE WITH EVE ORIENTATION RETAINED
Status: exact finite-channel identity and conditional positivity principle;
structured finite-envelope diagnostics do not give a new positive point.

1. A COMPLEMENT-FREE COMPUTATION OF EVE'S FULL CHERNOFF FUNCTION.
For two orthogonal pure inputs |psi_0>,|psi_1> to a channel Phi, let
B_i=Phi(|psi_i><psi_i|) and X=Phi(|psi_0><psi_1|).
Choose a Stinespring isometry and identify the resulting global pure states
with amplitude matrices A_i from E to B. Write the polar decompositions
A_i=sqrt(B_i) V_i. Then
E_i=A_i^dagger A_i=V_i^dagger B_i V_i,
X=A_0 A_1^dagger=sqrt(B_0) V_0 V_1^dagger sqrt(B_1).
Using inverse powers on supports, for 0<s<1 this gives exactly

Q_E(s)=Tr E_0^s E_1^(1-s)
      =|| B_0^((s-1)/2) X B_1^(-s/2) ||_2^2.             (P1)

Bob's function is Q_B(s)=Tr B_0^s B_1^(1-s).
At s=1/2, (P1) is the square-root-weighted cross-channel expression
||B_0^(-1/4) X B_1^(-1/4)||_2^2. It retains the coherent orientation carried
by Eve; replacing it by an unweighted output overlap is not valid.

For an involutive covariant pair, both functions are symmetric in s and
their minimum is at 1/2. A finite-envelope GKP pair after orthogonalization
need not retain that exact symmetry. The diagnostics minimize each function
separately; they do not assume that the observed near-1/2 minimum is exact.

2. THE PURE REPEATED-CODEWORD CAPACITY GATE.
Use equal prior codewords |psi_0>^tensor m and |psi_1>^tensor m. Each input
is pure, so the conditional B/E entropies agree exactly. Thus
I_c(m)=H(T|E^m)-H(T|B^m).
For binary state discrimination, conditional entropy is bounded between
-log2(1-P_error) and h_2(P_error). The quantum Chernoff theorem therefore
gives the same exponential rate for conditional entropy as for discrimination.
If C_B=min_s Q_B(s) is strictly smaller than C_E=min_s Q_E(s), a sufficiently
large finite m has I_c(m)>0. No negative mixed-seed information is omitted.
The primary finite-system Chernoff theorem used here is
https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.4282 (also quant-ph/0610027 and quant-ph/0607216).
This gives an existence gate, not an explicit m from a finite exponent gap;
a quantitative bound or direct finite-m entropy computation is still needed
to produce a fully concrete rate/blocklength certificate.

To use only finite-dimensional output/complement systems, physically project
the receiver onto the first M Fock levels and erase the remaining output into
one orthogonal flag. This is a deterministic CPTP receiver operation, not
postselection. The flagged output blocks are exactly
B_i^M=P_M B_i P_M +[1-Tr(P_M B_i)]|fail><fail|,
X^M=P_M X P_M -Tr(P_M X)|fail><fail|,
since Tr X=0. Restricted to a two-dimensional input code, this finite-output
channel has a finite minimal Stinespring complement. Apply (P1) to this full
flagged channel. A failure branch cannot be silently removed.

3. A FIXED STRUCTURED GKP FAMILY TESTED DIRECTLY ON THE THERMAL CHANNEL.
The one-mode base qunaught is approximated in position by
sum_(j in Z) exp[-width^2(j L)^2/2]
                  exp[-(x-jL)^2/(2 width^2)],
with L=sqrt(2pi)*aspect. The paired state is its momentum displacement
exp(i pi x/L). Their first 100 Fock coefficients are computed analytically
using displaced-squeezed-state recurrences, normalized, and Gram orthogonalized.
These physical finite-Fock codewords have finite photon number; the intended
comb approximation error is reported, not hidden as an exact GKP limit.

The thermal channel is evaluated through its exact loss/amplifier decomposition
G=1+(1-eta)nu, tau=eta/G, with output compression to 160 levels. Four fixed
seeds use aspects 1,1.68 and widths .25,.4. No Fock coefficient optimization
or fit to eta is performed. All four have C_E-C_B<0 at eta=.76 and eta=.8,
and >0 at eta=.85. None improves the existing threshold.

Examples at nu=1, eigenvalue cutoff 1e-14:
aspect1,width.25,eta.76: C_B=.922187278924,
                         C_E=.795087527527, gap=-.127099751397;
aspect1.68,width.25,eta.76: C_B=.967731985183,
                            C_E=.961908080931, gap=-.005823904252;
aspect1.68,width.25,eta.8: C_B=.966385324959,
                           C_E=.966096161239, gap=-.000289163720.

Cutoffs 1e-10,1e-12,1e-14 are retained in the JSON. The truncated inverse-power
sum is a positive termwise truncation of (P1) for a fixed compressed output
matrix; it is not by itself a lower bound for the exact infinite-output channel.
Reported output trace defects are at floating roundoff, so an exact flag
probability cannot be recovered by subtracting two rounded numbers here.
A certified computation must evaluate or bound it and preserve positivity.

For width .25 the omitted tail relative to the double-cutoff intended comb
is about 1.1e-5; for one aspect1.68,width.4 seed the two unorthogonalized
vectors have overlap .773. These diagnostics must not be confused with a
uniform ideal-code limit. The actually prepared truncated vectors are pure,
orthogonal by construction, and their finite mean photon numbers are saved.

4. RESOURCE AND NEXT GATE.
A fixed seed uses one thermal-channel mode per repeated letter; m repetitions
cost m modes and the average finite input energy is multiplied by m. No
sender amplifier, extra channel, success announcement, or shared shield is
used in this direct route. An outer code consumes additional blocklength.
Preparation, Gram orthonormalization, channel matrix evaluation, output cutoff,
matrix powers near small eigenvalues, and strict Chernoff gap all need
certified error bounds before this diagnostic could become a witness.

The useful continuation is a genuinely multimode correlated qunaught seed
or other pure lattice codeword pair for which the complete direct thermal
functions satisfy C_B<C_E at eta below .7841, ultimately all eta>.75.
The exact output-and-cross-channel formula (P1) allows such a search without
constructing the doubled thermal environment explicitly. The tested simple
seeds do not show that advantage.
