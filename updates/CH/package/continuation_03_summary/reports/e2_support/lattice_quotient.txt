E2 CONSTRUCTIVE COLLECTIVE INTERFACE: LATTICE QUOTIENT ENTROPY
Status: exact ideal-code identities and a conditional finite-code gate.
No lattice family satisfying the near-antidegradability gate is established.

1. PURE CODEWORDS, NO MIXED-SEED SUBTRACTION.
Use [q_j,p_k]=i delta_jk. Let Lambda be a full-rank physical displacement
stabilizer lattice with lambda^T Omega mu in 2pi Z. Its symplectic dual is
Lambda^perp={x:x^T Omega lambda in 2pi Z for all lambda in Lambda}.
Suppose vol(Lambda)=(2pi)^n d. Then
vol(Lambda^perp)=(2pi)^n/d and |Lambda^perp/Lambda|=d^2.
The ideal GKP code encodes a d-dimensional logical system; each logical
codeword is pure. The maximally mixed logical input has entropy log2 d.

For additive displacement xi~N(0,v I_{2n}), choose a fundamental cell C of
Lambda^perp and representatives ell_L for Lambda^perp/Lambda. Decompose
uniquely, except measure-zero boundaries,
xi=S+ell_L+K, S in C, K in Lambda.
The receiver measures the commuting stabilizer syndrome S and keeps the full
analog result. Its conditional logical Pauli probabilities are
p(L|s)=sum_{k in Lambda} f_v(s+ell_L+k) /
       sum_{z in Lambda^perp} f_v(s+z).
All stabilizer-related displacements are correctly treated as one logical
error. This is the exact place where collective degeneracy can help.

For the ideal maximally mixed logical input, the flagged logical channel has
I_inner=log2 d-H(L|S).
The conditional Choi state is Bell diagonal with the d^2 probabilities above;
the output logical state is maximally mixed. This proves the formula without
assuming a mixed thermal seed has nonnegative coherent information.
The analog record remains at the receiver; no message to the sender is used.

2. EXACT DEGENERACY ACCOUNTING.
The change of variables xi <-> (S,L,K) has Jacobian one, so
H(L,K|S)=h_2(xi)-h_2(S),
h_2(xi)=n log2(2pi e v).
Let D_S=D_2(p_S || uniform_C)=log2 vol(C)-h_2(S)>=0. Then exactly

I_inner = n log2[1/(e v)] - D_S + H(K|L,S).                 (Q1)

This separates the Gaussian hashing contribution, the nonuniformity cost of
the receiver syndrome, and the gain from actual channel-error degeneracy.
There is no free entropy source in (Q1). K is ambiguity among physical
errors that act identically on the logical subsystem, not input seed entropy.

At nu=1, use the deterministic sender pre-amplifier A_{1/eta}. Its induced
additive variance is v=2(1-eta). A sufficient constructive gate is

H(K|L,S) > n log2[e*2(1-eta)] + D_S.                    (Q2)

For eta approaching .75 from above, the required extensive ambiguity tends
to n log2(e/2)=0.442695040889... n bits, plus D_S.
At eta=.751, the first cost is 0.4369126... bits per mode.
At eta=.7841, it is approximately 0.231... bits per mode.
The required quantity is conditional degeneracy after the receiver syndrome
and logical coset are specified. A large raw stabilizer count is insufficient.

3. AN EQUIVALENT HEAT-FLOW DESIGN GATE.
Let p_{Gamma,v} be the Gaussian density wrapped onto R^{2n}/Gamma and
h_Gamma(v) its differential entropy in bits. Then
H(L|S)=h_Lambda(v)-h_Lambda^perp(v), and
I_Lambda(v)=log2 d-h_Lambda(v)+h_Lambda^perp(v).
Torus de Bruijn gives
dI_Lambda/dv=[J_Lambda^perp(v)-J_Lambda(v)]/(2 ln2)<=0.
The inequality follows because wrapping modulo the smaller cell is a
classical deterministic postprocessing and cannot increase Fisher information.

N_{1/2}=L_{1/2} o A_2 is antidegradable; therefore I_Lambda(1/2)<=0 for every
physical finite-energy approximation and hence for a valid ideal decoding
limit. Put A_Lambda=-I_Lambda(1/2)>=0. For epsilon>0,

I_Lambda(1/2-epsilon)=-A_Lambda+
 [1/(2 ln2)] integral_{1/2-epsilon}^{1/2}
       [J_Lambda(u)-J_Lambda^perp(u)] du.                (Q3)

Thus a family of collective lattices reaches every eta>.75 through this
route if for every epsilon>0 it has a finite member for which the integral
in (Q3) exceeds A_Lambda. Here epsilon=2(eta-.75).
This is an explicit optimization functional, not a proof that such members
exist. The principal missing step is a correlated lattice with extensive
stabilizer ambiguity whose syndrome cost is low enough to satisfy (Q2).

4. WHAT AN ACTUAL FINITE POSITIVE MARGIN WOULD BUY.
If a finite n,d,Lambda gives certified I_inner=Delta>0, an outer quantum code
achieves Delta/n qubits per additive-channel use in the ideal inner limit.
Each additive use consumes one thermal-channel use and sender amplification;
there is no additional channel or classical assistance. If the GKP encoding
and decoder are approximated by a physical channel whose decoded Choi state
is within trace distance 2t, a finite-reference conditional entropy bound is
|Delta I| <= 2t log2 d +(1+t) h2[t/(1+t)].
Choosing this below Delta establishes a finite-energy positive inner margin.
This requires a proved approximation/decoding limit, not an infinite-energy
state declared physical. A Gaussian envelope with width delta has energy
of order n/delta^2 times the lattice transformation's conditioning; its exact
bound, Gram orthogonalization, and output syndrome error must be charged.
The physical sender photon cost is N/eta+(1-eta)/eta per mode.

An exact decoder may sum exponentially many Gaussian lattice images.
For a finite computation, a tail radius R and lattice-image cutoff must bound
all discarded mass, and syndrome discretization must have a certified error.
The number of images can scale as (R/lambda_min)^(2n). No efficient encoding,
decoding, or state preparation is claimed by the capacity criterion.

5. NONCOMMUTING GAUGES DO NOT REMOVE THIS ACCOUNTING.
A subsystem lattice with noncommuting gauge generators must specify the
pure initial gauge state and the quantum residual gauge output. Quotienting
all gauge errors as if they were orthogonal classical syndromes can erase
coherences held by the environment and overestimate I_inner. One rigorous
route fixes a commuting set of gauge operators, yielding an ordinary
stabilizer subspace code; its full syndrome/coset entropy is then charged
as above. A genuinely different subsystem construction needs an explicit
joint reference-logical-gauge channel calculation.

6. CURRENT OUTCOME.
No new near-.75 lattice satisfying (Q2) has been found or certified.
The standard large-d GKP/polar-code limit makes the degeneracy contribution
vanish and approaches log2[1/(e v)], which misses the open interval.
The large-squeeze rectangular-GKP/repetition candidate has a useful
majority/parity window only for v<=1/(2sqrt2), and also misses that interval.
These are failures of particular constructions, not a converse for all
GKP lattices or all bosonic codes.
