CONVENTION
[q,p]=i, vacuum covariance I/2. Phi_{eta,nu}: V -> eta V +
(1-eta)(nu+1/2) I. N_v: V -> V+v I. A_g: V -> g V+(g-1) I/2.

1. EXACT PRE-AMPLIFICATION REDUCTION (also known: Noh et al. 1801.07271,
Lemma 6; checked against primary source after independent derivation).
Phi_{eta,nu} o A_{1/eta} = N_v, v=(1-eta)(nu+1).
For nu=1 and eta>3/4, v<1/2. Thus an all-v<1/2 positivity theorem for N_v
would suffice for the requested exact threshold, but it is an open gate.
The standard lattice/hashing lower bound is positive only for v<1/e,
i.e. eta>1-1/(2e)=0.8160602794 at nu=1. This reduction is weaker numerically
than the existing non-Gaussian single-use witness and is not a solution.
The physical sender energy for pre-amplifying an N-photon input is
N/eta+(1-eta)/eta; success probability is 1 and no classical messages are used.

2. EXACT D-RAIL ONE-PHOTON POSTSELECTION CHANNEL.
Encode a D-dimensional logical system in H_1=span{|1_i>:1<=i<=D}, across D modes.
Write Phi=A_G o L_tau, G=1+(1-eta)nu, tau=eta/G.
L_tau^D(rho)=tau rho+(1-tau)|vac><vac|.
Projecting the amplified output onto total photon number 1 gives the CP map
F_D(rho)=G^{-(D+1)}[tau rho+(1-tau)(G-1) I_D].
Its success probability is input independent:
p_D=G^{-(D+1)}[tau+D(1-tau)(G-1)].
After discarding all other sectors into a receiver erasure flag, the complete
logical channel is E_{p_D} o Depol_lambda, lambda=tau/[tau+D(1-tau)(G-1)].
This is a deterministic CPTP map; omitting the failure branch is invalid.
If p_D<=1/2 then E_{p_D} is antidegradable, so the full effective channel has
zero quantum capacity for every blocklength, irrespective of outer code.

For nu=1, r=1-eta:
p_D=[1-r+2D r^2]/(1+r)^(D+2).
For D>=2, p_{D+1}<p_D because the ratio comparison reduces to
-1+3r-2D r^2<0, which is strictly negative for all real r when D>=2.
At eta=3/4, p_2=0.4096, and larger D are worse.
For maximally mixed logical input, if q0=lambda+(1-lambda)/D^2 and
qj=(1-lambda)/D^2 (D^2-1 copies), then
I_c=(2p_D-1)log2(D)-p_D H(q0,qj,...).
Conditional purity/entanglement of the success block is not a capacity witness.
Uses per logical channel: D. Input total energy: 1 photon, or 1/D per mode.
Success record remains at receiver; announcing it backward changes the task.

3. NO DETERMINISTIC SCALAR GAUSSIAN PURE-LOSS SIMULATION.
Preprocess by a quantum-limited phase-insensitive map of gain u>0 and postprocess
by one of gain w>0. For net transmissivity kappa=u eta w<=1, the noise is
Y=[w eta |u-1|+w(1-eta)(2nu+1)+|w-1|]/2.
Optimizing over u,w at fixed kappa, for eta>nu/(nu+1), yields
Y_min=(1-kappa)/2+kappa(1-eta)nu/eta, 0<kappa<=eta;
Y_min=(1-kappa)/2+(kappa-eta)+(1-eta)nu, eta<=kappa<=1.
The excess over pure loss is strictly positive at nu>0. At eta=3/4, nu=1,
every kappa>=1/2 has excess at least 1/6. Thus this scalar deterministic
Gaussian route cannot approximate a positive-capacity pure-loss channel by
making the added noise disappear near the requested boundary.
This statement is deliberately restricted to scalar phase-insensitive maps;
it is not a no-go theorem for non-Gaussian, multimode, or heralded codes.

4. FIRST LARGE-SQUEEZE GKP/REPETITION DERIVATION.
For rectangular qubit GKP spacings d_q=sqrt(pi) R, d_p=sqrt(pi)/R and
independent additive quadrature variance v, hard nearest-lattice decoding
produces independent X/Z errors. As R->infinity,
p_X ~ poly(R)^(-1) exp[-pi R^2/(8v)],
delta_Z=1/2-p_Z ~ (2/pi) exp[-pi v R^2/2].
A repetition code correcting the nearly random Z errors needs
m delta_Z^2 -> infinity while avoiding X parity randomization needs m p_X ->0.
There is such a window only if 1/(8v)>v, or v<1/(2 sqrt(2))=0.353553...
This elementary large-bias route does not close v in [1/e,1/2).
It does not rule out general degenerate codes or analog decoding.
