2. LOW-MEMORY PHYSICAL CHANNEL

The retained loss-amplifier Kraus formula is identical to the frozen
e5_realization.py formula. This implementation constructs loss/amplifier
coefficient tables by positive probability recurrences and streams batches
of512 reference-output Kraus vectors into the finite RB Gram matrix.
The missing probabilities are computed by the positive finite
negative-binomial/binomial-tail identity. The reference erasure block is
C diag(t_n) C^dagger, including its nonorthogonal off-diagonal entries.

All retained and erasure blocks enter S(B)-S(RB). No environment cutoff,
renormalization of retained output, side-information flag, or free
postselection is used. A seeded complex-purification comparison against the
original evaluator gives maximum retained-matrix discrepancy4.16e-17 and
erasure-reference discrepancy6.99e-21. This is a numerical cross-check,
not a directed-rounding certificate.

The streamed method avoids storing all(N+1)(M+1) Kraus vectors at once.
Its accounted storage is O(N^2+M^2+r^2 M^2+batch*r*M), r=2. Accumulation
still has a dense finite Gram/eigenspectrum cost; it is not an efficient
unbounded-cutoff entropy algorithm.
