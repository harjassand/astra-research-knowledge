2. THE EXACT COMPLETE CRITERION, WITH ITS QUANTIFIERS

Let B=Phi_eta,nu and E=Phi_eta,nu^c, where E includes BOTH the bath output
E' and the purifying mode F of the thermal bath. Define the sufficient
condition C_RE by

 D((id_R o E)(rho_RA) || (id_R o E)(sigma_RA))
 >= D((id_R o B)(rho_RA) || (id_R o B)(sigma_RA))                 (C_RE)

for every finite-dimensional quantum reference R and every pair of input
states rho_RA,sigma_RA. Relative entropy is the extended Umegaki relative
entropy. It is enough for the capacity argument to require the condition
on pairs with finite input relative entropy; support inclusion alone does
not imply finite relative entropy in infinite dimensions. The pair may
have unequal R marginals. A criterion restricted to Gaussian states,
Gaussian tangent vectors, classical R, or equal R marginals is not the
complete criterion displayed in the sources.

For faithful finite-dimensional sigma and traceless Hermitian X, set

 g_sigma(X)=int_0^infty Tr[X(sigma+sI)^-1 X(sigma+sI)^-1] ds.

The local criterion C_BKM requires

 g_(id_R E)(sigma)((id_R E)(X))
 >= g_(id_R B)(sigma)((id_R B)(X))                              (C_BKM)

for every R,sigma,X. In finite dimensions C_BKM integrates to C_RE using
D(rho||sigma)=int_0^1 (1-t) g_(sigma+t(rho-sigma))(rho-sigma) dt.
Differentiating C_RE at rho=sigma gives the converse local implication.
For a bosonic implementation, a proof must specify domains and justify
this integration or a convergent finite-dimensional approximation. Checking
the local inequality only on Gaussian displacement tangents is a necessary
test, not a sufficient proof. The exact C_RE condition avoids ambiguity
about unbounded inverses and metric domains.

A bosonic finite-reference extension can be proved directly. Let C_d be a
CPTP reference compression retaining the first d basis vectors and mapping
the discarded part to an orthogonal flag. Embed its finite output into the
original separable reference space. Then (C_d o id)(rho) converges in trace
norm to rho, and likewise for sigma. The assumed finite-reference C_RE,
followed by data processing on E and lower semicontinuity on B, gives

 D_B(rho||sigma) <= liminf_d D_B(C_d rho||C_d sigma)
                 <= D_E(rho||sigma).

Thus C_RE for all finite R extends to all separable references for normal
bosonic channels. No Gaussian restriction, energy-uniform entropy
continuity, or infinite reference cutoff fixed in advance is needed here.

This justifies the otherwise delicate hybrid tensorization: apply the
comparison at the first input while treating all other outputs as part of
the separable reference, and then replace each remaining B by E. Hence
E^tensor m completely dominates B^tensor m for every finite m.

To see the operational consequence without subtracting possibly infinite
output entropies, take ANY finite-dimensional quantum message reference R
and an arbitrary encoded rho_RA^m. Apply the tensorized comparison to
(rho_RA^m,rho_R tensor rho_A^m). It gives I(R:B^m)<=I(R:E^m).
Weak monotonicity gives I(R:B^m)+I(R:E^m)<=2 S(R), even for a mixed joint
state. Thus I(R:B^m)<=S(R). A receiver decoder cannot increase this mutual
information. Reliable transmission of a d-dimensional maximally entangled
state requires mutual information tending to 2 log d, which is incompatible
with the bound log d. This proves zero unassisted quantum capacity if C_RE
is established. Finite classical message ensembles similarly have
chi_B<=chi_E at every block, proving zero private capacity. This argument
covers arbitrary non-Gaussian encodings and references.

C_RE is sufficient, not necessary, for Q=0. Its failure does not establish
Q>0. Even P=0 need only force the regularized unreferenced order in [H].

3. EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO THE SIGNED-LIFT CERTIFICATE ABOVE ANTIDEGRADABILITY

A usable bosonic version of the source certificate would provide one fixed
Hermiticity-preserving map J from receiver scores to full-environment
scores, satisfying unbiasedness for every input state and

 Tr E(rho) J(Y)=Tr B(rho)Y,
 Var_E(rho)(J(Y)) <= Var_B(rho)(Y)                              (LIFT)

for every bounded Hermitian receiver score Y (and, for completeness, every
quantum reference). Positivity of the weighted CP defect in [Z1] implies
this variance inequality. It is already impossible with no reference.

Take a displaced thermal input with mean photon number N>0 and q mean mu.
Set a=N+1/2, v=nu+1/2, c=sqrt(nu(nu+1)). Receiver q variance and displacement
slope are

 b=eta a+(1-eta)v,                    d_B=sqrt(eta).

Purify the bath by a two-mode squeezed vacuum on E,F. The q covariance of
the actual full environment E'F and its displacement slope are

 V_E,q=[[ (1-eta)a+eta v, sqrt(eta)c ],
        [ sqrt(eta)c,                 v ]],
 d_E=(-sqrt(1-eta),0).

For a Gaussian displacement model the symmetric logarithmic derivative is
L=(V^-1 d)^T(r-mean). This follows by differentiating its Weyl characteristic
function: the anticommutator with L has characteristic function
 i k^T V(V^-1 d) chi = i k^T d chi. Consequently the full environmental SLD
Fisher information is

 F_E=d_E^T V_E,q^-1 d_E
    =(1-eta)/[(1-eta)a+eta/(4v)].                               (1)

This is quantum Fisher information of BOTH environment modes, not merely
the Fisher information of a chosen homodyne measurement. Quantum
Cauchy-Schwarz in the SLD inner product gives, for any fixed Hermitian
finite-variance environment observable H,

 Var_E(H) >= (partial_mu E_E[H])^2/F_E.                        (2)

The receiver's quadrature variance would be b, whereas every unbiased
estimate of its quadrature mean from the environment has variance at least

 eta/F_E=eta a+eta^2/[4(1-eta)v].

Their difference is

 eta/F_E-b = [eta^2-4(1-eta)^2 v^2]/[4(1-eta)v],              (3)

which is strictly positive exactly when
eta>(1-eta)(2nu+1), i.e. eta>(2nu+1)/(2nu+2).

There is no unbounded-quadrature loophole. Use the bounded receiver score
Y_L=clip(q_B,-L,L). At mu=0 its mean is zero, its variance is at most b, and
its mean derivative is sqrt(eta) p_L with
p_L=erf(L/sqrt(2b)). Unbiasedness makes H=J(Y_L) have the same mean and
mean derivative. Equation (2) therefore gives

 Var_E(J(Y_L)) >= eta p_L^2/F_E.

Let r=b F_E/eta<1 and choose the finite cutoff
L=sqrt(2b ln(4/(1-r))). The Gaussian tail estimate
erfc(z)<=exp(-z^2) gives p_L^2 >= (1+r)/2 > r. Hence

 Var_E(J(Y_L)) > b >= Var_B(Y_L),

contradicting LIFT. All input energies and all receiver scores used in this
contradiction are finite. The estimate is valid against arbitrary
nonlinear environment scores, so it excludes any common signed lift,
not just Gaussian or linear-observable ansatzes.

At nu=1 one may simply take N=1. Then b=3/2 for every eta. Above eta=3/4
the obstruction is strict however small eta-3/4 is. At equality the
bound is equal and gives no contradiction, as expected from the actual
antidegrading channel.

Conclusion: the particular CP-defect / variance-lift mechanism of [Z1]
cannot produce a zero-capacity interval above 3/4 for this physical channel.
By the hierarchy in [H], this also rules out complete measured chi-square
domination. It does NOT rule out the weaker complete BKM / C_RE order.
The latter must be tested independently.

4. FINITE-ENERGY COMPLETE RELATIVE-ENTROPY COUNTERTEST AT ETA=0.7606

The following construction is entirely internal. It uses full physical
outputs, exact Gaussian identities, and convergent interval arithmetic.
It is an obstruction to C_RE itself, stronger than the lift obstruction.

Start with thermal variances u on reference R and w on input A. Apply a
two-mode squeezer of parameter r, and then a local input q squeeze s.
Write h=cosh(r), j=sinh(r), so

 r0=u h^2+w j^2,   a0=w h^2+u j^2,   k0=(u+w)hj.

Use quadrature order (q_R,q_A,p_R,p_A). The covariance blocks are
Q_RA=[[r0,k0 s],[k0 s,a0 s^2]],
P_RA=[[r0,-k0/s],[-k0/s,a0/s^2]].
This state sigma_RA is faithful, since u,w>1/2 and only Gaussian unitaries
have been used. Let rho_RA be its displacement in the p direction
(p_R,p_A)=(d_R,d_A). Both states have the same covariance and finite energy.
Their R marginals differ, which is permitted in the exact C_RE criterion.

For nu=1 set v=3/2,c=sqrt(2), e=sqrt(eta), l=sqrt(1-eta). The physical output
covariance blocks are

 Q_RB=[[r0,e k0 s],[e k0 s,eta a0 s^2+(1-eta)v]],
 P_RB=[[r0,-e k0/s],[-e k0/s,eta a0/s^2+(1-eta)v]],

 Q_RE'F=[[r0,-l k0 s,0],
        [-l k0 s,(1-eta)a0 s^2+eta v,e c],
        [0,e c,v]],
 P_RE'F=[[r0,l k0/s,0],
        [l k0/s,(1-eta)a0/s^2+eta v,-e c],
        [0,-e c,v]].

The output displacement vectors in the p blocks are
d_B=(d_R,e d_A) and d_E=(d_R,-l d_A,0).

For a faithful Gaussian state with covariance V=diag(Q,P), the density is
proportional to exp(-r^T G r/2), with
G=2 i Omega arccoth(2 V i Omega). This identity follows from Williamson
diagonalization and the one-mode thermal identity beta=ln[(lambda+1/2)/
(lambda-1/2)]. The p block has the convergent expansion

 G_p=P^-1 sum_(n=0)^infty [(Q^-1 P^-1/4)^n/(2n+1)].             (4)

Equal-covariance Gaussian displacement pairs have exactly
D(rho||sigma)=d^T G d/2 in nats, because their entropies are equal and
only the quadratic Gibbs expectation changes. Thus

 2(D_E-D_B)=d_E^T G_E,p d_E-d_B^T G_B,p d_B.                  (5)

There is no small-displacement approximation in (5).

The first exact parameter set is
 eta=3803/5000, u=11/2, w=1000000,
 tanh(r/2)=1/1800, s=140,
 d_R=3377/4000, d_A=-1.                                      (6)

Here h=(1+t^2)/(1-t^2), j=2t/(1-t^2), t=1/1800, so the input covariance
is specified exactly by rational numbers. Physical beam-splitter square
roots and c=sqrt(2) are enclosed by directed intervals.

The script interval_reference_certificate.py sums (4) through n=48 using
mpmath.iv at 90 decimal digits, with no numerical eigensolver. To bound
the tail, put A=P^-1 and B=Q^-1 P^-1/4. The matrix B is similar to the
positive symmetric matrix P^-1/2 Q^-1 P^-1/2/4. Directed interval arithmetic
verifies Tr(B^8)<(1/3)^8 for both outputs, so its spectral radius is less
than rho=1/3. The omitted positive-semidefinite tail has quadratic bound

 0 <= tail(d) <= (d^T P^-1 d) rho^49/[99(1-rho)].             (7)

Applying the positive environment tail and negative receiver tail to (5)
gives the internal directed enclosure, rounded further outward here,

 2(D_E-D_B) in [-4.511682491842e-12,-4.511682491841e-12] nats.  (8)

It is strictly negative. The mean input photon number is approximately
9.800012124 x 10^9 and the reference photon number approximately 6.234576;
both are finite. No limit is being used to obtain (8). This disproves
C_RE at eta=0.7606 and therefore
disproves the corresponding complete BKM order there. It does not disprove
zero unassisted quantum capacity there.

A second, easier parameter set is
 eta=761/1000, u=2, w=1000, tanh(r/2)=3/200, s=10,
 d_R=933/1000, d_A=-1.
It gives
 2(D_E-D_B) in [-2.865649241797e-9,-2.865649241796e-9] nats.
A third rational eta=5433561/7123561 approximately 0.7627591 countertest
has a larger negative gap; all three are saved in the certificate JSON.

The failure extends to every larger transmissivity, not just these isolated
points. Complete environmental relative-entropy domination is preserved
under receiver postprocessing: a Stinespring complement of D o B contains
the earlier environment E, so data processing gives
 D_E(composition) >= D_E >= D_B >= D_(D o B)
for every reference and pair. Complement choices are equivalent up to
isometries and reversible restriction to their used support. Thermal
attenuators at fixed nu obey
 Phi_eta1,nu = Phi_(eta1/eta2),nu o Phi_eta2,nu  (eta1<=eta2).
If C_RE held at any eta2>=0.7606, it would therefore hold at eta1=0.7606,
contradicting (8). Hence this complete-order zero-capacity proof route is
unavailable for ALL eta>=0.7606. No quantum-capacity positivity conclusion
follows from this monotonicity argument.

5. WHY A BOSONIC REFERENCE COUNTERTEST REALLY REFUTES THE FINITE-R CRITERION

The reference in (6) is one bosonic mode, but this is not an unsupported
infinite-reference substitution. For the strict finite-relative-entropy
Gaussian pair, compress only R with the finite-dimensional CPTP C_d from
Section 2. For either output channel, data processing bounds the compressed
relative entropy above by the original one. Trace-norm convergence and
lower semicontinuity bound its liminf below by the same original value.
Therefore BOTH compressed relative entropies converge to their original
finite values. Their strictly negative difference remains negative for
some finite d. This gives existence of a genuine finite-dimensional quantum
reference countertest. No explicit numerical d bound is claimed.

This reasoning needs normality of the bosonic channels and lower
semicontinuity/data processing of the extended relative entropy, not a
Gaussian-state closure assertion. It also explains why a Gaussian
reference counterexample is decisive while favorable Gaussian searches
cannot prove the universal criterion.

6. NEGATIVE AND INCONCLUSIVE SEARCH RESULTS; WHAT REMAINS UNPROVED

Support includes deterministic floating-point searches over two-mode
squeezed thermal references, local input squeezing, and equal-covariance
mean perturbations. They found no robust complete-RE violations at the
sampled eta values up to 0.7600. The smallest computed quantities were
frequently about machine precision, reflecting nearly common reference
information. Such values were not classified as counterexamples.

A formal hot-input limiting family motivated the finite construction;
its exploratory search is saved separately and is not used in the
certificate. These searches cover a narrow Gaussian family, not all
Gaussian covariance perturbations and certainly not non-Gaussian states.
They establish neither a threshold for C_RE nor any zero-capacity interval.

For unreferenced displaced thermal inputs, the large-N BKM displacement
gap has coefficient

 [(nu+1/2)(1-2eta)+eta^2 nu(nu+1) ln((nu+1)/nu)]
 /[eta(1-eta)N^2] + O(N^-3).

At nu=1 this coefficient changes sign at approximately
0.7841139609363543. This is only a diagnostic necessary test in one family;
it would entirely miss the referenced failure at 0.7606 and the known
non-Gaussian coherent-information witness at 0.7841. SLD Fisher-information
failure at 0.75 therefore must not be conflated with unreferenced BKM
failure, nor may a favorable unreferenced Gaussian entropy comparison be
promoted to complete order.

To establish a zero-capacity interval in (0.75,0.7606), remaining work would
need either a universal non-Gaussian, arbitrary-reference C_RE/C_BKM proof,
or a different converse that need not imply complete order. The signed
lift certificate is rigorously unavailable there by Section 3. A scalar
minimum-output-entropy or amplifier comparison alone supplies none of the
missing reference-dependent Hessian inequalities.
