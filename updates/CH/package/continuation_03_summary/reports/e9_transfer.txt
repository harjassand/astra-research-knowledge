E9 — one alternative frontier route: channel-level classicalization

Selection: the clearest selection-changing alternative among the latest BQ options I inspected is N533, “A self-compatible nearly idempotent channel is close to entanglement breaking” (BQ intake, gate G-BQ-N533, priority 90). It is materially different from Parisi no-gap and amplifier EPnI: its engine is Kitaev’s approximate C*-algebra factorization followed by a qubit no-cloning obstruction. The possible capability is a certified conversion of a noisy, repeatable, broadcastable finite-dimensional quantum channel into a measure-and-prepare channel with dimension-independent diamond error. That could support a channel-classification / error-modeling tool broader than one capacity calculation. N534 is a pure-family broadcasting route; N533 asks for a whole-channel extension and complete-norm idempotence control, so its conclusion is stronger at the cost of a much heavier input interface.

I reconstructed the decisive transfer. The pinned source imports universal bounds `||Φ−ED||⋄≤β=O(η)` and `||DE−I||⋄≤γ=O(η)` from Kitaev’s factorization theorem, where η=`||Φ²−Φ||⋄`. If a noncommutative summand M_m remains in the factor algebra, embed a qubit into a 2×2 corner and retract onto it. Feeding this through the approximate factorization and the supplied broadcaster gives a qubit broadcaster whose jth marginal is within `τ_j+β+2γ` of identity. A Bell-reference projection argument gives the exact no-cloning bound `d₁+d₂≥1`, so the noncommutative block is impossible whenever `τ₁+τ₂+2β+4γ<1`. The factor algebra must then be commutative, and `ED` is EB. In the exact case this yields a dimension-free `O(η)` EB approximation, conditional on the imported theorem.

I also independently checked the report’s local direct-sum unitary-design repair: sign-symmetrizing each block design preserves `U†⊗U` and its within-block identity while making the mean unitary zero, which annihilates cross-block terms. The W_d counterexample shows why the complete norm is load-bearing: W_d is self-compatible and its idempotence defect on every product input is `O(1/d)`, yet its Choi state has antisymmetric weight 1 while every separable Choi state has weight ≤1/2, forcing diamond distance at least 1 from every EB channel.

The opportunity is selection-changing only for a finite-dimensional channel-structure track. If Λ=ED is constructed, any t-use adaptive protocol changes by at most `tβ` when every Φ is replaced by Λ, giving an information-level classicalization guarantee. The unresolved capability bottleneck is substantial: Kitaev’s result has hidden constants and no stated system-size-efficient algorithm for extracting D,E; generic self-compatibility/diamond certification works on dense Choi matrices of size exponential in qubit count, and the theorem does not imply efficient classical simulation. A genuine new primitive would need a succinct/locality promise plus a polynomial-time construction and complete-norm certificates. The finite-dimensional theorem does not transfer directly to the infinite-dimensional thermal attenuator.

Verdict: credible and mathematically strong as a separate, narrowly scoped direction; not a demonstrated historic breakthrough and not a better immediate route to the thermal-attenuator capacity question without the new succinct factorization/certification primitive. If the parent objective allows a parallel algorithmic channel-classification line, this is the only route I found worth changing direction for. Otherwise, candidly no higher-value alternative was established.
