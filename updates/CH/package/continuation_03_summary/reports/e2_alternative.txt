1. AN EXPLICIT COLLECTIVE PURE-CODEWORD MECHANISM
Let Lambda_0 be a symplectically selfdual k-mode GKP lattice, with volume
(2pi)^k and consistent commuting Weyl phases. It defines one pure ideal
qunaught |Q>. Choose a nontrivial character chi:Lambda_0 -> Z_2 and a
halfshift t such that exp(i t^T Omega lambda)=(-1)^chi(lambda).
Then 2t belongs to Lambda_0. For n copies define

Lambda_n={lambda_j in Lambda_0: sum_j chi(lambda_j)=0 mod2}.

This actual index-two sublattice encodes a qubit across kn modes. Its pure
basis is |Q>^tensor n and [D(t)|Q>]^tensor n. There is no mixed thermal seed
whose negative coherent information is silently omitted. Arithmetic matters:
continuously scaling a selfdual lattice to volume 2 generally breaks integral
symplectic pairings and is not an alternative realization of this code.

For Gaussian displacement density f_v, on one base fundamental cell put
g(s)=sum_(lambda in Lambda_0) f_v(s+lambda),
f_chi(s)=sum_(lambda in Lambda_0) (-1)^chi(lambda) f_v(s+lambda),
r(s)=f_chi(s)/g(s), and g_t(s)=g(s+t).
Define two computable integrals

rho(v)=integral f_chi(s)^2/g(s) ds,
beta(v)=integral sqrt[g(s)g_t(s)] ds.

The ideal analog stabilizer decoder has exactly
I_c(n)=1-E_(g^n) h_2[(1+product_j r(S_j))/2]-H(T|Y^n),
where T is uniform binary and Y_j|T are iid with density g or g_t.
The first subtraction charges the parity of physical stabilizer lifts; the
second charges ambiguity of the global halfshift. This follows from the
logical-error chain rule and does not assume the two errors are independent.

The binary entropy series and the pointwise Bhattacharyya entropy bound give
the rigorous sufficient inequality

I_c(n)>=rho(v)^n/(2 ln2)-beta(v)^n.                       [D]

Consequently rho(v)>beta(v) gives an explicit finite ideal block: choose
n>ln(2 ln2)/ln[rho(v)/beta(v)]. A certified finite positive margin plus a
controlled physical GKP approximation would give an unassisted thermal-channel
rate through the sender amplifier, one channel use per physical mode.
The full analog syndrome stays at the receiver; no heralding or backward
communication is hidden in [D].

This is a precise conditional construction, not a theorem that suitable
correlated seeds exist. For every v<.5, finding a finite Lambda_0, chi with
rho>beta is a sufficient missing gate. The criterion is only sufficient for
the broader lattice problem, so failing it is not a general converse.

2. A GENERAL LATTICE QUOTIENT DESIGN FUNCTIONAL
For any valid n-mode commuting stabilizer lattice Lambda encoding dimension
d, write a Gaussian displacement uniquely as xi=S+ell_L+K, with S in a
fundamental cell C of Lambda^perp, L in Lambda^perp/Lambda, and K in Lambda.
The ideal decoder has I_inner=log2d-H(L|S). Exact entropy bookkeeping gives

I_inner=n log2[1/(e v)]-D_2(p_S||uniform_C)+H(K|L,S).      [Q]

Near eta=.75 with v=2(1-eta), positive rate requires actual conditional
stabilizer ambiguity exceeding .442695... bits per physical mode in the
limit, plus syndrome nonuniformity. A large raw stabilizer count is not
enough. For example, a rectangular single-qubit lattice at v=.49 has rising
stabilizer ambiguity as it is squeezed, but its syndrome cost rises too;
the checked inner information remains negative.

Equivalently, let h_Gamma(v) and J_Gamma(v) be wrapped-Gaussian entropy and
Fisher information on the torus Gamma. Then
I_Lambda=log2d-h_Lambda+h_Lambda^perp and
dI_Lambda/dv=(J_Lambda^perp-J_Lambda)/(2ln2)<=0.
Writing A_Lambda=-I_Lambda(.5)>=0, a finite lattice succeeds at .5-epsilon
exactly when
integral_(.5-epsilon)^.5 [J_Lambda-J_Lambda^perp]du >2ln2 A_Lambda.
This is a concrete deficit-versus-response gate for collective lattice
search, not evidence that its optimum reaches the AD boundary.

Noncommuting subsystem gauges cannot be quotiented as classical errors
without the joint reference-logical-gauge channel. Fixing a pure gauge and
commuting constraints is legitimate, but the actual inner coherent
information still must be calculated. No noncommuting construction passing
that test is supplied here.

3. DIRECT THERMAL RATIONAL TRANSDUCTION: THE DECODER OBSTACLE
At eta=p/q in lowest terms, forward-rescaled lattice commutation is not
sufficient. The Heisenberg action is
Phi^dagger D(z)=exp[-(1-eta)(nu+1/2)|z|^2/2] D(sqrt(eta)z).
An output stabilizer has input-independent statistics when its pullback is
an input stabilizer; the canonical compatible choice has inverse scaling.

For square lattices of dimensions kp and kq, input sqrt(2pi kp)Z^2 and output
sqrt(2pi kq)Z^2, output normalizers survive compression to the input code only
at labels q b, giving input labels p b. For gcd(k,pq)=1, CRT isolates a
k-dimensional logical system and pure p-dimensional input gauge. Full output
stabilizer dephasing leaves a maximally mixed, independent q-dimensional
output gauge and a conditioned k-to-k channel.

Its Choi Weyl algebra has multiplier (q-p)/k. Put g=gcd(k,q-p), d=k/g.
The conditional Choi state splits into g^2 central sectors, each a
d-dimensional state with multiplicity d. Thus its maximally mixed inner rate
is exactly log2g-H(Z|s)-sum_z p_z S(tau_z). For g=1 this is nonpositive.
For g>=2 at nu=1, Gaussian theta bounds make the center Fourier-L1 norm
less than .037, implying H(Z|s)>=2log2g-log2(1.037), again negative.

This internally reconstructed calculation rules out a positive maximally
mixed inner seed for this measured canonical implementation. It is not a
zero-capacity theorem for the induced channel, a coherent syndrome decoder,
correlated arithmetic lattices, or the thermal channel at arbitrary blocklength.
The exact algebra and scope are preserved in rational_transduction.txt. Finite
combined-Weyl matrices have the predicted multiplicities to 1.64e-16; that
checks the representation, not the Gaussian decoder or capacity conclusion.

4. OTHER SPECIFIC FAILED CONSTRUCTIONS
One-photon D-rail encoding followed by retaining only total output photon
number one gives an erasure-depolarizing channel. At nu=1 its success is
p_D=[eta+2D(1-eta)^2]/(2-eta)^(D+2). It decreases with D>=2. At eta=.75,
p_2=.4096 and larger rails are worse; p_D<=.5 makes the complete effective
channel antidegradable for every outer blocklength. Dropping failed sectors
or announcing success backward would misstate the task. This does not cover
the full multi-sector SU(D) channel or higher fixed-photon shells.

Scalar deterministic quantum-limited Gaussian pre/postprocessing leaves
strict excess noise over any pure-loss channel. At eta=.75,nu=1, every net
transmissivity at least .5 has excess quadrature noise at least 1/6. This is
only a restricted Gaussian simulation obstruction.

A direct thermal-complement Petz calculation finds an exact local
square-root-metric growth exponent absent from a naive Hilbert-Schmidt
collision calculation. The tempting collision scale 2nu+1 is replaced in
that local metric by (sqrt(nu+1)+sqrt(nu))^2. A diagonal Laguerre/Hille-Hardy
lower bound proves this is a real exponent, not just a loose upper estimate.
It defeats that candidate proof of AD-threshold recovery. It is not a
complete finite-energy thermal-GKP bound or a no-go for all GKP decoders.

5. RESOURCE AND VERIFICATION INTERFACE
For a pre-amplified N-photon physical input, sender energy is
N/eta+(1-eta)/eta per mode. Success probability is one. A lattice inner block
uses its full physical mode count, and an outer quantum code adds blocklength.
Ideal GKP states have infinite energy and do not themselves certify capacity.
For a fixed logical dimension d and ideal margin Delta>0, decoded Choi trace
norm error 2t changes coherent information by at most
2t log2d+(1+t)h_2[t/(1+t)]. A physical proof must make this below Delta through
finite envelopes, orthonormalization, syndrome implementation, and precision.
The finite-energy transfer task was coordinated with e6; no unproved physical
decoder limit is treated here as already established.

Gaussian image-sum decoding can cost exponentially many terms in dimension.
It needs a tail cutoff and a certified syndrome discretization error. No
efficient decoder, finite energy bound, or numerical certificate near .75
is claimed. The diagnostic scripts use local NumPy and no paid compute.
They preserve negatives and check quotient/de-Bruijn identities; Fock-kernel
truncations reproduce the closed expressions but do not certify capacity.

LATE DIRECT PURE-PAIR CHECK
For any orthogonal pure pair, write B_i=Phi(|psi_i><psi_i|) and
X=Phi(|psi_0><psi_1|). Eve's full Chernoff function can be calculated exactly
from receiver operators alone:
Q_E(s)=||B_0^((s-1)/2) X B_1^(-s/2)||_2^2.
If min_s Q_B(s)<min_s Q_E(s), pure repeated codewords eventually have positive
coherent information. This retains the orientation term responsible for
earlier failures and can test direct thermal codes without a sender amplifier.
Full proof, finite-output erasure-flag accounting, and the primary Chernoff
reference are in e2_support/direct_pure_pair_gate.txt.

Four fixed finite-envelope qunaught pairs (aspects1,1.68, widths.25,.4;
inputcutoff100, outputcutoff160) were evaluated without coefficient
optimization. All fail the Chernoff ordering at eta=.76 and .8 and succeed
numerically at .85, a known positive region. At eta=.8 the smallest observed
negative gap is -.00028916 for aspect1.68,width.25; at .76 its gap is -.00582390.
