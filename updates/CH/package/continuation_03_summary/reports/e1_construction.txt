Date: 2026-10-10
Status: NO EXACT THRESHOLD PROVED. No new positive-capacity witness in
        0.75 < eta < 0.7841 is established here.

The work below freezes two explicit asymptotic encoding mechanisms and
derives their failures. The first is binary cat repetition about an arbitrary
pure Gaussian seed, including collective entangled Gaussian seeds. The
second uses all photon-number output sectors of collective photon-shell
codes. A conditional-entropy bound also excludes a broad photon-sparse
uniform-shell limit. These are family-specific obstructions, not upper bounds
on the unrestricted regularized quantum capacity.

Section 10 adds the subsequent rare-mixture construction search about an
infinite-support non-Gaussian pure seed. It gives an exact dark-support
obstruction for an exponential-moment class, a singular cost for weak tail
regularization, and physical-cutoff diagnostics. Faithful complementary
outputs outside that class have not been constructed or excluded.

2. EXACT STINESPRING DILATION AND KRAUS OPERATORS

Conventions: [q,p]=i, a=(q+ip)/sqrt(2), V=2 Cov(q,p), so vacuum V=I.
Use a=1-eta only in scalar formulas below; the annihilation operator will
be written a_hat to avoid ambiguity. Put
  v=2nu+1, c_env=sqrt(v^2-1), b=(1-eta)nu,
  G=1+b, tau=eta/G.

The environmental thermal purification is exactly
  |T_nu>_{EF}=sum_{m>=0} sqrt(nu^m/(nu+1)^(m+1)) |m,m>.
The beam splitter has
  b_hat=sqrt(eta) a_hat+sqrt(1-eta) e_hat,
  e'_hat=-sqrt(1-eta) a_hat+sqrt(eta) e_hat.
Thus
  V_eta,nu |psi> = (U_eta,AE tensor I_F)(|psi>_A tensor |T_nu>_EF)
is an exact isometry into B E' F. Tracing E'F gives the thermal attenuator;
E'F is a true complementary output. Discarding F is not permitted in any
coherent-information comparison.

Equivalently Phi=A_G o L_tau, with exact Kraus operators
  L_l |n> = 1_{n>=l} sqrt(C(n,l)(1-tau)^l tau^(n-l)) |n-l>,
  A_k |j> = sqrt(C(j+k,k) b^k/G^(j+k+1)) |j+k>.
Hence K_{l,k}=A_k L_l, or
  K_{l,k}|n> = 1_{n>=l}
    sqrt(C(n,l)C(n-l+k,k)(1-tau)^l tau^(n-l)b^k/G^(n-l+k+1))
    |n-l+k>.
The double Kraus sum is trace preserving. All loss labels l and amplifier
labels k are environmental information; replacing them by only their
difference is an unjustified degradation of Eve.

3. FROZEN MECHANISM A: LARGE CAT REPETITION ABOUT A GAUSSIAN SEED

Let |psi_+> and |psi_-> be two displaced copies of a pure Gaussian state
with covariance V_A=diag(s,1/s), s>0. Their input mean difference is
d=(d_q,d_p); take symmetric means +/-d/2. Define the n-use input
  rho_n=1/2 (|psi_+><psi_+|)^(tensor n)
       +1/2 (|psi_-><psi_-|)^(tensor n).
This is a valid non-Gaussian rank-two input and its purification is a
multimode cat. It requires no feedback or preshared entanglement.

For every pure conditional input, S(B_+)=S(E_+) and S(B_-)=S(E_-).
The coherent information is therefore exactly
  Ic(rho_n,Phi^tensor n)=chi(X;B^n)-chi(X;E^n)
                       =H(X|E^n)-H(X|B^n).
Here X is an auxiliary classical ensemble label; this equality does not
assume the input codewords are orthogonal.

The covariance matrices of B are
  q_B=eta*s+a*v, p_B=eta/s+a*v, w^2=q_B*p_B, a=1-eta.
The q and p blocks of the true complementary covariance are
  V_E,q=[[a*s+eta*v, sqrt(eta)*c_env],
         [sqrt(eta)*c_env, v]],
  V_E,p=[[a/s+eta*v, -sqrt(eta)*c_env],
         [-sqrt(eta)*c_env, v]].
Eve's mean difference is (-sqrt(a)d_q,0) in the q block and
(-sqrt(a)d_p,0) in the p block. The complementary symplectic eigenvalues
are exactly w and 1. The second eigenvalue is a genuine pure Gaussian
normal mode, although Eve's other normal mode is thermal.

For equally covariant displaced Gaussian states, parity interchanges the
two hypotheses. Log-convexity and this symmetry put the minimum Chernoff
overlap at power 1/2. If V=S diag(lambda_j,lambda_j) S^T is a Williamson
decomposition, normalized sqrt(rho) has covariance
  V_half=S diag(lambda_j+sqrt(lambda_j^2-1)) S^T.
In the present quadrature convention the Chernoff exponent is
  C=1/2 d_out^T V_half^(-1) d_out.

Put t=sqrt(1-1/w^2). Bob's exact exponent is
  C_B=eta/[2(1+t)] * (d_q^2/q_B+d_p^2/p_B).
For Eve define the q spectral projector
  P_q=(V_E,q V_E,p-I)/(w^2-1).
Then
  (V_E,half,q)^(-1)=V_E,q^(-1)[I-t/(1+t)P_q],
and analogously with q,p swapped. Multiplying the two-by-two matrices gives
the following closed identity:

  C_B-C_E =
    [eta^2-a^2 v^2-a^2(v^2-1)/t]
    * [d_q^2/s+s*d_p^2] / [2(1+t)w^2].                 (A)

Useful algebra for checking (A):
  det(V_E,q)=a*s*v+eta,
  q_B det(V_E,q)=s*w^2,
  2(1+t)(C_Bq-C_Eq)
       =(eta^2-a^2v^2)/(s*w^2)
         -t*a^2(v^2-1)/(s*(w^2-1)),
and t/(w^2-1)=1/(t*w^2). Here C_Bq,C_Eq are coefficients per d_q^2.

Since t<1 at every finite s and nu>0, a necessary condition for a strictly
positive exponent difference is
  eta/(1-eta)>sqrt(2v^2-1).
The infimum over s is attained only as s->0 or s->infinity:

  eta_cat(nu)=sqrt(2(2nu+1)^2-1)/[1+sqrt(2(2nu+1)^2-1)]. (B)

At nu=1, eta_cat=0.8048058983988963. For an unsqueezed seed s=1 the
corresponding threshold is 0.8201941016011038. This entire asymptotic route
therefore performs worse than the existing thermal bound eta>0.8.
At eta=0.76, s=1e-6, d_q=1,d_p=0:
  C_B=0.26388873090281323,
  C_E=0.4473681717642984.
Eve's exponent is decisively larger; the difference is not a numerical
near-zero residual.

This obstruction also holds for arbitrary pure multimode Gaussian seeds,
including entangled ones. Bloch-Messiah expresses such a seed as a passive
interferometer applied to a tensor product of single-mode squeezed vacua.
Identical thermal attenuators commute with that passive interferometer.
Chernoff exponents of the transformed product outputs add, and every term
has the nonpositive sign in (A) when eta<=eta_cat. Thus passive entanglement
and an arbitrary displacement vector do not remove this obstruction.

Scope: below (B), every fixed nonzero pair of these seed codewords has
negative coherent information for all sufficiently large repetition n.
This is not a statement that every finite n or every non-Gaussian pair is
negative. It also does not prove a uniform theorem for arbitrarily coupled
n-dependent squeezing/displacement/covariance schedules.

4. FROM CHERNOFF ORDERING TO THE SIGN OF COHERENT INFORMATION

For any equiprobable binary cq state with minimum discrimination error Pe,
  -log2(1-Pe) <= H(X|Q) <= h2(Pe).
The left side is the cq min-entropy bound. The right side follows by
measuring an optimal Helstrom measurement and using binary Fano.

For fixed Gaussian hypotheses, Pe(n)=exp[-nC+o(n)]. In particular,
C_E>C_B implies
  H(X|E^n) <= O(n exp[-nC_E]),
  H(X|B^n) >= exp[-nC_B-o(n)]/ln(2),
so Ic(rho_n)<0 eventually. Conversely, C_B>C_E is a sufficient
large-repetition positivity mechanism for this family, although it only
applies above the unhelpful threshold (B).

Here is a countable spectral justification of the converse exponent, to
avoid an unexamined finite-dimensional assumption. Write
  rho=sum_i lambda_i |i><i|, sigma=sum_j mu_j |j><j|,
  p_ij=lambda_i |<i|j>|^2, q_ij=mu_j |<i|j>|^2.
These are probability distributions on a countable set. For any projection
test T, expanding <i|j>=<i|Tj>+<i|(I-T)j> and applying |x+y|^2<=2|x|^2+2|y|^2
gives
  Pe_quantum >= 1/4 sum_ij min(p_ij,q_ij).
The same argument applies to n copies; the corresponding distributions
are p^tensor n and q^tensor n. Their Chernoff overlap is exactly
  sum p_ij^z q_ij^(1-z)=Tr rho^z sigma^(1-z).
For the symmetric displaced Gaussian pair this overlap is analytic around
z=1/2 and minimized there. Under the tilted distribution
r_ij=sqrt(p_ij q_ij)/Q_half, the log likelihood has finite moments and
mean zero. The law of large numbers then gives, for every epsilon>0,
  sum min(p^n,q^n)
    >=Q_half^n exp[-n epsilon/2]
      Prob_r(|sum log(p/q)|<=n epsilon).
Its exponent is at most -log Q_half. The usual trace-class Bhattacharyya
upper bound Pe<=Q_half^n/2 gives the reverse inequality. All sums are
nonnegative or square summable, so the spectral steps use Tonelli rather
than a dimension cutoff. This proves the needed exponent for these pairs.

5. WHY GAUSSIAN SMOOTHING DOES NOT TURN SLD INFORMATION INTO ENTROPY

One tempting continuation is to mix a seed slightly, fill Eve's pure normal
mode, and replace Chernoff/entropy comparisons by SLD Fisher information.
There are two separately charged obstacles.

(i) For displaced copies sigma_+/- of a mixed seed sigma, repetition gives
  Ic(1/2 sigma_+^tensor n+1/2 sigma_-^tensor n)
       =n Ic(sigma)+chi(X;B^n)-chi(X;E^n).
The magnitude of the last difference is <=1 bit. A fixed negative seed
coherent information cannot be discarded. For n>1/|Ic(sigma)| it already
forces the total negative, irrespective of the ensemble advantage.

(ii) The weak-modulation Holevo curvature is the relative-entropy/BKM
metric, not SLD QFI/8. For a faithful Gaussian state with Williamson
eigenvalues lambda_j, its Gibbs quadratic matrix is
  H=S^(-T) diag(2 arccoth(lambda_j)) S^(-1).
Two symmetric small displacement means +/-d/2 have
  chi=d^T H d/8+o(||d||^2)                       [nats].
The coefficient arccoth(1+epsilon)~(1/2)log(2/epsilon) diverges as a filled
pure mode is emptied again. At an exactly pure mode the expansion is
singular and the leading contribution is d^2 log(1/d^2).

Example, eta=0.76,nu=1, isotropic input covariance cI, c=1.00000001:
  seed Ic = -5.1553681e-8 bits,
  SLD_B/8-SLD_E/8 = +0.00675675668919 per d_q^2,
  BKM_B-BKM_E = -0.84497005650646 nats per d_q^2.
The SLD comparison crosses at the AD threshold; the actual local entropy
comparison has the opposite sign in this example. Increasing the smoothing
noise also changes the seed term, which must be included.

This observation does not rule out non-Gaussian collective pure codewords
whose complementary normal directions are intrinsically mixed. It rules
out replacing the required entropy calculation by SLD information without
a theorem controlling the relative-entropy metric and the seed term.

6. FROZEN MECHANISM B: ALL-SECTOR COLLECTIVE ONE-PHOTON RAIL

This is distinct from postselecting the total output photon number to one.
Every noisy output sector is retained. Use d physical modes and the
one-photon code space C^d, with input rho=I_d/d. For L>=0 put
  D_L=C(L+d-1,L), D_-1=0,
  c_L=b^L/G^(L+d), beta_L=(1-tau)+tau*L/(b*d).
For a one-photon pure state |psi>, the output in the L-photon shell is
exactly
  Phi^tensor d(|psi><psi|)|_L
     =c_L[(1-tau) I_L+(tau/b) n_psi],
where n_psi=a_psi^dagger a_psi restricted to that shell.
Thus Bob's eigenvalue in shell L is c_L beta_L with multiplicity D_L.

For the purified uniform code, the RB block is
  c_L/d[(1-tau)I_R tensor I_L+(tau/b)T_L],
  T_L=sum_ij |i><j|_R tensor a_i^dagger a_j.
Define C: C^d tensor Sym^L(C^d)->Sym^(L-1)(C^d) by
  C(|i> tensor |phi>)=a_i |phi>.
Then T_L=C^dagger C and CC^dagger=(L+d-1)I_(L-1). The full RB spectrum is
therefore exactly
  lambda_L,+ =c_L/d[(1-tau)+(tau/b)(L+d-1)], multiplicity D_(L-1),
  lambda_L,- =c_L/d(1-tau), multiplicity d*D_L-D_(L-1).
This diagonalizes the whole reference-output state, not merely a selected
success branch.

Let r_L=D_(L-1)/D_L=L/(L+d-1), w_L=c_L D_L beta_L, and
  p_L=(r_L/d)[(1-tau)+(tau/b)(L+d-1)]/beta_L.
The exact coherent information is the convergent scalar sum
  Ic=sum_L w_L[-h2(p_L)-p_L log2(r_L)
                        -(1-p_L)log2(d-r_L)],           (C)
with 0 log0 interpreted by continuity. No exponentially large matrix is
needed to evaluate (C).

The distribution w_L is the mixture
  (1-tau) NB(d,b/G) + tau [1+NB(d+1,b/G)].
Consequently L/d->b in probability as d->infinity, r_L->b/G and p_L->tau.
The centered logarithmic moments are uniformly controlled by the geometric
negative-binomial tail. In particular (p_L-tau)log d->0 in expectation.
This yields the asymptotic formula

  Ic=-(1-tau)log2 d-h2(tau)-tau log2(b/G)+o(1).          (D)

For every fixed eta<1,nu>0, this diverges to minus infinity. The apparent
high-dimensional degeneracy cannot hide the missing single photon: the
loss branch contributes (1-tau)log d environmental information.

Examples from the exact scalar spectrum, nu=1:
  eta      d=2          d=16          d=1024          [bits/block]
  .7500   -.2420914    -1.2102113     -3.5778558
  .7600   -.2078795    -1.0999663     -3.3819813
  .7841   -.1200106     -.8148604     -2.8824547
  .8000   -.0576736     -.6101207     -2.5293054

The support program's sums omit only tails whose explicit geometric bound
is listed. These tiny tail bounds do not bound floating-point roundoff;
the numbers are numerical support, while (C) and (D) are analytic formulas.

7. GENUINELY COLLECTIVE EXTENSION: UNIFORM K-PHOTON SHELLS

Let rho_K=I_(Sym^K(C^d))/D_K, K>=1. K can grow with d. This input uses the
entire collective K-boson shell and is not a tensor product of rail states.
It has the exact Haar resolution
  rho_K=integral |psi;K><psi;K| dpsi,
  |psi;K>=(a_psi^dagger)^K |vac>/sqrt(K!).
This identity is a resolution of the symmetric-space identity, not a
claim that an arbitrary structured subcode has such a decomposition.

Apply the pure-loss part first. Let J be the retained photon number,
J~Bin(K,tau); E0 retains K-J photons. Conditioning on J=j gives
  rho_(BE0|j)=integral |psi;j><psi;j|_B
                         tensor |psi;K-j><psi;K-j|_E0 dpsi.
The conditioned state is separable, despite the original global pure-loss
dilation retaining coherence between different j values.

Now apply the d-mode amplifier. For the conditional input |psi;j>, its
complement F is, up to a passive mode rotation, NB(j+1,b/G) on one mode
and a thermal state of mean b on each of the other d-1 modes. Let H_j be
the Shannon entropy of NB(j+1,b/G), so
  S(F|psi,j)=(d-1)g(b)+H_j.
Conditional-entropy concavity applied to the preceding Haar mixture gives
  S(E0 F|J=j)>=log2 D_(K-j)+(d-1)g(b)+H_j.             (E)

Dephase the photon number of E0. This adds no more than H(J) to the entropy
of E0F. The dephased entropy is H(J)+sum_j p_j S(E0F|j). Therefore
  S(E0F)>=sum_j p_j S(E0F|j).                         (F)
The H(J) term cancels; it has not been silently discarded or supplied to
Bob. Bob's total mean photon number is db+eta K, so maximum entropy at
fixed energy and concavity of g give
  S(B)<=d g(b+eta K/d)<=d g(b)+eta K g'(b).
Combining with (E)-(F) gives the stronger exact upper bound
  Ic(rho_K)<=g(b)+eta K g'(b)
             -E_J[log2 D_(K-J)]-E_J[H_J].             (G)
Dropping H_J>=0 and using
  D_m=C(d+m-1,m)>=d^m/m!>=(d/K)^m, 0<=m<=K,
yields a simple fully explicit bound:

  Ic(rho_K)<=g(b)+eta K log2(1+1/b)
                      -(1-tau)K log2(d/K).             (H)

Hence every photon-sparse uniform-shell sequence with K/d->0 has strictly
negative coherent information eventually. K may be fixed, logarithmic,
or any sublinear divergent sequence. All output noise and loss sectors
are charged; no receiver postselection or free classical flag is used.

At eta=.76,nu=1, d/K=1000, the bound (H) is approximately
  g(.24)+K[.76 log2(1+1/.24)-(.38709677)log2(1000)],
which is negative already at K=1 and decreases linearly with K.

Scope of this collective obstruction: (G)-(H) apply to the maximally mixed
full photon shell. A structured nonuniform subcode can have quantum
entanglement between surviving and lost photons conditioned on J, so the
separability/concavity step used in (E) does not extend to that subcode.
Such conditional entanglement is the remaining concrete place where
non-Gaussian quantum degeneracy might change the entropy balance. Nothing
here proves that it actually does.

8. ENERGY, PREPARATION, CUTOFF AND PRECISION COSTS

Gaussian cat mechanism:
  E_seed=(s+s^(-1)-2)/4+||d||^2/8 photons per mode,
  total inner-block energy=n E_seed,
  squeeze parameter magnitude=|log s|/2.
For a fixed gap eta-eta_cat=Delta>0, making the Gaussian exponent positive
requires w^2=Omega(1/Delta), hence energy Omega(1/Delta) for the infinitely
squeezed approach. The coherent-information magnitude of large binary
repetition is exponentially small in n. Parameter and entropy precision
must resolve that exponentially small gap; a formal exponent ordering is
not a practical finite-block certificate. None of these costs yields a
construction in the desired .75-to-.7841 interval.

Uniform shell mechanism:
  input energy=K photons in d modes; energy per mode=K/d.
Its full Hilbert dimension D_K grows as d^K/K! for fixed K. The scalar
one-photon formula (C) avoids materializing the large matrix. A general
preparation/coding scheme for structured shell subcodes has not been
provided; uniform Haar averaging is only an exact state representation.

Safe finite output cutoffs:
For any rank-r input purification, use the physical cutoff-erasure channel
  T_M(X)=P_M X P_M+Tr[(I-P_M)X]|perp><perp|.
Ic(T_M o Phi)<=Ic(Phi) by data processing. Conditioning on P_M and
renormalizing the success branch is not this channel and cannot certify
unassisted capacity on its own.

For Gaussian seed outputs, the photon-number generating function is
  F(z)=2 exp[(z-1)d_B^T A_z^(-1)d_B]/sqrt(det A_z),
  A_z=(1+z)I+(1-z)V_B,
valid for real z>1 with A_z positive definite. At product block size n,
  Prob(total photons>M)<=z^(-(M+1)) F(z)^n.
For symmetric displacement means, their F(z) coincide. The coarse Markov
alternative is Prob(total>M)<=n E_B/(M+1), but it is much more expensive
when the desired entropy gap is exponentially small.

If p is the overflow probability, embedding the erased output into the
original output plus flag gives trace distance epsilon<=sqrt(p)+p. Since
the reference of the binary-cat input has dimension <=2, conditional
entropy continuity bounds the coherent-information change by
  2 epsilon+(1+epsilon)h2(epsilon/(1+epsilon)).
This avoids assuming entropy continuity in an unbounded output dimension.
To resolve a gap gamma, epsilon log(1/epsilon) must be much smaller than
gamma. A constant tail cutoff is insufficient for an exponentially small
cat-rate certificate.

For the all-sector single-photon calculation, the negative-binomial tail
ratio at photon number M is explicitly
  r_vac=(b/G)(M+d)/(M+1),
  r_one=(b/G)(M+d)/M.
Once these are below one, the remaining tail is bounded by the next
geometric series. The absolute missing contribution to (C) is at most
tail_probability * log2(d), because each conditional coherent information
lies between -log2(d) and +log2(d).

9. CONCLUSION AND PRECISE REMAINING GAP

No proof of Q(Phi_eta,1)>0 for every eta>.75 was found. No alternative exact
capacity-positivity threshold was proved. The primary bracket remains
unchanged: .75<=eta_c<=.7841.

Two asymptotic routes are now explicitly charged and fail:
  (a) arbitrary pure Gaussian seed cat repetition retains an extra pure
      environmental discrimination direction and has threshold .8048059;
  (b) collective photon-sparse uniform shells lose a diverging amount of
      logical entropy to the loss environment, even when every receiver
      output photon sector is retained.

The unclosed construction problem is concrete: find structured collective
non-Gaussian pure codewords whose conditional correlations between lost
and surviving photons lower S(E0F) enough to overcome Bob's entropy loss,
and compute that difference with all sectors and preparation costs. A
Fisher-information inequality, a success-conditioned state, or an
unvalidated amplifier entropy claim would not complete this step.

10. RARE MIXTURES ABOUT INFINITE-SUPPORT PURE SEEDS

This section records the distinct construction requested after the first
two family results were frozen. It does not claim a general no-go theorem.
The proposed positive mechanism would be:
  rho_epsilon=(1-epsilon)|psi><psi|+epsilon|phi><phi|,
  D(B_phi||B_psi)>D(E_phi||E_psi), both finite.
Because each conditional input is pure, Ic(rho)=chi_B-chi_E exactly. If both
relative entropies are finite, the right derivative at epsilon=0 is their
difference. Mere faithfulness of E_psi does not ensure finite relative
entropy; eigenvalue and tail estimates would still be required.

10.1 Exact complementary Gram operator

Use the loss-then-amplifier dilation already derived above. Its environments
are called C (loss) and F (amplifier). For normalized input Bargmann function
  f(z)=sum_n psi_n z^n/sqrt(n!),
the normalized global Bargmann wavefunction is
  Psi_f(z,x,y)=G^(-1/2) exp(gamma z y) f(alpha z+beta x),
  alpha=sqrt(eta)/G, beta=sqrt(1-tau), gamma=sqrt(b/G),
  b=(1-eta)nu, G=1+b, tau=eta/G.
Here z,x,y represent B,C,F. Thus the B=|m> Schmidt row is
  v_m(x,y)=[G m!]^(-1/2)
           [gamma y+(alpha/beta)partial_x]^m f(beta x).
E_psi=sum_m |v_m><v_m| is faithful precisely if these vectors have dense
linear span in the two-mode environmental Fock space. Infinite photon
support of f by itself does not imply that density.

For a two-mode Bargmann function h(x,y)=sum_l x^l h_l(y)/sqrt(l!), the
bilinear contraction with the global wavefunction is
  (T_f h)(z)=G^(-1/2)sum_l beta^l/sqrt(l!)
                       f^(l)(alpha z) h_l(gamma z).
The physical dark environmental ket has coefficients conjugate to those
of h. This conjugation does not affect its norm or the dark-mass formulas.

10.2 Explicit dark vectors; arbitrary phases do not cure the problem

Our first independently frozen dark vector was
  h(x,y)=x f(A y)-beta f'(A y), A=alpha/gamma.
Its contraction with T_f vanishes by the ordinary Wronskian identity.
It is normalizable if M(A^2) and M'(A^2) are finite, where
  M(u)=sum_n |psi_n|^2 u^n.

Agent E3 independently supplied the stronger entangled-multiplier family
  h_t(x,y)=exp(t x y)[x f(lambda_t y)-beta f'(lambda_t y)],
  lambda_t=A+beta t, |t|<1.
We checked the cancellation and derived its exact norm and its action on
every competing pure seed. The cancellation is
  T_g h_t = beta/sqrt(G) [g' f-g f']((alpha+beta t gamma)z),
so T_f h_t=0. For eta>b choose
  t_*=-beta/A,
  R=A^2-beta^2=(eta-b)/b,
  s_0=1-t_*^2=G(eta-b)/eta,
  kappa=(eta-b)/sqrt(eta).
Integrating the environmental x coordinate gives the exact positive norm
  ||h_t*||^2 = M(R)/s_0^2 + 4 beta^2 M'(R)/s_0.              (R1)
This depends only on the photon probabilities, regardless of their phases.
In particular,
  sum_n (n+1)R^n |psi_n|^2 < infinity
implies an exact nonzero dark environmental vector. The normalized vector
has probability under a distinct competitor phi with Bargmann g equal to
  c_h = beta^2/[G ||h_t*||^2]
        ||[g'f-g f'](kappa z)||_Fock^2.                    (R2)
The Wronskian of two entire functions is identically zero only if they are
proportional. Thus c_h>0 for every distinct pure competitor, whenever (R1)
is finite. Boundedness of the physical Stinespring contraction ensures the
norm in (R2) is finite; no separate formal-series assumption is needed.

For geometric photon probabilities p_n=(1-rho)rho^n, with arbitrary random,
quadratic, or cubic phases, this dark vector is normalizable whenever
  rho R<1.
At nu=1, eta=.75, R=2. Thus all rho<.5, whose mean photons are below one,
retain an exact dark direction. At general antidegradability boundary,
R=1+1/nu, so the geometric family with mean below nu is covered. This is a
statement about this specified photon distribution, not a bound based only
on mean energy: an arbitrarily small heavy tail can violate the moment.

As a numerical coefficient check, the support program contracts a random
degree-7 complex polynomial f with h_t* and with an independent polynomial
g. At eta=.76, nu=1:
  max |T_f h_t*| = 4.757327848411946e-17,
  max error against rescaled Wronskian = 5.231747341700053e-17.
The cancellation itself is exact analytic algebra; these values only check
its implementation in floating point.

10.3 Exact information penalty for the moment class

Let P be a projection onto an environmental dark direction of psi and
c=Tr(P E_phi)>0. Measuring {P,I-P} gives the binary channel with a zero
event probability for psi and probability c for phi. Therefore, exactly,
  chi_E >= h2(epsilon c)-epsilon h2(c).
If D_B=D(B_phi||B_psi)<infinity (in bits), the relative-entropy mixture
identity gives chi_B<=epsilon D_B, yielding the sharper bound
  Ic(rho_epsilon)
    <=epsilon D_B-h2(epsilon c)+epsilon h2(c)
    =-c epsilon log2(1/epsilon)+O(epsilon).

E3 subsequently supplied a strengthening which we checked: the finite-D_B
assumption can be removed. First every thermal-attenuator receiver output
is faithful for nu>0, eta<1. Indeed
  Phi_eta,nu=N_b o L_eta,
  N_b(sigma)=integral d^2z/(pi b) exp(-|z|^2/b) D(z)sigma D(z)^*,
and all weights are strictly positive. A zero expectation in a vector
would force it orthogonal to the Weyl orbit of every eigenvector of sigma,
contradicting irreducibility of the Weyl representation.

The general binary-Holevo lemma is
  supp(tau) subset supp(sigma)
    implies chi_p(sigma,tau)=o[p log2(1/p)],              (R3a)
even when D(tau||sigma)=infinity. To see this, take finite spectral
projections P_N of sigma on its support and normalized compressions
tau_N=P_N tau P_N/Tr(P_N tau), with trace distance e_N->0.
D(tau_N||sigma) is finite. The binary cq ensembles with rare states tau
and tau_N have trace distance p e_N; their H(X) agrees. Conditional entropy
continuity with the two-dimensional label X gives
  |chi_p(sigma,tau)-chi_p(sigma,tau_N)|
     <=2p e_N+(1+p e_N)h2(p e_N/(1+p e_N)).
Meanwhile chi_p(sigma,tau_N)<=p D(tau_N||sigma). Dividing by p log2(1/p)
and taking p->0 gives limsup<=e_N; then N->infinity proves (R3a).
The dimension-independent continuity bound used here is Winter, Lemma 2,
read live at https://arxiv.org/html/1507.07775v6 (2016). Its conditioning
system can be infinite dimensional; only the binary label is bounded.

Applying (R3a) to Bob and the exact dark leakage bound to Eve now gives
  Ic(rho_epsilon)
     <=-c epsilon log2(1/epsilon)
        +o[epsilon log2(1/epsilon)]<0                    (R3)
for all sufficiently small epsilon, for EVERY fixed distinct finite-energy
pure competitor phi in the specified seed moment class. This argument
requires no derivative of unbounded output entropy and no finite Bob
relative-entropy assumption. The rare-mixture no-go is stronger than the
first-order relative-entropy test alone.

10.4 An arbitrarily weak tail is not free regularization

Take an arbitrary nonfaithful pure seed psi_0 and an orthogonal tail chi,
  psi_delta=sqrt(1-delta^2)psi_0+delta chi.
Let P project onto ker E_psi0. The cross terms vanish exactly because
(I_B tensor P)|Psi_psi0>=0. Hence
  Tr(P E_psidelta)=delta^2 c_chi,
  c_chi=Tr(P E_chi).
For a fixed competitor phi with c_phi=Tr(P E_phi)>0, data processing gives
  D(E_phi||E_psidelta)
     >=d_binary(c_phi || delta^2 c_chi)
      =c_phi log2(1/delta^2)+O(1).                         (R4)
If c_chi=0, the relative entropy remains infinite. If c_chi>0, the bound
holds even if the tail happens to make E_psidelta faithful for every
delta>0. Whenever Bob's corresponding relative entropies stay bounded,
the desired strict advantage fails as delta tends to zero. Uniform
boundedness is immediate on a fixed finite physical output cutoff with
positive base spectrum; on the unbounded channel it is a separate tail
condition and has not been asserted from trace-norm convergence alone.

Competitors phi_delta whose old dark mass also tends to zero, or whose
energy/direction changes with delta, are not excluded by (R4). Their joint
entropy and approximation costs are the remaining construction problem.

10.5 The nonuniform input-cutoff limit is real, not a disproof

At rho R=1, finite geometric truncations have
  M_N(R)=Theta(N), M_N'(R)=Theta(N^2),
and the normalized dark-mass lower bound (R2) for phi=vacuum is Theta(N^-2).
At rho R>1, it is O([N(rho R)^N]^-1), up to nonzero fixed constants.
For example at eta=.75, nu=1, rho=.5:
  N=20: vacuum dark-mass lower bound .0009937888198757719;
  N=80: bound .000071396697902722;
  N=160: bound .000018290591776855.
At rho=.6, the same bounds are 8.18508e-5, 3.15165e-10, and 7.13157e-17.
The normalized dark vectors can escape weakly to infinity as N increases.
It would be invalid to infer an exact infinite-seed kernel simply because
every finite input truncation has one. Conversely, nonsingularity of any
finite environmental principal matrix does not prove an infinite Gram
operator injective. Neither inference was used.

For geometric tails, T_N=Prob(n>N)=rho^(N+1). Preparing the normalized
truncation changes a binary-input purification in trace distance by at most
sqrt(2 T_N). The reference dimension is at most two, so the coherent
information changes by at most
  2 epsilon+(1+epsilon)h2(epsilon/(1+epsilon)).
Resolving a putative gap gamma therefore requires a sufficient cutoff
  N=O(log(1/gamma)+log log(1/gamma)),
with factor 2/|log rho|. At rho R=1 and gamma of order epsilon_mixture,
such an N makes the particular dark penalty in (R3) only of order
epsilon_mixture/log(1/epsilon_mixture). Thus the finite-cutoff kernels do
not by themselves rule out a genuine infinite-seed first-order advantage.

For polynomial photon probabilities p_n=C(n+1)^(-s), s>2 gives finite
mean energy and evades every R>1 moment condition. Its tail satisfies
T_N<=C (N+1)^(1-s)/(s-1). A sufficient photon cutoff for gap gamma grows
as [log(1/gamma)/gamma]^(2/(s-1)), up to constants. Finite average energy
therefore does not mean a cheap maximum photon number or cheap precision.

For a normalized finite seed with N+1 coefficients, controlling each
complex amplitude to O(gamma/[sqrt(N) log(1/gamma)]) is sufficient for the
same rank-two continuity budget. For the cubic phase exp(i c n^3), a coarse
sufficient phase-parameter precision is O(gamma/[N^3 log(1/gamma)]).
These are preparation requirements, not a proposed hardware implementation.
For channel-parameter certification, truncate the thermal environment at
K=O(log(1/gamma)). Its tail is (nu/(nu+1))^(K+1). On input/environment
photon numbers N,K the beam-splitter generator norm is at most N+K;
away from eta=0,1, a sufficient eta precision is
O(gamma/[(N+K)log(1/gamma)]). The TMSV purification has derivative norm
1/[2 sqrt(nu(nu+1))], so fixed positive nu needs nu precision of order
gamma/log(1/gamma), up to constants. No free exact parameter oracle or
postselected energy cutoff is assumed.

10.6 Direct physical diagnostics of a non-Gaussian tail family

The extra program uses the exact Kraus operators to prepare finite pure
seeds
  psi_n proportional rho^(n/2) exp(i c n^3), 0<=n<=N,
and competitors vacuum or exp(i theta n)psi, with
  rho in {.25,5/12,.6,.8}, c in {.31,sqrt(2)},
  theta in {.3,pi}, epsilon in {.5,.1,.01,.001,1e-5}.
It keeps all receiver sectors up to M and sends every overflow to an
erasure flag. For each pair it evaluates S(B)-S(RB), where R is the true
two-dimensional purification label. No complementary-sector cutoff or
success-conditioned normalization is used.

At eta=.76, nu=1, none of the 120 stated tests was positive. Increasing
N,M from 28,50 to 80,130 gives best Ic=-2.8229535842250186e-6 bits at
rho=.25,c=.31,theta=.3,epsilon=1e-5. For balanced epsilon=.5, the best
value among the stated phases/shifts is:
  rho=.25:       -.02247784806493147 bits;
  rho=5/12:      -.03908324555997389 bits;
  rho=.6:        -.06273866042105491 bits;
  rho=.8:        -.106653261648741 bits.
Maximum N=28 to N=80 value changes at these rho are respectively
9.33e-15,9.65e-11,7.46e-7,5.02e-4 bits. These are finite diagnostic
negatives, not interval certificates, an optimization, a faithfulness

10.7 Precise remaining gap

No finite-energy pure seed with a proved faithful complementary Gram
operator was constructed, and no universal impossibility was proved.
Random phases, a slow photon tail, or positive finite principal determinants
are not adequate substitutes for that proof. Even a faithful seed would
still require finite and correctly ordered output relative entropies.

The current exact threshold remains UNKNOWN. The new information is a
charged obstruction to the proposed cheap rank regularization: the entire
exponential-moment class above has exact dark vectors for every phase, and
weak tail smoothing of a fixed seed/competitor incurs the logarithmic cost
(R4). Genuinely joint limits beyond those statements remain unclosed.
