E7 — Gaussian-past Fisher gate: endpoint-posterior cumulant reduction
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: new exact reformulation; conditional positivity remains open.

Scope

I used the sign-pattern lemma, adaptive25 entropy bridge, and Sol3 closure as
entry points. I did not edit their sources or run further parameter sweeps. The
calculation below is for the Gaussian-past bridge. It does not transfer the
actual curved-clock contact when the past coefficient is changed to a constant.

1. Exact posterior representation

On a constant plateau c>0, write its Gaussian-clock interval as [0,T], let
H_r(x)=exp(c U(r,x)), and let Y be the bridge endpoint. For r<T, put
Delta=T-r. The bridge marginal and its endpoint posterior are

  p_r(x) = Z^(-1) G_r(x) H_r(x),
  pi_(r,x)(dy) = Z_(r,x)^(-1) exp(-(y-x)^2/(2 Delta)) H_T(y) dy.

Set b=partial_x log H_r=c U_x(r,x). If m(x), V(x), kappa_3(x), and
kappa_4(x) are the posterior mean, variance, third cumulant, and fourth
cumulant under pi_(r,x), Gaussian differentiation gives

  b=(m-x)/Delta,
  b_x=(V-Delta)/Delta^2,
  b_xx=kappa_3/Delta^3,
  b_xxx=kappa_4/Delta^4.

The log-convexity of H_T follows from convexity of U(T,.); Gaussian convolution
preserves log-convexity. Thus b_x>=0 and A:=V-Delta=Delta^2 b_x>=0.

The Doob-bridge generator is L=partial_r+(1/2)partial_xx+b partial_x, and
Lb=0. Direct differentiation gives

  L(b^2)=b_x^2,
  L(b_x^2)=b_xx^2-2b_x^3,
  L(b_xx^2-2b_x^3)=b_xxx^2-12b_x b_xx^2+6b_x^4.

Since d E_[p_r] f / dr = E_[p_r] Lf, the plateau identities in posterior
cumulants are

  Q''(r) = c^(-2) Delta^(-6) E_[p_r][kappa_3^2 - 2 A^3],
  Q'''(r)= c^(-2) Delta^(-8) E_[p_r][kappa_4^2 - 12 A kappa_3^2 + 6 A^4].

So the Gaussian-past conditional gate is the following averaged statement:

  E_[p_r][kappa_3^2 - 2 A^3] <= 0
       ==> E_[p_r][kappa_4^2 - 12 A kappa_3^2 + 6 A^4] > 0.

This exposes the bridge gate as an inequality for posterior cumulants of the
terminal endpoint, with a nonnegative variance excess A. It is a more direct
conditional-variance formulation of the mixed Fisher derivative in
adaptive25_entropy_bridge.txt.

2. Pointwise SOS route fails even for an even convex Gaussian tilt

The integrand implication above is false pointwise, including when the local
second-derivative integrand is nonpositive. Take

  pi(dy) = p N(d,Delta) + (1-p) N(-d,Delta),  r=p(1-p).

Up to the common translation x, this is a posterior from the even convex
likelihood exp(phi(y))=cosh(a y): its component means are x+/-d with
d=a Delta, and p/(1-p)=exp(2 a x). Thus p varies with the observation x.
Direct cumulant calculation gives

  A=4 r d^2,
  kappa_3=8 r (1-2p) d^3,
  kappa_4=16 r (1-6r) d^4,
  kappa_3^2-2A^3 = 64 r^2(1-6r)d^6,
  kappa_4^2-12A kappa_3^2+6A^4
       =256 r^2(1-24r+90r^2)d^8.

At r=9/50, the target polynomial is negative, since
1-24r+90r^2=-101/250, while the contact integrand is also negative since
1-6r<0.
Thus neither a posterior-by-posterior implication nor an immediate pointwise
sum-of-squares proof can close the bridge gate. The example is only a generic
even convex Gaussian tilt; I have not shown that this likelihood is generated
by an admissible future Parisi profile from terminal |x|, and it is not a
counterexample to the target theorem.

3. Exact remaining gate and the contact-transfer obstruction

What remains is an averaged inequality for the Gaussian-past predictive law
p_r and its future profile H_T. The sign-pattern chord argument controls
E[H_r J_local] for an unconditional plateau-positivity claim after comparison
with Gaussian weights. It does not preserve the specified contact: replacing
the inherited past density by a Gaussian changes Q'' and the variational
minimum. Therefore it cannot turn the actual contact hypothesis into the
Gaussian-past conditional premise above.

The useful next theorem would need to exploit the joint x-dependence of
pi_(r,x), A(x), kappa_3(x), and kappa_4(x), together with the actual Parisi
future-profile constraints and the inherited density, to prove the averaged
strict inequality at the contact. The calculation here does not prove that
inequality or produce an admissible counterexample. It freezes the failure at
the averaged posterior-cumulant step rather than proposing numerical tuning.
