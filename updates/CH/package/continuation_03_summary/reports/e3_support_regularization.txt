E3: COMPLEMENTARY SUPPORT AND RARE-MIXTURE REGULARIZATION
2026-10-10. Internal analytic reconstruction; no external validation or novelty
claim. This supplements e3_closure.txt and does not change the unresolved
thermal-attenuator capacity boundary.

STATUS

No faithful finite-energy pure seed has been constructed here. No universal
nonfaithfulness theorem for all finite-energy pure inputs has been proved here.
The exact result is an enlarged family of normalizable dark vectors, with a
sharp domain criterion for that family. It rules out asymptotically rare pure
binary mixtures around many infinite-Fock non-Gaussian seeds, for every fixed
distinct finite-energy pure competitor. No finite receiver-relative-entropy
assumption is needed for that conclusion. A high-zero-density sigma-function
candidate also fails by a separate exact coherent-vector identity.

The central remaining obligation is to prove injectivity, or prove its
impossibility, for the full infinite Kraus-label map outside these analytic
domains. Even injectivity would not by itself establish finite relative
entropies or their desired sign above the anti-degradability boundary.

1. EXACT DILATION AND THE SUPPORT QUESTION

Take 0<eta<1, nu>0 and set

  b = 1-eta, G = 1+b nu, tau = eta/G, a = 1-tau,
  c = sqrt(eta)/G = sqrt(tau/G),
  d = sqrt((G-1)/G).

The thermal attenuator is A_G composed with L_tau. Let x be the loss ancilla,
y the amplifier ancilla, and z the receiver Bargmann variable. For normalized
pure input with Bargmann function

  f(w) = sum_{n>=0} psi_n w^n/sqrt(n!),
  sum |psi_n|^2 = 1,

the three-mode pure state is exactly

  Psi(z,x,y) = G^(-1/2) exp(d z y) f(c z+sqrt(a) x).       (1)

Its normalized environmental coefficient labels are |l,k>, l,k>=0, and its
Kraus operators obey

  (K_lk f)(z) = G^(-1/2) a^(l/2) d^k
                z^k f^(l)(c z)/sqrt(l! k!).              (2)

Let g(x,y)=sum_l x^l g_l(y)/sqrt(l!) be a two-mode Bargmann vector. Define the
linear coefficient map

  (T_f g)(z) = G^(-1/2) sum_{l>=0} a^(l/2)/sqrt(l!)
                         f^(l)(c z) g_l(d z).             (3)

The coefficient convention in (3) is bilinear: the physical contraction with
the environmental ket having coefficients conjugate to g is T_f g. Thus the
physical environmental density is the coefficient conjugate of T_f^* T_f;
faithfulness is exactly injectivity of T_f. The map T_f is Hilbert-Schmidt,
with squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm 1, so any genuine Bargmann vector in its
kernel is a genuine environmental support defect.

The two-vacuum-ancilla dilation here is minimal. Indeed suppose a square-
summable coefficient family w_lk gives sum w_lk K_lk=0. On input |0>, only
l=0 contributes, and the k terms have mutually orthogonal receiver outputs;
therefore every w_0k=0. Inductively, on input |n> all rows l<n have vanished,
and row l=n has nonzero amplitude G^(-1/2) a^(n/2) d^k in output |k>.
Consequently every w_nk=0. The contraction by any l2 vector is bounded by its
l2 norm, so this induction applies to infinite Kraus sums. A dark vector
depending on f therefore cannot be dismissed as dilation redundancy.

2. AN EXACT ENTANGLED-MULTIPLIER DARK VECTOR

For real |t|<1, put

  lambda_t = c/d+sqrt(a) t, C_t = d lambda_t.

The entire function

  g_t(x,y) = exp(t x y)
             [x f(lambda_t y)-sqrt(a) f'(lambda_t y)]    (4)

satisfies T_f g_t=0 whenever g_t belongs to the two-mode Bargmann space.
The square-integrability qualification is essential.

To check (4), for any entire F such that the relevant vectors belong to the
Bargmann domain, Taylor's formula gives

  T_f[exp(txy)F(y)] = G^(-1/2) F(dz) f(C_t z),
  T_f[x exp(txy)F(y)] = sqrt(a/G) F(dz) f'(C_t z).

The two terms in (4) cancel because lambda_t d=C_t. These identities are
also coefficient identities, and the boundedness of T_f justifies passage
from polynomial approximations whenever the vector is normalizable.

Set alpha_t=1-t^2. Integrating over x exactly by completing the square yields

  ||g_t||^2 = integral_C exp(-alpha_t |y|^2)
      { |f(lambda_t y)|^2
        +|t conjugate(y) f(lambda_t y)-sqrt(a) f'(lambda_t y)|^2 }
      d^2y/pi.                                         (5)

In particular the formal kernel need not be normalizable for a high-growth
entire f. Formal division, infinite Fock support, and finite input energy do
not resolve this domain issue.

Assume eta>G-1=b nu, which includes the target neighborhood of the supplied
anti-degradability boundary. Then c^2>a d^2, and the real choice

  t_* = -sqrt(a) d/c,
  alpha = 1-t_*^2,
  R = lambda_*^2/alpha = c^2/d^2-a
    = [eta-(G-1)]/(G-1),
  C = C_* = c alpha = [eta-(G-1)]/sqrt(eta)              (6)

minimizes lambda_t^2/(1-t^2) over real |t|<1. With

  M(r)=sum_{n>=0} |psi_n|^2 r^n,

the norm has the exact, phase-independent expression

  ||g_*||^2 = M(R)/alpha^2+4a M'(R)/alpha.              (7)

Thus the sufficient support-obstruction condition is

  sum_{n>=0} (n+1) R^n |psi_n|^2 < infinity.            (8)

For this particular optimized vector, (8) is also its exact normalizability
criterion. It is not a necessary condition for existence of some other dark
vector.

Derivation of (7): if I0, Iy, Id denote the weighted integrals of |f(lambda y)|^2,
|y f(lambda y)|^2, and |f'(lambda y)|^2 in (5), respectively, then

  I0=M(R)/alpha,
  Iy=[M(R)+R M'(R)]/alpha^2,
  Id=M'(R)/alpha,
  integral exp(-alpha|y|^2) conjugate(y)f(lambda y)
             conjugate(f'(lambda y)) d^2y/pi
       = lambda M'(R)/alpha^2.

Substitution gives M(R)/alpha^2 plus
alpha^(-1)[sqrt(a)-t lambda/alpha]^2 M'(R).
At t=t_*, t lambda/alpha=-sqrt(a), giving (7). Components originating from
different input Fock numbers have distinct environmental number differences,
so they are orthogonal. The norm formula therefore extends as a nonnegative
series, including divergent values, rather than relying on cancelling
infinite integrals.

Every nonzero f gives a nonzero g_*: for a y with f(lambda_* y) nonzero, the
bracket in (4) is a nonzero affine polynomial in x. Hence (8) gives genuine
nonfaithfulness on the minimal environment.

3. EVERY DISTINCT PURE COMPETITOR LEAKS THROUGH THIS DARK VECTOR

Let h be the Bargmann function of any other normalized pure input. Direct
substitution, now contracting g_* against the h dilation, gives

  T_h g_* = sqrt(a/G) [f h'-f' h](C z).                 (9)

Here C>0 under eta>G-1. The entire Wronskian in (9) vanishes identically if
and only if h is proportional to f. Proof: on a nonempty open set where f is
nonzero, the derivative of h/f vanishes; the analytic identity theorem then
gives h=constant times f everywhere.

Consequently, for every distinct pure competitor define

  q_h = ||T_h g_*||^2/||g_*||^2 >0.                    (10)

The normalized physical environmental vector conjugate to g_* is a zero-
probability outcome for the seed and a probability q_h outcome for the
competitor. It lies in ker sigma_E(f), not merely outside a chosen finite
truncation. This Wronskian consequence and (7) were independently cross-
checked with worker E1; no external review is implied.

For rho_p=(1-p)|f><f|+p|h><h|, finite input energy ensures finite receiver and
environment entropies. Since each ensemble component is pure, coherent
information is exactly chi_B(p)-chi_E(p). If

  D_B = D(sigma_B(h)||sigma_B(f)) < infinity,

Donald's relative-entropy identity and data processing by the two-outcome
environmental measurement imply

  chi_B(p) <= p D_B,
  chi_E(p) >= h2(p q_h)-p h2(q_h),
  I_c(rho_p) <= p D_B-h2(p q_h)+p h2(q_h).             (11)

All logarithms here are natural. Since

  h2(p q_h)=p q_h log(1/p)+O_h(p),

the upper bound in (11) is negative for all sufficiently small p, for each
fixed seed satisfying (8) and each fixed distinct competitor with finite D_B.
This supplies an explicit bound when D_B is finite. In fact that hypothesis
can be removed for the asymptotic sign, as follows.

3A. REMOVING THE FINITE RECEIVER-RELATIVE-ENTROPY ASSUMPTION

First, every receiver output of the thermal attenuator is faithful when
nu>0 and eta<1. The channel's characteristic function gives the exact
decomposition

  Phi_eta,nu = N_(b nu) composed with L_eta,
  N_t(omega)=integral_C exp(-|w|^2/t) D(w)omega D(w)^dagger d^2w/(pi t),

where L_eta is quantum-limited attenuation and D(w) is Weyl displacement.
If a nonzero receiver vector v had zero expectation in N_t(omega), the
strictly positive Gaussian weight and continuity would force
<v|D(w)|u>=0 for every w and every positive-eigenvalue eigenvector u of
omega. The Weyl orbit of any nonzero u spans the one-mode Hilbert space,
by Weyl irreducibility, a contradiction. This proves faithfulness without
making any statement about the finiteness of relative entropy.

Second, the following general binary-ensemble lemma holds:

  supp tau subset supp sigma
     implies chi_p(sigma,tau)=o[p log(1/p)] as p decreases to zero.  (11a)

To prove it, let P_N be increasing finite spectral projections of sigma on
its support, and tau_N=P_N tau P_N/Tr(P_N tau). Then
epsilon_N=||tau-tau_N||_1/2 tends to zero and D(tau_N||sigma)<infinity.
Thus chi_p(sigma,tau_N)<=p D(tau_N||sigma). The two binary cq states with
common prior (1-p,p) and rare states tau,tau_N have trace distance p epsilon_N.
The conditional-entropy continuity bound for the two-dimensional classical
label gives

  |chi_p(sigma,tau)-chi_p(sigma,tau_N)|
     <= 2p epsilon_N log 2 + g(p epsilon_N),
  g(x)=(1+x)h2[x/(1+x)].

Primary reference for this bound: Winter, arXiv:1507.07775v6, Lemma 2,
https://arxiv.org/html/1507.07775v6#S2. Its dimension dependence is only on
the finite label system, so the output space may be infinite-dimensional.
For fixed N, divide by p log(1/p) and let p decrease to zero: the limsup
is at most epsilon_N. Letting N increase proves (11a). This proof does not
assume finite D(tau||sigma) and does not exchange these two limits.

Apply (11a) to Bob's faithful seed output and combine with the environmental
measurement lower bound from (11). For every fixed seed satisfying (8) and
every fixed distinct finite-energy pure competitor,

  limsup_{p to 0} I_c(rho_p)/[p log(1/p)] <= -q_h <0.   (11b)

In particular I_c(rho_p)<0 for all sufficiently small p. This is the exact
fixed-seed rare-mixture obstruction claimed in STATUS. It is not uniform
over seeds or competitors varying with p. The continuity step and the Weyl
mixing argument were subsequently independently checked by worker E1.

4. GEOMETRIC TAILS WITH QUADRATIC OR CUBIC NUMBER PHASES

For the explicit infinite-Fock seed

  psi_n = sqrt(1-rho) rho^(n/2) exp(i theta_2 n^2+i theta_3 n^3),
  0<rho<1,

the input mean photon number is rho/(1-rho). Arbitrary real theta_2 and
theta_3 leave the moment and dark-vector norm unchanged. Equations (7)-(8)
hold if and only if rho R<1, with

  M(R)=(1-rho)/(1-rho R),
  M'(R)=(1-rho)rho/(1-rho R)^2.                         (12)

At the supplied anti-degradability value

  eta_AD=(2nu+1)/(2nu+2),
  R_AD=(nu+1)/nu,

so every such geometric seed with mean photon number strictly below nu has
this exact support defect. For nu=1 and eta=.75, R=2, t_*^2=1/6,
alpha=5/6, and C^2=1/3. Thus all rho<1/2 fail. This improves the t=0
two-row formal kernel criterion, which here only covers rho<5/12.

For the vacuum competitor, h=1, equation (9) gives

  ||T_vac g_*||^2 = (a/G)(1-rho)rho/(1-rho C^2)^2,      (13)

so even one explicit leakage probability is quantified by dividing (13)
by (7). At eta=.75, nu=1, rho=.3 the resulting lower bound is approximately
.0164609053498. This decimal is diagnostic; (7),(12),(13) are the exact
formulas. For rho R>=1 the vector (4) is nonnormalizable. This says nothing
about whether the actual complement is faithful in that regime.

The script support/e3_support_dark_vector.py checks the fixed Bargmann
coefficient cancellation for a geometric seed with nonzero quadratic and
cubic phases and checks norm-cutoff convergence. Its saved results are
support/e3_support_dark_vector_results.json. The largest checked receiver
coefficient residual for m=0,...,30 is about 4.6e-17; the truncated norm tends
to 5.04. These floating-point checks are not used in any infinite-domain
proof or as a capacity certificate.

5. A HIGH-ZERO-DENSITY CANDIDATE FAILS BY A DIFFERENT EXACT IDENTITY

A natural attempt to evade (8) uses an entire function near the maximal
Bargmann zero density. Let L>sqrt(pi), Lambda=L(Z+iZ), and take the square-
lattice Weierstrass sigma function sigma_Lambda, normalized in the Bargmann
norm. Set rho=pi/L^2 in (0,1). Its exact quasiperiodicity is

  f(w+L) = -exp[(pi/L)w+pi/2] f(w),
  f(w+iL) = -exp[-i(pi/L)w+pi/2] f(w).                 (14)

These formulas follow from the sigma quasiperiod formula and the Legendre
relation for the square lattice. Primary reference: NIST DLMF 23.2,
https://dlmf.nist.gov/23.2 (equations 23.2.14 and 23.2.15).

It follows directly from (14) that

  Q(w)=|f(w)|^2 exp(-rho|w|^2)

is bounded and doubly periodic, and is nonzero except at lattice zeros.
Consequently f belongs to Bargmann-Fock, has infinite Fock support, and has
every polynomial photon moment finite, because its norm and those moments
are controlled by integrals of a polynomial times exp[-(1-rho)|w|^2]. This
gives a genuine finite-energy non-Gaussian candidate at densities rho as
close to 1 as desired.

It also shows why the growth-domain problem is real. The zeros of f(lambda y)
have asymptotic number rho lambda^2 r^2+O(r) in a disk of radius r. Any nonzero
entire vector in the weighted Bargmann space with weight exp(-alpha|y|^2)
has zero density at most alpha, by Jensen's formula and the reproducing-
kernel pointwise bound. Multiplying by a nonzero entire function cannot
remove zeros. Thus if rho lambda_t^2>1-t^2, no nonzero multiplier can put
f(lambda_t y) in that weighted space. In particular rho R>1 defeats the
whole family (4) even with an additional entire multiplier h(y), as its first
positive term in (5) requires precisely such integrability. The equality
case is not claimed here.

Nevertheless this candidate has a different normalizable dark vector.
Put

  u=L/sqrt(a), v=pi c/(L d),
  g_sigma(x,y)=exp(u x)+exp(pi/2)exp(v y).              (15)

This is a sum of two ordinary unnormalized environmental coherent vectors,
so it belongs to the two-mode Bargmann space for every finite L. Equation
(3), followed by (14), gives

  T_f g_sigma = G^(-1/2)
      [f(cz+L)+exp(pi/2+pi c z/L)f(cz)] =0.            (16)

The vector is nonzero, with finite squared norm

  exp(u^2)+exp(pi+v^2)+2exp(pi/2).

Therefore even arbitrarily high lattice zero density does not establish
faithfulness. This is an exact failed ansatz, not evidence for a universal
no-faithful-seed theorem. Destroying quasiperiodicity could destroy (15), but
no proof excluding other infinite dark vectors is supplied.

6. EXACT CYCLIC-VECTOR REFORMULATION OF THE OPEN SUPPORT PROBLEM

Let xi_f(x,y)=f(sqrt(a)x), with the y mode in vacuum. Define the environmental
operator on its natural analytic-vector domain by

  L_env=(c/sqrt(a)) a_x+d a_y^dagger.

The receiver coherent slices of (1) satisfy

  exp(dzy) f(sqrt(a)x+cz) = exp(z L_env) xi_f.

Hence

  supp sigma_E(f)
     = closure span {L_env^m xi_f : m=0,1,...}.         (17)

All these powers are defined: for fixed complex z the coherent slice has
finite environmental norm, from the strict a<1 damping and a shifted
Bargmann pointwise bound. The coefficient comparison with (1) then gives
the receiver Fock-row vectors L_env^m xi_f/sqrt(m!).

Under eta>G-1,

  [L_env,L_env^dagger]=c^2/a-d^2>0.

A two-mode squeezing unitary conjugates L_env to sqrt(c^2/a-d^2) times
a_x tensor I_y. Thus faithfulness is precisely a cyclic-vector question
for an unbounded annihilation operator of infinite multiplicity, restricted
to the squeezed vector xi_f. Equation (17) neither proves nor disproves
existence of such a cyclic seed. Infinite rank, independence of finitely
many Kraus outputs, and positivity of every finite compression do not prove
density of this infinite cyclic orbit.

7. WHAT A SUCCESSFUL REGULARIZED DERIVATIVE WOULD STILL REQUIRE

Suppose for a fixed pure seed f and competitor h that both

  D_B=D(B_h||B_f)<infinity,
  D_E=D(E_h||E_f)<infinity.                            (18)

Then, writing B_p=(1-p)B_f+pB_h and E_p similarly, the exact identity is

  I_c(rho_p)=p(D_B-D_E)
               -D(B_p||B_f)+D(E_p||E_f).             (19)

For any pair sigma,rho with finite D(rho||sigma), Donald's identity gives
chi(p)/p<=D(rho||sigma), while the expression

  chi(p)=(1-p)D(sigma||sigma_p)+pD(rho||sigma_p)

gives chi(p)/p>=D(rho||sigma_p). Lower semicontinuity as sigma_p tends to
sigma therefore proves chi(p)/p tends to D(rho||sigma). Applying the first
identity again gives D(sigma_p||sigma)=o(p). Thus (18) rigorously yields

  I_c(rho_p)=p(D_B-D_E)+o(p).                          (20)

Faithfulness alone gives only support inclusion and does not give (18):
an infinite-dimensional logarithmic spectral tail can still make the
relative entropy infinite. Finite energy gives finite ordinary entropy
here, not automatically finite cross entropy against an arbitrary seed.
At and below anti-degradability, data processing forces D_B<=D_E whenever
these quantities are finite. Above it, failure of a degrading map alone
does not produce a pair with D_B>D_E.

There is also a precise singular cost for weakly adding a tail to a seed
that has an old dark projector P. Let chi be orthogonal to f and

  f_delta=sqrt(1-delta^2)f+delta chi.

Because P is in ker E_f, its probability under E_f_delta is exactly
delta^2 q_chi, where q_chi=Tr(P E_chi). For a fixed competitor with
q_h=Tr(P E_h)>0, measuring P gives

  D(E_h||E_f_delta)
     >= d_binary(q_h||delta^2 q_chi)
      = q_h log(1/delta^2)+O(1)                      (21)

if q_chi>0; if q_chi=0 the relative entropy is infinite. Thus an arbitrarily
weak tail cannot freely remove the support penalty for a fixed competitor.
This does not exclude a carefully coupled choice of seed, competitor, and
mixture probability; those quantifiers would require a new estimate.

REMAINING OBLIGATION / CONTINUATION GATE

The unresolved step is an explicit f with finite photon energy for which
T_f is injective on the full two-mode Bargmann space, or a proof that no such
f exists. A constructive approach must control all infinite environmental
vectors, not just annihilate the presently known kernel families. If such
a seed exists, a capacity mechanism additionally needs quantitative spectral
control proving (18) and an exact positive D_B-D_E, preferably uniformly for
every eta>eta_AD. No such sign proof is contained in this report.

All original amplifier EPnI claims remain frozen/unvalidated and were not
used. No source edits, external communications, or paid computations occurred.
