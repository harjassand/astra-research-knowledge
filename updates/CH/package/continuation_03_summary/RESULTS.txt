3. Strongest new internally reconstructed results

A. An exact environmental support obstruction, including infinite-support
non-Gaussian seeds. Set b=(1-eta)nu, R=(eta-b)/b, assume eta>b, and let
psi=sum_n psi_n|n> be a normalized pure state satisfying

   sum_n (n+1) R^n |psi_n|^2 < infinity.

An explicit normalizable vector lies in the kernel of its full environmental
output, and every distinct fixed pure competitor gives that vector positive
probability q>0. For finite-energy competitors, the binary mixture satisfies

 limsup_(p down to 0) Ic((1-p)psi+p phi,Phi)/[p log(1/p)] <= -q < 0.

The proof combines an exact Bargmann-space cancellation, a Wronskian
identity, positivity improvement of Bob's thermal-noise output, and a
finite-label entropy continuity argument. A finite receiver relative entropy
is not required in the strengthened version. Heavy photon tails outside the
moment condition, varying seeds/competitors, and general collective codes
remain outside this theorem. It is a restricted obstruction, not zero capacity.
Details: reports/e3_support_regularization.txt and e1_construction.txt.

B. An arbitrary-code resource lower bound near antidegradability. For nu=1,
a deterministic m-use qubit code with total mean input photon number E and
entanglement fidelity Fe obeys

 Fe <= 3/4 + |arccos(sqrt(eta))-pi/6| sqrt(3E+m).

Thus fixed per-use energy and target fidelity above 3/4 require blocklength
growing at least quadratically in 1/(eta-.75). For example eta=.751,
E<=10m, Fe>=.99 requires m>=1392 under the report's rounding. This does not
imply zero asymptotic capacity above .75. Details: reports/e4_adversarial.txt.

C. Constructive collective-code interfaces. A valid index-two defect in a
symplectically selfdual GKP lattice gives an ideal qubit repetition family
with the explicit lower bound

   Ic(n) >= R_lat^n/(2 ln 2) - B_lat^n.

Here R_lat is a parity-posterior second moment and B_lat is a classical
Bhattacharyya overlap, both explicitly defined torus integrals. Certifying
R_lat>B_lat would give an explicit positive ideal block. The tested square,
rectangular, and correlated examples did not meet the target. No efficient
decoder or complete finite-energy transfer near the target was proved.
Details: reports/e2_alternative.txt and e2_support/index_two_defect.txt.

A separate exact formula obtains Eve's pure-pair Chernoff function from
Bob's output and cross operators. This permits reference-sensitive tests
without pretending that Eve's coherent information is classical. Direct
finite-envelope tests found no new positive region.

D. A tractable local Fock experiment. Fixed finite priors in a large-Fock
scaling reduce to Bob observing rU+standard normal noise and Eve observing
U+standard normal noise plus coherent states with squared overlap
exp[-nu(nu+1)(u-v)^2]. The finite-prior transfer is internally derived using
exact Gram matrices and finite-label entropy tail bounds. No uniform
growing-alphabet or superposition transfer is claimed.

For a Gaussian prior of variance V the limiting difference is exactly
  0.5 log[(1+r^2 V)/(1+V)]
   -g((sqrt(1+4nu(nu+1)V/(1+V))-1)/2).
The coordinator proved that its positivity threshold is precisely the usual
thermal entropy threshold, using concavity of exp(2g(n(t))). This prevents
claiming an unearned gain from Gaussian modulation. Tiny positive numbers
in a coarse numerical prior scan disappeared under quadrature refinement;
they were not treated as evidence of a capacity improvement.
