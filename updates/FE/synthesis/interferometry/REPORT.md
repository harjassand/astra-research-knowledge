2. **A conservative N791 transfer.** With ALL original intended echo/residual,
   full-support reset-or-twirl, coherent control and state/history-uniform error

   failure. This count still has N791's conditional binomial lower-tail bound

   discarding losses can instead return a false zero-norm certificate. Actual
   molecular fringes are not identified with that intended echo by positivity
   alone; the packet does not supply N791's other physical inputs.
3. **Separate branch channels are insufficient.** Two exact identity molecular
   channels can have full shared-environment fringe 1 or exp(-2 gamma t),
   depending on environmental branch marking. This confirms the packet's
   cross-block prescription and rules out filling its coherence gate by two
   separately fitted molecular master equations. This limitation is established
   prior art [Oi](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0303178), not a new principle.
4. **Atomic common-mode mixing is not a scalar contrast.** An encoded-cat toy
   gives Re L unchanged but Im L multiplied by cos(chi). A blank visibility
   calibration can miss that distortion. No chi is inferred from a static
   polarizability. Exact equal-intensity matching still prevents a purely
   intensity-only background from creating an order-odd blank signal; this
   countermodel concerns calibration and false negatives, not such a false
   positive without an additional mismatched or phase-sensitive mechanism.
5. **Finite optical replicas need their own interface.** For real unequal beam
   amplitudes exchanged between patterns, counterpropagating pulse envelopes
   give instantaneous intensity difference proportional to
   |G(t-x/c)|²-|G(t+x/c)|². Time-integrated fluence at fixed x still matches.
   The ideal carrier-field identity therefore needs measured envelope/phase
   qualification before use as an instantaneous apparatus-background bound.
6. **Fourth connected response does not identify anharmonicity.** A mixture of
   two purely harmonic Gaussian sectors has nonzero fourth log-overlap
   derivative. This turns the source's rotational-mixture warning into a precise
   future false-positive control for the proposed nonlinear-response target.

## Acquisition and decisive next measurement

The source's 25/(C R)² estimate is conservative variance-based five-sigma
planning for two equally sampled independent completed Bernoulli settings. It
is not an exact finite-sample guarantee, an attempted-shot budget or a rate
measurement. At its C=.52,R=.03 scenario it gives 102,729 completed shots and
34.243 minutes at an assumed 50 Hz. Controls, reset preparation, switching,
rejection, chemical identity checks, calibration and replacement add cost.

For independent irreversible loss q over N shots, the exact 90% survival
ceiling is q<=1-.9^(1/N), not exactly -log(.9)/N. At N=102,729 the difference
is only 5.26e-13 absolute; the source's latter expression is an appropriate
first-order planning approximation. At least 2,995,731 failure-free independent
trials are required to exclude q>=1e-6 at 95% one-sided confidence. That is
evidence to acquire, not a qualified apparatus survival rate. Dependence/drift
needs a separate model.

The static differential Ca+ polarizability -44.32(32) atomic units is correctly
identified as a static quantity in [Huang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07828).
It does not predict the dynamic/tensor 2.7-micrometre phase. The known standing-
wave atomic control experiment [Ricci Vasquez et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.02597v2)
also does not qualify ultrafast molecular waveform stability or this readout.

The next measurement should acquire the full pulse-sequence joint law:
both ancilla quadratures, accepted/attempted counts, survival/identity, temporal
envelopes/spectral phase, programme swap, phase randomization, common-branch,
individual-pulse, atomic-background/off-resonant and beyond-overlap delay
controls. Its target is the controlled molecular order-odd probability
difference after actual backgrounds, with complete trial and systematic-error
costs. The packet's illustrative .015 signal/.0015 systematic gate can be
retained as a declared planning target. It is not achieved. If contrast or
survival fails, no theorem here repairs it by assigning a longer lifetime.
