Abstract
The strongest result developed in this campaign is a structural classification, not a new
capacity or Hamiltonian undecidability claim. For finite-dimensional channels, complete
dominance for any single finite-at-zero operator-convex standard Petz divergence with a
nonzero interior resolvent component is equivalent to complete SLD dominance. A coherent
auxiliary reference localizes the generator’s representing measure at one chosen support point.
At the pure quadratic endpoint, a different elementary operation compresses every violation:
reference dimension at most1 +b2 suffices when the target channel has effective output
dimensionb, improving to1 +v for a target POVM withv outcomes. Consequently quadratic
comparison is exactly decidable on rational POVMs. These two proofs do not require Dots’
nonlocal-game reduction. If that reduction is valid, they give a sharp decidable/coRE-
complete boundary, including Petz Rényi parameter2 versus every fixed parameter in(0, 2).
The manuscript received targeted adversarial reconstruction, and one complex coordinate
convention needs correction. Its hardness is retained as a named dependency, not promoted to
an independently certified theorem. No result here establishes historic 9–10/10 significance.
Read first. The complete mathematical arguments are in Sections 1–3. Section 4 records the
source reduction’s exact status and correction; Sections 5–7 give the consequences, falsification
results, and research decision. Every claim is either proved below, explicitly inherited, or marked
conditional/unresolved. The proofs were developed and cross-checked by AI agents in this
campaign. Agreement, compilation, and finite numerical checks are not external or formal
certification. The attached evidence archive preserves the independent investigations, diagnostics,
literature receipts, and unchanged source PDF.
Computational interface. An input measurement channel is a finite list of rational complex
Hermitian effects summing to the identity. “Strictly positive” means each effect is positive
definite. Physical dimension and outcome count may grow with the encoded machine. References
are arbitrary finite-dimensional untouched quantum systems, and their states/matrices are
universally quantified, not supplied as bounded auxiliary input. State pairs may have unequal
reference marginals. Exact decision and fixed-additive numerical approximation are distinct
tasks. No polynomial-time construction is asserted when a succinct nonlocal game is expanded
to its explicit rational table.

3 The resulting classification and its exact dependency
Corollary 2(Finite-at-zero classification). Fix a continuous operator-convex generator on[0,∞),
and compare its standard modular Petz divergence after two channels, on every faithful state pair
and every finite untouched reference. Modulo affine terms:
1. If the generator is zero, comparison is trivial.
2. If it is a positive pure quadratic, comparison has the finite cutoff of Theorem 3; it is
decidable for rational strictly positive POVMs.
3. If it has nonzero interior resolvent mass, comparison is exactly complete SLD order by
Theorem 1.
Assuming Dots’ claimed complete-SLD hardness reduction is correct, the third case iscoRE-
complete on the identical rational strictly positive POVM input class. Cases 1–3 exhaust the
stated generator class.
Proof. The representation and its affine normalization were proved above. Affine divergences
give the same constants at both outputs. A positive quadratic multiple induces preciselyD2
order, and all remaining functions have nonzero interior measure. The identity map on the two
input POVM lists transfers the manuscript’s SLD predicate to each fixed generator in case 3.
Membership and hardness are inherited from that equivalent predicate; no numerical evaluation
or effective reconstruction of the fixed representing measure is needed. This last conclusion
assumes the named hardness theorem, whereas the classification of preorders does not.

Finite certificates. For a rational POVM pair, failure of SLD order at a fixed reference
dimension is real algebraic and decidable. Strict violations have rational approximants, so
unrestricted failure is recursively enumerable. More generally, in an effective hierarchy of decidable
fixed levels, a total decision procedure for universal validity is equivalent to a computable sufficient
level cutoff: given a decision procedure, output 1 on valid instances and otherwise search decidable
levels until finding the first failure; conversely, test all levels up to the cutoff. Thus, conditional
on the hardness reduction, case 3 admits no computable universal cutoff or sound recursively
enumerable certificate system complete for its positive instances. If both validity and failure
were recursively enumerable the predicate would be decidable. This is an exact computability
obstruction, not an efficiency lower bound.
Why the two operations differ. For the quadratic form, normalization by the reference
marginal makes the comparison operator-valued. A negative direction can be retained by storing
the finitely many target inverse-action vectors. Compression preserves the actual physical domain
and lowers the source’s variational costs. In the SLD case, an off-diagonal tangent produces
a two-sided Sylvester inverse, represented on two reference factors. A joint compression need
not preserve their separate spatial tensor structure. The boundary investigation supplied an

not say that they take the same numerical values, that a reference can be prepared cheaply, or
that one channel physically simulates the other. The cutoff gives a finite exact algorithm only at
the quadratic endpoint and gives no practical complexity guarantee.

6 Falsification and priority assessment
Strongest mathematical attacks. The localization proof was tested against a purely
quadratic generator, a single atomic representing measure, diffuse and singular measures, rank-
deficient numerators, and tail integrability. The quadratic Hessian is killed exactly byFm(0) = 0,
so it cannot falsely produce SLD from the endpoint. The support-point exponential tilt handles
singular measures; the stated finite- at-zero condition justifies numerator regularization. Genera-
tors infinite at zero remain outside the theorem. Independent fixed-parameter derivations check
the signs on both sides ofα = 1; the Umegaki case uses its own nonzero measure rather than a
vanishing power-Hessian formula.
The explicit rational instrument above also establishes strict endpoint separation for general
channels without invoking undecidability. The cutoff proof was attacked at normalization, trace
gauge, common compression, target preservation, source decrease, and faithful restoration. Its
crucial inequality follows from the elementary inverse variational principle, not an unjustified
equality after compression. A noncommuting numerical instance compressed reference dimension
11 to 3: target quadratic errors were below5.7× 10−14 and both source terms decreased. This
is a finite diagnostic only. The general Hessian formula was also checked on 50 random finite
matrix instances with maximal relative discrepancy below8× 10−16, and the Chernoff flag on
ten instances. Exact symbolic checks support separate domain and packing calculations. None
replaces the displayed universal proofs.
Closest primary literature. The 2022 complete less-noisy framework already identifies
unrestricted reference relative-entropy comparison and tensorizes it [3]. Hirche’s October 2026
preprint proves the coherent-qubit SLD-to-entropy direction [4, Theorem 3.2, Proposition 3.5]; the
reverse from a single preassigned modular Petz generator is the relevant proposed addition here.
The earlier Astra operational note proves simultaneous all-generator comparison, whose converse
simply selects a resolvent; that is weaker than recovering SLD from each one fixed generator.
Hirche–Tomamichel’s coefficient collapse concerns their hockey-stick-integral divergences, a
different definition [5, Proposition 4.8]. Petz sufficiency/recovery results compare equality in
data processing for one channel, a different quantifier pattern [2]. The endpoint’s qualitative
decidability can also be reconstructed from Volčič’s quantitative rational Positivstellensatz [8,
Theorem 5.2, Remark 5.3]: normalized positive baselines form a monic free spectrahedron,
Hermitian tangents are unrestricted free variables, and the operator-valued RLD difference is
rational with positive denominators on the strictly positive POVM domain. Thus a generic
computable cutoff is already a consequence of that prior machinery. The elementary1 +b2 and
1 +v bounds, and the combined classification, are the more specific potential additions; their
exact historical priority remains under review. No matching predecessor for those precise claims
has been located in the focused search; that is not proof of originality.

7 Significance and the next indispensable gate
The strongest defensible outcome is a potentially original structural theorem with a sharp
computational endpoint, supported by complete internally reconstructed proofs. The classification
is broader than a specialist parameter extension: it explains why an entire operator-convex
class induces the same complete comparison and why one exceptional endpoint permits finite
certification. Conditional on Dots’ core, it puts exact decidability and coRE-completeness
arbitrarily close in a generator family.
There is currently no basis for rating this a civilization-changing 9–10/10 discovery. The
hardness derives from established MIP*=RE machinery; no new computational power, major
independently important conjecture resolution, or capacity/physical no-go transfer has been
completed. Priority and independent validation could substantially change the assessment. Even
if all claims survive, the evidence presently supports a substantial mathematical research result,
not historic significance.
The next indispensable gate is narrow: specialist proof reconstruction of Theorem 1’s
Hessian/localization and Theorem 3’s endpoint, an exact predecessor check, and validation of
Dots’ shared spatial-domain-to-global-POVM bridge with the conjugation convention corrected.
A stronger physical claim additionally needs a complement- or locality-preserving compiler.
Those gaps are explicit; they were not filled by inference from shared channels or by numerical
checks.
