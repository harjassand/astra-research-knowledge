RATES, HESSIANS, DEGENERACY, PARALLEL SADDLES AND LOOPS

For table minimum i and saddle a, E denotes energy, S the log product of
positive Hessian eigenvalues, and o the point-group/isometry order. The
pinned PyGT code implements

  k_(j<-i,a) = (o_i/o_a)/(2*pi)
                * exp[(S_i-S_a)/2 - beta*(E_a-E_i)].

The equilibrium masses and corresponding symmetric conductances are

  w_i = exp[-beta*(E_i-E0) - (S_i-S0)/2] / o_i,
  c_a = exp[-beta*(E_a-E0) - (S_a-S0)/2] / o_a.

Here c omits the common 1/(2*pi). Hence k_ji = sum_a c_a/w_i/(2*pi),
nu_HTST = C/(Z*2*pi), and MFPT_HTST = 2*pi*N/C. No additional 2*pi factor
is introduced into w. The ordinary audit uses E0,S0 from ID 1. The shared
Decimal verifier uses the global minimum E and a smaller common Hessian
shift; its w and c are therefore multiplied by the same constant. MFPTs and
normalized flux are invariant under this change. Raw capacities/partition
sums from differently shifted files must not be compared directly.

PyGT ignores the three tabulated inertia fields in this rate formula. The
calculation reproduces its harmonic unimolecular HTST convention in reduced
Lennard-Jones units; no experimental rate or model uncertainty is certified.

There are 616 same-index saddle rows. Actual PyGT/io.py:188 filters them out
before rate construction; the later self-loop multiplication at line 223 is
unreachable for these rows. The benchmark matches that behavior. There are
2,640 pairs with multiple saddle records (maximum 30, 3,135 excess records).
All their rate/conductance contributions are summed. No exact duplicate
eight-field TS rows or six-field minimum rows occur. Distinct saddle records
are not removed merely because they share endpoint IDs. Every endpoint ID
is in range, and no negative saddle-to-endpoint barriers were found. These
table checks do not establish that geometrically near-duplicate saddles are
distinct physical pathways; no all-TS coordinate identity audit was run.

Direct per-saddle directional HTST construction and the reversible c/w
construction agree within 5.11e-15 relative at T=0.2 and 6.22e-15 at T=0.1.
The unmodified pinned PyGT loader was actually executed on all tables at
beta=5. It retains 71,887 nodes and 239,706 directed nonzeros, and agrees with
the independent loader within 4.66e-15 for rates and 6.77e-15 for normalized
mass ratios. Its loader time was 28.7606 s; this slow grouping implementation
is not imposed on the stronger classical pipeline or used to inflate the
competitive comparison.

CLASSICAL ALGORITHM AND COMPLETE COST

The standard reversible positive elimination removes k with d=sum_j c_kj:

  c_ij <- c_ij + c_ik*c_kj/d,
  w_j  <- w_j  + w_k*c_kj/d.

Both updates are nonnegative and symmetric conductances are written from
one computed value. The sparse phase uses a dynamic minimum-degree heap,
switching at 500,000 current undirected edges to a bounded dense core. For
1<->7 it removes 67,864 nodes, leaving 4,023; no 100,000-by-100,000 or
71,887-by-71,887 dense matrix is allocated. The dense phase uses classical
positive symmetric rank-one star-mesh updates, shrinking storage in blocks.
All original component masses are transferred, not discarded.

At two retained states, c_st is capacity C, the final source mass is N_st,
and the two retained masses sum to the original component Z. Back-
substitution supplies both full potentials L_grounded^-1 e_s and
L_grounded^-1 w, using the stored elimination probabilities and positive
Poisson offsets. The shared verifier then reads every original saddle and
mass with outward Decimal coefficient intervals. Its theorem/software
correctness is conditional, not a formal proof of the implementation.
