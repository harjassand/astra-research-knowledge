The salt proposal has not acquired a selectively reactive, load-bearing, reversible NH4SCN interface. The primary salt experiment establishes a bulk hydrostatic dissolution/precipitation response. It does not establish the nonhydrostatic contact response needed by the supplied proposal. Its cooling-cycle capacity and efficiency are design/model results, distinct from the directly measured temperature response. Source: Zhang et al., Extreme barocaloric effect at dissolution, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10013-1 ; acquired helper main page, Extended Data Fig.7/8 captions and Supplementary Video 2 description; helper SI inspected. Original helper bytes are retained in sources/salt_helper_main.html and salt_helper_si.pdf.

One intervention changes the exposed common-solution assumption: export the dissolved inventory rapidly into a physically isolated reservoir, then close the flow path before warm regeneration. Pumps and valves are obtainable. The required simultaneous material response is not. Export can compete with growth on the original exposed faces, and isolation does not remove supersaturation or nuclei already exported. No same-platform NH4SCN loaded-contact data were acquired showing a usable interval between dissolution and competing precipitation. This intervention therefore remains an experimentally unresolved escape, not an acquired operation. No coating, calcite kinetics or assumed selectivity is credited.

The decisive test should be smaller than a refrigerator. On one NH4SCN specimen at a fixed cold temperature, apply a specified contact stress and pulse the solution flow into a separately sampled reservoir. Vary residence time only. Record contact displacement, actual wetted area, force, exported dissolved inventory, precipitation everywhere, calorimetric heat and pump/actuator work. After the cold pulse, take the exported inventory through the proposed warm regrowth stroke and record restored mass, geometry and solution composition. Exposed-face, zero-stress and inert mechanical controls are required to interpret this intervention. These are proposed measurements, not reported experiments.

Two necessary gates reject apparent successes early. The exported composition must exceed the measured warm-regrowth equilibrium threshold. In a restricted constant-enthalpy cold-stroke model,

  Qc = Delta_h_diss * n_retained - W_contact - E_other_cold.

With leading contact work W_contact ~ sigma*v_s*n_gross, positive cooling requires

  n_retained/n_gross > (sigma*v_s + E_other_cold/n_gross)/Delta_h_diss.

All quantities must be measured in that same material and regime. The enthalpy/work approximation cannot replace the calibrated first-law balance. If regrowth consumes salt elsewhere, fails to restore the load-bearing geometry, or requires an external separator, that state/resource enters the cycle boundary. Rapid visible retreat alone is not a pass. This condition extends the decision to finite-rate exports without claiming a universal impossibility theorem for all selective interfaces.

A genuinely different coupling already acquired by others provides a useful existence/control benchmark. Hsiau et al. mechanically couple a heat-actuated TiNi film to a superelastic TiNiFe refrigerant film and switch their heat contacts. External-source operation uses a roughly 130 C source and produces a 2.2 K unloaded device span. It demonstrates the coupling on one platform. It is published prior art, not an Astra invention. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41560-026-02122-6 ; main Methods and Figs.4-5; sources/heat_main.html. KITopen repository https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000196844 was also acquired and inspected; it supplies metadata, not an additional experimental dataset or public control code.

Its complete input boundary falsifies a consequential efficiency interpretation of this prototype. The SI reports external-source cooling of 2.09 mW at zero lift and 1.27 mW at a cold-side drop of 0.5 K below ambient. Including the two positioning actuators gives zero-lift electrical COP 3.17e-4. Thus the reported accounted input is approximately 6.59 W, of which about 6.22 W is positioning. The authors' auxiliary integral counts loading/unloading intervals; controller/idle power is not established as a complete wall-plug measurement. The analogous Joule configuration gives 2.79 mW and COP 6.69e-4, implying about 4.17 W accounted input. Work recovery is not implemented; the higher recovery COPs are theoretical cooling-subsystem bounds. Source: heat_si.pdf, PDF pp.4-6 and 30-32 (SI Note 2, Tables 3-4). These source pages were visually checked. Calculations are in calculate.py and calculation_results.json.

Source spreadsheets were actually read, not merely downloaded: heat_fig4.xlsx, Fig4d!D3:D5 gives 0.0853, 0.0327 and 2.61 for the thermal-input full system, actuator efficiency and cooling subsystem respectively. Their factorization agrees within rounding. heat_fig5.xlsx, Fig5d!D2:D10 contains the three external-source temperature/span/specific-power replicates. Specific power uses only 0.630 mg active refrigerant. It must not be scaled as though fixtures, positioning or heat-source losses were absent.

The thermodynamic boundary is also decisive. For a restored three-reservoir cycle,

  Qc*(T0/Tc - 1) <= Qs*(1 - T0/Ts) + W_electric + exergy_other.

Waste heat has value only through its temperature and delivered heat flux. Without a source above ambient, supplied work or another consumed resource, a periodic system cannot extract positive heat from below ambient. A hotter processor could in principle help cool another colder region. That does not supply autonomous below-ambient cooling from a single equal-temperature body. The demonstrated external-source film result must retain its hot-source and positioning inputs.

The larger measured alternative does not license splicing. Zhou et al.'s compressive NiTi device is a real regenerative cooling platform. Its SI Table 3 reports 177.5 W at Tc=287 K, Th=297 K, 950 MPa and 3.5 Hz using nanofluid, with calculated COP 0.9 under the paper's ideal work-recovery boundary. Its 1284.1 W point is at zero lift. SI Fig.13 explicitly records functional fatigue through two million cycles. Those cycles correspond to only about 6.61 days at 3.5 Hz, not a service lifetime. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08549-9 ; sources/kw_si.pdf, PDF pp.17,26-27. The locally acquired main page is subscription preview; full Methods were available through the primary publisher's indexed text, not a locally acquired full article. No full electric COP at this 10 K duty is asserted here.

The recent high-force work-recovery experiment uses disc-spring assemblies as stand-ins, not a coupled refrigerator. Its four-assembly data give 68.2% recovery at 0.0416 Hz, 56.3% at 1 Hz, and approximately 40% at 2 Hz. The data do not grant that recovery at the cooling device's 3.5 Hz or changing temperature-dependent NiTi load curves. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73314-7 ; main experimental section; drive_data.xlsx, Figure 6!P3:AD11. The arithmetic was independently recomputed from loading, unloading and shaft work. The paper's control code is not publicly deposited. No request was sent.

Incumbent comparison remains a real gate. For the proposed salt operation, no measured Tc, Th, delivered Qc, restoration work, life or electric COP exists, so no matched refrigeration advantage can be computed. At the measured milliwatt film scale, the proper electric control is a commercial thermoelectric cooler with the identical cold load, sink, package heat leaks and positioning boundary; a heat-driven control must additionally share source temperature and heat flux. At industrial scale, a complete efficient vapour-compression chiller is the relevant control. For context only, GEA's current BluGenium 900 rating is 860 kW, EER 5.98 at chilled water 12->6 C and condenser water 30->35 C, https://www.gea.com/en/products/chillers/gea-blugenium-compact-piston-chiller/ . Those conditions differ from all points above and are not used for a performance ratio. Ammonia also prevents treating absence of fluorocarbon refrigerant as sufficient advantage.

Matched comparison must include all electrical and thermal inputs, sensible-heat regeneration, pressure recovery actually implemented, pumps, valves, hot/cold exchangers and fans, containment, preparation, replacement, chemical loss, damage and lifetime accepted cooling. The first useful spend would be the single salt export/regrowth discriminator, only if that interface question merits investigation. The acquired sources do not justify another device design tranche. Physical validation, comparative advantage and historical novelty remain unestablished.
