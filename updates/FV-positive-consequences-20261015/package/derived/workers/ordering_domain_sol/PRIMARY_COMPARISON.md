# Primary comparison and significance decision

This is a scoped comparison as of 2026-10-15, not historical clearance. The main proof is self-contained. Source bytes and hashes are recorded under `sources/`.

1. Paul Busch and Teiko Heinosaari, *Approximate Joint Measurements of Qubit Observables*, arXiv:0706.1415v2 (2008), [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/0706.1415v2). Section 4.1, equation (39) and Proposition 4 give the compatibility criterion for unbiased qubit effects. Equation (21) in this worker's proof is an established unsharp joint-measurement family. Neither the qubit compatibility criterion nor the finite sign measurement is an originality claim here. The inspected theorem concerns measurement compatibility and approximation, rather than optimizing the variance of a fixed sharp MH characteristic operator multiplied by two positive scalar additive-noise factors.

2. Partha Patra and A. K. Pan, *Margenau-Hill distribution as a Necessary and Sufficient Signature of Measurement Incompatibility*, arXiv:2609.37144v1 (2026), [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37144v1). Theorem 2 and equations (12)–(18) identify positivity of the unsharp dichotomic MH effects with compatibility; Theorem 1 uses the anticommutator spectrum and finite-dimensional Jordan blocks. Their equation (13) is exactly the finite effects (21) used in the current proof. The independent-noise reduction lands in that prior family. The present central-operator proof retains arbitrary Hilbert space without a discrete block decomposition, but this extension alone is not asserted to be major or historically new. The scalar shift-constrained variance optimization is a different reporting problem.

3. Arpita Ghosh, Tim Roughgarden and Mukund Sundararajan, *Universally Utility-Maximizing Privacy Mechanisms*, [author-hosted primary paper](https://timroughgarden.org/papers/priv.pdf), and [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2841). The two-sided geometric mechanism and its strong utility guarantees for count queries are established machinery. Its adjacent-count likelihood ratio is the same fixed-shift type of classical constraint appearing in (11). The present proof gives an elementary full variance/equality argument for arbitrary real-valued measures by decomposing into residue classes; it does not claim invention of geometric optimal noise. Exact subsumption of that real-valued lemma by existing general mechanisms was not exhaustively checked.

4. Quan Geng and Pramod Viswanath, *The Optimal Mechanism in Differential Privacy*, arXiv:1212.1186v3 (2013; later journal version 2016), [primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.1186), [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.1186v3). Their continuous additive-noise optimization and staircase laws are an important comparator. The present constraint is only one fixed shift, whereas the usual real-valued sensitivity constraint compares every shift in an interval. That distinction allows lattice-supported minimizers here. The continuous staircase theorem is not used as a proof premise, and a continuous-density restriction would change the attainment question.

Targeted searches combining Margenau–Hill, geometric noise, variance, additive scalar convolution and differential privacy did not locate the exact product-variance/equality theorem stated here. Negative search results do not establish novelty.

The strongest surviving conclusion is an exact quantitative limitation with a usable finite-output error budget: the independent scalar MH contract has variance product at least the squared commutator norm, attained by geometric laws; removing their redundant classical magnitudes halves both added variances. This does not make the regularization a better quantum measurement. It also does not reject the broader mathematical theorem: the main uncontrolled frontier is the dependent-noise cost problem, which the qualitative FU theorem does not solve.

No evidence here justifies promoting this branch as an exceptional foundational discovery or a new physical/computational capability. Preserve the theorem as a precise reporting-cost result. Any further large investment should be tied to a consequence beyond the known unsharp measurement and classical-noise optimization ingredients.
