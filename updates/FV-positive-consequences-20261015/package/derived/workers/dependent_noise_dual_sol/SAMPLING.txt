NUMERICAL GUIDANCE AND FAILURES -- NOT PROOF

The main proof is reconstructed by verify_exact.py using only exact
rational arithmetic. None of the numerical assertions in this file are
needed by that verifier.

1. Initial constrained nonlinear search (search.py / search_results.json)

SciPy SLSQP was applied to D4-symmetric finite tables with a geometric
infinite exterior. Many runs returned failure and violated positivity;
some reported apparent objectives near 2 or below. Those values were
rejected as infeasible. They are retained only as failure evidence.

2. Unscaled cutting-plane LP (search_lp.py / search_lp_results.json)

Each LP used necessary finitely sampled cone directions and added
separating directions from its current candidate. Large tables suggested
values around 3.17636. However absolute solver tolerances allowed
nonnegligible relative violations in very small tail cells. A Boolean
success field in these files records only LP solver status. It does not
certify full positivity. In particular numerical values slightly below
the final rigorous lower endpoint are not accepted feasible laws.

3. Mean / coset guide (search_center.py / search_center_results.json)

Asymmetric finite-table guides were solved at several prescribed
centers. The centered lattice looked best; other sampled centers had
larger costs. This sampling did not establish the global mean argument.
The actual proof uses exact duals at 1/4-grid centers and exact quadratic
inequalities over the whole intervening squares.

4. Separable smooth dual fields (dual_separable.py)

Fields built from x and clipped linear functions produced sampled lower
bounds around 2.68 to 2.73. These were not certified universally and were
superseded by the rational local dual certificates. Their failed reach
suggested that nonseparable cell directions matter substantially.

5. Rational lattice dual generation

lattice_dual.py used fixed exact rational unit directions, obtaining an
initial fully certified universal covariance lower bound above 3.1655
after the all-mean extension. adaptive_dual.py added exact rational unit
directions suggested by floating-point cone violations. It then rounded
nonnegative dual multipliers and evaluated every dual coefficient exactly.
The final lower certificates are valid even if a guide run stops with
small floating-point cone residuals; primal guide feasibility is not an
assumption in the dual proof.

K=6 duals at the 15 symmetry-reduced 1/4-grid centers, and stronger K=8
and K=10 duals at center (0,0), are retained. K=10's centered guide
objective was approximately 3.1763614859491702. That number is guidance,
not a proved sharp optimum.

6. Exact upper generation

certified_upper.py rescales table variables by rho^(a+b) so that small
tail constraints are not ignored solely because their masses are small.
After rounding table entries to rationals, a small rational product-law
addition supplies cone slack. Every finite tail-pattern determinant is
then checked exactly before a law is accepted.

N=3,6,8,9,10 tables are retained. N=10 required a larger product repair
than N=9 and was not itself the best upper law. A rational convex mixture
of its un-repaired guide table and the exact N=9 law was subsequently
checked directly, producing the best final upper certificate.

An attempted N=12 scaled solve failed with:

  RuntimeError: The HiGHS status code was not recognized.
  (HiGHS Status 15: model_status is Unknown; primal_status is Infeasible)

No N=12 upper law was promoted or saved as a certified result.

7. Final proof boundary

Floating-point optimization chose coefficients. Exact integer/rational
arithmetic establishes the saved inequalities. The infinite tail,
unknown mean, and arbitrary measure-coset extensions are mathematical
arguments in PROOF.txt. The sharp minimum, uniqueness, symbolic form,
and external novelty remain unproved.
