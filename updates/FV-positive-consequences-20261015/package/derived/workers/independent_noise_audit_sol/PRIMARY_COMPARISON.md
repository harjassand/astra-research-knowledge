# Close primary-source comparison

Date: 2026-10-15. These primary texts were inspected directly; source bytes and receipts are saved separately. This is a targeted comparison, not historical clearance.

**Busch–Heinosaari, Approximate Joint Measurements of Qubit Observables, arXiv:0706.1415v2 (2008), Proposition 4 and equation (39).** The unbiased-qubit compatibility criterion is ‖p+q‖+‖p−q‖≤2. For p=η₁u, q=η₂v, with unit directions and c=u·v, squaring it gives η₁²+η₂²≤1+c²η₁²η₂², exactly the current qubit condition. The paper also notes older sufficient-and-necessary work for this case. The criterion cannot be credited as new. The present infinite-dimensional polar-symmetry proof is valid, but this audit does not establish priority for that extension. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/0706.1415v2).

**Patra–Pan, Margenau-Hill distribution as a Necessary and Sufficient Signature of Measurement Incompatibility, arXiv:2609.37144v1 (2026), Theorem 2 and equations (12)–(18).** Their equation (13) is precisely Fετ=[I+εη₁U+τη₂V+ετη₁η₂C]/4. They prove equivalence of positivity of these unsharp dichotomic MH effects and compatibility in arbitrary finite dimension, using the anticommutator's block eigenvalues. The scalar convolution construction therefore lands in a prior finite-effect family. What is being optimized here is the extra shape-constrained scalar reporting law, not a new quantum measurement family. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37144v1).

**Ghosh–Roughgarden–Sundararajan, Universally Utility-Maximizing Privacy Mechanisms (2012), Example 2.1, Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.3.** The additive two-sided geometric distribution and adjacent-count likelihood inequalities are explicit prior machinery. Their universal optimality concerns count-query users with priors and allowed losses, after user-specific remapping. This is not literally the current unbounded real-valued variance-and-equality lemma, so exact subsumption should not be asserted without proof. It decisively precludes claiming invention of the geometric mechanism or the idea that postprocessing improves risk. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://timroughgarden.org/papers/priv.pdf).

**Geng–Viswanath, The Optimal Mechanism in Differential Privacy, arXiv:1212.1186v3 (2013), Section VI-C, equations (75)–(78) and Theorem 11.** This is the closest classical comparator: for integer additive noise at unit sensitivity they explicitly prove that (1−r)r^|k|/(1+r) minimizes every symmetric monotone cost, including k². Their continuous staircase theorem instead imposes inequalities at every displacement up to the sensitivity; that stronger continuous constraint differs from the current single shift. The current arbitrary-real-measure lemma handles residue classes and arbitrary means, with an exact equality characterization, but the discrete optimizer is already established. The original comparison's emphasis on continuous staircase noise alone understates this close prior. [Primary full text, Section VI-C](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.1186v3#S6.SS3).

**Audit inference.** The variance product follows by combining the known compatibility polynomial with a geometric fixed-shift variance bound; the full real-valued proof is sound. This is a useful exact synthesis and reporting-cost formulation. Whether the precise combination and equality statement already occur in earlier operator-measurement or privacy literature is unresolved. Targeted non-discovery cannot establish novelty, and the continuous-spectrum extension alone does not justify exceptional significance.
