SCOPE-EXACT ELECTRODE CHARGE BOUND FOR THE SINGLE PIVOT
Internally reconstructed elementary proof. This is NOT a new physical device.

Contract
Planar slit y in [0,1], physical gap W, axial length L, span B. Mean axial
water speed U, relative velocity u(y)=6y(1-y), uniform inlet particle concentration.
Particles have supplied, constant electrophoretic mobility mu>0; a uniform
constant transverse field E points toward y=1. Define T=L/U and s=mu*E*T/W.
Here Brownian diffusion is set to ZERO. Conductivity sigma is homogeneous,
ohmic and constant. Electrode current is sigma*E*L*B. Product outlet is y<=b
with water recovery R=F(b), where F(y)=integral_0^y u(z) dz=3y^2-2y^3.
Particle/feed concentration target is epsilon. No dilution carrier is supplied.

Proof
A particle starting at y0 follows dx/dy=UW*u(y)/(mu*E). At x=L, if it has
not reached the wall, F(y_exit)=F(y0)+s. Thus it exits in product precisely
when F(y0)<R-s. Since inlet axial particle flux has density u(y0), the fraction
of inlet particle flux exiting in product is max(R-s,0). Product concentration
relative to inlet is max(R-s,0)/R. Therefore product purity requires
    s >= R*(1-epsilon).
Particles that reach y=1 can be held at the zero-velocity wall in this zero-D
limit. This proof is ONLY a necessary clean-outlet exposure bound, not a proof
that both streams recover all particles. The finite-D numerical calculation
uses reflecting boundaries and verifies both-stream particle-flux conservation.

Charge per product-water volume is
    q = [sigma E L B]/[R U W B] = sigma E T/(R W)
      = sigma s/(R mu) >= sigma*(1-epsilon)/mu.
This cancels W, L, R and throughput. Widening a separator or lengthening
residence does not remove this particular charge-transfer cost. For direct
water-electrolysis electrodes, a specified unrecovered electrode-pair voltage
V_e adds W_e=V_e*q J/m3 product, independent of the small liquid voltage EW.
It does NOT prove V_e>=1.23 for every possible electrode architecture: redox
electrodes, gas-energy recovery, and reversible reset can change net work and
must be charged under their own acquired contract. Here V_e=1.23V is a stated
water-electrolysis benchmark with gases not credited as returned energy.

For mu=3e-8 m2/V/s, epsilon=1e-4, and sigma=2.5094432e-4 S/m (computed from
the inherited LOCAL source-wall H/B values, not measured FFE inlet water),
q>=8363.9743 C/m3 and this specified electrode benchmark costs >=0.00285769
kWh/m3 even before liquid pumping, Joule heat, overpotential and reservoir work.
This numerical lower bound applies to the zero-D contract above. It is not
asserted as a universal lower bound for Brownian focusing. The executed finite-D
cases all use more exposure and charge; they are a finite numerical check.

Finite-D model
With tau=Dp*T/W^2 and Pe=mu*E*W/Dp, evolve axial particle flux p=u*n:
    d_tau p = d_y(d_y n - Pe*n), p=u*n.
Zero transverse particle flux holds at both walls; inlet n=1. The finite-volume
generator is exponentially fitted, so its stationary concentration is exactly
proportional to exp(Pe*y) on the grid. Columns sum to zero, and off-diagonal
transition rates are nonnegative. This gives particle-flux conservation and
positivity at the semidiscrete level. BDF integration and the numerical outlet
event are floating calculations; conservation and positivity are checked.
The full equilibrium flux measure is u*exp(Pe*y), not exp(Pe*y) alone.

At R=.95, b=0.8646496378. Numerical quadrature gives the minimum stationary
Pe=86.1752398 for epsilon=1e-4. This is a quadrature/root result, not an exact
symbolic constant. Executed Pe values:96,128,192,256,512,1024. They are sampled
operating points; no global optimizer is claimed. N=200/400/800 at Pe128,
N=400/800/1600 at Pe256, and N=800/1600/3200 at Pe512 check refinement.

Area and width scaling
One-wall productivity is R*W/T. Both electrically contacted faces are counted,
so total-plate productivity is R*W/(2T). At fixed Pe,tau, T scales as W^2,
productivity as 1/W, electrode charge and Joule work per product volume stay
constant, and fixed-length planar pumping scales as W^-4. The matched-area
comparison uses this exact scaling within the supplied model at 4 and10 LMH
TOTAL plate area. This avoids comparing two-plate FFE with one-face UF without
counting the second interface.

What the proof/model do not supply
mu for80nm particles at actual feed chemistry; feed conductivity; electrode
reservoir membranes, ion replenishment, salt/pH leakage, complete reset;
nonadsorbing walls; concentrated-colloid transport; absolute rejection assay;
UF80nm four-log/recovery performance on the same feed; lifetime and economics.
The bound is deliberately scoped. It cannot establish a universal no-go for
all electrokinetic separation or for the original catalytic material.
