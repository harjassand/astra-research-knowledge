RARE-PATH GATE: PRECISE PROOFS AND LIMITS
13 October 2026

I. Ten direct force queries remove the inherited privileged-coordinate oracle

Contract: an observed state is (x,w), w in R^9, where y=v^T w, ||v||=1.
The globally supplied family has physical x drift
 f_x(x,w)=-4 a x(x^2-1)-c(x-v^T w), with a>0,c>0.
Force calls are noiseless physical drift evaluations at selected restart states.
The force family is an assumption; finite queries do not establish it globally.

For j=1,...,9, query t_j=f_x(0,e_j)=c v_j. Hence c=||t|| and v=t/c.
A tenth query s=f_x(1/2,0) gives s=(3/2)a-c/2, so a=(s+c/2)/(3/2).
All nine hidden directions are probed; the learner does not receive v or a.
The oracle wrapper internally converts observed inputs to the simulator's
hidden coordinates, which is precisely what an observed force oracle means.
Thus the true two-dimensional drift needed for the inherited grid/Doob guide
is acquired in ten force calls in this family. The thermostat is separately
known, as it must already be for the exact Gaussian likelihood operation.

The code queried the original simulator read-only, with bytecode writes
suppressed. It obtained a=20, c=5.999999999999999, alignment 1 to displayed
precision, and maximum drift error 1.42e-14 on 100 separately charged heldout
force evaluations. This finite floating check illustrates the algebra; the
algebra is the global conditional proof. Initial states (0,e_j) and (.5,0)
use the same full-state restart/force access supplied to the inherited lane.
If physically admissible restarts are restricted or forces noisy, the contract
changes and this ten-call claim no longer applies.

The original 19,400,000 acquisition evaluations remain candidate costs.
Adding the ten calls to the *inherited reported* favorable oracle's 77,360
force-grid calls and 1,421,804 path calls gives 1,499,174. There is no new path
run behind that number. Since the recovered polynomial is exact in the stated
family, the grid's 77,360 evaluations may instead be charged as arithmetic,
leaving 1,421,814 physical calls plus grid/RNG work. These are different cost
conventions, stated explicitly. The original candidate total is 20,838,768.
No comparison of these empirical precisions becomes a confidence certificate.

II. What generic defensive mixtures, stopping and e-processes can establish

For an exact stopped physical path law P and a controlled law Qc, use a single
path-level mixture Q=alpha P+(1-alpha)Qc. For event A,
 Y=1_A dP/dQ has E_Q Y=p and 0<=Y<=1/alpha. Also E_Q Y^2<=p/alpha.
This is a valid bounded construction. Its generic Bernstein/range guarantees
still depend on n*alpha*p, so at p about 4.3e-10 it does not supply useful
relative accuracy at the inherited acquisition-inclusive budget. A mixture
at every step instead permits the much larger bound alpha^-4000. These bounds
are established defensive importance sampling, not new mechanisms.

For inherited Gaussian shifts, let theta_k=u_k sqrt(dt/2). Then
 L=exp(-sum theta_k xi_k - (1/2)sum theta_k^2).
When |u|<=80 and physical duration<=2, V=sum theta_k^2<=6400.
Conditional Gaussian exponential martingales yield E_Qc L^r<=
 exp(r(r-1)Vmax/2), r>=1. In particular E L^2<=exp(6400).
Stopping at a bounded time or finite control energy does not make L bounded.
These generic moments are too loose at the rare event scale. A cap derived
from an observed maximum or empirical standard deviation has no such proof.

For any nonnegative iid contributions with mean p, a predictable bet
0<=lambda_i<=1/m makes product_i[1+lambda_i(Y_i-m)] a nonnegative
supermartingale under p<=m. Inverting Ville gives lower confidence bounds.
It supplies no corresponding useful upper bound without a tail restriction.
That restriction is substantive: arbitrarily small contamination probability
can carry an arbitrarily large increment to a nonnegative mean. This statement
concerns a distribution class with only integrability, not a denial that the
fully specified Euler model contains additional exploitable structure.

III. Original-Euler environmental localization, with a useful bias budget

This is a new internally reconstructed bound for the inherited toy, not a
physical validation or a likelihood-envelope certificate.
Let dt=1/2000, N=4000, x0=-.75, y0=-1. Before its first x-boundary hit,
x_k is in (-.8,.8). The exact physical y recursion is
 y_(k+1)=r*y_k+(1-r)*x_k+sigma*xi_(k+1),
 r=49997/50000, sigma^2=1/50000, independent standard Gaussian xi.
The x control affects none of these physical-law bounds. Extend x after
stopping by any value in [-.8,.8] to make all comparison paths well-defined.

Use the same y noises in lower and upper processes that replace x_k by -.8
and +.8. Since r>0, induction gives y^-_k<=y_k<=y^+_k up to/at stopping.
Their deterministic means are
 m^-_k=-.8-.2*r^k, m^+_k=.8-1.8*r^k.
Both centered, scaled processes are the same Gaussian martingale
 M_k=r^-k(y^+-_k-m^+-_k)=sigma sum_(j=1)^k r^-j xi_j.
It has independent increments and variance
 s_k=sigma^2 (r^-2k-1)/(1-r^2).
Thus (M_k) has exactly the law of standard Brownian motion sampled at s_k.
Set C=(1-r^2)/sigma^2, S=s_N, R=r^-N=sqrt(1+C*S),
 kappa=(11/5)*(R-1)/S=(11/5)*C/(R+1).

If y_k<=-3, then M_k<=-(11/5)*sqrt(1+C*s_k)+1/5.
This convex function lies below the chord -2-kappa*s on [0,S]. Therefore
lower exit implies inf_(0<=s<=S)(B_s+kappa*s)<=-2.
If y_k>=3, then M_k>=(11/5)*sqrt(1+C*s_k)+9/5.
This concave function lies above its chord 4+kappa*s. Upper exit therefore
implies sup_(0<=s<=S)(B_s-kappa*s)>=4.
Embedding in continuous Brownian time enlarges the discrete crossing event.

For a>0,kappa>0, the drifted reflection formula gives either crossing bound
 H(a,kappa,S)=Q((a+kappa*S)/sqrt(S))
              +exp(-2*a*kappa)*Q((a-kappa*S)/sqrt(S)).
It follows either by reflecting Brownian paths at their first hit and tilting
the terminal normal density, or by integrating the drifted first-passage law.
H is increasing in S and decreasing in kappa by pathwise coupling. The code
uses rational S_upper and kappa_lower and enclosing Q/exp evaluations. It proves
 P(any |y_k|>=3 before/at x stop, k<=4000)
 <= H(2,kappa_lower,S_upper)+H(4,kappa_lower,S_upper)
 <= 7.6783851477e-16.

Hence replacing the target event by the same event confined to y in [-3,3]
changes its physical probability by at most that absolute amount. Relative to
the inherited *uncertified point estimate*, this is about 1.78e-6. The absolute
bound is rigorous; the relative statement is only a scale diagnostic. This
removes a state-tail obstacle to a compact-domain committor/Doob residual
certificate. It does not certify the residual, normalize a nonlinear tilted
kernel, bound full path likelihoods, or prove the original rate estimate.

IV. A distinct coupled two-channel exact Euler example

Full latent state is (x1,x2,y,z1,...,z7). With dt=1/8, apply exactly the Euler
recursion with drift
 b1=-x1+y, b2=-x2-y, by=.01*x1-.01*x2-.03*y, bz_j=-5*z_j.
Independent step noise standard deviations are (1/2,1/2,1/20,1/2,...,1/2).
Initially x1=x2=0 and the eight remaining coordinates are iid N(0,1).
The observed eight-dimensional nonreaction vector is a rational Householder
rotation R of (y,z), with R=I-2vv^T/(v^Tv), v=(1,...,8). x1,x2 are outputs.
The slow environmental y pushes the two reaction channels in opposite
 directions and receives feedback from x1-x2. Its relevant eigenvalue is near
one per step; this is a coupled dynamic environment, not an independent mark.

The declared target is p=P(some x_c(k)>=17/2, c in {1,2}, k=1,...,16).
It is an Euler-grid first crossing of a union of two channels, with finite
horizon 2. It has no stationary-rate or continuous-time claim.
The exact global affine family, preparation covariance and Gaussian thermostat
are supplied. Eleven noiseless full-vector force queries, at 0 and each e_j,
recover the affine drift exactly over the rationals. Finitely many queries
would not prove an arbitrary force law affine. All acquisition is charged.

Write the complete path as a rational linear map of W~N(0,I_170). Each of the
32 constituent crossing events has the form E_j={m_j^T W>=b}, b=17/2.
Its variance v_j=||m_j||^2 is rational and p_j=Q(b/sqrt(v_j)). We compute exact
rational lower/upper bounds l_j<=p_j<=u_j. Let U=sum_j u_j. The union bound
proves p<=U. The two final-time channels give a lower bound
 p>=2*l_final-P(x1(16)+x2(16)>=2b).
The sum's variance is rational, so its Gaussian tail has a rational upper
bound. No random data are involved in the resulting certified enclosure
 2.63120471048755217825e-10 < p < 6.87333158621074989028e-10.
All numbers are stored as exact numerator/denominator endpoints.

Across all 16x16 cross-channel time pairs the covariance is nonpositive
(exact finite check). A union bound on their Gaussian sum tails bounds
P(channel1 crosses AND channel2 crosses) by the receipt's exact rational
number. By symmetry, using only a channel1-conditioned path proposal without
support for channel2-only paths returns roughly half p. This example exposes
a real support/acquisition operation: covering both channels is needed for
unbiased rare-event evaluation. It does not establish superiority over a
competent full-state classical sampler, which covers both channels too.

V. Rare-scale bounded mixture and finite-sample confidence theorem

Choose label j with probability u_j/U, and then draw the *original full path*
conditioned on E_j. Conditional sampling is explicit: h_j=m_j/sqrt(v_j),
 Z~N(0,I_170), T~N(0,1) conditioned on T>=b/sqrt(v_j), independent, then
 W=Z+(T-h_j^T Z)h_j.
All coordinates and all times remain represented. The normalizers are scalar
Gaussian tails, not an undisclosed nonlinear committor. A log-CDF/inverse-CDF
implementation avoids subtraction of a Gaussian CDF from one.

The actual mixture density is
 q(w)=p_W(w)/U * sum_(j:E_j(w)) u_j/p_j.
Every q path lies in the target union, and q covers every constituent event.
The full path probability ratio contribution is therefore
 Y(w)=U / sum_(j:E_j(w)) u_j/p_j.
Integration gives E_q Y=p. Since u_j>=p_j and at least one event occurs,
 0<Y<=U=6.8733315863e-10.
There is no unseen large-weight tail in this ideal mathematical sampler.
For each observed event mask, its contribution lies between
 U/sum_(j:E_j) u_j/l_j and U/(number of occurring events).
The code outward-rounds rational endpoints before accumulating them.

For n=20000 independent draws, Hoeffding on Y/U in [0,1] gives
 P(|Ybar-p|>U/100)<=2 exp(-2*20000/10000)=2 exp(-4)
 <0.036631277778.
Thus [mean_lower-U/100, mean_upper+U/100], intersected with the deterministic
bounds, has at least 96.3368722% coverage under the specified ideal sampler.
Its computed demonstration endpoints are
 [5.71620465884839828244e-10, 5.85367134526449132822e-10],
about 1.188% relative half-width. No empirical variance or observed maximum
enters this theorem. n and the bound are fixed before the random run.

This is the established conditional-event union mixture known as ALOE,
with conservative rational component masses. Its Gaussian conditional sampler
and inverse event-multiplicity correction are prior art, not our discovery.
Owen, Maximov, Chertkov (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06965v4.

VI. Arithmetic, sampling, and strongest control boundaries

The scalar tail enclosures are exact rational certificates. Machin's formula
pi=16 atan(1/5)-4 atan(1/239), alternating finite arctangent bounds, positive
Taylor bounds for exp, and integer-square-root brackets supply all constants.
Normal tails use a finite integration-by-parts identity with signed remainder:
 Q(z)=phi(z)/z [sum_(k=0)^m (-1)^k (2k-1)!!/z^(2k)+R_m],
with R_m of the next sign and magnitude bounded by the next term. The code
uses m=12 where z^2>36, and the simple Mills upper bound elsewhere. It does not
pretend the infinite asymptotic expansion converges. Fixed rational grids are
rounded outwards to prevent uncontrolled integer-size growth.

The execution uses NumPy/SciPy floating normal draws and conditional inverse
CDF. It is not a formally verified exact random-bit sampler or an interval
proof of every event sign. Consequently the deterministic interval is an
unconditional exact arithmetic result, while the displayed narrow random
interval is the numerical instantiation of a rigorous ideal-sampler theorem.
Its ideal Gaussian/uniform assumption and implementation precision boundary
are explicit, not silently merged with a machine-checked confidence claim.
The event masks, rational mass endpoints and independent aggregation checker
make the deterministic and contribution computations reproducible.

The strongest same-interface data-free control is the same eleven-query
affine reconstruction followed by classical ALOE. It has exactly the same
rare-scale weight bound, finite-sample guarantee and arithmetic/RNG cost.
At 20000 paths the operational work is 320000 full-state steps if each step
queries a force oracle, plus eleven acquisition calls. Once the supplied
affine family is acquired exactly, all paths can instead be generated by
matrix arithmetic with zero additional physical force queries; this option
is equally available to the control. The initial Gaussian preparation, 170
Gaussian coordinates per path, event evaluations, rational tail work, storage
and checking are separate work. A candidate unnecessarily retaining inherited
19.4M acquisition would cost at least 19,720,000 propagation-inclusive calls
under the per-step convention and have no accuracy benefit on this example.

Full-state FFS/weighted ensemble is also applicable, but running a weaker
splitting baseline is unnecessary to establish this non-advantage: an existing
same-input ALOE control is identical to the proposed operation. Exact union
Doob sampling would have zero variance but requires the union normalizer/law
not supplied here. Neither it nor a hidden oracle is used to claim a win.

Status: useful exact local bound and a rigorously bounded solvable-model
operation; no new acquisition-inclusive capability, physical validation,
novelty-clearance, continuous-rate certificate or mission completion.
