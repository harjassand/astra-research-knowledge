2. EXACT SHARED-BATH THERMODYNAMIC STOP

Let t=RT/F; C and A denote activities of Cu(I), Ag(I) in ONE homogeneous
well-mixed electrolyte. Let a_c,a_a be Cu and Ag metallic activities in the
anode, consistently relative to the pure phases used at the cathode. Unknown
salt standard potentials are E_c^0,E_a^0; they need not be acquired for this
identity. Write Delta=E_a^0-E_c^0 and R_ion=A/C.

Thermodynamic Cu stripping without favorable Ag oxidation at the anode needs
  E_Cu,anode <= E_anode <= E_Ag,anode,
so its available potential window is
  W_anode=Delta+t ln(R_ion)-t ln(a_a/a_c) >=0.
Cu deposition without favorable deposition of pure Ag at a pure-Cu cathode needs
  E_Ag,pure <= E_cathode <= E_Cu,pure,
so its available window is
  W_cathode=-Delta-t ln(R_ion) >=0.
Adding gives EXACTLY
  W_anode+W_cathode=-t ln(a_a/a_c).
If a_a>a_c, both nonnegative windows cannot exist. Equivalently, anode requires
R_ion>(a_a/a_c)exp(-Delta/t), cathode requires R_ion<exp(-Delta/t), with equality
only allowing zero-rate reversible boundaries. The contradiction is independent
of every unknown standard potential. Testing against a separate pure Ag phase
is conservative: Ag mixing into Cu can make trace Ag deposition still easier.
For other positive valences z_c,z_a, the sum is
  t ln(a_c)/z_c - t ln(a_a)/z_a;
the same obstruction holds whenever a_c^(1/z_c)<a_a^(1/z_a).

SCOPE: equilibrium reaction selectivity in a homogeneous shared bath, not an
impossibility theorem for electrochemical separation. Kinetic passivation,
different local anolyte/catholyte activities, selective transport/speciation,
sequential Ag/Cu collection, or subsequent Ag recovery are valid escape routes.
They add measured interfaces, inventories, heat/work and product handling.
An unspecified membrane is not a solution. In a divided monovalent design,
necessary reservoir ratios satisfy R_anode/R_cathode > a_a/a_c; this is an
activity-ratio requirement, NOT a claimed membrane permeance selectivity.
No such operating salt/barrier or kinetic selectivity was acquired here.
