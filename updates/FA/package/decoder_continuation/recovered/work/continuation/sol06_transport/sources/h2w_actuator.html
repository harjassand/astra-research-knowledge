Non-Comm DC Voice Coil Linear Actuator - NCC05-18-060-2X

This moving coil actuator (NCC05-18-060-2X) comes supplied with an aluminum bobbin which allows for heat to be conducted away from the motor windings. This actuator also comes supplied with a thru hole, should your application require a hollow actuator.

Moving coil actuators do not come with a bearing system, if you would like to see actuators with a built-in bearing system please look at our moving magnet models. Or if you would like to see how we have integrated our voice coils into linear motion stages.

To determine your motion profile needs, use our linear motion calculator.
