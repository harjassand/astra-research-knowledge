2. A data-nonidentifiability witness

Claim scope: frequency-dependent cycle-averaged dissipative response surfaces,
the class implicitly used in the inherited model. This is not a constructed
causal particle model, proof that the actual silica fails, or impossibility of
all actuation. A validated rate-ratio invariance law could exclude the witness;
it is an additional unacquired premise.

Let M_A be any data-consistent extension of the measured parallel and complex
orthogonal viscosities, and let M_0 be its passive unactuated response. Assume
parallel viscosity is nonnegative and the real orthogonal viscosity is
nonnegative; these are the cycle-averaged dissipation conditions. Set
omega_s=94.2478 rad/s and

  x=clip((omega-omega_s)/omega_s,0,1),
  q(omega)=1-3*x^2+2*x^3,
  M_B(omega) = q(omega)*M_A(omega)+(1-q(omega))*M_0.

Then q is C1, 0<=q<=1, q=1 on the entire used physical source band, and
q=0 for omega>=188.4956 rad/s. Therefore M_A and M_B agree on every used
measurement, including its complex phase, while M_B gives no axial thinning
at any of the three saved standing modes. Convexity preserves nonnegative
parallel viscosity and real orthogonal viscosity, hence nonnegative mean
dissipation. The source data alone do not select M_A over M_B.

At equal flow under M_B the axial hydraulic requirement is the unactuated
one. Nonzero passive oscillation adds nonnegative fluid, structural, winding,
and driver losses. Thus the active total is strictly worse than the same pipe
passive total whenever any added dissipation is positive. This defeats a
claim of source-certified active advantage, including under a hard inventory
cap. It does not establish which extension the actual material follows.

The finite check evaluates q=1 at every deposited Fig1d physical frequency
and q=0 at every saved standing mode. Convexity is the argument for all
intermediate frequencies; the numerical sampling is only a diagnostic.

3. A mechanical frequency gate independent of guessed Q/support stiffness

Contract: the inherited fixed-end tube, G=79GPa, radius0.0625m, thickness
0.0077m, L=20m, steel density7850kg/m^3, slurry density1700kg/m^3, drive-arm
point inertia I_a=0.132833kg*m^2. Cross viscosity is frozen real and positive,
as in the preferred phase-pi/2 model. Grounded support stiffness is
nonnegative. Additional inertial hardware and elastic fluid resonances are
outside this contract.

For the radial angular velocity u with wall value1, its viscous diffusion
equation has real positive radial conductance and imaginary inertia term.
Taking the real part after multiplication by conjugate(u) gives a
subharmonic equation for |u|^2. The maximum principle gives |u|<=1.
Angular-momentum balance then gives local reactive added inertia at most the
rigid-fluid polar moment I_f=rho_f*pi*R^4/2=0.0407464kg*m per unit length.
Steel inertia per length is I_s=rho_s*J=0.111307kg*m, where
J=pi*((R+t)^4-R^4)/2. Real fluid damping is nonnegative by energy balance.

For every fixed-end trial shape theta in H_0^1(0,L),

  integral |theta|^2 <= L^2/pi^2 * integral |theta'|^2,
  |theta(L/2)|^2 <= L/4 * integral |theta'|^2.

The second inequality follows by applying Cauchy-Schwarz on each half and
adding: reaching a midpoint value c costs at least4|c|^2/L in gradient energy.
Consequently the mass quadratic form is bounded by

  M[theta] <= [(I_s+I_f)*L^2/pi^2+I_a*L/4] * integral|theta'|^2,

while K[theta]>=GJ*integral|theta'|^2. If

  omega^2 < GJ / [(I_s+I_f)*L^2/pi^2+I_a*L/4],

the real part of the dynamic stiffness remains positive definite, including
any nonnegative support stiffness. If A has positive-definite real part and
e is the drive vector, Re(e* A^-1 e)>0: write w=A^-1e and use
Re(e*w)=Re(w*A w)>0. The driving-point compliance and its inverse therefore
cannot have zero real part there, irrespective of passive damping magnitude.

Numerically, this lower bound is64.4698Hz. The original11-node discretization
with MAXIMUM rigid-fluid inertia and ZERO support stiffness has first
eigenfrequency64.7535Hz. The unloaded steel frequency is79.3083Hz. Increasing
positive support stiffness cannot lower these. Adjusting Q cannot move a
zero-reactance operating point into the15Hz source band. That requires
changed tube dimensions, added inertia, a longer device, or an acquired
elastic-fluid response, each with fresh costs and validation.

4. An acquisition-ready total-power gate

Prescribe sinusoidal central angular velocity Omega(t)=Omega_p*cos(omega*t),
and measure the filled/support-loaded fundamental torque impedance Z=T_1/Omega_p.
This Z includes fluid, tube and support dissipation and reactive loading;
there is no need to posit a structural Q or support stiffness.

Two opposite coils with force constant K_f, each cold resistance R_c, and
lever ell require T=2*ell*K_f*I. For a sinusoidal torque,

  P_mech = (Omega_p^2/2)*Re Z,
  P_copper = R_c*Omega_p^2*|Z|^2/(4*ell^2*K_f^2).

For a nonlinear torque waveform the fundamental still determines mechanical
work under the prescribed sinusoidal velocity, but all higher torque
harmonics add copper loss. Use measured RMS currents or charge their losses
in P_extra, along with driver idle/conversion, coil temperature changes and
other unequal ancillary costs. Coil inductance is reactive, not free driver
power; actual voltage/current waveforms are part of the gate.

Using Omega_p=0.16rad/s, R_c=11.5ohm, K_f=27.8N/A, ell=0.5m and the saved
conditional axial pump input6.6558677W, the necessary-and-sufficient budget
within this specified accounting contract is

  0.0128*Re Z +0.000380932664*|Z|^2 +P_extra
       < P_strongest_allowed_passive -6.6558677W.

At the saved modeled Z, Re Z=67.85566 and |Z| is almost equal to Re Z. The
budget reproduces9.278383W before extra costs. Against the125mm passive

This ellipse is not an acquired feasible device. Full commissioning must

5. Substantive access/residence pivot and its strongest control

Task: a fixed aperture sees an externally fixed reciprocal pressure waveform,
P(t+T/2)=-P(t). Can externally timed actuation create directed release without
changing that aperture or adding a foreign carrier? Under a unique quasisteady
odd passive law Q(-P)=-Q(P), every static pipe diameter and coaxial stable
carrier film gives zero net cycle volume. This follows by pairing the two
half periods. It is a restricted access task, not a general statement about
hysteretic or geometrically asymmetric passive devices.

If actuation supplies states Q_L(P)>Q_H(P)>0 at +/-P, use the low resistance
state on the forward half and high resistance on the backward half. Then

  V=(T/2)*(Q_L-Q_H)>0,
  W_axial=(P*T/2)*(Q_L+Q_H).

This is a concrete conditional directed-release construction that static
enlargement cannot supply under the same fixed reciprocal input. The
mechanism is prior art: acoustic viscosity modulation and reversed average
response are demonstrated by Sehgal et al. (2022 preprint;2024 publication).
Those data do not supply a calibrated fixed-aperture pipe switching law.

If pressure control IS allowed, use the SAME thinned state at +P for
t_direct=(T/2)*(1-Q_H/Q_L), then set pressure to zero. This delivers the SAME
V before the same deadline, at the same aperture and peak pressure. With
constant nonnegative actuation power A and identical preparation/reset count,

  E_rectifier-E_direct = P*T*Q_H + A*T*Q_H/(2*Q_L) >0.

Thus a controllable pressure source dominates this pivot within the stated
quasisteady/on-time-cost contract. Unequal pump switching, latency, training,
reset and driver costs require acquisition; none are declared zero in a
physical device claim. A passive-only direct pressure comparison would not
automatically meet the same deadline and is NOT used as the dominance proof.

Finally, zero average volume is not controlled residence: equal forward and
backward strokes can cross a protected boundary and exchange material even
when their signed sum is zero. Retention requires a stroke/guard-distance and
diffusion/migration bound, or a genuine shutoff. The Lin fixed-torque impeller
control slows rather than stops when vibration is off (primary lines1124-1139).
It is not an acquired zero-leak valve. The motor restart demonstration is
limited-torque stalling, not proof that a geometrically clogged pipe clears.

Six flow-ratio cases and30 full-cost cases are checked with exact Fraction
arithmetic in pivot_checks.py. This establishes the algebraic construction
and comparator, not constitutive switching, clearing probability or novelty.
