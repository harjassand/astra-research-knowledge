## Result earned by this investigation

The earlier finite-alphabet obstruction was real: a one-step retention coefficient equals one once the number of states exceeds the alphabet size. The successful replacement is a matched-Gaussian KL energy that decreases both under random rotation mixing and under the conditional-mean contractions induced by arbitrary stochastic encoders. At the spatial resolution forced by m atoms, a spherical spectral gap supplies a uniform per-step energy drop. The finite initial energy yields an O(m) survival time, and submultiplicativity supplies exponential long-run contraction. A polytope construction matches the rate.

This is not merely a restatement of the old Haar quantization integral. The proof bounds arbitrary successive positive kernels, and a single-step norm bound provably cannot do so. Its exact second application concerns unbiased numerical interpolation, with a sharp exponential range cost. Nevertheless it remains a quantitative result about a specific family of positive realizations; general positivity/representation theory and the relevant entropy ingredients were not invented here.

## Independent conceptual exploration and negative conclusions

A complete family of Gaussian log-likelihood probes characterizes compact-support convex order. Its raw scalar energy subfamily is incomplete, as an exact reflection counterexample proves. The full profile is a reformulation of all convex tests through log-sum-exp limits; it currently provides neither a smaller finite certificate nor an efficient general algorithm. That candidate does not warrant promotion to a new foundational primitive.

Likewise the fixed-gate state law is log(T) classical state-register bits versus one qubit, not T bits, and it does not establish a general quantum computational separation. The compiler/readout model includes ideal transition probabilities as primitives, although a rational nonuniform implementation is supplied. Full implementation workspace and sampling complexity are not given by the register count alone.

The mathematical capital should be preserved, but continuing only to optimize constants, grids, or the same state-count exponent is not justified as Genesis's principal historic-discovery programme.

## Single consequential open mathematical gate

For a symmetric lazy finite law nu generating a dense subgroup of SO(3), prove or refute

    inf_{m>=1} m(1-rho_m(nu)) > 0
       implies ||Q_nu||_{L2_0(S2)} < 1.

The reverse implication is proved here. The question is open in this investigation; its global novelty/open status is unverified. A negative answer would sharply delimit the invariant. A positive answer would turn the finite positive-lift profile into a characterization of the infinite-dimensional spectral property. Neither outcome should be called historic without independently important consequences.

The attempted converse currently fails at an explicit interface: an almost-invariant L2 function need not provide a localized finite point cloud with a near-permutation positive realization. No quantitative conversion from the former to the latter has been established. Do not silently assume this interface, and do not import the planar Liouville example as a counterexample to the SO(3)-restricted statement.
