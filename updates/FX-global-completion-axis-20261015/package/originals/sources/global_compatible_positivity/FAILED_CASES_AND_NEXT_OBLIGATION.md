# Failure scopes and the exact continuation frontier

## Three distinct negative statements

1. **Old r=-8/3 continuation:** the incoming package contains an exact negative quadratic value for one selected reduced Gram. Its replay still passes. This is not failure of all heat certificates, not failure of compatible dissipation, and not non-quasiconvexity. The new theorem permits a larger H coefficient at that ratio; it does not thereby decide the original alpha=3 polynomial.

2. **Curved parameter r=-2:** every finite alpha fails rank-one convexity for alpha H+u^2+j^2-2uj. The principal acoustic minor is exactly -4. This is a genuine rank-one obstruction and rules out quasiconvexity for those particular coefficients.

3. **The determinant square:** j^2 is polyconvex and quasiconvex, but an actual compatible periodic gradient has negative integrated constant-heat Hessian dissipation. It rules out universal monotonicity of that heat operation and any null correction making the same dissipation pointwise nonnegative. It is not negative quasiconvex energy.

## Exact positive state available for continuation

The JSON contains a universal isotropic identity for every r!=-1; a rank-eight convex H Gram; a constant ten-dimensional kernel; a positive six-dimensional acoustic block whenever r(r+2)>0; a single closed null correction that contributes diag(0_6,I_4) on that kernel; and a fixed positive eight-dimensional convex complement. Both completion coefficients are explicit.

The entire shear-contact coefficient projection is now characterized by the two alternatives in Theorem C. No additional feasibility oracle is missing for that projected problem.

## The decisive missing theorem

For a fixed admissible triple (beta,gamma,delta), compare the least alpha yielding rank-one convexity with the least alpha yielding quasiconvexity. The current proof establishes that both thresholds are finite and supplies a sufficient upper bound; it does not prove equality. The sharp fixed-alpha boundary is the principal unresolved issue inside this face.

Beyond it, invertible acoustic contacts with nonzero trace(A0^-1 R0), singular backgrounds, and simultaneous contact intersections require distinct analysis. The current nilpotent/trace-free normalization does not absorb them.

A proposed universal operation must not require the raw constant-heat cone to contain all rank-one-convex polynomials: j^2 disproves that premise. A meaningful extension must allow a minor-Jensen addition, another Lyapunov quantity or flow, or a different compatibility argument. Non-SOS searches within the same fixed dissipation cannot repair its negative integral.

## Conservative bounds are not boundaries

The compiled sufficient coefficients are not optimized. At r=-5/2 the older alpha=3 certificate is much better than this global compiler. Near r=-2 the necessary coefficient diverges, but the rapid blow-up of the particular sufficient formula is not proved sharp. A failure of a requested alpha to exceed the compiler bound is not a negative mathematical result.
