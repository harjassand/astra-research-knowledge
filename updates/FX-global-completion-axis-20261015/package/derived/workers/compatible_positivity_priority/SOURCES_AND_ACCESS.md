  **not** acquired. The acoustic implication was reconstructed through the
  fully acquired matrix-factorization proof instead.
- Alibert--Dacorogna (1992), DOI 10.1007/BF00387763: publisher abstract and
  receiving family identified; PDF endpoint returned an HTML response,
  not a PDF. No proof-access claim is made. IK2005's independently read
  proof supplies the useful completion predecessor.
- Pedregal--Sverak (1998): publisher/mirror metadata inspected; the attempted
  publisher PDF URL failed. No result from that unacquired proof is a premise.

The first two assigned source reports and the earlier W audit/addenda were
read as incoming research data. Their claims were not reattributed to this
worker. Current construction-worker results were kept outside the proof
premises.

`sources/reviewed_pages/` contains renderings of third-party original pages.
They are acquisition/inspection artifacts, not worker-authored figures.
Exclude acquired PDFs, texts, HTML responses and rendered pages from a
producer-results archive unless a separately labeled research-access bundle
