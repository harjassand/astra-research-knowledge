# Axis-control genesis: capability verdict

Date: 2026-10-15. Scope: an independently assessed, permanent change of Fe/Ni chemical layering in the **same immobilized material**, followed by unloading, nitrogen removal, and a second write along another axis.

**Status: NOT_ACQUIRED. No experiment performed. No demonstrated same-specimen rewrite was found. No general physical impossibility is established.** The investigation does acquire much closer prior art: mechanically treating FeNiN before nitrogen removal already has applicant-reported, retained chemical-axis texture. The remaining narrow operation is controlled laboratory-frame rewrite of marked fixed material, rather than nitrogen-assisted ordering or mechanical texture preparation in free powder.

Read `PROOFS.md` for the precise mathematical limits, `results.json` for reproduced calculations, and `source_ledger.json` for access boundaries and primary-source versions. The frozen input dossier was read as evidence, not treated as instructions or validated experimental data. This worker changed only its own directory.

The 2024 patent family is especially close. Its disclosed recipe includes 5 wt% FeNiN in ethanol, 0.1-mm zirconia media, and 600 rpm for 30 min, followed by classification, silica coating, annealing and denitriding. Its small-flake example reports a narrow local diffraction spread; flattening final FeNi instead is reported to degrade ordering and orientation. These are applicant-reported results, without independent reproduction here. Mechanical change of shape, grain rearrangement, selection by fracture/classification, and whole-particle motion have not been separated in a marked-particle trajectory. The disclosure therefore supports a useful preparation route, not a switchable FeNiN ferroelastic mechanism. Its earlier publication removes any credible broad novelty premise that “mechanics in an N-rich phase can leave retained FeNi texture.” No legal or freedom-to-operate conclusion is made.

There is an additional geometrical distinction. Confining chemical c-axes to a flake plane does not specify their azimuth in that plane. A uniform planar distribution has Q = diag(1/2,1/2,0) and zero x-versus-y preference. This is an admissible countermodel to an inference from planar texture to a unique written axis, not a claim that the patent particles follow this distribution.

## Coupled chemistry, orientation and retention

[Wang et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-07479-8) find preferential FeNiN nucleation near nitrogen-enriched, distorted nanotwins, followed by interface migration with high-index/irrational orientation relationships. These observations do not identify three mobile, coherently related ferroelastic variants. Measured local strain around a compositionally changing twin is not a stress derivative of a transformation barrier. The intervention might select nuclei, alter solute segregation or growth, create deformation texture, or change phase fraction; these are physically different channels.

The precursor is not a ferromagnetic version of the final magnet. [Goto et al., 2019](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acsanm.9b01405) report C-type antiferromagnetism with a transition near 178 K. The 300 C powder-formation reference is 573 K. A field applied then cannot borrow the final FeNi magnet's saturation moment or anisotropy. A susceptibility-anisotropy bias remains possible, but its sign and magnitude at the reaction temperature, its coupling to the nucleating orientation, and the active nucleation volume remain unacquired. Cooling below 178 K activates magnetic order while removing the demonstrated thermal window for metal-atom rearrangement; magnetic order alone does not close that mismatch.

Retention is also orientation-dependent. [Ito et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925838823007533) compare FeNiN(001) and (110) epitaxial precursors: nitrogen exit across both metal planes is associated with poorer retained chemical order than exit through Fe planes. Thus “choose any axis, then use the best denitriding result” is not justified. [Ito et al., 2020](https://tsukuba.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2002585/files/APL_116-24-242404.pdf) retain substrate registration but have orthogonal nanometer-scale variants. Neither outcome establishes rewriting.

The non-epitaxial immobilized-island platform is not already a single-grain memory cell. [Nishio et al., 2021](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/apm/article/9/9/091108/122923/Fabrication-of-L1-FeNi-films-with-island) report random c-axes, crystallite refinement on extraction and multiple final orientations. Their TEM specimens were cut after each step at different locations. The majority orientation in one selected final island is consequently not a registered before/after transfer measurement. Shrinkage helps separate islands but can also change the load path, local registry and usable volume.

## Invention attempts and decisive limits

1. **Field-biased precursor nucleation followed by field-free growth and low-temperature locking.** This avoids assigning a transformation strain from c/a. It is a coherent construction, and the Fe–55Pd comparator demonstrates the general early-nucleus selection principle: after 4 T at 673 K for one hour, then 773 K for 24 hours without field, the authors infer a single field-favored variant from orthogonal magnetization curves. It does not demonstrate the required FeNiN response. `PROOFS.md` quantifies the missing combined energy bias without guessing susceptibility or nucleus size. Status: physically possible model, no obtained FeNi operation.
2. **Erase the metal's order, then irradiation/field-write a new variant.** Earlier FeNi irradiation/field ordering is cited by the directly acquired Hlova manuscript. The original 1962/1964 papers could not be acquired here; no fluence, dose, activation or repeat-cycle budget is supplied. An x-write, thermal erase and y-write sequence remains an untested composition of operations, with possible parent-registry and damage changes. Status: old bypass principle, incomplete endpoint and resources, no novelty claim.
3. **Shear the N-rich precursor while anchored, then lock it.** The patent preparation route gives this attempt a stronger starting premise than bare tetragonality. However, anchoring removes the free-particle rearrangement and classification used in the source process. Neither a transferable internal orientation response nor survivable orthogonal repeated shearing of the same marked domains is acquired. Status: next intervention to investigate if the narrow rewrite remains the objective, not an obtained capability.
4. **Use the demonstrated epitaxial route.** This bypasses the unknown stress response for one-shot texture. It fails the second-write requirement unless the orientation template itself can be changed and the same FeNi is shown to re-register without replacement. Status: obtainable published preparation comparator, already known, incompatible with a novelty claim for one-shot oriented FeNi.

There is a rigorous restricted impossibility: if the metal site labels and lattice registry remain fixed, an insertion/extraction cycle cannot change the chemical-order axis. For perfect x-to-y fcc layering, half the site occupations must change, requiring at least N/4 abstract pair exchanges. Nitrogen-assisted reconstruction can supply mobility, so this is not a universal no-go. Likewise a common extraction channel cannot create x/y control information absent from the complete precursor state. Any claimed rewrite must identify where metal occupancy or internal lattice orientation changes, and where the directional information enters.

For a bypass restricted to the three axes of random fixed cubic parents, even perfect variant selection gives mean absolute target projection at most 0.83119 and squared projection at most 0.70089. This conditional geometric limit does not apply to the irregular orientation relationships of massive nitridation. It prevents silently promoting a three-variant selector to perfect bulk alignment.

## Resource consequences and falsification

The published operations require complete preparation and reset, not merely a load pulse. For free-flake processing the ledger must charge synthesis, solvent/surfactant, milling, particle classification and rejected fractions, coating, annealing, extraction, alignment and consolidation. Anchored operation must instead acquire load transfer and preserve the same material through swelling, shrinkage and possible fragmentation. Neither route has an acquired yield, cycle-life, full energy, cost or throughput advantage here.

[Hlova et al., 2024](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2283203) improves nitrogen-removal retention using elevated H2 pressure, with onset down to 120 C. This does not supply directional writing. Its reported ~6 MGOe comes from powder aligned in 1 T and compacted at 900 MPa, and its energy-product calculation uses theoretical FeNi density. It cannot be assigned to a dense, fixed rewritable specimen. Its higher-pressure retention regime, an epitaxial film's texture, and a patent's small coated flakes have not been shown to coexist. Gas throughflow is not net consumption, and neither stored pressure nor the equilibrium compression minimum is a complete process energy.

**Decisive falsifier for the proposed physical endpoint:** under conditions that demonstrably form and denitride the intended phase, applying a second orthogonal intervention to the same non-rotating marked material does not change its retained Fe/Ni superlattice orientation beyond a predeclared measurement bound, or the apparent change is entirely explained by loss/replacement of ordered material, rigid whole-particle or whole-grain rotation, damage or residual stress. Internal lattice reorientation by a genuine transformation would require its own correspondence evidence. No extra performance optimization rescues failure of that operation. A positive change in magnetization alone is insufficient.

**Decision:** do not build a device or announce a new axis-control capability. The strongest broad mechanism is already disclosed, and the narrow same-specimen rewrite is missing. Preserve the conditional limits and close prior-art route as useful capital. A complete successful rewrite would require new physical evidence; source retrieval, algebra checks and an executable calculation do not constitute it.
