1. Strongest concrete advance: sparse exact reaction clocks

The obstruction is explicit in Petrack--Doty, arXiv:2508.04079v2 (12 Feb 2026):
discrete reaction batching can save about sqrt(n) work, but exact timestamps have
an additional adaptive-rejection cost, with an assumed density-evaluation bound.
The relevant complete proof and fixed-time algorithm were acquired and read.

For positive indexed rates lambda_i<=b, let q_i=1-lambda_i/b. Independent geometric
extras G_i with P(G_i=m)=(1-q_i)q_i^m give exactly

 H | (G_i) ~ Gamma(k+sum_i G_i, rate b),
 H =_law sum_(i<k) Exp(lambda_i).

The Laplace transform proves equality of the full law, not just moments. A known
bound q_*>=max_i q_i permits candidate indices to be generated by geometric
skipping; query lambda_i only at a candidate, retain it with probability q_i/q_*,
and draw its positive geometric extra. The expected rate-query count is k q_*.
There is no array scan or precomputed density hidden in this account. An equivalent
rational-envelope compound-Poisson construction avoids a costly implementation of
tiny-success geometric skips by repeated Bernoulli trials.

For transformed CRN rates lambda_i=a binom(n+i g,o), fixed o,g and k<=C sqrt(n),

 k q_* <= o g k(k-1)/(n-o+1)=O(1).

The physical rate multiplier a is retained. Tight endpoint bounds are essential:
a loose upper envelope can make the correction cost linear. Random collision
lengths are admissible only with their actual second-moment/tail proof, not merely
the assertion E K=O(sqrt(n)).

The fixed-time cutoff is also exact. Conditional on the extras and H=h, there
are A=k+sum G_i equal-rate exponential microphases. Their A-1 interior arrivals
are uniform order statistics on (0,h). For u<h, one draw

 W~Binomial(A-1,u/h),   J=max{j: j+sum_(i<j)G_i<=W}

samples the correct joint endpoint/prefix law. The inverse is evaluated by a
single scan of the sparse marked stages. Thus cutoff work is expected O(1) in
the explicit binomial-variate model. A gamma/beta-tree alternative costs O(log k)
and is also proved. Multiple cut queries require jointly sampled counts or cached
conditional splits. Fresh independent cutoffs would give the wrong joint path.

With deterministic transformed-event blocks, corrected mass-action uniformization,
an exact collision-batching content routine of cost D(n_max), and passive-event
slowdown bounded by rho before absorption, the fixed-time theorem is

 E work = O(I_net + [D(n_max)+1]
                     [1+rho E L_t/sqrt(n_min)]).

Here L_t is the number of original active reactions by the requested horizon;
initialization I_net is charged; population bounds are unconditional promises or
stopped-run bounds, never future-trajectory postselection. Terminal states are
checked at block boundaries and a possible absorption-straddling block is charged.
The gamma-only cutoff replaces +1 by +log(n_max+2). In the source's matched model,
D=O((2q)^o log n_max). With fixed network, bounded slowdown, n_min~n_max~n and
E L_t=Theta(n), this reaches the sqrt(n) batching speedup where the source has
an n^(2/5) speedup. It returns configurations/aggregate counts or sparse observations;
an explicit list of every reaction and timestamp still has linear output cost.

Independent source audit found a repairable factorial mismatch: with the stated
binomial propensity convention, padding a reaction by delta identical catalysts
requires k'=k V^delta/binom(m,delta). The printed falling-factorial divisor omits
delta!. Two cells and root checked the order-three competing-reaction example.
No author was contacted and no upstream source was edited.

Prior-art boundary. Moschopoulos (1985) already gives the positive gamma series;
Fiems (2024) gives exact phase-type uniformization, a discrete count and one gamma,
with substantial generic precomputation. ER-leap/HiER-leap already use gamma totals
and conditional simplex allocations. Potential novelty is only the indexed sparse
compiler, its charged cutoff, and this complexity substitution. These identities
are not new. Continuous exact-bit sampling and an end-to-end finite-bit CRN
implementation remain open. Floating diagnostics are not an exact implementation.

2. Higher-upside opening: acquiring whole-path model certificates

For a frozen bounded visible-history predictor vhat, a frozen controller and
finite horizon T, stop repeated independent reset/replay trials at an independent
occupation time U. The acquired quantity is

 D=E ||Q^(J_U) E(X_U,:)-vhat(U,H_U)||_2^2.

The coupling proof gives

 TV(actual visible path, predictor path)
       <=delta_init+T sqrt((k-1)D),

where k is the visible-label count and delta_init the initial visible-law error.
This controls every bounded path event simultaneously. The probe moment identities
acquire D without reconstructing the microscopic chain, dividing by rare-label
probabilities, supplying a hidden coverage ratio, or cloning a hidden state.

The operation uses actual compatible product gains and selective within-label
neutrality, or an actual all-microscopic-event sensor. A nonstationary response
identity and fixed pre-stop predictor cross term allow separate fresh preparations
for each raw moment. An independent exponential stopping clock with bounded
importance weights can realize uniform occupation. Reset/replay, clock accuracy,
probe synthesis, composition errors, predictor evaluation and observation noise
are all charged. Chebyshev endpoint interpolation has proved polynomial weight
norms and factorial analytic bias; no free derivative oracle is assumed.

The certificate has finite-confidence acquisition bounds, but they can be very
large (conservative epsilon^-4 or epsilon^-8 dependence, with horizon factors).
An explicit four-label prefix-tree family has exponential passive testing cost
and polynomial active certification under the specified stronger probes. This is
a matched interface separation, not an assertion that active experiments generally
beat passive data or that the separation phenomenon itself is new.

The hard limit is important: D includes hidden conditional rate variance. A perfect
visible predictor can have D>0; an exact four-state fixture gives D=1/64. The result
is a sufficient on-policy certificate, not complete tolerant testing of all good
visible models, counterfactual policy certification, or an infinite-horizon result.
The noisy physical/unknown-simulator assay has not been implemented.

A stronger obstruction is now proved. With six visible labels, horizon T=O(m),
known generator and exact compatible probes, one perfect predictor has D_cond=0;
every member of a hidden context/sign-association family has D_cond>1/(256T)
and visible-path error>1/32. Yet even an oracle revealing the full hidden context
and sign at every reset needs Omega(2^(m/2)) resets to distinguish the null from
worst alternatives. All raw same-state discrepancies D are identical across this
family. The transcript domination and birthday bound were checked by root.
The obstruction changes the initial hidden preparation association, not the
generator; its hidden dimension and alternative description are exponential.
It is not a lower bound under a known small conditional-state representation.

There are three exact alternatives: observable predictable critics give lower
discrepancy witnesses; independently prepared posterior replicas give conditional
mean squares; a verified finite conditional closure gives tractable upper bounds.
Cloning the actual hidden state gives the same-state square and does not supply
independent posterior replicas. These are distinct experimental operations.

The complete current-primary comparison with Hilder--Sharma (2023/2024) and
Hilder--van Meurs--Sharma (Feb2026) credits their marginal coarse-graining and
functional-inequality results. Neither inspected full proof supplies the active
finite-experiment acquisition or whole-path frozen-history certificate here.
This comparison narrows the priority question; it does not settle originality.

3. Independent invention: an observable inverse despite global slow modes

The initial universal stationary-simulation ambition met a proved two-state
work/second-moment lower bound C M>=1/(144 epsilon). Exact local symbolic access
is a different interface. Strictly graded local relations give factorially bounded
Neumann paths and a bounded observable-specific Poisson inverse without a global
mixing assumption. A rational geometric estimator closes exact unbiased evaluation
for rational local data. An acquired within-grade killed walk extends the theorem
when absolute diagonal dominance and microscopic work are explicitly bounded.

After Boolean factors failed under reuse, the inventor changed the mathematics.
For any finite directed stochastic network P, unit phases update at rate one by
x_v<-y x_w, w~P(v,.), with one shared Brownian circle phase of diffusivity gamma.
The exact local identity is

 L(x_v y^k)=-(1+gamma k^2)x_v y^k+sum_w P_vw x_w y^(k+1).

Thus h_v=sum_n y^n(P^n x)_v / product_(j=0)^n(1+gamma j^2)
solves -Lh_v=x_v and is the actual integrated transient response. A local P-walk
and rational block-geometric evaluator require O(gamma^-1/3) expected row queries
with O(gamma^-2/3) second moment. The response scale and oracle cost are sharp in
the stated scope. No global joint-state enumeration or Brownian simulation is used.

Root then proved the signed-phase extension: independently choose y or y^-1 on
each copy. Fourier charge becomes a symmetric walk, yielding sharp response
Theta(gamma^-1/2), O(gamma^-1/2) expected row work and O(gamma^-1) second moment.
An independent Sol audit checked the full block constants. A deterministic
charge-cancelling two-cycle instead has response Theta(gamma^-1).

Root further proved a quantitative conjugate-feedback certificate. Integer edge
charges of either sign are allowed if a verified potential obeys
c_vw+a_v-a_w>=delta>0 on every positive-probability edge. With B=osc(a), response
and expected row work are O(1+B/delta+(gamma delta^2)^-1/3), and a rational
unbiased evaluator has the square of that second-moment scale. This includes
the (+2,-1) feedback cycle. Acquiring/verifying all support edges and the potential
is charged; a row sampler alone cannot certify it. Qualitative positive cycle
winding is insufficient: a long alternating cycle retains inverse-gamma response.
Exact arithmetic also depends on charge magnitudes, not just their encoded bits.

This is an exact distinction between ballistic, diffusive and trapped Fourier
charge in constructed models. It changes the dynamics and does not invert the
original unmodified chain. One-sided Hill operators, complex Airy cubic damping,
Mathieu/harmonic-oscillator behavior, duality and random Neumann evaluation are
established prior mechanisms. The model-specific cost statements remain possible
contributions, with priority UNKNOWN. A consequential physical application and
locally acquired charge-occupation criterion for conjugate feedback remain open.

4. Other capital retained and mechanisms rejected

* Capacity: root derived a relative Schur/Poincare transfer for all terminal sets
  and a resolvent certificate; rare_rates supplied a succinct layered-hypercube
  family. These use established variational mathematics and require acquired
  structural rules/gaps. Reversible generator Grams can already be passively
  obtained from correlation derivatives; active access is not needed there.
* Inverse problems: strong bias improves a pair's scalar read count from
  epsilon^-8 to epsilon^-6, but the quasistatic input-energy lower bound retains
  epsilon^-8. No free information or transient identification theorem follows.
* Sampling geometry: the transport-action proposal collided with existing optimal-
  transport robustness results. A three-state counterexample separates acceptance/
  transport cost from conditional-law fidelity; it supplies no sampler advantage.
* Chemical dynamics: an exact robust finite LP gives absorbing-region bounds under
  its first-order/common-factor assumptions. Finite interior kinetic queries cannot
  certify boundary permanence. The positive-system tools are established.
* Quantum: an orthogonal guarded-history representation improves a conditional
  lift threshold and allows purification of a full kernel chart. Exact chart/
  occupation merging, logical transport, circuit acquisition and state preparation
  are unresolved. A truncated approximate chart is not exactly kernel supported.
* OpenAI/math: actual Fourier-catalyst and solenoidal-shear proofs were retrieved.
  The shear proof yields a finite-horizon precision guarantee; unbounded robustness
  fails by a distant encoded marker. A finite-word Fourier stability candidate is
  preserved with incomplete source/global implementation gates and huge constants;
  it is not promoted to a practical stable arbitrary-length Fourier transform.

5. Questions that deserve sustained continuation

(a) Which experimentally acquired intervention algebra can certify a useful
    scientific model's full future path law without microscopic reconstruction?
    Next decisive test: implement the complete noisy random-stop assay on an
    unknown hidden simulator, including broken product laws, timing errors,
    rare states and a perfect predictor with hidden-rate variance. Then identify
    a real kinetic platform satisfying the declared probe law under matched costs.
(b) Can exact batching remove passive-event slowdown, rather than only timestamps?
    Next concrete theorem: rate-hierarchy/low-reactant mechanism with acquired
    selection and clocks; retain the source's unresolved slowdown obstruction.
    Separately build a finite-bit sparse clock and compare actual resource costs
    against direct sums and the strongest phase-type/ER-leap implementations.
(c) Can a succinct local charge-occupation certificate handle physically natural
    conjugate feedback and give controlled response without solving the original
    huge inverse? The positive-cycle-mean potential theorem closes a sufficient
    algebraic case, but acquiring its support/potential and handling recurrent or
    trapped charge remain hard. Counterexamples forbid calling all feedback or
    all noise benign.
(d) Can occupation histories in the quantum chart be merged while preserving
    exact kernel membership and efficiently acquired logical source transport?
    The improved conditional representation does not supply that bridge.

For sustained high-upside research, (a) is the strongest scientific opening;
(b) is the nearest concrete algorithmic improvement; (c) should remain an
independent high-risk direction. None is compulsory merely because it survived
