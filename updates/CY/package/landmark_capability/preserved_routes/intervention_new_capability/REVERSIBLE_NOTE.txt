REVERSIBLE ENDPOINT PREPARATION (CLASSICAL, SHORT NOTE)
For reversible finite CTMC Q with stationary pi, prepare a target state y,
wait for its first exit y->x, discard the holding time at y, and run the
ordinary chain forward from x for time T. Reverse the resulting path.
Detailed balance gives the stationary entrance-Palm predecessor law
pi(x)q_xy/[pi(y)q_y]=q_yx/q_y, equal to the prepared first-exit law;
the adjoint dynamics of the past equal Q. Thus the reversed recording is
an exact stationary entrance precursor, including prior returns to y.
Cost per sample is preparation cost plus mean first-exit time 1/q_y plus
T observation time. This is a direct use of classical time reversal and
does not extend to nonreversible dynamics by itself.
