NONREVERSIBLE PRECURSORS: CLOCK-CHANGE IDENTITY AND ACQUISITION BOUNDARY
2026-10-10. Internal mathematical note; known time-change/Palm primitive,
not a landmark novelty claim.

Let Q be any irreducible finite-state CTMC, with stationary law pi, and let
c_x>0 be a known, state-dependent speed multiplier. A feedback actuator
that multiplies EVERY outgoing rate from x by exactly c_x implements
Q'=C Q, C=diag(c_x). Its stationary law is

  pi'_x=(pi_x/c_x)/Z,   Z=sum_x pi_x/c_x.

Consequently, for every directed edge x->z,

  pi'_x q'_{xz}=pi_x q_{xz}/Z.                         (1)

Thus the stationary directed-edge Palm law, the full stationary jump-chain
law, and the jump-sequence law before an entrance into any target are
unchanged. This holds without detailed balance or knowing the time-reverse
generator. If an accelerated holding time at x is W'~Exp(c_x q_x), then
c_x W'~Exp(q_x), independently of its outgoing destination. Multiply each
recorded holding time by c_x to reconstruct the exact original precursor
path in physical time under the stationary entrance-Palm law. The identity
requires an exact common multiplier for all outgoing channels, including
unobserved ones; selective acceleration changes the jump chain.

Discriminating nonreversible example. Let y->a=y->b=1,
a->y=b->y=e, a->b=s e, b->a=t e. Take e=2^-n and either
(s,t)=(2,1/2) or (1/2,2). The stationary entrance rate at y in both
models is J_y=2e/(2+e), and pi_y=e/(2+e). Yet the fraction of entrance
edges a->y is (1/2+t)/(1+s+t), namely 2/7 versus 5/7. An ordinary
post-exit trajectory from prepared y does not sample this predecessor
law. Accelerating a and b by c=1/e, with c_y=1, gives pi'_y=1/3 and
J'_y=2/3. Its observed entrance-edge fractions stay 2/7 versus 5/7,
and rescaling holds by c recovers the original precursor timing.

The example's wall-clock gain costs a peak actuation factor 1/e=2^n.
This factor is not hidden in binary-description or sample complexity. If
uniform speed control is unavailable, target preparation and forward
observation alone do not identify the incoming law cheaply: from a
prepared y, a path of physical duration L sees a post-exit transition
with probability at most 3eL, so the two models' observation transcripts
have TV at most O(eL), while their predecessor laws differ by 3/7. The
same slow-transition barrier applies to direct preparations of a or b
unless the control changes their outgoing clock. More generally, (1)
preserves the embedded jump chain, so clock control cannot reduce the
number of jumps needed for a combinatorially rare target; it only reduces
time spent waiting between jumps. The exact primitive therefore does not
remove general rare-event acquisition costs.

Operational requirements: identify the current state before applying its
multiplier; implement known factors on ALL outgoing rates with negligible
switching delay; wait for the accelerated chain to mix (charged); count
every accelerated jump; and record each c_x for time correction. If any
of these fails, equation (1) alone does not certify the physical sample.
