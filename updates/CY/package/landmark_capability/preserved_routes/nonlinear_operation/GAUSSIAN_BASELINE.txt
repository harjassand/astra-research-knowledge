PRESERVED BASELINE, NOT A CAPABILITY CLAIM

For independent bits X_i with rational probabilities, rational weights w_i,
and Gaussian data likelihood L_y(x)=exp[-(y-sum_i w_i x_i)^2/(2 sigma^2)],
put R=(|y|+sum_i |w_i|)/sigma. Standard-normal Gauss-Hermite quadrature
with M positive nodes evaluates the evidence and every product-observable
numerator by n-factor products. For each state, Taylor expansion of
exp(i G(y-sum w_i x_i)/sigma) to degree 2M-1, Gaussian quadrature
exactness, and positivity of the missing 2M-th Gaussian moment give

 |integral - M-node rule| <= 2 R^(2M)/(2^M M!).

The evidence is >= exp(-R^2/2), so M=O(R^2+log(1/epsilon)) suffices for
certified additive posterior queries with polynomial-precision node/weight
evaluation. This is a familiar Gaussian/Fourier latent-variable method.
Its cost is polynomial in numerical R, not in log R. It does not establish
a materially new operation beyond known transform methods and sum-DP.
