Parked candidate: exact huge-index sparse linear transfer
=========================================================

For A in F_p^{N x N} with m explicit nonzeros and u,v in F_p^N, compute
s_n=u^T A^n v. The minimal recurrence is acquired deterministically by
interleaving first-dependence searches on (v,Av,...) and
(u,A^T u,...). If d is the smaller cyclic dimension, scalar order is at most
d. Incremental elimination, Berlekamp-Massey on 2d terms, and binary
polynomial reduction yield O(md+N d^2+d^2 log(n+1)) field operations and
O(Nd+d^2) memory.

The executable reference implementation is sparse_transfer.py. Its finite
checks passed for all 6,561 (A,u,v) triples in dimension 2 over F_3 at ten
indices each, plus 200 random sparse/dense cases with 250-bit indices,
verified against independent dense matrix powering.

This is not a novelty candidate. Wiedemann's 1986 primary source explicitly
forms scalar sequences (u,A^i b), says their shortest recurrence is recovered
from 2N terms by Berlekamp-Massey, and uses the resulting polynomial for
sparse linear-system solving. Bostan and Mori give O(M(d) log n) evaluation
for a supplied order-d recurrence (SIAM J. Comput. 50 (2021), 1–32).

* Wiedemann (1986), https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1986.1057137
* Bostan and Mori (2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08822
