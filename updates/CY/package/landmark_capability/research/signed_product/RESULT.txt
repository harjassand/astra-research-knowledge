OPERATIONAL SAMPLING FROM ACQUIRED SIGNED PRODUCT REPRESENTATIONS
2026-10-10. Root implementation and exact rational fixture checks.

sampler.py consumes rational signed weights c_r and product Bernoulli laws.
It provides two distinct sampling operations. Neither acquires the input
mixture; the controlled_product proof describes a proposed acquisition.

1. Add a DEAD outcome of weight 1-sum c_r. Draw from the absolute-weight
mixture and accept x with v(x)_+/a(x). This samples the normalized global
positive part exactly, assuming ideal fair randbelow. If the alive signed
law is eta-close in L1 to the true alive subprobability, the resulting
whole outcome is eta-close in TV. Expected attempts are at most the sum
of absolute coefficients including DEAD. All products and ratios are exact
Fractions. No enumeration is used by the sampler.

2. For conditional alive sampling, compute the two signed child-prefix
masses at each coordinate, clamp to nonnegative values and normalize.
If both vanish, use any fixed branch law. If the true alive mass is s,
the output TV error is at most n*eta/s. This uses O(nR) product updates
and has no 1/s rejection factor. When the mixture is an exact positive
subprobability, this is exact conditional sampling regardless of s.
For approximate acquired input, charge precision eta <= epsilon*s0/n
for a known positive rational s0 <= s. See controlled_product/RESULT.txt
for the coupling proof and the cost of acquiring such a mixture.

The two methods generally produce different approximating laws. The prefix
method does not claim to sample the global positive part of a signed law.

Executable checks enumerate only small fixture laws to verify algebra.
Three signed fixtures, including one with negative point masses, satisfy
the exact proposal-times-acceptance identity. A conditional fixture has
survival mass 15/2^64, coefficient cancellation, and a uniform conditional
law on 15 non-target configurations. The prefix method produces that exact
law with four branch decisions, without inverse-survival rejection. This
fixture supplies its representation; it does not demonstrate acquisition
of a physical rare-conditioned law. An approximate-input example has
exact output TV 1/15 under the conservative proved bound 4/15.

The seeded demonstrations use Python pseudorandom bits. Exact sampling as a
mathematical statement assumes independent fair bits; sample frequencies
are illustrative. Interval comparison and prefix sampling are familiar
operations, not claimed as historically novel.
