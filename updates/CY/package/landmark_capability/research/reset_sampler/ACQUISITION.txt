LOCAL ACQUISITION AND RARE-CYLINDER STABILITY FOR PRODUCT RESET CHAINS
2026-10-10. Internal constructive theorem candidate, not a novelty or
independent-certification claim. Companion to work/reset_sampler/RESULT.txt.

Model and output claims
-----------------------
Use n two-state coordinates with local flip rates q_i(0,1),q_i(1,0)>0.
There are k active reset channels. Channel j has additive nonnegative
hazard a_j(x)=sum_i a_{ji}(x_i), and on firing replaces the entire state by
an independent draw from the product law nu_j=otimes_i Bernoulli(r_{ji}).
Let a(x)=sum_j a_j(x). Assume known bounds

  0<H_min<=a(x)<=H_max for every x,
  0<q_min<=q_i(z,1-z)<=q_max,
  every positive a_{ji}(z)>=h_min>0,
  b<=r_{ji}<=1-b, 0<b<=1/2.                            (1)

The zero pattern of local hazard entries is known. All k channels have at
least one positive entry. Bounds may be rational and are charged as
numerical condition parameters. In the acquisition corollary, their
inverses/ratios are required to be polynomially bounded, not merely to
have short binary encodings.

Consider a second model with the same local-hazard zero pattern. Suppose
every positive local flip rate, positive hazard entry, and each of the two
probabilities in every reset Bernoulli coordinate differs by a factor in
[exp(-delta),exp(delta)], where 0<delta<=1/4. Both models obey (1), up to
the harmless exp(+-delta) enlargement made below. Define alpha=exp(delta)-1.
There are explicit constants C_TV and C_A, polynomial in n,k and in the
numerical ratios q_max/H_min, H_max/H_min, q_min^{-1}H_max,
h_min^{-1}H_max and log(1/b), such that

  TV(pi, pihat) <= C_TV alpha when C_TV alpha is small,       (2)
  |log[pihat(A)/pi(A)]| <= C_A alpha                    (3)

for EVERY cylinder A specifying any selected coordinates. Formulae for
constants are given below. In particular, (3) remains a relative guarantee
when pi(A) is exponentially small in n. Neither bound contains inverse
stationary probability of a reset label, inverse pi(A), or inverse global
mixing time. Equation (2) also gives bounded-observable error through
|E_pi g-E_pihat g|<=2||g||_infinity TV(pi,pihat).

These stability bounds lead to polynomial-local-experiment acquisition in
the bounded-ratio regime described later. They do not turn the EXACT
stationary sampler for a supplied rational model into an exact physical
sampler for unknown parameters: after acquisition it samples the estimated
model exactly and the true law approximately in TV.

Regenerative representation
---------------------------
Let Q0=sum_i Q_i, L=Q0-diag(a), R=(-L)^{-1}=int_0^infinity exp(tL)dt.
Because a>=H_min, R is a nonnegative substochastic resolvent with
||R||_infinity<=1/H_min. Put

  P_lj=nu_l R a_j,
  r_l(A)=nu_l R 1_A,
  tau_l=nu_l R 1.                                       (4)

P is the k-by-k transition matrix of successive reset labels, r_l(A) is
the expected occupation time of A in the cycle begun by reset l, and
tau_l is its expected duration. Every row of P sums to one because a
reset occurs almost surely. If sigma is the stationary law of P, then

  pi(A)=[sum_l sigma_l r_l(A)]/[sum_l sigma_l tau_l].   (5)

For a finite set of states, (5) holds equally as a vector identity for
the full stationary law. It follows from the renewal reward theorem and
requires no full-state enumeration in the algorithm. The product-resolvent
compiler in RESULT.txt evaluates the inputs of (4) from rational data.

Uniform positive and tail bounds for a cycle
--------------------------------------------
All following bounds hold along the linear interpolation of each positive
flip rate, each nonnegative hazard entry, and each reset Bernoulli
coordinate between the two models. At each interpolation point the model
remains a valid product-reset chain. For concise uniform constants put

  H_-=exp(-delta)H_min, H_+=exp(delta)H_max,
  q_-=exp(-delta)q_min, q_+=exp(delta)q_max,
  h_-=exp(-delta)h_min, b_-=exp(-delta)b,
  Lambda_+=exp(delta)sum_i max_z q_i(z,1-z),
  R_u=2(Lambda_++H_+).                                (6)

The total base flip rate is at most Lambda_+, while each cycle's first
reset time T has Pr(T>t)<=exp(-H_- t), E T^2<=2/H_-^2. Uniformization at
rate R_u gives a reset probability at each tick at least H_-/R_u. The
number N of actual local flips before reset is no greater than the tick
count, which is stochastically dominated by a geometric variable of
success probability H_-/R_u. Consequently, with theta_N=H_-/(8R_u),

  E exp(theta_N N)<=4,
  E[T exp(theta_N N)]<=3/H_-;                    (7)

the second inequality follows by Cauchy-Schwarz. Also
E exp(H_- T/2)<=2 and E[T exp(H_- T/2)]<=4/H_-.

Every reset-label transition has an explicit lower bound independent of
its source label. To reach channel j, choose any local positive entry
a_{ji}(z)>=h_-. If coordinate i already equals z, the selected channel-j
clock fires before any other reset or a flip of i with probability at
least h_-/(q_++H_+). If i starts opposite, it flips to z before any reset
with probability at least q_-/(q_-+H_+); flips of other coordinates need
not be forbidden. Thus, for every l,j and interpolation point,

  P_lj>=p_*:=[q_-/(q_-+H_+)] [h_-/(q_++H_+)]>0.      (8)

The all-positive reset-law class gives a similarly explicit lower bound
for a cylinder A fixing r coordinates. Start within A, an event of
nu_l-probability at least b_-^r, and allow no local flip or reset for
1/(Lambda_++H_+) physical time. Hence

  r_l(A)>=r_*:=b_-^r/[4(Lambda_++H_+)]>0.           (9)

Only log(1/p_*) and log[1/(H_- r_*)] enter the sensitivity coefficient.
Even for r=n, these logs are polynomial in the numerical class-condition
bits. Equations (8)-(9) certify positivity rather than assuming a rare
probability oracle.

Relative stability of P and rare cycle rewards
----------------------------------------------
For a stopped path with N local jumps, duration T, initial reset product
draw and final reset label j, its likelihood density is the initial
nu_l-probability times local-jump rates, a_j at the final state, and the
exponential of minus the integrated total flip-plus-hazard rate. Linear
parameter interpolation and the multiplicative endpoint assumptions imply
that the absolute derivative of its log density is at most

  alpha[n+N+1+(Lambda_++H_+)T].                       (10)

Zeros cause no singularity because their support is fixed; the final
channel-j hazard is positive on paths ending in channel j. Differentiate
P_lj as an integral over its positive path density. Jensen applied to
(7), conditional on final label j, gives

  E[N | J=j]<= (8R_u/H_-) log(4/P_lj),
  E[T | J=j]<= (2/H_-) log(2/P_lj).                    (11)

Using (8) in (10)-(11), and allowing a round numerical constant, yields

  |partial_theta log P_lj|<=alpha C_P,
  C_P:=n+1+(10R_u/H_-) log(4/p_*).                   (12)

Therefore each P_lj changes multiplicatively by at most exp(+-alpha C_P)
across the two models. A tiny P_lj increases only the logarithmic score
budget; no 1/P_lj sampling or perturbation factor appears.

For the reward r_l(A), let W_A=int_0^T 1_A(X_t)dt. It is a nonnegative,
parameter-independent functional of a stopped path and satisfies W_A<=T.
Weight the path distribution by W_A/r_l(A). The moment bounds (7) give

  E_reward[N]<= (8R_u/H_-) log[3/(H_- r_l(A))],
  E_reward[T]<= (2/H_-) log[4/(H_- r_l(A))].          (13)

For example, E[W_A exp(theta_N N)]<=E[T exp(theta_N N)]<=3/H_-,
and Jensen proves the first bound. The analogous bound with exp(H_-T/2)
proves the second. Combining (9)-(10) and (13) yields

  |partial_theta log r_l(A)|<=alpha C_A^cycle,
  C_A^cycle:=n+1+(10R_u/H_-)log[4/(H_- r_*)].       (14)

This is the key relative-rare-cylinder step. For an arbitrary positive
observable with no computable lower bound on its cycle reward, (14) does
not automatically give a polynomial relative guarantee; (2) still gives
absolute bounded-observable control.

Occupation TV and the k-state normalization
-------------------------------------------
Set C_occ=n/H_-+(2Lambda_++H_+)/H_-^2. Differentiating the killed
resolvent R_theta=(-L_theta)^{-1} gives R_theta'=R_theta L_theta'R_theta.
The row-sum norm bounds ||R_theta||_infinity<=1/H_- and
||L_theta'||_infinity<=alpha(2Lambda_++H_+) hold. The product reset law
obeys ||nu_l,theta'||_1<=n alpha. Thus, as finite measures,

  ||partial_theta(nu_l,theta R_theta)||_1<=alpha C_occ,
  |partial_theta log tau_l,theta|<=alpha H_+ C_occ,
  TV(r_l/tau_l, rhat_l/tauhat_l)<=alpha H_+ C_occ.  (15)

The last two statements use tau_l,theta>=1/H_+ and integration over
theta. These are absolute occupation-measure bounds, independent of how
small a particular state's occupation is.

Because all P_lj>0, the Markov-chain tree theorem expresses the reset-label
stationary weight of root l as a positive sum T_l of products of k-1
transition entries. Equation (12) implies

  |partial_theta log T_l|<=(k-1)alpha C_P.             (16)

Using sigma_l=T_l/sum_j T_j in (5), the mixture weight of cycle l is
w_l=tau_l T_l/sum_j tau_j T_j. Define

  C_W:=(k-1)C_P+H_+ C_occ.

Its endpoint ratio satisfies exp(-2alpha C_W)<=what_l/w_l<=
exp(2alpha C_W). Mixture decomposition and (15) give the explicit bound

  TV(pi,pihat)
    <=min{1, [exp(2alpha C_W)-1]/2+alpha H_+ C_occ}.  (17)

When alpha C_W<=1/4, this is <=[2C_W+H_+ C_occ]alpha,
so one may take C_TV=2C_W+H_+ C_occ. This has no inverse stationary reset
label mass even if its tree weight is exponentially small.

For a cylinder A, eliminate sigma directly from (5):
pi(A)=[sum_l T_l r_l(A)]/[sum_l T_l tau_l]. Positive sums,
(12), (14), (15), and (16) give

  |log[pihat(A)/pi(A)]|
      <=alpha[2(k-1)C_P+C_A^cycle+H_+ C_occ].        (18)

The bracket is C_A in (3). Thus a local relative tolerance
delta=O(epsilon/C_A) makes an exponentially small pi(A) predictable to
multiplicative error exp(+-epsilon), without observing A even once.

Costed local calibration interface
----------------------------------
The following intervention access is explicit. For each coordinate and
starting bit, an experiment can isolate its flip clock and record whether
its first flip occurs in a chosen interval. For each known-positive local
hazard contributor a_{ji}(z), a separate clock probe can hold that local
condition fixed and record whether it fires in a chosen interval. Each
reset channel l can be forced once and its n-bit product output observed.
These are stronger operations than merely watching an unmodified global
trajectory; their count and physical duration are charged.

For a positive exponential-clock rate r in a known interval [r_0,r_1],
probe for tau=1/r_1. The event probability is 1-exp(-r tau), between
r_0/(2r_1) and 1-exp(-1). From N=O[(r_1/r_0)^2 delta^{-2}
log(M/zeta)] independent probes, Hoeffding yields a certified relative
log-rate estimate <=delta (after a rational approximation of -log(1-p))
with joint failure probability <=zeta over M queried rates. Each probe
uses physical duration tau and one preparation; a device-specific reset
or hold cost must be added. There are at most 2n flip rates and 2nk
positive local-hazard entries. Forced draws from channel l estimate all
n reset Bernoulli parameters at once. If their probabilities lie in
[b,1-b], N_reset=O[b^{-2}delta^{-2}log(nk/zeta)] draws per channel suffice
for both local log-probabilities. This costs k N_reset forced resets and
nk N_reset observed bits, plus any forcing duration.

When q_min^{-1},q_max,h_min^{-1},h_max,H_min^{-1},H_max,b^{-1},n,k are
polynomially bounded, choose delta=epsilon divided by a polynomial upper
bound for the bracket in (18) and the coefficient in (17). The displayed
local experiment counts and physical observation durations are then
polynomial in n,k,1/epsilon,log(1/zeta), with no dependence on a rare
stationary cylinder probability. The empirical rates are rounded to
positive binary rationals; on the calibration-success event the exact
supplied-model algorithm in RESULT.txt can predict stationary product
observables or draw exact samples of the ESTIMATED model. The resulting
draws are within (17) TV of the unknown true stationary law. A cylinder
query is certified to relative error through (18), plus the requested
deterministic compiler precision. Relative prediction to epsilon requires
absolute numerical precision proportional to an explicit lower bound on
pi(A). Equations (5) and (9), together with tau_l<=1/H_-, give the direct
bound pi(A)>=H_- r_*; its LOGARITHM is polynomial in the class-condition
bits and the cylinder description length.

Resource boundary and structural caveat
---------------------------------------
The proof's sensitivity bound tolerates exponentially small positive
local inputs through their logarithms when H_min and rate-ratio terms stay
controlled, but event-count calibration of such a tiny local rate or
hazard needs inverse numerical sample/physical time. Similarly, passively
waiting to observe a naturally rare reset label cannot replace a forced
labelled-reset draw at polynomial cost. Unknown structural zeros cannot be
certified from finite absence-of-event data; their support is part of the
supplied model class.

In the two-state additive-hazard regime, (8) shows that if every positive
local flip/hazard and total-hazard bound is inverse-polynomial, all active
entries of the k-state reset-label transition matrix are themselves at
least inverse-polynomial. Its reset-label mixing is then polynomial by
Doeblin minorization. Thus this bounded-away acquisition class establishes
relative prediction of exponentially rare stationary CYLINDERS and
simulation without full-state enumeration; it does not by itself exhibit
an exponential reset-label mixing separation. Obtaining such a separation
while retaining passive polynomial acquisition would require an additional
mechanism or a different observation interface, not an assertion from
this theorem.

All derivations here are internal and unimplemented at interval-certified
level. The exact supplied-parameter sampler and quadrature remain the
conditional algorithmic inputs described in RESULT.txt. No publication or
historical priority assertion is made.

A floating-point algebra check on a four-coordinate, two-channel instance
compared the stationary vector found by solving the full 16-state generator
with (5) computed from the 2-by-2 reset kernel. Their maximum coordinate
difference was 2.8e-17; the all-ones probability was .01749651304749 by
both methods. This checks the regenerative identity on one instance only,
not the general stability bound or certified acquisition costs.
