DIRECT STATIONARY ACQUISITION WITHOUT ABEL EXTRAPOLATION
Root construction, 2026-10-10. Internal theorem and implementation.
The renewal reward identity and Markov-chain tree theorem are classical.
Originality of this complexity composition is UNKNOWN.

This strengthens/simplifies the computational route in RESULT.txt. Retain
its explicit finite rational local generators Q_i, additive nonnegative
reset hazards a_j, and product reset laws nu_j. Suppose at least one
channel is active, remove identically zero channels, and retain local
irreducibility. There is no need to wait for global mixing, or take an
Abel parameter s to zero, to compute the stationary law.

Let L=sum_i[Q_i-diag(sum_j a_ji)], R=(-L)^(-1),
P_lj=nu_l R a_j, r_l(g)=nu_l R g, and tau_l=nu_l R 1.
Then P is a positive stochastic k-by-k matrix and

  pi(g) = sum_l T_l r_l(g) / sum_l T_l tau_l,                 (1)

where T_l is the directed spanning-tree weight rooted at l for P.
Equivalently T_l is the l-th principal cofactor of I-P. Cofactors can be
computed in polynomial time; enumerating trees is unnecessary. Their
positive tree expansions are used for rigorous perturbation bounds.

Why positivity and polynomial condition bits are acquired
--------------------------------------------------------
Some local killed generator has genuine killing. For such coordinate i,
compute its positive rational stationary law p_i by a local linear solve.
The rational symmetric matrix

 C_i = -(diag(p_i) Q_i + Q_i^T diag(p_i))/2
       + diag(p_i) diag(a_i)

is positive definite: zero energy would force a constant vector by local
irreducibility, and killing at any state forces that constant to vanish.
Its inverse is rational with polynomial bit length. Set
gamma0=1/max_row_sum(abs(C_i^(-1))). Since C_i is symmetric,
f* C_i f >= gamma0 ||f||_2^2 >= gamma0 ||f||_p_i^2.
The killed product generator therefore has real numerical range at least
gamma0, with log(1/gamma0) polynomial in input bits. Thus R exists,
the cycle lifetime has finite mean, and P has row sums one.

For an active channel j choose a coordinate i and state z with
a_ji(z)=h_j>0. From any initial coordinate state, use an irreducible local
path of at most d_i-1 jumps to z, then the selected reset clock. Other
coordinate changes need not be forbidden. If R_i=2(Lambda_i+Amax), each
specified jump has probability at least qmin_i/R_i and the final reset
probability at least h_j/R_i under a uniformized local-plus-reset clock.
The success probability is bounded below uniformly over other-coordinate
histories by

 p_j = min(qmin_i/R_i,1)^(d_i-1) min(h_j/R_i,1) > 0.          (2)

The path has no earlier reset by construction. If d_i=1 take the first
factor as one. Full input irreducibility and activity guarantee the
needed paths/rates; the trivial all-singleton case is immediate. Hence
P_lj>=p_j for every source l, independent of its reset-law support.
Only polynomially many bits describe these lower bounds. Huge inverse
probabilities may affect physical experiments but not numerical bit size.

Acquiring R contractions at zero discount
----------------------------------------
Each required R contraction is the integral of product local killed heat
kernels described in RESULT.txt. The local-dimension sector bound remains
valid. On a sufficiently narrow complex time cone, adding the numerical
range lower bound gives exponential decay at least exp(-gamma0 Re t/2).
Use dyadic real-time quadrature with a cone-contained Bernstein ellipse,
small-time cutoff O(error/M), and large-time cutoff
O(log[M/(gamma0 error)]/gamma0). The number of panels and precision depend
on log(1/gamma0), not its inverse. Certified local matrix exponentials and
quadrature therefore acquire P, r(g), and tau to absolute error 2^-b in
polynomial(input bits,b) time, exactly as in the positive-s construction.

The general nonreversible quadrature theorem is internal mathematics,
not implemented here. The code implements two-state reversible local
chains and a positive statewise total-hazard floor Hmin. In this case the
symmetric killed product spectrum is in [Hmin,Lambda_trace], and the
positive geometric quadrature already implemented has scalar relative
error delta. Its absolute operator error is at most delta/Hmin. Local
heat enclosures and exact rational norm bounds supply each bilinear error.

Stable finite-channel stationary normalization
----------------------------------------------
Suppose interval acquisition encloses every off-diagonal P entry around
a positive midpoint M_ij with relative radius at most theta<1.
No approximate row normalization is required. Form the row Laplacian
with off-diagonal -M_ij and diagonal sum_{j!=i} M_ij. Let W_l be its
positive principal cofactors, computed with exact rational elimination.
Every tree has k-1 edges, so positivity gives

 (1-theta)^(k-1) W_l <= T_l <= (1+theta)^(k-1) W_l.          (3)

For a nonnegative cylinder observable, multiply each acquired reward
interval by W_l, sum, and apply the two factors in (3). Do the same for
tau. Dividing the resulting numerator/denominator intervals encloses (1).
The true denominator is strictly positive: tau_l>=1/Amax and some W_l>0.
Equation (2), positivity of tree sums, and positive mean lifetime ensure
that all additional guard bits are polynomial. Signed product observables
can use absolute numerator error and the same positive denominator.

This establishes the same stationary-expectation and lazy-prefix exact
sampling operation without Abel bias or a global mixing certificate.
The point-mass lower bound in RESULT.txt still suffices for sampling.
The theorem permits unbounded precision/refinement with finite expected
polynomial cost. Implementations with caps are partial implementations:
returned paths agree with the ideal sampler, but conditioning on success
need not leave a stationary law.

Executed implementation
-----------------------
regenerative_solver.py imports certified_solver.py and the outward-rounded
dyadic core in ../certified_boundary/solver.py. It acquires all products
and local killed exponentials from rational input, integrates at s=0,
uses exact rational cofactors, and certifies stationary cylinder intervals.
No dense product-state matrix or exact stationary oracle occurs in this
implementation. Dense small fixtures may be used separately as comparators.
The first n=3 run used 4,080 positive nodes and 224-bit interval arithmetic,
returning [14824077,14824078]/2^27 in about 2.1 seconds. Its recorded input
and analytic budgets are in regenerative_receipt.json. This host timing is
not a general runtime theorem or a matched-baseline superiority claim.

Runtime in the local-contraction kernel is O(nodes*n*k*(k+number_of_events))
fixed-precision arithmetic operations, after local spectra are prepared;
prefix/suffix survival products avoid a redundant quadratic coordinate
loop. Matrix cofactors cost polynomial(k) exact rational operations.
The current node grid is stored explicitly, using O(nodes) interval pairs;
streaming is possible but not implemented. Input representation, interval
bits and rational denominator growth are all additional charged costs.

Baseline and significance boundary
----------------------------------
Renewal methods are the strongest natural baseline for this model class.
Equation (1) is itself that baseline, and considerably improves the first
Abel-based implementation. Consequently, this result cannot support a
claim of advantage over regenerative methods. The substantive unresolved
question is historical priority and reach of the fully acquired polynomial
bit-accuracy and exact-sampling composition for these succinct inputs.
That is not a demonstrated historic breakthrough. The investigation must
continue to a different mechanism rather than inflate this scope.
