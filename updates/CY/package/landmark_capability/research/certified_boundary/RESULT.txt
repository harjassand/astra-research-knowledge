CERTIFIED SMALL-BOUNDARY RESOLVENT PRIMITIVE
2026-10-10

Scope
-----
solver.py implements a positive log-time quadrature for the free resolvent
of an independent product of two-state continuous-time chains.  It returns
exact rational interval endpoints for the symmetrized boundary resolvent
K_B(s), the initial-law cross row w_mu(s), and the boundary hitting Laplace
transform.  The dense linear algebra is only m by m; no product state is
enumerated.  Inputs to certified_boundary_resolvent are exact Fractions.

The recorded instance has n=12, m=2, p_i=1/2, heterogeneous relaxation rates
3/4, 1, 5/4, ..., 7/2, product initial probabilities r_i=(i+1)/(i+3),
B={0,4095}, and s=2^-12.  Thus the implicit state space has 4096 states.
The run used 9,915 positive log-time nodes and completed in 6.44 seconds on
the recorded host.  The receipt contains the exact entries and all input
rationals.

Formula evaluated
------------------
For D=diag(pi), A=-D^(1/2) Q D^(-1/2), and beta_z=sqrt(pi(z)), define

    K_B(s) = [ (sI+A)^(-1) ]_{B,B},
    w_mu(s) = mu^T (sI-Q)^(-1)[:,B],
    c_z(s) = w_mu(s)_z / beta_z.

The strong Markov property gives

    E_mu exp(-s H_B) = c(s) K_B(s)^(-1) beta.

At each positive node u, K's integrand is e^(-su)[e^(-uA)]_{B,B}.  Its
entries are products of the two-state heat-kernel factors.  The cross-row
integrand is e^(-su) mu^T P_u(:,B), also evaluated as a product of scalar
two-state probabilities.  Every quadrature weight is positive.

Certified quadrature bound
--------------------------
Set k=100, delta=2^-k, J=k+10=110, and h=log(111/110).  The exact log-time
trapezoid for alpha>0 is

    Q_h(alpha) = h sum_{j in Z} exp(jh) exp(-alpha exp(jh)).

Poisson summation and the Mellin transform give

    alpha Q_h(alpha) = sum_{ell in Z} Gamma(1-2 pi i ell/h)
                                      alpha^(2 pi i ell/h).

For y real, |Gamma(1+iy)|^2=pi y/sinh(pi y).  Since
h<1/J and pi>3, t=2 pi^2 ell/h>18 J ell for ell>=1.  Using
sqrt(t/sinh t)<=2 sqrt(t) exp(-t/2) for t>=1 and e>2 bounds the nonzero
Fourier terms by

    4 sum_{ell>=1} sqrt(18 J ell) 2^(-9 J ell)
      <= 4 sum_{ell>=1} 2^(-8 J ell) <= 2^-J.

Here sqrt(18x)<=2^x for x=J ell>=110.

The omitted negative nodes have normalized sum at
most alpha q_L<=delta/64 because q_L<=delta/[64(s+Lambda)].  The omitted
positive nodes have normalized sum at most exp(-alpha q_U)<=2^-(k+8)
because q_U>=(k+8)/s.  Therefore the retained infinite-precision quadrature
has relative scalar error below delta uniformly for alpha in [s,s+Lambda].
The construction uses rational node ratio 111/110.  The logarithmic step,
each node, and every exponential are enclosed by integer dyadic intervals.

The exponential enclosure range-reduces to z in [0,1], encloses the
alternating Taylor series for exp(-z), and adds a one-unit remainder once
the next term is at most one internal fixed-point unit.  For arguments at
least 224, exp(-x)<=2^-x gives the enclosure [0,2^-224].  The logarithm is
enclosed by its positive atanh series with its geometric tail bound; square
roots use integer square root.  All additions, products, divisions, and
weighted sums round outwards on the 224-bit dyadic grid.

By spectral calculus, the quadrature matrix T satisfies

    (1-delta) K_B(s) <= T <= (1+delta) K_B(s),

so ||T-K_B(s)||_2<=delta/s.  Each entry interval in receipt.json is widened
by delta/s to include the true K entry.  For c, the same operator bound
gives ||c-c_quad||_infinity <= ||D^(-1/2)mu||_2 delta/s; the recorded
product law has a certified norm upper bound 3.9151794249058667.  The w
intervals include the corresponding beta-scaled error.

Inverse and final error budget
------------------------------
The spectrum of K_B(s), a principal compression of (sI+A)^(-1), is bounded
below by 1/(s+Lambda)=4096/104449.  With the recorded interval arithmetic,
the spectral error bound for the 2 by 2 midpoint matrix is
6.462348535570529e-27, less than half this lower eigenvalue.  Exact rational
inversion of the midpoint is therefore stable under the stated enclosure.
The three contributions to the final absolute error are:

    cross-row uncertainty:       6.451881546513959e-25
    inverse perturbation:         2.1530557284274827e-21
    beta uncertainty:              0
    total before output rounding:  2.153700916582134e-21

The returned 48-bit dyadic interval is

    269614546136653 / 281474976710656
    <= E_mu[exp(-s H_B)] <=
    269614546136654 / 281474976710656.

Its width is 2^-48, which is below the requested 30-bit width 2^-30.  The
interval and the exact K_B(s), w_mu(s) endpoint fractions are in receipt.json.

Limitations
-----------
This is a computational primitive with an explicit certificate for the
recorded case and a parameterized implementation for other rational inputs.
The current run fixes 224 internal bits and 100 bits of relative quadrature
accuracy; users should increase those settings in the source for harder
conditioning or narrower output requests.  The result certifies one
Laplace-transform value.  It does not by itself certify a full hitting-time
CDF, a sampler, theorem novelty, or a general polynomial bit-complexity
implementation.
