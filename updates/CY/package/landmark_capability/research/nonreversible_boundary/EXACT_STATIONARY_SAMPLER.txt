EXACT INDEPENDENT STATIONARY SAMPLES FROM UNBALANCED GLOBAL-GATE CHAINS
Internal constructive corollary, 2026-10-10. No priority claim.

Input class: the rational product-chain plus arbitrary recurring k-gate
generator Q=Q0+E_G Gamma E_G^T of UNBALANCED_STEADY_STATE.txt. Every local
Q_i is irreducible; Gamma needs no balance condition. The product state
space has N=prod_i d_i configurations, potentially exponential in input.

Theorem. A randomized algorithm outputs a configuration with distribution
EXACTLY equal to the unique stationary law pi of Q. It uses fair random
bits and has expected bit complexity polynomial in the full explicit input
length, n, k, and local dimensions. It neither enumerates N nor receives
pi or a mixing-time oracle. Independent repetitions give independent exact
stationary samples. Almost-sure termination and finite expected cost are
guaranteed; no worst-case finite running-time bound is asserted.

Proof 1: an explicit rational lower bound for every state and prefix

Use L,M,p,T,eta of equation (3) in UNBALANCED_STEADY_STATE.txt. The path
argument works with ANY destination y, not just one fixed x*: from every
x to every y there is a coordinate path of length at most L, padded by
self-loops to exactly L uniformization ticks. Thus

    [exp(TQ)](x,y)>=eta=(p/4)^L>0   for every x,y.   (1)

Stationarity gives pi(y)=sum_x pi(x)[exp(TQ)](x,y)>=eta. Every nonempty
prefix cylinder C_h={x:x_1=h_1,...,x_j=h_j} contains a full state and
therefore pi(C_h)>=eta. eta is rational and log(1/eta) is polynomial in
input length. This lower bound is deliberately conservative; it is never
paid as a numeric inverse in the sampler's running time.

Proof 2: polynomial-precision prefix mass oracle

For any prefix h, its indicator is a SINGLE product observable:

    1_{C_h}(x)=prod_{i<=j}1_{x_i=h_i} prod_{i>j}1.

The deterministic steady-state algorithm of UNBALANCED_STEADY_STATE.txt
therefore returns a rational p_hat(h,delta) satisfying

    |p_hat(h,delta)-pi(C_h)|<=delta                    (2)

in bit time polynomial in input length and log(1/delta). All errors,
including quadrature, matrix exponentials, small solves and Abel bias,
are included in this certified absolute bound. No exact expanded rational
stationary probabilities are required.

Proof 3: lazy exact inverse transform for each coordinate

At an already sampled prefix h of length j-1, let the d=d_j children have
true masses p_a=pi(C_{ha}) and parent mass P=sum_a p_a>=eta. Their exact
conditional cumulative thresholds are

    q_a=(p_1+...+p_a)/P, a=1,...,d-1.             (3)

Draw an independent U uniform on [0,1] by revealing fair binary digits
one at a time. After ell bits, U is known to lie in a dyadic interval
I_ell of width 2^(-ell). At the same stage, query all d child masses by
(2) with rational tolerance

    delta_ell=eta*2^(-ell)/(64*d).                (4)

Let tilde P and tilde S_a be their approximate total and cumulative sum.
Since d*delta_ell<=eta/128<=P/128, tilde P>0, and direct division gives

    |q_a-tilde S_a/tilde P|<=4*d*delta_ell/eta
                                <=2^(-ell)/16.  (5)

Form rational enclosing intervals J_{a,ell} of this radius around each
tilde S_a/tilde P, clipped to [0,1]. If I_ell lies strictly between two
successive threshold intervals (using 0 and 1 as outer endpoints), output
the corresponding child. Otherwise reveal one more bit and refine the
certified mass approximations. Every output decision is correct for the
actual thresholds (3), so its categorical law is exact. The probability
of an equality U=q_a is zero, hence termination is almost sure.

For fixed h and ell, continuing means I_ell meets one of at most d-1
uncertain threshold intervals. By (5), U is then within less than
2^(-ell)+2*2^(-ell)/16 of an exact q_a. A union bound gives

    Pr(continue after ell | h) <=3*d*2^(-ell).  (6)

The bound is uniform over all prefixes. Stage ell uses d calls to (2)
with log(1/delta_ell)=O(log(1/eta)+ell+log d), hence polynomial bit cost
in input length and ell. Summing that polynomial against the geometric
tail min(1,3d*2^(-ell)) proves polynomial expected cost for one
coordinate, hence for all n. Fresh independent U streams at successive
prefixes give the chain-rule distribution pi for the final configuration.

This is an exact distributional sampler, stronger than a TV-epsilon
sampler, but not a deterministic exact formula for pi. It obtains exactness
by lazy random comparison with certified approximations. Very rare near
ties make its worst-case work unbounded, while (6) controls expectation.

Practical finite-precision fallback

If a bounded-runtime sampler is desired, query each of the d_j child
masses once at delta=epsilon*eta/(4*n*max_i d_i), clamp negatives to zero,
and normalize. For each prefix, the categorical total variation error is
at most d_j*delta/eta<=epsilon/(4n). A coupling over n stages gives
total variation <=epsilon/4. The number of oracle calls is
sum_i d_i, and each precision has polynomial bit length. This fallback
also confirms that the exact sampler does not need an exponentially
accurate probability oracle on every run.

Novelty boundary

Sampling by refining certified probabilities until a uniform random
number separates from a threshold is a standard exact-sampling idea.
The substantive input here is the proved polynomial-precision stationary
prefix oracle for a nonproduct chain and the uniform polynomial-bit
positive lower bound eta, which make the expected whole-configuration
cost polynomial. No priority or broad black-box sampling claim is made.
