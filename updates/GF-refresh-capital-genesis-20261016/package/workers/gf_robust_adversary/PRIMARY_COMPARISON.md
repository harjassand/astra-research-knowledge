# Primary-method comparison

All papers below were inspected directly and acquired into the separate acquired/ folder. Raw PDFs and extracted text are **excluded from publication**. Their URLs, versions, hashes and acquisition status are recorded in the ledger. These are closest-method comparisons, not a priority search sufficient to establish novelty.

| Primary method | What it already establishes | Relevance and remaining interface here |
|---|---|---|
| [Watrous, 2009, Section 4, pp. 14–15](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/Papers/SDPforCBnorms.pdf) | An exact SDP for half the diamond norm of a channel difference. | The source additive CP majorant is a direct consequence. Optimizing the reference coefficient uses the same convex channel-testing machinery. This is established mathematics, not a new norm or fault probability. |
| [Gutoski–Watrous, 2007, Theorem 9, p. 13](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/Papers/GeneralTheoryQuantumGames.pdf) | A positive measuring-strategy outcome is bounded by its maximum forcing probability times a normalized causal strategy. | This is the all-pass tensorization engine. Shared bath memory belongs to the joint prover. The work-specific obligation is the independent local tester embedding and unchanged-action/lifecycle simulation. |
| [Gutoski, arXiv:1008.4636v4, Section 4.1, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4636) | Multiround strategy distinguishability and the normalized-strategy domination characterization. | A physical finite window with live intervention ports requires this type of process comparison. An identical terminal channel without interventions does not imply an identical strategy. The acquired PDF is v4, dated 14 March 2012, rather than the first 2010 submission. |
| [Terhal–Burkard, 2005; acquired arXiv v2, Sections 1.1–1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0402104) | Local non-Markovian fault tolerance with coherent interaction strength measured by half spectral width times gate time. | The spectral-radius/action and amplitude-squared distinction are old. That paper imposes a local-bath assumption during noninteracting gates; it should not alone be cited as the most permissive common-bath model. No inference from a reduced prepared-state calibration follows. |
| [Aharonov–Kitaev–Preskill, 2006, pp. 2–3, Eqs. 8–9 and 18–22](https://preskill.caltech.edu/pubs/preskill-2006-long-range.pdf) | Earliest-microfault resummation leaves later evolution normalized; prescribed local faulty-location amplitudes multiply, without bounding the bath Hamiltonian. Pair couplings require contraction accounting. | This is serious prior art for first-fault grouping and non-Markovian local coherent errors. A multi-reservation error cannot be charged independently to every parent. Here the optimized CP envelope couples those old tools to the specified ideal tester value and causal product. |
| [Viola–Knill, 2003; arXiv:quant-ph/0208056, Eqs. 2–3](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0208056) | Eulerian decoupling with bounded-strength paths; the toggling-frame drift depends on the whole control path, with a leading average-Hamiltonian limit. | The supplied finite pulse witness is consistent with this theory: endpoint equivalence does not make intervening baseline drift free. It is not a no-go theorem against optimized bounded controls. A finite-time residual and locality contract must still be supplied. |
| [Khodjasteh–Viola, 2009, arXiv:0906.0525, Eqs. 10–12 and 25](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.0525) | Dynamically corrected gates explicitly use a toggling-frame error-action operator and control higher Magnus terms within stated convergence conditions. | These methods can supply an improved isolated local residual angle if the complete joint operation is qualified. The paper's bath-modulo conventions, approximation error, available controls, private seed and concurrent live ports must be reconciled before importing a residual into this scheduled tester theorem. |

The generic state/Bures-angle probability tradeoff and convex optimization ingredients are established. The stronger statement in DERIVATION.md is a source-relative corollary: use optimized local CP majorants inside the causal first-erasure product to replace a linear coherent-action charge by an angle-composed parent factor. No new physical pulse, reservoir, detector, autonomous control primitive, or hardware threshold was found or qualified.

A plausible concrete next operation is an isolated, bounded-control refresh gadget with a confidence-bounded complete residual unitary/process contract. Pulse design methods can reduce its residual error action. The supplied packet does not contain the control inventory, full-joint bath bound, propagated correctable footprint, protected endpoint instrumentation, or local live-port qualification needed to instantiate that operation.
