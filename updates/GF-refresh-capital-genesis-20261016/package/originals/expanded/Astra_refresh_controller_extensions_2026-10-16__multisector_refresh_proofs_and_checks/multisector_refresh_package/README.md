# Exact multisector partial refresh

This packet establishes the sharp one-node endpoint-bad value with one good sector and K finite bad sectors, and its coordinate product extension when every fine projector commutes with every coarse parity. The actual full-sector channel must be given, its discarded environments private and independent, and node ordering fixed. Independent finite witnesses attain the product over the unrestricted finite-bath class.

Read proofs/SINGLETON_PROOF.md, then proofs/PRODUCT_PROOF.md. The three prerequisite documents are included unchanged in sources/ and identified by SOURCE_HASHES.json. The underlying ideal causal-tester and unchanged-action proof is included as sources/causal_tester_PROOF.md. The causal positive-domination theorem is an explicitly cited external mathematical prerequisite. Checks corroborate the proof; they are not a hardware validation. Historical priority is not established. No new license grant is made for this analysis or the included source prerequisites.

## Critical boundary

This packet is separate from finite-width private-Z refresh. Do not automatically replace binary F by F_K in a finite-width remainder R. First prove the actual multisector controller channel, commutation, privacy and approximation assumptions. A physical random phase from one Z parity yields binary dephasing, not full stabilizer-group pinching. Endpoint-bad bounds also do not independently establish syndrome-history support, reservation, or completion claims.
