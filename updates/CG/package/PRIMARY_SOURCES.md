# Targeted primary-source check

The mathematical candidates were derived before these checks. Search absence is not evidence of novelty. This is a boundary check, not a broad literature audit.

## Direct antecedent that closed the pivot-chart branch

Clément de Seguins Pazzis, *Local linear dependence seen through duality I*, arXiv:1306.1845, revised 2014; Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 219 (2015).

https://arxiv.org/html/1306.1845

- Proposition 3.6 and its proof use the same lower-/strictly-upper-triangular pivot decomposition as the independently derived binary-safe update.
- Theorem 3.8 states the sharp LLD low-rank existence fact over all fields.
- The small-field counting proof and the large-field Flanders-Atkinson route distinguish existence from the particular efficient acquisition mechanism.

No novelty is claimed for the pivot-chart alternative, its high-rank existence premise, or the use of triangular perturbations.

## General bilinear-system baseline

Charles R. Johnson, Helena Šmigoc, Dian Yang, *Solution Theory for Systems of Bilinear Equations*, arXiv:1303.4988 (2013).

https://arxiv.org/html/1303.4988

The paper treats common bilinear equations, solution-preserving transformations, linear fibers after fixing one side, and solvability for all right-hand sides. Merely fixing one block and solving a linear system is not new. The present sampler's tested extra operation is exact singular-fiber reweighting by dual incidence.

## Rank-metric / linearized-polynomial certificate baseline

Sven Puchinger and Antonia Wachter-Zeh, *Fast operations on linearized polynomials and their applications in coding theory*, Journal of Symbolic Computation (2018).

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsc.2017.11.012

This is a primary algorithmic source for linearized-polynomial operations and the Gabidulin-code setting. The root-count/rank bound and Moore interpolation used in the proposed native certifier are established ingredients. They are not claimed as inventions.

## Exact sampling already exists for a single quadratic equation

Chandan Dubey and Thomas Holenstein, *Sampling a Uniform Solution of a Quadratic Equation Modulo a Prime Power*, APPROX/RANDOM 2014.

https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol028-approx-random2014/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2014.643/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2014.643.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.0281

The work gives randomized polynomial-time exact uniform sampling for one integral quadratic equation modulo a prime power. Therefore neither exact uniformity on a quadratic variety nor the single-constraint case is a new capability by itself.

## Scope of the unfinished novelty question

The targeted searches included exact uniform sampling of high-rank bilinear/quadratic fibers, low-bias polynomial systems, rank-metric code structure, and kernel-incidence/rejection mechanisms. They did not locate or rule out an exact antecedent for the specific multi-constraint dual-kernel law with arbitrary one-sided target circuit and nonlinear graph-restriction closure. Only a formal wider priority check could establish that. No publication or foundational novelty assertion is justified by this pass.
