## What changes the synthesis

The circuitwise rate-free gate theorem is a legitimate conditional extension
of N769/N771 in the source's complete-network interface. Diagonal scaling
preserves the whole stoichiometric kernel exactly when it is constant on
matroid circuit components; its short support-minimal proof checks out.
A dormant zero-baseline exchange collapses that independence and violates
the relocated state. Thus a baseline zero net flux is not a certificate that
the channel is absent. FO's calibrated contrast/robust decision machinery
can reduce the measurements needed for one outcome, but cannot supply
structural coverage or valid legal gates on an unknown reaction network.
The source's equally informed direct allocation/ECM comparison remains strong.
No new physical chemical-control capability follows from combining them.

The remote-response family makes a particularly useful companion to the
source-current certificate. Its exact product ground state and arbitrarily
many source-near moments do not reveal a remote uncertain coefficient.
An exact feasible current still upper-certifies the response when the complete
factor/source are supplied; verifying that current needs the actual remote
row or a rigorous uncertainty envelope. Avoiding a global spectral-gap
number does not avoid that coefficient acquisition. The exact symbolic path
solve remains O(m), while the stated evolution-only interface needs order1/e
summed interrogation time to distinguish the two worlds. This is consistent
with, not a refutation of, the conditional N816/N798 current construction.

The rare-channel lower bound has the same acquisition lesson in a different
interface. A learned committor with zero residual only on visited states is
not a global certificate. Free conditioning through known barriers does not
acquire the unknown terminal branch. Nothing in the profile storage, current
duality or temporal-label identities supplies such an unknown-row oracle.
The lower bound is for the explicitly stated classical local-row access,
not a universal molecular-computation no-go.

The DNA-frame route makes the distinction between a valid target and actual
repair explicit. Surviving sequence, primer and complement do not determine
erased routing at an ambiguous junction. A retained typed covalent route or
unique valid matching can specify a restricted repair target; enzyme access,
end chemistry, fidelity, retention and total cost remain separate. The source
credits real staple-resupply, designed gap-fill and ligation comparators.
FO's retained-kernel discipline applies as a requirement for measurement,
not as a theorem transferring GaAs/ferroelectric rates to DNA.

If a scientific task needs only a function of topology rather than exact
routing, the information requirement can be weakened to constancy of that
function on each survivor-equivalence class. This is the same established
target-estimability principle as FO's chemical contrast, not an invented
repair operation; no physical functional-restoration advantage is shown.

The late capital therefore strengthens the supplied-versus-acquired audit and
exposes specific handoffs, but closes none of FO's unrestricted-profile,
chemical-state or phase-selected-retention gates. The independent Pro/Ultra
judgement and the draft's NOT_ACHIEVED capability status are preserved.
