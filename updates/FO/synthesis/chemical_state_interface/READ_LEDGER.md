## Access limits and unknowns retained

- Hwang’s isotope fractions require ≥99 atom-% labelled methanol, quench, acid dissolution, filtration, flash chromatography, and quantitative NMR. The full work does not report total study cost, complete instrument hours, neutrality controls for the labels, or an industrial yield/lifetime/economic comparison.
- The intervention intentionally conditions the methylbenzene pool. It shows a measured subsequent selectivity response, but does not isolate every collateral change in catalyst history or establish a commercially useful lifetime benefit.
- H-ZSM-5 FASPA isotope responses are pooled mass-fragment fits. The C3+ channel combines multiple aliphatic species, and the SI says the fit assumes similar isotope contributions across them and admits no further refinement. Intact propylene-only isotope-count detection fraction and covariance/interference model for the proposed higher-order statistic remain UNKNOWN.
- A same-regime, internally selective poison or spectroscopic chemical-state tag for high-temperature MTO was not acquired. DTBP data at 403 K in MFI do not establish access, neutrality, stability, competitive adsorption, or interpretability at 673 K, and do not transfer to HSAPO-34 CHA.
- Conditioning, feed, isotope, catalyst, apparatus, operator, and full acquisition costs are not available as a matched head-to-head comparison. No numerical resource advantage is claimed.
- The downloadable FASPA dataset and its MD5 establish a data-access/integrity fact only. They do not make it the ACS isotope dataset, validate its chemistry, or supply absent ACS raw data or fitting code.
