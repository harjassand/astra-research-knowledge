# Acoustic faces obstruct positive synthesis of strict generators

This is a root generalization of the separately saved Fstar-to-G obstruction
in `profile_prior/cone_followup/REPORT.md`. The specific obstruction was
derived after root suggested the acoustic-null test; the worker supplied the
closure argument, exact strict bound and Jensen witness. The theorem here
isolates the reusable mechanism. No historical priority is claimed.

Let F be a homogeneous quartic polynomial on R^{2×2} satisfying, for some c>0,

\[
 D^2F(X)[a\otimes b,a\otimes b]\ge c|X|^2|a|^2|b|^2
 \quad\text{for all }X,a,b.
\]

Let C be the coefficient-closed cone generated by F(A S), S∈GL₂(R),
and arbitrary homogeneous polyconvex quartics. Fix an invertible B₀ and
nonzero vectors a₀,b₀. If the homogeneous quartic polynomial H∈C has

\[
 D^2H(B_0)[a_0\otimes b_0,a_0\otimes b_0]=0,
\]

then for **every finite probability law** μ with
EμA=Ā and Eμdet A=det Ā,

\[
 \mathbb E_\mu H(A)-H(\bar A)\ge0. \tag{1}
\]

Consequently a single negative lifted Jensen witness for a target with such
an acoustic zero separates it from C. This is a criterion for positive
transfer from a strictly acoustic generator, not a quasiconvexity criterion.
The witness law need not be a compatible gradient law.

## Proof

Normalize |S|=1 by absorbing its fourth power into positive coefficients.
For h_S(A)=F(AS), the null-probe functional ℓ obeys

\[
 \ell(h_S)\ge c\,\sigma_{\min}(B_0)^2|a_0|^2|S^Tb_0|^2.
\]

For any fixed orthonormal input basis e₁,e₂, the sum Λ of the two acoustic
probes at B₀ in output direction a₀ obeys

\[
 \Lambda(h_S)\ge c\,\sigma_{\min}(B_0)^2|a_0|^2>0.
\]

Both ℓ and Λ are nonnegative on polyconvex quartics, since these are
rank-one convex. If positive orbit sums plus polyconvex terms converge
coefficientwise to H, Λ bounds their total normalized orbit weight. The
vanishing target ℓ makes the weight outside every neighborhood of
Z={S:|S|=1,Sᵀb₀=0} tend to zero. This compact set consists of rank-one
matrices, because there are exactly two input columns and b₀≠0.

For S=v⊗w in Z, h_S(A)=F((Av)⊗w) is convex in A: every difference of
arguments in the fixed-row subspace {z⊗w} is rank one, so rank-one
convexity of F restricts to ordinary convexity on that whole subspace.
Thus its Jensen functional Jμ(h_S) is nonnegative on Z. The functional
S↦Jμ(h_S) is continuous on the compact normalized sphere. Its negative
part is uniformly small near Z; its weighted contribution away from Z
tends to zero. Bounded total weight then gives liminf Jμ(orbit sum)≥0.
The polyconvex part has Jμ≥0 by convex Jensen in (A,det A), because μ
has the specified lifted barycenter. Taking the coefficient limit proves (1).

Neither compactness of the polyconvex summands nor a closedness theorem for
their cone is used. Their acoustic probes and this one Jensen functional
are individually nonnegative. Signed combinations, position-dependent
right transforms, other generators lacking the strict bound, or sums of
unrelated certified mechanisms are outside the asserted obstruction.

## Exact Astra instance

Fstar from N794 satisfies the strict bound with c=1/16, independently
reconstructed from its rational acoustic Gram. For G from N803/N817,
B₀=I,a₀=e₁,b₀=e₂ gives G(I+t e₁⊗e₂)=−1/24+3t⁴. The finite law on
±36I and ±diag(1,−1), with total pair masses1/1297 and1296/1297,
has zero matrix/determinant barycenter and EμG=−1350/1297.
Equation (1) would forbid that negative value. Hence G lies outside the
coefficient-closed positive right-orbit/polyconvex cone of Fstar.

This explains why a stronger G certificate needs another mechanism rather
than a positive recoding of this particular completed Astra theorem. The
continuous profile storage is one such restricted-family mechanism. The
result does not show G is nonquasiconvex, and does not exclude an independent
global proof of G or a transfer from other non-strict generators.
