## Original derivation: make latent reversible reactions silent at the target

Take N769's ideal isothermal, reversible elementary mass-action law with known detailed-balance reference `q`, known modeled reaction support, known positive baseline and independently multiplied forward/backward gates. For a modeled nonzero-current reaction, N769's factor `rho_r(h)` moves concentrations from `c` to `c'=c exp(h)`; setting `e'_r=e_r rho_r(h) gamma_r` makes `j'_r=gamma_r j_r` without knowing its absolute rate constant. N817 proves that `S diag(gamma) ker S ⊆ ker S` holds exactly when gamma is constant on each active circuit component of the *complete supplied* stoichiometry. Its fifth-edge counterexample shows the danger of an omitted, baseline-zero exchange.

Now suppose a specified candidate set `H` of omitted reversible reactions has fixed, known internal stoichiometric columns `s_r≠0`, a compatible known `q`, positive gates, and **independently uncertain** absolute coefficients `kappa_r` varying over a box with nonempty interior. Let modeled reactions be exactly balanced at the chosen target. The unknown contribution to the internal steady-state residual is

`R_H(c')=Σ_(r∈H) s_r e_r kappa_r[(c'/q)^(a_r)−(c'/q)^(b_r)]`.

Because each coefficient varies independently, `R_H(c')=0` for **every** admissible coefficient vector if and only if each vector coefficient is zero. Since `s_r≠0` and `e_r>0`, this is equivalent to `s_r·log(c'/q)=0` for each hidden reaction. If each is known to be equilibrated at baseline and reservoirs are fixed, the target condition reduces to the linear constraints **`s_r·h=0`**. The forward direction follows by varying one `kappa_r` while holding the others fixed; the reverse follows by substitution. This is an elementary robust-identifiability result original to this report, under its exact independent-box and detailed-balance premises. If an omitted reaction has zero internal column but affects an external product, this only certifies *internal stationarity*, not the product objective.

This does **not** contradict N817's maximal all-`ker S` theorem. N817 asks for stationarity for every possible current of the complete network. The present operation asks for stationarity of the particular physically admitted family in which each candidate hidden channel is zero at both endpoints. The condition can preserve distinct modeled pathway scales even if adding a hidden edge connects the complete network's circuit matroid. It needs an acquired candidate support and equilibrium relation; it cannot guard against arbitrary unseen reactions, driven cycles or unknown saturation. A hidden rate can still alter transient stability, noise, enzyme resource use and side products even when its net current vanishes at the target.
