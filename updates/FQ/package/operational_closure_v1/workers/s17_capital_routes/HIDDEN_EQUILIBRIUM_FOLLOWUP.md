## A finite-snapshot ring witness and the tighter path comparison

For any visible binary indicator `b_i=1{h(i)=B}` and fixed `t>0`, let `a` be a **preselected** number in `(0,1)` and observe only `Y_{−t},Y_0,Y_t`. Put

`W=b(X_0)(b(X_{−t})−a)(b(X_t)−a)`.

The hidden `X` in this notation defines the law, while the score itself uses only the three visible labels. It is reversal even. Under every stationary reversible hidden CTMC, conditional independence of past and future at `X_0` gives `E W=Σ_i π_i b_i [P_t(b−a)(i)]²≥0`. Its minimum is `−a(1−a)` and its range length is `max(a,1−a)`.

There is a sharper transfer than N512's comparison to S for this particular reflected square. Let `p` and `q` be the full hidden path densities of Q and Q* on `[0,2t]`; set `ν=√(pq)` and `μ=(p+q)/2`. The **unnormalized** measure ν has initial π and a killed semigroup with `G_ij=√(Q_ij Q*_ij)` for `i≠j` and `G_ii=Q_ii`. It is π-selfadjoint; row sums are nonpositive by arithmetic-geometric mean against S. Thus, centering the window at t,

`E_ν W=Σ_i π_i b_i [e^{tG}(b−a)(i)]²≥0`.
