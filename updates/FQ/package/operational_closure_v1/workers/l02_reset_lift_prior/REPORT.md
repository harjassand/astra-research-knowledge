# Prior-art report: reset lift, parity, and binary renewal output

Date: 2026-10-15  
Scope: primary prior art relevant to the proposed stationary (Q/Q^*) doubled CTMC, with copy parity swapped by time reversal and copy changes allowed at a single reset state; its binary single-reset observation; odd-parity entropy production; and equilibrium nonmonotonic dwell laws.

## Finding

The strongest direct antecedent is Barra, Clerc, and Tirapegui (1997): its abstract says that *any* stationary Markov process can be put in generalized detailed balance by adding odd parameters, “by explicit construction.” This is close enough that the generic idea of adjoining an odd label to restore generalized detailed balance is not safely novel. The accessible abstract does not reveal whether their construction is the exact two-copy (Q/Q^*) generator, whether it restricts label changes to the reset state, or whether it preserves a specified coarse observation law. The exact specialization is therefore **UNKNOWN** pending the article body.

Separately, the proposed binary output has a standard exact reversibility antecedent. Wang and Qian (2007) state that a stationary semi-Markov process is time-reversible exactly when its embedded chain obeys detailed balance and its transition direction is independent of the sojourn time. A two-state alternating-renewal process has only one possible successor from each state, so direction-time independence is automatic; its embedded two-state chain is reversible. Thus the whole stationary binary path law is time-reversal invariant, regardless of whether the hidden `Q` has positive all-even entropy production. This is an output-level fact, not a finite-state equilibrium realization of every hidden generator.

I found no inspected source that states the exact reset-only `2Q/2Q*` lift and proves equality of its full binary output law for every finite irreducible single-reset CTMC. That non-finding is not a novelty claim: the 1997 generalized-detailed-balance construction is a close broad preemption, and its full construction was not available for comparison. **Novelty remains UNKNOWN.**

## Construction check and boundary of the claim

Let `Q` be irreducible on finite `E`, with stationary law `π` and stationary reverse `q*_{ij}=π_j q_{ji}/π_i` for `i ≠ j`. On `E × {+,-}`, place `Q` in the `+` copy and `Q*` in the `−` copy, add symmetric rates `κ > 0` between `(a,+)` and `(a,−)`, and use `θ(i,+)=(i,−)`, `θ(i,−)=(i,+)`. With `π/2` in each copy, within-copy generalized detailed balance follows from `π_i q_{ij}=π_j q*_{ji}`; the reset flip satisfies it by symmetry. This is a direct algebraic specialization of stationary time reversal plus an odd-parity involution, not an independent literature theorem established here.

The parity-aware Markov path entropy pairs rates `q(x,y)` and `q(θy,θx)`, not the all-even pairing `q(y,x)`. Consequently zero parity-aware EP does not imply zero ordinary all-even CTMC EP and does not itself establish a physical implementation of the parity coordinate. A physical equilibrium claim needs a model and local-detailed-balance interpretation for that odd variable.

The observed process `Y_t = 1{X_t=a}` is a stationary alternating renewal process: its `a` sojourn is exponential with rate `−q_{aa}`, and its off-sojourn is the return-time PH law of the killed chain on `E\{a}`. Stationary reversal preserves the full observed path law. This conclusion follows directly from the semi-Markov reversibility criterion below; it does not imply that the hidden path of `Q` is reversible.

## Strongest inspected sources

1. **Felipe Barra, Marcel Clerc, and Enrique Tirapegui (1997), “Detailed balance in non-equilibrium systems,” *Dynamics and Stability of Systems* 12(1), 61–70, DOI [10.1080/02681119708806236](https://doi.org/10.1080/02681119708806236).** The author-uploaded article page and abstract were inspected at [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/245326839_Detailed_balance_in_non-equilibrium_systems), lines 0–30. The abstract explicitly claims an explicit construction for any stationary Markov process by adding odd parameters and says the construction has a timescale interpretation. The full text link returned access denied in this run, so the construction’s state space, assumptions, and overlap with reset-only flips were not checked. This is the key unresolved preemption check.

2. **Hongyun Wang and Hong Qian (2007), “On detailed balance and reversibility of semi-Markov processes and single-molecule enzyme kinetics,” *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 48, 013303, DOI [10.1063/1.2432065](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2432065).** The primary author-hosted PDF [qiandetbal2.pdf](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/~miekisz/qiandetbal2.pdf) was indexed and its abstract and definitions were inspected. The abstract states the iff criterion: embedded-chain detailed balance plus direction-time independence. Their definitions formalize time reversal for the stationary semi-Markov process and DTI as `Q(i,j,t)=p_{ij}Q_i(t)`. For a two-state alternating renewal, `p_{01}=p_{10}=1`, so DTI is automatic. This is exact preemption of any claim that the binary output itself has an asymmetric stationary path law; it is not a hidden CTMC realization result.

3. **Felipe Barra, Clerc, and Tirapegui’s broader mathematical framework is also consistent with the standard parity-aware EP formula in Christian Maes and Karel Netočný (2003), “Time-Reversal and Entropy,” [arXiv:cond-mat/0202501](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0202501).** I inspected the primary PDF, especially its continuous-time Markov expression (6.11) on p. 24, which compares `q(M,M')` with `q(πM',πM)` under a time-reversal involution `π`. This confirms that zero EP under an involution is a standard generalized-reversibility notion. It does not state the reset-only paired-copy construction.

4. **David Hartich and Aljaž Godec (2024), “Comment on ‘Inferring broken detailed balance in the absence of observable currents’,” *Nature Communications* 15, 8678, DOI [10.1038/s41467-024-52602-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52602-0), [full primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11458581/).** The full article was inspected. Its explicit detailed-balanced hidden-intermediate model has direction-conditioned waiting density \(\psi_x(t)=\frac{8}{3}(e^{-2t}-e^{-8t})\), which starts at zero and has a positive-time maximum. It is an equilibrium counterexample to treating a humped transition-conditioned waiting density as a universal nonequilibrium signature. The model is a hidden-reaction motor and does not reproduce the proposed binary single-reset observation or parity lift.

5. **Allan T. Andersen, Marcel F. Neuts, and Bo Friis Nielsen (2004), “On the Time Reversal of Markovian Arrival Processes,” *Stochastic Models* 20(2), 237–260, DOI [10.1081/STM-120034131](https://doi.org/10.1081/STM-120034131).** The author-university record and abstract were inspected at [DTU](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/on-the-time-reversal-of-markovian-arrival-processes/); it establishes that the stationary time reverse of a MAP is also a MAP and that many interval/counting descriptors are orientation-insensitive. The primary article full text was not available in the retrieved record. A same-author thesis quotes its Theorem 4.1 as listing phase-type renewal processes and two-state MAPs among reversible MAPs ([thesis, chapter 4](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/60557791/Bo_Friis_Nielsen_Doctoral_Thesis.pdf), pp. 37–38); this theorem wording is secondary evidence here, not independently verified from the article body. It concerns event-process reversibility, not an all-even reversible CTMC realization of arbitrary PH waits.

6. **Dong-Kyum Kim, Sangyun Lee, and Hawoong Jeong (2022), “Estimating entropy production in a stochastic system with odd-parity variables,” [arXiv:2112.04681v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.04681v2).** The primary full HTML was inspected, especially its odd-velocity ring CTMC (Sec. III, Eqs. 15–26) and the parity-corrected trajectory-EP formula. It confirms that discrete odd variables and parity-aware jump EP are standard. It studies EP estimation, not a generic stationary reverse-generator lift.

## Unsupported assumptions and remaining decisive check

- The accessible 1997 abstract does not tell whether the “odd parameters” are a two-copy state label with `Q/Q*` blocks, nor whether the authors prove exact observational equivalence. Do not call the reset-only construction new until that body is obtained and compared.
- A parity-aware zero of the Markov path-ratio EP is mathematically exact for the specified involution; it does not by itself imply ordinary detailed balance or a physically realizable equilibrium device.
- MAP/renewal reversibility is about the observed stationary event/path law. It is not evidence that every PH law has a finite all-even reversible internal CTMC representation.
- The Hartich–Godec humped law is event-conditioned in a multi-channel hidden-intermediate model. It should not be silently relabeled as a binary alternating-renewal dwell distribution.
- **Decisive next prior-art test:** retrieve and inspect the full Barra–Clerc–Tirapegui paper, reconstruct its odd-parameter generator and involution, and compare state by state with the proposed `Q/Q*` blocks and reset-only symmetric switch. Also check whether it proves equality of the chosen coarse observation process, rather than only generalized detailed balance of an augmented Markov model.

No experiment or simulation was performed for this literature task. No claim of physical realization, all-even reversibility, or novelty is made.
