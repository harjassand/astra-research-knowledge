# S09 — Amplitude-cancelled pulse-order magnetometry at a buried battery interface

15 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane. **Status: invented operation, mathematically checked on an explicit model, physically untested; consequential advantage and priority UNKNOWN.** No laboratory data were produced. This is a task-specific pulse/readout primitive, not a quantum-over-classical speedup claim. It uses an experimentally demonstrated quantum spin interaction at a material interface and asks a new, directly falsifiable question about local current partition.

## Scientific task and physical opening

The target is the hidden spatial distribution of *order-sensitive surface charge storage* in a formed Fe3O4/Li conversion electrode, particularly the Fe/Li2O low-voltage regime. An aggregate zero-net-charge pulse can leave different individual particles in different states even when terminal coulometry reports no net charge. The actionable output is a map of locations where the **order of otherwise matched charging/discharging pulses changes the local magnetic state nonlinearly with pulse amplitude**. A verified map could tell a materials researcher which contact networks or interfacial storage regions fail under a proposed fast-charge waveform, before committing to long cycling. It is not itself an oxidation-state map, a safety certificate, or proof of irreversible damage.

The physical coupling is real but the proposed operation has not been run. Liu et al. embedded micrometre-scale NV-bearing diamond particles inside a Fe3O4/carbon/PVDF electrode and optically read their spin resonances through a quartz window while discharging a Li cell. A roughly 89 G bias field made nearby Fe3O4/FeO/Fe conversion visible in the local magnetic stray field. The P0 field fell from 91.4 to 71.4 G in the first 50 minutes, then rose to 106.4 G by 115 minutes, while the bulk conversion took about 567 minutes; they saw different histories at five sensor positions. This is a demonstrated local sensor–specimen interaction, not a demonstration of the pulse protocol or a calibrated single-particle state inversion. Their focused optical beam heated some locations, and their reported 270 mW laser power was an apparatus setting, not a universal harmless-power bound. Their in situ XRD scans took 900 s. See the [open primary paper PDF](https://www.iop.cas.cn/xwzx/kydt/202409/P020240924528848148977.pdf), pp. 3–7, especially experimental procedures on p. 7.

Li et al. used *bulk* in situ magnetometry on Fe3O4/Li and found magnetic evidence consistent with substantial additional low-voltage charge storage on Fe nanoparticle surfaces. Their interpretation makes the formed Fe/Li2O interface consequential, but it supplies neither a local NV calibration nor a proof that this region is reversible under the pulse amplitudes below. See the [primary Nature Materials article](https://ciqm.harvard.edu/uploads/2/3/3/4/23349210/li2021.pdf), p. 1 abstract and introduction. The first gate is therefore to establish a bounded, repeatable surface-charge window in the exact proposed cells.

## The operation

At a fixed formed-electrode baseline, fabricate several independently addressable matched microelectrode lanes under one optical/thermal environment, with embedded NV diamonds near active Fe/Li2O regions and reference NV diamonds away from active material. A programmable potentiostat applies two equal-duration, equal-magnitude, opposite-current segments. The two orders are `+U then −U` and `−U then +U`; each has zero commanded net charge. Repeat both orders at amplitudes `U` and `rU`, where `r>1` is chosen below measured voltage and heat limits. Record current, voltage, temperature, magnetic bias, optical power and actual cell energy for every lane. Randomize the four arm assignments across lanes/chips and image each lane before and after the pair at the same zero-current rest delay. Because a damaged conversion electrode cannot be assumed to reset exactly, the four arms require matched sacrificial/replicate lanes or empirically verified resetting; sequentially cycling one site does not provide four independent initial states.

Use optically detected magnetic resonance of the NV spin. For a calibrated orientation in the approximately axial regime, its two transitions satisfy `nu_± ≈ D(T,strain) ± gamma_NV B_parallel`; their separation gives `y=(nu_+−nu_-)/(2 gamma_NV)`, suppressing the common zero-field shift `D` from temperature to first order. The four NV orientations, cross-axis fields, strain and changing line widths require a full spectral fit and coil calibration; the simple expression is only the readout intuition. Make the primary outcome the measured local field or a prespecified raw spectral contrast if the spectrum is not identifiable. Read at zero electrode current so the direct Oersted field of the pulse is absent. Subtract the change of a nearby reference NV to reduce coil and ambient drift. Do not turn this relative field into lithium concentration without independent calibration.

Let `d_{+-}(U)` be the active-minus-reference field change after `+U,−U` and `d_{-+}(U)` after `−U,+U`, each from its own baseline. Define the order contrast `C(U)=E[d_{+-}(U)]−E[d_{-+}(U)]`. The **amplitude-cancelled order operation** is

`K_r(U) = C(rU) − r C(U)`.

Any linear input-to-measured-field system, including a convolution with *any unknown linear relaxation kernel*, has `C(rU)=r C(U)` and hence `K_r=0` exactly. Thus the extra amplitude pair cancels the fatal alternative to a raw order difference: a mundane linear RC or magnetic relaxation process can make `C(U)≠0` even in the absence of nonlinear interfacial physics. This cancellation assumes the same input waveform shape, initial-state distribution and detector gain across amplitudes; saturation or heating of the NV readout also generates a false `K_r` and must be calibrated.

## Original mathematical mechanism and a counterexample

Write the unobserved electrode state as `X`, its dynamics under fixed current `u` as `dX/dt=f_u(X)`, and local magnetic output as `m(X)`. For two fields `f_+`, `f_-` applied for duration `tau`, the two final states differ by

`X_{+-}−X_{-+} = tau² [f_+,f_-](X_0) + O(tau³)`,

where `[f_+,f_-]=D f_- f_+ − D f_+ f_-`. Consequently `C(U,tau)=tau² Dm(X_0)[f_{+U},f_{-U}]+O(tau³)` when matched lanes share the same initial-state law and readout map. This is an operationally acquired *projected vector-field bracket*, not an acquired microscopic kinetic model.

If `f_u=f_0+u g+u² h`, algebra gives `[f_{+U},f_{-U}]=−2U[f_0,g]−2U³[h,g]`. The first term includes ordinary relaxation. At leading order in `tau`, `K_r=−2(r³−r)U³ tau² Dm[h,g]+O(tau³)`; the `U` term is removed. Higher powers of `u`, nonlinear magnetic readout, and finite-`tau` effects can also contribute. This expansion therefore identifies a structured hypothesis, not a unique chemical species.

An exact illustrative model makes the mechanism and limit concrete. Let `x` be local active-particle charge, `z` an initially hidden current-blocking/contact state, and `u=±U`:

`dx/dt = v u(1−z),        dz/dt = a u²−b z,        y=k x`, with `a,b,v,k>0`.

The missing fraction of local active-particle current is routed elsewhere in the electrode; this is a local partition model, not a whole-cell charge-balance model. If both arms start at `z=0`, the two orders yield exactly

`C(U,tau)= 2 k v a U³ (1−exp(−b tau))² / b²`,

and `K_r(U,tau)=(r³−r)C(U,tau)`, so for short pulses `K_r=2 k v a(r³−r)U³ tau²+O(tau³)`. If the matched arms start at a common nonzero `z_0`, `C` has an additional term linear in `U`, and the same amplitude combination removes that term. The retained `K_r` is a possible readout of even-in-current gating of local charge partition without first estimating `a`, `b`, or `z_0`.

There is a decisive **counterexample to overinterpretation**. A purely linear relaxing state `dq/dt=−bq+c u`, read out as `y=q`, has a nonzero `+−` versus `−+` contrast proportional to `U(1−exp(−b tau))²`; it nevertheless has `K_r=0`. Conversely, a nonzero `K_r` need not mean damage: reversible nonlinear diffusion, field-dependent magnetization, detector saturation, local heating `∝U²`, or different initial states can all produce it. Chemical assignment requires independent measurements. The symbolic derivation and exact toy formula were executed in [verify_order_contrast.py](verify_order_contrast.py); its output is a calculation receipt, not a hardware result.

## Specimen, loss, calibration and total cost

The proposed sample is a research microcell, not a commercial sealed battery. Pattern matched Fe3O4 composite lanes with separate electrical leads in a shared transparent-window Li half-cell; immobilize sparse NV-diamond inclusions at known positions, provide microwave and optical access, and hold a calibrated bias magnet. Test the formed low-voltage Fe/Li2O window first with conventional cycling and post hoc chemistry. The published apparatus supports embedded NV readout on discharge; lane patterning, repeated reversal, time resolution for these pulses, spatial registration and electrical isolation are new engineering obligations. Interspersed diamond could alter porosity/contact/chemistry; use otherwise identical undoped lanes to measure that perturbation. Optical access depth and particle-to-sensor geometry must be measured rather than inferred from a nominal NV size. Existing analysis notes that depth resolution, optical/microwave access, and invasiveness constrain embedded NV battery sensing; see [Pollok et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63409-y), especially “Prospects and limitations.”

Calibrate `y` against an external field coil at every relevant SOC and temperature; track NV charge-state/contrast, line width, all selected orientations, sensor position and bias-field drift. Alternate active and reference measurements in time. Run nonmagnetic electrode and open-circuit pulse shams to measure optical, microwave, Joule-heating and potentiostat pickup. Measure `C` and `K_r` at at least three `tau` and several `U`, and require a stable small-pulse scaling region before using the bracket interpretation. Record full spectra, not only fitted peaks. A raw order effect disappearing after the same zero-current rest delay is compatible with relaxation; persistent changes still need chemical corroboration. For a field noise floor `eta_B` in T/sqrt(Hz), four independent before/after active/reference readings per arm and two arms give ideal readout standard error proportional to `sqrt(8) eta_B/sqrt(N t)` for `N` replicates with `t` integration per reading; specimen heterogeneity, drift and spectral-fit uncertainty add separately. `K_r` propagates both amplitudes' errors, roughly by `sqrt(r²+1)` when independent. The actual `eta_B` and optical integration time must be measured in the assembled cell; source-level sensitivity from a different NV microscope cannot fill this gate.

Cost per four-arm block includes four cells/lanes and their fabrication/formation, eight current segments, all state-matching/reset assays, eight before/after NV spectra plus references, optical/microwave/bias-field calibration, replication, and destructive endpoint chemistry. Applied charge cancels *within each two-pulse arm*, but the dissipated cell energy `E_cell=∫ V(t)I(t)dt` need not cancel and can differ between orders. Charge `E_laser=P_inc t_on`, microwave source power, bias-field supply, potentiostat, temperature control and data acquisition; measure absorbed optical heat rather than equating it to incident power. The 270 mW incident laser figure in Liu et al. is a warning to audit dose in the new geometry. Added interfaces can shorten cell life or change the very kinetics being measured. No numerical total-cost advantage is established here.

## Strongest equally informed competitors and priority

The strongest quantum competitor is **the same embedded NV sensor with ordinary operando spectra and optimized current-pulse design**. It receives identical specimens, controls, calibration and readout time, and can compute `K_r` from the four raw arm means once the operation is known. Thus this proposal has no information-theoretic advantage over that competitor; its possible contribution is the choice of active excitation and a physically interpretable contrast that rejects unknown linear relaxation. A comparator handed the identical four-arm raw data cannot be beaten by deterministic post-processing of those data. The 2024 embedded-NV experiment already measured heterogeneous local kinetics, so merely producing another local magnetic movie is not new.

The strongest non-NV competitors are (i) identical four-arm electrochemical voltage/current/temperature measurements with nonlinear impedance or pulse models and (ii) material-appropriate operando single-particle optical imaging or X-ray methods. Gasper et al. experimentally collected about 50,000 DC pulse measurements across commercial cells, found capacity prediction errors of 2–9%, and reported failure of the studied rapid pulses to predict safety targets; their [primary 2025 study](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/addd50) establishes serious pulse incumbents, though its materials and target differ. Merryweather et al. directly tracked individual LiCoO2 particles during cycling by [optical scattering microscopy](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03584-2); it is a strong spatial comparator on optically suitable specimens but not automatically transferable to opaque buried Fe/Li2O sites. Pollok et al. give scanning/wide-field NV and SQUID/atomic-magnetometer tradeoffs; their current-imaging battery figures include simulations, so one must not count simulated buried-current reconstructions as the proposed experiment.

Pulse-order diagnostics and nonlinear electrochemical spectroscopy have prior art. The exact `K_r` combination with embedded local NVs was not found in this limited search, but **novelty is UNKNOWN**; an exhaustive pulse-sequence, magnetic spectroscopy and patent search has not been done. No patentability or unique quantum advantage is claimed.

## Decisive test and go/no-go result

Use at least two independently fabricated batches of formed Fe3O4/Li microcells. Freeze the four-arm randomization, SOC window, `r`, `U`, `tau`, zero-current rest, optical settings and analysis before collecting target data. First establish that both amplitude levels stay in a repeatable electrochemical window, and that identical-arm replicate differences quantify specimen variance. Measure `K_r` per registered NV site, its confidence interval, amplitude/`tau` scaling, and sham-electrode distribution. In parallel, run the same pulses without NV inclusions to measure sensor-induced kinetic changes. On matched sacrificial sites, use chemical/structural analysis (for example Fe oxidation state and local Fe/Li2O morphology) to distinguish altered conversion or surface storage from pure magnetic reorientation or heating. Do not infer microscopic chemistry from a fitted local field alone.

Then make the scientific decision consequential. Randomly assign subsequent charge schedules chosen with or without the pre-acquired `K_r` map, while giving existing operando-NV, electrical-pulse, optical/X-ray and bulk-magnetic protocols the same physical specimens, attainable controls and full resource budget. Freeze a predicted location-level heterogeneity or local fade outcome and a cell-level capacity/energy-efficiency outcome before long cycling. A positive *operational* result requires: (1) `K_r` above full calibration/specimen-error floor and absent in linear/sham controls; (2) a reproducible chemical or charge-partition correlate at the predicted sites; (3) a blinded held-out decision improvement over the best practical incumbents after all fabrication, imaging, energy and damage costs. The identical-data NV policy remains an exact upper comparator and cannot be outperformed by `K_r` alone. If `K_r` is zero, explained by sensor/thermal nonlinearity, or fails the decision comparison, the operation does not establish a new capability. No part of that decisive test was performed here.
