# Conditional exchange: a matching extension and a stronger acquisition obstruction

**Status:** internally derived conditional results and exact counterexamples;
synthetic finite checks passed. No new physical implementation, independent
validation, historical novelty, cost advantage, or civilization-scale
capability is established.

The most useful positive result removes a genuine record-matching cost:
matching need only preserve the environment's response to the selected future
tasks. If a certified coarse record Z captures that response, quantum states
can be exchanged within Z while their full fine records C and original
references remain attached to the jobs. The finite unmatched mass then
depends on Z, not on the potentially enormous alphabet of C.

The strongest negative result concerns the more important physical gate.
A hidden exchange of the two baths, accompanying the desired data SWAP,
passes accessible gate tests, double-SWAP tests, complete bath-marginal
tomography, and every reset-input continuation-channel test. It nevertheless
undoes the intended decorrelation. A task-specific audit of bath marginals
or independent probe responses is therefore insufficient to certify the
router's action on native correlations.

## Exact input/output/cost contract

Input is a specified cq preparation \(\rho_{CRSE}\), the actually observed
record C, a predeclared menu of bounded scored continuations, independent
preparations or a weaker selection-stable cross-independence certificate,
and a physical routing/instrument implementation. No unknown conditional
state, bath model, reset independence, decoder, or environmental action
certificate is supplied by notation alone.

Output is a tagged job/reference score comparison under an actual finite
batch operation, or a bounded inference of the ideal conditional product
functional. Tagged reference reassignment is part of the estimand. Its
effect need not imply changed resident processor dynamics.

Preparation histories, environment reset/mixing, condition acquisition,
reference storage/readout, matching, physical routing, local measurement
and reset, calibration trials, latency, within-batch dependence, loss,
switching work and readout cost are charged. Replicas are preparations, not
copies cloned from an unknown quantum input. All error bounds refer to
predeclared tasks and the stated condition/support interface.

## Retained theorem: certified coarse matching

Let \(Z=f(C)\), \(q_z=\Pr(Z=z)\), and
\(\bar\tau_z=\mathbb E[\rho_E^C|Z=z]\). Within coarse classes cycle the S
registers, keep singletons native, and follow each donor's full C and R.
For N iid preparations the exact tagged output is

\[
 \rho_N^Z=\sum_c p_c|c\rangle\langle c|\otimes
 \left[(1-q_{f(c)})^{N-1}\rho_{RSE}^c+
 \bigl(1-(1-q_{f(c)})^{N-1}\bigr)
 \rho_{RS}^c\otimes\bar\tau_{f(c)}\right].
\]

If the selected continuation channels induced by \(\rho_E^c\) and
\(\bar\tau_{f(c)}\) agree, every reference-inclusive scored task agrees with
ideal fine-C decorrelation up to coarse singleton mass
\(s_N(Z)=\sum_zq_z(1-q_z)^{N-1}\). Approximate channel differences add their
probability-weighted half-diamond distances; two-policy contrasts add the
two per-policy bounds. If the environment states themselves agree, the
full fine CE marginal also stays fixed. Task equivalence alone does not
preserve that physical marginal.

The exact cost removal is large in a supplied sufficient-record family.
For C=(Z,U), fair Z and an independent 128-bit nonce U, with bath marginal
depending only on Z, N=32 fine matching leaves at least
\(1-31\,2^{-129}\) of jobs untouched. Coarse matching leaves only
\(2^{-31}=4.657\times10^{-10}\) unmatched. Fine U may change the donor RS
state through genuinely local controls. The operation preserves that state
by tracking its donor, rather than demanding identical RS states within Z.

This is a conditional mechanism, not a universal discovery algorithm for Z.
Equality of selected bath-induced channels can be calibrated using an
adequate system-only causal-break probe frame when those primitives and
conditional preparations are available. For unique informative records,
neither learning Z nor uniform/average channel equivalence is free.

A stronger conditional corollary handles continuously varying informative
labels with no exact repeats. Suppose an observed scalar t(c) lies in a
known interval of width R, with a paid uniform response bound
\(\frac12\|\Lambda_a^{\tau_c}-\Lambda_a^{\tau_{c'}}\|_\diamond
\le L|t(c)-t(c')|\) for every scored donor-specific policy. Sort an even
batch by t and swap adjacent pairs. The expected tagged batch score differs
from the sample ideal fine-C graft mean by at most \(2LR/N\), since the
paired gaps are disjoint subsets of the interval. This gives O(1/N) task
bias even when exact fine-label collisions have probability zero. For odd
N, retaining one singleton adds at most 1/N. Conditional joint-source
independence given the full fine record list, the response certificate and
the router operation certificate remain necessary. Fine CE preservation is
not claimed. The Lipschitz bound must be acquired; ordinary smooth record
values do not supply it.

## Partial independence and what it buys

The exact tagged state after an ideal permutation is the original cross
marginal \(\Omega_{R_jS_jE_i|T}\), conditional on the full selection
transcript T. Full N-replica iid can therefore be weakened to factorization
of that cross marginal, with the correct intended one-body marginals.
Global high-order correlations can remain. Three fair hidden phase bits
conditioned on even parity give an explicit non-iid success example: every
routed pair is independent, so a fixed 3-cycle has the desired mean.

This weakens the mathematical premise but does not acquire it from local
statistics, supply the iid singleton formula for arbitrary labels, or make
endpoints independent. Confidence statements still use independent batches
or a separately justified dependence model. Adaptive selection must retain
the condition after its full transcript is revealed.

## Decisive stronger falsifier

Compare ideal \(SWAP_S\) with \(SWAP_S\otimes SWAP_E\) on two independently
prepared identical native states. The entire bath marginal is
\(\tau_E\otimes\tau_E\), invariant under bath exchange. For every accessible
probe xi initially independent of these baths, both operations produce
the same complete joint output state. No amount of calibration restricted
to this reset-input class can distinguish them.

On native correlated phase states with u=0.2, the ideal tagged echo score
is 0.68; the hidden bath exchange gives 1.00. All stated audits have zero
error. The extra bath exchange carries each state's old environmental
association along with it. Environmental state preservation is strictly
weaker than a correlation-safe operation certificate.

The quantum_information_operation worker invented an additional interpretive
falsifier, relayed by root and independently checked here:
\(\rho_{RSE}=|0\rangle\langle0|_S\otimes\Phi_{RE}\). Exchanging blank
S registers changes no physical state. A subsequent local SWAP_SE gives
Bell score 1 with resident references but 1/4 with donor references. Thus a
tagged contrast of 3/4 can be reference/job reassignment alone. The algebra
is correct; a causal claim about altered physical processor behavior needs
a separately justified workload interpretation.

## A lower-hardware comparator: known wire cutting

An exact signed estimator can replace coherent intermodule exchange by
local donor measurements, classical communication and local recipient
replacement, provided those local instruments have the required bath-action
certificate. For one qubit, randomly measure X,Y or Z. Let D+ prepare the
same outcome eigenstate at the recipient and D- the opposite eigenstate.
Then \(id=2D_+-D_-\), including an arbitrary donor reference. Applying the
future and scoring gives an unbiased ideal tagged product-state score.

The centered estimator has coefficient norm 3 and a factor-9 bounded-score
sampling budget. For an m-qubit collective cut the known optimum is
\((2^{m+1}-1)^2\); local collective-basis synthesis and readout are additional
costs. Uniform per-branch task bias epsilon can amplify to
\((2^{m+1}-1)\epsilon\). This procedure estimates a scalar and never
prepares a reference-preserving entangled routed state. Local reset/bath
collateral action remains a physical premise, so it does not solve the
arbitrary black-box environment problem.

This is established wire-cutting machinery, reconstructed as a compulsory
competitor to the exchange proposal. For a one-qubit assay whose coherent
transport takes more than nine times the complete local protocol cost, the
virtual estimator can win under equal error/bias conditions. That is a
conditional resource threshold, not a measured platform advantage. Bell
fidelity obtained from separate Pauli correlators has raw score range
[-1/2,1], which changes the absolute concentration budget if the coherent
baseline has a binary Bell projection.

## Strongest missing premise and next decisive test

The strongest missing physical premise is an operation-on-correlations
certificate: the router must not transport, recreate or change the relevant
environmental association while passing marginal tests. This is stronger
than unchanged bath density matrices. A universal system-only reset-probe
shortcut is ruled out by the explicit bath-exchange counterexample.

The smallest useful next test is a controlled two-module memory platform
with independently auditable bath registers, kept hidden from the diagnostic
algorithm. Predeclare a fine record C with deliberately redundant components
and a coarse response Z. Include native correlated preparations, independent
product probes, a deliberately inserted hidden bath SWAP, the source's
hidden bath flip, and the blank-S/Bell-RE reference control. A valid router
audit must reject both hidden operations on the native correlation family;
product-probe agreement alone is a known false pass. Then compare physical
coarse exchange with the factor-9 qubit virtual estimator and targeted
response measurements using the same complete histories and task accuracy.

Only after that acquisition succeeds should the assay be evaluated on a
held-out controller/routing decision. Without that evidence, retain coarse
matching as a conditional cost reduction, the virtual method as existing
baseline machinery, and the bath-swap construction as negative research
capital. The broader scientific/technology mission is not achieved.
