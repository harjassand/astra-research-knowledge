## Mathematical transition and unresolved leads

The observed transition is from a cancellation obstruction, to a tensor-level rank-drop encoding mechanism, and then back to the separately identified channel-level lead. The third report's central contract is a trace-preserving LOCC device with the whole protocol and all outcomes retained, a resource-neutral tomographic average input, and an exactly simulable separable initialization. The tensor candidate instead concerns state-specific stochastic local filters. The different contracts explain why N683 does not contradict or validate N680.

Potential next questions, not established results, include: whether a direct-product argument extends the resource bound from causal sequential simulation to unrestricted parallel blocks; what dimension- or energy-dependent return metric could replace exact return without being defeated by the flagged-battery example; which operational resource theories admit the required free tomography; and whether one can compute useful separating tests or cost thresholds. The report itself gives counterexamples to unconditional approximate-return and incomplete-tomography extensions. These leads remain open and are not promoted as theorem claims.
