The strongest operation

The selected scientific task is to certify a local static response of an
interacting quantum system without reconstructing all excitations. For a supplied
exact frustration-free parent Hamiltonian H=sum_j L_j^*L_j and exact MPS ground
state psi, let b=(V-<V>)psi. The target is chi=<b,H^+b>/<psi,psi>, the negative
second-order energy coefficient for a local perturbation lambda V.

The construction searches for local tensor rows w_j with sum_j L_j^*w_j=b exactly.
That identity supplies a rigorous upper sum_j||w_j||^2/<psi,psi>. An arbitrary
primal vector supplies the familiar variational lower; their difference is exactly
sum_j||w_j-L_j x||^2/<psi,psi>. The abstract primal/dual principle is old. The
developed computational operation is exact completion, physical-row metric
optimization and verification through compact MPS transfer contractions.

Frustration-free annihilation makes the completion Gram block banded: sufficiently
distant local defects have exactly zero overlap. It permits an O(N)-dimensional
coefficient problem at fixed local width and MPS bond dimension. Rational
contractions, exact rank tests and norm optimization have polynomial bit cost in
N and encoded input length under those fixed-parameter assumptions. This is a
conditional algorithm with UNKNOWN on failed membership; it is not a proof that
fixed width always suffices or provides useful accuracy. The exact tensors are
essential inputs, generated analytically here rather than measured from matter.

The decisive competitor

An absent supplied gap is not a capability advantage when the same local
projectors let a competitor acquire one cheaply. Exact rational LDL checks on an
open four-spin patch prove B^2 >=(2/5)B. The established Knabe finite-size argument
then proves H^2 >=(1/10)H for these periodic chains N>=6. The same six-step primal
with this acquired gap gives the certified upper1.4537037411509957. Its interval
width0.00336640557764 is smaller than our dual width0.00666432213619.

This sufficient matched competitor defeats the claimed advantage on the tested
instance. It uses the same Hamiltonian, source and primal, and includes actual
gap acquisition instead of assuming an oracle. Stronger known gap methods may
improve it further. The calculation is a comparison of rigorous widths; it is not
a completed MPS wall-time/memory benchmark. The program does not claim to have
implemented every best response solver. Exact finite-sector response is itself an
additional strong comparator at these small sizes.

Failures and corrections that must survive

The right-placement-only ansatz fails exactly at N6 (rank71 vs augmented72), with a
saved integer left-null witness dotting b to-3552. That does not refute the merged
two-placement ansatz. Nearby-bond subsets fail; successful local-width rows still
spread across the chain. An initial root probe incorrectly charged overlapping
placements as separate physical rows and produced a spuriously halved norm; the
probe was corrected before any reported exact certificate. Within-bond cross terms
are now included in the metric. Old audit reports precede the transfer-only
implementation; root/PRINCIPAL_DERIVATION.txt and CLAIM_LEDGER.json record the later
evidence rather than silently editing those source reports.

Centering b against one ground state does not ensure orthogonality to every
ground vector. Exact dual feasibility establishes the necessary range condition;
approximate feasibility does not. A small omitted component at energy epsilon
can cost residual_squared/epsilon. A two-spin open-AKLT kernel counterexample and
a pinned exclusion-chain example preserve those limitations. All-N AKLT
feasibility, robustness to uncertain inputs and a useful source-specific
advantage remain open. The virtual-current commutator and transfer eigenvalue
-1/3 are exact but do not supply the missing parent-row peeling identity.

Priority, reach and investment

The construction does not establish a new abstract duality. Complementary
variational bounds, MPS Hylleraas and analytic/correction-vector response, local
MPS/PEPS patch bounds, and finite-size gap criteria are prior art. Recent locally
gapped response theory also blocks a generic “no global gap” novelty claim. The
focused search did not locate an identical compact local-row upper, but priority
remains UNKNOWN. Source-level comparison details and primary links are retained.

The plausible technological reach is rigorous response computation for selected
quantum materials/models if exact or robustly certified inputs, useful completion
and matched advantage can be acquired. This campaign establishes none of that
application reach, no physical control and no TypeI/II enabling operation.

REDESIGN before major investment. Require a scientifically meaningful,
nonintegrable source/input family where existing coercivity, response and SDP
methods genuinely struggle; prove/implement the low-cost completion; then compare
at matched accuracy, input acquisition, preparation, tensor width, bit cost,
verification and failure rate. An irrelevant decoupled tiny-gap spectator is not
evidence of advantage. An all-N AKLT theorem alone would deepen mathematics but
would not rescue the defeated capability claim. Preserve the finite result and
all failures rather than replacing the objective with an easier exact fixture.
