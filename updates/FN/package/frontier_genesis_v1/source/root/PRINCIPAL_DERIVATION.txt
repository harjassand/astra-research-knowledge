1. The actual scientific task and acquired interface

The target is certified static response to a local perturbation of an interacting
quantum system. For an isolated, nondegenerate zero-energy ground state psi of a
positive Hamiltonian H, perturb H by real lambda times Hermitian V. With Z=<psi,psi>,
b=(V-<psi,V psi>/Z)psi, the second-order energy coefficient is -chi, where

    chi = <b,H^+ b>/Z.

The usual second derivative of energy is -2 chi. This convention matters.
Response, rather than all excitations or a reconstructed microscopic trajectory,
is the scientific output. Reliable response calculations could inform quantum
materials and molecular design. This packet establishes no such acquired material
or industrial outcome. Its tested inputs are an exactly specified AKLT parent
Hamiltonian and an analytic exact MPS, generated from explicit integer tensors.
An approximate fitted ground MPS or a measured material Hamiltonian is not an
equivalent interface. Acquiring and certifying those inputs is an open obligation.

Selection preceded concentration: molecular-response, rare-event, experimental
matter/energy/control, capital interfaces and independent foundational routes were
challenged for novelty, information sufficiency and available validation. The
quantum task survived as a testable computational invention; it was not selected
because an AKLT dataset happened to exist. The exact input allowed us to separate
an actual certificate operation from an unacquired physical input assumption.
The subsequent comparator defeat prevents promotion to a new useful capability.

2. Exact dual completion (known variational principle)

Let H=L^*L=sum_j L_j^*L_j on a finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space.
Assume b is in Ran H. For any x and any row vectors w_j satisfying

    sum_j L_j^* w_j = b,

define

    lower(x) = (2 Re<b,x>-<x,Hx>)/Z,
    upper(w) = sum_j ||w_j||^2/Z.

Then lower <= chi <= upper, and

    upper-lower = sum_j ||w_j-L_j x||^2/Z.                 (1)

Proof: put y=H^+b. The primal error is ||L(x-y)||^2/Z.
For a feasible w, w-Ly is in ker L^*. It is orthogonal to Ly, hence
||w||^2=||Ly||^2+||w-Ly||^2. Expanding ||w-Lx||^2 proves (1).
The infimum of upper is attained by w=Ly; the supremum of lower by x=y.
These are ordinary quadratic/Fenchel/Hylleraas/Thomson consequences, not a new
physical principle or a newly discovered abstract duality.

Exact feasibility also proves b is in Ran H: for z in ker H, Lz=0 and
<z,b>=<Lz,w>=0. Thus the certificate itself handles the full ground-space kernel.
Centering against one selected psi alone does not do so in a degenerate kernel.
If b has a kernel component the primal supremum is infinite, whereas the formal
Moore-Penrose expression discards that component. They must not be equated.

A small dual residual is not a certificate. With H=diag(epsilon,1) and omitted
residual r=delta e_1, the missing response is delta^2/epsilon. No residual-only
finite upper correction is uniform as epsilon tends to zero. Return UNKNOWN
unless the residual is completed exactly or a valid coercivity bound is acquired.

3. The operation developed here: exact compact local-row completion

Input contract: a one-dimensional chain/ring of N sites; fixed local dimension d;
finite-range rational matrices L_j; a nonzero rational MPS psi of bond dimension
D; exact frustration-free constraints L_j psi=0; a fixed-support rational V;
and a finite dictionary O_(j,a) on an interval of width at most R containing the
support of h_j=L_j^*L_j. All matrices/tensors and rational bit lengths are input.
There are at most s dictionary columns per physical row, with s fixed when d,D,R
are fixed. Verify Z>0 and every ||L_j psi||^2=0 by exact contractions; the promise
need not be trusted. Check V=V^* for the energy-curvature interpretation. This
does not verify uniqueness of the ground state, which is
unneeded for the algebraic bound after exact dual feasibility but needed for the
simple physical nondegenerate energy-derivative interpretation above.

Write w_j=sum_a c_(j,a) L_j O_(j,a)psi and a_(j,a)=h_j O_(j,a)psi.
Let A be the column map with these a columns. Compute only

    K=A^*A,  g=A^*b,  h=<b,b>,
    M_(j,a),(k,b)=delta_(j,k)<L_j O_(j,a)psi,L_j O_(j,b)psi>.

Each entry is an exact MPS transfer contraction. Solve Kc0=g, retain a basis T
for ker K, and compute rho=h-g^*c0. Normal equations imply

    rho=||b-Ac0||^2.                                    (2)

If rho is not exactly zero, the specified dictionary returns UNKNOWN.
This is a decision-relevant refusal, not a proof that no other dictionary works.
If rho=0, all feasible coefficient vectors are c=c0+Tz. Minimize c^*Mc by

    (T^*MT)z=-T^*Mc0.                                   (3)

Within-row dependent columns may first be removed using their exact row Gram.
Then M is positive definite and (3) has a unique solution for an independent T.
Alternatively quotient its kernel. The resulting upper is c^*Mc/Z. Equations
(2), (3), the tensors and rational coefficients form an exactly checkable witness.

M is NOT K and is NOT the coefficient identity matrix. It includes all cross
terms within each physical row. In particular two overlapping placements for a
bond are merged before charging that bond's norm. Splitting one physical row into
two independently charged copies changes the factorization and can spuriously
reduce the norm. An initial root numerical probe made this mistake; corrected
probes and every reported exact upper use the physical row metric.

4. Why the Gram is local although the coefficients need not be

If supp h_i is disjoint from supp O_(j,b), the latter support also contains h_j.
Then h_i commutes with both h_j and O_(j,b), and

    h_i h_j O_(j,b)psi=h_j O_(j,b)h_i psi=0.

Consequently K_(i,a),(j,b)=<psi,O_(i,a)^*h_i h_j O_(j,b)psi>=0.
This is an exact frustration-free identity. It is not exponential clustering and
requires neither a spectral gap nor an inverse transfer matrix. Similarly g_(j,a)
vanishes when supp h_j is disjoint from V, because h_j V psi=V h_j psi=0.
M is block diagonal by the direct-sum definition of physical rows. In one
dimension K is block banded, with a fixed cyclic bandwidth on a ring.

The nonzero contractions insert operators on constant-width intervals. Prefix and
suffix transfer products allow their environments to be obtained with O(N)
arithmetic for fixed d,D,R,s (or a polynomial naive contraction routine suffices).
No inverse transfer or spectral conditioning is presumed. There are O(N) nonzero
blocks and O(Ns) coefficient variables. The implementation takes advantage of
AKLT translation invariance, calculates three distinct blocks, and still loops
over N transfer factors to calculate overlaps. Its exact dense coefficient solve
is polynomial but is not claimed linear time. In general a banded singular Gram
can have dense null vectors and a dense reduced system (3). Chain-wide completion
is entirely compatible with fixed local block width.

Bit costs: for rational input tensor/operator entries of at most B bits and fixed
d,D,R,s, multiplying O(N) constant-dimensional transfer matrices produces entries
of polynomial bit length in N and B. Denominators can be cleared by products of
the input denominators; numerator growth is bounded by the logarithm of the
number of fixed-dimensional contraction paths plus their entry sizes. The Gram
dimension is O(N). Rank, nullspace and exact quadratic minimization can use
fraction-free/rational elimination with polynomial bit complexity; determinant
bounds make output coefficient bit lengths polynomial as well. Exact rational
norm checks remain polynomial. This is a conditional algorithmic statement for
fixed width/bond dimension and exact encoded inputs, NOT a uniform feasibility,
useful-accuracy, floating-stability or practical-runtime theorem. Exponential
dependence on increasing R or D, input acquisition and ground-state certification
are all charged rather than hidden.

6. Strong equally informed competitor defeats the current advantage claim

The absence of a supplied global gap is not a fair advantage if a competitor can
cheaply ACQUIRE a valid gap from the same local projectors. The root implementation
uses exact rational sectorwise Schur/LDL arithmetic on the open FOUR-spin patch
B=P12+P23+P34 to prove B^2 >= (2/5)B. All nine magnetization sectors are checked,
with maximum dimension19 and total dimension81. Zero LDL pivots require zero
remaining row, so semidefinite rank defects are handled exactly.

For periodic N>=6 define B_i=h_i+h_(i+1)+h_(i+2). Let Q1=sum_i{h_i,h_(i+1)} and
Q2=sum_i{h_i,h_(i+2)}. Projector and disjoint-bond commutation identities give
sum_i B_i^2=3H+2Q1+Q2. In 2H^2+H the terms are 3H+2Q1+2Q2 plus twice all further
disjoint-pair anticommutators. Those further terms and Q2 are PSD, since the
respective projectors commute. Therefore

    (6/5)H <= sum_i B_i^2 <= 2H^2+H,
    H^2 >= (1/10)H.

Thus gamma=1/10 is an ACQUIRED full-ring gap lower bound. This is an established
Knabe finite-size mechanism, not a new operation (doi:10.1007/BF01019721).
For the SAME six-step primal x, let r=b-Hx. The standard residual certificate is

    chi <= L+||r||^2/(gamma Z).

The exact normalized residual squared is

This sufficient competitor already defeats superiority on the matched finite
