1. SCOPED COMPUTATIONAL RESULT

Input: an explicitly supplied rational, symmetric, entrywise nonnegative 2m-by-2m
Bargmann matrix K and nonnegative rational displacement ell, ket/bra exchange
symmetry, spectral radius rho(XK)<1, one herald count h encoded in binary,
a nonzero herald event, and tolerance epsilon. Physical interpretation
additionally requires a positive density operator; the listed algebraic
promises do not imply it. Arbitrary signed/complex kernels and multiple
herald modes are outside this result.

The theoretical construction samples the remaining physical photon counts within
epsilon total variation in polynomial bit work in complete input length, m,
log(h+1), and log(1/epsilon), using certified polynomial-time elementary functions.
The actual Arb implementation was audited and tested; its uniform worst-case
precision-refinement runtime has not been proved. This is a supplied-model conditional distribution claim. It does
not acquire K, prepare the state, or increase a physical herald's probability.

With zero displacement, elimination leaves S=[[a,b],[b,a]]. The number k of cross pairs has parity h and
weight proportional to
  a^(h-k)b^k / [k! 2^(h-k) (((h-k)/2)!)^2].
Its adjacent ratio is (b/a)^2(h-k)^2/[(k+1)(k+2)], which decreases. An aggregate
integer draw replaces h individual pair choices. Reverse Schur reconstruction
then uses aggregate binomial routing and negative-binomial loop counts. The
marked probability-generating-function identity has been reconstructed, including
fixed boundary-edge multiplicities, nonreversibility, and physical ket/bra count
identification.

The final displaced extension adds singleton weights (c,c). Its cross-count
weights are b^k H_(h-k)(a,c)^2/k!, where H_n=[z^n]exp(a z^2/2+c z).
The full law can be bimodal, but each parity subsequence is log-concave.
Two approximate-anchor rejection envelopes avoid both exact mode comparison
and parity normalizers. Given k, the own-pair laws have decreasing ratios;
their residual singleton counts and a source Poisson count seed reverse Schur.

The coefficient oracle is acquired constructively. Fold the real Gaussian
moment integral onto t>0 before approximation, retaining the positive parity
factor 1+(-1)^n exp(-2dt), d=c/sqrt(a). Normalize at
m=(d+sqrt(d^2+4n))/2. The resulting integral has a uniform positive lower
bound, Gaussian tails, and a bounded complex extension near its retained
interval. Certified quadrature computes log H_n to absolute error 2^-p in
polynomial bit work in input length, log(n+1), and p. A separate rational
interpolation proof closes arithmetic-cost obligations independently of the
implemented Taylor recurrence. Tiny odd displacement is handled by expm1
or factoring its common zero, rather than subtracting saddle contributions.

This is a transferable representation technique, not a new special function:
the coefficients are classical Gaussian moments and parabolic-cylinder values.
The displaced code produced a coupled h=10^20 sample in about 81 seconds.
Repeated coefficient evaluations are a substantial practical cost. The earlier
undisplaced endpoint reached h=10^100 much faster; neither comparison is a
hardware experiment or a proof of superiority over contemporary software.

Transferable mechanism: nonnegative path and loop expansions can permit aggregate
count reconstruction without materializing individual paths or tokens. Rational
minor bounds control pivot bit lengths; explicit tails, finite support, enclosure
width checks, and rejection caps are needed for the stated finite-bit TV contract.
The final endpoint repairs enforce capped tickets and integer inputs. The
software audit identifies the remaining library-complexity and verification limits.

2. EXACT PHYSICAL ACQUISITION SEPARATION

For integer s>=1 and h=4s^2, consider unknown pure two-mode Gaussian state j in
{0,1} with pair matrix B_j=[[1/4,t_j],[t_j,0]], t_0=0, t_1=1/(4s). Both candidates
are known; only j is hidden. The matrices are nonnegative and uniformly below
instability, with bounded unconditioned energy. Access consists of independent
raw copies, allowing arbitrary collective quantum processing. Every invocation
must return an unconditional classical sample of mode B given A=h.

The internally reconstructed lower bound is
  N >= (15h/8)(1/3-2epsilon)^2, epsilon<1/6.
It follows because the conditional laws have TV separation at least 1/3, whereas
N-copy trace distance is at most sqrt(8N/(15h)). Thus even this bounded-energy
family needs Omega(h) copies for fixed accuracy: exponential in the encoding
length of h. Supplied selector, preparation information identifying j, or freely
postselected copies change the interface and evade this statement.

Sharper result, internally derived and independently model-audited: for exact always-output sampling the necessary and sufficient
fixed copy budget is
  N* = ceil(log(Z_h)/(-log(F_h))),
  Z_h = sum_{j=0}^{h/2} prod_{r=0}^{j-1}(1-2r/h)^2 / (2j)!,
  F_h = sqrt(1-(8h-1)/(15h^2)).
Here F_h is squared pure-state fidelity. Fidelity monotonicity gives necessity;
detecting any mode-B photon across copies, followed by probability rebalancing,
gives sufficiency when 1-F_h^N >= 1-1/Z_h. Moreover
N*/h tends to (15/4)log(cosh(1)), approximately 1.6267. The classical positive-count
law has a rational geometric proposal with expected at most 4/3 proposals and
polynomial expected bit cost in log h. This special construction does not require the
generic Gaussian implementation. The supplied-model sampler uses fair bits and rational comparisons. Exact
acquisition rebalancing uses algebraic comparisons reduced to rational arithmetic;
its exact setup has polynomial(h) cost with O(h log h)-bit operands. No hardware
demonstration is claimed.

Transfer: compare conditional-output separation against raw-input fidelity before
proposing tomography-free rare-conditioning capabilities. This is a sharp
information-resource distinction, not a demonstrated new physical capability.
The complete exact/approximate/expected-copy proofs and exact sampler are in
work/calibration_audit/audit.txt and diagnostics.py.

3. ADDITIONAL MECHANISMS WORTH TRANSFERRING

Rare-event simulation. With only membership queries on M unknown atoms, adding
one hidden event atom to a known singleton forces at least (1-2beta)M queries
for uniform relative error epsilon<1/3 and failure beta<1/2. Computation and
learned proposals cannot reveal an unqueried component. Conversely, a SUPPLIED
global downset promise on [0,1]^2 and exact rational membership permit deterministic
relative area brackets using O(epsilon^-1 log(1/(epsilon p))) queries. This
structural escape is useful, but monotone reliability bounds are established
prior art. A hidden disconnected square defeats the promise while preserving
the algorithm's transcript. Known-core subtraction is the required sampling
baseline; bounded relative variance and fixed-probability accuracy differ.
Read work/simulation/REPORT.txt.

Robust importance sampling. Exact all-box second-moment verification remains
coNP-complete even for arbitrarily narrow fixed positive continuation intervals;
the reduction establishes weak hardness, not approximation hardness. A rational
fixed-proposal FPTAS returns witness L and upper U<= (1+epsilon)L. Mittal--Schulz
(Operations Research, 2013, Section 7) already supplies the relevant mechanism.
More decisively, an O(d log d) secant/continuous-knapsack relaxation usually
outperformed the implemented DP and sometimes certified the exact answer. For
positive bounds its factor is max_i (l_i+u_i)^2/(4l_i u_i). None acquires the
continuation intervals. Relative accuracy for second moment R is not relative
accuracy for variance R-1. Read work/variance_algorithm/REPORT.txt,
work/variance_prior_art/report.txt, and work/rare_event_transfer/HANDOFF.txt.

System identification. Supplied order cap d and pole radius rho<1 permit a noisy
finite-window certificate of an infinite scalar LTI impulse-response tail,
without identifying a well-conditioned realization. Decimation trades precision
for observation horizon; all failed acquisition epochs and resets are charged.
Exact resets, unit impulses, and the specified sensor-noise model are premises.
The engine is classical Turan/de Bruijn extrapolation, and rational Bernstein
inequalities give stronger horizon dependence. Unknown order admits invisible
nilpotent delays; unknown stability margin admits weak near-unit modes. Neither
promise follows from finite noisy taps. Read work/learning_acquisition/RESULT.txt.

Inverse science and safe experimentation. Adaptive coordinate auditing has sharp
power bound alpha+(1-alpha)q/n; arbitrary noisy linear sensing instead pays total
energy. Fresh random audit probes are established cross-validation tools and
must be physically available. For all L-Lipschitz safety functions with exact
seed c and adversarial sensor error eta, the favorable constant instance still
has exact robust certified reach min(D,[c+n max(c-2eta,0)]/L). If c<=2eta,
exploration stalls. Analyticity, finite derivative observations, or RKHS
membership do not acquire a missing global structural bound. LoSBO already uses
the optimal cone mechanism. Read work/inverse_science/result.txt and
work/independent_frontier/result.txt.

No exact fixed Gaussian-shift decoder exists for the nondegenerate finite integer
