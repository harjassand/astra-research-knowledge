Independent critique: one-mode large-herald extension
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: internal mathematical audit, not independent expert validation.

VERDICT
The proposed aggregate law is correct under the supplied-rational, entrywise nonnegative, real exchange-symmetric, zero-displacement contract. I found no mathematical obstruction to replacing the herald DP by a sampler polynomial in log(h), input bit length and log(1/epsilon), using the already stated dyadic-envelope lemma and certified scalar evaluations. This is a narrowly useful extension/composition; the univariate law and unknown-normalizer log-concave sampling are established mathematics. No historic-scale claim is justified by this extension alone.

1. COMBINATORICS
Let k be the number of cross pairs between h ket and h bra tokens. Then k=h mod 2, 0<=k<=h, and each side has (h-k)/2 internal pairs. Choose cross endpoints, biject them, and pair each remaining side. The number is
  choose(h,k)^2 k! [(h-k)!/(2^((h-k)/2)((h-k)/2)!)]^2
= h!^2/[k! 2^(h-k) ((h-k)/2)!^2].
Multiplication by a^(h-k)b^k gives the proposed w(k).
For a,b>0,
  w(k+2)/w(k)=(b/a)^2 (h-k)^2/[(k+1)(k+2)].
This decreases strictly over adjacent valid support points, proving unimodality. An independent recursion enumerating labeled matchings for h=0,...,8 exactly reproduced all coefficient multiplicities.

2. MODE AND BOUNDARIES
Use a contiguous index j=0,...,floor(h/2), with k=(h mod 2)+2j. Binary-search the first support point whose next ratio is <=1, with the last point as fallback. Compare rational numerators by integer cross multiplication; do not use floating square roots. A tie gives two adjacent modes and either is admissible.
- h=0: empty matching, k=0, for all a,b>=0.
- a=0<b: k=h deterministically.
- b=0<a: k=0 for even h; odd h has zero herald weight.
- a=b=0 and h>0: zero herald weight.
- For h=1 and b>0, k=1 deterministically.
Negative or complex coefficients are outside the proof.

3. LOG MASS AND AGGREGATION
The common h!^2 factor must cancel before numerical evaluation. If m is a valid support point, a convenient formula is
 log[w(k)/w(m)] = (k-m)log(2b/a)
 + logGamma(m+1)-logGamma(k+1)
 + 2logGamma((h-m)/2+1)-2logGamma((h-k)/2+1).
Set aggregate directed boundary counts C00=C11=(h-k)/2, C01=k, C10=0, and ghost counts zero. The source note's marked boundary functions are symmetric after identifying the physical ket/bra markings, so fixed orientations are legitimate. Every labeled matching with the same k induces the same aggregate marked factor. Reverse elimination subsequently uses only integer multiplicities and matrix-size loops, not individual tokens.

4. HIDDEN LINEAR WORK / SOFTWARE GATES
The current RationalGaussian constructor unconditionally invokes RationalLoopMatching. A new sample method alone is insufficient: the special path must bypass the matching DP at construction. Also avoid generic exact-partition, exact-herald-probability and derivative-DP diagnostics on huge h. Those diagnostics can intentionally remain small-h-only.
Computing mode and O(log h) block endpoints uses O(log h) rational comparisons of polynomial bit length. Log-gamma cancellation requires precision growing with log h, not h. Any theorem should distinguish a mathematically certified scalar oracle from the present experimental mpmath.iv backend. Its fixed precision-refinement cap can raise ArithmeticError; successful runs do not prove the implementation total or establish correct enclosure semantics. A rigorous algorithm may increase precision until the proved error condition holds, with an explicit analytic polynomial bound.
The final output bit length necessarily depends on generated output counts. Under finite rational pivot data and h encoded in binary, the conditional expected occupations grow at most linearly in h times rational resolvent factors, so logarithmic tail bounds can be obtained; state them instead of treating arbitrary-size integers as unit cost.

5. PRIOR ART / STRONG BASELINES
With J=(h-K)/2 the unnormalized weights are proportional to
 (a^2/(4b^2))^j / [(h-2j)!(j!)^2].
The normalizer is h! Pi_h(a^2/4,b), with Pi the classical generalized central trinomial / hybrid polynomial. Equivalently it is
 h! (b^2-a^2)^(h/2) P_h(b/sqrt(b^2-a^2)),
interpreted as a polynomial identity; complex intermediate square roots should not be used numerically for b<a. Blasiak, Dattoli, Horzela, Penson and Zhukovsky, JIS 11 (2008), Eq.19 and Prop.9:
 https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL11/Penson/penson131.pdf
Unknown-normalizer discrete log-concave adaptive rejection is established, e.g. Sheldon (2013):
 https://people.cs.umass.edu/~sheldon/papers/discrete_ars.pdf
Devroye (1987)'s classic constant-expected-proposal construction assumes normalized mode mass p_m, so it must not be credited with a free normalizer in this comparison:
 https://luc.devroye.org/devroye-discretelogconcave-1987.pdf
The most credible contribution is the proved aggregate interface that removes the remaining numerical-h dependence from this specific Gaussian reconstruction method. Historical priority for that composition remains unestablished. A comparison only against the old DP would overstate contemporary algorithmic novelty.

6. CONCRETE PHYSICAL ACQUISITION LIMIT
The physical pure Gaussian input B=[[a,t],[t,0]], with norm<1, gives Schur boundary S=[[a,t^2],[t^2,a]]. Its conditional unobserved photon count equals K exactly. At t=1/2 and a=t^2=1/4 this is an ordinary finite-energy, physically valid state. At a=b=t^2, for h>=2,
 E[K]=h^2/(2h-1),
 Var(K)=2h^2(h-1)^2/[(2h-1)^2(2h-3)] ~ h/4.
These identities were also checked by exact enumeration h=2,...,8. Since theta=log(b/a) is the natural exponential-family parameter, conditional Fisher information in theta is Var(K)=Theta(h). Therefore model perturbations on the scale h^(-1/2) become consequential to conditional statistics. A claimed end-to-end method acquiring unknown physical coefficients must analyze this precision demand. Under a regular bounded-information-per-copy observation model, obtaining that precision costs order h copies by the usual estimation bound; this last statement is conditional on that acquisition model, not a universal theorem about arbitrary experimental access.
Actual preparation of h-photon heralds still expends photon energy and suffers herald probability. The classical conditional sampler does not remove those physical costs. Exact digitally supplied rational inputs provide a legitimate computational interface, but cannot silently stand in for laboratory acquisition.

RECOMMENDED CLAIM
For one zero-displacement observed physical mode in an explicitly supplied nonnegative rational real Gaussian kernel, the conditional binary-count sampler can replace the finite-token matching DP by a unimodal scalar sampler, achieving bit-polynomial dependence on the encoded herald count under the existing scalar-arithmetic and reconstruction assumptions. Provide tests of degenerate cases, exact small-h distributions, and a large-h run; retain the prior-art and physical-input limitations above.
