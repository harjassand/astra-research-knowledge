SINGLE-MODE BINARY-LARGE-HERALD SAMPLING: PRIOR ART AUDIT
Audit date: 2026-10-10, Australia/Brisbane
Scope: supplied rational nonnegative Gaussian Bargmann kernel, zero displacement,
one photon count h encoded in binary; epsilon-TV sampling of remaining count vector.
Status: targeted primary-source search, not exhaustive novelty clearance.

MAIN FINDING

The proposed one-dimensional aggregate law is already a standard-type symmetric
trinomial bridge, and polylogarithmic oracle-query sampling follows from general
discrete log-concave sampling. Its derivation or fast oracle-model sampler alone
is not a defensible fundamental novelty claim. The potentially substantive result
is a certified finite-bit implementation of the entire single-herald Schur
reconstruction, with costs polynomial in the input length and log(h+1), uniformly
in rare event probability. That full interface was not located in this search.

AGGREGATE IDENTIFICATION (DIRECT ALGEBRA, NOT A LITERATURE CLAIM)

For k in {0,...,h} with k=h mod 2, the proposed weight is
 w(k)=a^(h-k)b^k / [k! 2^(h-k) (((h-k)/2)!)^2].
Set j=(h-k)/2 and n=floor(h/2). For a,b>0,
 v(j)=a^(2j)b^(h-2j)/[4^j(h-2j)!(j!)^2], j=0,...,n,
 v(j+1)/v(j)=(a^2/(4b^2)) (h-2j)(h-2j-1)/(j+1)^2.
This ratio decreases, giving log-concavity and a mode accessible by exact rational
comparison and binary search. Multiplication by h! identifies a trinomial
coefficient law: counts (j,j,h-2j) from h categorical trials, conditioned that
the first two counts agree. Category weights are a/2,a/2,b.

The normalizer is also a terminating hypergeometric/Legendre expression:
 sum_j v(j) = b^h/h! * 2F1(-h/2,(1-h)/2;1;(a/b)^2)
            = (b^2-a^2)^(h/2)/h! * P_h(b/sqrt(b^2-a^2)),
where the latter is interpreted by its polynomial expansion if b<=a.
Do not naively evaluate the latter through complex branches or cancellation.
The positive sum and ratio are the useful algorithmic representations.
This is not generally an ordinary integer-parameter Fisher noncentral
hypergeometric distribution; that analogy does not justify calling an urn API.

Boundary cases: a=0 gives j=0; b=0 permits only k=0 and hence even h; a=b=0
permits only h=0. The requested conditional distribution does not exist when
the herald event has zero probability. Handle h=0 and singleton supports exactly.

PRIMARY SOURCES AND MATCHING LIMITS

[1] Chewi, Gerber, Lu, Le Gouic, Rigollet, Rejection sampling from
shape-constrained distributions in sublinear time, arXiv:2105.14166v1 (2021).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14166v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.14166
Theorem 17 and Algorithm 3 treat unnormalized discrete log-concave weight access.
After locating the mode and splitting its two sides, their result supplies a
constant-acceptance envelope with logarithmically few queries. This is the
strongest direct conceptual comparator for the proposed aggregate sampler.
The paper's access model is not itself a finite-bit evaluation theorem.

[2] Quesada and Arrazola, Exact simulation of Gaussian Boson Sampling in
polynomial space and exponential time, PR Research 2, 023005 (2020), v3.
https://arxiv.org/html/1908.08068v3
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.023005
Section II.2 uses conditional photon probabilities; II.4.4 uses randomized
determinants for nonnegative kernels. The abstract's polynomial approximation
wording must not be promoted to a uniform epsilon-TV FPRAS. The body qualifies
accuracy as subexponential errors and retains explicit photon-count dependence.
Also, its covariance-to-nonnegative-kernel argument is not a substitute for the
candidate's explicitly supplied nonnegative-kernel premise.

[3] Uvarov and Vinichenko, On randomized estimators of the Hafnian of a
nonnegative matrix, arXiv:2312.10143v2 (2024 revision of 2023 preprint).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10143v2
They exhibit simple nonnegative-matrix examples with exponentially large sample
requirements for the Barvinok and Godsil-Gutman determinant estimators. Thus the
2020 approximate method is useful empirically but does not settle the proposed
worst-case bit/TV contract.

[4] Bulmer et al., The boundary for quantum advantage in Gaussian boson
sampling, Science Advances 8, eabl9236 (2022).
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8791606/
https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abl9236
Collision-aware repeated-pair loop-hafnian formulas reduce inclusion/exclusion
terms to a product over repeated pair multiplicities. This is a stronger exact
small/moderate-count comparator than expanding every photon independently.
It still does not establish uniform poly(log h) for the present conditional task.
Report actual implemented cost, including precision, rather than implying a
generic 2^h lower bound for every existing classical method.

[5] Hu and Zhang, New approach for normalization and photon-number
distributions of photon-added (-subtracted) squeezed thermal states,
Chinese Optics Letters 10, 082701 (2012), arXiv:1112.0854.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.0854
https://doi.org/10.3788/COL201210.082701
Photon-added/subtracted squeezed-thermal normalizations and count probabilities
were already written using Legendre polynomials. A special-function closed form
for a single-mode herald normalizer is consequently weak novelty evidence.

[6] Budinger, Andersen, van Loock, General Photon Subtraction from a Gaussian
Perspective, arXiv:2609.34803v1 (2026-09-28).
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34803v1
Their state representation applies Gaussian convolution, coordinate change and
filtering to measured Fock Wigner functions. For one PNR detector they analyze
a two-parameter state space; numerical FFT evaluation includes photon numbers
above 150. This is a strong contemporary resource-state analysis comparator,
not a claimed poly(log h) full photon-output TV sampler. Count sampling does not
by itself provide Wigner negativity, state preparation, or loss tolerance.

[7] Efficient classical sampling from Gaussian boson sampling distributions on
unweighted graphs, Nature Communications (2025).
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64442-7
The double-loop Glauber construction has proved polynomial mixing for specified
dense unweighted graph families. The article explicitly leaves mixing analysis
for general nonnegative weighted graphs open. Its collision-free graph-output
contract must not be conflated with binary-large-h conditional number sampling.

[8] Fitzsimmons and Rosen, Markovian loop soups: permanental processes and
isomorphism theorems, Electronic Journal of Probability 19(60), 2014.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5163
https://doi.org/10.1214/EJP.v19-3255
This supplies a broad existing loop-soup/permanental representation connection.
Before claiming a new positive determinant/Schur sampling principle, compare
ordered-loop and excursion constructions against the exact paired Bargmann
interface. The full reduction and compressed conditional runtime were not
established by this prior-art audit.

MATCHED BASELINE AND EXPERIMENT DESIGN

1. Aggregate law: mode/ratio plus generic log-concave rejection, charged with
   exactly the same high-precision weight oracle as the candidate. Compare with
   stable positive-sum enumeration for moderate h to verify distributional error.
   An O(h) CDF routine is a validation oracle, not the strongest algorithmic rival.
2. Full conditional law: use direct conditional probabilities and repeated-row/
   repeated-pair hafnians on small/moderate h; include exact analytic easy cases
   such as split thermal light, and symmetry/independence cases.
3. Use the inherited unconditioned Schur sampler plus herald rejection only as
   a transparent additional comparator. Its cost proportional to 1/P(H=h) does
   not prove every classical conditional sampler has that cost.
4. If benchmarking optical protocol usefulness, select an actual constrained
   photon-subtraction family covered by the nonnegative input premise. Supply
   the covariance-to-kernel acquisition cost and show the premise holds.

UNRESOLVED LOAD-BEARING OBLIGATIONS

A. Exact unnormalized weights can have Theta(h log h) numerator/denominator
   bits. Quoting an exact-real or exact-weight oracle hides the original cost.
   Implement log ratios/weights with rigorous error enclosures, manage
   cancellation, and charge O(log h) scale-dependent guard precision explicitly.
B. Prove stable envelope construction with approximate comparisons near its
   thresholds. Tiny probability errors must not be amplified by uncharged rare
   event normalization or rejection probabilities.
C. Analyze every subsequent Schur reverse step, not just the one-dimensional
   law: number of draws, large-parameter negative-binomial/multinomial sampling,
   finite-bit probabilities, event feasibility, and accumulation of TV error.
D. State whether runtime is expected, high-probability, or worst-case. Bound
   binary output length and photon-count tails. A compressed count vector is
   a different output contract from enumerating h individual photons.
E. Exclude arbitrary signed/complex kernels, arbitrary multiple heralds and
   adaptive non-Gaussian measurements unless separately proved. Classical
   simulation of a supplied rare conditional distribution is not the physical
   ability to prepare that state cheaply.

CONCLUSION

The strongest credible claim surviving this audit is a possible finite-bit,
rare-single-herald extension of an existing positive-kernel Schur sampler.
The one-dimensional mechanism is well aligned with established probability
theory, and the aggregate fast sampler has direct generic prior art. Full
algorithmic correctness and novelty remain separate unresolved questions.

Independent distribution-specific investigation is preserved in
work/gaussian_transfer/distribution_prior_art.txt. It includes primary references
for Devroye, Hormann, adaptive rejection, Bessel simulation, Good's 1958
trinomial-walk paper, and certified gamma evaluation, plus the exact
input-bit/oracle distinction. The precision specialization is an internal
derivation requiring implementation review, not an externally checked theorem.
