DECISION
Reject generic learned/defensive importance sampling as an invention candidate.
Its hidden barrier is finding or excluding missed failure components. Preserve
the exact acquisition lower bound below. A supplied global monotonicity promise
does remove the inverse-rare-probability query cost in two dimensions; an exact
rational implementation and proof make this a useful comparator/transfer tool.
The general monotone-reliability mechanism is established prior art. The precise
geometric-grid complexity was not located in this selective search, which is not
evidence of novelty. Do not market the toy Monte Carlo ratios as an advance over
the strongest methods.

1. THE REAL BLACK-BOX BARRIER

Access model: uniform probability on M+1 named atoms {0,...,M}; event membership
queries can be chosen adaptively, with no geometry or extra observations.
The known event core is {0}. Unknown instance is either A0={0} or AJ={0,J}
for some J in {1,...,M}. Probabilities are p0=1/(M+1) and pJ=2/(M+1).

Theorem. If epsilon<1/3, beta<1/2, and a randomized adaptive algorithm makes at
most n membership queries and achieves relative error <=epsilon with probability
at least 1-beta on EVERY instance, then n >= (1-2 beta) M.

Proof. Couple the algorithm's random seed across the null and alternative worlds;
choose J uniformly. Its path on the null world queries at most n distinct unknown
atoms. Until it queries J, both transcripts and answers are identical. Thus the
probability of any transcript discrepancy is at most n/M. The acceptable output
intervals [(1-eps)p0,(1+eps)p0] and [(1-eps)pJ,(1+eps)pJ] are disjoint when
eps<1/3. Under the coupling, on identical transcripts at least one world fails.
The summed failure probabilities are at least 1-n/M; by the assumptions they are
at most 2 beta. Rearrangement proves the result. This permits active queries,
proposal training, and arbitrary computation; charging only simulation queries
already proves the obstruction. It does not cover stronger geometry/value oracles.
This elementary lower bound is reconstructed here, not claimed historically new.

2. STRONGEST SIMPLE COMPARATOR INVALIDATES THE FIRST CANDIDATE

Let B be a known event subset of exact probability b, and D=A\B have unknown
probability delta; c=1-b and p=b+delta. Assume exact sampling conditional on B and
B-complement is available and charged. A random mixture putting probability t on
B gives ordinary importance-sampling second moment
  b^2/t + c*delta/(1-t).
Its minimum is (b+sqrt(c*delta))^2, at t=b/(b+sqrt(c*delta)).

But the competent comparator knows b. It should spend zero simulations on B:
  p_hat = b + c/n sum_{i=1}^n 1_D(X_i), X_i iid ~ P(.|B-complement).
This has n Var(p_hat) = c*delta-delta^2. It improves the optimized random-mixture
variance by exactly 2b(sqrt(c*delta)-delta) >= 0. This is a standard zero-variance
stratum / known control-variate calculation, not an invention.

If p tends to zero and b asymptotic to p, bounded per-sample relative variance
requires and suffices for delta=O(p^2). To certify delta<=d by observing no
residual failures, an exact one-sided (1-beta) binomial upper bound is
  delta_upper = c*(1-beta^(1/n));
getting this below d takes n >= log(beta)/log(1-d/c), asymptotically
c*log(1/beta)/d. At d proportional to p^2 this is inverse-p-squared cost.

SCOPE: bounded relative variance is stronger than fixed-probability accuracy.
If delta/p<epsilon, reporting exact b alone already satisfies relative error
epsilon. It is wrong to infer universal p^-2 sample complexity for rare-event
estimation. Section 1 deliberately uses a missed component comprising half the
true event probability, so its inverse-p lower bound avoids this loophole.

Contemporary finite-sample defensive IS likewise preserves a range term that
costs inverse p for relative certification when its learned component is useless.
Full support restores unbiasedness; it does not by itself locate important modes.

3. CONSTRUCTIVE STRUCTURAL ESCAPE: 2D MONOTONE PROBABILITY BRACKETS

Supplied assumptions, not acquired from data:
  * Input is uniform Lebesgue measure on [0,1]^2.
  * A is a measurable coordinate downset: z in A and 0<=w<=z imply w in A.
  * An exact event-membership oracle accepts rational coordinates.
  * p=area(A)>0, epsilon in (0,1].
For other independent continuous distributions, a supplied exact monotone
coordinatewise quantile transform could transfer this result. Its numerical
precision, tails, and oracle cost must then be charged separately. Arbitrary
dependence does not satisfy the present theorem.

Theorem. A deterministic algorithm returns rational L,U with
  L <= p <= U <= (1+epsilon)L
using O(epsilon^-1 log(1/(epsilon p))) membership queries, the same order of
rational arithmetic operations, and coordinate bit length
O(log(1/(epsilon p))+log(1/epsilon)). No initial lower bound on p is needed.
No uniform positive-probability termination bound is claimed when p=0.

Construction. Set m=2 ceil(4/epsilon). For J=1,2,4,8,..., use zero plus the
positive grid coordinates
  2^-j (1-i/m), j=0,...,J-1, i=0,...,m/2-1,
and tau=2^-J, sorted increasingly. The adjacent positive-coordinate ratio is
at most r=1+2/m. There are N=Jm/2+1 intervals on each axis.

A staircase walk of the monotone (N+1)-square Boolean node matrix uses at most
2N+1 membership queries. For each cell, its upper-right corner being in A proves
the whole cell is in A; its lower-left corner being outside A proves the whole
cell is out. Let L be the total certainly-in area and U the area not certainly
out. Accumulate these from the last-true row heights in O(N), without enumerating
N^2 cells. Stop when U<=(1+epsilon)L. Reuse cached queries between stages.

Proof of bracket size. Let script-L and script-U denote those cell unions.
For z in script-U outside the two width-tau axis strips, its positive grid
lower corner a is in A and z/r<=a. Since [0,a] is contained in script-L,
  script-U outside strips is contained in r*script-L.
Therefore U <= r^2 L + 2 tau. Also
  r^2-1 <= epsilon/2+epsilon^2/16 <= 3 epsilon/4.
If tau<=epsilon p/16, then
  L >= (1-epsilon/8)p/r^2 >= p/(1+epsilon) >= p/2.
The middle inequality follows by expanding
  (1-epsilon/8)(1+epsilon)-(1+epsilon/2+epsilon^2/16)
  =3 epsilon(2-epsilon)/16 >= 0.
Hence U-L <= (3 epsilon/4)L+epsilon p/8 <= epsilon L.
Termination occurs by J>=ceil(log2(16/(epsilon p))). Doubling J increases this
by a factor below two; the sum of stage queries is geometric. All grid-coordinate
denominators divide m 2^J, and cell-area denominators divide m^2 2^(2J), so exact
arithmetic has the stated bit lengths. With M(B)-cost B-bit multiplication,
standard rational operations add the usual multiplication/gcd factors; oracle
runtime is an additional sum over the actual queried inputs.

Checks passed:

  * All enclosures contain exact p and have U/L<=1.1 in the 25 main examples.

Preserved broken-assumption test:
  Start with [0,2^-12]^2. Add a hidden disconnected failure square
  [3/4,7/8]^2, violating downset structure. Both cases generate exactly the same
  queried transcript (809 calls). The algorithm still emits its conditional
  bracket [1/16777216,1681/26843545600], whereas the changed true probability is
  262145/16777216, far outside it. Internal bracket checks do NOT certify
  monotonicity. This is a deliberate negative, not a failing theorem instance.

Comparator scope:

6. COMPACT CROSS-BRANCH HANDOFF

Useful principle: a globally certified order relation can substitute logarithmic
coordinate precision and deterministic queries for inverse-event-frequency
sampling. The 2D implementation provides an honest acquisition baseline when
that relation is supplied. Core subtraction is mandatory before claiming an
importance-sampling improvement. Probabilistic and variance efficiency are
different targets.

Remaining decisive tasks for a real new capability:
  (i) obtain/prove global order structure from a consequential physical simulator,
      without granting its answer in the oracle;
  (ii) handle numerical membership ambiguity and distribution-transform errors
       with explicit rational intervals;
  (iii) compare full end-to-end costs to MRM/Deep-PrAE and modern bifidelity methods;
  (iv) show useful higher-dimensional structural compression and charge acquiring it;
  (v) establish novelty and obtain expert or formal verification.
