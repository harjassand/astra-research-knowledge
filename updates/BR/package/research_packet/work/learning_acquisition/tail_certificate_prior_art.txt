RECURRENCE / FINITE-PREFIX TAIL CERTIFICATE: NOVELTY AND COMPARATOR AUDIT
2026-10-10. Distinguish direct source statements from algebraic corollaries below.

A. Exact classical source for the extrapolation engine.
N.G. de Bruijn, On Turan's first main theorem, Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 11 (1960), 213--216, DOI 10.1007/BF02020939.
Original scan, visually read pp213--216:
https://pure.tue.nl/ws/files/2449781/597522.pdf
Section 3 attributes to Makai the positivity of the relevant symmetric interpolation-coefficient polynomials. Section 5 equation (5.2) gives the optimal constant
 N_(m,d) = sum_(j=0)^(d-1) 2^j binom(m+j,j).
With m=k-d this equals sum_(j=0)^(d-1) binom(k,j)binom(k-j-1,d-j-1), the uniform-initial-data sharp recurrence extrapolation constant at radius one. Positivity also explains coefficientwise domination by coalescing roots. Original references: Makai 1959, Makai 1958, Dancs 1958. The broad sharp-extrapolation mechanism is prior art.

Batenkov--Yomdin, Taylor Domination, Turan lemma, and Poincare-Perron Sequences, arXiv:1301.6033, theorem 3.1 and discussion p6.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.6033
The source explicitly applies Turan to coefficient-independent extrapolation of rational Taylor series from order and root radius, and cites the exact de Bruijn constant.
Hou--Mu, Recurrent sequences and Schur functions, Adv. Appl. Math. 31 (2003), 150--162, p13.
https://cfc.nankai.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/98/dc/494307c44cd49cd148906a0b21e2/5a941775-e280-4453-b972-13527637cb6d.pdf
Companion-matrix powers are Schur-hook entries; cites Chen--Louck 1996 and Lascoux 1986. An anisotropic or summed-tail reformulation needs novelty qualification as a corollary of this machinery.

C. Stronger polynomial-prefix comparator from rational Bernstein inequalities.
R. Zarouf, Asymptotic sharpness of a Bernstein-type inequality for rational functions in H2, arXiv:1003.0297; St. Petersburg Math. J. 23 (2012), 309--319.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.0297
For the degree-d model space with poles corresponding to |lambda|<=rho, the n>=2 theorem bounds derivative H2 norm by (1+rho+1/sqrt(d))*d/(1-rho) times function H2 norm. We use the coarser K=3d/(1-rho), and verify d=1 directly. IMPORTANT SOURCE-AUDIT NOTE: the arXiv scan's exact displayed n=1 norm appears wrong; direct calculation for (1-lambda*z)^-1 gives |lambda|sqrt(1+|lambda|^2)/(1-|lambda|^2), not its displayed |lambda|/sqrt(1-|lambda|^2). This does not refute the coarse bound, but the upper theorem remains source-used until its proof/published version is independently audited.

R. Zarouf, Application of a Bernstein type inequality to rational interpolation in the Dirichlet space, arXiv:1103.5018, theorem A.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.5018
An analogous Bergman derivative bound gives sum k|g_k|^2 <= K sum|g_k|^2. This supplies another route to a finite window capturing a fixed fraction of H2 mass.

D. Explicit algebraic corollary of the H2 derivative bound (derived here; no novelty claim).
Let g_k obey an order-at-most-d homogeneous recurrence with poles |lambda|<=rho<1. Any shifted tail h_k=g_(m+k) has the same property. Its generating function lies in a degree-d model space; zero roots allow polynomial factors. Put K=3d/(1-rho) and choose integer L>=2K. Parseval gives
 sum_(k>=0) k^2 |h_k|^2 <= K^2 ||h||_2^2.
Writing S^2=sum_(k=0)^(L-1)|h_k|^2,
 sum_(k>=L)|h_k|^2 <= (K/L)^2 ||h||_2^2,
 so ||h||_2 <= 2 S/sqrt(3).
Cauchy-Schwarz and sum_(k>=L) k^-2 <= 1/(L-1) then give
 sum_(k>=L)|h_k| <= [2K/sqrt(3(L-1))] S.
Thus a window length O(d/(1-rho)) controls the entire following l1 tail with O(sqrt(d/(1-rho))) times the window l2 norm, or O(d/(1-rho)) times its linfinity norm. This is stronger asymptotically than the elementary decimation length O(d log(d)/(1-rho)). The comparison uses identical structural promises and does not use a supplied envelope constant, known poles, Hankel conditioning, or model fitting.

Finite-noise acquisition consequence. With N=m+L impulse coefficients observed with simultaneous coordinatewise errors <=eta, the FIR consisting of their estimates obeys
 ||g-g_FIR_est||_1 <= N*eta + [2K/sqrt(3(L-1))]*(||estimated_recent_L||_2 + sqrt(L)*eta).
For R independent zero-state impulse experiments and sigma-subgaussian observation noise, fixed N and
 R >= 2 sigma^2 eta^-2 log(2N/delta)
suffice for the coordinatewise event (unit impulse). Costs: R resets, R unit-energy inputs, RN observations/system time steps, O(N) storage with streaming sample means; FIR evaluation O(N) operations per output. Process noise/reset error requires a separate known output-noise bound and bias accounting. Adaptive choice of N or eta requires a confidence schedule or fixed prespecified maximum; repeatedly using a fixed delta is invalid. Unknown amplitude may delay stopping, and d,rho remain supplied structural promises. If applying this to the difference of two d-order models, use order bound 2d.

E. Wiener norm prior art independently confirms the polynomial scale.
Baranov--Zarouf, H-infinity interpolation constrained by Beurling-Sobolev norms, MJPAA 9(2) (2023), 157--167, theorem 2.
https://doi.org/10.2478/mjpaa-2023-0012
Auxemery--Borichev--Zarouf, arXiv:2603.03908v1 (2026), theorem 1 restates the upper bound and theorem 2 gives asymptotic sharpness:
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03908v1
For rational f of bidegree at most (d,d), poles outside (1/rho)D, ||f||_W <= C sqrt(d/(1-rho))||f||_H2. The 2026 sharpness statement is as d tends to infinity for fixed rho; it does not by itself prove sharpness of a finite-prefix acquisition procedure.

Status: Exact Turan mechanism is classical. Polynomial-window acquisition certificate is a derived application of strong existing rational inequalities; historical novelty NOT ESTABLISHED. Self-contained decimation proof can be retained as a transparent fallback, with the stronger Bernstein comparator disclosed. No claim of new observability, stability-margin acquisition, or breakthrough.
