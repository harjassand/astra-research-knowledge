RESULT: A REPRODUCIBLE FIXED-PROPOSAL FPTAS COROLLARY, WITH A DECISIVE NEGATIVE BASELINE
Research date: 2026-10-10, Australia/Brisbane.
Historic objective: NOT ACHIEVED. Novel mathematical mechanism: NOT CLAIMED.

The exact-rational implementation certifies the supplied fixed-q objective
  R*=max_{0<=l_i<=z_i<=u_i<=1, sum P_i z_i>0}
       [sum B_i(P_i z_i)^2/q_i]/[sum P_i z_i]^2
by returning a corner witness L and upper U with
  L <= R* <= U <= (1+epsilon)L.
The proof, zero-denominator handling, witness reconstruction and polynomial
bit-cost accounting are in proof.txt. An independent internal agent audited
the trimming invariant in independent_critic.txt. This is not formal or external
verification. The local coefficients and uncertainty intervals are supplied;
the algorithm neither learns them nor constructs/optimizes the proposal q.
In a valid importance-sampling interpretation, U bounds relative second moment
and U-1 bounds single-sample relative variance. A relative approximation for
R is NOT a relative approximation for R-1 near zero.

PRIOR ART
The separate source audit in ../variance_prior_art/report.txt finds that an
FPTAS follows from Section 7 of Mittal and Schulz, Operations Research 61(2),
386--397 (2013), DOI 10.1287/opre.1120.1093. Their framework directly covers
products/ratios of positive affine objectives on binary feasible sets. The
present simple one-dimensional trimming specialization is a reconstruction
and implementation of a conventional mechanism, not a breakthrough.
Author-hosted primary source: https://web.mit.edu/schulz/www/epapers/ms-or-2013.pdf

THE STRONGER COMPARATOR CHANGES THE CONCLUSION
The exact O(d log d) secant/continuous-knapsack relaxation supplies its own
feasible corner lower witness and upper bound. It beat the raw DP upper in
25/26 cases. It beat the DP upper after elementary/Kantorovich clipping in
20/26 cases, tied in five, and lost in one. On the six larger cases it was
approximately 10--267 times faster than the DP in single-run timings.
Its lower and upper bounds matched EXACTLY as rational numbers in 13/26 cases,
including the dimension-64 narrow case and dimension-128 heterogeneous case.
Thus those cases are certified exactly without exhaustive enumeration.
Its witness was at least as good as the DP witness in 20/26 cases.

The exact input is example.json. This is an illustration of a remaining
worst-case guarantee advantage, not an empirical breakthrough.

The ordinary interval upper bound was often much looser. Reporting only that
comparison would have overstated the contribution. The DP remains useful as
a generic configurable worst-case fallback when the secant gap exceeds the
requested tolerance. No end-to-end advantage over strongest contemporary
importance-sampling procedures has been established.

TRANSFER AND REMAINING OBLIGATIONS
1. Mathematical branch: a compact standard FPTAS specialization; retain the
   bit-cost and punctured-zero-box proof rather than invoke an unqualified
   approximate oracle. Exact hardness does not imply fixed-accuracy hardness.
2. Applied/scientific branch: always price acquisition of P,l,u,B and compare
   to the cheap secant relaxation before paying for a Pareto DP. A sharp
   relative-second-moment certificate is only useful if local assumptions
   can be justified independently of already solving the rare-event problem.
3. Any capability claim still needs a realistic acquisition interface,
   representative event-generation problem, matched strong IS baselines,
   complete sampling/verification costs, and independent expert or formal
   verification. None was delivered by this supplied-input benchmark.
