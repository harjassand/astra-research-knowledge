INVERSE SCIENCE: ROBUST ADAPTIVE SENSING — REJECT ELEMENTARY CANDIDATE
2026-10-10. Status: internally reconstructed obstruction, no novelty claim.

Barrier: learned-prior adaptive CT/MRI can acquire too little evidence about
anomalies excluded by its prior. More accurate posterior inference cannot
supply absent measurement information.

Candidate: independent random audit measurements of a frozen reconstruction.
Decision: useful but already established by Ward (2009), Compressed Sensing
with Cross Validation, https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1845v2 . No credible historic
advance from this mechanism alone; concentrate deep invention elsewhere.

These sources treat posterior uncertainty, physical operator constraints, or
prior mismatch directly. Their empirical claims were not reproduced here.

Decisive attack and verified-internally result:
1. Exact diagonal-Gaussian-prior counterexample: 15 noiseless queries in R^16
   yield posterior trace 0 and data residual 0, but squared actual error 1.
2. Sharp bound: any randomized adaptive noiseless coordinate tester using <=q
   queries, FPR<=alpha, has average power on {A e_j} at most
   alpha+(1-alpha)q/n. Thus uniform power>=1-beta requires
   q >= n[1-beta/(1-alpha)]. Null-transcript coupling proves it; uniform random
   q-subset auditing attains it. Applies to disjoint Fourier lines with stated
   real/conjugacy and single-coil qualifications, NOT general CT/MRI.
3. Arbitrary adaptive noisy linear sensing, expected null sensing energy B,
   iid N(0,sigma^2) noise: testing 0 against EVERY A e_j with both errors<=delta
   requires B >= (2n sigma^2/A^2) kl(1-delta,delta). Proof: Gaussian adaptive
   likelihood chain rule, sum coordinate energies, binary data processing.
   This is consistent with established adaptive-sensing lower bounds:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.4646 . No novelty asserted.
4. A fresh Gaussian audit has a standard exact chi-square error certificate.
   Reusing an exposed audit after arbitrary reconstruction invalidates it.
   Dense random probes must physically exist; normalizing their energy exposes
   the noisy cost. Computing them after a full scan saves no acquisition.
