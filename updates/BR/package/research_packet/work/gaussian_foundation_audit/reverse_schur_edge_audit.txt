Reverse-Schur directed edge-count audit
Status: no counterexample found; the one-step full marked-PGF identity establishes the stated count reconstruction, provided alpha >= 0 and rho(W_UU) < 1. Alpha=1/2 is within scope.

Let a=W_vv, p=1-a, and attach independent formal markers x_ij to each original directed edge. Conditional on reduced counts C_ij, independent binomial routing and S=C_vv~NB(alpha+K,p) yield exactly

 [p/(1-a*x_vv)]^alpha * product_ij [ (W_ij*x_ij + W_iv*W_vj*x_iv*x_vj/(1-a*x_vv)) / W'_ij ]^C_ij.

Proof: conditional on the routed counts R, the self-loop PGF is [p/(1-a*x_vv)]^(alpha+sum R). Multiply markers for retained and routed edges; sum each R_ij by the binomial theorem. Since theta_ij=W_iv*W_vj/(p W'_ij), the displayed expression follows. It is the complete directed-edge marked Schur substitution, not merely the occupation substitution. Zero W'_ij edges must have C_ij=0 in any supported reduced realization.

This identity can be composed with any polynomial/PGF of reduced edge counts. Correlations between reduced edges, repeated copies, nonreversibility, and arbitrary fixed boundary-edge multiplicities do not invalidate it. The determinant factor uses only the internal block; boundary vertices must not be used as internal vertices. Full walk connectivity is not retained, but its loss does not invalidate the joint edge-count or occupation-count law.

Stress checks:
1. One internal vertex v, m fixed boundary edges s->t. Write W_st=d, W_sv=b, W_vt=c, W_vv=a. The reconstructed occupation PGF is
 [(1-a)/(1-a*z)]^alpha * [(d+b*c*z/(1-a*z))/(d+b*c/(1-a))]^m.
 This agrees exactly with a loop soup plus m independently expanded boundary edges. It includes direct-path mixtures, repeated edges, collisions, and self-loops.
2. Pure directed three-cycle 1->2->3->1, product t<1, no self-loops. Reverse elimination gives equal counts on every edge, all L~NB(alpha,1-t). The determinant law is [(1-t)/(1-t*z1*z2*z3)]^alpha. Thus an irreducible genuinely nonreversible example works without spurious independently sampled cycle edges.
3. Asymmetric two-vertex matrix [[1/10,1/5],[3/10,1/20]], alpha=1/2. Exact rational checks of the square of the reconstructed PGF versus det(I-W)/det(I-D_z W) at z=(1/3,2/5),(4/5,7/8),(1,1),(0,3/4) yielded respectively 2385/2818,1060/1117,1,318/385 on both sides.

No novelty judgment is made. These are internal mathematical checks, not external verification.
