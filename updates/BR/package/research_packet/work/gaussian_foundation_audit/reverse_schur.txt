1. PRECISE TARGET LAW

Let U be a finite internal set and B a finite boundary set. Boundary vertices are endpoints only, never interior vertices. Let W >= 0 entrywise and M=W_UU satisfy rho(M)<1. No reversibility or substochastic row-sum assumption is needed. Set
  S = W_BB + W_BU (I-M)^(-1) W_UB.
Fix nonnegative integer boundary multiplicities c_bc, with c_bc>0 permitted only when S_bc>0.

The target count vector is the sum of:
(a) an alpha loop soup on U, alpha>0;
(b) for each of c_bc labeled boundary edges b->c, one independent b-to-c path whose interior lies in U, chosen with probability (product of its W-edge weights)/S_bc.
A direct boundary edge is a path with no internal vertices. Repeated internal vertices, internal self-loops, and a boundary self-edge b->b are permitted. The boundary path labels only define the product measure and are discarded in the output.

For full directed-edge markers x_ij in [0,1], write A=W elementwise-multiplied by x and
  S(x)=A_BB+A_BU(I-A_UU)^(-1)A_UB.
The target joint edge-count PGF is
  G_c(x)=[det(I-M)/det(I-A_UU)]^alpha
           product_(b,c) [S(x)_bc/S_bc]^(c_bc).                 (J)
A factor with c_bc=0 is interpreted as 1, including S_bc=0.

Loop soup existence follows directly, without a stochastic transition-kernel representation. For every k>=1 and every ordered internal tuple (i_0,...,i_(k-1)), put Poisson intensity (alpha/k) product_j W_(i_j,i_(j+1)), with i_k=i_0, on that cyclic edge multiset. The sum of intensities is alpha sum_k tr(M^k)/k = -alpha log det(I-M), finite. Its edge PGF is the determinant factor in (J). This ordered-tuple construction counts periodic cycles correctly; roots and repeated rotations need not be quotiented by hand.

Thus 'arbitrary boundary path counts' must mean fixed compatible endpoint multiplicities, or a random law over those multiplicities subsequently mixed over. It cannot mean that arbitrary externally prescribed internal path topologies remain unchanged. Section 4 is correct under the former natural meaning, but should state that target measure explicitly.

2. EXACT ONE-VERTEX REVERSE KERNEL

Eliminate v. Write a=W_vv, p=1-a, and
  W'_ij=W_ij+W_iv W_vj/p
for i,j != v. The pivot p is strictly positive. With fixed reduced edge multiplicities C_ij, let independent
  R_ij~Bin(C_ij, (W_iv W_vj/p)/W'_ij), K=sum_ij R_ij.
Draw L~NegBin(alpha+K,p), conditionally independently given the routing draws. The reconstructed counts are
  E_ij=C_ij-R_ij,
  E_iv=sum_j R_ij,
  E_vj=sum_i R_ij,
  E_vv=L.
(Zero-denominator cases have C_ij=0 and are inert.)

The exact conditional all-edge PGF is
  E[product_(i,j) x_ij^(E_ij) | C]
   = [p/(1-a x_vv)]^alpha
      product_(i,j != v)
       [(W_ij x_ij + W_iv W_vj x_iv x_vj/(1-a x_vv))
         /W'_ij]^(C_ij).                                    (K)

Derivation: conditional on all R, the NB PGF contributes
  [p/(1-a x_vv)]^(alpha+sum R_ij).
Multiply by the retained-edge and routed-edge monomials and sum each binomial draw. Each routed term is
  (W_iv W_vj/(p W'_ij)) x_iv x_vj * p/(1-a x_vv),
which cancels the excursion normalization p exactly and yields (K).

Equation (K) resolves both disputed multiplicities:
* The binomial coefficient is required because the C_ij identical reduced-edge tokens independently choose direct versus routed realizations. Aggregation does not remove this coefficient.
* The shape alpha+K is required. K supplies geometric self-loop repetitions attached to K excursions; alpha supplies independent loop immigration at v. Shape K alone omits the determinant factor and already fails on a graph consisting of one internal self-loop.
No factorial correction involving incoming/outgoing route counts is missing: (K) retains the entire original directed-edge joint law, hence also all aggregate occupation correlations.

3. MULTIVERTEX INDUCTION AND DETERMINANT TELESCOPING

Let W^q be the ordinary successive Schur-reduced matrices. Independently define marked matrices A^q from A^0=W elementwise-multiplied by x, using the same elimination order and the identical rational Schur update, now with denominator 1-A^q_vv.

Repeated conditional application of (K) gives the final PGF
  product_eliminated-v [(1-W^q_vv)/(1-A^q_vv)]^alpha
      * product_(b,c) [A^final_bc/W^final_bc]^(c_bc).
This induction is valid even though an effective marked edge represents a path series: at the next stage simply substitute the effective edge marker A^q_ij/W^q_ij in (K).

Block determinant factorization gives
  det(I-M)=product_eliminated-v (1-W^q_vv),
  det(I-A_UU)=product_eliminated-v (1-A^q_vv).
Associativity of Schur complementation gives W^final_BB=S and A^final_BB=S(x). Therefore the returned PGF is exactly (J).

All pivots remain positive: rho(M)<1 makes I-M a nonsingular M-matrix, and principal Schur complements retain that property. An elementary alternative is path convergence: each reduced edge sums nonnegative paths through eliminated vertices; the remaining internal Green function is a principal block of (I-M)^(-1), finite. Thus every eliminated effective self-loop has weight <1. Markers in [0,1] preserve convergence. One can first prove identities with markers strictly positive and extend to zero by continuity.

For occupation markers choose x_ij=z_i for internal source i and x_ij=1 for boundary source i. Internal directed flow is balanced, so incoming and outgoing degrees agree and the occupation of a freshly inserted v is
  N_v=K+L.
Later reversal of an earlier-eliminated vertex subdivides effective edges but preserves every already-inserted internal vertex's incoming/outgoing degree. Hence recorded N_v values are the final occupations. Formula (J) proves their full joint law, not only their marginals.

4. SECTION 5 GAUSSIAN SOURCE AND ORIENTATION

For the displayed W in (5.1), with D assigning the same physical z_i to ket and bra copy,
  W_UU = X_U K_UU,
  W_UH = X_U K_UH, W_Ustar = X_U ell_U,
  W_HU = K_HU, W_starU = ell_U^T.
With internal departure markers, the effective Green bridge is
  (I-D X_U K_UU)^(-1) D X_U
   = D (I-X_U K_UU D)^(-1) X_U = J(z).
Thus the marked reduced boundary block is exactly
  [[S(z), t(z)], [t(z)^T, v(z)]].

Because D commutes with X_U, for strictly positive markings
  J(z)^(-1)=X_U D^(-1)-K_UU,
which is symmetric. This also holds by continuity at zero markings. Therefore S(z) is symmetric and the two orientations of any boundary matched pair have exactly the same full physical occupation PGF. Fixing one orientation introduces no bias in the reported physical counts. With independent ket and bra markings, J need not be symmetric; the construction does not claim an orientation-independent doubled auxiliary law under those unmatched markings.

A weighted observed-token pair/singleton matching drawn at z=1, then independently expanded, contributes
  L_r(S(z),t(z))/L_r(S(1),t(1)).
Pairs of distinct token labels with the same type are included with weight S_aa; they are not singleton terms. No extra half factor accompanies a matched edge.

P~Poisson(v(1)/2) independent star-to-star boundary edges contributes
  exp{(v(1)/2)[v(z)/v(1)-1]}=exp{[v(z)-v(1)]/2}.
When v(1)=0, nonnegativity implies v(z)=0 for all z in [0,1], and P=0 is the correct convention. The source vertex must remain a boundary vertex. There is no additional alpha immigration at star and no alteration of the internal alpha by displacement.

The internal loop soup at alpha=1/2 contributes the determinant square-root factor. Multiplying the three factors gives precisely (3.4). Summing the ket and bra occupations for a physical mode is correct: both marked copies receive the same physical variable z_i, so exponents add.

5. DECISIVE SMALL CHECKS / FAILURE DIAGNOSTICS

(a) One internal vertex, no boundary. Returned N~NB(alpha,1-a), PGF ((1-a)/(1-a z))^alpha. Omitting alpha would return N=0 and is false whenever a>0.

(b) One internal vertex v, m fixed s->t boundary edges, weights d=W_st, b=W_sv, c=W_vt, a=W_vv. Correct PGF:
  ((1-a)/(1-a z))^alpha
   * [(d+bc z/(1-a z))/(d+bc/(1-a))]^m.
The routing draw is Bin(m,bc/[d(1-a)+bc]), followed by NB(alpha+R,1-a); substitution gives exactly that PGF, including self-loop and route multiplicities.

(c) A genuinely nonreversible directed 3-cycle with no other edges. Let product of the three edge weights be t<1. Every vertex occupation is the same L~NB(alpha,1-t); reverse elimination gives exactly that common L. This tests cycle correlation, not just three separate NB marginals.

(d) One physical thermal mode K=[[0,t],[t,0]] gives M=t I_2. The two internal NB(1/2,1-t) counts sum to NB(1,1-t), the geometric thermal photon law.

(e) One physical displaced vacuum mode K=0 and ell=(g,g). Here v=2g^2, alpha loop soup is empty, P~Poisson(g^2), and every source bridge visits exactly one doubled internal vertex. The physical count equals P, with no spurious factor of two.

(f) One physical squeezed vacuum mode B=[b]. M=[[0,b],[b,0]], and loop occupations of the two doubled vertices agree; physical N is twice NB(1/2,1-b^2), giving the expected even-only PGF sqrt((1-b^2)/(1-b^2 z^2)).
