1. EXACT IDENTITY
Let Q(x;z_1,...,z_m) be a deterministic measurable routine for a FIXED oracle,
with scale r>0. Suppose a fixed deterministic vector v in R^m satisfies

 Q(x+Delta; z+v tensor Delta/r)=Q(x;z)

for every x,Delta and seed list, with exactly the same physical oracle queries.
Let D_s>0, ||v||<=D_s, n=r/(2D_s), and

 P=(I-vv^T/(4D_s^2))^(1/2).

For deterministic x and mutually independent standard Gaussian d-vectors
Z,G_1,...,G_m,

 Q(x+nZ; PG) = Q(x; PG-v tensor Z/(2D_s))
              has the same law as Q(x;G).

Proof: apply the deterministic shift identity with displacement nZ and initial
seed PG-v tensor Z/(2D_s). The rewritten seed is jointly centered Gaussian with
covariance (PP^T+vv^T/(4D_s^2)) tensor I_d = I_m tensor I_d. Thus all rewritten
slots are independent standard Gaussians. P has eigenvalues in [sqrt(3)/2,1].
The rewritten seed can remain correlated with Z, but Z is no longer an input of
the rewritten call. This establishes an UNCONDITIONAL marginal law identity.

No extra oracle query is required by this identity. It does require the exact
pathwise symmetry, a known shift vector, controlled independent Gaussian slots,
and implementing P. P is a rank-one update: when v!=0,
 P=I+(sqrt(1-||v||^2/(4D_s^2))-1) vv^T/||v||^2.
Applying it costs O(md) arithmetic and random generation remains charged. The
identity is in exact real arithmetic; it alone supplies no finite-bit error bound.

2. WHAT THE SOURCE DOES WITH ACTUAL ERRORS
The source does NOT identify an inaccurate center with its desired center for
free. After conditioning on parent anchors, its proof couples the computed
center to the IDEAL center x_i+n_i Z. It uses the same independently drawn child
seeds and pays fixed-seed Lipschitz constant times the coupling error. Only at
the ideal noisy center does absorption restore the standalone child law. This
is explicit at implementation.tex lines 639-648 and reiterated at 661-664.
Numerics.tex likewise requires specific ideal independent anchor laws for its
distributional estimates. Deterministic scalar circuit coefficients and fixed
scales are part of that interface, not learned parameters.

3. CONCRETE CALIBRATION COUNTEREXAMPLE
Take one seed, r=D_s=1, v=-1, Q(x;z)=x+z, and let its sole physical query be to
the identity oracle at x+z. Then Q(x+Delta;z-Delta)=Q(x;z), including identical
physical query. The absorbed routine is x+Z/2+sqrt(3)G/2 ~ N(x,1).

If the supplied center contains an unknown deterministic error e, the exact
same operation yields

 Q(x+e+Z/2; sqrt(3)G/2) ~ N(x+e,1),

not N(x,1). At x=0 its probability of being nonnegative is Phi(e), versus 1/2.
The total variation distance is 2 Phi(|e|/2)-1. Thus even the simplest routine
with exact symmetry and query preservation retains the entire offset. Correcting
the offset through a deterministic seed translation would require knowing e.
If instead the identity oracle returns y+e, the shift identity still holds, but
it preserves the erroneous oracle's output law N(x+e,1).

An exactly known, independent, centered Gaussian calibration error is different:
its covariance can in principle be budgeted in P if the remaining covariance is
positive semidefinite. Unknown bias, unknown covariance, dependence on child
seeds, and oracle-parameter perturbations are not covered by this escape.

4. RARE CONDITIONING IS NOT FREE
For the same example x=0, define W=sqrt(3)G/2+Z/2. Although W~N(0,1),

 E[W | Z>t] = phi(t)/(2(1-Phi(t))) != 0.

Q(0;G), using independent G, remains mean zero under Z>t. Thus conditioning on
the injected displacement destroys the marginal transfer unless the joint event
is transformed and preserved as well. Its conditional bias grows like t/2.
This is a counterexample to an unrestricted conditioning transfer, not a claim
that the source uses such a transfer.

5. DIRECT MISMATCH WITH THE CONDITIONAL PHOTON-COUNT SAMPLER
The frozen local candidate takes a rational Bargmann matrix K, exact herald h,
and tolerance as inputs. It uses discrete inverse transforms, log-mass tests and
aggregate binomial/negative-binomial routing. No finite Gaussian seed-slot
representation with the source's fixed affine query-preserving shift symmetry
has been constructed. Converting random bits to Gaussians does not establish
that symmetry. Perturbing K changes the target probability law; it is not a
translation of a query center for one fixed oracle.

There is an exact finite physical example already within the candidate's input
class. Take a two-mode pure Gaussian Bargmann matrix
 B=[[a,1/2],[1/2,0]]. Both a=1/4 and a=1/8 have operator norm below one. Its
eliminated boundary has b=1/4, and at herald h=2 the remaining count K is 0 or 2:

 Pr(K=2)=2b^2/(a^2+2b^2).

The two supplied values of a give 2/3 and 8/9 respectively, a total variation
distance 2/9. An algorithm told one value cannot acquire the other value by
redistributing its private random seeds.

6. SHARPER OBSTRUCTION: SHIFT BUDGET MUST PAY FISHER INFORMATION
This is an elementary necessary condition reconstructed here, independently
checked algebraically by a second model. It is NOT a novelty claim or an audited
theorem of the upstream paper.

Suppose, hypothetically, an EXACT sampler Q(theta;G) for a finite exponential
family p_theta(k)=c_k exp(theta k)/Z(theta), c_k>0 on fixed finite support, had
the source's shift identity locally in scalar theta, with fixed r,v and a common
theta-independent decoder. Then

 Q(theta;G)=Q(0;G-v theta/r).

It is therefore a statistic of a Gaussian location experiment. Data processing
for relative entropy gives

 KL(p_theta || p_(theta+Delta)) <= ||v||^2 Delta^2/(2r^2).

Writing A(theta)=log Z(theta), the left side is
 A(theta+Delta)-A(theta)-Delta A'(theta)
 = (Delta^2/2) Var_theta(K)+o(Delta^2).
Consequently

 Var_theta(K) <= ||v||^2/r^2 <= D_s^2/r^2,
 and hence n=r/(2D_s) <= 1/(2 sqrt(Var_theta(K)))

whenever the variance is positive. Additional theta-independent randomness in
the decoder does not change this argument. The condition is on exact output
laws; total variation approximation alone does not justify differentiating an
approximate sampler's law, so we do not silently extend the Fisher bound to it.

For the matching law in frozen_theorem_v1.txt, theta=log(b/a). At a=b and h>=2,

 Var(K)=2 h^2 (h-1)^2 / ((2h-1)^2(2h-3)) ~ h/4.

Self-contained verification: at equal weights the law is a uniform perfect
matching of 2h labeled vertices, h on each side. K=sum_{i=1}^h I_i, where I_i
indicates that first-side vertex i matches the other side. Then
 P(I_i=1)=h/(2h-1),
 P(I_i=I_j=1)=h(h-1)/((2h-1)(2h-3)), i!=j.
Computing E K and E K(K-1) yields the variance above. Thus every hypothetical
exact symmetry-preserving transfer must have D_s/r=Omega(sqrt(h)), and its
absorbable Gaussian center SD is at most order h^(-1/2). The covariance budget
cannot erase the already identified calibration scale.

7. STRONGER EXACT-INTERFACE OBSTRUCTION FOR THIS FINITE INTEGER FAMILY
There is, in fact, no exact fixed Gaussian-shift decoder for a nondegenerate
finite integer exponential-tilt family on ANY open theta interval. This rules
out the exact transfer interface itself, not approximate samplers or different
representations. The following elementary analytic proof was supplied by the
critic and reconstructed here; no historical originality is claimed.

Let b=v/r and q_k(y) in [0,1] be the conditional probability that the common
decoder outputs k on Gaussian-location input y. Its output probability extends
from real theta to the entire complex function

 F_k(z)=exp(-||b||^2 z^2/2) E[q_k(G) exp(-z b dot G)].

Gaussian exponential moments dominate the integrand uniformly on every compact
complex set, proving analyticity under the integral. For a deterministic decoder
q_k is simply an indicator. If F_k(theta)=c_k exp(k theta)/Z(theta) on an open
real interval, the identity theorem implies

 F_k(z) Z(z)=c_k exp(kz)

for every complex z. But for finite integer support containing at least two
points, Z(z)=exp(k_min z) p(exp(z)), where p is a nonconstant polynomial with
nonzero constant term. By the fundamental theorem of algebra p has a nonzero
complex root w. Choose z with exp(z)=w. Then Z(z)=0, whereas c_k exp(kz)!=0,
a contradiction. The matching family at every h>=2 and a,b>0 satisfies these
conditions. A theta-dependent decoder, nonlinear/non-Gaussian seed transport,
or approximate output would leave this exact interface and need a new proof.

VERDICT AND VALID TRANSFER
Keep the source identity as a conditional compiler device for deliberately
Gaussian-smoothed nested estimators with a proved pathwise query symmetry.
Reject it as a mechanism for acquiring unknown matrix parameters, removing
systematic calibration errors, or preserving arbitrarily conditioned laws.
For this branch the exact Fisher-information/shift-budget inequality is useful
as an audit criterion. It gives no new sampler or end-to-end acquisition advance.
The historic objective remains unachieved; full source validity and historical
priority were not assessed in this bounded check.
