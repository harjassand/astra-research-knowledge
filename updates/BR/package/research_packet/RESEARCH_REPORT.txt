HISTORIC OBJECTIVE: NOT ACHIEVED.

The strongest result of this session is an explicit separation between computing
a rare conditional distribution from its supplied description and acquiring that
distribution from an unknown physical source. For a two-mode Gaussian family we
derive the exact minimum number of copies for exact conditional sampling. It is
asymptotically 1.626678... times the herald count h, even though the unconditional
mean photon number is at most 1/4 and the conditional output mean stays bounded.
When the model label is supplied, a short exact rational sampler instead uses a
constant expected number of proposals and polynomial expected work in log h.

A more general construction samples the remaining photon counts after one
specified count h in a supplied positive Gaussian model, now including
nonnegative displacement. It avoids enumerating h photons. The programme
produced undisplaced samples at h=10^100 and a coupled displaced sample at
h=10^20. A positive-integral coefficient evaluator and two parity envelopes
remove the displaced case's apparent coefficient and bimodality obstacles.
This extends the archived implementation's scope. It does not establish a new
general sampling principle, a physical preparation method, or superiority over
all contemporary methods.

All proofs and reviews here are model-assisted internal work. No independent
human expert, proof assistant, laboratory, or peer review certified these results.
Historical novelty of the exact witness family and complete compiler interface
is unresolved. Several underlying mechanisms are explicitly established prior
art. The packet preserves failures and comparator results alongside successes.

1. EXACT ACQUISITION–COMPUTATION SEPARATION

The input interface is essential. The procedure knows the following two formulas
and h, but not which source it receives. It may use arbitrary collective or
adaptive measurements of independent, unconditional copies. Its ancillas,
classical advice and random bits do not reveal the hidden label. Preparation
unitaries, their inverses, and conditional-state oracles are not supplied. It must
return an unconditional output, rather than discard failed attempts for free.

For integer m>=1, set
  h=4m^2, a=1/4, t=1/(4m),
  B0=[[a,0],[0,0]], B1=[[a,t],[t,0]].
The normalized pure Gaussian state is
  |B> = det(I-B^2)^(1/4) exp((a_dagger)^T B a_dagger/2)|vacuum>.
Both matrices have operator norm below one. Let P_i be the photon-count law of
the second mode conditional on first-mode count h in state B_i.

Define
  A_j = product_{r=0}^{j-1}(1-2r/h)^2,
  Z_h = sum_{j=0}^{h/2} A_j/(2j)!,
  F_h = sqrt(1-(8h-1)/(15h^2)).
The empty product is one. Direct expansion of the Gaussian generating function
gives
  P0 = delta_0,
  P1(K=2j)=A_j/((2j)! Z_h).
F_h is the squared overlap |<B0|B1>|^2, not the overlap amplitude.

THEOREM A — EXACT FIXED-COPY OPTIMUM.
The minimum fixed number of copies permitting these exact unconditional output
laws, for both possible sources, is
  N_star(h)=ceil(log Z_h / (-log F_h)).
Its limiting rate is
  N_star(h)/h -> (15/4) log(cosh 1) = 1.626678...
The exact criterion is F_h^N<=1/Z_h. An integer boundary can be decided using
the rational inequality
  [1-(8h-1)/(15h^2)]^N Z_h^2 <= 1.
This is copy optimality, not a claim of optimal classical runtime.

Proof of necessity. An always-output protocol defines a POVM. Write M0 for the
effect returning zero. Exactness under source 0 implies M0|B0>^N=|B0>^N.
Since 0<=M0<=I, Cauchy–Schwarz yields
  F_h^N <= <B1|^N M0 |B1>^N = P1(0)=1/Z_h.
This proof allows arbitrary collective measurements and classical processing.

Proof of sufficiency. Measure whether the second mode is vacuum on each copy.
Source 0 never clicks. The probability of no click on source 1 is F_h^N, because
its vacuum component in that mode is proportional to |B0>. Let s=1-F_h^N and
q=1-1/Z_h. If any click occurs, emit a positive draw from P1 conditioned on K>0
with probability q/s; otherwise return zero. The criterion makes q/s<=1. Under
source 1, the positive mass is exactly s(q/s)=q, with the correct positive law;
under source 0 the output is always zero.

No arbitrary-real coin or free conditional oracle is needed. Z_h is a finite
rational sum, and the positive law is sampled as described below. Put
v=F_h^(2N), a rational number. Comparing a rational u>q with q/(1-sqrt(v)) is
equivalent to comparing v with (1-q/u)^2. Lazy fair-bit comparisons therefore
implement the algebraic rebalancing coin exactly. Exact setup uses polynomial
work in h, with O(h log h)-bit operands; it is not poly(log h) setup.

THEOREM B — ROBUST LOWER BOUNDS.
If each output law need only be within total variation 1/12 of its target,
every always-output fixed-copy method still needs
  N >= 5h/96.
More precisely, N >= log(36/35)/(-log F_h).
For arbitrary almost-surely terminating procedures, including adaptive copy
counts, max_i E_i[number of copies] >= 5h/9216 is a valid weaker bound.

Proof. A_0=1 and A_1=1 imply Z_h>=3/2, so TV(P0,P1)>=1/3. Approximate output
laws must be separated by at least 1/6. The trace distance of the two N-copy
pure states is sqrt(1-F_h^N), so
  1/36 <= 1-F_h^N <= N(1-F_h) <= 8N/(15h).
The expected-copy version truncates at floor(24M), where M is the maximum
expected count, and charges the truncation probability. Full details and the
success-probability bound for heralded attempts are in the acquisition audit.

THEOREM C — SUPPLIED-MODEL SAMPLING IS EASY FOR THIS FAMILY.
When the label is supplied, source 0 returns zero. For source 1, propose J>=0
with probability 2^(-J-1). Reject when 2J>h; otherwise accept with rational
probability 2^J A_J/(2J)!, and return 2J. Proposal times acceptance is exactly
A_J/[2(2J)!]. Total acceptance is Z_h/2>=3/4, so the expected proposal count
is at most 4/3. Geometric moments and exact rational coin sampling give expected
bit work polynomial in log h. There is no bounded worst-case random-bit claim.

The input mean photon number is at most 1/4: ||B1||<=1/2 and
tr(B1^2)<=3/16 imply tr[B1^2(I-B1^2)^(-1)]<=1/4. The target mean output is at
most sinh(1)<1.2. The copy barrier is therefore not caused by a growing typical
output or growing input energy. Directly waiting for the herald is much worse:
its probability is of order 4^(-h)/sqrt(h). A one-copy attempt conditioned on
its own successful herald can be exact; its success probability cannot be
omitted from the cost ledger.

The two-state quantum discrimination mechanism is classical prior art, including
Chefles–Barnett (1998), https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9808018, and Fuchs–van de
Graaf, https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9712042. This note supplies an explicit
Gaussian conditioning witness and exact operational specialization. It does not
claim priority for quantum discrimination, fidelity contraction or postselection.

2. SINGLE-HERALD GAUSSIAN COUNT COMPILER: UNDISPLACED BASELINE

Contract: rational, symmetric, entrywise nonnegative Bargmann kernel K with
ket/bra exchange symmetry, spectral radius rho(XK)<1, and globally zero
displacement. For a physical interpretation the kernel must describe a valid
density operator; positivity of its entries alone does not certify physicality.
One physical mode is observed at a nonzero-probability photon event h, encoded
in binary. Return the remaining count vector in binary to requested TV error
epsilon. The theorem does not cover arbitrary complex/signed kernels, multiple
herald modes, or displacement coupled into the herald.

Eliminate the unobserved doubled modes using rational Schur operations. The
effective herald matrix is [[a,b],[b,a]]. If k is the number of cross pairs,
then k=h modulo two and its unnormalized weight is
  w(k)=h!^2 a^(h-k)b^k /
       [k! 2^(h-k) (((h-k)/2)!)^2].
Its consecutive ratio is
  w(k+2)/w(k)=(b/a)^2 (h-k)^2/[(k+1)(k+2)],
which decreases. Exact ratio comparisons locate a mode using O(log(h+1))
comparisons. A dyadic rejection envelope samples this one integer using
certified log-factorial ratios, without constructing h tokens or an h-sized
factorial. Reverse Schur sampling then reconstructs aggregate counts using
binomial and negative-binomial laws. The loop shape is 1/2 plus the routed
edge count; omitting that count would be incorrect.

The ideal finite-bit construction has polynomial cost in the complete rational
input length, number of modes, log(h+1), and log(1/epsilon), using certified
polynomial-time elementary-function evaluations. Graph operations, stored
routing coefficients, and the number of scalar calls are O(modes^3). Small
Schur pivots and large output counts are charged through their bit lengths.
The implementation uses FLINT/Arb ball arithmetic. Its enclosure logic and
randomness caps were audited, but a uniform polynomial performance proof for
the actual Arb refinement implementation has not been supplied. Timings are
observations, not such a proof.

The final endpoint allocates epsilon/4 to capped uniform-integer ticket draws,
3epsilon/8 to the matching law, and 3epsilon/8 across reverse scalar calls.
It charges truncation, downward acceptance rounding, rejection caps and fixed
fallbacks. Guarantees assume independent unbiased random bits and valid
library enclosures; seeded runs are reproducibility diagnostics.

This law is a classical trinomial bridge. Generic log-concave rejection already
provides the relevant oracle mechanism: Chewi et al.,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14166. The complete compiler is an extension of Astra
N417/N418, whose supplied research status remains unverified externally. It is
not a new general solution to Gaussian boson sampling. Prior-art comparisons
include repeated-pair hafnians, photon-subtracted Gaussian formulas and existing
loop-soup constructions; the packet records their exact interface differences.

2A. DISPLACED EXTENSION AND ITS COEFFICIENT GUARANTEE

The final targeted extension admits an explicitly supplied rational nonnegative
displacement vector ell with the same ket/bra exchange symmetry. The positivity,
normalizability, physicality and single-herald promises still apply. Displacement
must be nonnegative in this representation; this does not include arbitrary
complex phases. Schur elimination now leaves [[a,b],[b,a]] and singleton
weights (c,c), all nonnegative. Put
  H_n(a,c)=[z^n] exp(a z^2/2+c z).
The cross-count law becomes
  w(k)=b^k H_(h-k)(a,c)^2/k!,  0<=k<=h.
It need not be unimodal: an exact h=2 counterexample is preserved. However,
H_(2j) and H_(2j+1) are each log-concave in j. Each is a convolution of the
log-concave sequence (a/2)^q/q! with respectively c^(2r)/(2r)! or
c^(2r+1)/(2r+1)!. Convolution preserves log-concavity with no internal zeros.
Multiplication by the reciprocal factorial then proves log-concavity of each
parity subsequence of w. Two envelopes can therefore be combined without
acquiring either parity normalizer.

THEOREM D — UNIFORM COEFFICIENT EVALUATION.
For rational a,c>=0, binary n>=0 and p>=1, one can recognize H_n=0 or produce
an absolute-width 2^(-p) enclosure of log H_n in deterministic bit work
polynomial in the input bit length, log(n+1), and p. An exact expanded rational
coefficient is not promised: that output can itself require Omega(n) bits.

Proof mechanism. For a,c>0 define d=c/sqrt(a), sigma=(-1)^n and
m=(d+sqrt(d^2+4n))/2. Folding the Gaussian moment integral before numerical
evaluation gives the positive identity
  H_n=a^(n/2)/(n! sqrt(2pi)) integral_0^infinity
      t^n exp[-(t-d)^2/2] [1+sigma exp(-2dt)] dt.
Normalize the integrand by its value at t=m. Its integral I is at least 1/3,
its tails outside |t-m|<=R are at most 6 exp(-R^2/2), and its modulus is less
than 32 on radius-one disks about the retained interval when m>=4(R+1).
Odd parity uses -expm1(-2dt), preserving the common zero as c tends to zero.
These bounds are uniform in n and displacement. Take R=O(sqrt(p)); small n
then needs only O(p) terms of the positive finite sum.

For large n, certified Taylor quadrature supplies the implemented evaluator.
An independent explicit proof uses rational Lagrange interpolation on panels
of width 1/2. With q+1 equally spaced nodes, the pointwise error is at most
(128/3)(2/3)^(q+1), and the total absolute quadrature weight is at most
2R(2e)^q. Thus q=O(p+log R) and polynomial guard precision suffice. This
alternative closes the existence proof without assuming a derivative oracle
or an unproved conditioning bound for the implemented Taylor recurrence.
The logarithmic prefactor uses m-d=n/m and certified logGamma evaluation;
its cancellation requires O(log n) extra precision, not O(n) precision.
Full bounds, elementary cases and executable alternate quadrature are in
work/gaussian_displaced_audit/report.txt and work/gaussian_transfer/displaced_followup.txt.

For sampling, exact peak comparisons would hide an unnecessary precision cost.
Instead, bisect the monotone within-parity log ratio, stopping when its entire
enclosure lies in [-delta,delta], delta=1/[4(h+1)]. Any rise away from this
anchor is then less than a factor two. Inflating dyadic-block heights by two
gives a combined envelope with mass at most 12 times the target mass.
A common exact dyadic log reference, certified downward rounding, and capped
rejection give the requested TV approximation without a normalizing oracle.

Given k, two independent own-pair counts q have weights proportional to
  (a/2)^q c^(n-2q)/[q!(n-2q)!], n=h-k.
Their successive ratios decrease, so the established scalar sampler applies.
The remaining singleton counts are n-2q. A source Poisson count and reverse
Schur reconstruction complete the sample. The endpoint allocates epsilon/4
to ticket failures and 3epsilon/4 across at most max_scalar_calls+4 scalar
draws, including the three matching draws and source Poisson draw.

Consequently the supplied-model, one-herald polynomial-bit construction extends
to nonnegative displacement. The actual Python/Arb implementation remains
ordinary audited numerical software; uniform worst-case runtime and complete
formal verification of that implementation are not claimed. These coefficients
are classical Gaussian moments/Hermite and parabolic-cylinder quantities; see
https://dlmf.nist.gov/12.7#E3 and https://dlmf.nist.gov/12.10. Neither the integral
identity nor generic log-concave rejection is a novel principle. Priority of
this complete encoded conditional interface remains unresolved.

3. VALIDATION AND COMPLETE COST LIMITS

The sampler passed exact matching, mode, interval and small-program identities;
independent auditors also tested mixed thermal kernels with known negative-
binomial output laws. Experiments included h=10^100 and 3, 5 and 8 modes.
The final JSON receipts, not rounded prose, record seeds, parameters, timings,
output bits, routing entries, ticket budgets and software versions.

The supplied-model records distinguish rational compilation and sampling where
measured. They include no physical calibration, state preparation or detector operation.
Python-traced memory excludes native FLINT allocations. Scalar precision
counters describe their stated instrumented scope, not every native operation.
Finite samples do not empirically verify a 1e-10 TV error guarantee.

Displaced validation included 80 exact cross-count laws and 920 exhaustive
interval/envelope checks, including the bimodal h=2 example. The normalized
rounded law's maximum exact TV error was 2.23e-12 at scalar tolerance 1e-9.
A separate three-mode displaced Fock recurrence at h=3, cutoff 22, had
certified missing mass below 9.34e-12; 1,600 samples agreed with its selected
probability-generating-function controls within 0.79 standard errors. Independent
coefficient checks included n=2^1000+1 and displacement 2^-1000, with a separate
quadrature construction as an additional comparison. These are finite controls;
the analytic bounds carry the general guarantee.

The separate software audit tested 14,000 whole-output draws against coherent,
squeezed-coherent, mixed displaced, and coupled pure-state controls. All 18
selected PGF comparisons were within 1.38 standard errors. It independently
verified 35 exact parity laws, 540 conditional own-pair bounds, random-bit caps,
source Poisson routing, and degenerate cases. A serious large-log reference
rounding bug was repaired; final narrow regressions also verified precision
retry and the exact quadrature-domain boundary. The full audit records source
hashes honestly: broad tests precede the last two narrow evaluator repairs,
whose changes and targeted final-code tests are recorded separately.

A three-mode displaced sample at h=10^12 took about 29.2 seconds and used 390
coefficient evaluations, with maximum recorded coefficient precision 873 bits.
The two-mode h=10^20 sample took about 81.4 seconds and 659 coefficient calls
at tolerance 1e-6. That timing includes scalar-law setup inside sampling but
excludes model construction; peak memory was not measured. Concurrent audit
workloads were present, so it is not an isolated performance benchmark.
At h=80, the displaced compiler's setup took about 0.000423 seconds versus
0.1025 seconds and 3,321 states for the archived dynamic programme; its first
draw was slower, about 0.00582 versus 0.00264 seconds. These matched small
controls preserve the negative timing as well as the setup improvement.
Repeated coefficient evaluation is the displaced implementation's main observed
cost. It is not evidence of a fastest known implementation.

The inherited exact dynamic programme is a matched small-count comparator,
not the strongest possible generic log-concave algorithm. Its setup was more
expensive as h grew, while some small cases were faster. No global fastest-
implementation or historic capability claim follows from this comparison.

Independent criticism found uncapped random-integer rejection and silent
fractional-count truncation. Both were repaired and retested; the earlier
receipts remain in the packet. Numerical precision/library complexity and
historical novelty remain explicitly separate from those repairs.

For the two-source acquisition example, the exact rational minimum-copy
checks give N_star=6,26,104 for h=4,16,64. Its classical simulator and algebraic
rebalancing procedure were executed; no quantum hardware was operated. The
measurement upper bound assumes ideal independent preparations and ideal
vacuum/nonvacuum detection. The lower bound allows arbitrary measurements, but
only the specified copy access, rather than coherent preparation-unitary access.

4. OTHER RESULTS AND NEGATIVE DECISIONS

Rare-event variance: exact fixed-proposal box verification is coNP-complete
even for arbitrarily tight common positive intervals, P=q, and unit downstream
moment bounds. A rational FPTAS was proved and implemented, but its existence
follows from Mittal–Schulz, https://web.mit.edu/schulz/www/epapers/ms-or-2013.pdf.
A simple O(d log d) secant/continuous-knapsack comparator beat its raw upper
bound on 25/26 frozen cases, certified 14 exact optima, and was 10–213 times
faster on the larger tested cases. The FPTAS approximates the second moment R,
not relative variance R-1. The continuation intervals remain supplied.

Inverse sensing: posterior variance and measured residual can both vanish
while an unseen anomaly remains. Exact acquisition lower bounds were retained;
independent random reconstruction audits are already in Ward's 2009 work.

System identification: an acquired finite-impulse-response tail certificate
was implemented under supplied order/stability and reset/noise assumptions.
Its core follows classical Turan–de Bruijn extrapolation, and stronger rational
Bernstein bounds improve its horizon. It is not a new learning capability.

Safe control: an exact noisy Lipschitz exploration frontier was reconstructed.
Finite observations cannot acquire the omitted regularity bound. Existing LoSBO
already contains the relevant mechanism; no new control principle was claimed.

Rare-event acquisition: generic black-box sampling retains inverse-probability
cost. A two-dimensional monotone membership model admits efficient deterministic
brackets, but its structural premise is indispensable; a hidden nonmonotone
component invalidates them. These are synthetic checks, not molecular evidence.

The OpenAI/math Gaussian absorption lemma was reconstructed from pinned source.
It redistributes deliberately injected Gaussian noise under an exact shift
identity; it does not remove unknown calibration error. A concrete counterexample
and stronger exact-interface obstruction are preserved in the handoff.
