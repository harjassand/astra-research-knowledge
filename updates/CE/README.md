# CE — Uniform entanglement-breaking powers and nonstationary words

This intake preserves three overlapping editions of the CP/PPT work and indexes only distinct interfaces:

- [N574](../../frontier/dossiers/N574.txt): a closed convex semialgebraic CP mapping-cone principle turning pointwise eventual entanglement breaking into one common arbitrary-word length. Source applications include PPT maps and the cone of maps that are both completely positive and 2-copositive.
- [N575](../../frontier/dossiers/N575.txt): a finite invariant-corner criterion for eventual entanglement breaking throughout such cones.
- [N576](../../frontier/dossiers/N576.txt): a separate finite algebraic criterion for eventual entanglement breaking of an individual algebraic CP map.
- [N577](../../frontier/dossiers/N577.txt): an explicit effect-matched EB approximation for nonstationary CP ∩ 2-copositive words, with a stated dimension-dependent rate and output-trace-relative trace-norm bound.

The earlier bistochastic-PPT sequence proof remains in the source editions but has no duplicate card: the broader arbitrary-PPT word result in N574 covers that stated application. The source manuscripts, differing manifests, scoped reviews, exactness barriers and later additions remain preserved as distinct immutable editions. N574's later cone/unrestricted-word arguments are outside the earlier focused review reports. For N575 and N576, the exact coverage of source reviews controls; intake does not extend those reviews. N577's quantitative approximation is separate from exact finite existence and does not provide an efficient measure-and-prepare decomposition.
