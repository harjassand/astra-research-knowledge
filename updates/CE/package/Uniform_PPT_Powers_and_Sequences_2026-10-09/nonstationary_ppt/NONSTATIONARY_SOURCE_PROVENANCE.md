## Mathematical dependency

- Kenneth R. Davidson, Laurent W. Marcoux, Heydar Radjavi, Transitive Spaces of Operators, arXiv:0706.2449v1. HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/0706.2449 . Theorem 4.9 supplies addition of transitivity degrees under product spans and hence full Kraus span after d strictly output-positive factors. Proposition 5.1 separately confirms that a transitive matrix space contains an invertible element, but the final proof uses the stronger Theorem 4.9 directly. Author-hosted PDF inspected through web extraction: https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~krdavids/Preprints/DavMarRadTrans2.pdf .

HTML inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/1801.05542 . Theorem 3.2 is the relevant projection-to-projection PPT block splitting. Theorem 4.4 proves eventual EB under equal-map iteration; it is not an arbitrary-product theorem.
