# Primary source comparison for uniform PPT iteration

Checked 9 October 2026. This is a narrow comparison, not a certification of priority or an exhaustive literature search.

## Park 2026

Sang-Jun Park, Every PPT channel has finite entanglement breaking index, arXiv:2608.13551v2, revised 17 August 2026. Primary version: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13551v2 . Submission history verified at https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13551 .

Theorem 1.1 gives n_0=n_0(Phi). Section 1.2 explicitly says: “it is still unknown whether there is a channel-independent bound of EB index.” Theorem 1.2 states bounds 3 and 5 under additional DSP_2 and DSP_3 hypotheses; Section 5 gives composition variants. These statements do not supply N(d) for the entire PPT cone. The paper's pointwise conclusion and PPT corner splitting are prior; the candidate contribution here is uniformization at fixed dimension. The phrase channel-independent can include the stronger dimension-independent ambition, so the precise comparison is to the absence of any stated bound depending only on d, not a claim to resolve all intended versions of that question. We do not audit the paper's unrelated Section 5 results.

## Published faithful input

Hanson, Rouze and Stilck Franca, Eventually Entanglement Breaking Markovian Dynamics: Structure and Characteristic Times, Annales Henri Poincare 21 (2020), 1517-1571; arXiv:1902.08173v2. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00023-020-00906-4 . Theorem 3.14 is the arbitrary-CP faithful left/right Perron input. Theorem 3.10 is its faithful-channel counterpart. These establish finite map-dependent indices; their mixing and separability margins do not provide the present uniform primitive-boundary step.

## Other close interfaces and search boundary

Lami and Giovannetti, arXiv:1411.2517, introduced iteration and filtering indices; its filtered questions are an important prior interface. Rahaman, Jaques and Paulsen (2018) established finite index for unital PPT channels. These results are credited as background, not claimed as new.

Targeted searches for PPT uniform EB-index bounds, dimension-dependent PPT indices, semialgebraic mapping-cone uniformization, and Lojasiewicz entanglement breaking did not locate a primary source stating the general theorem or the fixed-dimension all-PPT consequence. This negative search result is limited evidence. Search-engine summaries and machine reviews were not used as mathematical authorities. No priority, historical-significance or external-validation claim follows from the search.
