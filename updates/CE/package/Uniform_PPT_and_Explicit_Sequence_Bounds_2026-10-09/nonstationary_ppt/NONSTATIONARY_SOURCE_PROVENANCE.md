## Mathematical dependency

- Kenneth R. Davidson, Laurent W. Marcoux, Heydar Radjavi, Transitive Spaces of Operators, arXiv:0706.2449v1. HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/0706.2449 . Theorem 4.9 supplies addition of transitivity degrees under product spans and hence full Kraus span after d strictly output-positive factors. Proposition 5.1 separately confirms that a transitive matrix space contains an invertible element, but the final proof uses the stronger Theorem 4.9 directly. Author-hosted PDF inspected through web extraction: https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~krdavids/Preprints/DavMarRadTrans2.pdf .

- Mizanur Rahaman, Sam Jaques, Vern I. Paulsen, Eventually Entanglement Breaking Maps, arXiv:1801.05542v2. Version locator: https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05542v2 . HTML inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/1801.05542 . Theorem 3.2 is the relevant projection-to-projection PPT block splitting. Theorem 4.4 proves eventual EB under equal-map iteration; it is not an arbitrary-product theorem.

No PDF was downloaded into this workspace during this continuation, so no locally verified PDF hashes are asserted. The inspected version identifiers above concern the arXiv records; the author-hosted PDF has not been given an inferred matching version.

## Comparison that did not supply the conclusion

- Sang-Jun Park, Every PPT channel has finite entanglement breaking index, arXiv:2608.13551v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13551v2 . Inspected the introduction, Theorem 1.1, and the low-Schmidt-number composition discussion. Its eventual-EB theorem is equal-map iteration. Its restricted low-dimensional composition mechanisms do not establish unrestricted arbitrary words in fixed dimension.

The stored complete proof is an internally developed candidate for arbitrary bistochastic PPT words. No publication, priority, or externally certified novelty claim follows from this narrow comparison.

## Sources added for the exponential approximation synthesis

- Christandl, Mueller-Hermes, Wolf, When Do Composed Maps Become Entanglement Breaking?, arXiv:1807.01266v2, https://arxiv.org/html/1807.01266v2 . Theorem II.1 was checked directly: d-1 independently varying CP 2-entanglement-breaking maps compose to an EB map. Corollary III.1 is the arbitrary two-map qutrit PPT composition theorem used in the faithful-trajectory dimension-four consequence. Conjecture II.1 is explicitly not assumed.

- Li, Zhao, Fei, Fan, Liu, Mixed maximally entangled states, arXiv:0906.5445v2 (26 February 2012), https://arxiv.org/html/0906.5445v2 . Directly inspected Theorem 1 and the subsequent remarks: maximally entangled mixed states have the orthogonal Bell-flag structure; the discussion extends the classification to convex-roof concurrence.
