# Moving-support splitting and exact normalization for PPT words

9 October 2026. Rigorous reductions for arbitrary serial nonstationary composition. These do not establish the unrestricted finite-word theorem. The complete restricted bistochastic theorem is in BISTOCHASTIC_VARYING_WORD_THEOREM.md. No priority claim is made.

## 1. Rectangular moving-support split

Let Phi:M_a -> M_b be PPT. Let P on C^a and P' on C^b be projections, with Q=I-P and Q'=I-P'. Suppose

 Q' Phi(P) Q'=0.

The input and output ranks need not be equal. Every Kraus matrix has block form

 K_i=[[A_i,B_i],[0,C_i]]

between P+Q and P'+Q'. Positivity of the partially transposed Choi matrix implies

 sum_i A_i X C_i*=0                    for every X in P M_a Q.       (1)

Indeed the partial-transpose diagonal block indexed by Q' output and P input vanishes. Every row of a positive matrix having zero diagonal quadratic form vanishes; the resulting cross entries are precisely the coefficients in (1).

The coefficient-vector spaces generated by entries of all A_i and of all C_i are orthogonal in Kraus-index space. Rotate the Kraus index coordinates by a unitary adapted to these spaces. One collection of resulting Kraus matrices has C=0, and the other has A=0. Matrices with both A=C=0 may be assigned to either collection. Thus

 Phi=F+G,

where F is CP and output-supported on P', and G is CP and input-supported on Q. Neither F nor G is asserted to be PPT. The two diagonal corner maps, however, equal the corresponding CP compressions of Phi and are PPT.

This is a rectangular transport statement, not an invariant-subspace assumption. The related projection-to-projection splitting for bistochastic PPT channels is credited to Rahaman–Jaques–Paulsen, arXiv:1801.05542v2, Theorem 3.2, https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05542v2 . The proof above records the precise more general rectangular support interface used here, without a historical claim.

## 2. A whole moving-support word has only one-switch summands

Let Phi_i:M_d -> M_d, 1<=i<=n, be PPT. Suppose there are proper nonzero projections P_0,...,P_n such that

 supp Phi_i(P_(i-1)) <= P_i.

Apply Section 1 at every step to obtain Phi_i=F_i+G_i. Since F_i outputs in P_i and G_(i+1) is supported on input Q_i,

 G_(i+1) F_i=0.

Every nonzero expansion term therefore consists first of G factors and then of F factors in temporal order. In composition notation,

 Phi_n ... Phi_1
   = sum_{j=0}^n F_n ... F_(j+1) G_j ... G_1.            (2)

All summands are CP; no subtraction or summand-extraction from an EB map is used.

A run of m F factors contains a product of m-1 P-corner maps, with arbitrary CP maps at the two endpoints. A run of m G factors similarly contains m-1 Q-corner maps. This follows by inserting the output supports for F runs and the input supports for G runs.

Suppose every word of N_P PPT corner maps along the P spaces is EB and every word of N_Q along the Q spaces is EB. Then (2) is EB for

 n>=N_P+N_Q+1.                                         (3)

For each summand, either its F run has at least N_P+1 factors or its G run has at least N_Q+1 factors.

If all P_i have rank p, one may use the putative unrestricted lower-dimensional bounds N_P=N(p), N_Q=N(d-p). With varying proper ranks, rectangular corners embed into M_(d-1), so a valid unrestricted N(d-1) would give the conditional bound 2N(d-1)+1.

This conditional result does NOT use the bistochastic lower-dimensional theorem for nonbistochastic corners.

## 3. The strictly-positive boundary for full-output CPTP words

Assume, conditionally, the unrestricted arbitrary-PPT-word theorem in dimensions below d. Set B=2N(d-1)+1. Consider B CPTP PPT factors with Phi_i(I)>0.

If their product is not strictly output-positive, start from a pure density matrix with singular final output and set P_i to the supports of the successive outputs. Trace preservation makes every P_i nonzero. Full support can never shrink through a map with Phi_i(I)>0, so all P_i remain proper. Section 2 then shows the product is EB.

Thus on this full-output factor class, a sufficiently long fixed-window product has

 min_output_eigenvalue=0  ==>  EB.

This class is not compact as stated. Extending its boundary control to singular-output factors, and converting nonstationary small-margin estimates to exact EB, remain separate obligations.

For bistochastic factors the stronger plateau argument in BISTOCHASTIC_VARYING_WORD_THEOREM.md removes these obligations within that subclass.

## 4. Non-TP versus TP is an exact same-dimension reduction

For a fixed word length N, the following statements are equivalent:

(A) Every product of N arbitrary PPT CP maps M_d -> M_d is EB.
(B) Every product of N PPT CPTP maps M_d -> M_d is EB.

Only (B)=>(A) needs proof. Given Phi_1,...,Phi_N, replace each by

 Phi_(i,epsilon)=Phi_i+epsilon D,       D(X)=tr(X)I,       epsilon>0.

Set Y_N=I and recursively

 Y_(i-1)=Phi_(i,epsilon)*(Y_i).

All Y_i are positive definite because the depolarizing term has strictly positive action on every nonzero positive matrix. Define

 Theta_i=Ad_(Y_i^(1/2)) Phi_(i,epsilon) Ad_(Y_(i-1)^(-1/2)).

Each Theta_i is PPT by CP filter invariance, and

 Theta_i*(I)=I.

Hence each is CPTP. The filters telescope:

 Theta_N ... Theta_1
   = Phi_(N,epsilon) ... Phi_(1,epsilon) Ad_(Y_0^(-1/2)).

By (B) the left side is EB. Compose on the right by Ad_(Y_0^(1/2)); the regularized original word is EB. Let epsilon tend to zero and use the closedness of the EB cone.

There is no Perron eigenmatrix or stationary similarity in this argument. It does not normalize all factors to bistochastic maps.

## 5. Low-Schmidt-number separators give a precise alternative induction gate

Let E_0,...,E_k be CP maps on M_d with Kraus representations of rank at most r. Let Psi_1,...,Psi_k be PPT maps. If the unrestricted arbitrary-word bound N(r) is valid and k>=N(r), then

 E_k Psi_k E_(k-1) ... Psi_1 E_0

is EB.

For each chosen Kraus operator write K_j=U_j V_j through C^r. Expanding the E_j gives positive CP summands. Each summand factors into the outer CP maps Ad_(U_k), Ad_(V_0) and the k PPT maps on M_r

 Ad_(V_j) Psi_j Ad_(U_(j-1)),       1<=j<=k.

The middle product is EB by hypothesis; sum all resulting EB summands.

Consequently, an inclusion PPT_d subset SP_(d-1) would give the recurrence

 N(d)<=2N(d-1)+1

by using every other factor as a separator. This note does not establish or assume that inclusion. Singular global output or input support does give such a low-rank Kraus decomposition, but singular Choi rank with full marginals does not automatically do so.

For a fixed PPT seed whose entire output support has rank r<d, every filtered word with N(r)+1 occurrences is EB under the same lower-dimensional unrestricted hypothesis: compress each intermediate seed/filter block to its common output support. The input-support version follows by adjoints. These statements leave the full-marginal, Choi-singular seed case genuinely unresolved.
