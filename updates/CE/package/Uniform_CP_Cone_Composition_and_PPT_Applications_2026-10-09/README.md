## General composition principle

Let C be a nonzero closed convex semialgebraic cone of CP maps on M_d, invariant under arbitrary CP pre- and postcomposition. If EVERY member of C has some entanglement-breaking power, then there is a finite N(C) such that EVERY word of N(C) independently chosen members of C is entanglement breaking.

Under these full cone hypotheses, pointwise eventual EB, a common equal-map EB exponent, and a common arbitrary-word EB length are equivalent. Thus arbitrarily long entanglement-preserving words force the cone to contain some single map with no EB power. This implication is not asserted for arbitrary compact sets or classes lacking the cone hypotheses.

The full proof is nonstationary_ppt/SEMIALGEBRAIC_CONE_COMPOSITION_THEOREM.md. It isolates a nonzero transported cross coefficient by CP input/output filters into a two-dimensional triangular map. That map would preserve a nonzero coherence lambda^n and a qubit NPT witness at every power, contradicting the pointwise premise. This works even when the moving support ranks differ. A fixed zero-padded smaller cone inherits every hypothesis. The exact normalized-boundary and positive-anchor induction then supplies the uniform product length.

The proof gives existence, not a practical numerical exact exponent. The bound depends on the cone as well as dimension. Canonical PPT and CP-plus-2-copositive cones give dimension-only applications.

## Stronger application beyond PPT

TWO_COPOSITIVE_EXACT_WORD_EXTENSION.md verifies that arbitrary words of CP and 2-copositive maps also have a dimension-only finite exact EB length. A two-dimensional partial-transpose test directly supplies the same support split, and every normalization and corner interface is checked. These maps include non-PPT factors in dimensions at least three. 2-copositivity is not 2-entanglement breaking, and serial composition is not a tensor power.

The abstract cone principle covers still more classes: its final section constructs mapping cones generated by strictly output-positive seeds, including noncopositive qubit depolarizing seeds. That example verifies genuine scope beyond the copositive applications.

## PPT application

For every finite dimension d there is a finite N(d) such that EVERY serial product of N(d) arbitrary PPT completely positive maps on M_d is entanglement breaking.

The maps may differ at every step and need not preserve trace or the identity. No common state, stationarity, commutativity or finite alphabet is required. The exponent depends only on dimension. Its practical numerical value is not supplied, and the result does not assert N(d)=2. This is a serial-composition statement, not a tensor-power statement.

The full proof is nonstationary_ppt/UNRESTRICTED_PPT_WORD_THEOREM.md. Its key new step controls degenerating output normalization while retaining the actual factors. Fixed-trace forward states and unital normalized maps satisfy inverse-free intertwining and dual-state equations on a compact semialgebraic relation. At a zero-tag boundary, either a faithful intermediate state gives an EB prefix or singular intermediate states create a proper moving-kernel chain killed by lower-dimensional exactness. This gives a uniform normalized Kraus estimate. Multiplication strengthens its exponent; a maximal positive-margin anchor then supplies an actual separable interior margin in either direction.

## Explicit quantitative result retained

The exact theorem uses a non-numerical semialgebraic exponent. The independent explicit approximation remains useful. For d>=2, any length-n serial word T of CP, 2-copositive maps on M_d, n>=d−1, admits EB CP maps E and F with

 E=q_n T+F,  E*(I)=T*(I),  F*(I)=(1−q_n)T*(I),
 q_n=[1+4sqrt(2)d^2(1−1/(8d))^floor(n/(d−1))]^(-(d−1)).

For every reference system and positive joint input X,

 ||(id tensor (T−E))(X)||_1
 <= 2(1−q_n) tr[(id tensor T)(X)].

Thus normalization of a successful output introduces no inverse-success-probability penalty. The same E works for all inputs and references. For TP words E is an EB channel; a sufficient length for diamond error at most eta in (0,2) is

 n >= (d−1) ceil[8d log(8sqrt(2)(d−1)d^2/eta)].

This conservative O(d^2 log(d/eta)) rate follows from the Gaussian concurrence gap c_d<=1−1/(8d), explicit depolarizing repair and the published 2-EB composition theorem. It makes no optimal-rate or efficient Holevo-decomposition claim.
