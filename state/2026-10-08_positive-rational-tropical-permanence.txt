NEW PRINCIPLE A — ABSTRACT TROPICAL-RATE PERMANENCE
Consider
  xdot = sum_e a_e(t) rho_e(x) nu_e,
on R^d_{>0}, with a_e(t) measurable in [kappa,K], rho_e positive locally Lipschitz.
Assume there exist continuous/Lipschitz tropical gauges tau_e on [-1,1]^d and constants 0<c<=C<infinity such that for all sufficiently small h, all p in [-1,1]^d,
  c h^{tau_e(p)} <= rho_e(h^p) <= C h^{tau_e(p)}
uniformly in e (edge-specific constants can be globalized).

Define r-relevant edges E_r={e:r.nu_e !=0}. Call the system TROPICALLY ENDOTACTIC if for every r with E_r nonempty, every e in E_r attaining
  min_{f in E_r} tau_f(r)
satisfies r.nu_e>0.

Candidate theorem: conditional on the N33/OAI149 affine-minimum plateau machinery, every tropically endotactic system above has a common compact convex absorber in each positive stoichiometric class, uniform over all measurable coefficient paths a_e(t). Hence all trajectories are global and eventually uniformly separated from 0 and infinity classwise.

PROOF RECONSTRUCTION
1. Use the OAI149 affine approximation lemma with a pointwise tolerance E(r) small enough to preserve (i) the sign of each nonzero r.nu_e and (ii) every strict ordering tau_e(r)<tau_f(r). Lipschitz tau gives explicit order tolerance |tau_e(r)-tau_f(r)|/[2(L_e+L_f)] when nonzero. The lemma tolerates E with no positive global infimum.
2. The finite active-slope family yields positive uniform minima over its nonzero projection gaps and nonzero tau-order gaps.
3. At x=h^p and an active slope r, choose j minimizing tau among r-relevant edges. Tropical endotacticity gives r.nu_j>0. Every edge e with r.nu_e<0 has tau_e(r)>tau_j(r), hence tau_e(p)>=tau_j(p)+H for a finite-family H>0.
4. Rate envelopes give rho_e/rho_j <= (C/c)h^H for all negative-projection edges. Therefore
   r.f >= h^{tau_j(p)}[kappa*c*A - K*C*B*h^H] > 0
for sufficiently small h, uniformly in state/rate path. Since tau_j is bounded above on the compact p-cube, this supplies a positive fixed-scale drift delta_h.
5. N33 plateau/scale-absorption uses only this all-active-slope positive drift, so the remainder of the common-absorber proof transfers unchanged.

NEW PRINCIPLE B — EXACT POSITIVE-RATIONAL LIFTING (STRONGER PROOF ROUTE FOR BIOCHEMISTRY)
For each edge let
  rho_e(x)=P_e(x)/Q_e(x),
where P_e,Q_e are finite generalized polynomials/Laurent polynomials with strictly positive coefficients (real exponents allowed on the positive orthant). This includes standard positive Hill/Michaelis-Menten/competitive-inhibition factors and many King-Altman positive terms; reversible net laws must be split into positive forward/reverse directed terms.

Define
  tau_e(r)=min_{alpha in supp P_e} alpha.r - min_{beta in supp Q_e} beta.r.
Then rho_e(h^p) is bounded above/below by fixed positive constants times h^{tau_e(p)}.

There is also a direct reduction to N33:
- Let D(x)=product_f Q_f(x)>0.
- Reparametrize time by dt/ds=D(x). Then
    dx/ds = D(x) dx/dt
          = sum_e a_e(t(s)) P_e(x) product_{f!=e}Q_f(x) nu_e.
- Expand each positive generalized polynomial. Every monomial becomes a labelled power-law edge gamma -> gamma+nu_e with positive coefficient. Correlation among labels sharing a_e is harmless because N33 allows the larger set of independently variable bounded rates.
- For direction r, the minimum lifted source level for reaction e is
    min_gamma r.gamma
    = min supp(P_e).r + sum_{f!=e} min supp(Q_f).r
    = tau_e(r) + C(r),
  where C(r)=sum_f min supp(Q_f).r is common to all e.
Therefore ordinary endotacticity of the lifted power-law diagram is EXACTLY the tropical-endotactic condition above.
- N33 then gives a common absorber in s-time. Positive orbit-equivalent time reparametrization preserves the state trajectory. Globality/finite entry in original t follows because D is continuous positive and the transformed trajectory is bounded on every finite s-interval; s(t) cannot terminate at a finite value while t->infinity once the transformed solution is regular.

Thus, conditional only on N33, every positive-rational tropically-endotactic biochemical system has robust all-dimensional permanence under arbitrary bounded measurable multiplicative rate modulation.

FINITE VERIFICATION / ALGORITHM
For rational data with rational exponents, tropical endotacticity is finitely checkable:
- tau_e is piecewise linear from Newton polytopes of P_e,Q_e.
- Refine the normal fans of all numerator/denominator Newton polytopes by hyperplanes r.nu_e=0 and tau_e=tau_f on each linearity cone.
- On each relative-open cone/face, relevant-edge signs and tau order are fixed; check that every tau-minimal relevant edge has positive projection.
This avoids explicit product-denominator monomial expansion, though worst-case fan complexity can still be exponential. No polynomial-time claim.

BIOLOGICAL COROLLARY / SANITY CHECK
Two-gene Hill repression toggle:
  xdot = a_x(t)/(K_x(t)^n + y^n) - d_x(t)x
  ydot = a_y(t)/(K_y(t)^m + x^m) - d_y(t)y
with all a,d,K bounded in compact positive intervals.
Tropical exponents:
  birth X: max(-n r_y,0); death X: r_x;
  birth Y: max(-m r_x,0); death Y: r_y.
Quadrant proof: if any r_i<0, a death edge has negative exponent and the tau-minimal relevant death points inward (r.(-e_i)>0); if all r_i>=0 and r!=0, birth exponents are 0 and every relevant minimal birth has r.e_i>0. Hence the system is tropically endotactic and has a common absorber independent of arbitrary measurable parameter switching.
Bounded diagnostic: 20,000 angular directions for n=2,m=3 found no violation. This finite check is not proof evidence; the quadrant argument is exact.
Falsifier control: 1D xdot=+x^2-x fails the criterion in both boundary directions; at r=+1 the tau-minimal death edge points outward and x=0 is attracting for small x, as expected.

REAL-WORLD SCOPE / NONCLAIMS
This is a mathematical model theorem. It does not prove a biological pathway actually obeys a chosen Hill/MM model, that a treatment works, or that phenomenological rational propensities are valid molecular jump laws. Parameters/functional forms must be measured or experimentally justified. The strongest empirical falsifier is a calibrated model whose inferred positive-rational supports violate tropical endotacticity or whose trajectories leave the predicted robust compact regime under parameter paths inside the measured bounds.
N35 stochastic safety does NOT automatically transfer to Hill/MM propensities; a mechanistic mass-action expansion or a new jump-process theorem is needed.
