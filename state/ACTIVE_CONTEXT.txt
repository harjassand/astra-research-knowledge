A. KLS / LOG-CONCAVITY ATTACK: SHARPENED OBSTRUCTION
============================================================

A1. Existing mechanism reconstructed
Family 093 obtains a dimension-free LSI under a uniform linear-subgaussian hypothesis. Its contradiction mechanism first manufactures a near-critical eigenfield w and then a hierarchy whose frozen limit has a lognormal fluctuation L=exp(sqrt(8t) Z - 4t), E L=1. The decisive final step is an integrated information comparison for a Gaussian covariance observation. The lower bound grows like log t because a rare size-biased tail yields a conditional event with constant probability while its marginal probability is exponentially small in the scale parameter. The upper bound is finite because LSI controls entropy/Fisher information of the observation likelihoods.

N75 replaces the initial LSI information by a covariance-only Poincare plateau under Gaussian convolution:
for 0<s<P0,
P0 <= P(mu_s) <= P0 + ||Cov mu||op * s/(P0-s).
A hypothetical normalized KLS counterexample P0=1, ||Cov mu||op ->0 therefore has P(mu_s)->1 at each fixed s<1. The missing interface is not spectral; it is the entropy-like upper bound on observation likelihoods.

A2. Failed Hellinger / bounded-likelihood replacement
Poincare naturally controls quadratic/Hellinger-type dependence quantities, but these are bounded per scale. The family-093 scalar lower bound requires an unbounded contribution proportional to the rare-scale parameter h so that the scale integral produces log t. Replacing KL by a bounded divergence loses this logarithmic divergence.

A3. New scale-homogeneous quadratic no-go identity
Let L>0 with E L=1 and let phi be any measurable probe for which the integrals converge. Then
    int_0^infty E[phi(x L)^2] dx/x^2
      = E[L] * int_0^infty phi(u)^2 du/u^2
      = int_0^infty phi(u)^2 du/u^2.
Proof: for fixed L=l, substitute u=xl, so dx/x^2 = l du/u^2, then average.
Consequences:
- Any Poincare-native quadratic probe whose scale dependence enters only through xL is distribution-insensitive beyond E L.
- In particular it cannot see the growing lognormal time t and cannot reproduce the family-093 KL lower-bound divergence.
- Matrix-valued versions obtained by averaging bilinear probes inherit the same obstruction eigenvalue-by-eigenvalue.
This is a genuine failed-route certificate: simply replacing the KL observation functional by a scale-homogeneous variance/Hellinger functional cannot close N75 to KLS.

A4. Sharp entropy family 101 does not presently close the gap
Family 101 proves h(X)>=n+(1/2)log det Cov(X), equality only for affine products of one-sided exponentials. This supplies rigidity of entropy extremizers, but no derived argument forces a normalized KLS counterexample or the family-093 critical sequence into a near-equality regime. Do not treat family 101 as a KLS solution.

A5. What a successful KLS continuation now appears to require
At least one of:
(i) an unbounded dependence functional controllable from Poincare/covariance data;
(ii) an amplification/tensorization device that converts a bounded dependence measure into an unbounded scale contribution without paying an uncontrolled likelihood-ratio cost;
(iii) a new structural theorem forcing the critical KLS sequence into a class with an entropy upper bound;
(iv) a route that avoids the family-093 observation hierarchy entirely.
Do not retry plain Hellinger, chi-square of raw likelihoods, or homogeneous quadratic scale probes without a genuinely new ingredient.

B. POSITIVE-FIELD SPIN STATES: RANDOMIZED APPROXIMATE CT INTERFACE
============================================================

B0. Model and premise
Consider
H = (1/2) sum_{uv}[ d_uv Z_u Z_v - f_uv (X_u X_v + Y_u Y_v)]
    + sum_i h_i Z_i - sum_i g_i X_i
with rational f_uv >= |d_uv| and g_i>0 for every site. Let
A=cI-H >=0 entrywise,
V >= sum of local generator operator norms
as in Z/N109-N110, and let g_min=min_i g_i.

Algorithmic statements below are CONDITIONAL on Chen-Liu arXiv:2610.06724v1 Theorem 1.6 plus its every-execution finite-bit implementation. Polynomial costs are numerical in V/g_min (or V/Delta); if g_min is exponentially small in its bit encoding, the resulting runtime is correspondingly large. For a BPP-style statement impose g_min >= 1/poly(input size).

B1. Unique ground state
Because every g_i>0, A has a positive off-diagonal edge between every pair of computational basis strings differing in one bit. Hence the configuration graph is the n-dimensional hypercube and A is irreducible. Perron-Frobenius gives a unique positive normalized top eigenvector u. Therefore H has a unique ground state psi_0=u. This removes the selected-vector/degeneracy caveat of N110 in the strictly positive-field subfamily.

B2. Improved componentwise ground-amplitude floor
Let lambda=lambda_max(A). Since ||A||<=V, lambda<=V. For Hamming neighbors x,y differing at bit i,
lambda u(x)=sum_z A_xz u(z) >= g_i u(y) >= g_min u(y),
so u(x)>= (g_min/V) u(y).
Choose y with u(y)>=2^{-n/2}. Every x is at Hamming distance at most n, hence
    u(x) >= gamma_plus
    gamma_plus := 2^{-n/2} (g_min/V)^n.
This is much stronger than the general N111 floor 2^{-n} min(1,r/V)^{2n^2}. It uses all g_i>0 and hypercube diameter n.
Thus
    log gamma_plus^{-1}
       <= (n/2) log 2 + n log(V/g_min),
which is polynomial in the numerical resource ratio.

B3. Imported physical gap
Astra Z maps this subfamily by a one-qubit basis rotation into the Suzuki-Fisher class of Bravyi-Gosset-Liu-Wong. Their imported Lee-Yang theorem supplies
    Delta >= 2 g_min
for the corresponding site-dependent positive-field instance (retain source-scope check before publication).
This gap is prior work, not an Astra discovery.

B4. Uniform componentwise cooling
N110 gives
    ||psi_tau-psi_0||_2 <= sqrt(2) 2^{n/2} exp(-tau Delta),
psi_tau = e^{-tau H}|+>/||...||.
Choose tau so the RHS is <= (eta/16) gamma_plus. Then every coordinate obeys
    |psi_tau(x)-psi_0(x)| <= (eta/16) psi_0(x),
simultaneously for all x.
It is enough that
    tau Delta = O(n + log(1/eta) + log gamma_plus^{-1})
              = O(n log(V/g_min)+log(1/eta)).
With Delta>=2g_min this gives tau V polynomial in V/g_min, n and log(1/eta).

B5. Relative point-amplitude oracle
N109 supplies a positive Euler-product vector 	ilde v with componentwise 1+-alpha relative approximation to e^{tau A} 1, using k0=O(tau V+n+log1/alpha), m=O(k0^2/alpha). A fully pinned output amplitude and the global squared norm are positive Chen-Liu contractions. Relative FPRAS estimates therefore yield, for any requested x,
    p_0(x)=psi_0(x)^2
to relative error eta, and because psi_0(x)>0, psi_0(x)=sqrt(p_0(x)) to relative O(eta).
The cooling precision depends only logarithmically on gamma_plus^{-1}, not on gamma_plus^{-1} itself.

B6. Classical Born sampler
For a prefix a define w(a)=sum_z 	ilde v_{az}^2. N109 already shows these are positive doubled contractions. Sequentially:
- estimate w(a0), w(a1) relatively;
- sample the next bit from their estimated ratio.
If both child weights are within 1+-rho, the conditional probability is multiplicatively within (1+rho)/(1-rho). Taking rho=Theta(epsilon/n) makes the entire path distribution multiplicatively 1+-O(epsilon) close to the exact 	ilde v Born law. Fresh FPRAS randomness permits an ordinary union bound over the O(n) actually queried prefixes; no exponential prefix union bound is needed.
Combining with B4-B5 gives a randomized classical sampler for the unique ground-state computational-basis Born distribution, to arbitrary inverse-polynomial total variation / multiplicative path accuracy.

B7. Candidate theorem: randomized approximate computationally tractable (RACT) ground state
The positive-field unique ground state has:
(1) a randomized polynomial-time classical Born sampler;
(2) a randomized polynomial-time relative point-amplitude oracle for every basis string.
This is the randomized approximate analogue of Van den Nest's computationally tractable (CT) state interface. It is not strict exact CT because both capabilities are approximate/randomized.

Nearest prior art:
- Van den Nest's CT framework is old; the new content is placing this interacting ground-state family into an approximate randomized CT-like interface using the October 2026 counting theorem.
- Wei 2609.26802v2 gives deterministic ground energies and stable-product relative-amplitude queries for bounded-degree, bounded-strength, fixed-positive-field Suzuki-Fisher systems. The present candidate differs by arbitrary graph degree (network size polynomial in |E|), direct Born sampling, basis-amplitude queries for every x, rare conditional sampling, and the sparse-observable consequences below. B8. Sparse-observable simulation
Let O be Hermitian and efficiently row-sparse in the computational basis, with at most s=poly(n) nonzeros per row and polynomially bounded entries. For exact psi_0 define
    F(x) = (O psi_0)_x / psi_0(x),
sampled at x~p_0.
Then
    E[F] = <psi_0|O|psi_0>,
    E[|F|^2] = ||O psi_0||_2^2 <= ||O||^2.
Thus ideal Monte Carlo has variance bounded by ||O||^2.

With relative amplitude estimates, define F_hat by replacing each ratio psi(y)/psi(x). On the event that all queried amplitudes are relative-eta accurate,
    |F_hat-F| <= O(eta) F_abs(x),
    F_abs(x)=sum_y |O_xy| psi(y)/psi(x).
Moreover
    E[F_abs^2] = || |O| psi_0 ||^2 <= || |O| ||^2.
For Hermitian s-sparse O with max entry C, || |O| || <= s C.
Hence eta=poly^{-1} suffices. FPRAS failure probabilities can be made as small as poly*gamma_plus^2 at only logarithmic cost in gamma_plus^{-1}, controlling the worst-case ratio 1/psi(x).
Conclusion: inverse-polynomial additive expectation values of polynomial-sparse observables are classically estimable in randomized polynomial time. In particular this covers polynomial-size sums of Pauli strings and standard local Hamiltonian/correlation observables, not only O(log n)-supported observables.
This is the approximate-randomized analogue of the standard CT + efficiently-computable-sparse (ECS) theorem.

B9. Logarithmic-subsystem tomography and the q=1 bypass
For a specified subsystem S of k qubits,
    rho_S[a,b] = sum_z psi_0(az) psi_0(bz).
Each numerator is a positive ket/bra contraction after the Z bra-complement sewing, and the denominator is the norm contraction.
For d=2^k, estimating every d^2 entry to absolute eta_entry gives
    ||rho_hat-rho||_1 <= d^{3/2} eta_entry
by Frobenius plus rank.
If k=O(log n), d=poly(n), so choosing eta_entry=epsilon/d^{3/2} is polynomial.
Therefore a specified O(log n)-qubit reduced density matrix can be reconstructed to inverse-polynomial trace distance. Consequences:
- full local entanglement spectrum to additive accuracy;
- von Neumann entropy S_1(rho_S) to additive accuracy using Fannes-Audenaert continuity;
- any continuous spectral functional of that logarithmic subsystem.
This bypasses the N98 black-box q=1 obstruction only for logarithmic-size subsystems. It does NOT yield S_1 for an extensive subsystem.

B10. Global Born Shannon entropy
Because every p_0(x)>=gamma_plus^2,
    -log p_0(x) <= 2 log gamma_plus^{-1}.
Sampling x~p_0 and relatively estimating p_0(x) therefore gives an additive Monte Carlo estimator of the full n-bit computational-basis Shannon entropy H(p_0), with sample complexity polynomial in log gamma_plus^{-1} and epsilon^{-1}. This is a classical measurement-distribution entropy, not the von Neumann entanglement entropy.

B11. Rare-cylinder conditional RACT without rejection
For any nonempty computational-basis cylinder E, strict positivity implies
    p_0(E)>=gamma_plus^2.
Use the longer B4 cooling so every ground amplitude is approximated relatively. Pin E in all contractions.
Then:
- p_0(E) has a relative FPRAS;
- the conditional Born distribution p_0(.|E) can be sequentially sampled from pinned prefix contractions;
- conditional amplitudes psi_{0,E}(x)=psi_0(x)/sqrt(p_0(E)) can be relatively queried;
- the conditional state therefore has the same randomized approximate CT interface;
- polynomial-sparse observables and O(log n)-subsystem density matrices of the conditioned state are classically estimable.
The runtime has no 1/p_0(E) or 1/sqrt(p_0(E)) factor; rarity enters only through log gamma_plus^{-1} in the cooling time and confidence amplification.
Interpretation: this is a model-specific rare-event conditional simulator, not a general solution to rare-event sampling.

C. EPR* HIGHER-SIGNIFICANCE TARGET: CURRENT EXACT OBSTRUCTION
============================================================

C1. Why this target matters
Marwaha-Sud 2604.13026 reduce the remaining positive-weight symmetric-interaction classification to EPR* and conjecture EPR* in BPP. Closing this would complete their phase classification and is higher-significance than B7-B11.

C2. Existing Z obstruction reconstructed
For |s|<1, after a common rotation,
K'_s=(1/2)(-XX+sYY+ZZ).
With A=(1/2)I-K'_s, a positive Euler step has critical eight-vertex weights
a=1, b=1+t, c=t(1-s)/2, d=t(1+s)/2,
so b=a+c+d.
The complemented local symbol contains a positive constant and nonzero positive quadratic Hessian at the origin, hence is not log-concave at any sufficiently small step. Exact local exponentials remain on the same critical surface. The local-cone theorem rules out every differentiable nonnegative near-identity representation of the same 4-port form.

There is an exact positive three-connection decomposition, but the d-connection is a bit-flipped SWAP. Around a triangle the signed-swap word can have trace 0 while erasing flips gives trace 4. Thus ordinary loop counting by 2^{#loops} is false.

For on-site charge rotations, the weak singular axis is uniquely Y and the edge commutator vanishes iff vertex signs alternate. Therefore an odd physical cycle gives an exact Z2 holonomy obstruction to the number-conserving easy-plane reduction.

C3. Attempted Chen-Liu two-measure escape
The fact that a single local gate polynomial is not log-concave does not by itself exclude an auxiliary-variable representation as a coefficient/convolution of two global log-concave measures. This was examined conceptually via the three-connection decomposition.
A fixed connection configuration is a signed permutation/affine bit map. Its spin trace is
    2^{#cycles} if every cycle has even flip parity, else 0.
Equivalently it is the number of solutions to (I-P)x=f over F2.
The parity condition is a genuine global cycle-space constraint. A loop-count-only reduction discards exactly the information that makes odd cycles frustrated.
No acquired factorization was found that converts the selector/parity constraint into two log-concave measures covered by Chen-Liu while retaining positive coefficients and polynomial support acquisition.

Useful algebraic observation:
for a signed permutation matrix S associated to one connection configuration, the affine fixed-point trace equals
    product_cycles (1 + sign_product_on_cycle).
This is also permanent(I+S) cyclewise, but I+S is signed and the permanent representation introduces cancellation; it does not yield a nonnegative FPRAS. Determinant variants acquire cycle-length-dependent signs and likewise do not remove the frustration on arbitrary nonplanar graphs.
This does not prove hardness or rule out a more sophisticated auxiliary lift.

C4. Current strongest obstruction / next missing lemma
The strongest route to the field-changing EPR* result now requires an acquired polynomial-time representation/sampler for the frustrated critical eight-vertex loop model that retains Z2 cycle parity. Plausible interfaces:
- an auxiliary-variable two-log-concave factorization compatible with Chen-Liu;
- a positive double-cover/gauge lift whose projection cost is polynomial;
- a rapidly mixing cluster/worldline chain whose state includes parity holonomy;
- a determinant/Pfaffian or matroid representation eliminating cancellation;
- a new gadget reducing EPR* to the admitted positive-kernel cone without exponential precision.
Absent one of these, the existing local LC/onsite-basis routes are genuinely blocked, not merely unfinished.
