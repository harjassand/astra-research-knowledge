## Strongest unconditional result

On the even cycle `C_(2r)`, exact degree one at every vertex is the common-base
constraint for two partition matroids. Its only feasible allocations are the
two alternating perfect matchings. The target `y=(1/2,...,1/2)` forces their
fair mixture. Feasible counting is exactly two; an exact uniform sample costs
one fair bit. Granting these ideal services cannot improve the following
output barrier.

Give even edges information contribution `diag(1,0)` and odd edges
`diag(0,1)`. These are positive rank-one matrices of norm one. The fractional
information is `F=(r/2)I`, and `V=sum H_e^2=rI`. Every feasible integral
allocation is singular and has operator error `r/2`. Its error divided by
`sqrt(||V||)` grows as `sqrt(r)/2`, even in matrix order two. Thus a universal
dimension-independent rank-one spectral rounding guarantee for arbitrary
two-partition common bases is impossible.

Retain exact marginals and hard cardinality `r`, and write `q` for the
probability that all degree budgets are exact. For
`Z=sum (-1)^e b_e`, every such law satisfies the sharp bounds

`Var Z >= q r^2+(1-q)(r mod 2)`,

`E ||sum b_e H_e-F||^2 >= [q r^2+(1-q)(r mod 2)]/4`.

A four-atom law attains both bounds for every rational `q`. Its two repair
atoms each have exactly one degree-zero and one degree-two resource, which
is the minimum total degree-error cost of two. At `q=0`, the information
matrix has minimum eigenvalue `floor(r/2)` and hard error zero for even `r`,
or `1/2` for odd `r`. Any hard nonsingularity promise forces `q=0` in this
instance. This gives a concrete protocol-admission test and an explicit
budget relaxation. Alternating-cycle geometry is classical; historical
priority of this exact Pareto formulation is unestablished.
